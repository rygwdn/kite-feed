    More than two dozen technology companies and organizations, including Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Palantir, Hugging Face, IBM and Dell, backed a public letter titled “Open Weights and American AI Leadership” that urged U.S. policymakers to avoid “premature restrictions” on open-weight AI models [8][19][28][29].   • Cambridge scholar transcribes Alan Turing’s unpublished short story

The New York Times described the manuscript as presenting a different side of the mathematician in the year before his death [4].
