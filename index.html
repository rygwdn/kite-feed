    The Federal Communications Commission on July 28 added foreign-produced “advanced robotic devices” and connected power inverters to its Covered List, generally barring new models from receiving the equipment authorization required for import, marketing or sale in the United States [3][6][8][11]. The rule covers many ground-based mobile robots, including robot vacuums, lawn mowers, delivery robots, humanoids and quadrupeds.
