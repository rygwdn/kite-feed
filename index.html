  • OpenAI scientist proposes shared limits on frontier AI development

    AI 2026-11-23
    OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has proposed that frontier AI developers agree on shared capability limits based on perplexity and other measures, and refrain from crossing them until their ability to monitor and understand model behavior meets defined safety milestones. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman endorsed the proposal, but Pachocki specified no initial threshold, start date or enforcement mechanism. It remains a call for an industry agreement, not an adopted restriction [21][33][2][25]. Perplexity measures how well a model predicts the next token, with lower scores indicating better predictions. Pachocki says the metric can track pretraining progress as task-based tests become outdated. He argues that AI could accelerate research into pretraining methods as early as 2027, potentially outpacing improvements in safety monitoring [33][21]. He also acknowledged that perplexity alone cannot provide a common yardstick: labs use different training mixtures and evaluation data, and the measure misses some capability gains from reasoning after pretraining. Pachocki’s proposal calls for shared evaluation, red-team and transparency standards alongside staged limits, with further development dependent on concrete advances in monitoring [3][18][19].
