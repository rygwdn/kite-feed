  • Adult T. rex trackway discovered in North Dakota

    Researchers at the Denver Museum of Nature and Science and Liverpool John Moores University have identified the first known trackway made by an adult Tyrannosaurus rex. The trackway stretches about 23 feet and contains two left and two right three-toed prints, each roughly 3 feet long. Researchers said the sequence provides evidence of movement that fossil bones alone cannot show [2].

  • Nighttime light linked to structural heart changes in 11,071 people

    Nighttime light exposure was associated with changes in heart structure and function in a study of 11,071 UK Biobank participants led by Tulane University researchers. Participants wore wrist light sensors for seven days, and researchers examined their hearts with cardiovascular MRI about three years later.
