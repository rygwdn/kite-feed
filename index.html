  • Butterfly wing patterns confuse predators during flight, study finds

    Science 2026-10-01
    Researchers at the universities of Exeter and Essex reported in Nature that butterfly wing patterns and wingbeats combine to create misleading motion cues, making it harder for predators to judge the insects' speed and direction. The team used high-speed filming, computer modelling and a virtual butterfly-catching experiment to investigate the effect [4][5][7].
