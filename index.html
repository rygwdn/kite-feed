    Researchers propose that supercritical water in magma could explain how volcanic deposits formed more than 6,000 meters down the Mariana Trench wall, building on the discovery of unusually deep pyroclastic rocks. The team says this may have enabled magma to fragment without the force of a land eruption [9][6][1]. The team plans further analysis of the volcanic material and aims to explore farther along the plate boundary.
