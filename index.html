    Scientists have formally identified Leopardus tilcayo, a previously unknown wild cat living in Bolivia’s Yungas cloud forests.   • Stanford researchers find the human brain has two developmental origins

    A Stanford Medicine-led study published in Nature Neuroscience found that the human brain develops from two distinct groups of embryonic progenitor cells rather than a common source.
