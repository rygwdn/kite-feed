  • Stanford team creates 16 AI-designed bacterial viruses

The researchers used Evo, a genome language model trained on large DNA datasets rather than text, and narrowed the design target to Phi X-174, a small bacteriophage that infects E. coli [10][20][27][57].     Researchers at Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis and Princeton University reported in Neuron that early-life stress in mice altered dopamine-producing neurons in the ventral tegmental area, a brain region involved in reward, motivation and stress responses [3][4][5][6]. The study examined a possible biological mechanism by which severe childhood stress can increase vulnerability to anxiety, depression and other mood disorders later in life [4][6].
