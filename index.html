  • Astronomers use stellar spectra to model first green white dwarf

    University of Warwick astronomers used stellar spectra and a model of human vision to identify a white dwarf that would appear green, the first such star reported. The object, WD J1330+6435, has a temperature of about 4,000 degrees Celsius and lies 284 light-years from Earth.   • Study finds water triggers reactive chemistry on microplastics
