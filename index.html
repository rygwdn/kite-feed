  • T. rex had human-like body temperature, tooth study finds

    Science 2026-09-17
    A study published in Science Advances estimates that Tyrannosaurus rex had a body temperature of about 36.3 degrees Celsius, or roughly 97 degrees Fahrenheit, based on chemical analysis of fossilized tooth enamel.   • Climate change helped destabilize Nepal glacier collapse, study finds

    Science 2026-09-17
    A World Weather Attribution assessment concluded that human-caused warming helped create conditions for the Aug. 26 collapse of rock and glacier ice from Langtang Lirung near Nepal’s border with China. The collapse unleashed a destructive flood of water, ice and debris through Himalayan valleys. The disaster killed more than 1,300 people, with death estimates approaching 1,400, while more than 5,000 people remained missing; Nepali authorities later revised the missing-person figure to 6,150 [1][2][8][10]. Researchers described a compound process rather than a single trigger: warmer temperatures drove glacier retreat, permafrost thaw and increased meltwater, while pre-existing geological weaknesses—including possible damage from Nepal’s 2015 earthquake—left the slope vulnerable. The collapse involved roughly 110 million cubic meters of rock and glacier ice and occurred at an elevation of about 5,150 meters. Scientists said climate change destabilized the slope but did not establish that the collapse would not have occurred without warming [1][8][10][17].
