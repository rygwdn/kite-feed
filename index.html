  • Labor data complicate warnings over AI job losses

    AI 2026-07-01
    New labor-market data and expert commentary are challenging broad claims that generative AI is already causing widespread job losses, even as companies continue to cite AI in layoff announcements.     NASA delayed the launch of a $30 million robotic rescue mission for the Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory because of unfavorable weather, setting the next attempt for no earlier than July 1 at 09:43 GMT from Kwajalein Atoll in the Marshall Islands [15][19][22]. The mission will use Katalyst Space Technologies’ LINK spacecraft, launched on a Pegasus rocket released from an aircraft, to reach Swift in low Earth orbit and attempt to raise the telescope before it re-enters the atmosphere [7][15][13]. The rescue plan calls for LINK to find Swift, maneuver around it, latch on with three movable arms and tow it roughly 300 kilometers higher over at least a month [14][15][22]. Katalyst built the spacecraft for the mission, which NASA officials said includes several firsts [15][14].
