Carney marks sixth Truth and Reconciliation Day in Edmonton

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney spoke at an Edmonton ceremony on Sept. 30, 2026, marking Canada’s sixth National Day for Truth and Reconciliation. He dedicated his remarks to Indigenous children who survived residential schools and those who never returned home, and described the country’s history as a “dark chapter” [6][1][5]. Carney said some progress had been made, but deep wounds and wide gaps remained. He said the government had a responsibility to reflect and act together [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Reconciliation is a journey, not a destination. It's an attitude, not an achievement

— Mark Carney (CBC News)

Key Points

  • Ceremony venue: The Edmonton ceremony took place at Fort Edmonton Park [5].
  • Ottawa observance: On Parliament Hill, Governor General Louise Arbour helped steady a post as residential school survivor Bernard Andreason tied off ropes during the reraising of the Survivor’s Flag [6].
  • Hometown visit: Carney returned to Edmonton, his hometown, for the National Day ceremony [7].
