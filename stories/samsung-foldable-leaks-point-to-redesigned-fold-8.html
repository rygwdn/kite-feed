Samsung foldable leaks point to redesigned Fold 8 hinge

Category: Technology

Samsung has reportedly redesigned the hinge for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 series to sharply reduce the visible display crease, Android Authority reported, citing tipster Ice Universe [1]. The claim covers both the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, whose crease performance was described as comparable to OPPO’s Find N6 [1]. The same report said the new folding mechanism could make opening and closing feel more deliberate, but may also make the phones less able to stay propped open at certain angles [1]. Google News linked to separate reports on additional Galaxy foldable leaks and promotions, including reported prices for the Galaxy Z Flip 8, Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra, a reported S Pen return for the Fold 8 Ultra with a limitation, leaked images of a wider foldable, and a RM700 Samsung Members e-voucher for the next foldable [2][3].
