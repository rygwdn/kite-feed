South Korea targets sovereign AI model and factory adoption with 2027 plan

Category: AI

South Korean President Lee Jae Myung’s 2027 budget proposal allocates 4.7 trillion won to developing a domestic AI model, alongside a 21.3 trillion won package for semiconductors, physical AI and AI data centers. Lee told parliament on Nov. 2 that the model should achieve world-leading performance. The proposal also calls for training 2,000 specialists in semiconductor design and regional industries and expanding AI education for students, job seekers and workers [6][8][18]. Korean industry and academic experts say investment in chip plants and data centers must translate into broader industrial use and stronger domestic capabilities. The government describes the three projects as a platform linking chips, AI computing infrastructure and manufacturing applications. Lawmakers must still approve the budget by Dec. 2 [5][20][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Infrastructure: The proposal sets aside 2.1 trillion won for power, water and industrial-park infrastructure to prevent bottlenecks from delaying corporate investment [18].
  • Strategic technologies: Lee said 13.8 trillion won would go toward 10 strategic technology fields intended to drive future growth [18].
  • Research funding: The budget plan allocates a record 39.5 trillion won to research and development, with an increase of 4 trillion won for future technologies [18].

Perspectives

Lee Jun, head of the strategic-industry research center at the Korea Institute for Industrial Economics and Trade, warned that the semiconductor buildout risks concentrating on memory production. He called for policy support to extend demand to fabless chip designers and companies involved in advanced packaging [5].

Sources: Electronic Times

Park Min-won, president of Changwon National University, argued that manufacturing data and shop-floor expertise should become domestic AI assets. He proposed setting rules in advance for where data is stored and how it can be reused, used for retraining, retrieved or deleted [5].

Sources: Electronic Times

Jung Byung-sun, a professor at Sogang University’s graduate school of technology management, said the investment plan emphasizes factories and data centers but leaves a gap between hardware construction and productivity gains on factory floors [5].

Sources: Electronic Times

Timeline

  • September 2026: Government submitted its proposed 2027 budget to the National Assembly [4]
  • 2 November 2026: President Lee presented the budget and urged lawmakers to pass it [4]
  • 2 December 2026: Statutory deadline for parliamentary approval of the 2027 budget [4]

Technical Details

  • Fab schedule: The industry minister said streamlined permitting and a proposed super-regional special-zones law would aim to bring semiconductor fabrication plants into operation more than two years earlier [20].

Industry Impact

  • Private investment: The three megaprojects are expected to draw 1,600 trillion won in private investment, which the industry minister said was equivalent to about 60% of GDP [5].
