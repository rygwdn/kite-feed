Deutsche Telekom invests €100 million in Mistral for German AI

Category: AI

Deutsche Telekom’s investment arm, T.Capital Tech Fund, invested €100 million in French artificial-intelligence company Mistral AI’s Series D funding round, calling the deal a first step toward a strategic partnership to develop AI services for German businesses and public institutions [13][29]. The companies say they will focus on industrial and regulated sectors, combining Telekom’s infrastructure and customer access with Mistral’s models, enterprise software and deployment capabilities [13][25]. The companies say they aim to give customers local control of data and computing: Telekom says its infrastructure can support sovereign AI, while Mistral says European enterprises should be able to run its models on their own data and systems [13][29]. Mistral raised €3 billion in September, taking its valuation above €21 billion [7][29]. Telekom has not disclosed the equity stake represented by its investment, a revenue contribution or a launch date for joint products; the companies have announced plans for a partnership, not launched joint offerings [15][25]. Telekom is also expanding its Munich AI factory, which it opened in February, after strong demand, with capacity set to double [9][30].

Key Points

  • Existing products: Telekom already uses Mistral’s Small 3 model in Voice AI Notes, which summarizes business calls in the Open Telekom Cloud; a mobile version based on Mistral’s Small 4 model followed in September [25].
  • Compute expansion: Telekom plans to raise its Munich AI factory’s capacity from 12 to 20 megawatts, adding nearly €250 million to its investment in the site [25][15].
  • Round backers: Samsung led Mistral’s September financing, with the EU-backed Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity as co-leads; ASML, BlackRock-managed funds and Luxembourg also invested [12][7].
  • Customer footprint: Mistral says it serves more than 125 global enterprise customers in 20 countries, including Airbus, ASML and HSBC [30].
  • Customer uses: Industrial customers including Siemens and Daimler Truck use Telekom’s Munich computing infrastructure for language-model training, robotics development and production-process simulation [30][27].

Perspectives

T.Capital Tech Fund: Partner Maneesh Sharma says the fund sees significant potential for Mistral to become a global enterprise-AI leader, describing the investment as confidence in the team, technology and market [13].

Industry Impact

  • German competition: SAP's Delos Cloud is working with OpenAI on a public-sector offering, while the Schwarz Group is building a sovereign cloud with Stackit and Schwarz Digits [11].
  • Multiple infrastructure partners: Mistral and Microsoft announced a strategic partnership to expand AI computing capacity in Europe and integrate Mistral's models into Microsoft platforms [29].
