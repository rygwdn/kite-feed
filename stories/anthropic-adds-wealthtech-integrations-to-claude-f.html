Anthropic adds wealthtech integrations to Claude financial adviser platform

Category: AI

Anthropic expanded Claude for Financial Advisors with connectors and workflow skills from financial technology and investment firms, bringing prospecting data, client meeting records, private-market information and portfolio tools into the AI platform [1][10][5]. The additions include CAIS, Conquest, FINNY, Invesco, MSCI and Jump, allowing advisers to use partner services through Claude to review fund commitments, analyze portfolios, track client decisions and prepare follow-up messages [1][6][9]. Anthropic introduced the adviser-focused suite on Sept. 14 as a set of connectors and skills for preparation, analysis and administrative work. Its updated list includes 22 connectors, in addition to tools such as Microsoft 365 and Salesforce [2]. Financial-services firms have adopted AI more readily for back-office work than for customer-facing tasks, according to PYMNTS Intelligence [2]. Anthropic says regulated recommendations and client communications still require human review, while Invesco warns that AI outputs can be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated [9].

Advisors get the best output from Claude when it understands their clients.

Key Points

  • Verified calculations: Conquest’s Strategic Advice Manager uses a deterministic calculation engine to rank and verify strategies against standards including U.S. fiduciary rules, Canadian CRM2/CRM3 and U.K. FCA Targeted Support [1].
  • Client records: Jump’s new skills include meeting recaps, decision logs, commitment tracking and document lookup; firms control recording, transcript retention, consent and access settings [10].
  • Private markets: CAIS says nearly 25,000 advisers have access to its platform’s CAISey intelligence agent. The company serves more than 2,500 wealth firms [6].
  • Recordkeeping: Anthropic recommends its Enterprise plan for registered investment advisers because it includes audit logs [9].
