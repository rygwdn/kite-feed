NASA astronaut returns to Earth after 241-day ISS mission

Category: Science

NASA astronaut Chris Williams and Roscosmos cosmonauts Sergey Kud-Sverchkov and Sergei Mikaev returned to Earth on Sunday, July 26, after an eight-month mission aboard the International Space Station [1][2][6][10]. The Soyuz MS-28 spacecraft landed by parachute at 5:27 a.m. CDT (3:27 p.m. in Kazakhstan) in a designated area southeast of Dzhezkazgan after undocking from the station at 2:03 a.m. CDT [1][3][8][15]. After launching to the ISS on Nov. 27, 2025, the crew spent 241 days in space, completed 3,856 Earth orbits and traveled more than 102 million miles [1][3][16][15]. NASA said Williams supported scientific investigations and technology demonstrations. The return ended Expedition 74, while Expedition 75 continued aboard the station [1][5][27]. After post-landing medical checks, recovery teams planned to fly the crew by helicopter to Karaganda, where Williams would board a NASA aircraft to Johnson Space Center in Houston and the two cosmonauts would travel to Star City near Moscow [1][6][8][10].

Key Points

  • Reentry sequence: CBS News reported that the Soyuz fired braking rockets for four minutes and 42 seconds before the crew module descended under its main parachute for about 10 minutes [15].
  • Crew records: The flight marked the first space mission for Williams and Mikaev, while Kud-Sverchkov completed his second trip to space [1][3][6][10].
  • Station maintenance: Williams completed two spacewalks to prepare ISS power-system upgrades [1][3][20][25].
  • Next crew: The Expedition 75 crew aboard the ISS included NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Jack Hathaway, ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot and Roscosmos cosmonaut Andrei Fedyaev after the July 14 Soyuz arrival [6][8][9].

Perspectives

NASA: NASA said Williams' work aboard the station helped prepare for future human missions to the Moon and Mars.

Sources: NASA

Roscosmos: Roscosmos showed the returning crew smiling in a livestream after landing, and Sergei Mikaev said in a brief statement that he felt good.

Timeline

  • 27 November 2025: The Soyuz MS-28 crew launched to the ISS [1][3].
  • 14 July 2026: Menon, Dubrov and Kikina arrived for Expedition 75 [6][8].
  • 25 July 2026: Kud-Sverchkov handed ISS command to Jessica Meir [12][23].
  • 26 July 2026: The Soyuz MS-28 undocked from the Rassvet module [15][21].
  • 26 July 2026: The crew landed southeast of Dzhezkazgan, Kazakhstan [1][2].

Scientific Significance

  • Biomedical research: Williams supported investigations linked to cancer-treatment research [1][3][20].
  • In-space manufacturing: The mission included experiments aimed at improving orbital production of materials used in high-performance computers and electronics, including work relevant to semiconductor and advanced-materials research [1][3][25].
  • Solar observation: Roscosmos said a Russian spacewalk installed equipment for the Sun-Terahertz experiment, which will study terahertz radiation from the Sun [20][25].

Historical Background

The United States and Russia competed in the Cold War space race, but they now cooperate on the International Space Station and related projects [6][10][18]. Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine strained Washington-Moscow relations, but ISS crew exchanges have continued, with American and Russian crew members flying on each other's spacecraft under a seat-exchange arrangement [6][8][27].
