Canada-wide warrant issued for Panama Papers businessman

Category: Canada

A Canada-wide arrest warrant has been issued for Western Canadian businessman Wentao Yang after he failed to appear in court on tax-evasion charges linked to the Panama Papers leak [2][4][1][3]. The Canada Revenue Agency charged Yang on Nov. 12, 2025, with offences including tax evasion and fraud, against him personally and as director of resource company Kailas Energy Corp. [4][1]. CBC reported that an Alberta provincial court issued a bench warrant in December and Alberta's Court of King's Bench upgraded it on July 13, 2026, to a Canada-wide warrant; the warrant seen by CBC says Yang appears to be "no longer a resident of Canada" [2]. The CRA alleges Yang facilitated purchases of Canadian oil-and-gas assets by investor groups in China, failed to report consulting fees in the 2015 and 2016 taxation years, and used foreign bank-account transfers before funds reached Canadian accounts [4][2]. CBC reported Yang is the first person known to be charged in Canada in relation to the 2016 Panama Papers leak, which exposed offshore financial records and prompted a CRA investigation into him [2][4]. CBC also reported the CRA accused Yang of evading more than $860,000 in tax on consulting fees connected to oilpatch deals [2].
