Apple reportedly preparing red HomePod mini and AirPods Max editions around Dec. 1

Category: Technology

Apple is preparing (PRODUCT)RED editions of its HomePod mini 2 smart speaker and AirPods Max 2 headphones, according to Bloomberg. The speaker is expected to feature a red mesh exterior; the headphones would have a silver finish, white cushions and headband, and a red Digital Crown [21][24][5]. The release is reportedly planned for on or around Dec. 1, World AIDS Day, but Apple has not confirmed the products [21][11]. The editions would mark the return of Product(RED) hardware; Apple’s longstanding partnership with (RED) supports the Global Fund through sales of branded products [2][10]. Bloomberg said the new colors would give Apple additional products to sell during the holiday season [21]. Apple has not announced pricing or availability for the special editions, and a company spokesperson declined to comment on the report [11][18][24]. The reported plans involve color changes rather than new device specifications; no technical changes have been reported [6][16]. The Dec. 1 timing remains unconfirmed, and reports also describe red versions of two accessories in development [5][23].

Key Points

  • Other accessories: Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman also reported that Apple was developing a red AirPods wrist strap and Apple Watch Sport Band; 9to5Mac separately reported that a red AirTag may have been developed [5][2].
  • Current prices: The standard HomePod mini 2 is listed at $149 in the United States, while the AirPods Max 2 is listed at $549; prices for the reported special editions have not been announced [11][27].
  • Fundraising record: Apple says it has contributed more than $250 million to the Global Fund through its partnership since 2006 [10][27].
  • Product scope: The reported AirPods Max design would restore a silver finish, which Apple does not currently offer on the AirPods Max 2 [21][24].

Timeline

  • 2006: Apple began its partnership with (RED) and released a special-edition iPod nano [9][11].
  • 2022: The iPhone 14 and iPhone 14 Plus were among Apple’s last red iPhones [2][5].
  • 2023: Apple released a Product(RED) Apple Watch Series 9 [9][18].
  • March 2026: Apple discontinued its last remaining red iPhone case, according to reports [5][17].
  • 1 December 2026: Reported launch target for the special editions; Apple has not confirmed the date [21][11].

Historical Background

The Global Fund says it has saved more than 70 million lives across more than 100 countries since 2002. Apple says grants supported by its contributions have provided HIV testing to more than 220 million people, HIV prevention services to 45 million young people, and treatment to nearly 6 million HIV-positive mothers [10].

Q&A

Q: Are the special editions expected to cost more?

A: Historically, Product(RED) products have not cost more than standard colors, but Apple has not announced whether that will apply to these editions [13][18].
