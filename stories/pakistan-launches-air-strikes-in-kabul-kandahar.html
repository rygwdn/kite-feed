Pakistan launches air strikes in Kabul, Kandahar

Category: World

Pakistan carried out air strikes in Afghanistan, hitting targets in Kabul and Kandahar province, according to Afghanistan’s Taliban-led government and other local officials [2][4][8]. Afghan officials said the strikes hit residential areas and killed at least four civilians [4][6][8]. A Pakistani security official said the operation targeted the Pakistani Taliban (TTP), as tensions and cross-border fighting between the neighbors stretch into a third week [1][5]. The Taliban said Pakistan bombed an airline fuel depot near Kandahar airport used by private carrier Kam Air [3][7].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Timing and spread: DW said Taliban officials described overnight Pakistani air force strikes in Kabul, as well as in several Afghan border provinces [2].
  • No immediate Pakistani comment: The Hindu said Pakistan’s military did not respond to a request for comment on the alleged strike near Kandahar airport [3].
  • Conflict scale: Reuters, via Al-Monitor, called this a major escalation in the worst fighting in years along the roughly 2,600 km border [7].
  • Reported lull before escalation: Al-Monitor said neither side reported Pakistani air strikes for more than a week, and ground clashes had eased before the Kandahar bombing [7].
