Anthropic raises $30 billion, reaches $380 billion valuation

Category: AI

AI startup Anthropic, which makes the Claude assistant, said it raised $30 billion in a Series G round, putting its post-money valuation at $380 billion [4][5][47][10]. Anthropic said it will use the new capital to scale its enterprise AI models and the Claude platform [4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Lead investors: Axios reported the Series G was led by D. E. Shaw Ventures, Dragoneer, and Founders Fund, among others [15].
  • Strategic participants: Seeking Alpha reported the round included funding from Microsoft and Nvidia, among others [30].
  • What to watch next: Axios said Anthropic’s new scale puts it “on M&A watch,” raising the possibility it could start acquiring smaller companies [31].
  • Bigger than rivals: PitchBook said the raise put Anthropic ahead in the “AI cash race” in its comparison with OpenAI [38].
  • Founder wealth: Forbes reported the jump in valuation boosted the fortunes of Anthropic’s seven billionaire cofounders [18].

Perspectives

Elon Musk: Musk criticized Anthropic’s AI models in a social media post, calling them “misanthropic and evil,” according to Fox Business and Mint [1][7].

Sources: Fox Business, Mint

Barron's: Barron's argued that AI leaders are pulling in a bigger share of tech spending even as traditional software companies face pressure, pointing to Anthropic’s surge in value as evidence of that shift [19].

Sources: Barron's

TechCrunch: TechCrunch framed the funding as coming as Anthropic competes with OpenAI for customers and cultural attention [47].

Sources: TechCrunch

Historical Background

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers and has positioned itself around “constitutional AI,” a safety-oriented training approach [common]. Generative AI investment surged after ChatGPT’s 2022 release, speeding up a funding and product race among a small number of frontier-model labs [common]. The New York Times said Anthropic’s valuation more than doubled from its previous funding round in September [10].
