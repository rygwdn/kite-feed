Ontario police report 1 death after boat overturns

Category: Canada

A 41-year-old Ottawa man died after a 15-foot aluminum boat overturned Saturday night in the St. Lawrence River near Grenadier Island, in the township of Leeds and the Thousand Islands, about 50 kilometres downstream from Kingston, Ontario [1][3][2]. Ontario Provincial Police said several people were on board when the boat capsized. One person was initially reported missing before firefighters found the man [1][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: The St. Lawrence River is part of the Great Lakes–St. Lawrence system, one of North America's most important inland waterways [common].

Key Points

  • River location: Police said the incident happened just north of Grenadier Island in Leeds and the Thousand Islands, on a stretch of the St. Lawrence used by boaters in eastern Ontario [3].
  • Boat type: Police described the vessel as a 15-foot aluminum boat, a detail that helps show the scale of the craft involved in the fatal incident [1].
  • Victim identified: Coverage across outlets identified the victim only as a 41-year-old man from Ottawa [1][2].

Action Items

  • Check local marine conditions before boating: Review current weather, water, and navigation notices from Ontario and federal marine authorities before launching, especially on large waterways such as the St. Lawrence River [common].
  • Wear properly fitted life-jackets: Make sure everyone on board has and wears an approved personal flotation device throughout the trip; Canadian authorities recommend this as a basic boating safety step [common].
