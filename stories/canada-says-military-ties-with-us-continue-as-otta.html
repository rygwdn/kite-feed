Canada says military ties with U.S. continue as Ottawa diversifies

Category: Canada

Canadian Defence Minister David McGuinty said he had seen no change in military cooperation with the United States despite a reported Pentagon directive limiting some contacts. He said Canadian officials had not seen the document [2][5]. Republican Sen. Mike Rounds said the guidance mainly requires U.S. military officials to obtain permission to attend Canadian think-tank events or social functions and does not affect operations [10][4]. McGuinty cited ongoing cooperation between Canadian and U.S. forces, including ships operating together against drug trafficking in the Caribbean, integrated missile systems and fighter jets dispatched across North America when needed [7][17]. He said Canada had decided 18 months ago to diversify its military relationships and described its relationship with the U.S. as strong [2]. Ottawa is also seeking to take greater responsibility for its own defence, including in the Arctic, and is developing a communications system to compete with Starlink, McGuinty said [7][17]. Canada has formally joined NATO’s Innovation Fund, a venture-capital fund for advanced technologies, and is contributing more than $100 million to support investment in Canadian start-ups [4][5].

Key Points

  • Domestic production: Defence Minister David McGuinty said Canada wants to buy less military hardware from the U.S. and build more at home across 10 core areas, turning first to partner countries for foreign purchases [9].
  • Innovation funding: Canada became the fund’s 25th member; the NATO-backed fund invests in areas including artificial intelligence, quantum technology, biotechnology, space and communications [4].
  • Senate criticism: Democratic Sen. Tim Kaine called the directive’s tone “bad” and “unnecessary,” while Sen. Chris Coons warned against further straining relations between the allies [2][10].
  • U.S. assessment: Rounds said Canadian officials viewed the described restrictions as consistent with practices already in place for about a year and a half [5][10].
