Montreal Pride holds 20th parade with heightened security

Category: Canada

Thousands of people gathered in downtown Montreal on Sunday, Aug. 9, for the 20th Fierté Montréal parade after a week of daily Pride events [1][2][5][4][6]. The parade marked the end of Pride Week under the theme “Brillons ensemble!” and featured participants from diverse communities, including Indigenous and Haitian cultural contingents [4]. Organizers also scheduled a separate Wild Pride march [1][5].

Primary Source: View original article

I've known the good years, when everything was allowed... Everyone was beautiful, we had fun. Montreal is a fantastic city for that

— Marc Venne (CBC News)

Key Points

  • Civic presence: Montreal Mayor Soraya Martinez Ferrada said before the march that “Montreal is an ally and will always be an ally,” describing the parade as a space to celebrate identity [4].
  • Community concerns: Marc Venne, 72, said he attended the first Montreal Pride parade 20 years ago and has noticed rising homophobia in recent years [2][5].

Perspectives

Wild Pride organizers: The group organized a separate march and said it opposed corporate sponsorship, police presence and colonialism at Pride events.

Sources: Global News

Geneviève Gagnon: Gagnon attended with her daughter Florence, who came out as pansexual at 18, and said people should let others live and be happy.

Sources: CBC News

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: Fierté Montréal organizers increased security for the parade [7]. Expect heightened security near Pride events.
  • Access: Pride events, including the Fierté Montréal parade and a separate Wild Pride march, took over downtown streets on Sunday [1][5]. Allow extra time in downtown Montreal.
