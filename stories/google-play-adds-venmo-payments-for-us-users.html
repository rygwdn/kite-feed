Google Play adds Venmo payments for US users

Category: Technology

Google Play added Venmo as a U.S. payment option on Aug. 10, letting users link a Venmo account in Play payment settings and use a Venmo balance, connected bank account or linked card to buy apps, games, subscriptions and in-app content or tip creators [12][2][4][3][10]. After setup, users can also use Venmo across Google services including YouTube, according to Engadget and IntoMobile [2][1]. Venmo joins U.S. Google Play payment options that already include PayPal, Cash App and major card networks [2][4][3]. The launch follows Google's decision to allow outside billing in the U.S., U.K. and Europe under its settlement with Epic Games, meaning some developer-run payment routes may not accept Venmo [2][1]. Google said users can add Venmo through payment methods in the Google Play Store [12].

Primary Source: View original article

Whether you use your Venmo balance or a linked payment method, you’ll have a new way to level up in your favorite games, manage your subscriptions and tip the creators who inspire you.

— Google (Google Blog)

Did you know: Google Play added Cash App more than two years before Venmo, and PayPal has been available on Play for more than a decade [7][9].

Key Points

  • App spending: Consumers spent more than $167 billion on in-app purchases across iOS and Google Play in 2025, up 10.6% year over year, according to Sensor Tower figures cited by TechCrunch, The Next Web and Digital Trends [4][3][8].
  • Market limits: Android Headlines and 9to5Google noted that Venmo is effectively a U.S.-only service, limiting the Google Play integration mainly to U.S. residents [7][9].
  • Cash testing: Outside the U.S., Google has tested Pending Transactions that let users pay for digital purchases with cash at participating stores in supported markets [3][4][8].

Perspectives

Google: Google described the launch as a checkout-flexibility feature, saying users can bring the digital wallet they already use to apps, games and digital content on Google Play.

Sources: Google Blog

Android Authority: Android Authority said Venmo may appeal to users who want to spend an accumulated Venmo balance or avoid sharing payment details directly with Google.

Sources: Android Authority

The Next Web: The Next Web linked the addition to Google's effort to reduce checkout drop-off by adding payment methods suited to different markets.

Sources: The Next Web

Historical Background

Google has also added localized payment options such as UPI in India and carrier billing through more than 170 carriers worldwide [3][8].

Business Angle

  • Payment convenience can increase completed transactions; investors can track whether Venmo expands purchase volume or only changes how users pay [common].
  • Developer-run billing under the Epic settlement could reduce Google's control over checkout consistency, including whether Venmo is available in every purchase path [2][1].
  • Venmo owner PayPal gains another channel for Venmo balances inside a major digital storefront [9][common].

Action Items

  • Add Venmo to Google Play: Open Google Play, go to payment methods in account settings, select Add Venmo and link the account [12][10][2].
  • Check checkout availability: If a purchase uses a developer's alternative billing page or website, confirm that payment path supports Venmo before relying on it [2][1].
