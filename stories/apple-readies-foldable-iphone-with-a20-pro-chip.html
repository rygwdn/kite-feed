Apple readies foldable iPhone with A20 Pro chip

Category: Technology

Multiple Apple-focused outlets report that Apple is expected to introduce its first foldable iPhone in September 2026 alongside the iPhone 18 Pro line, with the foldable model using the same next-generation A20 Pro chip as the Pro phones [1][6][5][3]. The reports, attributed to analyst Jeff Pu, suggest Apple is aiming to pair new form-factor hardware with a leading-edge chip platform built on TSMC’s 2nm “N2” process [2][6]. If accurate, the setup could deliver a more “tablet-like” experience when opened while keeping performance consistent across Apple’s top-tier iPhones—an upbeat sign for people who want foldables without compromising on speed or longevity [4][1].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: MacRumors says N2 introduces new SHPMIM capacitors that more than double capacitance density and cut resistance metrics by about 50%, aiming to improve power stability and efficiency [6].

Key Points

  • Split launch: One report says Apple may shift to a split-release schedule, with iPhone 18 and a lower-cost “iPhone 18e” not expected until spring 2027 [6].
  • Display sizes: The foldable is described as having a 5.3-inch outer screen and a 7.8-inch inner screen, targeting a phone-to-small-tablet footprint [2][4].
  • Camera layout: The spec notes describe two rear 48MP cameras and two front cameras—one intended for use when closed and another when opened—with the front sensors described as 18MP [2][4].
  • Biometrics choice: Android Headlines reports the foldable may use Touch ID rather than Face ID, potentially due to design constraints in a folding chassis [4].
  • Materials mix: The body is described as combining aluminum and titanium components, aligning with durability goals for a device designed to fold repeatedly [2][4].

Perspectives

Jeff Pu (GK Securities analyst): Pu’s investor note projects a September debut and a shared A20 Pro/2nm platform across the iPhone 18 Pro models and the foldable, plus other shared high-end specs like 12GB RAM and Apple’s C2 modem.

Sources: MacRumors

AppleInsider editors: AppleInsider cautions that while Pu can be accurate, his assumptions and timelines have also been “too aggressive,” framing the spec table as plausible rather than definitive.

Sources: AppleInsider

Macworld editors: Macworld notes the specs align closely with existing leaks and says it’s hard to tell what is independently verified versus repeated from the same rumor sources, while still emphasizing it feels more likely than in past years that a foldable iPhone ships this year.

Sources: Macworld

Android Headlines editors: Android Headlines highlights curiosity around how Apple will approach foldables and flags the reported Touch ID decision as a notable tradeoff compared with Face ID.

Sources: Android Headlines

Technical Details

  • TSMC N2 (2nm) process: The A20 Pro is expected to use TSMC’s N2 (2nm) manufacturing node, which one report says could bring up to ~15% higher performance and ~30% better efficiency versus A19 chips [6].
  • WMCM packaging: MacRumors says the A20 Pro may use TSMC’s wafer-level multi-chip module (WMCM) approach, integrating RAM onto the same wafer as the CPU/GPU/Neural Engine to improve speed, reduce package size, and potentially help battery life and on-device AI workloads [6].
  • Apple C2 modem: The foldable and iPhone 18 lineup are both described as using Apple’s upcoming C2 modem, indicating Apple may standardize a new cellular platform across its premium phones [2][6].

Industry Impact

  • Semiconductor manufacturing: If Apple moves flagship iPhones to TSMC’s N2 node as reported, it reinforces demand for leading-edge 2nm capacity and packaging innovations like WMCM [6].
  • Foldables competition: A premium Apple foldable entering the market could intensify competition around thinness, hinge durability, and app experiences optimized for dual-display/folding use cases [4][2].
  • Mobile AI performance: MacRumors ties WMCM and efficiency gains to faster Apple Intelligence performance and longer battery life, which could push competitors to emphasize similar on-device AI and efficiency claims [6].

Historical Background

Foldable smartphones have been commercially available for several years, most notably through Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold and Z Flip lines [common]. The current reports frame Apple’s approach as arriving later but pairing the new form factor with a top-tier chip (A20 Pro) rather than creating a separate performance tier for the foldable [1][6].

Q&A

Q: What hinge materials are rumored, beyond the phone’s aluminum/titanium body?

A: Android Headlines notes prior chatter about a titanium and “liquid metal” hinge concept, though it presents this as part of the wider rumor mill rather than a confirmed spec [4].

Q: Which iPhone models might be excluded from the fall 2026 event if Apple splits launches?

A: MacRumors reports the regular iPhone 18 and a more affordable iPhone 18e may not debut until spring 2027 under a split-launch strategy [6].

Q: What could replace Face ID on the foldable, and why might that matter for design?

A: Android Headlines says Touch ID is rumored for the foldable; this can simplify sensor placement when space is constrained by folding screens and hinge mechanics, though the report does not state Apple’s reason [4][common].
