British police rearrest two men in suspected Iran-linked RAF Fairford plot

Category: World

British police on Oct. 20 rearrested two men suspected of assisting Iran’s intelligence service in connection with activity near RAF Fairford, a British air base used by U.S. bombers in strikes on Iran. The suspects, a 24-year-old British citizen and a 25-year-old British-Iranian dual national, were detained under the National Security Act 2023 after previously being arrested and released on bail [2][8][24]. The investigation began after police responded to three suspicious vans near the Gloucestershire base on Sept. 27. Officers found gasoline but no explosives, and residents were evacuated as police and bomb-disposal teams searched the area. The United States later withdrew its bombers from Fairford [2][24][25]. Counter Terrorism Policing chief Laurence Taylor said investigators consider Iranian state involvement “highly likely” but are still examining its extent and the suspected operation’s purpose. Iran denies involvement, and none of the seven men arrested in the case has been charged; five remain on bail [2][8][7].

  • Base protection: Armed Forces Minister Louise Sandher-Jones told Parliament that the Defence Investment Plan provides an additional £470 million over four years for site protection [26].
