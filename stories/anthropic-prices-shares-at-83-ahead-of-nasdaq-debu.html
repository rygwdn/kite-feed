Anthropic prices shares at $83 ahead of Nasdaq debut

Category: AI

Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI assistant, has priced shares in its initial public offering at $83 ahead of its Nasdaq debut, becoming the first major frontier-AI model developer to enter public markets [58][13]. The offering expanded to 980 million shares, valuing the San Francisco company at about $1.5 trillion [29][58]. Anthropic’s annualized revenue run rate reached $81 billion on Oct. 31, up $10 billion in one month. It had 11,900 enterprise customers generating at least $100,000 a year as of Sept. 30, compared with 1,500 at the end of 2025 [61][13]. Third-quarter revenue was $16.96 billion, while the company reported a $567 million operating loss [61]. Anthropic has committed nearly $900 billion to computing and infrastructure with partners, including Amazon and Google, to train and deploy advanced models [13][61]. In San Francisco, a windfall for employees and investors could increase demand for housing and services while making the city less affordable for service workers [20]. Shares are scheduled to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Nov. 19 [58].

Key Points

  • Customer growth: Anthropic reported 11,900 business customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue as of Sept. 30, up from more than 1,500 at the end of 2025 [40].
  • Founder control: A founders’ entity holds one Class F share with 50.1% of voting power. The founders and an independent trust will retain control after listing [68].
  • Employee windfall: Private-market firms estimated that roughly 2,800 current and former employees could hold at least $1 million in pretax vested equity at a $1.35 trillion valuation; the estimates are model-based, not independently verified [105].
  • Index demand: MSCI could add Anthropic to its global equity indexes as early as Dec. 1. Nasdaq-100 inclusion has been reported as possible on Dec. 21, subject to eligibility [149].
  • Compute commitments: Anthropic spent nearly $9 billion on computing in the first half of 2026 and another $5 billion in the third quarter; it has signed agreements requiring $70 billion in computing and infrastructure spending over the next 12 months [13][19].
  • Strategic placement: Seven technology companies agreed to buy $26 billion in nonvoting shares at the IPO price through a separate private placement [114].
  • Operating pressure: Anthropic reported a third-quarter operating loss of $567 million despite rapid revenue growth [13].
  • Revenue growth: Third-quarter revenue reached $16.96 billion, up from $1.4 billion a year earlier [117].
  • Charitable pledges: Anthropic’s seven co-founders pledged to donate 80% of their personal equity to charity, and the company said it would allocate 5% of its shares to a public-benefit fund [114].
  • Investor stakes: At the IPO valuation, Iconiq Capital’s Anthropic stake was estimated at nearly $30 billion, while Lightspeed Ventures’ stake was valued at about $27 billion [28].
  • Adjusted result: Excluding stock-based compensation and donations, Anthropic's operations posted a $2.5 billion third-quarter profit [150].

Industry Impact

  • Index funds: If Anthropic meets eligibility requirements, inclusion in MSCI and Nasdaq-100 benchmarks could prompt funds tracking those indexes to buy shares [149].
  • San Francisco technology sector: The San Francisco Standard reported that the IPO could create further wealth for local investors and employees, while adding pressure to housing costs and making it harder for service workers to remain in the city [20].
  • Index funds: Anthropic could join the Nasdaq 100 within weeks; funds tracking the index, including Invesco’s QQQ, would then automatically buy its shares [93].
  • Enterprise AI software: More than 1,900 customers generated at least $1 million in trailing 12-month revenue, while about 250 generated at least $10 million [62].
  • Technology partners: Google, Microsoft, Nvidia and AMD are among the technology firms participating in a $26 billion private placement. Technology companies also supply Anthropic’s computing systems [31][7].
  • AI financing: Anthropic’s record offering comes ahead of a potential listing by its closest rival, OpenAI, which is expected to consider going public next year [40][4].
  • Strategic investors: Nvidia, AMD and other technology companies participated in a $26 billion private placement alongside the IPO [12].
