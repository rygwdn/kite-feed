Chinese AI firm Zhipu unveils model trained on Huawei

Category: AI

China-based Zhipu AI (also referred to as Z.ai in some coverage) says it has trained and released a new AI model using a Huawei-built hardware and software stack, rather than relying on US-origin AI chips [1][2]. The rollout is being framed as a milestone for running advanced model training on domestic Chinese infrastructure amid ongoing geopolitical and supply-chain pressure around AI computing [1][2]. If the company’s claims hold up in practice, it could broaden the set of viable options for teams building AI systems inside China—an incremental step toward resilience and continuity for local developers and customers [1][2].
