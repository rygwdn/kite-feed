Update: Swiss prosecutors probe ski resort bar managers

Category: World

In the latest development, Swiss authorities have opened a criminal investigation into the two managers of the Le Constellation bar in Crans-Montana after a New Year’s Eve fire killed 40 people and left well over 100 injured [2][8][15][9][10]. Prosecutors say the suspected offences include homicide by negligence, bodily harm by negligence and arson by negligence, while stressing that the presumption of innocence applies [2][10]. Investigators have pointed to a preliminary scenario in which sparklers on champagne bottles were carried too close to the ceiling and likely ignited foam insulation in the basement, causing the blaze to spread [8][11][10]. Identification of victims and support for affected families continues as the community mourns [7][3][4].

Did you know: Crans-Montana is an internationally popular ski destination, so officials expect foreign nationals may be among the injured and dead [10].

Key Points

  • Victim identification: Swiss police have formally identified 24 of those killed so far, with reports noting teenagers among the dead as families await news about missing loved ones [3][4][7].
  • Medical transfers: Authorities said many of the badly burned were taken to nearby countries for urgent treatment, reflecting cross-border emergency cooperation in the Alps [10].
  • Unidentified injured: Officials said six injured people remained unidentified as of Saturday, complicating efforts to notify relatives and coordinate care [10].
  • Public mourning: A memorial mass was planned for Sunday, and makeshift memorials with candles, flowers and messages have grown outside the venue [3][10].
  • Rescue tribute: Firefighters who responded to the blaze were applauded when they visited the memorial site, highlighting public gratitude amid grief [13].

Perspectives

Wallis prosecutors and police: Authorities say they opened a criminal case against the bar’s two managers for suspected negligent manslaughter, negligent bodily harm and negligent arson, while emphasizing the presumption of innocence and that the pair had not been detained at the time of reporting.

Technical Details

  • Foam insulation ignition: Investigators said small fireworks (sparklers) on champagne bottles likely ignited foam insulation on the basement ceiling, helping the fire spread quickly [11][8].
  • Negligence offences: Prosecutors described suspected offences including homicide by negligence, bodily harm by negligence and arson by negligence, which focus on unsafe conduct rather than intent [2][10].
  • Victim identification process: Police reported formal identifications were still underway, with some victims and injured initially unidentified, indicating the slow, careful forensic and administrative work required after a mass-casualty fire [4][10].

Industry Impact

  • Alpine nightlife safety: The incident has put renewed attention on fire-safety practices in crowded resort venues—particularly indoor celebratory effects—after investigators highlighted sparklers near ceiling material as a likely trigger [8][11].
  • Emergency care capacity: The transfer of severely burned patients to neighboring countries underscores how specialist burn-care networks may be relied upon when a local area faces many simultaneous critical injuries [10].
