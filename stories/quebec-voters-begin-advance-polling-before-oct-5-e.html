Quebec voters begin advance polling before Oct. 5 election

Category: Canada

Advance polling for Quebec’s Oct. 5 provincial election opened Sunday, Sept. 27. Polls are open Sunday and Monday from 9:30 a.m. to 8 p.m. [1][3][5][6] Five parties are seeking seats: the Coalition Avenir Québec, Parti Québécois, Quebec Liberals, Quebec Conservatives and Québec solidaire [2][3][6]. The campaign included claims of possible foreign interference. CAQ Leader Christine Fréchette said Americans might support the sovereigntist Parti Québécois to destabilize Canada, while acknowledging there was no indication of interference in the campaign [3].
