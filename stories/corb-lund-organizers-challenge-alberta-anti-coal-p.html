Corb Lund organizers challenge Alberta anti-coal petition rejection

Category: Canada

Organizers of country musician and rancher Corb Lund’s “Water Not Coal” petition filed for judicial review after Elections Alberta rejected the citizen initiative. The petition sought to ban new coal mining on the eastern slopes of the Rockies [2][3]. The Globe and Mail reported that the court request seeks to overturn Elections Alberta’s finding that the petition did not collect enough verified signatures to move forward [1]. The Edmonton Journal reported that Water Not Coal proponent Corb Lund has launched a legal challenge of the rejection. It also reported that Stay Free Alberta petition proponent Mitch Sylvestre is seeking a legislative review of the verification process for his separatism-related petition [4].
