Google launches Gemini Video Remix in Photos

Category: Technology

Google launched Video Remix for Google Photos on July 8, introducing a Gemini Omni-powered editing tool that lets users transform existing videos from the app’s Create tab with preset AI templates [14][6][1]. The feature can apply cinematic relighting to dark clips, replace plain backgrounds and render footage in styles such as watercolor, raw sketchbook and oil painting [14][4][5]. Google said Video Remix is starting to roll out to eligible Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers in select countries [14][6][7]. TechCrunch, Droid Life and 9to5Google listed availability in the U.S., Argentina, Bangladesh, Brazil, Colombia, Egypt, India, Indonesia, Japan, Mexico, Pakistan, the Philippines, South Korea and Turkey [6][7][1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Template examples: Google showed prompts such as setting a clip in a greenhouse, relighting it with a morning glow and painting it in dreamy watercolor [1][5].
  • Photos roadmap: TechCrunch said Video Remix follows recent Google Photos AI updates including touch-up tools for subtle fixes and the removal of scratches, blemishes and other distractions [6].

Perspectives

Google: Google presented Video Remix as a way to make edited video clips without professional editing skills or hours of work, using a template library inside Google Photos.

Sources: Google Blog

TechCrunch: TechCrunch framed the launch as part of Google’s effort to bring generative AI into consumer apps while competing with Apple, OpenAI and Adobe, and said putting the tool inside Photos gives users another reason to stay in Google’s ecosystem.

Sources: TechCrunch

Chrome Unboxed: Chrome Unboxed called the tool useful for people with many untouched clips but highlighted the paid-tier restriction as the main drawback.

Sources: Chrome Unboxed

Timeline

  • May 2026: Google introduced Gemini Omni with video as an early focus [2][9].
  • June 2026: Android Authority spotted signs of an upcoming Video Remix feature [3].
  • 8 July 2026: Google began rolling out Video Remix to eligible subscribers [14][6].

Industry Impact

  • AI subscriptions: Google is limiting Video Remix to paid Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra plans in select countries [14][6][7].
  • Mobile editing: HardwareZone said the template-based workflow avoids timelines, layers, color wheels and export settings for users editing short clips already backed up in Google Photos [12].

Historical Background

Google Photos added AI Remix for still images about a year ago, allowing users to reimagine photos in different styles before extending the approach to videos [3][4].

Business Angle

  • Google is using Photos to promote adoption of its AI subscription plans [14][6].
  • The 10-second clip limit means longer-form creators still need full editing software [5][common].
  • Embedding AI editing directly into a default photo library lets users edit clips without exporting them to third-party tools [6][12].

Action Items

  • Check availability in Google Photos: Open Google Photos and look in the Create tab; if Video Remix appears, you can start using it.
  • Trim long clips before remixing: If a video is longer than 10 seconds, trim it first because Video Remix currently supports clips up to 10 seconds [5].
