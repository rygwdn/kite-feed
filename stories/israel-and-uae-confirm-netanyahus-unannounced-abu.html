Israel and UAE confirm Netanyahu's unannounced Abu Dhabi meeting

Category: World

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu met UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan in Abu Dhabi on Sunday, Sept. 27. Both governments confirmed the visit the next day. The Emirati state news agency said they discussed bilateral ties and ways to strengthen them. Sara Netanyahu and senior Israeli security and foreign policy officials accompanied the prime minister [4][7][12]. The meeting followed Israeli media reports that Sheikh Mohamed had warned Netanyahu before Hamas’ Oct. 7, 2023, attack that the group was preparing a major operation and that Netanyahu did not pass the warning to security officials. Netanyahu has denied receiving such a warning. The Associated Press, citing a person with firsthand knowledge, reported that Netanyahu asked Sheikh Mohamed to deny the account; Netanyahu’s office denied that he made the request. The governments’ public readouts of the meeting did not address the allegation [60][40][4].
