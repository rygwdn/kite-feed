Quebec premier begins first Washington trade mission

Category: Canada

Quebec Premier Christine Fréchette is set to begin her first foreign mission in Washington on Monday, putting trade and economic ties with the United States at the center of her debut trip abroad [1][2][3]. The visit comes about two months before a scheduled July 1 CUSMA milestone referenced in the coverage, giving the mission added weight as Quebec tries to advance its interests ahead of upcoming North American trade discussions [1][3]. La Presse reports that the trip is also unfolding at an unsettled moment in the U.S. capital, with Washington shaken by what appeared to be another assassination attempt targeting President Trump [3]. Even so, the mission shows Quebec’s intent to stay engaged with its most important economic partner and make its case directly in Washington at a consequential moment for North American trade [1][2][3].
