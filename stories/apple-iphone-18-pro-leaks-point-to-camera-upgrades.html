Apple iPhone 18 Pro leaks point to camera upgrades

Category: Technology

Apple has scheduled its “Surprise and Shine” event for September 9, and reports expect the company to introduce the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and a possible first foldable iPhone, often described in leaks as the iPhone Ultra or iPhone Fold [26][60][16][66]. Reports also describe the event as John Ternus’s first major iPhone keynote as Apple CEO after Tim Cook became executive chairman on September 1 [46][48]. MacRumors said forum member “pdfu” found iOS 27 beta code pointing to five unannounced Camera app features: exposure scopes, focus peaking and highlight warnings, repeated self-timer shots, manual focus, and aperture-based image correction tied to rumored variable-aperture hardware [8][15][18][19][21]. A separate video posted on Naver appeared to show iPhone 18 Pro demo units in Dark Cherry and blue finishes, but 9to5Mac and AppleInsider questioned the clip’s authenticity, citing easy-to-obtain dummy units and an unconvincing retail setting [4][6][1][44]. Before the event, TrendForce estimated the iPhone 18 Pro Max could start at $1,349 to $1,399, while KeyBanc forecast $1,399 [17][52]. Apple has not confirmed the final device lineup, features, colors or pricing ahead of the September 9 presentation [26][32].

Key Points

  • Foldable details: Leaks describe the rumored iPhone Ultra as a book-style foldable with a roughly 5.4- to 5.5-inch outer display, a 7.8-inch inner display, Touch ID instead of Face ID, and main and ultra-wide rear cameras [16][59][67].
  • Memory costs: Counterpoint data cited by The Verge showed smartphone DRAM prices rose about 56% in the first quarter of 2026 from the previous quarter and another 83% in the second quarter, adding cost pressure for handset makers [17].
  • Lower build estimates: KeyBanc modeled about 80 million iPhone 18-series builds across Apple’s fiscal fourth quarter of 2026 and fiscal first quarter of 2027, down from roughly 91 million in the comparable prior-year period [52][39][55].
  • Next redesign: MacRumors said Apple is expected to skip an iPhone 19 name and reserve larger design changes, including an all-glass quad-curved display concept, for a 20th-anniversary iPhone in 2027 [9][33].

Technical Details

  • Aperture correction: iPhone in Canada said iOS 27 code can blend lens corrections across up to four reference points, a software clue consistent with physical variable-aperture camera hardware [19].
  • Scope presets: Cult of Mac reported that the leaked Camera app implementation includes RGB Composite, RGB Parade and Luminance presets for waveform and histogram exposure monitoring [18].
  • Chip packaging: Giga, citing semiconductor analyst Tim Culpan via MacRumors, said Apple’s A20 Pro production links processor and DRAM during manufacturing, making memory availability a constraint before completed chips can be stockpiled [38].

Industry Impact

  • Retail channels: Newskart reported India deals that brought the effective iPhone 17 price as low as Rs. 47,405 with exchange and bank offers, while Apple’s official starting price remained Rs. 82,900 [10].

Historical Background

Apple introduced the original iPhone in 2007, establishing the product line that anchors its hardware business [common]. The iPhone X removed the home button in 2017, which ARY News cited as the last major iPhone design break before the rumored foldable model [28]. Rival smartphone makers have sold foldable devices for years ahead of Apple’s expected entry into the category [68][64].

Business Angle

  • A higher Pro mix can support revenue per device, but only if customers do not delay upgrades after price increases [52][common].
  • DRAM prices affect both cost of goods sold and early availability, two variables that can pressure margins and sales timing [17][38].
  • A foldable model above $2,000 could expand Apple’s premium tier, but first-generation hardware carries risks around yields, durability and demand forecasting [16][common].
  • Retail discounts on iPhone 17 models in India, Vietnam and Germany show channel pricing can soften ahead of launches even when Apple has not officially cut list prices [10][41][40].
