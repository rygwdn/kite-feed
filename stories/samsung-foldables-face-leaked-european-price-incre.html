Samsung foldables face leaked European price increases

Category: Technology

Samsung is expected to announce new foldable phones and smartwatches at a Galaxy Unpacked event in London on July 22, with leaks pointing to higher European prices for several models [3][5]. Information from WinFuture cited by Roland Quandt indicates the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra would start at €2,199 for 256GB, €100 above the previous flagship Fold model, while the 512GB and 1TB variants would rise to €2,399 and €2,799, respectively [4][5][3]. The same leak puts the new, wider Galaxy Z Fold 8 below the Ultra at €1,999 for 256GB, €2,199 for 512GB and €2,599 for 1TB, matching the previous Fold starting price rather than exceeding it [4][3]. Samsung has also begun official teasers for the wider foldable under the phrase “New Shape, New Joy,” while SamMobile reported that Samsung’s software revealed the names of upcoming earbuds and Galaxy Watch models ahead of launch [2][1].

Key Points

  • Storage premiums: The leaked Fold 8 Ultra prices show a larger premium at higher storage levels, with the 1TB model listed €280 above last year’s comparable version [4][5].
  • Flip increase: The leak lists the Galaxy Z Flip 8 at €1,299 for 256GB and €1,499 for 512GB, increases of €100 and €180, respectively [3][5].
  • Regional scope: 9to5Google described the figures as German pricing, while Digital Trends and TechJuice described them as European pricing based on retailer information [4][5][3].
