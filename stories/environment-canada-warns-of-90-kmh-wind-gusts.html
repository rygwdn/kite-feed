Environment Canada warns of 90 km/h wind gusts

Category: Canada

Environment Canada issued wind warnings for parts of Ontario and Quebec as a cold front moves through, with gusts forecast to reach roughly 70–90 km/h in the Ottawa area and up to 90 km/h in Montreal, alongside periods of rain in Montreal [1][2]. The agency said winds in the capital are expected to strengthen and shift to the west as the front passes Friday afternoon into the evening [1]. Coverage elsewhere in Ontario included London, where an update reported the wind warning was later lifted [3]. Utility crews also responded to wind-related outages in parts of southern, central and eastern Ontario, with Hydro One cautioning some customers could face longer restoration times [4].
