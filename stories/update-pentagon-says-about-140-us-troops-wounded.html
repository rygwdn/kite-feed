Update: Pentagon says about 140 US troops wounded

Category: World

In the latest development in the Israel-Iran war, the Pentagon says about 140 U.S. service members have been wounded since the fighting began. It’s the first official snapshot of injuries tied to Iranian rocket and drone attacks on U.S. positions in the region [1][2][4][5][6]. U.S. officials say eight troops are in critical or severe condition, and many of the wounded have already returned to duty [2][4][5]. The update highlights both the human toll and the operational strain of a conflict now about 10 days old. Reuters-cited sources have suggested the wounded total could be higher than the Pentagon’s public estimate [3]. The White House says the campaign is making progress, while Iran says it’s prepared to keep attacking and has threatened to halt oil shipments from the Gulf—raising broader worries about regional stability and global energy flows [2][7].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Fatalities reported: The New York Times says seven Americans have been killed in the Iran war so far, alongside the Pentagon’s injury tally [5].
  • Oil route warning: President Trump warned Iran not to disrupt oil traffic through the Strait of Hormuz. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the U.S. would respond far more forcefully if Iran acts on that threat [8].
  • Kuwait attack: The Globe and Mail reported that a drone strike hit a command center in Kuwait and killed a U.S. Army Reserve soldier, part of a wider pattern of retaliatory attacks on U.S. facilities [6].
