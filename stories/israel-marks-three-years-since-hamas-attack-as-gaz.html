Israel marks three years since Hamas attack as Gaza strikes continue

Category: World

Israel held memorials on Wednesday, Oct. 7, marking three years since the Hamas-led attack that killed about 1,200 people and took 251 hostages. This is the first anniversary since all living hostages and the remains of those who died were returned [5][6][12]. Families gathered at the Nova festival site and Kfar Aza, while ceremonies and protests took place across the country [2][27]. The attack triggered Israel’s war in Gaza. More than 74,000 Palestinians have been killed, according to Gaza’s Health Ministry, and most of the territory’s roughly 2 million residents have been displaced. Israeli strikes continued despite a ceasefire agreed in October 2025 [7][5][26]. Gaza officials said at least two people were killed in strikes overnight into Wednesday [28][5]. The anniversary falls three weeks before Israel’s Oct. 27 election. Responsibility for the security failures and the government’s handling of the war are major campaign issues [26][15]. Families and opposition figures continue to demand an independent inquiry, which Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has resisted [27][30].

Did you know: At Nir Oz, a five-person volunteer defense team held off the attackers for at least two hours before all were shot, according to a kibbutz caretaker [17].

Key Points

  • Election stakes: An Israel Democracy Institute poll found that 68% of Jewish Israelis said the Oct. 7 events were a very or fairly central factor in their choice of party [26].
  • Gaza control: Israel retained control of about 53% of Gaza under the ceasefire arrangement, according to RTÉ [26].
  • Nir Oz toll: 47 people were killed and 76 were taken to Gaza; the final living hostages from Nir Oz returned on Oct. 13, 2025, and the last hostage’s remains were recovered two weeks later [18].
  • Commemoration scale: Memorials, demonstrations and other events were planned at roughly 400 locations across Israel [27][29].
