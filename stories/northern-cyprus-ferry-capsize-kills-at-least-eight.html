Northern Cyprus ferry capsize kills at least eight

Category: World

At least eight people died and 18 were missing after the privately owned Filo Jet passenger ferry capsized and sank off northern Cyprus on Sunday during a voyage from Kyrenia to Taşucu in southern Turkey, Turkish Cypriot officials said [2][6][15][17][29]. The vessel was carrying 259 passengers and eight crew members, and officials said rescuers had pulled 241 people from the water by late Sunday and early Monday [6][15][17][29]. The ferry left Kyrenia at about 12:30 p.m. local time and began taking on water shortly after departure; the cause remained under investigation [2][26][21][29]. Prime Minister Ünal Üstel said authorities had detained the captain and seven crew members as part of the inquiry, while search teams from Turkey and northern Cyprus continued operations after the vessel sank in water about 514 meters deep [2][1][11][14][29].

Historical Background

Cyprus has been divided since 1974, after Turkey intervened following an Athens-backed coup by supporters of union with Greece [25][38][55]. Turkish Cypriots declared a separate state in the north in 1983, but Turkey is the only country that recognizes it [25][55]. The ferry route links northern Cyprus with Turkey, a connection several sources described as a regular shuttle service [6][15][21].

Humanitarian Impact
