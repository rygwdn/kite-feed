Apple iPhone 18 Pro rumors point to corner cutout

Category: Technology

A cluster of supply-chain and leaker reports says Apple’s iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could shift toward a cleaner “all-screen” look by moving Face ID’s TrueDepth components under the display while leaving only a small selfie-camera cutout visible [4][2][9]. Several sources add that the selfie camera cutout may land in the top-left corner—where the time currently sits—which could also force a redesign or relocation of the Dynamic Island interface [4][2][3]. Separately, display-focused rumors say Apple may adopt “LTPO+” OLED panels (linked to under-display infrared support) as part of enabling under-screen Face ID, with potential power-efficiency benefits [9][8][12][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: LTPO (low-temperature polycrystalline oxide) display backplanes enable variable refresh rates, letting phones drop to very low Hz on static content to save power [common].

Key Points

  • Leaker render: YouTuber Jon Prosser’s render claims the Dynamic Island could shift to the top-left and sometimes expand outward from the corner to cover the upper display region during activities, rather than living centered [4][6].
  • Conflicting takes: One account, ShrimpApplePro, says the Dynamic Island may remain but become “shorter,” because some Face ID components move under-screen while the front camera and Face ID’s infrared camera stay visible [9].
  • Design open question: 9to5Mac notes there is still an unresolved design question: whether Apple keeps a Dynamic Island at all once Face ID moves under the display [1].
  • Display suppliers: Android Headlines reports that only Samsung and LG are said to be capable of manufacturing the rumored LTPO+ panels, implying a narrower supplier pool if Apple adopts them broadly [12].
  • Infrared pathway: ETNews, as summarized by MacRumors, describes under-screen infrared tech (attributed to Samsung) as a key enabler for hiding Face ID hardware behind the OLED [9].

Technical Details

  • Under-display Face ID (UDIR/under-display infrared): Reports describe Face ID’s infrared-related hardware moving behind the OLED using under-display infrared techniques, aiming to keep biometric authentication while reducing visible cutouts [8][9].
  • LTPO+ OLED panels: Multiple sources claim Apple may use LTPO+ displays on iPhone 18 Pro models; the upgrade is discussed as both an enabler for under-screen Face ID and a potential battery/power-efficiency improvement [9][12][10].
  • Dynamic Island UI relocation: Some rumors propose the Dynamic Island software could move from a centered position to the top-left to align with a relocated selfie camera cutout, while still expanding to show ongoing activities [4][2].

Industry Impact

  • Display manufacturing: If LTPO+ adoption is real and limited to a small set of capable manufacturers, it could shift premium OLED demand toward the few suppliers able to produce panels that support under-display infrared use cases [12][9].
  • App and UI design: A top-left Dynamic Island could push developers and Apple’s own UI conventions to rethink how “live activity” overlays coexist with status items like the clock, especially on larger screens [2][4].
  • Accessory ecosystem: Any visible change to the top cutout/overlay behavior can influence screen protector and case-window designs, which often track iPhone front-panel geometry closely [common].

Historical Background

Apple introduced the Dynamic Island on iPhone 14 Pro as a pill-shaped, interactive area that visually masks the front camera and Face ID sensor cutouts while surfacing live activities [4]. Since 2022, iPhone displays have carried a prominent black oval/pill region at the top; one cited expectation is that under-screen Face ID would remove that larger shape and leave only a smaller camera opening [4].

Q&A

Q: If the selfie camera moves to the top-left, what happens to the clock and status icons?

A: Reports that place the cutout in the upper-left note it sits where the time currently appears, implying Apple would need to rearrange status-bar elements, but the final layout is not confirmed in the sources provided [2][4].

Q: Would a smaller or relocated Dynamic Island change how Live Activities present across apps?

A: Some descriptions suggest the Dynamic Island could “fly out” from the corner to cover more of the top area during activities, which could preserve functionality while changing placement; the sources do not confirm any finalized developer behavior [4].

Q: Do these reports indicate whether under-display Face ID affects performance or accuracy?

A: The articles describe the goal of placing Face ID sensors behind the display using under-display infrared, but they do not provide measured performance results, emphasizing that early rumors should be treated cautiously [8][9].

Action Items

  • Sanity-check iPhone 18 Pro rumor screenshots you see: When a render claims a top-left cutout or a smaller Dynamic Island, compare it against multiple independent write-ups (e.g., leaker-based vs. supply-chain-based reports) before treating it as settled [4][9][1].
  • Prepare for possible UI changes if you build iOS apps: Review how your app’s full-screen layouts, live activities, and top-left touch targets behave near the status bar area, since multiple reports discuss a potential shift of camera/Dynamic Island elements toward that corner [2][4].
