Bangkok pub fire kills 27, injures 63

Category: World

A fire tore through the Rong Beer Na Lat Phrao pub in Bangkok’s Chatuchak district late Sunday, killing at least 27 people and injuring 63, including 22 in critical condition, Thai officials said [27][10][18][20][39]. Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul visited the scene and said rescuers had recovered 27 bodies; Bangkok Governor Chadchart Sittipunt said authorities had opened an investigation [27][35][39]. Officials cited witness and survivor accounts saying smoke appeared near a circuit breaker or stage area before the power went out, an explosion was heard and smoke rapidly filled the venue [7][13][10][39]. Officials said many victims were found near the restrooms or toward the back of the venue, and investigators are examining whether ceiling materials accelerated the fire and whether emergency exits were blocked or inadequate [27][4][22][37][39]. Firefighters brought the blaze under control in about half an hour. By Monday morning, forensic officers had cordoned off the site and were searching the burned interior for evidence [27][19][25][39].

Key Points

  • Victim profile: Bangkok Post and Kyodo reported the dead included 18 women and nine men, with Kyodo citing police as saying the victims were 20 to 35 years old [39][40].
  • Hospital response: Bangkok Post reported that the injured were taken to 16 hospitals, citing official updates [39].
  • Venue layout: Euronews cited a Facebook promotion saying the venue had a stage, two bars, a kitchen, capacity for more than 300 people and four fire exits [20].

Perspectives

Bangkok Governor Chadchart Sittipunt: Chadchart said investigators would check whether emergency exits had been blocked, which could have made evacuation harder.

Timeline

  • 12 July 2026: The fire broke out at the Rong Beer Na Lat Phrao pub at 11:57 p.m. local time [39][46].
  • 13 July 2026: Firefighters brought the blaze under control at about 12:26 a.m., roughly 30 minutes after it was first reported [46][19].
  • 13 July 2026: Forensic officers cordoned off the site and began searching the charred venue for evidence in the morning [27][25].

Technical Details

  • Smoke inhalation: Officials attributed many deaths to smoke rather than direct flame exposure, and Bangkok Post said burning plastic ceiling decorations produced toxic smoke inhaled by victims [20][39].
  • Possible electrical ignition: Investigators are assessing accounts of smoke from a circuit breaker near the stage, while BBC cited a preliminary disaster-department assessment pointing to a possible short circuit in an air conditioner [7][10][13].

Industry Impact

  • Nightlife venues: Bangkok Post reported the pub had a license, allowed musical performances and had passed an April inspection [39].
  • Tourism district: DW and Al Jazeera described the pub as being in the same area as Chatuchak market, a weekend draw for tens of thousands of local and international visitors [12][15].

Historical Background

Thailand has faced previous deadly nightlife and entertainment-venue fires. The Santika club fire during New Year celebrations in Bangkok in 2009 killed 67 people and injured more than 200 [20][36][58]. BBC reported that Thai authorities had promised improvements to fire and electrical safety standards after earlier incidents, but enforcement remained uneven [10].

Humanitarian Impact

Hospitals treated 63 injured people, including 22 in critical condition, while authorities worked to identify victims and notify families [18][19][40]. Nurses handed out masks near the burned-out building to protect people from smoke and lingering fumes [25].
