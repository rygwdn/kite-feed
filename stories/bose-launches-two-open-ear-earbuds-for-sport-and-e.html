Bose launches two open-ear earbuds for sport and everyday use

Category: Technology

Bose announced two clip-on open-ear earbuds on Sept. 17: the $199 Sport Open Earbuds for exercise and outdoor use, and the $299 Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) for premium everyday listening [4][6][12]. Both leave the ear canals open, allowing users to hear surrounding conversations and traffic; neither includes active noise cancellation [13][11]. The Ultra model adds redesigned drivers designed for deeper bass and higher volume, Bose’s TrueSpatial head-tracked audio with a new Cinema mode, clearer voice pickup and up to nine hours of battery life, compared with 7.5 hours for the first generation [4][6][9][3]. The Sport model has an IP54 rating, physical controls and up to seven hours of playback, with its case providing up to 22 hours of total playback time [10][13]. Both models are available for preorder from Sept. 19 and are scheduled to ship Oct. 1 [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: The first-generation Ultra Open Earbuds launched alongside a Kith collaboration without visible Bose branding [9].

Key Points

  • Connectivity upgrades: Both models use Bluetooth 5.4 and multipoint pairing, while the Ultra adds aptX Adaptive and Snapdragon Sound support for compatible Android devices [9][10][13].
  • Case redesign: The new charging cases stand upright and support wireless charging, with the Ultra case holding nearly 19.5 additional hours of playback [7][11][13].
  • Sport fit: The Sport earbuds use a silicone C-shaped cuff designed to remain secure during vigorous activity [13][1].
  • Color options: The Ultra comes in Black, White, Cherry Chocolate and Olive Green, while the Sport is offered in Black, White and Eucalyptus Green [4][10].

Perspectives

Bose: Bose regards the Ultra as its flagship open-ear experience; Engadget noted that the retained cuff-clip silhouette, though divisive in appearance, proved comfortable in the first generation [11].

Sources: Engadget

CNET: CNET highlighted that the new Sport is a distinct design from the original Ultra, rather than a simple lower-priced version of the same hardware [6].

Sources: CNET

Les Numériques: Les Numériques reported a €199.95 European launch price for the Sport, which Bose positions as its first more accessible clip-on model [15].

Sources: Les Numériques

Timeline

  • 17 September 2026: Bose announced both open-ear models and provided preorder information [4][11]
  • 19 September 2026: Preorders are scheduled to begin for both new earbuds [6]
  • 1 October 2026: Both earbuds are scheduled to ship and go on sale [5][6]

Technical Details

  • OpenAudio processing: Bose uses digital signal processing to preserve bass and clarity while reducing sound leakage by directing audio toward the ear [13][2].
  • Voice isolation: SpeechClarity uses dedicated microphones and AI-assisted voice isolation to separate the wearer’s speech from surrounding noise during calls [13].
  • Spatial audio: TrueSpatial provides head tracking, while Cinema mode keeps dialogue centered during spatial processing [6][3].

Historical Background

Bose introduced the original Ultra Open Earbuds in 2024, more than two years before this second-generation launch [6][9]. The first model used a cuff-style clip design that helped establish Bose in the open-ear segment, where Sony, Nothing and Samsung have since introduced competing products [4][12].

Business Angle

  • The Ultra retains its $299 price as Bose competes at the high end of a growing category [9][15].
  • The $199 Sport gives Bose a lower-priced entry point in the clip-on market, where it competes with Sony, Huawei and other rivals [15][4].
  • The Sport's case provides up to 22 hours of total playback, while the earbuds have an IP54 rating [10][13].
