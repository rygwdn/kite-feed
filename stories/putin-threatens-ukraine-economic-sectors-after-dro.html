Putin threatens Ukraine economic sectors after drone strikes

Category: World

Russian President Vladimir Putin said in comments published Saturday that Ukraine had opened “Pandora’s box” by striking Russian economic targets and warned that Moscow would hit Ukraine’s “most sensitive economic sectors” in response [16][10][9][36][48]. Ukraine has expanded drone attacks this summer on Russian economic infrastructure, including oil refineries and warehouses used by online retailers, saying those facilities support Russia’s war effort [2][16][10][18][63]. Russia has answered with strikes on Ukrainian economic infrastructure, including facilities tied to grain exports through the Black Sea [16][10][9][13][23]. Putin said those attacks would not create global food shortages because Russia could replace Ukrainian exports, while acknowledging difficulties shipping Russian goods [16][10][9][36][48]. He also repeated that Moscow was open to peace talks only on the basis of “realities on the ground” and rejected Ukrainian proposals made through intermediaries as “exotic” [16][13][18][65].

The Kyiv regime set out to damage our economy. What did it do? It opened this Pandora’s box itself. Well then, expect a response targeting your most sensitive economic sectors

— Vladimir Putin (Business Recorder)

Key Points

  • Retail targets: Several accounts identified Russian e-commerce logistics sites, including Wildberries and Ozon facilities, as recent Ukrainian targets, with Kyiv linking such warehouses to Russian military supply chains [55][75][66][24].
  • Air defense: Putin called for strengthening Russia’s air defenses and improving coordination between civilian and military agencies after the strikes on logistics infrastructure [59].
  • Russian messaging: Russia’s Defense Ministry reposted Putin’s “Pandora’s box” remarks with an image of a missile launch and the caption “There is always an answer,” according to Echo24 [75].
  • Military rationale: Putin said Russian strikes disrupt Ukraine’s ability to move equipment, ammunition and personnel rotations to the line of contact, a claim Sky News Arabia said could not be independently verified [52][73].

Perspectives

Vladimir Putin: Putin told Russian state television that Ukrainian intermediaries were trying to prevent the situation from developing into a “catastrophic scenario,” while calling the proposals Moscow received “exotic and unacceptable.”
