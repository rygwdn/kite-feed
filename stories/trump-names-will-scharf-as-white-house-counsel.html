Trump names Will Scharf as White House counsel

Category: World

President Trump announced Sunday that White House Staff Secretary Will Scharf will become White House counsel and assistant to the president on September 1, replacing David Warrington, who will leave the administration for the private sector [2][3][4][5][6][7][9][10][16][27][30]. Scharf is a longtime legal ally who previously served as a federal prosecutor and represented Trump in cases before the Supreme Court [3][6][7][9][16][27][22]. Scharf oversees Oval Office administrative work as staff secretary and chaired the National Capital Planning Commission, which approved Trump’s $400 million White House ballroom project [2][3][6][7][27][24][28]. The White House counsel advises the president on legal and policy questions, serves as a main White House contact with the Justice Department, handles pardons, judicial appointments and legislation review, and helps manage congressional investigations and official-capacity lawsuits against the president [7][16][27][14][22][23][25].

Will is Tough, Strong, and Smart! He also loves our Country, and respects the Law

— President Donald Trump (The Guardian)

Key Points

  • Midterm timing: The change takes effect months before the November midterm elections. Democrats say they would pursue investigations into Trump and his administration if they win [6][7][9][16][27][14][30].
  • Ballroom challenge: A federal appeals court on Friday ordered construction of the $400 million ballroom halted, saying Congress must approve the project; Trump said he would appeal to the Supreme Court [14][22].
  • Court confirmations: During Trump’s first term, Scharf worked on matters related to the confirmations of Supreme Court Justices Brett Kavanaugh and Amy Coney Barrett [22][26].
  • Warrington history: Warrington served as Trump’s personal lawyer and as legal counsel for Trump’s 2024 presidential campaign before joining the White House [6][24][28][26].

Perspectives

In a Truth Social post, Trump said Scharf “has done an amazing job in his current role as White House Staff Secretary, and has also served as Chairman of the National Capital Planning Commission,” and noted Scharf “previously represented me in a series of cases, including in the Supreme Court, during the campaign of persecution and the corrupt and illegal political weaponization of the justice system by the Biden administration.”
