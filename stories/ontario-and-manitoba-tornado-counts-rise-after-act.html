Ontario and Manitoba tornado counts rise after active storms

Category: Canada

Researchers have confirmed additional tornadoes in southwestern Ontario and Alberta, while Manitoba has recorded 15 tornadoes so far in 2026. In Ontario, the findings include a tornado and a downburst in Huron-Perth and an EF1 tornado in Wellesley Township following the Sept. 2 storms; a satellite review by an Ontario university also identified several previously unreported Alberta touchdowns from late July [1][3].
