GPT-6 Astra can coordinate agent swarms, LessWrong post says

Category: AI

AI researcher Florian Brand wrote on LessWrong that GPT-6 Astra can delegate tasks to agents, create nested subagents and coordinate their work when prompted effectively. He described the model as an early, error-prone example of a system trained to operate as a recursive language model [2]. Separately, OpenRouter co-founder and CEO Alex Atallah discussed the platform’s role as a model-routing service on the Latent Space podcast. The episode says OpenRouter serves more than 10 million developers [3].

Key Points

  • Brand said Astra’s current issues should be resolved over time, comparing its development with o1-preview [2].
