SpaceX schedules 31-payload rideshare as Texas launch plan faces review

Category: Science

SpaceX was scheduled to launch its Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission Monday morning with the Starfall reentry capsule and 31 customer payloads, according to Spaceflight Now’s live coverage [1]. Separately, the company has proposed increasing Starship launches from its Texas site to 456 a year, a plan under review [2]. A New York Times explainer focuses on Starshield, SpaceX’s military-grade satellite service [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • FAA meetings: The Federal Aviation Administration is holding public meetings on SpaceX’s proposed increase in Starship launches [2].
  • Ukraine scrutiny: The Starshield article’s related coverage includes a reported Starlink disruption in Ukraine and a call from U.S. Rep. Mike Turner for a congressional probe [3].
