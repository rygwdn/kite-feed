Quebec floodwaters stabilize as hundreds of buildings remain affected

Category: Canada

Flooding across parts of Quebec showed early signs of easing, with provincial monitoring showing water levels were stable or falling in several of the hardest-hit areas, including along the Ottawa River [1][3][4]. That offered some welcome relief, even as damage remained extensive. In Gatineau, nearly 500 buildings were affected by floodwaters, and communities including Rivière-Rouge and parts of the Montreal region were still dealing with submerged streets, pumps, and cleanup pressures as the spring thaw continued [1][2][3][4].

Did you know: The Ottawa River forms part of the boundary between Ontario and Quebec, so spring flooding along it can affect communities on both sides of the provincial border [common].

  • Red Cross aid: The Red Cross was helping Gatineau residents as Ottawa River levels stabilized, offering practical support to households dealing with flooding and displacement [1].

  • Cost concerns: La Presse reported that residents in Rivière-Rouge said the 2026 spring floods are shaping up to be especially expensive, with pumping and property protection already dominating daily life [2].
