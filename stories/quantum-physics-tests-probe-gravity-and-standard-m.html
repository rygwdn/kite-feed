Quantum physics tests probe gravity and Standard Model

Category: Science

Two recent physics reports described separate tests of foundational theories: a quantum experiment in which Einstein's equivalence principle passed a new test, and a BESIII Collaboration analysis that used entangled particles to revisit a 35-year-old Standard Model test [1][3]. Discover Magazine described the equivalence-principle experiment as a step toward one of physics' major unresolved problems, while Phys.org said BESIII used a technique that experimenters had largely not used for 35 years [1][3]. A Google News listing citing Nature and the Chinese Academy of Sciences said the BESIII work examined baryon semileptonic decays through polarization and entanglement [2]. The listing also said BESIII achieved the world's most precise measurement of the Lambda hyperon's electric dipole moment [2].
