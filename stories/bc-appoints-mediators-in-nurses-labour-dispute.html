B.C. appoints mediators in nurses' labour dispute

Category: Canada

British Columbia Labour Minister Jennifer Whiteside appointed veteran mediator Vince Ready and Amanda Rogers as special mediators July 10 in the labour dispute between the Health Employers Association of BC and the B.C. Nurses’ Union [6][3][5]. The government said the mediators will work with the parties for 10 days to help reach a settlement and, if needed, provide recommendations to the minister [6][3][5]. The appointment followed the union’s statement that negotiations with the employer had broken off, putting the dispute into what it called a “significantly new phase” of job action with expanded pickets at care centres across B.C. [3][5]. BCNU president Adriane Gear said she welcomed the appointments and would recommend that the union board proceed with mediation, but said members would continue planned job action, including picket lines in Victoria starting Sunday and Nanaimo on Monday [4][5]. Premier David Eby said the dispute has drawn attention to nursing working conditions and safety, and said the province has taken steps such as nurse-to-patient ratios and security-screening pilots while acknowledging more issues remain [3][4]. Gear said the issues include working conditions, understaffing and safety, and argued the province cannot fill thousands of vacancies unless it addresses systemic problems beyond wages [4][5].
