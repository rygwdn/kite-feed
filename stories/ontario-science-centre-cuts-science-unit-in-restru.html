Ontario Science Centre cuts science unit in restructuring

Category: Canada

The Ontario Science Centre has laid off roughly 30 workers in a restructuring. The Canadian Press said the cuts included the entire science department, while OPSEU Local 549 said they eliminated the centre’s standalone science unit [1][2][4]. CEO Paul Kortenaar told employees in a Thursday video meeting that business development, science, security and live exhibits would not be part of the new organizational structure [1]. The layoffs follow Ontario’s closure of the east Toronto science centre in summer 2024 after the province cited an engineering report that found roof deficiencies. Critics later questioned that explanation, noting the roof withstood record snowfall and rainfall over the next two years [1]. The government is building a new $1-billion science centre at Ontario Place, aims to open it by 2029, and says interim programming at Harbourfront Centre and future programming will continue without change [1][2][4].
