Nintendo Switch 2 quarterly sales fall 37% as lifetime total reaches 26.35 million

Category: Technology

Nintendo sold 6.49 million Switch 2 consoles from July through September 2026, down 37.4% from the 10.36 million sold in the same quarter a year earlier, when the system launched [2]. Lifetime sales reached 26.35 million, nearly seven million ahead of the original Switch and PlayStation 5 at comparable points after launch [2]. Nintendo said it has enough inventory to meet its fiscal-year target of 16.5 million Switch 2 units by March 2027 and has no near-term plans for another price increase [4]. Nintendo president Shuntaro Furukawa pointed to Pokémon: Winds / Waves, scheduled for 2027, as a key release for the console’s third year [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Game sales: Switch 2 software sales rose 18.8% year over year in the quarter; Rhythm Heaven Groove sold 2.89 million copies, Splatoon Raiders 2.42 million and Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave 1.34 million [2].
  • Memory costs: LevelUp attributed the hardware slowdown partly to September price increases following global memory shortages and higher component costs [4].
  • Profit decline: Nintendo’s operating profit fell 16% and total sales fell 15%, according to company results cited by LevelUp [4].
