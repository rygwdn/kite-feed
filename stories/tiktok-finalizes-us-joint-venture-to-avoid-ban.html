TikTok finalizes US joint venture to avoid ban

Category: Business

TikTok said it has finalized a deal to create a new U.S. unit backed by major American investors, a structure intended to keep the app operating in the United States and head off the threat of a nationwide ban [2][6]. Several outlets described the arrangement as a joint venture that shifts operational control to a U.S.-based investor group while leaving parts of TikTok’s broader business with parent ByteDance [1][10]. A White House official told CBS News the agreement was finalized with China, and noted a 2024 sell-or-ban law had taken effect but has not been enforced by President Trump [3]. For users and creators, the immediate upside is continuity while governance and data-security safeguards are designed to address U.S. concerns [2][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: The TikTok joint-venture safeguards are designed to cover CapCut and Lemon8 as well, not just TikTok itself [10].

Key Points

  • Ownership split: Business Insider reported ByteDance will hold roughly a 20% stake in the new U.S. venture, while Oracle, Silver Lake and Abu Dhabi-based MGX will each own 15%, with affiliates of existing ByteDance investors holding around 30% [10].
  • Leadership setup: TikTok said Adam Presser will lead the new venture, and the majority-American board includes TikTok CEO Shou Chew, according to Business Insider’s account of the announcement [10].
  • Scope of safeguards: TikTok said the joint-venture safeguards will also cover CapCut, Lemon8 and other apps and websites in the U.S., expanding the deal’s reach beyond the flagship TikTok app [10].
  • Hawk criticism: The Financial Times said the deal has been criticized by China hawks even as TikTok launched a U.S. joint venture with American investors, signaling continued political scrutiny [1].
  • Algorithm question: Firstpost said U.S. investors receive majority control under the deal, but reported uncertainty remains over who ultimately controls TikTok’s recommendation algorithm [7].

Technical Details

  • Joint venture structure: Several outlets described the solution as a new U.S. joint venture that shifts aspects of operational control and governance to American investors to meet U.S. legal requirements [1][6][10].
  • Sell-or-ban compliance: Business Insider said ByteDance’s stake of just under about 20% meets the divestment requirement set by the 2024 sell-or-ban law targeting apps owned in countries the U.S. deems foreign adversaries [10].
  • Recommendation algorithm: Firstpost reported that control of the algorithm remains a key open question in assessing how much independence the U.S. entity will have [7].

Industry Impact

  • Digital advertising: By preventing an immediate U.S. shutdown, the deal preserves a major channel for brands and small businesses that buy TikTok ads and depend on continuity of audience access [2][6].
  • Creator economy: Business Insider said TikTok’s U.S. workers can "finally breathe a sigh of relief," signaling improved stability for employees and the broader creator-and-agency ecosystem built around the platform [10].
  • M&A playbook: The multi-investor structure involving Oracle, private equity and a sovereign-backed fund (MGX) may serve as a template for how geopolitically sensitive apps attempt to localize ownership while keeping global parent-company ties [10][1].
  • App portfolios: TikTok said safeguards cover CapCut and Lemon8 too, which could reduce regulatory overhang for adjacent consumer apps and their monetization plans in the U.S. [10].

Historical Background

U.S. scrutiny of TikTok has centered on national-security concerns tied to its Chinese ownership and culminated in a 2024 law requiring a sale or ban, which CBS News said went into effect but has not been enforced by President Trump [3]. Chicago Tribune and MarketWatch described the agreement as the endpoint of a multi-year legal and political fight over whether TikTok could keep operating in the U.S. [8][6].

Action Items

  • Advertisers: confirm account continuity plans: Ask your TikTok account representative whether campaigns will be served by the new U.S. entity and whether invoicing, data access or brand-safety settings change under the joint venture [6].
  • Creators: review connected apps and tools: If you use CapCut or Lemon8 alongside TikTok, check whether any U.S.-specific terms or account settings update as the safeguards roll out across TikTok’s U.S. app portfolio [10].
