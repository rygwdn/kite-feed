French gambling regulator orders ISPs to block Polymarket

Category: Technology

France’s gambling regulator, the Autorité Nationale des Jeux, ordered internet service providers to block access to Polymarket, saying the prediction-market platform offers unauthorized gambling services under French law [2][5][7][6]. The order was issued July 16, Crypto.news and CoinDesk reported, and follows a dispute that began with a November 2024 restriction on financial transactions by French residents on Polymarket [5][7]. The ANJ said the earlier transaction restrictions did not stop French users from reaching the site, with French traffic reaching 578,751 visits and 205,057 unique visitors in June 2026 [2][3][5][7][6]. The regulator also warned that advertising an unauthorized betting or gambling site can carry fines of up to €100,000, about $114,000 [2][1][5][7].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Safety concerns: The ANJ cited addictive mechanics, missing self-exclusion tools and high levels of attempted access by French users as reasons for the ISP block [7][10].
  • Market integrity: Meteo-France filed a complaint in April after officials said hackers tampered with a digital weather probe to affect weather-related Polymarket outcomes [4].
  • Product classification: French authorities classify Polymarket’s event contracts as gambling, while CoinDesk reported the regulator rejected treatment of the site as a financial trading platform [7][5].
  • European pressure: Spain has also ordered blocks on Polymarket and Kalshi while investigating whether the platforms violate gambling laws [2][6].

Perspectives

Polymarket: Polymarket did not immediately respond to CoinDesk’s request for comment on the French ISP block order.

Sources: CoinDesk

Timeline

  • November 2024: ANJ imposed financial transaction restrictions on French residents [2][5][7].
  • April 2026: Meteo-France filed a complaint over weather-probe tampering claims [4].
  • June 2026: Polymarket recorded 578,751 French visits in June [2][5][7].
  • 16 July 2026: ANJ ordered French ISPs to block Polymarket [5][7].

Technical Details

  • Self-exclusion controls: French regulators faulted Polymarket for lacking effective tools that let users cap activity, set limits or lock themselves out, protections that regulated gambling environments commonly require [7][10].
  • Real-time odds display: The ANJ treated Polymarket’s dynamically updated odds on its homepage as advertising for unauthorized gambling, not merely informational market data [7][5].

Industry Impact

  • European regulation: Spain, Germany and Italy have imposed restrictions on prediction markets, with Spain ordering blocks on Polymarket and Kalshi [2][4].
  • US state regulation: Minnesota passed a law banning prediction markets from operating in the state, while other US states have filed lawsuits against Polymarket and Kalshi [2][6].

Business Angle

  • When regulators classify event contracts as gambling rather than financial products, prediction-market operators face greater licensing and enforcement risk [7][5].
  • Polymarket’s French audience showed demand despite payment restrictions, but access blocks can limit the market and make it harder to reach users [2][7].
  • Platforms may need gambling licenses in each jurisdiction, adding regulatory costs and complexity that can slow growth [7][5].

International Reactions

  • 🇨🇿 Czech Republic: Crypto.news reported that Czech action forms part of wider pressure on prediction markets under several regulatory frameworks [5].

Action Items

  • Avoid promoting unlicensed betting sites in France: Do not advertise, publish promotional odds or share payout ratios for unauthorized gambling services, because the ANJ says violations can carry fines up to €100,000 [5][2].
