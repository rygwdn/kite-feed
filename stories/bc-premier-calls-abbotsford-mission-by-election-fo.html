B.C. premier calls Abbotsford-Mission by-election for Sept. 26

Category: Canada

B.C. Premier David Eby has called a by-election in Abbotsford-Mission, setting final voting day for Saturday, Sept. 26. The seat became vacant when Conservative MLA Reann Gasper resigned on Aug. 22 [2][1][4][6][7]. The riding is where B.C. Conservative Leader Kerry-Lynne Findlay is expected to run as she seeks a seat in the legislature [1][4][6][7]. Findlay was elected party leader in May but has faced internal pressure since six MLAs left her caucus to sit as independents, including five in one week. Defectors have questioned her leadership and the party’s direction [1][4][6][7]. Vancouver Sun reported that leaked July audio allegedly captured Findlay discussing senior adviser roles for MLAs who stepped aside; Global News reported Findlay said suggestions that the recording showed illegal job offers in exchange for resignations were false [6][7].
