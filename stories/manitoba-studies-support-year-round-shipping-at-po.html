Manitoba studies support year-round shipping at Port of Churchill

Category: Canada

Manitoba Premier Wab Kinew said new studies show year-round shipping may be possible from the Port of Churchill, strengthening the case for expanding the Arctic deep-sea port [1][3]. CBC described Churchill as Canada's only rail-accessible deepwater port on Arctic waters, and The Globe and Mail said backers see the research as supporting a multibillion-dollar expansion [4][2]. The studies cite advances in marine technology and declining sea ice linked to climate change as reasons the current roughly four-month shipping window could be expanded [1][4]. Kinew said Churchill could operate year-round with current technology, using icebreakers and ships that cost less than previously expected [1]. Arctic Gateway Group, which owns the port and the Hudson Bay Railway, partnered with Fednav to study what year-round shipping would require [4]. The federal and Manitoba governments also commissioned the Arctic Research Foundation to study whether the port's operating season can be extended [4].
