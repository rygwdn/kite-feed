OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra; Nvidia CEO says AGI has arrived

Category: AI

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra, its latest artificial intelligence model, calling it a “new generation of intelligence” and saying it performed better in software engineering, science, mathematics, cybersecurity and professional workflows [4][24][26]. The model debuted for paid ChatGPT users, including Plus, Pro and Enterprise customers, with API access also available. Amazon Web Services promoted access to GPT-6 Astra through Amazon Bedrock [22][3]. After the launch, Nvidia Chief Executive Jensen Huang said, “AGI has arrived,” and OpenAI President Greg Brockman said it was not an exaggeration to say the industry had entered the AGI era [5][9][24]. OpenAI also confirmed that GPT-6 Astra is its first broadly deployed model to reach a “Critical” level for cybersecurity capabilities, including the ability to find zero-day vulnerabilities. BleepingComputer and Xataka reported concerns that the model is harder to monitor and that its stated reasoning may not reliably reflect its internal reasoning process [27][28].

Did you know: A Russian report said GPT-6 Astra completed all 48 levels of an “I’m not a robot” CAPTCHA challenge [25].

Key Points

  • API pricing: Capital reported developer pricing of $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens for GPT-6 Astra API access [22].
  • Training scale: Huang said GPT-6 Astra used more than 100,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell NVLink72 GPUs during training and said the next step would bring 400,000 GPUs online [24].
  • Agentic tasks: TechRadar, cited by Taiwan’s Central News Agency, described a major upgrade in agentic tasks that lowers the risk of AI going off track or misinterpreting instructions during multi-step delegated work [24].
  • Developer tooling: Simon Willison released llm 0.35, adding support for OpenAI’s gpt-6-astra model in his command-line large-language-model tool [1].

Historical Background

Artificial general intelligence generally refers to AI systems that can perform most intellectual work at about a human level, but no single universally accepted technical or regulatory definition exists [24][common]. OpenAI’s ChatGPT launch in 2022 turned large language models into mainstream consumer software, and Huang said the path from ChatGPT and o1 to Astra took four years [24][common].

Business Angle

  • Nvidia has the most specific hardware link: Huang said Astra training used more than 100,000 Grace Blackwell NVLink72 GPUs and that the next step would involve 400,000 GPUs [24].
  • Cloud providers could gain from distribution if enterprises adopt Astra through managed services such as Amazon Bedrock rather than direct model access [3][common].
  • Taiwan’s server, cooling, PCB, power and data-center supply chains could benefit if higher inference use raises per-rack power density and component value [26].
  • Investors should discount AGI claims unless they translate into recurring revenue, lower unit costs or defensible customer retention [common].

Action Items

  • Review API costs before testing Astra: Use the reported $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens to estimate workload costs before moving prototypes to production [22].
  • Tighten model monitoring: Security teams using Astra should reassess logging, access controls and human review after reports said the model reached a Critical cybersecurity capability level and is harder to monitor [27][28].
