India’s market regulator plans AI rules requiring human oversight

Category: AI

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), the country’s capital markets regulator, plans detailed rules for the use of artificial intelligence in financial markets that would require disclosures, accountability from regulated firms and human oversight of critical decisions, Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said [3][4]. The rules aim to protect investors and market integrity as market participants adopt AI [4]. The proposal follows a SEBI consultation paper on AI and machine learning issued in June 2025 [3][4]. Pandey also cited cyberattacks on financial institutions and cryptocurrency platforms, as well as digital fraud involving social media influencers, as regulatory concerns [4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Cyber response: SEBI introduced a structured cyber-incident reporting mechanism in May 2026, aligned with the Financial Stability Board’s FIRE reporting template [4][5].
  • Quantum risks: SEBI is inventorying cryptographic systems to identify vulnerabilities; Pandey said quantum-computing risks could become more pronounced around 2032-35 [4][3].
