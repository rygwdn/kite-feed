Surrey police probe three drive-by shootings at home

Category: Canada

Police in Surrey, B.C., say a home in the Guildford neighbourhood was hit by gunfire in three separate drive-by shootings in December, and investigators believe the incidents were targeted at a resident [2][3]. No injuries have been reported among residents or neighbours, a relief in a residential area near an elementary school [2]. Police have made no arrests, and the RCMP has taken over the investigation [2]. Separately, police are also investigating a different Surrey shooting that they believe may be tied to extortion [2][4].
