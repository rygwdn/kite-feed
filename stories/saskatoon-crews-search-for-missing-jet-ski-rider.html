Saskatoon crews search for missing jet ski rider

Category: Canada

Saskatoon emergency officials shifted from rescue to recovery after a personal watercraft rider went over the weir on the South Saskatchewan River and did not resurface Saturday afternoon [1][2][3]. Police and fire crews responded to multiple reports near the weir beside the train bridge and 33rd Street East [1][2][3]. The Saskatoon Fire Department said water rescue crews were called at 4:49 p.m. CST Saturday after reports that the rider was in distress near the weir [2]. As of Sunday, a Saskatoon police spokesperson said no one had been recovered from the water [3].

Key Points

  • Witness account: Witness Mattia Salis said first responders arrived within about 15 minutes, after the rider had gone under [2].
  • Access limits: Salis said the rider was in the middle of the river, leaving him unable to reach the person from shore [2].

Perspectives

Witness Mattia Salis said he was fishing from rocks along the shore when he saw the rider head toward the weir. "I see a person on a boat or a jet ski just coming down the middle of the river towards the weir and in a second [they] fell down and disappeared," he said, adding that he called 911.

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: A witness at the scene described the weir area as dangerous for boats after a rider went over the structure and disappeared [2]. Avoid boating or riding personal watercraft near the Saskatoon weir.
