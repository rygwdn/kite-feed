President Trump meets Irish leaders before Doonbeg golf tournament

Category: World

President Trump arrived in Dublin on Saturday for a two-day visit to Ireland, holding separate meetings with President Catherine Connolly and Taoiseach Micheál Martin before speaking to U.S. and Irish business leaders at the U.S. ambassador’s residence. He later planned to travel to Doonbeg in County Clare, where his Trump International Golf Links is hosting the Irish Open, which concludes Sunday [1][2][3]. Trump told Martin that the U.S.-Ireland relationship was “stronger than ever.” The talks were expected to cover trade and other issues, including the Iran war, Palestine and economic relations [4][65]. The visit is not an official state visit and has drawn protests and criticism from some Irish residents because it combines presidential engagements with an event at a resort he owns [5][3].
