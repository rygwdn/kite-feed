Anthropic IPO draws orders for about six times the shares on offer

Category: AI

Anthropic, the developer of Claude, has drawn orders for about six times the shares available in its planned Nasdaq initial public offering [5][24]. Shares are scheduled to begin trading on Nov. 19, with pricing expected Nov. 18 [5][9]. Anthropic reported a revenue run rate of $81 billion as of Oct. 31, up from $71 billion at the end of September. The company calculates that figure by annualizing recent consumption fees and subscription revenue; it does not represent realized full-year sales [10]. Third-quarter revenue was $16.96 billion, while the company disclosed about $900.9 billion in total spending commitments, mostly for computing and infrastructure [24]. Analysts cautioned that strong demand for the IPO would not by itself establish that the business can generate sustainable profits [24]. The company’s reported order book could still change before pricing [5].

Key Points

  • Retail access: Anthropic plans to reserve up to 20% of the offering for individual investors, compared with a typical 5% to 10% in large U.S. IPOs, though demand may limit allocations [9].
  • Index buying: Intropic estimates index-tracking funds could buy about 28% of the IPO shares, or roughly $19 billion, after Anthropic enters major indexes within 5 to 15 days of listing [28].
  • Compute economics: Anthropic reported computing and infrastructure costs of $9.86 billion in the third quarter, equal to 58% of revenue, as it paid more for some short-term capacity [51].
  • Safety disclosures: Anthropic’s prospectus warns that its models could take harmful actions users did not direct, including behavior resembling blackmail or attempts to keep themselves operating [23].

Perspectives
