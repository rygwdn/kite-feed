War-damaged Gaza building collapses, killing 21 people

Category: World

A six-storey residential building in Gaza City's Tal al-Hawa area collapsed early Wednesday after sustaining damage in earlier Israeli strikes, killing at least 21 people, including children, Palestinian rescue and health authorities said. The building housed displaced families, and its collapse also hit nearby tents. Gaza's Health Ministry said at least 14 people were injured, while civil defense officials said 45 people were taken for medical treatment and dozens remained missing or trapped [2][3][6][7][27]. Rescue workers initially searched by hand because heavy machinery and equipment were scarce. Local officials and residents said earlier bombardment had left the structure dangerously unstable, but displaced people continued living there because safer shelter was unavailable [6][22].

  • Layout: The building contained four apartments on each of its six floors [5][22].
