Poland says Russian missiles and drone crash near military drills

Category: World

Poland said two Russian missiles and a likely jet-powered drone entered its airspace and fell at three sites in the southeast on Friday during Russia’s overnight assault on Ukraine. Polish authorities reported no injuries or significant damage in Poland [1][22][25]. Prime Minister Donald Tusk called the incursion a likely deliberate provocation, noting that European forces were conducting exercises nearby to prepare for a possible postwar mission in Ukraine. NATO scrambled aircraft, and its secretary-general, Mark Rutte, called the airspace breaches “grave violations.” Moscow had not commented on the incident [12][25][2]. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia fired more than 400 drones and over 110 missiles in the attack on Ukraine; Ukrainian authorities reported at least eight people killed and 28 injured [34][35]. Warsaw plans to seek additional NATO support for its eastern flank, and Rutte was due to visit Poland on Saturday, Nov. 21 [18][27].

Key Points

  • Impact sites: Polish officials identified a Kh-101 cruise missile at Nowy Lipowiec, a second suspected missile near Rudnik nad Sanem and a likely jet-powered drone at Mołodycz [7][22].
  • Defense request: Poland is seeking additional surveillance aircraft, including an AWACS radar plane [27][22].
  • NATO provisions: Article 5 does not activate automatically and requires a political decision by all alliance members; Article 4 provides for emergency consultations [1][2].

Perspectives

Polish government: Tusk argued that Poland must take part in decisions on Ukraine’s and Europe’s security, saying the country is especially exposed to Russian aggression [12].

Technical Details

  • Flight analysis: Preliminary Polish military analysis put the objects’ speed above 800 kilometers per hour [20].
  • Air defense response: Polish Patriot systems and other ground-based air defenses were activated [34][31].

Historical Background

In November 2022, missile debris fell in the Polish village of Przewodów, killing two people; investigators concluded it came from Ukrainian air defenses, though Western leaders said Russia ultimately bore responsibility for the circumstances [7]. In July 2026, a Russian Kh-101 missile also fell in Poland’s Lublin region, in an uninhabited area [23].

Humanitarian Impact

Strikes caused power outages in Kyiv and several regions, while nuclear power plants reduced output as crews worked to repair the grid [36][35].

International Reactions

  • 🇪🇺 European Union: European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen condemned the airspace breach and expressed solidarity with Poland [20][26].
  • 🇬🇧 United Kingdom: Prime Minister Andy Burnham condemned the incursion and said Britain would support Poland; Defence Secretary Wes Streeting was in Poland for the exercises [10][25].
  • 🇫🇷 France: France called for a UN Security Council meeting after Russia’s attack on Ukraine struck the Austrian embassy and landed near the Netherlands embassy in Kyiv [34].
