Drone strike in Kuwait kills two Italian defense technicians

Category: World

Two Italian technicians were killed when drones struck a Kuwaiti military facility on Thursday, Nov. 26, and falling debris injured a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani national, Kuwaiti and Italian officials said [7][20][2]. The men worked for Rheinmetall Italia, the Italian unit of German defense contractor Rheinmetall, maintaining detection antennas for Kuwait’s Skyguard air-defense system [12][19]. Kuwait and Italy blamed Iran; Tehran denied responsibility. Iran’s armed forces said they had conducted no operation in the region, while Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi offered to join Kuwait and Italy in investigating the incident [3][18]. Kuwait said its forces detected and intercepted hostile drones, but the defense ministry did not identify the facility or describe the damage [20][52]. Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani said the drones were Iranian-made and noted that Iran-aligned armed groups also operate in the region [20]. Attacks on Gulf states hosting U.S. forces had resumed after a relative lull, while negotiations to end the U.S.-Iran war remained stalled [7][41]. Kuwait condemned the attack as a violation of its sovereignty and said it reserved the right to take measures to defend its territory [42]. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen urged increased pressure on Tehran, while Araghchi proposed a joint investigation with Kuwait and Italy [3][59].
