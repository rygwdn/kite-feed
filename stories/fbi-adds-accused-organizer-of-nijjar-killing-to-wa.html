FBI adds accused organizer of Nijjar killing to wanted list

Category: Canada

The FBI added Satinderjeet Singh, known as Goldy Brar, to its Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list on Sept. 28 and offered up to US$1 million for information leading to his arrest [1][6][8]. U.S. authorities allege Brar ordered and aided the June 2023 killing of Sikh activist Hardeep Singh Nijjar in Surrey, British Columbia, and leads the Lawrence Bishnoi crime group’s North American operations [6][4]. Brar faces U.S. federal charges of racketeering conspiracy, extortion and controlled-substance distribution; the allegations have not been proven in court [18][16]. The FBI says it believes he is in the United States and considers him armed and dangerous [6][16].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • List history: Brar is the 543rd fugitive featured since the list began in 1950 [18].
  • Nijjar case: Four other people charged in Nijjar’s killing are in custody awaiting trial [6].
  • Public warning: The FBI also described Brar as an escape risk and advised the public to avoid interacting with him [16].

Timeline

  • 2023: Brar appeared on Canada’s 25 most-wanted fugitives list [12].
  • 1 July 2026: A federal arrest warrant was issued for Brar in California [22].
  • July 2026: U.S. authorities announced new charges against Brar [6].
