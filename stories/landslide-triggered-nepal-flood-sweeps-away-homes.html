Landslide-triggered Nepal flood sweeps away homes and bridges, prompts evacuations

Category: World

A flash flood on Nepal’s Budhi Gandaki River swept away homes and bridges in Gorkha district on Sunday, Oct. 11, after a landslide reportedly blocked the river and sent muddy water downstream. Authorities ordered riverside communities to evacuate. Four people were reported missing, while officials said there were no confirmed casualties in initial updates [7][8][23]. Damage estimates rose as officials assessed the area. Gorkha’s chief district officer initially cited about a dozen houses and two suspension bridges, while local reports said at least 35 homes and six bridges were swept away. The flood also damaged a hydroelectric project camp and washed away a highway bridge linking Gorkha with Dhading [8][7][23]. Authorities told residents in Gorkha, Tanahun, Chitwan, East Nawalparasi and West Nawalparasi to move to safer ground. The army rescued six people near Jagat, and 18 workers escaped from the damaged project camp. Police and army personnel reached the area and monitored conditions from a helicopter [8][23].

Key Points

  • Flood alert: Nepal’s flood forecasting section issued an SMS alert at 6:40 a.m., warning people to stay away from the river as the surge moved toward Trishuli Dobhan [10][16].
  • Early warning signs: Residents heard unusually loud water around 4 a.m.; local officials said the Syar and Ungilung streams had also risen sharply [20][10].
  • Flood-hit settlements: Homes were swept away in Salleri, Paiyuka and the riverbed at Jagat, according to local officials [12].
  • August flood aftermath: Nepal estimated rebuilding would cost $4.77 billion; nearly 33,000 people were affected and about 7,500 homes were damaged or destroyed [3].

Perspectives

Nepal National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority: The agency called for assistance with search-and-rescue operations in at-risk locations [23].

Sources: Channel NewsAsia

Historical Background

In August, a glacier collapse caused a catastrophic flood in a tributary area upstream from the Trishuli River, killing 1,457 people, with thousands still missing [23][8]. The Budhi Gandaki flows into the Trishuli, the river system downstream from that disaster [3][23].

Humanitarian Impact

A resident said the flood came so early that people had no time to take their belongings as they fled [12].

Q&A

Q: How were people warned to leave?

A: Local police issued a safety advisory, and authorities used loudspeakers to direct residents toward safer locations [1][20].
