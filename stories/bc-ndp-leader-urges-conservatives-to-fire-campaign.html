B.C. NDP leader urges Conservatives to fire campaign director over anti-Islam post

Category: Canada

British Columbia NDP Leader David Eby called on interim Conservative leader Lorne Doerkson on Saturday to remove campaign director Katy Merrifield over a social media post criticizing Islam [2][3]. Eby’s call followed Doerkson’s dismissal of Conservative candidate Joachim Agou for comments about Muslims. Agou later apologized [2][3]. Merrifield said she deleted the post within hours and that her anger was directed at extremism, not Muslims or their faith. She said she is a rape survivor and that reading the report, which she described as detailing sexual abuse of girls and failures by authorities, brought her to tears [3]. Conservative candidate Zeeshan Wahla called the original language unacceptable but said it differed from Agou’s comments, which he described as inciting violence [3].

  • Agou’s posts: One post by the fired candidate showed a car driving into pro-Palestinian protesters in Switzerland; Agou commented that the driver should have reversed as well [3].
  • Merrifield’s post: Her June 17 post called Islam an “abhorrent ideology” and urged readers to defend “Western civilization” [2][3].
  • Agou later apologized, and Victoria-Beacon Hill, where he was running, is considered a safe NDP seat [3].
