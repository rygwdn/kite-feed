Washington resident pleads not guilty in Tumbler Ridge shooting conspiracy

Category: Canada

James Cody Bryant, 30, of Bellingham, Washington, pleaded not guilty Thursday in federal court in Seattle to a charge of conspiring to murder people in a foreign country. Bryant is accused of helping plan the February mass shooting in Tumbler Ridge, British Columbia. Bryant’s lawyers say she uses the name Melody and she/her pronouns. A grand jury indicted her; she remains in custody, and her trial is scheduled to begin Dec. 21 [4][3][5]. The FBI alleges Bryant communicated online with shooter Jesse Van Rootselaar, advised her about the attack and helped arrange a livestream. Van Rootselaar killed eight people, including five students and an educational assistant, before dying by suicide. The allegations against Bryant have not been proven. Her lawyers argued that she had sometimes tried to dissuade Van Rootselaar and disputed that she posed an ongoing danger. A judge denied bail [4][3][2].

Key Points

  • Chat evidence: The FBI complaint says Bryant and Van Rootselaar exchanged thousands of Discord messages over several months about attack logistics, weapons and livestreaming [7][4].
  • Brief livestream: The stream lasted about 20 seconds. Only one account viewed it, which the FBI identified as Bryant’s [4][7].
  • Defense argument: Public defenders Greg Murphy and Dennis Carroll cited the seven-month gap between Bryant’s FBI interviews and her Oct. 6 arrest in arguing against detention [3][5].
  • Possible penalty: The charge carries a maximum sentence of life in prison and a US$250,000 fine [4][3].

Perspectives

James Cody Bryant’s defense lawyers: Public defenders Greg Murphy and Dennis Carroll argued there was no evidence their client had ever communicated with anyone as deeply disturbed as Van Rootselaar. They said the government lacked clear and convincing evidence that Bryant posed a danger that could not be mitigated [3][5].

Historical Background

The FBI complaint says Bryant and Van Rootselaar first met three years before the attack on the now-defunct chat-roulette website Omegle, then reconnected after periods when Van Rootselaar was institutionalized [4]. Bryant told the FBI they had never met in person [4].
