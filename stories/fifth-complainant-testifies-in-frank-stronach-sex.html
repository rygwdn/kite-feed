Fifth complainant testifies in Frank Stronach sex-assault trial

Category: Canada

A fifth complainant testified in Toronto in the criminal trial of billionaire businessman Frank Stronach, becoming emotional as she described an alleged sexual assault in a hotel suite, according to courtroom reporting [1][2][4]. Stronach has pleaded not guilty to a dozen charges involving seven complainants, and the case continues in Ontario court [1]. The woman told the court that Stronach became “a different person” once they were inside the suite and that he grew more aggressive as she pleaded with him to stop, details also reported by French-language outlet La Presse [2][4].

Key Points

  • Additional detail on her allegation: In separate reporting, the woman told the court Stronach raped her in a hotel room in 1990 and recalled saying, “Frank … please don’t do this” [3].
  • Trial pacing: Her testimony came as the Crown continues calling complainants in the Toronto trial, with proceedings ongoing in Ontario court [1][2].
