Microsoft names Asha Sharma to lead Xbox

Category: Technology

Microsoft is reshuffling the leadership of its gaming business: long-time Xbox chief Phil Spencer is retiring, and the company has named former CoreAI Product president Asha Sharma as executive vice president and CEO of Microsoft Gaming. Matt Booty is also moving into an expanded role overseeing content [1][4][7]. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella told employees that, as Xbox marks its 25th year, the company still sees gaming as central to its consumer ambitions—even as cloud computing and AI remain major priorities [1]. One early signal from the incoming gaming chief is a clear tone on generative AI. Sharma said she doesn’t want the Xbox ecosystem flooded with “endless AI slop,” casting AI as something to use carefully rather than to churn out low-quality volume [2][3]. The transition also includes the departure of Xbox president Sarah Bond, who said she is leaving Microsoft to “begin a new chapter.” Together, the moves suggest Microsoft is resetting how it runs—and talks about—the Xbox brand at a moment when players are especially sensitive to how AI could affect creativity, jobs, and game quality [1][4][7].

Key Points

  • Hardware hint: PCMag says Sharma promised a “return of Xbox,” but did not give a date for when any new physical hardware might arrive [6].
  • Spending backdrop: Reuters notes the shakeup comes as cloud computing and AI drive growth at Microsoft, while also demanding massive infrastructure spending to support those efforts [1].
  • Booty’s remit and track record: Matt Booty’s promotion to chief content officer builds on his previous role as president of game content and studios at Microsoft, according to his LinkedIn page cited in coverage [1].
