UK court sentences comedian Russell Brand to 10½ years for rape, sexual assaults

Category: World

British comedian and actor Russell Brand was sentenced on Nov. 9 to 10 years and six months in prison for raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others. He is expected to serve two-thirds of the term in custody before release on licence and will face lifelong sex-offender notification requirements [8][10]. A jury convicted Brand on Nov. 5 after a four-week trial. He was acquitted of two other rape charges and a charge of attempted indecent assault. He had denied all the charges, maintaining that his sexual encounters were consensual [4][35]. The convictions concerned offences between 2004 and 2009, including the rape of a 21-year-old fan and sexual assaults at a London bar, a radio station and a TV studio. Six women made allegations at trial; the court heard that four of them provided victim impact statements describing lasting trauma [2][20]. London’s Metropolitan Police is investigating two further rape allegations against Brand, one involving a person who was 16 at the time. Police have appealed for victims to come forward [19][6]. After sentencing, TV producer Jemima Goldsmith, Brand’s former partner, said he had subjected her to violence and coercive control. She said she called police during a 2014 incident but did not press charges [18].

Key Points

  • Sentence terms: The rape conviction carried a nine-year term; the three sexual-assault sentences were nine months, 18 months and eight months. The court ordered Brand to serve two-thirds of the total term in custody [6][47].
  • Police inquiry: Investigators said they traced more than 100 witnesses, collected over 400 statements and examined more than 1,400 documents to build the case [6][28].
  • Victim testimony: One complainant said the assault left her with lasting self-doubt, while another said hearing Brand’s voice or seeing his image made her jump [20].
  • Further reports: Metropolitan Police Commissioner Mark Rowley said he would not be surprised if more women came forward as officers continue investigating [4][49].

Perspectives
