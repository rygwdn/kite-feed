Pearson heist witness says snitching is never positive in prison

Category: Canada

Arsalan Chaudhary, a Mississauga man sentenced to four years for his role in the 2023 Pearson Airport gold theft, told a Brampton court Monday that snitching is never viewed positively in prison. Crown prosecutor Jelena Vlacic asked whether giving evidence against someone in custody was difficult. Chaudhary struggled to recall details he had previously provided about the case [2]. Chaudhary was also ordered to repay $22 million over 40 years [1]. The trial concerns allegations that former Air Canada employee Parmpal Sidhu supplied a doctored waybill that enabled another man to collect more than $22 million in gold and currency from Pearson airport. Sidhu has pleaded not guilty to theft and conspiracy charges [1][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Scale of theft: Peel regional police said the shipment included 6,600 gold bars weighing more than 400 kilograms [1][4].
  • Chaudhary’s share: Chaudhary testified that he took 30% of the boxes of one-ounce gold bars, melted some of the gold and later travelled to Pakistan, where he was kidnapped and abused until he surrendered the loot [1].
