Amazon weighs $7 billion buyout of AI startup Decart

Category: AI

Amazon is in talks to acquire AI startup Decart for about $7 billion, The Wall Street Journal reported. An agreement could be reached soon if negotiations hold and no rival buyer emerges [2][4][19]. The discussions are not final: Reuters said it could not immediately verify the report, while Amazon declined to comment and Decart did not respond to a request for comment [15][19]. Decart develops Lucy, an Nvidia-backed model that edits live video in real time, and Oasis, which creates simulated environments for training and testing robotics and autonomous-driving systems [12][25]. It also makes software that helps developers move AI workloads between chips. Amazon Web Services is promoting its own Trainium processors as an alternative to Nvidia hardware [17][18]. The proposed price would exceed Decart’s nearly $4 billion valuation in May, when it raised $300 million in a funding round led by Radical Ventures [11][15]. Anthropic explored a possible acquisition for about $6 billion in August but decided not to proceed after due diligence in September. Amazon’s talks follow that abandoned effort [19][17].

Key Points

  • Funding base: Decart has raised more than $400 million from investors including Sequoia Capital, Benchmark and Radical Ventures, according to PitchBook data cited in coverage [24][25].
  • Company origins: Israeli engineers and brothers Dean and Orian Leitersdorf founded Decart in 2023 with Moshe Shalev [11][25].

Timeline

  • 2023: Israeli engineers founded Decart in San Francisco [25].
  • 2025: AWS gave Decart early access to Trainium3 after discussions [16].
  • May 2026: Nvidia joined Decart’s May funding round as a new investor [15].
  • August 2026: Anthropic explored a possible Decart acquisition for about $6 billion [19].
  • September 2026: Anthropic ended acquisition efforts after its due diligence [11].
  • 11 October 2026: The Wall Street Journal reported Amazon’s talks at about $7 billion [2].
