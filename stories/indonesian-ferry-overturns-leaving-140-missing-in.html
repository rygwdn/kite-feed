Indonesian ferry overturns, leaving 140 missing in Java Sea

Category: World

Indonesian rescuers were searching Sunday for 140 people after the passenger ferry Virgo Transport 8 overturned in bad weather in the Java Sea. The vessel carried 243 passengers and crew on a route from Surabaya in East Java to Banjarmasin in South Kalimantan; 103 people were evacuated, and one later died. Rescue officials said the captain reported rough seas and that the ship was listing before authorities learned it had overturned [1][6][41]. The ferry lost contact at about 2 a.m. local time, and its last known position was about 148 kilometers from a pier in Banjarmasin. Authorities deployed rescue vessels and a helicopter, coordinated with maritime agencies and monitored conditions; officials said waves reached up to 2.5 meters and made the operation dangerous [3][11][5].
