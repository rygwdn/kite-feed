B.C. charter boat sinking toll rises to seven

Category: Canada

Richmond RCMP said one of four people rescued after the June 28 sinking of a fishing charter near Roberts Bank off Metro Vancouver had died in hospital, raising the suspected death toll to seven. Six people remain missing and are feared drowned [2][3][5][6]. Police identified the vessel as the Top Ocean, operated by Top Vancouver Fishing Charter, and said 10 people were aboard: eight charter guests, the operator and one person assisting the operator [2][5][6]. Police said the people involved were in their 20s and 30s and came from British Columbia, Ontario and Washington state [3][5][6]. Searchers using an underwater drone found the vessel 153 metres below the surface, and police said no bodies had been found [1][2][6]. Police said investigators are examining whether the vessel's operation played a role in the sinking [2][6]. Ashley Lin, whose son Chen Ming is among the missing, told Postmedia that Top Vancouver Fishing Charter had two boats on the water in poor weather that day and that the owner, on the second boat, lost sight of the vessel that sank [1].
