Bridge owner’s $1mn donation fuels US-Canada tension

Category: Canada

A new political flashpoint has formed around the busy Windsor–Detroit crossing after filings showed the owner of the privately run Ambassador Bridge made a $1 million donation to MAGA Inc., a pro-Trump political action committee. The contribution came weeks before President Trump publicly attacked a rival project, the publicly backed Gordie Howe International Bridge [1][2]. The PAC and the White House said the donation had no connection to the President’s criticism and threats involving the new bridge project [2]. For Canadians, the dispute matters because the Detroit–Windsor corridor is one of the country’s most important trade gateways, and the Gordie Howe bridge is meant to add redundancy and ease congestion alongside the existing Ambassador Bridge [2]. While the rhetoric has added strain to Canada–US relations, it also highlights a shared reality: both countries do best when border logistics are reliable and infrastructure plans stay on a long time horizon [1][2].

Historical Background

The Ambassador Bridge is a privately run crossing that has long carried a large share of freight between Detroit and Windsor, while the Gordie Howe International Bridge is a publicly backed project planned and financed through government-led arrangements [2]. The rivalry between a dominant private asset and a new state-backed competitor has played out for years; this latest episode ties that long-running competition to campaign-finance disclosures and renewed political scrutiny [1][2].
