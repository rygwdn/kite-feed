Apple’s first touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro may reach stores November 6

Category: Technology

Apple is expected to introduce its first touchscreen MacBook Pro later this month, with redesigned 14- and 16-inch models reportedly featuring Tandem OLED displays and a smaller Dynamic Island. A November 6 retail release is possible, based on a planned overnight store reset, but Apple has not confirmed the products, announcement or release date [2][4][3]. The touch interface is expected to adapt on-screen controls for finger input, and the computers are reportedly thinner and lighter. Reports describe the OLED touchscreen models as separate from a 14-inch MacBook Pro that retains the current design and uses an M6 chip. Apple has not confirmed any of the specifications [2][10][14].

Primary Source: View original article

For instance, if users touch a button or control, the interface will bring up a new type of menu surrounding their finger that provides more relevant options for touch commands.

— Mark Gurman, Bloomberg technology journalist (9to5Mac)

Key Points

  • Chip plans: The redesigned high-end touchscreen models reportedly use M5 Pro and M5 Max chips, while a separate 14-inch MacBook Pro with the current design and an iMac could receive M6 chips [2][6][10].
  • Software clues: macOS 27 reportedly includes Sidecar touch support, pull-to-refresh and slightly larger menu-bar items. Further changes for touch input and the Dynamic Island are expected with macOS 27.2 [2][14].
  • Tablet rumors: An OLED iPad mini may be part of the same product cycle. One report estimates a price near $799, compared with $599 for the current model, but calls that figure a guess [12][7].
