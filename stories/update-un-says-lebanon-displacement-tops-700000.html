Update: UN says Lebanon displacement tops 700,000

Category: World

In the latest development in the escalating Hezbollah–Israel conflict, the UN said evacuation orders and Israeli strikes have driven roughly 700,000 people from their homes in Lebanon in just over a week — including more than 100,000 in a single day [1][7]. Lebanon’s disaster management unit reported 759,300 people registered as displaced, with about 122,600 staying in government shelters, as fresh strikes hit Beirut’s southern suburbs and other areas after evacuation warnings [1][10][5]. Fighting has also reached central Beirut in recent days. Reports described strikes hitting residential buildings and a luxury hotel, with differing accounts of who was targeted [9][11][2]. Aid agencies warned that overcrowded shelters and host homes can raise public-health risks and disrupt routine care, even as many families rely on community networks and stay with relatives and friends when they can [1]. Some residents have voiced rare public criticism of Hezbollah as repeated displacement upends daily life. Israel says it is striking Hezbollah-linked targets and operatives as the wider regional escalation continues [4][7][11].

Lives have been upended on a massive scale

Key Points

  • Where people fled: UNHCR said many families fled within minutes, heading toward Beirut, Mount Lebanon, northern districts, and parts of the Beqaa — often carrying almost nothing [1].
  • Registration figures: UNHCR said more than 667,000 people had registered as displaced on the Lebanese government’s online platform, while Lebanon’s disaster management unit later put the registered total at 759,300 [1].
  • Central Beirut strike: Lebanese state media said an Israeli strike hit an apartment building in central Beirut. Reuters said there was no immediate Israeli military comment and no immediate reports of casualties at the time [9].
  • Prior strike in Beirut: Reuters said an earlier strike in Beirut on Sunday killed five members of Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps [9].
  • Political ripple: DW reported Lebanon postponed parliamentary elections as the escalation deepened and displacement grew [6].

Historical Background

Hezbollah and Israel fought a major war in 2006, and since then periodic cross-border escalations have repeatedly displaced civilians in southern Lebanon and northern Israel [common]. UNHCR said displacement in the current escalation is moving faster than during Israel’s last war against Hezbollah in Lebanon in 2024, and that some families have now been displaced a second time since that conflict [1].
