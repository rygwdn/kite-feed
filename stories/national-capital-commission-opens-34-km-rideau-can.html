National Capital Commission opens 3.4 km Rideau Canal skateway

Category: Canada

Ottawa’s Rideau Canal Skateway is set to open for the first time this winter at 8 a.m. on Dec. 31, giving residents and visitors a chance to skate on a limited section on New Year’s Eve [1][4][5][6][2]. The National Capital Commission said the opening will cover a 3.4-kilometre stretch of the canal, rather than the full route, as conditions allow [1][4][6]. For Canadians ringing in the new year in the capital, it’s a classic winter tradition returning—at least in part—right on time for holiday skating plans [1][4].
