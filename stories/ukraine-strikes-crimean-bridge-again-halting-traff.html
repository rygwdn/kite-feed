Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge again, halting traffic before summit

Category: World

Russian-installed Crimean officials said a new attack halted traffic on the Kerch Bridge overnight into Thursday, the third reported strike on the crossing this week. Russia’s Transport Ministry said rail services were suspended and no one was hurt; officials were assessing the damage [2][3][14][9]. The latest strike came two days after Ukrainian drone and missile attacks brought down a major road span, destroying a 227-metre section. Rail traffic had resumed on one track before the latest suspension [3][10][25][1]. The 19-kilometre bridge carries road and rail traffic between Russia and Crimea, which Moscow annexed in 2014, and has served as a supply route for Russian forces. The road damage has also disrupted shipping through the Kerch Strait, according to Deutsche Welle [11][12]. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the earlier operation took weeks and unfolded in seven stages. Kyiv has described strikes on Russian infrastructure as retaliation for attacks on Ukrainian infrastructure [25][12]. Officials were still assessing the bridge’s condition after the latest attack, and Ukrainian authorities had not commented on that strike by Thursday morning [25][7]. The attack occurred hours before Russian President Vladimir Putin, U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping were due to meet in Shenzhen [2][10].

Perspectives

Timeline

  • 2014: Russia annexed Crimea, setting the context for the bridge project [12].
  • 2018: The Kerch Bridge opened, linking Crimea with mainland Russia [10].
  • October 2022: A truck bomb damaged the bridge and caused months of disruption [13][12].
  • July 2023: Ukrainian naval drones struck the bridge, killing a visiting Russian family [13].
  • June 2025: Ukraine reported an underwater attack on the bridge’s supports [12].
  • 17 November 2026: Ukrainian strikes collapsed a major section of the bridge’s road span [3][10].
  • 19 November 2026: Russian authorities reported another strike and temporarily suspended traffic [2][14].
