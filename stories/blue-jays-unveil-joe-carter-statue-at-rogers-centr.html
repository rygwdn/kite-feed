Blue Jays unveil Joe Carter statue at Rogers Centre

Category: Canada

The Toronto Blue Jays unveiled a statue outside Rogers Centre on Saturday, July 18, honouring the franchise’s 1992 and 1993 World Series championship teams [1][2][7]. The installation, titled “Back-to-Back,” depicts Joe Carter mid-jump after his walk-off home run clinched the 1993 World Series and includes the names of players from both title teams and Tom Cheek’s historic radio call [1][2]. The statue stands at the southeast corner of the ballpark near Gate 5 [2][7]. The team installed it partly to mark the Blue Jays’ 50th season in Major League Baseball. It replaced a statue of former team owner Edward S. Rogers, which will move to the Rogers Communications campus in midtown Toronto [2][3]. The ceremony included video tributes, an on-field introduction and the outdoor unveiling [2][7][6]. Carter also threw the ceremonial first pitch to George Springer, and Vladimir Guerrero Jr. put the team’s home run jacket on Carter [8].

  • Ceremony program: Former manager Cito Gaston, who managed both championship teams, joined the alumni on the field [1].

Perspectives

Joe Carter: Carter called the statue a tremendous honour, said he was humbled to see it completed and noted that his family and former teammates attended the ceremony.

John Schneider: The current Blue Jays manager said he grew up a Yankees fan in New Jersey but rooted for Toronto’s 1993 team as a pre-teen because the roster, SkyDome and its playing surface appealed to him as a baseball fan.
