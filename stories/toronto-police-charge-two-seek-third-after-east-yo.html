Toronto police charge two, seek third after East York break-in

Category: Canada

Toronto police arrested two men and are searching for a third suspect after an attempted break-in at an East York home on Saturday evening. Police allege three people tried to enter the residence while the homeowner was inside. When the homeowner chased them, they fled in a vehicle that crashed into a nearby store. The homeowner was not injured [3][2]. Police identified the accused as Liban Mohammed, 19, and Dontae Kerr, 20, both of Hamilton. They face charges related to the attempted break-in and other alleged conduct; the allegations have not been proven in court. Investigators are asking anyone with information to contact Toronto police [3][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Charges filed: Mohammed was also charged with dangerous operation of a conveyance, while Kerr faces a charge of disguise with intent [3][2].
  • Suspect description: Police describe the outstanding suspect as 18 to 22 years old with a slim build. He was last seen wearing all black [3][2].
  • Police previously described the incident as targeted [2].
