Australia orders dingo cull after B.C. teen death

Category: Canada

Australian officials say they will humanely euthanize a pack of dingoes deemed a public safety risk after the animals were found circling the body of 19-year-old Piper James of Campbell River, B.C., on a beach on K’gari (Fraser Island) [6][4]. Authorities said rangers had monitored the pack following James’s death and cited the animals’ “aggressive behaviour” as the reason for the cull [6][1]. Preliminary autopsy findings reported that James was bitten by dingoes before and after her death; her family has said she went for an early-morning swim and likely drowned [3][6]. The decision has renewed attention among Canadians travelling abroad to wildlife-safety rules and the difficult balance between conservation and visitor protection [1][6].
