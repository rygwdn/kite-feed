Sequoia plans investment in Anthropic AI round

Category: Technology

Sequoia Capital is reportedly preparing to invest in Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude model, by joining the startup’s next funding round [1][2]. The Financial Times reports the round is being led by Singapore’s sovereign wealth fund GIC and Coatue, with each expected to put in $1.5 billion [3]. The move stands out because venture firms have traditionally avoided backing direct competitors in the same category, and this signals how intensely investors want exposure to leading AI builders [1].

Did you know: Sovereign wealth funds like GIC typically invest with long time horizons, which can make them influential backers in capital-heavy technology sectors [common].

  • Fundraising target: Anthropic is aiming to raise at least $25 billion in the round, according to sources cited by the Financial Times [3].
  • Round leadership: GIC and Coatue are described as the round’s leaders and are each expected to invest $1.5 billion [3].
  • Silicon Valley signal: TechCrunch notes Sequoia’s participation would break with the long-standing VC tendency to pick one “winner” in a sector rather than fund rival teams simultaneously [1].
  • Track record context: Silicon Republic points to Sequoia’s history of early bets on companies including Google, Apple, and Stripe as context for why its interest in Anthropic draws attention [2].
