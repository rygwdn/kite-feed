Samsung launches Galaxy Card with Barclays in U.S.

Category: Technology

Samsung Electronics announced the Samsung Galaxy Card, its first U.S. credit card, in partnership with Barclays U.S. Consumer Bank and Visa ahead of its July 22 Galaxy Unpacked event [1][2][3][4][8][7][16][19][23]. The card has no annual fee and works in Samsung Wallet, with Barclays issuing the card and Visa providing the payment network [1][8][17][21][22][23]. Samsung set rewards at 5% cash back on eligible purchases made directly with Samsung, 3% on purchases made through Samsung Wallet, 2% on select streaming services and 1% on other purchases [1][2][4][7][8][16][17][21][18][23]. New cardholders can earn $200 after spending $2,000 within the first 90 days, and Samsung said eligible customers could begin applying before applications open to the general public on July 22 [1][7][16][8][22][23].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Wallet controls: Users can apply, manage accounts, track spending and redeem rewards in Samsung Wallet. Barclays described it as a digital wallet-native credit card with no separate banking app [19][8][21].
  • Card design: Samsung plans a virtual card and a premium black metal physical card, with the physical version carrying Samsung branding [2][7][17][22][23].
  • Membership perk: Cardholders receive 20% off Samsung VIP Advantage, a membership tied to Samsung Care+, Samsung.com rewards, early access and other benefits [2][1][8][16].

Perspectives

Samsung Electronics: Chai Won-cheol, executive vice president and head of the Digital Wallet team at Samsung's mobile business, said the card would give users rewards on payments while expanding Samsung Wallet.

Sources: The Korea Times

Barclays: Barclays CEO Denny Nealon called the Samsung partnership an example of the bank's partner-first strategy and its work with global brands.

Sources: Yonhap News Agency

Samsung North America fintech unit: Sih Lee said the Barclays relationship is strategic and long term, and that Samsung does not yet have every future service lined up.

Sources: MyJoyOnline

Industry Impact

  • Co-branded cards: Barclays ranks ninth among U.S. card issuers with 20 million customers and co-branded programs, giving Samsung an established issuer for its first U.S. card [5].
  • Mobile payments: Reuters, cited by MyJoyOnline, said the launch places Samsung in direct competition with Apple Pay in the U.S. payments market [19].
  • Device ecosystems: Samsung is adding a financial product to an ecosystem that already spans phones, foldables, watches, TVs, appliances, smart-home products and digital services [10].

Historical Background

Apple introduced Apple Card in 2019 with Goldman Sachs and Mastercard, linking Apple's hardware, digital wallet and consumer credit offerings [8][23]. Samsung already offered a Samsung Card in South Korea before launching the Galaxy Card in the United States [1][3]. Goldman Sachs has been exiting its Apple Card partnership, with JPMorgan Chase taking over the issuer role in a transition cited by Apple-focused and Korean business coverage [8][22][23].

Business Angle

  • The 5% Samsung-direct category can support device sales, but it also reduces net economics unless it drives purchases that would not otherwise happen [1][8][common].
  • Samsung Wallet usage is the more durable target because recurring payment behavior can deepen customer lock-in across devices and services [19][common].
  • Barclays supplies the regulated credit-card infrastructure, limiting Samsung's direct lending exposure while letting Samsung test a financial-services product under its own brand [8][19].
  • No annual fee lowers adoption friction, but rewards-funded cards need high-quality spending volume to create long-term value [8][common].

Action Items

  • Check eligibility before applying: Eligible customers could apply early, while applications open to the general public July 22 online or at Samsung retail stores in the United States [1][4][22][23].
  • Use Samsung Wallet as your default payment method: Add the Galaxy Card to Samsung Wallet and set it as the default to earn 3% cash rewards on mobile purchases, complementing the 5% back on direct Samsung orders [1][8].
