Anthropic expands Claude access for vetted critical-infrastructure security testing

Category: AI

Anthropic on Oct. 6 expanded its Cyber Verification Program, giving vetted cybersecurity organizations tiered access to Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Mythos 5.1 and future models for defensive research and authorized testing [14][7][4]. The highest tier covers testing of systems such as power grids, flight systems, telecom networks, interbank transfers and government networks, with Anthropic reviewing applicants alongside the U.S. government [4][3]. The three tiers—Defense, Red Team and Specialized—consolidate Project Glasswing and the earlier verification program; Anthropic says its publicly available models will retain stricter cyber safeguards [4][1]. Anthropic’s benchmark found sharply different results on complex cyber tasks under different access settings, while its partner vulnerability counts include findings that were not necessarily patched [4].

Key Points

  • Access reviews: Anthropic expects to respond to Defense Access applications within days, while Red Team reviews can take weeks; applicants may receive Defense access while awaiting Red Team approval [4][1].
  • Open-source scanning: Anthropic said its own scanning identified another 5,500 vulnerabilities between April and October 2026; across both scanning efforts, more than 33,000 findings were rated high or critical [4][1].
  • Open-source funding: Anthropic announced a $35 million Defender Advantage Fund for work including patching live open-source vulnerabilities and developing automated scanning methods [1].

Perspectives

Anthropic: Existing Project Glasswing members move to Specialized Access without needing renewed approval for current models [4].

Sources: Cybersecurity News

Technical Details

  • Tiered safeguards: Defense Access blocked 46 of 50 complex tasks in Anthropic’s test, while Red Team Access completed 34 and recorded no blocks; these company-run results do not establish that safeguards cannot be bypassed [4].
  • Misuse monitoring: The program generally requires user data to be retained for misuse monitoring, with temporary exceptions for eligible customers that require zero data retention [4].
  • Controlled data retention: Anthropic plans Enterprise Frontier Safeguards that would allow eligible organizations to keep monitoring data in cloud infrastructure they control [4].

Industry Impact

  • Cybersecurity vendors: Wiz, Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike and Accenture had built cyber products on Claude Opus by May 2026, according to TechJuice [1].

Q&A

Q: Which organizations can apply for Defense Access?

A: Anthropic lists corporate security teams, universities, government bodies, hospitals, utilities, smaller security firms, open-source maintainers and individual researchers with a vulnerability-reporting record [4].

Q: How can organizations access the models?

A: The program is available through Claude Platform, Google Cloud Vertex AI and Microsoft Foundry; Amazon Bedrock access requires Enterprise Frontier Safeguards eligibility [4].
