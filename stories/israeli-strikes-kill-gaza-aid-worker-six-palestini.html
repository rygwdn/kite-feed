Israeli strikes kill Gaza aid worker, six Palestinians

Category: World

An Israeli missile strike hit a taxi in Gaza City’s Sabra neighbourhood on Tuesday evening, killing Palestinian aid worker Mohammed al-Wahidi and three others. The strike came shortly before the Egypt-Argentina World Cup match he had helped organise for public viewing in Gaza [2][3][6][12][20]. Hospital and rights sources identified the other dead as Ahmed Daghmush, also reported as Ahmed Jehad Rajab Doghmosh, and two brothers, Hamza al-Deri, 10, and Fari al-Deri, 8 [3][6][11][20]. Al-Wahidi worked with the Egyptian Relief Committee in Gaza, helping coordinate food assistance, displacement camps and community initiatives, including World Cup screenings that drew residents to watch matches among damaged buildings and rubble [2][3][6][10]. The Israeli military said it struck a Hamas operative travelling in a vehicle, that al-Wahidi was not the intended target, and that it was aware of claims that uninvolved civilians were harmed [2][6][11][20]. On Thursday, Gaza’s civil defence agency and health officials said Israeli attacks killed six people across the territory, including two in a drone strike on Nuseirat camp [4][7][15]. A Gaza security source said a separate Israeli drone strike targeted a vehicle linked to Hamas spokesman Hazem Qassem, who was not inside, while his bodyguard was killed [1][4][7][22][24].

Key Points

  • Ceasefire toll: The Palestinian Ministry of Health said deaths since the ceasefire began had risen to 1,094, with 3,507 injured and 799 bodies recovered [27].
  • Strike locations: Palestinian sources reported Thursday's deaths in Nuseirat, west of Gaza City, east of Deir al-Balah and Khan Yunis [27][25].
  • Additional strikes: The IDF said strikes this week killed an Islamic Jihad commander and a Hamas sniper during the ongoing ceasefire [1].

Historical Background

Hamas took control of Gaza in 2007 after fighting with the Palestinian Authority, which still administers parts of the Israeli-occupied West Bank [common][28]. Palestinian sources say the current ceasefire took effect on October 10, 2025, but Israeli strikes, demolitions and movement restrictions have continued inside Gaza [27][26].
