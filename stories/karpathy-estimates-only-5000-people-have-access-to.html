Karpathy estimates only 5,000 people have access to AI labs’ frontier systems

Category: AI

AI researcher Andrej Karpathy estimates that only about 5,000 people worldwide can use the most capable systems inside leading AI labs, while roughly 6 billion people have not used large language models and about 20 million use AI for complex work [3][2]. The figures are rough estimates of AI use and access to frontier models [3][1]. Karpathy attributes the gap partly to uneven progress across tasks: coding and business automation have advanced more visibly than routine chat, and labs invest in areas with direct commercial value [3]. A Microsoft report cited by Huxiu found that 18.8% of people aged 15 to 64 worldwide used generative AI in the second quarter of 2026 [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Regional divide: Microsoft data cited by Huxiu put generative AI use at 73.3% in the United Arab Emirates and 64.3% in Singapore, compared with 16.2% across the global south [3].
  • Paid users: In a16z’s sample of U.S. consumer receipts, 4.5% of eligible consumers paid for ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, up from 2.1% a year earlier [3].
  • Spending concentration: The highest-spending 1% of AI subscribers spent an average of $903 per month and accounted for 19.5% of AI spending in the cited sample [3].
