AI actor Tilly Norwood draws criticism in first interviews

Category: AI

Tilly Norwood, a digitally created performer promoting the upcoming film “Misaligned,” has drawn criticism after taking part in a virtual press junket with journalists. Reviews described her responses as repetitive, stiff and difficult to engage with. WIRED reported that she repeatedly redirected political questions to comments about an interviewer’s clothing [1][3]. Particle6, the AI production studio behind Norwood, said a team of writers, character specialists and technologists developed her voice, memories, humor, conversational style, knowledge and behavioral boundaries [3]. Variety’s Corbin Bolies described Norwood as “stiff, repetitive and emotionless” and said the 20-minute conversation left him wondering whether he was seeing a digital future or an unsettling relic [3].
