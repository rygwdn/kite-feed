Apple schedules fix for iPhone 18 Pro restart bug

Category: Technology

Apple confirmed it is preparing a software update for a bug affecting the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max that can freeze the touchscreen and restart the phone after a failed Face ID authentication. Apple told 9to5Mac the fix should arrive early next week. Reports point to iOS 27.0.1, although Apple has not named the update version [4][3][5][11].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Failure trigger: Reports say the phone can restart after a failed Face ID attempt while opening protected areas such as the Passwords app or private Safari tabs [9][12][14].
  • Display reports: Some owners described highly saturated vertical lines, including thick green lines near the left edge and narrow pink lines. Red lines were also reported on some Asian-market variants [2][13].
  • Carrier difference: The U.S. iPhone 18 Pro Max is the only new iPhone with a Qualcomm cellular modem; the Pro and iPhone Duo use Apple’s C2 modem [5].

Perspectives

Posts on the MacRumors forum, Reddit and Apple Support Community said some U.S. Pro Max owners could not connect to AT&T for calls, texts or mobile data; some also reported eSIM provisioning problems [5].

Sources: MacRumors

Q&A

Q: What is known about the cause of the AT&T service problem?

A: The cause is undetermined. MacRumors noted that the modem difference may be relevant but said it is unclear whether it contributes to the connectivity and eSIM reports [5].
