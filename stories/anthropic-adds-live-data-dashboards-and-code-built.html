Anthropic adds live data dashboards and code-built animations to Claude

Category: AI

Anthropic has added Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion, two beta tools that turn natural-language requests into live data views and editable animated explainers [3][2]. Dashboards can connect to business data platforms and refresh as source data changes. Motion creates animations with editable code rather than a video-generation model and does not generate footage or people [2][13]. Dashboards is available on paid plans, while Motion is limited to Team and Enterprise customers [2][13]. Docs, Slides and Design are now available on all plans, including Free [2]. Anthropic says the standalone Claude Design site will remain available until December 14. Users can migrate team design systems, but standalone chats, comments and public project links will not carry over [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Data connections: Dashboards supports Redshift, BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks and Snowflake, and can also use connectors such as Salesforce [2].
  • Inspectable charts: Users can see when each chart last refreshed and inspect the query behind a data point or ask Claude to explain it [2].
  • Team controls: Enterprise administrators can control external sharing, use customer-managed encryption keys and select templates. Dashboards and Motion are disabled by default for Enterprise customers [2].
  • Artifact scale: Anthropic says users have created more than 45 million documents, slide decks and designs in Claude [2].

Timeline

  • 16 September 2026: Claude Design began working inside conversations with context and connectors [2]
  • 8 October 2026: Anthropic announced beta Dashboards and Motion features for Claude [3][2]
  • 15 October 2026: Docs, Slides and Design are scheduled to be enabled by default [2]
  • 14 December 2026: The standalone Claude Design site is scheduled to close [2]

Industry Impact

  • Analytics software: Looker, monday.com and Tableau support is listed as forthcoming [2].
  • Creative software: Motion exports MP4 files, and support for Canva and Captions is planned [2].
