Manitoba, Ottawa and Indigenous leaders tour Pimicikamak damage

Category: Canada

Leaders from the federal government, Manitoba and national Indigenous organizations travelled Wednesday to Pimicikamak Cree Nation in northern Manitoba to assess damage after a days-long power outage crippled the community’s water system and led to frozen pipes [1][3][4][2]. The outage contributed to a “frozen water catastrophe” that buckled floors and ceilings in homes, caused sewage flooding, and forced thousands of residents to leave, with no clear timeline for return reported [1][4]. The visiting delegation included Manitoba Premier Wab Kinew, federal Northern Minister Rebecca Chartrand, and Assembly of First Nations National Chief Cindy Woodhouse Nepinak, alongside local leadership, as cleanup and repairs begin [1][3][2].
