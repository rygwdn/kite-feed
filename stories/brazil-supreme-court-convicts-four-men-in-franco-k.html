Brazil Supreme Court convicts four men in Franco killing

Category: World

Brazil’s Supreme Federal Court has convicted four men for the 2018 assassination of Rio de Janeiro city councilor Marielle Franco and her driver, Anderson Gomes [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]. Two brothers with political influence in Rio de Janeiro—former congressman João Francisco “Chiquinho” Brazão and Domingos Inácio Brazão—were found guilty of ordering the murders and each received a sentence of 76 years and three months in prison; several outlets described the decision as unanimous [1][4][5][6][7][8]. The court also convicted two former police officers, according to reporting [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Charges cited: France 24 said the court convicted the Brazão brothers of double aggravated homicide, participation in an armed criminal organization, and attempted murder [8].
  • Paramilitary links: El País described the brothers as political strongmen who defended the interests of paramilitary criminal groups in Rio de Janeiro [4].
  • Victims remembered: The Guardian noted Franco was a gay Black woman and rising political figure in Rio, and Gomes was 39 when he was killed [5].
  • Public response: The BBC reported Franco’s murder at age 38 sparked protests across Brazil and intensified calls to identify who ordered the killing [2].
