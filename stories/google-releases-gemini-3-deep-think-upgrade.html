Google releases Gemini 3 Deep Think upgrade

Category: AI

Google has released a major upgrade to Gemini 3 Deep Think, describing it as a specialized reasoning mode aimed at helping tackle challenges across science, research, and engineering [3]. Separate coverage of the launch—circulated via a Google News item—has tied the upgrade to ARC-AGI-related performance claims [1]. Early testers have also shared instruction-following probes that use complex SVG-generation prompts as a practical way to assess structured output, with examples spreading via community forums [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • External coverage framing: A Google News-linked GIGAZINE headline characterizes the upgrade as breaking an “incredible record” in an ARC-AGI intelligence measurement [1].
  • Official positioning: Google’s announcement presents the release as a “major upgrade” to its specialized reasoning mode, with the post framed around “advancing science, research and engineering” [3].
  • Developer reaction: Simon Willison described the model’s “pelican riding a bicycle” SVG as the best result he’s seen so far and pointed readers to his prior collection of similar experiments [2].
  • Escalated constraint test: After the basic pelican prompt, Willison tried a more demanding “California brown pelican” SVG request (spokes, correct frame shape, pouch, feathers, pedaling, breeding plumage) and shared the output, noting it was spotted via Hacker News [2].
