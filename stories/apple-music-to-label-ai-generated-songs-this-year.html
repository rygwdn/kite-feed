Apple Music to label AI-generated songs this year

Category: AI

Apple Music told music industry partners it will introduce visible “Made With AI” labels for songs and other content later this year when artificial intelligence was used to create a material portion of the work [3][2][6][10]. The company said content providers must include AI Transparency Tags in those cases, including for tracks primarily derived from a generative AI service [3][2][6][10][9]. The visible labels expand AI Transparency Tags that Apple announced in March for artwork, tracks, compositions or lyrics, and music videos [2][3][6][9][11]. Apple did not give a specific launch date, and several accounts said the company did not detail how it will enforce the requirement beyond relying on labels and distributors to disclose how content was made [3][2][1][5][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Content providers will be required to include AI Transparency Tags in any instance where AI was used to create a material portion of the content, including tracks that are AI platform generated

— Apple Music (The Hollywood Reporter)

Did you know: Apple has an internal system to detect AI-generated content, but it is currently relying on content providers to disclose how material was created [7].

Key Points

  • Upload scale: Apple Music VP Oliver Schusser said more than a third of monthly Apple Music uploads are 100% AI-generated, while those tracks account for less than 0.5% of listening [2][7][12][11].
  • Industry pressure: The RIAA, IFPI and other entertainment groups called for streaming services to label AI music more clearly, with some seeking disclosures similar to explicit-content labels [1][5][10].
  • Platform moves: Spotify announced its own AI-labeling initiative, while Deezer has excluded fully AI-generated tracks from recommendations and editorial playlists [5][6][10][12].

Perspectives

Apple Music: The company told creators its goal is to “provide listeners with as much transparency as possible” and directed them to its Music Style Guide for more information.

Sources: 9to5Mac

Music industry groups: RIAA- and IFPI-led groups urged streaming services to add clear AI labels, pushing platforms to identify synthetic music rather than leave disclosures hidden in metadata.

Sources: Digital Music News

Industry Impact

  • Music distributors: Labels and distributors will need to supply AI Transparency Tags when qualifying content is delivered to Apple Music [3][6][10].
  • AI music supply: Deezer said fully AI-generated music surpassed 50% of its daily new submissions, while an earlier Deezer study cited about 50,000 AI-generated songs uploaded to Spotify each day [6][1].

Historical Background

Apple introduced AI Transparency Tags in March 2026 as optional metadata disclosures for new artwork, tracks, compositions and music videos uploaded to Apple Music, leaving it to content providers to decide what qualified as AI-generated [2]. The new step changes those disclosures from back-end tagging into listener-facing labels and makes tags mandatory when AI generated a material portion of the content [3][6][9].

Action Items

  • Check new release metadata: Labels and distributors submitting to Apple Music should review whether AI materially generated the track, artwork, composition or video before delivery [2][11].
  • Watch for visible labels: Apple Music users can look for “Made With AI” labels when the feature rolls out later in 2026 [3][6].
