Apple leaker says development of camera AirPods suspended

Category: Technology

A leaker and prototype collector known as Kosutami said on X that development of Apple’s rumored camera-equipped AirPods Pro has been “suspended,” revising an earlier June post that described the product case as “concluded” [2][7][9][1]. Apple has not announced the device, and the reports do not establish whether the claim refers to a temporary pause or a longer-term halt [9][1]. The claim conflicts with earlier reporting cited by MacRumors and IntoMobile that Bloomberg had described the camera AirPods as nearing completion or in an advanced testing stage, with early mass production potentially close [2][7]. Separate findings in iOS 27 beta 2 point to an unreleased Apple product codenamed B790 that can process “two images from cameras on either side of [a] user’s head,” a description developers and Apple-focused sites linked to rumored AirPods with cameras rather than conventional photo or video capture [4][5][6][8]. The reported purpose of the cameras is to provide visual context to Siri and Apple Intelligence, allowing features such as object, landmark and text recognition through Visual Intelligence [2][4][6][7][8]. Several reports say Apple could delay the product while work continues on the upgraded AI version of Siri, which appears in the current iOS 27 beta and is expected to reach users when iOS 27 launches in September 2026 [2][7].

Technical Details

  • Code location: Gold found the prompt inside a MobileAsset directory path ending in system_prompt.json, with metadata describing camera inputs from both sides of the head [4][5].
  • Depth mapping: ITC.ua reported that Apple had received a patent covering cameras similar to Face ID’s dot projector for distance detection and 3D depth mapping, a possible technical basis for spatial awareness in earbuds [10].

Industry Impact

  • Wearables roadmap: Reports describe Apple as working on both camera-equipped AirPods and smart glasses, with Bloomberg’s latest reporting cited as placing both the “AirPods Ultra” and glasses on a late-2027 schedule [6][8].

Historical Background

Apple’s AirPods line began as wireless earbuds closely tied to the iPhone ecosystem, while later Pro models added features such as active noise cancellation and spatial audio [common]. The cited reports say Apple has been developing camera-equipped AirPods for about four years, with early plans to sell them in the first half of 2026 before the schedule slipped [2][7].

Business Angle

  • A paused or delayed launch would push out any near-term hardware revenue from a higher-end AirPods variant, leaving Apple dependent on existing wearables refresh cycles [7][1].
  • Reports cite unfinished AI features as execution risks that can delay mass production even after a device reaches advanced testing [2][7].
