U.S. clears Anthropic's Mythos 5 for more than 100 organizations

Category: Technology

The Trump administration allowed Anthropic to restore access to its Claude Mythos 5 artificial intelligence model for selected “trusted” U.S. organizations after a two-week suspension prompted by national security concerns [17][4][2][7][12]. More than 100 companies and institutions, including many Fortune 500 companies and government agencies, will now have access to Mythos 5, sources familiar with the directive said [4][7][12][21][22]. Anthropic disabled Mythos 5 and Fable 5 for all users after a June 12 export control order, following concerns that powerful AI systems could be misused by military or intelligence users in China, Russia or other countries of concern [4][18][42]. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick wrote that Anthropic had worked with the government to address risks tied to the models and that “appropriate safeguards” now permit access for certain trusted partners [3][7][21][27]. The permission does not restore access to Fable 5, a related model that had been available more broadly before the suspension [2][3][15][35]. Anthropic said it can redeploy Mythos 5, which it described as its strongest cybersecurity model, to U.S. organizations that operate and defend critical infrastructure, and said it will keep working with the government to expand Mythos 5 access and make Fable 5 available for general use again [8][12][21][31].

Did you know: Anthropic had confidentially filed for an initial public offering and was valued at more than $900 billion, Star Headline and CnYes reported [32][41].

Key Points

  • Export access: Under the new arrangement, approved companies deemed trusted and their non-U.S. employees, along with Anthropic’s own non-U.S. employees, no longer need export licenses to access Claude Mythos 5, while unapproved organizations remain restricted [12][41][42].
  • Security warnings: The June 12 directive followed warnings from Amazon and other companies that Anthropic’s models could be jailbroken for malicious purposes, and U.S. authorities were also reportedly concerned that a China-linked group had accessed Mythos 5 [6][7][11].
  • Rival limits: OpenAI said it was delaying a full public launch of GPT-5.6 at the U.S. government’s request and limiting access to a small group of vetted partners whose details it shared with authorities [4][18][34][41].
  • Negotiations continue: People familiar with the discussions told CNN and Semafor that talks were expected to continue into the weekend, including on possible restoration of Fable 5 access, though no timeline was set [3][7][27][32].

Perspectives

Anthropic: Anthropic said on X that Mythos 5 can return to organizations that operate and defend critical infrastructure. It said it is still trying to expand Mythos 5 access and restore Fable 5 for general use.

Timeline

  • 12 June 2026: Export control order led Anthropic to disable both models [4][6].
  • 26 June 2026: A Commerce letter cleared Mythos 5 for trusted partners [7][21].
  • 27 June 2026: Anthropic said restoration for approved organizations was underway [12][8].

Technical Details

  • Mythos vulnerability workflow: Dagens Industri, citing Anthropic, described a workflow in which Mythos reads source code in a computer isolated from the internet, forms vulnerability hypotheses, tests them, chains smaller vulnerabilities into stronger attacks and writes a bug report [39].
  • Fable 5 positioning: Fable 5 was described as a weaker or more locked-down version of Mythos that brought many Mythos-class capabilities to the public, but the new Commerce Department permission did not cover it [2][3][39].

Industry Impact

  • Critical infrastructure: Approved U.S. organizations that operate and defend critical infrastructure can regain Mythos 5 access, Anthropic said [8][12][27].
  • Frontier AI launches: OpenAI restricted GPT-5.6 to government-approved or vetted partners on the same day Anthropic received limited Mythos 5 clearance [4][34][41].
  • Customer selection: Critics said government vetting leaves organizations outside the approved list restricted while selected institutions receive access [4][34][41].

Historical Background

On June 12, the U.S. government issued an export control order that led Anthropic to shut off access to Mythos 5 and Fable 5 across its customer base [4][6][18]. The order barred access by foreign nationals, including some people in the United States and Anthropic’s own non-U.S. employees, according to reports on the directive [6][12][35]. Anthropic had previously opposed the export-control decision in a blog post and warned that no model provider could fully prevent jailbreaking, according to Star Headline [32].

Business Angle

  • The June 12 order forced Anthropic to disable two leading models for all users [4][6].
  • Selective access may benefit approved infrastructure and cybersecurity customers but can limit product availability for broader commercial users if Fable 5 remains blocked [2][15].
  • OpenAI’s restricted GPT-5.6 rollout suggests U.S. government review is affecting more than one leading AI developer, not just Anthropic [4][34].
