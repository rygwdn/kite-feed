Amazon refreshes Fire HD 10 with 4GB of RAM

Category: Technology

Amazon refreshed its 2023 Fire HD 10 tablet by adding a 4GB RAM configuration, up from the 3GB that shipped across earlier versions of the model [1][2][3][4][5][6][7]. The new configuration costs $154.99, about $15 more than the prior $139.99 version, and several reports said Amazon introduced it without a formal launch announcement [1][2][3][5][6][7]. The 4GB option is limited to the 32GB storage model with lock-screen ads, while buyers who choose 64GB storage, other colors or an ad-free purchase option are still directed to 3GB versions, according to the product coverage [2][3][5][7]. Amazon's previous Fire tablet release was the 2024 Fire HD 8 refresh [2][3][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Unchanged hardware: The tablet keeps a 10.1-inch Full HD display, 2GHz octa-core processor, claimed 13-hour battery life, microSD expansion and USB-C charging [1][3][7].
  • Charging change: Engadget reported that the refreshed model can charge in about four hours, compared with five hours for the earlier version [1].
  • Lineup gap: The cheaper Fire HD 8 reached 4GB of RAM on its 64GB model in 2024; the larger Fire HD 10 remained at 3GB until this update [2].
  • Performance limits: Android Authority said the unchanged chipset means buyers should not expect a large jump in gaming or processing power [6].

Perspectives

Android Authority: The site framed the extra RAM as useful for streaming, reading, browsing, video calls and light productivity, while warning that the same chipset limits performance gains.
