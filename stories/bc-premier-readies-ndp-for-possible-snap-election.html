B.C. premier readies NDP for possible snap election

Category: Canada

British Columbia Premier David Eby said Wednesday that the NDP and Elections BC have been told to prepare for a possible provincial election. Eby said the party is “election ready,” citing economic and political disruption he attributes to President Donald Trump’s tariffs. The province’s next scheduled election is Oct. 21, 2028 [1][4][5]. The government’s majority has been reduced to one seat following the death of MLA Joan Phillip, while nine members have left the B.C. Conservative caucus, criticizing leader Kerry-Lynne Findlay. Findlay is running in a Sept. 26 byelection for a legislative seat [1][5].
