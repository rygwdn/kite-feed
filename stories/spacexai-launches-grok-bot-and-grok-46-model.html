SpaceXAI launches Grok Bot and Grok 4.6 model

Category: Technology

SpaceXAI, formerly known as xAI, introduced Grok Bot this week as an early-beta AI agent platform for delegating workplace tasks to always-on bots that can run in the background [2][4][6][10]. The company says each bot can use apps and websites, retain context from prior work, exchange information with other bots and bring the user back in for judgment calls or approvals [2][4][25][13]. SpaceXAI also released Grok 4.6 on August 12 as a frontier model aimed at long-running agents, coding, knowledge work and interactive or visual projects [3][1][11][24]. The model is available through Cursor, Grok Build, the SpaceXAI API, OpenRouter, Vercel and Cloudflare, with standard API pricing starting at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens [1][11][24][26]. CNET said SpaceXAI and Cursor jointly developed Grok Bot, while Deccan Chronicle reported SpaceX reached a $60 billion agreement in June to acquire the AI coding startup [4][10]. SpaceXAI said it has used Grok Bot internally across engineering, growth and marketing teams, including sales-account research, overnight email drafting and finance receipt collection [10][13].

Key Points

  • Benchmark standing: Grok 4.6 scored 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, tying OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Max and trailing Anthropic's Fable 5 Max by one point [3][1][21][24].
  • Training changes: SpaceXAI attributed Grok 4.6's gains to a longer supplemental training run, stronger engineering data and expanded reinforcement learning for coding and knowledge-work tasks [1][11][26][27].
  • Limited beta: Computerworld Denmark reported Grok Bot beta access through SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra and Cursor Teams Premium, with enterprise customers able to join a waitlist [22].
  • Subscription pricing: Cult of Mac and ITavisen reported that Grok Bot beta access is included with SuperGrok Heavy at $300 per month, while enterprise plans are listed at $120 and $200 per user per month [6][23].

Perspectives

SpaceXAI: The company described Grok 4.6 as built for complex multi-step work, saying it can stay on task, work through unfamiliar codebases and increasingly test its own output before continuing.

Technical Details

  • Cloud workstations: Each Grok Bot runs on a dedicated cloud machine or isolated cloud environment, allowing tasks to continue without relying on the user's local device [6][25][22].
  • Context window: iPhone in Canada reported Grok 4.6 includes a 500,000-token context window for large codebases or technical document sets in a single prompt session [12].
  • Agent training: CNYES said SpaceXAI used reinforcement learning environments covering general programming, knowledge work, kernel optimization, web development and computer-aided design [27].

Industry Impact

  • Enterprise software: Deloitte estimates 75% of companies plan to incorporate AI agent systems into operations by 2028. CNET listed Grok Bot alongside agent products including OpenClaw, Claude Computer Use, Manus and OpenAI Operator [4].
  • AI model pricing: VentureBeat reported that Grok 4.6 tied GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index; Wccftech said it offers comparable performance to Anthropic's Fable 5 at roughly 80% lower API cost, while CNYES said real workload costs depend on token consumption, reasoning intensity, task duration and tool calls [3][18][27].
  • Agent ecosystem: SpaceXAI launched Grok Bot alongside Grok 4.6. Computerworld Denmark listed Anthropic's Claude Cowork and OpenAI's ChatGPT Work as other workplace-agent products [22].

Historical Background

Computerworld Denmark said SpaceXAI had entered the agent market before Grok 4.5, which Deccan Chronicle described as built with Cursor. Anthropic launched Claude Cowork in January and made it generally available in April, while OpenAI followed with ChatGPT Work in July [22].

Business Angle

  • Rapid release cadence: SpaceXAI shipped Grok 4.6 less than a month after Grok 4.5 [27].
  • Price-performance ratio: Wccftech noted Grok 4.6 offers comparable performance to Anthropic's Fable 5 at roughly 80% lower API cost [18].
  • Grok Bot asks users to grant access to accounts and apps, making security, permissioning and approval flows central to commercial adoption [6][28][25].

Action Items

  • Limit agent permissions during testing: Use separate accounts or scoped credentials where possible because Grok Bot is designed to sign into apps, inboxes and websites on the user's behalf [6][25].
  • Compare total task cost before switching models: Measure tokens, tool calls and completion rates on your own workloads because lower API list prices do not always translate into proportionally lower end-to-end costs [27].
