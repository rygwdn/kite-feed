Google adds AI image generation to redesigned Google Images

Category: Technology

Google marked the 25th anniversary of Google Images with a redesigned desktop homepage built around a personalized, browsable image gallery and AI image generation in Search AI Overviews [17][1][6][9]. The new Google Images homepage shows images from across the web before a user enters a query, updates in real time and tailors the feed to a signed-in user's interests [17][15][10]. The redesign will roll out over the coming weeks on desktop in the U.S. in English, and users must sign in to a Google Account to try it [17][1][6][9]. Google also said image generation in AI Overviews will roll out over the coming weeks in English in countries that support AI Mode image creation, using its Nano Banana model to turn text prompts into custom visuals [17][5][6][14].

Primary Source: View original article

Sometimes, the perfect image is out there on the web, waiting to be found. But other times you might have a highly specific vision where an image doesn’t yet exist.

— Brad Kellett (Google Blog)

Did you know: In 2015, former Google CEO Eric Schmidt wrote that Jennifer Lopez's 2000 Grammy dress had been the most popular search query Google had ever seen at the time [2].

Key Points

  • Collections tabs: Saved image collections will appear as tabs above the main gallery, letting users return to visual projects such as travel ideas, outfits or room designs [17][1][4].
  • Pinterest comparison: TechCrunch, PCMag and Android Police compared the redesign's discovery feed and saved collections with Pinterest-style visual browsing [1][14][2].

Perspectives

TechCrunch: TechCrunch wrote that Google is adopting Pinterest's discovery playbook and could keep users inside Google's ecosystem instead of sending them to third-party image-generation services such as ChatGPT.

Sources: TechCrunch

Engadget: Engadget said the interface does not look much different from the current design, despite Google's claims about real-time updates and interest-based personalization.

Sources: Engadget

Timeline

  • 2000: Lopez's Versace dress drove heavy demand on Google Search [17][8].
  • July 2001: Google launched Google Images for visual web search [17][9].
  • 2011: Google introduced Search by Image for reverse image search [17][22].
  • 14 July 2026: Google announced the anniversary redesign and AI image creation features [17][11].

Business Angle

  • The rollout initially applies to desktop users in the U.S. in English, rather than a global mobile launch [17][9].
