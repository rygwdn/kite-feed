Apple plans a four-stage iPhone camera upgrade roadmap

Category: Technology

Multiple Apple-focused outlets report that Apple is testing a four-part camera upgrade plan for future iPhones, with the first change expected to arrive on the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max in September 2026: a variable-aperture main rear camera [1][2][4][6][8][9][7][3]. The reports trace the claim to Weibo leaker Digital Chat Station, and note that analyst Ming-Chi Kuo also reported in December 2024 that Apple was preparing variable aperture for the iPhone 18 Pro line [1][4]. If Apple adopts it, this would be the first time variable aperture appears on an iPhone, replacing the fixed ƒ/1.78 setup used on recent Pro models [1][6]. The broader roadmap in these reports includes three more upgrades that Apple is said to be evaluating for later iPhones: a larger 1/1.12-inch-format main sensor, stronger optical image stabilization for the ultra-wide camera, and a 200-megapixel periscope telephoto lens [1][4][6][8][3]. For readers, the immediate significance is practical: variable aperture could help future iPhones better balance low-light capture, highlight control, and depth-of-field options. The other rumored changes also suggest Apple is still investing heavily in computational photography and camera hardware as a key selling point for premium phones [1][8][9].

Key Points

  • Launch window: Reports widely frame variable aperture as a September 2026 addition for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, while the timing of the other three upgrades remains unclear beyond being under evaluation for later models [1][4][8].
  • Competitive context: Several reports note that some Android rivals already use variable aperture. Android Headlines cites Samsung’s Galaxy S8 as an early example and Huawei’s recent premium phones as current implementations [8].

Perspectives

Digital Chat Station: As several reports summarize the post, the leaker presents these upgrades as part of an implementation plan rather than a one-off experiment, with variable aperture positioned as the first feature to ship on the iPhone 18 Pro line.

Technical Details

  • Variable aperture: Unlike a fixed ƒ/1.78 lens, a variable-aperture system can physically change how much light reaches the sensor. It can open wider in dim scenes and stop down in bright scenes to improve exposure control and potentially change natural depth of field [1][8][9].
  • 1/1.12-inch format: The rumored '1/1.12-inch' sensor designation is an optical-format convention, not a direct measurement, and larger sensors generally gather more light and improve image quality [1].
  • Periscope telephoto: A periscope telephoto uses a folded optical path inside the phone chassis to achieve longer zoom reach than a conventional straight lens stack. That is why a rumored 200-megapixel version would be a substantial hardware step if Apple adopts it [8].

Historical Background

Apple has steadily emphasized camera improvements in its Pro iPhones for years, but reports say the main camera has stayed fixed at ƒ/1.78 from the iPhone 14 Pro through the iPhone 17 Pro [1]. That makes variable aperture a notable possible shift in Apple’s hardware approach, bringing a feature that other smartphone makers have already explored to the iPhone lineup [8]. The current rumor cycle also builds on a December 2024 prediction from analyst Ming-Chi Kuo, suggesting the idea has been circulating through Apple supply-chain reporting for well over a year [1][4].
