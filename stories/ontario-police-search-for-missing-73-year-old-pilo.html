Ontario police search for missing 73-year-old pilot

Category: Canada

Durham Regional Police are searching for David, a 73-year-old Kawartha Lakes man who did not return after leaving Oshawa Airport in a small home-built aircraft Friday morning, Aug. 14 [1][3]. Police said he departed at 8:30 a.m. in a blue and yellow single-engine aircraft and was expected back at 9:30 a.m. [1]. Police said David is an experienced pilot. Durham officers are working with the Ontario Provincial Police, and police have asked the public for help locating him and the home-built plane [1][2].
