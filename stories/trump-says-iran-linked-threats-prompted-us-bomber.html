Trump says Iran-linked threats prompted US bomber withdrawal from UK

Category: World

President Donald Trump said Monday that the United States withdrew its B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford after threats against the British base that he linked to Iran. Trump said Washington knew who made the threats but did not identify them or describe the nature of the threats [3][7][6]. The Pentagon confirmed Sunday that all U.S. bombers deployed at Fairford had returned to their home stations in the United States. Trump tied the withdrawal to the threats, while Secretary of State Marco Rubio declined to directly connect the move to the suspected plot [8][24]. The comments followed a Sept. 27 security incident near the base. Five men arrested that day and a sixth British-Iranian man arrested later have been released on bail. Investigators found petrol in vans but no explosives. British officials have cited indications of Iranian involvement, which Tehran denies, and police say the investigation is continuing [6][8][25].
