Apple adds practical AI tools in iOS 27

Category: Technology

Apple is adding a set of practical AI features across iOS 27 that work inside existing iPhone apps rather than only through the planned Siri overhaul, including tools for payments, passwords, messages, calls, calendars, automation and information organization [5][6][7]. Apple presented the updates at WWDC 2026, and several features are already available in the developer beta [5][6]. One of the most detailed additions is Apple Cash bill splitting: users can photograph or upload a restaurant receipt, and Apple Intelligence extracts items, quantities, tip and total before letting participants choose what they ordered and settle through Messages or Wallet [5][7][9]. iOS 27 also identifies weak or compromised passwords and can replace them automatically, while Messages can suggest reminders, photos or calendar actions based on chat context [6][7].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Interface control: iOS 27 adds a Liquid Glass slider that lets users adjust the interface between a clearer glass effect and a more tinted appearance after earlier debate over readability [2].
  • Landscape expansion: Apple expanded landscape support to built-in apps including Music, Podcasts, Fitness, Health, Reminders, Home, Shortcuts, Find My, Weather, Voice Memos and Apple TV Remote [3].
  • Notes upgrades: The Notes app gains an 'Add to Notes' shortcut from Messages based on conversation context, and Siri AI can create generated notes after retrieving information from the internet [11].
  • System polish: Apple’s iOS 27 beta notes list faster AirPlay connections, more relevant Spotlight suggestions, faster PDF saving, improved Mail Top Results and enhanced Safari power efficiency [13][12].

Perspectives

Apple said the AI features are aimed at making daily tasks easier, safer and more automated for iPhone users.

Sources: ARY News

TechCrunch described Apple’s approach as focused less on chatbot-style interaction and more on making built-in software handle specific tasks with less manual effort.

Sources: TechCrunch

Digital Trends reviewer: The reviewer said the Liquid Glass slider gives users a way to balance visual depth against readability instead of forcing one design treatment on everyone.

Sources: Digital Trends

Tom's Guide reviewer: The reviewer framed the updated Shortcuts app as easier to use because Apple Intelligence can help build automations from simpler instructions.

Sources: Tom's Guide

Timeline

  • 8 June 2026: Apple released iOS 27 Beta 1 build 24A5355q [13].
  • June 2026: Apple presented iOS 27 features at WWDC 2026 [5].
  • July 2026: Apple plans to release a public beta [3][11].
  • September 2026: The full iOS 27 release is expected in September [3][11].

Technical Details

  • Receipt itemization: The bill-splitting flow can assign individual receipt items and quantities, including half portions when two people share an item, before applying tax and tip [7].
  • Markdown transfer: Notes can paste Markdown into formatted text and can also export selected formatted text through a new “Copy as Markdown” option [11].
  • Shortcuts branching: The iOS 27 beta notes list “Else if” support in Shortcuts, adding more granular conditional logic to user automations [13].

Historical Background

Landscape-oriented iPhone interfaces were more common years ago, and the Home Screen grid supported landscape orientation on the iPhone 6 Plus, iPhone 6s Plus, iPhone 7 Plus and iPhone 8 Plus before that function ended with the iPhone X [3]. iOS 26 already supported landscape mode in apps such as Apple Maps, Calendar, Files, Notes and Mail, while iOS 27 extends landscape support to more Apple apps [3].

Business Angle

  • Apple is adding small AI conveniences across default iPhone apps, including payments, passwords, calendar actions and automations [5][common].
  • Payments and password automation can affect money movement or account access if they fail [5][7][common].
  • Some Siri AI capabilities in Notes require an iPhone 15 Pro or newer [11].
