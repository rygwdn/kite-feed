Trump board ties Gaza pullout to Hamas disarmament

Category: World

President Trump's Board of Peace told Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Monday that Israeli forces would withdraw beyond Gaza's Yellow Line only after Hamas completes weapons decommissioning. Israel had objected to an earlier framework that called for a phased pullout and an immediate halt to Israeli strikes [3][7][8][10][16]. Nickolay Mladenov, the board's high representative for Gaza, met Netanyahu and his team in talks the board described as "constructive and detailed," and the board said it had reached a "common understanding of the shared objectives" with Israel [2][3][7][11]. The clarification followed President Trump's Friday statement calling Hamas's agreement to hand over weapons to a new Palestinian governing committee a "major milestone" in the next phase of the Gaza ceasefire process [7][10][13][20]. Two people familiar with the meeting said Mladenov also pressed Netanyahu to halt Israeli strikes in Gaza. Hamas said it remained committed to the second phase of the ceasefire agreement and was awaiting a clear official response from Mladenov and the mediators [3][6][17][7].

Key Points

  • Weapons scope: The board said decommissioning applies to light weapons, heavy weapons and tunnels, not just formal arsenals held by Hamas [8][15][31][54].
  • Cabinet pressure: Two members of Netanyahu's security cabinet urged a vote to scrap the Trump-backed deal after the board's earlier text called for Israel to start a phased withdrawal [7][10][13][14].
  • Ground control: Ynet reported that the board did not address Netanyahu's May claim that the Israeli military controlled about 70% of Gaza, while The National reported Israeli control had expanded to two-thirds of the territory [32][51].

Perspectives

Board of Peace: The board said the objective was "the complete decommissioning of weapons in the Strip and the transition away from rule by the gun to civilian governance." It said the process would be set out in the next phase.

Technical Details

  • Yellow Line: The Yellow Line marks the boundary between areas where Israeli forces remain based and areas still under Hamas control in Gaza [8][35].
  • Decommissioning verification: The proposed monitoring structure pairs an International Stabilization Force with an implementation verification committee involving the United States and the Board of Peace [49][44].

Humanitarian Impact

Israeli strikes killed 19 Palestinians from Sunday through Monday, according to a report citing the mediators' statement [48]. Mladenov said recent Israeli strikes had killed civilians and destroyed medical supplies that people depend on [36].

International Reactions
