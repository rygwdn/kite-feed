North Dakota teacher finds first adult T. rex trackway

Category: Science

Kent Hups, a Colorado high school science teacher and volunteer research associate, found two unusually large footprints in North Dakota's Hell Creek Formation in July. Researchers identified a sequence of four tracks as the first known trackway left by an adult Tyrannosaurus rex; the tracks date to about 66.5 million years ago [3][2]. The poorly preserved tracks suggest the animal was moving at about 1.6 meters per second, roughly a quick human walking pace, Science News reported [1].

Key Points

  • Site conditions: Hell Creek sediments commonly erode before footprints can be preserved, making track discoveries there uncommon compared with dinosaur bone finds [3].
  • Geographic context: The Hell Creek Formation spans parts of Montana, Wyoming, North Dakota and South Dakota [3].
