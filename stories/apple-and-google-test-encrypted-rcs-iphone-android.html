Apple and Google test encrypted RCS iPhone-Android texts

Category: Technology

Apple’s iOS 26.4 developer beta 2 broadens end-to-end encrypted RCS testing to include chats between iPhone and Android users, moving past the earlier beta that only enabled limited iPhone-to-iPhone RCS encryption when iMessage was turned off [1][14][18]. Apple says the feature is still in testing and will arrive in a future 26 release, not iOS 26.4 itself [14]. Alongside the RCS update, Apple shipped a second wave of developer betas across its platforms (including iOS 26.4, iPadOS 26.4, macOS Tahoe 26.4, watchOS 26.4, tvOS 26.4, and visionOS 26.4), continuing its usual cycle of small refinements and feature work ahead of a broader public release window this spring [2][15][16][17][10]. For everyday users, the practical takeaway is simple: if cross-platform RCS encryption lands in a later 26.x release, iPhone-to-Android texting could get meaningfully more private without switching apps—an interoperability win both ecosystems have been edging toward [14][19][20].

Primary Source: View original article

This feature is not shipping in this release and will be available to customers in future iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and watchOS 26 releases.

— Apple (developer release notes) (MacRumors)

Key Points

  • iOS UI tweak: In iOS 26.4 beta 2, the Home Screen “Edit” menu shifts to a more transparent Liquid Glass look [13].
  • Standards work: Apple says it worked with the GSM Association to implement end-to-end encryption for RCS, aiming to strengthen security in cross-platform messaging [14].
  • Carrier limits: In testing, encrypted RCS isn’t available on all devices or carriers, so some chats may fall back to non-encrypted behavior depending on your setup [13][14].
  • MacOS tweaks: macOS Tahoe 26.4 beta 2 adds a Charge Limit setting (80–100%) and brings back Safari’s Compact tab layout option, while also warning Apple silicon users about the planned Rosetta phase-out after macOS Tahoe 27 [15].
  • Other platform updates: watchOS 26.4 adds an Average Bedtime metric, and tvOS 26.4 adds a Continuous Audio Connection option for HDMI output [17].

Perspectives

Apple (developer documentation): Apple’s beta notes describe encrypted RCS threads as end-to-end encrypted when labeled as such, meaning messages can’t be read while being sent between devices.

Google (ecosystem requirement): To participate on Android, users need the latest version of Google Messages, aligning the rollout with Google’s RCS client updates.

Timeline

  • September 2024: Apple rolls out RCS support with iOS 18 [18].
  • February 16: Apple seeds first iOS 26.4 developer beta [2].
  • February 23: iOS 26.4 beta 2 expands RCS E2EE tests to Android [1][14].

Historical Background

Apple added RCS support to the iPhone with iOS 18 in September 2024, bringing richer features than SMS to cross-platform chats [18]. iMessage has long offered end-to-end encryption for iPhone-to-iPhone messages, while Android’s RCS has supported end-to-end encryption for Android-to-Android conversations in Google Messages. That left iPhone-Android threads as a notable gap—until this testing began [14][common].

Action Items

  • Try encrypted RCS in the beta (developers): Install iOS 26.4 developer beta 2 on iPhone and use the latest Google Messages on Android, then look for the encrypted indicator (such as a lock icon) in eligible RCS conversations [14][13].
  • Protect battery health on Mac (beta testers): On macOS Tahoe 26.4 beta, enable the new Charge Limit setting and choose an 80–100% cap based on your usage [15].
