Bill Gates warns AI cyberattacks could intensify over next year

Category: AI

Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates warned that AI-driven cyberattacks could intensify over the next year, identifying cyber threats as the area likely to see the greatest change from AI. In an ABC News interview on Sunday, he said the more immediate danger is malicious actors using existing AI systems, rather than AI escaping human control [8][1][9][13]. Gates said AI becoming uncontrollable with major consequences remains years away, while misuse is a present concern. He called for stronger restrictions on malicious uses and criticized the view that AI companies can ensure safety through self-regulation alone [1][2][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Employment concerns: In an August essay, Gates warned that the world was poorly prepared for AI-driven disruption, particularly its effects on jobs [14][9].
  • Safety controls: He warned that users can remove AI systems’ safeguards, leaving newer models accessible without those protections [1][10][2].
  • Bioterrorism risk: Gates also urged restrictions to prevent malicious actors from using AI for bioterrorism [9][1][12].

Perspectives

President Donald Trump: The reports describe Trump as viewing AI companies’ self-regulation as sufficient to ensure safety [9].
