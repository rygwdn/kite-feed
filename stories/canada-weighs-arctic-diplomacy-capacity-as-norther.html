Canada weighs Arctic diplomacy capacity as northern security shifts

Category: Canada

Canada plans to expand defence and economic activity in the Arctic, while regional experts question whether Global Affairs Canada has enough diplomats to build relationships across the region. Canada closed its Canadian International Arctic Centre in 2023, and the department forecast annual savings of $550,000. Inuit Tapiriit Kanatami President Natan Obed said the department lacks “huge bench strength” in Arctic diplomacy [1]. Foreign Affairs Minister Anita Anand said threats to Canada’s North have shifted significantly, with the Russian threat moving farther north toward the Arctic Circle [3].
