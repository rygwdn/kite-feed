B.C. emergency plane landing kills Prince George driver

Category: Canada

A Beechcraft B200 Super King Air aircraft made an emergency landing on Foothills Boulevard in Prince George, B.C., on Aug. 20 after losing power in one engine and collided with vehicles on the road, killing one person in a vehicle [1][2][3][4][6][8][9][13]. The aircraft had been headed for Prince George Airport [1][2]. Investigators said the plane was forced down on Foothills Boulevard, where it collided with vehicles; Global News and The Globe and Mail reported four vehicles were hit, while earlier RCMP accounts cited by the Vancouver Sun, the Toronto Star, Canada’s National Observer and AFP said two vehicles were involved [1][3][2][6][8][13]. The Transportation Safety Board of Canada has taken over the investigation, and airport officials said they put emergency crews on standby after the pilot warned Prince George Airport that the aircraft needed to make an emergency landing [1][2][9].

Primary Source: View original article

All of a sudden it was on top of me

— Jaime Pawliuk (CBC News)

Key Points

  • Survivor account: Driver Jaime Pawliuk said the aircraft passed over his SUV before a wing sheared off the vehicle’s roof, leaving him with superficial cuts and glass in his hair [9][5].
  • Aircraft operator: The Vancouver Sun, citing the Prince George Citizen, identified the plane as a Beechcraft B200 Super King Air operated by Sekani Air [2].
  • Airport response: Prince George Airport activated its emergency command centre and later stood down crews after the landing occurred on Foothills Boulevard, according to spokesperson Chrissy Blok [2].
  • Scene debris: Canada’s National Observer reported that debris, including a propeller, lay scattered after the aircraft came down on the road [8].

Perspectives

Prince George RCMP: Police said two crew members and six passengers were aboard the aircraft when it departed the Sturdee Airstrip.

Sources: Global News

Prince George Airport: Chrissy Blok said airport crews were on standby after the pilot reported the emergency, adding, “We just wish that the plane would have made it to YXS.”

Sources: Vancouver Sun

Jaime Pawliuk: Pawliuk shared photos of his destroyed SUV and said he was able to walk away despite glass injuries and a metal rod piercing the passenger seat area.

Sources: CBC News

Humanitarian Impact

CBC reported that multiple people were taken to hospital after the emergency landing, in addition to the one person killed in a vehicle on the road [9][1][4].

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: The crash occurred on Foothills Boulevard about 1.5 kilometres north of North Nechako Road, according to the Vancouver Sun’s account of RCMP information [2]. Check local traffic and police advisories before driving through the area.
