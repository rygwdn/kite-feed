Earthshot Prize names five climate innovators in Mumbai, awards £1 million each

Category: Science

Prince William, Prince of Wales, announced five winners of the 2026 Earthshot Prize in Mumbai on Nov. 17, awarding each £1 million ($1.3 million) to expand environmental projects. The recipients were Indian electric-vehicle maker Mahindra Last Mile Mobility, campaign group PFAS-Free France, Niue Ocean Wide Trust, offshore wind company Ørsted and Bhutan, which was recognized for forest conservation [5][6]. They were selected from 15 finalists working on clean transport, pollution, marine protection, renewable energy and conservation [5]. The awards coincided with the United Nations COP31 climate talks, where countries have struggled to agree on fossil fuels and climate finance. A U.N. report released in October said the world was on course to exceed the 1.5-degree Celsius warming threshold [4][9]. William called for urgent action, saying, “They will not judge us by the promises we made, but by the world we leave behind” [4].

Key Points

  • Student projects: A separate children’s challenge reached nearly one million students in 15,000 schools across 24 Indian states; five school teams received awards [8].
  • Air filter: A student-built, electricity-free filter using jute, biochar, bamboo silica, zeolite and cotton lowered reported PM2.5 readings from 98 to 12–30 in tests [8].
  • Forest protection: Bhutan’s conservation efforts were recognized for safeguarding water security for 1.6 billion people in South Asia; the country plans restoration across 50,000 hectares [6].
  • Ocean area: Niue Ocean Wide Trust’s conservation initiative covers more than 327,000 square kilometres of ocean, according to Sky News [10].

Perspectives

Prince William, Prince of Wales: He said he believed people were rising to meet the climate challenge and that positive change could become “irreversible” this decade [4].

Sources: Reuters

Jason Knauf, chief executive of the Earthshot Prize: He said children’s winning projects showed that young people are already taking innovative environmental action rather than waiting for the future [8].

Princess Catherine, Princess of Wales: She warned that species are being lost as habitats disappear and landscapes change, and said innovators are finding ways to protect and restore the natural world [10].

Timeline

  • December 2019: William introduced the Earthshot idea after two years of development [12].
  • 2020: The Royal Foundation established the environmental prize [5][12].
  • 17 November 2026: Five winners were named at the Mumbai awards ceremony [5][6].
  • 30 November 2026: The awards ceremony is scheduled to stream on Prime Video [12].

Historical Background

Inspired by former U.S. President John F. Kennedy’s Moonshot challenge, the Earthshot Prize aims to help repair the planet by 2030 [12][6].

Q&A

Q: What is the children’s Earthshot challenge?

A: It invited students aged 9 to 17 to identify environmental problems and develop solutions across the prize’s five areas [8].

Q: What support do winners receive beyond the prize money?

A: The prize connects winners with partners, investors and experts who can help expand their projects [5][12].
