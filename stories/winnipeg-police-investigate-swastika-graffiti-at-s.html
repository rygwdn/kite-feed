Winnipeg police investigate swastika graffiti at synagogue

Category: Canada

Winnipeg police are investigating after swastikas and other antisemitic graffiti were spray-painted on a Jewish synagogue as the Sabbath was about to begin [1]. Members of the congregation still gathered for Sabbath services afterward, describing it as a moment of solidarity and refusing to be intimidated [3]. The incident has renewed local attention on community safety and the importance of reporting hate-motivated vandalism so it can be properly investigated [1][3].
