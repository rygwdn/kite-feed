Calgary probes northwest water main break flooding Highway 1

Category: Canada

A large water main break in northwest Calgary on Tuesday night caused significant flooding near the Sarcee Trail and 16th Avenue N.W. interchange, with water reaching the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1) and stranding vehicles [2][5][4]. Calgary firefighters rescued people from cars caught in fast-rising water, and the city said thousands of customers experienced service impacts while crews assessed damage and water pressure [2][1][5]. City officials said it was too early to confirm the cause, though the city indicated the location and initial assessment suggest a link to the Bearspaw South feeder main, which is part of Calgary’s drinking-water backbone [1].

  • Recent precedent: The break occurred just west of where a similar rupture happened in June 2024, raising questions about repeat vulnerability along the corridor [2].
