Apple explains watchOS 27 cutoff for five watches

Category: Technology

Apple said watchOS 27 will not support five Apple Watch models: Series 6, Series 7, Series 8, the second-generation SE and the original Apple Watch Ultra [1][4][3][8][9][13]. Those devices will remain on watchOS 26 with basic security updates, while the full watchOS 27 update will run on Apple Watch Series 9, Series 10, Series 11, Ultra 2, Ultra 3 and SE 3 [3][4][8][11]. Apple Watch and Health product marketing manager Cait Dooley said Apple made “power and performance” the priority because watchOS 27 features including Siri AI and a new tap gesture work best with the processing power in newer watches [1][3][4][5][13]. Apple senior watchOS software engineering director David Clark said one goal was to “expand the intelligence story on Apple Watch” and make it a “true co-partner to Apple Intelligence,” with Siri interactions designed to feel consistent across the watch and iPhone [14][11]. Apple said older watches can still pair with iPhones running the latest iOS and will continue to receive security updates [1][4][8][13]. Digital Trends said the decision appears to place the Siri AI cutoff at the S9 chip generation, while iPhone-Ticker said Apple’s explanation points to the move from S8-era chips to newer S9 and S10 processors as the technical basis for the AI features [8][10].

Primary Source: View original article

With every software release across every single one of our platforms, we always want to ensure that you have the best experience, so we make power and performance a priority.

— Cait Dooley (MacRumors)

Did you know: The first Apple Watch Ultra was introduced in 2022, making it one of the youngest models excluded from the full watchOS 27 upgrade path [9][10].

Key Points

  • Support break: MacRumors, Cult of Mac and Macerkopf said Apple is removing three model years from full Apple Watch software support in one update, a shift they described as unprecedented or unusual for the line [1][7][11].
  • New features: Cult of Mac said watchOS 27 brings a dedicated Siri AI app, a redesigned dynamic app grid and deeper Apple Intelligence integration across devices [7].
  • Wrist role: German Apple-focused sites reported that Siri AI is being designed around the watch’s constant wrist presence, microphone access and voice-control role for quick requests [10][12][11].

Perspectives

9to5Mac reader comment: A highlighted reader comment questioned why Apple could not keep older watches on watchOS 27 without Siri AI, comparing the situation with iPhones that lack Siri AI but still receive other iOS 27 benefits.

Sources: 9to5Mac

Timeline

  • 2022: Apple introduced the first Apple Watch Ultra [9]
  • June 2026: Apple announced watchOS 27 at WWDC [3][13]
  • 19 June 2026: Apple explained the compatibility cutoff in a TechRadar interview [1][4]
  • July 2026: watchOS 27 public beta expected next month [3]
  • September 2026: Cult of Mac says watchOS 27 arrives this fall [7]

Technical Details

  • Paired iPhone compute: iPhone in Canada reported that Siri AI on Apple Watch also requires a nearby paired iPhone compatible with Apple Intelligence to handle the heaviest computational work [14].

Business Angle

  • Apple said its cutoff is based on power and performance, leaving Series 6, 7, 8, SE 2 and the original Ultra without full watchOS 27 eligibility [1][5].
  • Owners who want Siri AI or the new tap gesture will need Series 9 or newer, Ultra 2 or newer, or SE 3; excluded models remain on watchOS 26 security updates [8][common].

Action Items

  • Verify your Apple Watch model against the official watchOS 27 compatibility list to see if you'll receive the full update [4].
  • If your watch is not eligible for watchOS 27, ensure automatic security updates are enabled to continue receiving protection while staying paired with a supported iPhone [1].
