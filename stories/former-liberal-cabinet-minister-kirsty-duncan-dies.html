Former Liberal cabinet minister Kirsty Duncan dies at 59

Category: Canada

Former Liberal cabinet minister and longtime Toronto-area MP Kirsty Duncan has died at age 59 after a cancer battle she publicly discussed following a 2023 diagnosis [4][3]. Duncan represented Etobicoke North for multiple terms and served in federal cabinet between 2015 and 2019, including responsibilities tied to sport and science [3]. Political leaders across party lines issued tributes, noting her public service and community focus [1][4].
