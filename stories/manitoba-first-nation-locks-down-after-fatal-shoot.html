Manitoba First Nation locks down after fatal shooting

Category: Canada

Pimicikamak Cree Nation in northern Manitoba remained under lockdown Saturday after a fatal shooting left one person dead and prompted an RCMP major crimes investigation [1][2]. Chief David Monias said in a social media update that a shooter was still at large [2].

Primary Source: View original article

The shooter is still on the loose. We're under lockdown.

— Chief David Monias (CBC News)

Key Points

  • Police search: Local RCMP officers joined Major Crime Services in the search after the shooting [1][2].
  • Community distance: The First Nation sits about 500 kilometres north of Winnipeg by air and 720 kilometres by road [1].

Perspectives

Chief David Monias: Monias posted a video update around 10:30 a.m. Saturday telling residents the shooter remained at large and the community was under lockdown.

Sources: CBC News

RCMP: RCMP said its major crimes unit and local officers were still searching for a suspect who may still be in the community.

Sources: CBC News

Pimicikamak Cree Nation leadership: Community leadership cancelled Treaty and Indian Days celebrations until further notice and told residents to stay inside, secure doors and windows, keep children indoors and avoid opening doors to unknown people.

Sources: Global News
