Rockstar says GTA VI generated record $3.79 billion on launch day

Category: Technology

Rockstar Games said Grand Theft Auto VI generated an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on its first day, Nov. 19, calling it the biggest entertainment launch in history. The new entry in the crime-game series sold 32.5 million copies before release and is available only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S [10][21][2]. The $3.79 billion represents estimated customer spending at retail, not the amount Rockstar or parent company Take-Two Interactive received after retailers and platform operators took their shares [4]. The figure is nearly five times the roughly $800 million estimated for predecessor Grand Theft Auto V’s first 24 hours in 2013; GTA V later sold nearly 235 million copies [3][12]. Neither a PC edition nor the online multiplayer component has a confirmed release date. Analysts at Wells Fargo forecast sales of 65 million copies by Take-Two’s fiscal year-end in March 2027, while TD Cowen analyst Doug Creutz said the scale and timing of future recurring spending in GTA Online matter more to the company’s stock than unit sales alone [12][6].

We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you

— Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games (Associated Press, published by Channel 3000)

Key Points

  • Review divide: Metacritic showed a 99/100 average from about 150 professional reviews but a 5.5/10 user score from nearly 45,000 ratings, with some players criticizing bugs, originality and the lack of a 60-frame-per-second mode [12].
  • Player engagement: Circana data cited by TechSpot found 61% of active U.S. PlayStation 5 owners and 56% of active Xbox owners worldwide played the game on launch day [5].
  • Audience beyond consoles: Stream Charts counted more than 132 million hours of GTA VI viewing across Twitch, YouTube and Kick on launch day [4].
  • Launch-day absences also rose: BrightHR reported a 56% increase in UK sickness absence versus the previous Thursday, while provisional data suggested about 35,000 more secondary school pupils missed school [5].

Perspectives

Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick: Earlier this month, Zelnick said the company was not ready to declare success before the launch because it avoids claiming success before it occurs [6].

Sources: The Indian Express

U.S. police departments: Several departments warned players not to imitate the game’s car thefts, reckless driving and violence; Atlanta police wrote, “GTA is not a real place, but Atlanta is!” [21].

Sources: The Guardian

Timeline

  • 2013: Grand Theft Auto V recorded about $800 million in first-day sales [3]
  • 2022: A hack exposed stolen gameplay footage from the game [22][9]
  • 2025: Rockstar moved the planned release from fall 2025 [2]
  • 26 May 2026: The announced May release date was later postponed [2]
  • 19 November 2026: Grand Theft Auto VI launched on PlayStation and Xbox consoles [10][21]

Technical Details

  • Independent measurement: Sensor Tower estimated roughly 24.6 million units and $2.36 billion in revenue, while Rockstar reported 32.5 million pre-release units and $3.79 billion in launch-day retail sales. The figures use different methodologies and time windows [10][4].

Industry Impact

  • Telecoms: Preloads pushed Virgin Media O2’s evening traffic 9.4% above its previous record, while GTA VI accounted for as much as 24% of Comcast traffic at peak; BT and EE also reported new home-broadband peaks [1][5].

Historical Background

GTA VI was the first new mainline entry in the series in 13 years. Rockstar spent more than eight years developing it, with its production budget estimated at $1 billion to $1.5 billion [10][12].

Business Angle

  • An extended promotional trailer released in August helped drive a sixfold increase in pre-orders, according to Sensor Tower estimates [6].
