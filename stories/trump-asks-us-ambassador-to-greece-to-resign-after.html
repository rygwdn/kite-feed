Trump asks U.S. ambassador to Greece to resign after misconduct reports

Category: World

President Donald Trump’s administration asked U.S. Ambassador to Greece Kimberly Guilfoyle to resign on Tuesday, Oct. 13, and she announced her departure less than two hours later [4][10][11]. Trump said he disliked what he had read about her conduct; the White House provided no further details when it announced the request [18][9][11]. Trump donor Eric Deters alleged that Guilfoyle asked him for $100,000 to pay an American Express bill in exchange for access to senior administration officials. Deters said he did not pay. Guilfoyle’s lawyers dispute the authenticity and characterization of the messages, saying the money was owed for speaking and consulting work; she has denied wrongdoing [20][11][10]. Reports also alleged that she accepted luxury travel and hospitality from Greek business figures and threatened a Greek official; Greece’s energy minister denied that she threatened the country [3][13]. The State Department inspector general is investigating, NBC reported, citing people familiar with the matter, while Democratic lawmakers have called for further inquiries [20][3]. Guilfoyle says she will defend her reputation, and Deters has said he plans legal action against her [11][3].
