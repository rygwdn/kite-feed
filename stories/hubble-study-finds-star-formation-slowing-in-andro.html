Hubble study finds star formation slowing in Andromeda

Category: Science

NASA said a new study using Hubble Space Telescope data found star formation in the Andromeda galaxy has declined for 500 million years, with a sharper drop in the last 40 million years [2][3]. Andromeda is a spiral galaxy similar in size to the Milky Way and lies about 2.5 million light-years from Earth, close enough for astronomers to examine its stellar populations in detail [2][3]. NASA said that proximity makes Andromeda useful for studying the past of galaxies similar to our own [2][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Visible neighbor: Andromeda is visible to the unaided eye from areas with dark skies [2][3].
  • Research release: NASA’s Hubble Mission Team at Goddard Space Flight Center released the study summary July 27, 2026 [2].
  • Study focus: NASA said the work examined Andromeda’s stellar populations rather than directly observing new stars forming in real time [2].
