Tesla launches driverless Robotaxi service in Miami

Category: Technology

Tesla has started offering its Robotaxi service in Miami, extending the autonomous ride-hailing program to a limited service area in the western and central parts of the city, according to posts cited by PCMag, Mashable and Blognone [1][3][5]. PCMag said Tesla’s map excludes downtown Miami, Miami Beach and the area directly around the airport [1]. The Miami launch follows earlier service in Austin, Houston and Dallas, with PCMag describing Miami as Tesla’s first fully autonomous rollout outside Texas while noting that limited rides are running in San Francisco [1][3][5]. Techmeme, citing The Information, said the Miami service operates without a safety monitor and that Tesla aims to expand Robotaxi to a dozen U.S. states by the end of 2026 [2]. Tesla enters a Miami market where Waymo has operated since January 2026 and Amazon’s Zoox moved in during April, PCMag reported [1]. Tesla has also identified Orlando, Tampa, Las Vegas and Phoenix as planned 2026 expansion cities, a narrower footprint than CEO Elon Musk’s earlier estimate that the service would “probably” cover 50% of the U.S. population by the end of 2025 [1][5].
