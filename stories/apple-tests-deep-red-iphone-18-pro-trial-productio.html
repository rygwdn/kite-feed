Apple tests deep red iPhone 18 Pro, trial production begins

Category: Technology

Apple is testing a new “deep red” finish for next-generation iPhone 18 Pro models, according to multiple outlets citing Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman [1][3][4][6][7]. For now, it’s just a test—there’s no guarantee the color makes it to launch [1][3][7]. Separately, supply-chain and leaker reports say the iPhone 18 Pro has moved into small-batch test (or trial) production, part of Apple’s typical manufacturing validation work ahead of an expected September launch window [2][5][9]. The same coverage suggests the outside may stay close to the iPhone 17 lineup, with the bigger changes aimed at internals—like a new main camera system that could add a variable aperture—while Gurman also says Apple’s first foldable iPhone is likely to stick to simpler color options such as black and white [9][8][3][7]. If this all pans out, the upside is simple: more choice. A deep red Pro could add a premium-looking finish, while camera-focused upgrades may make everyday photos—especially in tricky lighting—more adaptable, without forcing everyone to adjust to a dramatically new exterior design [1][8][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Release cadence/validation: MacRumors says the Pro models entering late-stage production testing fits Apple’s usual path toward a September launch. The same leak also mentions checks beginning for the regular iPhone 18—even though that model is widely expected later (potentially early next year)—which would likely put it in an earlier validation stage than the Pro [9][1].
  • Foldable colors (more specific): Gurman’s reporting, as relayed by Mashable and Android Headlines, says Apple’s first foldable iPhone is expected to launch in conservative finishes—described as dark gray/black and white/silver—rather than the bolder experimentation rumored for the Pro line [3][7].
  • China preference: Mashable says the iPhone 17 Pro’s orange finish has been especially popular in China, which it cites as a reason Apple might keep orange alongside any new red option [3].
  • Camera rumor trail: MacRumors says analyst Ming-Chi Kuo first flagged a variable-aperture main camera for iPhone 18 Pro models in December 2024. Later reporting (Oct. 2025) claimed Apple was still moving ahead and discussing components with suppliers [8].
  • Materials rumor: A Weibo leaker cited by MacRumors says there are no major materials changes, and that the iPhone 18 Pro continues the design specifications from the iPhone 17 lineup—suggesting any outward tweaks could be limited [9].

Perspectives

Mark Gurman (Bloomberg), via other outlets: Apple is testing a “deep red” color for iPhone 18 Pro models, while the first foldable iPhone is expected to stick to simpler black-and-white finishes.

Sources: Mashable

MacRumors (rumor roundup): Reports have linked a variable-aperture main camera for iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max to a long trail of sourcing—starting with Ming-Chi Kuo in late 2024, then followed by claims Apple was still pursuing components with suppliers into 2025.

Sources: MacRumors

Fixed Focus Digital (Weibo leaker), as cited: The leaker says the iPhone 18 Pro models have entered “mass-production testing” (which MacRumors frames as late-stage manufacturing validation). They also claim production testing has begun for the regular iPhone 18—despite expectations that the non-Pro model may launch later—suggesting it would be in an earlier validation stage.

Sources: MacRumors

Instant Digital (Weibo), as cited: The iPhone 18 Pro series has entered production testing, and production lines for the Pro line have started up alongside iPhone 18 checks.

Sources: AppleInsider

Historical Background

Samsung introduced a variable-aperture main camera on the Galaxy S9 and S10 in 2018 and 2019, then later dropped the feature from subsequent flagships [8]. Separately, Apple has released multiple red iPhones in the past (including PRODUCT(RED) editions), but Gurman’s reporting says it’s been a few years since the last red iPhone [3].
