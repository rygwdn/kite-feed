Canadian Armed Forces deploy seven to help Pimicikamak

Category: Canada

Pimicikamak Cree Nation, about 500 kilometres north of Winnipeg, is dealing with severe water problems that include flooding, backed-up sewage and frozen pipes after a power outage [4][2]. Chief David Monias says seven Canadian Armed Forces members are expected to arrive Monday to provide technical help, assessments and support tied to critical infrastructure and recovery operations [4][5]. The immediate aim is to stabilize essential services and support local crews as the emergency continues [2][3].
