Anthropic offers Blackstone more than 10% yield for $60 billion chip financing

Category: AI

AI lab Anthropic is offering Blackstone a yield of more than 10% as part of a $60 billion debt package to buy Google tensor processing units, or TPUs, the Financial Times reported [3][2][10]. The proposal links the chip financing to Anthropic’s lease payments over five years [9]. Blackstone funds would invest $9 billion in the debt, according to the Financial Times report [3][9]. High borrowing costs complicate Anthropic’s goal of achieving investment-grade status, Techmeme said in its account of the report [10].
