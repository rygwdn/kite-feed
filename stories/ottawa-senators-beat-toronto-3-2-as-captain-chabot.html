Ottawa Senators beat Toronto 3-2 as captain Chabot leads Ottawa in opener

Category: Canada

The Ottawa Senators opened their 2026-27 season with a 3-2 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs on Saturday in Toronto. New captain Thomas Chabot scored a goal and assisted on another [9][2]. Stephen Halliday scored the third-period winner, Michael Amadio also scored and Linus Ullmark stopped 27 shots [9][6]. Ottawa was the final NHL team to play its season opener [9]. Ottawa defenceman Jake Sanderson left after the first period with a lower-body injury he had already been managing. Coach Travis Green said the team would know more after further examination [2][6]. The Senators were already without defenceman Artem Zub and played the final 47 minutes with five defencemen [2]. Toronto coach Jim Hiller said two third-period penalties hurt the Leafs’ momentum; Green said Ottawa wanted to stay aggressive while protecting its lead [9][11].

Key Points

  • Shot swing: Toronto outshot Ottawa 14-4 in the second period, but Ottawa responded with a 16-5 advantage in the third [9].
  • Toronto penalties: Toronto’s Kirill Marchenko took a high-sticking penalty and Jack Roslovic was called for tripping in the third; the Senators scored the winner just after a Leafs penalty kill [11][9].
  • Leafs milestones: John Tavares played his 600th game with Toronto, while William Nylander recorded his 400th assist with the Maple Leafs [9].
  • Captaincy change: Chabot began his captaincy after Brady Tkachuk was traded to Florida in June following a trade request [6].

Perspectives

Ottawa coach Travis Green: After a week-long wait, Green said Toronto was probably his preferred place to open the season outside Ottawa, citing the added emotion of playing the Leafs at Scotiabank Arena [6].
