B.C. wildfire remains active as Summerland residents return

Category: Canada

A staged re-entry is underway around Summerland, B.C., after the Bald Range wildfire expanded on the night of Aug. 7 and forced evacuations [4][3]. More than 4,500 properties near Summerland have been removed from the evacuation list, but they remain under evacuation alert while the fire continues to burn away from the community [1][6]. B.C.’s emergency management minister said 150 structures have been lost in the fire, while local officials said the total includes about 110 structures in Faulder and Meadow Valley and 40 in the District of Summerland [2][4][3]. Provincial Health Officer Dr. Bonnie Henry warned returning residents about wildfire smoke and about homes and neighbourhoods affected by ash and power-related food spoilage [2][4][3].

Key Points

  • Rural damage: Erick Thompson of the Regional District of Okanagan-Similkameen said he toured scorched rural areas outside Summerland and saw “serious damage” this week [4][3].
  • Neighbourhood protection: Thompson said firefighters and structural protection crews saved houses after flames came down the hill and reached backyards in Summerland [4].

Perspectives

Erick Thompson, Regional District of Okanagan-Similkameen: Thompson said the return home was different for each resident and that nothing he could say would make people who lost homes feel better.
