Canadian mountain runner missing during Matterhorn record attempt

Category: Canada

Athletics Canada said Canadian skyrunner and mountaineer Kalie McCrystal, who its website lists as from Squamish, British Columbia, has gone missing during a mountain-running record attempt on the Italian side of the Matterhorn, near the Switzerland-Italy border [1][2][3][5]. She was last seen on Tuesday, Sept. 1. The Matterhorn is a roughly 4,481-metre, horn-shaped peak overlooking Zermatt, Switzerland [3][5]. The governing body said McCrystal had years of experience in technical, high-altitude terrain and had previously climbed the Matterhorn. It gave no details of the record attempt and said her family was working with mountain-rescue services [3][5][4]. European reports said Aosta Valley rescuers and Cervinia police were searching on the Italian side after her last known sign near the Jean-Antoine Carrel hut, at about 3,830 metres [6][7].

Kalie is an elite athlete who trains for these conditions and we know in our hearts she is a survivor. Please pray with us for the safe return of Kalie McCrystal.

— Amelia Boultbee (Vancouver Sun)

Key Points

  • Record target: McCrystal had announced on Instagram that she planned to climb the Matterhorn by the Italian Lion ridge and return in under five hours [6][7][4].
  • Previous benchmark: A few weeks earlier, she completed a solo ascent and descent on the Swiss Hörnli ridge in 6 hours, 47 minutes and 33 seconds [6][7].
  • Search assets: Crews searched the Italian normal route Thursday with helicopter support, a Recco search device and phone-location attempts, but initially found no trace [6][7].
  • Visibility problem: Rescuers told Rai News that McCrystal’s white jacket was difficult to distinguish among rocks and snow [6][7].
