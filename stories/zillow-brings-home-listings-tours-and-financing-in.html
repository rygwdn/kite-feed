Zillow brings home listings, tours and financing into Claude

Category: AI

Zillow, the U.S. real estate marketplace, launched a connector on Oct. 19 that lets users search for homes for sale or rent inside Anthropic’s Claude assistant, view maps and home-value estimates, and check affordability [1][3]. Users can then follow listing links, request a tour, contact an agent or explore financing in the conversation [1][3]. Zillow said it does not send data to Anthropic; listings shown in Claude use Zillow’s multiple listing service (MLS)-approved presentation, including broker and MLS attribution [1][3]. Zillow previously launched integrations with ChatGPT and Google Gemini [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Finding a home is one of the most important decisions people make, and it deserves specialized intelligence — real listings, real home values and real affordability math, built on two decades of housing data.

— Josh Weisberg, senior vice president of AI at Zillow (PR Newswire)

Key Points

  • Fair housing: Zillow says its ethical AI team reviews its tools and that its open-source Fair Housing Classifier is designed to flag potential bias in real estate conversations [1][3].
  • Gemini tours: Renters can see listings with available tour times in chat; after they link their accounts, confirmed tours appear in their Zillow itinerary [1].
  • In-app AI: Zillow's AI mode launched in beta for a limited group, and the company said it planned to expand access throughout 2026 [1].
