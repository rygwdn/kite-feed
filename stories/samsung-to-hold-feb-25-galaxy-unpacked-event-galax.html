Samsung to hold Feb. 25 Galaxy Unpacked event; Galaxy S26 lineup expected

Category: Technology

Samsung will hold a Galaxy Unpacked event on Feb. 25 in San Francisco, where it’s expected to unveil the Galaxy S26, S26 Plus and S26 Ultra [2][3]. With the event just days away (as of Feb. 22), much of the coverage has focused on leaks and rumor roundups about what may change in Samsung’s 2026 lineup—and what may stay familiar [2][3]. One prominent leak of promotional materials suggests the Galaxy S26 Ultra’s battery life and charging could look much like last year’s model, while also spelling out a “Privacy Display” feature in more detail than earlier previews [1]. At the same time, rumor reporting points to camera upgrades as a key battleground this year, not only for Samsung but also for rivals such as OnePlus [6]. For buyers, Samsung’s event should settle which rumored upgrades are real, along with final specs and pricing [2][3].

  • How to watch: Samsung will stream Galaxy Unpacked 2026 live, so anyone with an internet connection can watch the S26 launch remotely [7].
  • Battery/charging expectations: Leaked S26 Ultra promotional materials suggest overall battery life may be similar to the prior generation, even as the promo copy points to faster charging [1].

  • Camera race: TechRadar says fresh rumors point to two “smart” camera upgrades on the Galaxy S26, while also flagging the OnePlus 16 as a potential challenger with an elite new telephoto sensor [6].
  • Wish-list upgrades: PCMag lays out six upgrades it says would give the S26 series a competitive edge, setting expectations ahead of its planned on-the-ground coverage of the Feb. 25 event [5].
