Apple sets weeks-long celebrations for 50th anniversary

Category: Technology

Apple says it will mark its 50th anniversary on April 1, 2026, with celebrations “over the coming weeks,” though it hasn’t said yet what those plans include [1][9][12][6]. To start, CEO Tim Cook published a “50 Years of Thinking Different” letter that traces Apple’s roots back to 1976 and leans into the company’s long-running “Think Different” theme. He credits users for what they build and achieve with Apple devices and services, and says Apple is “more focused on building tomorrow than remembering yesterday” [2][3][10][7].

Primary Source: View original article

At Apple, we're more focused on building tomorrow than remembering yesterday, but we couldn't let this milestone pass without thanking the millions of people who make Apple what it is today.

— Tim Cook (MacRumors)

Key Points

  • Cook’s rollcall: Cook’s letter traces Apple’s arc from early computers to the Mac, iPod, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. He also name-checks services including the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple TV [2].
  • Marketing callback (specifics): Coverage points to clear textual and visual nods to the original “Think Different” era—for example, Cook quotes the “Here’s to the crazy ones” passage. Apple also launched @helloapple branding that uses a rainbow-style Apple mark and ends with a signed “Here’s to you” [1][4][12].
  • Product speculation: DigiTimes says the anniversary has also fueled speculation about software updates and even new devices tied to the milestone, though Apple hasn’t confirmed any product plans [6].
  • User spotlight (examples): Cook’s letter moves beyond a general thank-you and lists specific ways people use Apple products—for instance, launching businesses, cheering up loved ones in the hospital, capturing a child’s first steps, running marathons, writing books, rekindling friendships, and finding and sharing new music and stories [11].

Perspectives

Apple (company statement): Apple says the anniversary is a time to reflect on its journey and celebrate the people and communities who have “thought different” with Apple technology.

Sources: MacRumors

Tim Cook (CEO): Cook frames “Think Different” as a user-driven idea, arguing that progress starts with people who imagine a better way. He credits customers for the most meaningful “chapters” written with Apple’s tools.

Sources: MacRumors

Macworld: Macworld reads the anniversary posts as part of a social-media refresh aimed at younger audiences, noting the new @helloapple Instagram account appears timed to promote products like the MacBook Neo alongside broader Apple messaging.

Sources: Macworld

TechRadar: TechRadar welcomes the milestone but also notes a sense of “hoping for more,” arguing the coming weeks give Apple a chance to do something especially memorable.

Sources: TechRadar

DigiTimes: DigiTimes says the anniversary has raised expectations for marketing pushes and possible platform updates, while stressing that Apple hasn’t announced details yet.

Sources: DigiTimes

Timeline

  • 12 March 2026: Apple announces weeks-long 50th anniversary celebrations are coming [1][9].
  • 12 March 2026: Tim Cook publishes “50 Years of Thinking Different” letter [2][10][3].
  • 12 March 2026: Apple launches new “Hello Apple” Instagram account [1][4].
  • 1 April 2026: Apple turns 50 years old on April 1, 2026 [1][12].

Historical Background

Apple’s “Think Different” branding is closely tied to its late-1990s and early-2000s campaign—especially the “Here’s to the crazy ones” text—that ran from 1997 to 2002. Apple’s current anniversary messaging is explicitly pulling from that era through excerpts and references [1][2][4]. Cook’s anniversary letter also puts the theme back at the center, presenting it as a through-line from Apple’s early personal-computing roots to today’s mix of devices and services [2].
