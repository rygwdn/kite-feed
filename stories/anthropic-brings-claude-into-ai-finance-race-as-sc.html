Anthropic brings Claude into AI finance race as Schwab limits agent access

Category: AI

Anthropic began a U.S. beta on Nov. 5 that lets subscribers connect selected bank and card accounts to its Claude chatbot through Plaid, a financial-data network [4][24]. The rollout follows OpenAI’s similar finance feature for its ChatGPT chatbot, introduced in May [6][13]. Anthropic’s April usage report identified financial advice as one of the main topics users ask Claude about [14]. Charles Schwab says it blocks outside personal agents from independently completing financial tasks and has built an assistant using its internal data [18][21]. Crypto investor Anthony Pompliano publicly accused Anthropic representatives of approaching engineers at his startup Silvia in April to seek information about its product [25]. Anthropic says it is exploring ways for Claude to work with licensed professionals and regulated firms on larger financial decisions, and has invited banks and wealth managers to help shape that work [26][22].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Account analysis: Claude can flag duplicate charges, unexpected fees and bills that have risen over time, and generate charts or due-date calendars [23][14].
  • Plaid network: Plaid connects to more than 12,000 financial institutions, including Citi and JPMorgan Chase’s Chase unit [23][18].
  • Privacy contrast: Anthropic says it excludes Personal Finance hub conversations from model training; Engadget reported that ChatGPT’s existing data controls include users in training by default unless they opt out [23][18].
  • Product expansion: Anthropic introduced Claude services for legal work and financial-services companies before adding account-linked analysis to consumer subscriptions [13][16].

Perspectives

Will Robinson, Plaid’s chief technology officer, said consumers are willing to share personal information with financial AI when they see a clear benefit. He said connected records enable tailored answers based on real account figures [26].

Q&A

Q: Can Claude take action on connected accounts?

A: Anthropic says access is read-only: Claude cannot move, manage or invest money, and the feature is not designed to provide investment advice [13].
