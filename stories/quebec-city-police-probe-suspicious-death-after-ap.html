Quebec City police probe suspicious death after apartment fire

Category: Canada

Quebec City police are investigating a fatal apartment-building fire in the Saint-Sauveur neighbourhood as a suspicious death, after a blaze on Saint-Mathias Street on Saturday left one man dead [2][3][4]. Police identified the victim as 42-year-old Ahmed Yassir Ismaili-Alaoui [1]. Authorities have not publicly detailed what specifically makes the death suspicious, and the investigation is ongoing [1][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Quebec City’s Saint-Sauveur is one of the city’s central neighbourhoods, located just west of Old Quebec [common].

Key Points

  • Where it happened: Responders were called to an apartment building on Saint-Mathias Street in Quebec City’s Saint-Sauveur area late Saturday afternoon [3][4].
  • Police stance: Quebec City police have said they are treating the case as a “suspicious death” while continuing their work at the scene and through follow-up inquiries [2][1].
  • Victim identified: Police released the victim’s name as Ahmed Yassir Ismaili-Alaoui, and said he was 42 years old [1].
