Victoria police arrest two after Beacon Hill assault

Category: Canada

Victoria police arrested two people after three people were injured in an alleged assault with a weapon near Beacon Hill Park late Friday night [1][3][2]. Officers were called to Dallas Road near the park shortly after 10 p.m. after reports of a confrontation involving multiple people [1][3]. Police said the three injured people suffered non-life-threatening injuries; two were taken to hospital and later released [1][3]. The two people arrested at the scene were not injured, one is a youth, and both remained in custody [1][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Crime unit: Victoria police said the department’s major crime unit is investigating [3].
  • Weapon unclear: Police did not disclose what type of weapon was allegedly used [3].
  • Witness request: Police asked anyone who saw the incident and has not spoken to investigators to call 250-995-7654 ext. 1 and cite file number 26-27379 [3].

Action Items

  • Contact Victoria police with witness information: Call 250-995-7654 ext. 1 and cite file number 26-27379 if you saw the incident and have not already spoken with police [3].
