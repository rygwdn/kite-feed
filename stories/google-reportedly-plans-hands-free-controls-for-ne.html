Google reportedly plans hands-free controls for next TV streamer

Category: Technology

Google is reportedly developing a successor to its Google TV Streamer with built-in far-field microphones and presence sensing. The features would let users issue voice commands without the remote and prompt the TV to display ambient information when someone enters the room [2][3][1]. The device is expected to have a similar but slightly narrower design [3][4]. The rumored upgrades also include a faster processor, more storage than the current model’s 32GB and two additional shortcut buttons on the remote [3][1]. Google has not announced a launch date or price [3][4]. The current Streamer launched in August 2024 as Google discontinued standalone Chromecast devices [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Price change: Google recently raised the current Streamer’s U.S. price by 50% to $149.99 [4][3].
  • Wireless standard: Frandroid noted that the current box supports Wi-Fi 5 and said no details about the successor’s wireless standard had surfaced [5].
  • Smart-home competition: 9to5Google cited Apple’s reported HomePad display and planned collaboration with LG as competing smart-home efforts [2].

Perspectives

9to5Google: Senior editor Ben Schoon argued that Google should prioritize updating aging Nest cameras and the Nest Hub Max and improving integration with third-party smart-home devices rather than developing another streamer.

Sources: 9to5Google

Frandroid: The publication raised privacy concerns about adding microphones and presence sensing, while noting that Google TV boxes have not been caught spying on conversations.
