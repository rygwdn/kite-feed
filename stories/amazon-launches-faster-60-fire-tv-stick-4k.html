Amazon launches faster $60 Fire TV Stick 4K

Category: Technology

Amazon announced a redesigned Fire TV Stick 4K on Sept. 30, priced at $59.99 in the United States. The company said the device reaches playback from power-on 20% to 40% faster than a similarly priced competing 4K streamer. It also said apps open more than 35% faster and playback begins nearly 20% faster. Amazon did not name the rival device, and Engadget said it could not verify the company’s test methodology [2][8][10][7]. The slimmer streamer has a quad-core 1.7GHz processor, 8GB of storage, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3, and can draw power from a compatible TV’s USB port. It runs Amazon’s Vega OS and supports Alexa+; unlike Android-based Fire TV devices, Vega OS does not support sideloading Android apps. Amazon has reduced the Fire TV Stick range from five models to three—the HD, 4K and 4K Max—while the Fire TV Cube remains available [1][11][16][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Remote design: The redesigned remote has an asymmetric shape and distinct, indented buttons intended to make controls easier to find by touch; its Star button can be customized [12][11].
  • Sale pricing: Amazon scheduled a U.S. Prime event price of $29.99 for the new stick, half its $59.99 list price [10][11].
  • European offer: A German report listed an introductory price of €34.99 through Oct. 12, against a €74.99 recommended price [14].
  • Remote upgrade: TechRadar reported that a separate Fire TV Remote Plus with a built-in Find My Remote feature is planned for 2027 [7].

Perspectives

CNET reviewer Ty Pendlebury: After handling the stick, Pendlebury described it as solid and more premium-feeling than previous Fire TV Sticks, but noted the redesigned remote no longer has a dedicated Alexa button [8].

Sources: CNET

Technical Details

  • Video formats: The stick decodes AV1, H.265, H.264 and VP9, and supports HDR10, HDR10+ and HLG output up to 2160p at 60 frames per second [1][21].
  • Dolby Vision: The new Stick 4K does not support Dolby Vision; that format remains available on Amazon’s Fire TV Stick 4K Max [21].
