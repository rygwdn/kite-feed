Canada’s Governor General Mary Simon begins Arctic tour

Category: Canada

Governor General Mary Simon is set to visit Norway, Denmark and Greenland next week, with stops that include taking part in the Arctic Frontiers Conference in Tromsø and meeting Norway’s King Harald in Oslo [2]. The trip also includes a visit to Greenland for the opening of Canada’s first consulate in Nuuk, a step aimed at strengthening Canada’s presence and relationships in the Arctic region [3]. The coverage notes Simon’s longstanding Arctic and diplomatic experience, including prior postings connected to Denmark and circumpolar affairs [1].
