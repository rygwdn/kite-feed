Researchers report new solar materials and underwater photovoltaic tests

Category: Science

Oregon State University researchers developed a family of materials that use sunlight to produce hydrogen from water, offering a route for converting solar energy into chemical fuel [2]. The article description does not provide efficiency figures or further experimental details [2]. Separately, researchers working near China’s Weizhou Island generated electricity from sunlight underwater. Futurism reported that the perovskite-based cells were adjusted to use blue and green light that penetrates the ocean [3].

Key Points

  • Underwater depth: The test generated electricity from sunlight at a depth of 10 metres, or nearly 33 feet, according to Futurism [3].
