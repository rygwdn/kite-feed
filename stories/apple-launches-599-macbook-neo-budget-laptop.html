Apple launches $599 MacBook Neo budget laptop

Category: Technology

Apple introduced the $599 MacBook Neo as a new low-cost Mac laptop, while keeping familiar design touches such as a 13-inch Liquid Retina display [3][1][14]. Several outlets cast the Neo as Apple’s clearest push in years to bring the Mac to more people than premium pricing allows. Early hands-on impressions describe it as capable for everyday work at its entry-level price [1][8][12]. The big twist: the MacBook Neo runs on an iPhone-class A18 Pro chip. It also arrived as part of a broader wave of Apple hardware announcements, including refreshed MacBook Air and Pro models and new display options [3][14].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Retail deals: MacRumors’ weekly deals roundup says Best Buy is bundling free gift cards with select Apple pre-orders—such as $25 with MacBook Neo, $50 with M5 MacBook Air, and $100 with M5 Pro/M5 Max MacBook Pro—while Amazon is showing early cash discounts like $40 off the M4 iPad Air [2].
  • Lineup role: In 9to5Mac’s Overtime discussion, the hosts floated the MacBook Neo as a potential “best budget laptop” pick, and walked through the trade-offs and perks of Apple’s new entry point to macOS [12].
  • Podcast reaction: On the Cult of Mac podcast, the hosts called the MacBook Neo the standout in a “tsunami” of new Apple product announcements that week [6].
  • Brand strategy: Fast Company argued the Neo’s low-end position is meant to sit alongside—not replace—Apple’s premium machines, pointing to the same-week debut of devices like a $2,699 16-inch M5 MacBook Pro and the $3,299 Studio Display XDR [8].

Perspectives

PCMag (hands-on review): After an initial hands-on session, PCMag called the MacBook Neo sleek and surprisingly capable for the price, and suggested it could become a breakout budget hit [1].

Sources: PCMag

Fast Company (brand analysis): Fast Company argued the Neo helps round out Apple’s shift toward being seen as more affordable, even as it continues to launch much pricier flagships in the same week [8].

Sources: Fast Company

TechRadar (buyer guidance): TechRadar framed the Neo and an iPad 11 plus Magic Keyboard Folio as two similarly priced “affordable Apple setup” paths, and advised choosing based on whether you want a laptop or a tablet-first experience [4].

Sources: TechRadar

CNET (lifestyle angle): CNET focused on personalization, treating the MacBook Neo’s four color options as a meaningful part of its appeal beyond the spec sheet [7].

Sources: CNET

Technical Details

  • A18 Pro in a Mac: MacRumors reports the MacBook Neo uses the A18 Pro—an iPhone-class chip—making it the first consumer Mac to run an iPhone chip rather than an M-series processor [3].
  • Display configuration: The 13-inch Liquid Retina display is listed at 2408×1506 resolution, 500 nits brightness, and an anti-reflective coating, with uniform bezels instead of a notch [3].
  • On-device AI claims: Apple’s performance messaging, as relayed by MacRumors, includes a claim of up to 3× faster on-device AI workloads than a bestselling PC with an Intel Core Ultra 5 [3].

Historical Background

Apple started its multi-year shift from Intel CPUs to Apple-designed silicon for Macs in 2020 [common].

Action Items

  • Compare your lowest-cost Apple laptop options before pre-ordering: Price out a MacBook Neo versus an iPad 11 plus Magic Keyboard Folio based on how you work (laptop-first vs tablet-first) [4].
  • Check early promotions if you were planning to buy anyway: Look for retailer incentives like Best Buy gift cards on MacBook Neo pre-orders and Amazon discounts on M4 iPad Air listed in weekly deal roundups [2].
