Canada’s Greens elect former MP Mike Morrice as party leader

Category: Canada

The Green Party of Canada elected former MP Mike Morrice as leader on Nov. 14, after he became the only candidate in the race. Morrice represented Kitchener Centre from 2021 to 2025 and lost the seat to Conservative Kelly DeRidder by fewer than 400 votes in the last federal election [1][3][4]. Morrice has said he wants to make affordability a central issue and challenge what he calls oil and gas giants. He plans to seek his former Kitchener Centre seat at the first opportunity, but said earlier this fall he would not run in the six federal byelections scheduled for Dec. 7 [5][6][3]. Prime Minister Mark Carney’s government is rolling back several emissions-reduction policies introduced under former Prime Minister Justin Trudeau [4][5].

  • Carney’s rationale: In June, Prime Minister Mark Carney described the previous government’s climate plan as too costly and divisive and said Canada’s emissions would rise under his approach [4][5].
