B.C. skyrunner's family accepts likely Matterhorn death

Category: Canada

The family of Canadian skyrunner and mountaineer Kalie McCrystal said Tuesday that evidence from a two-week search suggested she fell while descending the Matterhorn in the Alps. McCrystal, 38, was last seen Sept. 1 while preparing for a mountain-running record attempt on the 4,478-metre peak along the Switzerland-Italy border [1][2][3]. Her mother, Wendy Cooper, and sister, Jody McCrystal, thanked search-and-rescue crews and said they were confident every possible effort had been made to find her. The family said friends and loved ones around the world mobilized to help find McCrystal and bring her home [1].
