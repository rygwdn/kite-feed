King Charles begins four-day US visit with Trump

Category: World

King Charles III and Queen Camilla are beginning a four-day state visit to the United States hosted by President Trump, with events including a garden party, an address to a joint session of Congress, and a state banquet [4][6]. Buckingham Palace said the visit will go ahead as planned despite the shooting at the White House Correspondents’ Association dinner on Saturday, and British and U.S. security teams have been working closely on arrangements [7][8][10]. The trip carries broader diplomatic weight for London, which wants the monarch to help steady and reaffirm the U.K.-U.S. relationship at a moment of strain over issues including Iran, immigration, and energy policy [1][3]. Several reports describe the visit as a carefully prepared effort by the British government and the palace to create room for warmer ties between the two allies [2][3][5].

Key Points

  • Security backdrop: The suspect in the White House correspondents’ dinner attack allegedly left writings calling for Trump administration officials to be targeted, according to President Trump [9].
  • Domestic debate: Some U.K. politicians argued that Charles should not make the trip at all, reflecting concern in Britain over how closely the monarchy should be drawn into current political tensions [3][1].

Perspectives

Buckingham Palace: The palace decided the state visit should continue after the Washington shooting and said Charles was greatly relieved that President Trump, First Lady Melania Trump, and other guests were unharmed.

Sources: South China Morning Post

U.K. government: British officials have framed the trip as a mission to “reaffirm and renew” bilateral ties, after months of planning between the government and palace over the king’s goals and speech.

Sources: Politico Europe

Political analysts: Commentary in British and U.S. outlets portrays the visit as unusually sensitive because the monarch must balance ceremonial duties with a high-stakes diplomatic role at a time of strain in the so-called special relationship.

Sources: Politico

Security officials: Senior U.K. minister Darren Jones said British and American security teams were working closely to make sure arrangements for the visit were in place after the shooting.

Sources: The Hindu

  • 28 April 2026: Charles is expected to address a joint session of Congress [4][3]

Historical Background

The modern idea of a U.K.-U.S. “special relationship” grew out of close wartime cooperation during World War II and remained central to British foreign policy through the Cold War [common]. Queen Elizabeth II visited the U.S. in 1991, a benchmark now being used to compare the scale of Charles’s planned remarks [3]. The current visit comes during the 250th anniversary of American independence, giving both sides a symbolic backdrop to emphasize continuity despite recent tensions [1][3].
