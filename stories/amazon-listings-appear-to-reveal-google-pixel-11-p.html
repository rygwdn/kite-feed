Amazon listings appear to reveal Google Pixel 11 prices, colors

Category: Technology

Deleted Amazon listings and screenshots attributed to Google's official Amazon storefront appeared to show much of the Pixel 11 lineup before Google's expected Made by Google event on Aug. 12, including Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold models [12][15][10]. The listings showed the base Pixel 11 in colors labeled Frost, Pistachio, Hibiscus and Obsidian by some sources, while other listing text used Midnight, Fuchsia and Moss, leaving the final marketing names unconfirmed [6][12][15][24]. The screenshots indicated U.S. pricing starting at $899 for a 256GB Pixel 11, with a 512GB version listed at $1,019; Droid Life and Android Authority also reported that the listings suggest Google may make 256GB the base storage tier across the family [6][15]. Separate FCC filings listed five new Google devices classified as phones, adding to earlier Pixel 11 Pro Fold paperwork and pointing to expected connectivity features including 5G, mmWave and satellite across the lineup [4][11][16]. The leaked retail pages and FCC documents remain unofficial because Google has not announced the phones, and several sources noted inconsistencies in the listings, including differing color names, placeholder-style information and pages that were removed after they circulated [12][10][24]. Google has confirmed an August hardware event where the new Pixel phones are expected to be introduced [2][16][23].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Pro pricing: Android Authority reported a $999 starting price for the Pixel 11 Pro with 256GB storage, while Droid Life described price increases of $100 on base Pro models, $120 on 512GB Pixel 11 Pro models and $230 on 1TB Pixel 11 Pro models [15][5].
  • RAM split: The leaked listings suggested Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL 256GB variants may use 12GB of RAM, while 512GB and 1TB versions retain 16GB [5][15].
  • Item tracker: Some Amazon listing text referenced a compatible Google Pixel Tag accessory, although Google has not announced such a product [13][17][24].

Perspectives

9to5Google: 9to5Google said the Amazon pages appeared to be placeholders or drafts that went live early, listed the products as sold and shipped by Amazon, and showed them as part of the Google Store on the platform. It advised caution because Amazon also includes third-party sellers.

Sources: 9to5Google

Droid Life: Droid Life treated the listings as convincing because they contained matching device identifiers and were removed after circulation, but it also warned that Amazon pages can include placeholder information that cannot be independently confirmed.

Sources: Droid Life

Android Authority reader Ali Choudary: Ali Choudary told Android Authority he found the removed pages by searching internal model numbers 4CS4, CGY4, PKK4 and 9YI4 on Google's official Amazon storefront.

Sources: Android Authority

Technical Details

  • MediaTek modem references: The FCC filings reference MediaTek modem-related material, which Droid Life, 9to5Google and Android Authority interpreted as evidence that Pixel 11 devices may move away from Samsung Exynos modem hardware [4][11][16].
  • UWB and Thread split: FCC documentation indicated three of five listed devices include UWB and Thread, while two do not, suggesting Google may reserve those short-range tracking and smart-home radio features for higher-tier models [11][18].
  • Connectivity stack: The filings showed support for 5G mmWave and satellite connectivity, along with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi 6+, NFC and wireless charging, according to Droid Life and 9to5Google [4][11].

Industry Impact

  • Retail leak controls: The early Amazon postings made partner storefront metadata visible before Google's official announcement, as seen with the Pixel 11 leak [12][10].
  • Smart-home accessories: If accurate, the Pixel Tag reference would put Google in the item-tracker category already associated with Apple's AirTag and Android-compatible tracker networks [13][17][common].
  • Component suppliers: A move to MediaTek modems would change Google's smartphone bill of materials and could alter supplier dynamics that have long centered on Samsung Exynos components [11][29].

Business Angle

  • A higher $899 starting price with 256GB storage could lift average selling prices, but it also narrows Pixel's value gap against premium rivals [15][common].
  • Reserving 16GB RAM for higher-capacity Pro models would differentiate the more expensive storage tiers, but base Pro buyers would get less memory than buyers of higher tiers [5].
  • A MediaTek modem shift would change a component tied to cellular reliability, a recurring Pixel concern cited by Android Central [4][29].

Action Items

  • Wait for official specifications before buying accessories: Treat case, charger and tracker compatibility as unconfirmed until Google announces the Pixel 11 lineup because the Amazon pages were removed and contained inconsistent details [12][24].
  • Compare RAM before choosing a Pro storage tier: If the leaked listings prove accurate, check whether the 256GB Pro model has 12GB RAM while higher storage versions have 16GB [5][15].
