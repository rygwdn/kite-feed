Apple readies 20-product rollout through 2027

Category: Technology

Apple is preparing roughly 20 product releases for the rest of 2026 and 2027, Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reported, according to MacRumors, Macerkopf and Spider’s Web [4][7][9]. The roadmap includes the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, a foldable iPhone Ultra, new Apple Watch models, refreshed Macs, a new entry-level iPad, a smart home hub, and updated Apple TV and HomePod mini devices [4][3][7]. Several products were reportedly delayed until Apple’s more intelligent, more personal Siri was ready; the new Siri arrived in beta two years after Apple first previewed it at WWDC 2024 [4][7][9]. Gurman’s reported list puts Apple’s smart home push and some living-room hardware on the 2026–2027 schedule, while Apple’s camera-equipped AirPods and first smart glasses are described as 2027 products designed to give Siri and Apple Intelligence real-world context [2][11]. The reported iPhone schedule would break from Apple’s traditional fall launch pattern: the premium iPhone 18 Pro models and foldable iPhone Ultra are expected in September 2026, while the iPhone 18, iPhone 18e and iPhone Air 2 are expected in the first quarter of 2027 [8][10]. iFanr cited a Wall Street Journal interview in which Tim Cook said Apple had tried to shield customers from supplier price increases but called the situation “unsustainable” [10].

Primary Source: View original article

We’re doing our best to mitigate the huge increases that are being passed to us, and we’ve been trying to shield our customers from the increases, but the situation has become unsustainable.

— Tim Cook (iFanr)

Key Points

  • Foldable specs: The reported iPhone Ultra would use a 7.7-inch inner display, a 5.3-inch outer display, two rear cameras, one front camera, and Touch ID in the power button instead of Face ID [4][7].
  • Mac redesign: Gurman’s reported MacBook Pro update is expected around late 2026 or early 2027 with M6 Pro and M6 Max chips, an OLED touchscreen, Dynamic Island and a thinner design [6][8].
  • Home devices: Updated Apple TV and HomePod mini models are said to be in advanced testing, with Apple Intelligence and the revamped Siri as the main changes rather than major hardware redesigns [2][3].
  • Air model: The iPhone Air 2 is reported for spring 2027 with a second rear camera, longer battery life and an A20-family chip after Apple abandoned earlier smaller-phone lines such as mini and SE [12][7].

Perspectives

Mark Gurman: Gurman described Apple's product roadmap as unusually ambitious, citing planned releases across the iPhone, Mac, wearables and home-device lines.

Sources: Digital Trends

Timeline

  • 2024: Apple first previewed the more personal Siri at WWDC [4]
  • June 2026: New Siri arrived in beta after a two-year wait [4][7]
  • September 2026: Premium iPhone 18 models and foldable Ultra expected [8][10]
  • 2027-Q1: Standard iPhones and iPhone Air 2 expected to launch [8][10]
  • 2027: Camera AirPods and Apple smart glasses appear on roadmap [11]

Technical Details

  • A20 Pro process: RBC Ukraine reports that the iPhone 18 Pro’s A20 Pro chip would use TSMC’s 2-nanometer process, a change that iFanr says could help battery life alongside iOS 27 optimization [8][10].
  • Satellite 5G modem: The reported C2 modem in iPhone 18 Pro models would support 5G web browsing via satellite, expanding satellite use beyond emergency messaging if implemented as described [4][7].
  • Foldable multitasking: iOS 27 is expected to adapt to the foldable iPhone’s screen format with side-by-side apps and iPad-like multitasking functions [4][7].

Industry Impact

  • Smartphones: Apple’s reported foldable iPhone Ultra would put the company into a foldable category that Samsung and other rivals have pursued for years [3].
  • Smart home: A new Apple TV box and smart home hub with Apple Intelligence would bring Siri AI into Apple’s living-room and home-automation stack, according to Computerbase [6].
  • Wearables: Camera-equipped AirPods and smart glasses would shift Apple wearables toward environmental sensing for AI assistance rather than audio playback or notifications alone [11].

Business Angle

  • A foldable iPhone Ultra could lift Apple’s average selling price if demand holds, but a high price could slow upgrade cycles [4][10][common].
  • Apple Intelligence on Apple TV, HomePod mini, AirPods and glasses could make Siri more useful across the ecosystem, improving hardware stickiness if the software works reliably [2][11][common].
  • Reported cost pressure and expected price increases would test Apple’s pricing power, which is central to its long-term margin profile [10][6][common].
  • A split iPhone launch calendar could smooth revenue across fiscal quarters, but it may also complicate consumer buying patterns if buyers wait for spring models [8][10][common].
