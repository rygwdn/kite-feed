China docks Shenzhou-24 at Tiangong with Pakistan's first astronaut

Category: World

China’s Shenzhou-24 spacecraft docked with the Tiangong space station on Wednesday, carrying Pakistan Air Force pilot Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign astronaut to join a Chinese crewed mission. The spacecraft arrived at 11:56 a.m. Beijing time, three hours and 38 minutes after launching from Jiuquan aboard a Long March-2F rocket [9][17]. Ali will spend about a week aboard Tiangong as a payload specialist, then return to Earth aboard Shenzhou-23 with two departing crew members. The Shenzhou-24 crew, led by Chinese astronaut Tang Hongbo and including flight engineer Wu Yi, will conduct more than 90 scientific experiments and technology tests [9][2][4]. The flight is the first to carry a foreign astronaut on a Chinese crewed spacecraft and Pakistan’s first crewed orbital mission. Ali is due to conduct four joint China-Pakistan experiments. Tang and Wu will remain on the station for a six-month rotation, while Shenzhou-23 astronaut Zhu Yangzhu is staying for a year to study long-duration spaceflight [9][17][24][21].

Key Points

  • Repair mechanism: Researchers will test whether solar heating outside the cabin can restore the mechanical properties of self-healing carbon-fiber composites after microcracks form [17].
  • Research projects: Pakistan’s space agency says Ali’s work includes cultivating microalgae as a potential source of food and oxygen and testing how spaceflight affects quinoa seeds [9][46].
  • Rocket safeguards: China’s Long March-2F team made 12 technical improvements for the flight, including a manual backup channel for separating the rocket from its launch tower [17].
  • Seed samples: The quinoa study will expose 32 seed samples outside the station to assess how microgravity and cosmic radiation affect seed longevity and nutritional properties [17].
  • Training demands: Mission commander Tang Hongbo said language and cultural differences complicated joint preparation; Ali trained in Beijing for seven months and learned Mandarin [25][15].
  • Mission record: The flight also makes Tang Hongbo the first astronaut to visit Tiangong three times, after earlier missions aboard Shenzhou-12 and Shenzhou-17 [33][45].
  • Microbe study: Proposed by Pakistan’s Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission (SUPARCO), the cyanobacteria test will run in the station’s life-ecology experiment cabinet [17].
  • Docking method: Shenzhou-24 used an autonomous 3.5-hour rendezvous and docking mode; controllers can switch to a five-hour or longer approach if needed for safety [17].
  • Crew handover: The departing Shenzhou-23 crew includes Lai Ka-ying, Hong Kong’s first astronaut, and Zhang Zhiyuan [13][14].
  • Pakistan’s ambitions: Pakistan has no spacecraft of its own for sending astronauts to orbit, and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has said he hopes a Pakistani astronaut will eventually reach the Moon [24][26].

Technical Details

  • Rapid rendezvous: Shenzhou-24 used the autonomous rapid rendezvous and docking mode, the fourth use of this mode by a Shenzhou spacecraft [3][17].
  • Rocket safety changes: The Long March-2F team made 12 technical improvements, including revised propellant-system seals and a manual backup channel for separating the rocket from the launch tower [17].

Scientific Significance

  • Potential life-support research: Findings from the cyanobacteria experiment could inform oxygen regeneration for future deep-space missions [17].
  • Crop-breeding applications: Quinoa results could help identify varieties suited to space cultivation and provide reference data for breeding on Earth [17].
