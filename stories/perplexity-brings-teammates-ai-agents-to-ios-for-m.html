Perplexity brings Teammates AI agents to iOS for Max users

Category: AI

Perplexity’s Teammates workplace agents are now available on iOS as well as the web for Max subscribers, according to launch coverage. The agents are designed to manage ongoing projects rather than answer one prompt at a time [1][18]. Max costs $200 a month or $2,000 a year; Perplexity says Teammates use will be free during a promotional period, then draw on the plan’s credits [12][6]. The agents can take on assigned roles, work in parallel and continue tasks in the background. Users can monitor or redirect them through individual threads and add them to Slack channels, where colleagues can assign work or request updates [10][11]. Teammates are initially available to individual Max subscribers, not Enterprise Pro or Enterprise Max customers, and Perplexity has not set a wider rollout schedule, TechRepublic reports [3]. The company has described mobile and desktop support as planned, while launch coverage now reports iOS access [3][1].

Primary Source: View original article

Over time, we envision every org having hundreds of teammates working for each person

— Aravind Srinivas, Perplexity CEO (Biggo)

Key Points

  • Persistent context: Teammates retain project goals, previous instructions, files and feedback across sessions, reducing the need to repeat requirements [11].
  • Agent coordination: A Teammate can manage other agents and bring in additional ones as a workload grows [1][10].
  • Reactive monitoring: A sales agent can notice when a customer stops ordering, then review order records and support issues to investigate what changed [10].
  • Cross-agent handoffs: One agent can identify a website bug and pass its findings to another agent to prepare a fix [2].

Perspectives

Anthropic: Anthropic has said its Claude Duet agent checks connected accounts every few hours rather than continuously and can misjudge urgency, distinguishing its stated operating approach from Teammates’ around-the-clock design [6].

Technical Details

  • Agent isolation: Each Teammate has a separate computer and browser environment. Biggo reports that stored credentials are encrypted and users can take over the desktop [10].
  • Permission inheritance: Teammates use connected tools and follow the permissions already set in Perplexity Computer [3][2].

Industry Impact

  • Competitive access models: OpenAI Dots are available to Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets, while Claude Duet is waitlisted for U.S. Claude Pro and Max subscribers [6].

Q&A

Q: Which apps can Teammates connect to?

A: Reported integrations include Slack, GitHub and Gmail, with access following the permissions connected to Perplexity Computer [10][3].
