Amazon adds AI lip-syncing to Prime Video dubs

Category: AI

Amazon Prime Video has introduced an AI-assisted lip-sync feature that digitally alters actors’ mouth movements so English-dubbed dialogue aligns more closely with the video [1][3][4][6][8][9][10]. The rollout starts with the German-language series “Maxton Hall”; the first two seasons are already available worldwide in an English-dubbed version with adjusted lip movements [3][4][8][9]. Prime Video said the process uses AI and visual-effects tools after human dubbing has been recorded, changing the video rather than generating replacement voices [3][4][6][8][10]. Amazon said the feature will also apply to the show’s third and final season, which premieres on Dec. 9, 2026, and that it plans to bring the technology to additional titles without naming them [3][4][8][9].

Syncing mouth movements to audio, it eliminates the disconnect that can occur with traditional dubbing
