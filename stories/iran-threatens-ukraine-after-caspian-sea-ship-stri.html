Iran threatens Ukraine after Caspian Sea ship strike

Category: World

Iran threatened to respond after it accused Ukraine of attacking an Iranian commercial vessel in the Caspian Sea on Saturday, killing one sailor and injuring at least one other crew member [4][7][2][35][29]. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said the strike “cannot go unanswered,” called it a violation of the UN Charter and said he raised the issue in calls with EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas and Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov [4][7][8][2]. Ukraine gave a different account of the target: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukrainian forces carried out long-range strikes in the Caspian Sea against vessels used to carry Iran-linked military cargo and a warship, while Ukraine’s SBU said drones hit sanctioned cargo vessels used to transport military cargo between Iran and Russia [7][16][35][29][61]. Iran said it has “never intervened” in the Russia-Ukraine conflict and summoned Ukraine’s chargé d’affaires in Tehran to protest what it called a “hostile and criminal act” [7][16][35][29].

Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022 [common]. Iran publicly acknowledged in November 2022 that it had supplied a limited number of drones to Russia before the invasion, while denying post-invasion drone or missile deliveries [59]. Kyiv has spent much of the war countering Iranian-designed drones used by Moscow and has offered drone-interception expertise to Middle Eastern countries attacked by Iran [28][29].
