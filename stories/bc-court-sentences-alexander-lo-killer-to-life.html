B.C. court sentences Alexander Lo killer to life

Category: Canada

A British Columbia Supreme Court judge sentenced Dwight Kematch to life in prison for the January 2024 second-degree murder of Alexander Lo, in a case that resurfaced painful connections to the later Lapu Lapu festival tragedy in Vancouver [3][6][5]. Prosecutors told the court that Adam Kai-Ji Lo—Alexander Lo’s brother and the man charged over the April 2025 vehicle attack at the Lapu Lapu festival—watched the sentencing hearing by video link with his microphone muted [1]. Kematch apologized in court and described the subsequent festival attack as a “ripple effect,” calling what followed a “horrendous tragedy” [3][4][6]. The hearing brought two separate criminal cases into the same courtroom narrative, underscoring how violence can echo outward through families and communities [3][4].

  • Video attendance: Crown counsel said Adam Kai-Ji Lo attended the sentencing remotely via video link, observing while muted [1].

  • Case linkage: Reporting noted the murder of Alexander Lo occurred about 15 months before the festival attack that killed 11 people [3][4].
