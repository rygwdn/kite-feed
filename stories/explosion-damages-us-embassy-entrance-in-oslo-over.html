Explosion damages US Embassy entrance in Oslo overnight

Category: World

An explosion hit at or near the U.S. Embassy compound in Oslo around 1 a.m. local time on Sunday. Authorities said it caused minor material damage, and no injuries were reported; witnesses also described thick smoke in the area [1][5][4][2]. Norwegian police said they were treating the incident as a high-priority investigation, and the cause was still unclear in early reporting [4][8].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Search operation: Oslo police used dogs, drones, and helicopters to search for one or more potential perpetrators [4][7].
  • On-scene response: Police commander Michael Dellemyr said investigators were speaking with witnesses, and TV2 reported a bomb squad was at the scene [7].
  • Damage location: Police said the blast caused only “minor material damage” to one of the building’s entrances [4][7].
  • Safety update: Several hours later, police said the area around the building was safe for residents and passersby [7].

Perspectives

Oslo police: Police said they were investigating the blast as a high priority, with substantial resources, and were searching for one or more potential perpetrators.

Sources: Dawn

Norwegian police commander Michael Dellemyr: Dellemyr said police would not comment on what exploded or the type of damage because the investigation was in its early stages. He later said investigators had an idea of the cause, and it appeared to be an act carried out by someone.
