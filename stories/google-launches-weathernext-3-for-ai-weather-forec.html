Google launches WeatherNext 3 for AI weather forecasts

Category: AI

Google DeepMind and Google Research released WeatherNext 3 on Sept. 3, 2026, calling it their most advanced global AI weather forecasting model [1][2][6]. Google says the system uses live geostationary satellite data and other raw observations to produce hourly, higher-resolution forecasts, and is being integrated into Search, Gemini, Maps, Google Maps Platform and Cloud services [1][11][14]. The model departs from earlier AI weather systems that relied heavily on outputs from numerical weather prediction models, which Google says can carry about a six-hour lag and create bias for fast-changing variables such as rain and surface temperature [12][6][11]. Google says WeatherNext 3 can produce a global picture roughly five times sharper than WeatherNext 2, including some surface conditions at up to 5-kilometer resolution, and the accompanying arXiv paper says it establishes a new state of the art for probabilistic medium-range forecasting skill [1][5][15].

This is going to be the first time that some of the core variables feed and power a lot of the Google products

— Samier Merchant (TechCrunch)

Key Points

  • Benchmark results: WeatherNext 3 ranked first among leading AI and traditional systems tested on Brightband's Operational WeatherBench, including models from Google, Microsoft, Nvidia and ECMWF, as well as traditional forecasts from the U.S. National Weather Service and ECMWF [2][10].
  • Rain accuracy: Google says precipitation forecasts made one day or more ahead can be up to 50% more accurate, with the largest gains in regions where forecasting has historically been less reliable [6][12][14][15].
  • Energy variables: The model forecasts 100-meter wind speeds near turbine height, along with high-resolution cloud cover and solar radiation, variables Google says can support wind and solar power planning [1][14][10].
  • Observation sources: For precipitation training, Google cited NASA's Integrated Multi-satellite Retrievals for GPM and its own global precipitation reanalysis based on satellite radar [12].

Perspectives

WeatherNext research team: The arXiv paper identified two shortcomings in leading AI weather models: lower spatial and temporal resolution than top physics-based systems and biases inherited from training and initialization on analysis data.

Sources: arXiv

Samier Merchant: Merchant told TechCrunch that machine learning approaches weather forecasting as a problem of approximating noisy physics from incomplete information and finite compute, learning patterns from large data sets.

Sources: TechCrunch

Timeline

  • 2018: ECMWF released decades of weather data [2]
  • November 2025: Google introduced the WeatherNext 2 model [12]
  • 3 September 2026: Google publicly released WeatherNext 3 [1]
  • 4 September 2026: The WeatherNext 3 paper appeared on arXiv [5]

Technical Details

  • Multi-resolution output: WeatherNext 3 produces temperature and moisture forecasts at 5-kilometer resolution, other surface variables at 10 kilometers and wind speed at 25 kilometers [12][10].
  • 0.1-degree variables: The paper says the model reaches hourly time steps and 0.1-degree resolution for single-level variables such as solar radiation and cloud cover [5].
  • Sparse station modeling: WeatherNext 3 learns from sparse station observations to predict 2-meter temperature and dewpoint at any location and time while conditioning on local geographic features [5][14].

Industry Impact

  • Developer platforms: Google is making WeatherNext 3 available through Google Cloud and mapping tools including Google Maps Platform, the Google Maps Weather API and Google Earth Engine [1][14].

Scientific Significance

  • Physical consistency: The model maintains physical consistency from global wind patterns down to local topography, according to Habr [10].
  • Operational WeatherBench: TechCrunch said WeatherNext 3 outperformed leading AI and traditional forecast systems on variables including temperature, wind speed and humidity [2].
  • Remaining limit: The sources report Google and benchmark results but do not provide peer-reviewed publication status, operational error margins for all variables or real-world emergency-management validation beyond the arXiv submission [5][2].

Historical Background

The European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts released more than half a century of weather data in 2018, giving deep-learning researchers a large training base for weather models [2]. Google introduced the WeatherNext 2 model in November 2025, and that system produced forecasts in six-hour increments on a 25-kilometer grid [12][15]. WeatherNext 3 shifts that product line to hourly forecasts and finer grids for some variables [1][12].

Business Angle

  • Benchmark comparisons include other AI weather efforts from Microsoft, Nvidia and ECMWF [2].
