Sony restores PlayStation Network after outages around GTA 6 launch

Category: Technology

Sony restored PlayStation Network services after disruptions affected players trying to sign in, launch games and use network features around the Nov. 19 release of Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI). The company’s status page acknowledged problems on Nov. 18 and marked them resolved later that day. On launch day, it reported further trouble with PlayStation 4 (PS4) and PlayStation 5 (PS5) game streaming and PS5 gaming and social features before showing services restored by 12:57 p.m. ET [8][2][3]. The disruptions coincided with the staggered global launch of the Rockstar Games title, but Sony did not confirm that launch traffic caused the network problems. Players reported login failures, stalled downloads and connection errors, while the Xbox status page showed its services operating normally [4][21][3]. GTA VI requires a PS5 system software update, and players need an internet connection for initial setup and activation. Sony’s latest status update said services were back online, though some players continued to report occasional problems [2][6].

Industry Impact

  • Internet providers: Comcast said the 48-hour pre-download period was its largest single network event and expected launch day to set a new traffic record [2].

Historical Background

The outage came 15 years after a PlayStation Network breach in 2011 that left the service offline for 23 days, according to Frandroid [23]. Grand Theft Auto VI is the first new entry in its series in 13 years, according to The Guardian [4].
