Apple brings Qwen-powered Apple Intelligence to China in December

Category: Technology

Apple said on Oct. 31 it will bring its first Apple Intelligence AI features to mainland China with iOS 27.3 in December, with developer testing set to begin Nov. 4 [4][5][21]. Alibaba’s Qwen AI model will be available for selected requests in Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence, while Apple says its own models will power the other features [21][1]. The initial release includes tools for rewriting and summarizing text, removing objects from photos, summarizing notifications and translating conversations live in Messages, FaceTime and Phone. It does not include the rebuilt Siri AI or other next-generation features shown at Apple’s 2026 developer conference. Apple says support for iPad, Mac and other platforms depends on further regulatory approvals [6][9][21]. China’s cyberspace regulator registered Apple’s on-device generative AI service in July after a lengthy approval process. Apple’s China launch announcement sets a testing date ahead of the December public release [21][1]. The features will initially work on supported iPhones, including iPhone 15 Pro and newer models, with the device and Siri set to English or Simplified Chinese [9][21].

Drawing on Alibaba's deep AI capabilities, Qwen will be deeply integrated into experiences across the Apple ecosystem, and together we will bring new intelligent services to Apple users in China.

Key Points

  • Qwen tasks: Users can ask Qwen about selected photos, PDFs and presentations, or use Compose with Qwen to generate original content [21][4].
  • Privacy controls: Apple says it will ask permission before sending a question, document or image to Qwen; Alibaba says it will not use requests or generated replies to train its models [21][17].
  • Translation: Live Translation will work in Messages, FaceTime and Phone. Apple also lists AirPods translation among the initial China features [6][7].
  • Account access: Users can access Qwen by signing into an existing Qwen account or using Sign in with Apple [6][21].
