Update: Saskatchewan RCMP investigate gunshots on Cree Nation

Category: Canada

Saskatchewan RCMP say they are investigating reports of gunshots on Big Island Lake Cree Nation, about 400 kilometres northwest of Saskatoon, after receiving calls late Friday night [1][2]. Officers searched but did not find anyone injured, and police are working to determine whether the reports connect to an earlier fatal shooting in the community [3][2]. The earlier incident left a 31-year-old man dead and three others hospitalized with non-life-threatening injuries, and the major crimes investigation remains active as police try to identify and locate suspects [3].

Primary Source: View original article

We are working as efficiently and diligently as possible to try to determine who’s responsible for this homicide and this shooting, and make sure that we can restore that safe mind concept to the community as best as we can

— Insp. Ashley St. Germaine (The StarPhoenix)

Key Points

  • Search outcome: RCMP said they received multiple reports of a firearm discharging around 10:50 p.m. Friday and searched the area but located no injured people [3].
  • Earlier alert: After the Dec. 30 shooting, RCMP issued a dangerous persons alert for two suspects described as armed and dangerous, then cancelled it later the same day, saying there was no longer an imminent risk to public safety [3].
  • Suspect hunt: Police have said they are still working to identify and locate two suspects linked to the Dec. 30 incident, with no further public updates on them reported in these articles [3].
  • Motive question: Investigators have said they were examining whether the Dec. 30 shooting was random or targeted [3].
  • Community focus: An RCMP officer said the goal is to determine responsibility for the homicide and help restore a sense of safety in the community [3].
