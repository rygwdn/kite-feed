Cranial scans challenge dodo intelligence stereotype

Category: Science

Cranial scans indicate the extinct Mauritian dodo had brain features linked to sensory adaptations, challenging its long-standing image as a foolish or simple bird [1][2]. Phys.org described the dodo as a global symbol of human-caused extinction and one of the world's largest pigeons, while the research suggests it may not have deserved its reputation as a "silly simpleton" [2]. The findings add anatomical evidence to a reassessment of a bird that disappeared more than 300 years ago after humans arrived on Mauritius [3]. The study also raises the possibility that the dodo was the only member of its group to live in the dark [2].

Key Points

  • Extinction timing: The species was wiped out within decades of humans arriving on Mauritius, according to the research summary republished by Phys.org [3].
