Core Devices launches Pebble Round 2 for $199

Category: Technology

Core Devices, led by original Pebble creator Eric Migicovsky, has announced the Pebble Round 2 as a follow-up to Pebble’s 2015 round smartwatch, with preorders open now and shipping expected in May 2026 [1][4][7][9]. The reboot keeps the slim, round look but modernizes the experience with a larger 1.3-inch touchscreen and substantially longer battery life than the earlier model, aiming to fix the compromises that drew criticism last time [1][8][4][5]. The watch supports both iOS and Android, positioning it as a simple, cross-platform alternative in today’s smartwatch market [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: The original Pebble Time Round debuted in 2015 as Pebble’s slim, circular smartwatch experiment [8][4].

Key Points

  • Battery focus: Reported longevity ranges from about 10 days to more than two weeks depending on configuration and outlet testing/briefing details, underscoring that endurance is a primary design goal for this model [3][4][8][9].
  • Display upgrades: Engadget says the color e-paper touchscreen is now bonded to the glass to reduce glare and improve viewing angles, addressing readability complaints tied to the original hardware design [8].
  • Thinner profile: Engadget reports the Round 2 is 8.1mm thick, keeping Pebble’s “thin watch” identity even as many modern smartwatches have grown bulkier [8].
  • New sensors: Android Authority notes the Round 2 adds two microphones alongside step and sleep tracking, suggesting improved voice and health-adjacent features compared with older Pebble-era basics [4].
  • Open software: Liliputing reports Core Devices’ revived Pebble lineup runs an open-source version of Pebble OS, a community-friendly approach that can encourage tinkering and long-term support [7].

  • May 2026: Shipping is expected to begin for preorder customers [1][4][7].

Technical Details

  • Color e-paper display: The Round 2 uses a color e-paper touchscreen (rather than OLED/LCD), a choice typically associated with low power draw and outdoor readability, and Engadget says the panel-to-glass bonding reduces reflectivity/glare [8].
  • Touchscreen size and bezels: The display grows to 1.3 inches and removes the thick bezel seen on the original Round, increasing usable screen area without changing the watch’s compact feel [1][4].
  • Multi-platform compatibility: The watch is designed to work with both iOS and Android, which matters for buyers who want one wearable that can survive future phone switches [1].

Industry Impact

  • Battery expectations: By making multi-day endurance a headline feature, Pebble’s reboot highlights consumer appetite for wearables that prioritize longevity over always-on, power-hungry features [8][9].
  • Retro-tech revival: The return of a cult-brand watch more than a decade later shows how nostalgia plus modernized hardware can create viable demand in mature gadget categories [1][5].
  • Open-source wearables: Shipping watches on an open-source Pebble OS can encourage third-party apps and hobbyist development, a contrast to more tightly controlled wearable platforms [7].

Historical Background

Pebble was an early smartwatch pioneer, and its Pebble Time Round (released in 2015) stood out for being notably thin and truly round, but reviewers at the time criticized trade-offs like battery life, durability, and price [8][4]. In 2025, Migicovsky returned with new Pebble-branded watches through his company Core Devices, rebuilding momentum among long-time fans [4][7]. The Round 2 fits into that revival as another attempt to modernize a beloved design while keeping the lightweight, long-battery ethos that helped Pebble develop a cult following [8][7].
