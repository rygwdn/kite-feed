Canadians vote in three federal byelections Monday

Category: Canada

Voters in British Columbia, Ontario and Quebec cast ballots Monday, Aug. 31, in three federal byelections after MPs left their seats [1][2][3][4][5]. The races are in ridings in B.C., the Toronto area and Chicoutimi-Le Fjord in Quebec; the Canada-U.S. trade war and a tariff deadline are expected to be issues for voters [3][4][5]. Prime Minister Mark Carney’s Liberals are trying to add seats. La Presse reported that the B.C. and Ontario ridings are Liberal strongholds that former ministers previously represented [2][4]. Chicoutimi-Le Fjord has elected Conservatives four consecutive times. In the 2025 federal election, Conservative Richard Martel beat Bloc Québécois candidate Marc St-Hilaire by 1,499 votes and Liberal Stéphane Proulx by 1,536 votes [4].

Key Points

  • Quebec vacancy: Richard Martel created the Chicoutimi-Le Fjord vacancy when he left the Conservative caucus this spring to accept a Senate appointment [4].
  • Liberal candidate: Daniel Gobeil, a former president of Les Producteurs de lait du Québec, is running for the Liberals in Chicoutimi-Le Fjord [4].

Timeline

  • 2025: Richard Martel narrowly retained Chicoutimi-Le Fjord [4]
  • 23 August 2026: Advance voting was underway for the three byelections [5]
  • 31 August 2026: Voters cast ballots in all three ridings [1][2][4][5]
