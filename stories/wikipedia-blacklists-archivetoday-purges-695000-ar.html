Wikipedia blacklists Archive.today, purges 695,000 archived links

Category: Technology

English-language Wikipedia editors have blacklisted links to Archive.today (also known via domains like archive.li) and started removing Archive.today citations that were already used across the encyclopedia [3][6][4]. Editors pointed to two main issues: concerns that the service was used to help direct a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack, and claims that some archived snapshots were tampered with—both of which undermine the reliability Wikipedia expects from reference links [3][6][2][4][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Domain aliases: Archive.today runs under several mirror domains (such as archive.li and archive.is), so editors swapping out citations need to catch more than just the main hostname [1][5].
  • Alleged target: Coverage said the DDoS incident centered on a blogger investigating the service’s origins. Reports allege Archive.today’s anti-bot/CAPTCHA flow was modified to push visitors’ browsers to hit the target site [5][6].
  • Operator opacity: Boing Boing noted that the service’s operators are anonymous, a longstanding controversy that has kept scrutiny on the project [1].
  • Replacement choices: As Archive.today links disappear, editors have to choose between restoring original URLs and using alternative archives (such as the Internet Archive)—especially when an Archive.today snapshot had become the easiest accessible reference [3][6][1].

Perspectives

Wikipedia editors (English Wikipedia): English Wikipedia editors decided to blacklist Archive.today and begin removing existing links, citing reported DDoS-related abuse and concerns about the integrity of archived snapshots [3][6].

Sources: TechCrunch

Archive.today operator (as characterized in reports): Reports said the operator was caught tweaking or manipulating archived pages/URLs—something critics argue breaks the basic promise of an archive: preserving an unchangeable record [2][6].

Sources: Tom's Hardware

Action Items

  • Audit your citations if you edit Wikipedia: If you maintain Wikipedia pages, search for Archive.today links and replace them with original sources or other accepted archives as editors remove blacklisted URLs [3][6].
  • Save critical sources in more than one archive: If you need a durable citation for research, store the same page in multiple reputable archives so one service’s failure or a policy change doesn’t break your references [common].
