SpaceX Starship reaches orbit on 14th test flight, deploys 26 satellites

Category: Science

SpaceX’s Starship reached Earth orbit for the first time during its 14th test flight and deployed 26 third-generation Starlink satellites [2][3]. The flight also tested the vehicle’s heat shield during its return to Earth [1]. Engine anomalies cut short a mission planned to last about 10 hours: Starship deorbited after roughly three hours and splashed down in the Pacific Ocean earlier than planned [2][1]. Starship is part of NASA’s Moon plan [2].
