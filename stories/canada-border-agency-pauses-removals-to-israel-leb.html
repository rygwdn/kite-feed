Canada border agency pauses removals to Israel, Lebanon

Category: Canada

Canada’s border agency has temporarily paused the enforcement of removal orders to Israel and Lebanon, pointing to a volatile, unpredictable security situation and risks to civilians in both countries [5][3][2][6][4]. The Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) is doing this through an Administrative Deferral of Removals [5][2][6]. For people in Canada who are under a removal order and would be sent to Israel or Lebanon, the pause means their removals won’t go ahead while the deferral remains in place [5][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Removal orders vs enforcement: CBSA’s administrative deferral delays removals to Israel and Lebanon, but the removal orders themselves still stand and could be enforced again if the deferral is lifted [5][6].
  • Reassessment: CBSA says the deferral is temporary and may lift it if conditions change, which would allow removals to resume [5][2][6].
  • Timing: CBSA announced the suspension on March 7, 2026, and it applies to both Israel and Lebanon [5][4].
