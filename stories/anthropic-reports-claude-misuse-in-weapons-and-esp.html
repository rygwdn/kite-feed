Anthropic reports Claude misuse in weapons and espionage

Category: AI

Anthropic said its 154-page threat-intelligence report documented actors using Claude for weapons development, cyber operations, surveillance, propaganda, fraud and biological research between December 2025 and August 2026. The company said it banned the accounts involved, added safeguards and reported relevant criminal activity to authorities. It said many findings came from its own investigations [22][69][84]. The cases included five instances of biological research that Anthropic said could support weapons development, including work involving avian influenza, chikungunya and toxins. Other reported activity involved a Yemen-based weapons cell working on guided missiles, an Iran-linked actor compiling information on U.S. naval forces, Russian-linked hackers automating malware evasion, and Russian and Chinese actors developing military software [22][86][91][74]. Anthropic said it could not always determine whether the biological research had a malicious purpose, but it treated attempts to evade safeguards as grounds for intervention [26][92].

Did you know: Anthropic said the only documented use of its newest Fable and Mythos models involved a distillation case, while the biological cases used older Claude variants [22].

Key Points

  • Model range: The documented activity involved Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus, while Anthropic said its newest Fable and Mythos models appeared only in a separate model-distillation case [22][77]
  • Biological cases: Anthropic said one user sought help preparing a grant application for chikungunya research aimed at increasing transmission and immune evasion, while other cases involved mammalian adaptation of bird flu and work on orthopoxviruses [26][70].
  • Surveillance system: A Mali-based consultant reportedly used Claude Code to develop Lakana 360, a system designed to monitor about 25 million SIM cards and identify people by voice [79][78].
  • Drone software: A Russian group reportedly used Claude to build shared swarm memory, fault-tolerant coordination, terminal guidance and attack behaviors, then tested the software on physical hardware [86].
  • Broader misuse: The report documented six military-use cases involving actors in China, Russia and Yemen, including software for weapons design, targeting and control systems [107][77]
  • Influence campaigns: Anthropic described nine influence operations spanning Russia, Iran, Turkey, the Gulf, South Asia, Africa and Europe that used AI to plan campaigns, generate content and manage fake profiles [107][109]
  • European political campaign: Anthropic said Claude assisted a campaign targeting European political groups [45].
  • Cyber campaign: Anthropic attributed an operation matching the tradecraft of Russia-linked Midnight Blizzard to malware that automatically rewrote and rebuilt itself after security tools detected it [91].
  • Report structure: Anthropic grouped the wider misuse findings into seven categories, including biological research, conventional weapons, cyber operations, distillation, influence campaigns, scams and fraud, and surveillance [22]

Technical Details

  • Model distillation: Distillation involves extracting capability from a stronger model through large volumes of prompted exchanges, which Anthropic said Chinese laboratories used to train competing systems [7][29].
  • Model restrictions: In an avian-influenza case, Anthropic’s controls limited the work to its less capable models rather than allowing access to stronger systems [96][98].
  • Actor attribution: Anthropic identified the Russian state-sponsored actor in one cyber case as GTG-20006 and said its tradecraft overlapped with Midnight Blizzard, also known as APT29 or Cozy Bear [84].
  • Target scope: The Russia-linked hackers targeted more than 20 organizations, including Ukrainian and European government ministries and defense and intelligence bodies [91].

Industry Impact

  • AI governance: Anthropic called on governments and other AI developers to help prevent comparable misuse [75].
  • Security operations: Anthropic said it reported relevant criminal activity from the documented misuse cases to authorities [22].
  • Defense technology: Anthropic reported that Claude was used in work involving drone swarming, missile guidance and anti-torpedo systems [86][72].
  • AI security: Anthropic said the documented cyber operations used Claude for reconnaissance, exploitation and data exfiltration [84].
  • AI governance: AI providers are publishing threat-intelligence reports and adding controls for biological, military and cyber misuse [95][75].

Historical Background

The report was Anthropic's third misuse report since March 2025, according to coverage of the company's disclosure [85].

Business Angle

  • Incident scale: Anthropic said the Russia-linked hackers targeted more than 20 organizations, including government, defense and intelligence entities [91].
  • Model-theft scale: Anthropic said Chinese labs routed at least 35 million user requests to Claude during the summer [29][7].
  • Regional access controls alone are insufficient when users can route requests through intermediaries [98].
  • Enterprise controls: The cases involved chained reconnaissance, exploitation and data exfiltration [84].
