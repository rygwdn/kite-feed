Rumors point to foldable iPhone and redesign

Category: Technology

A cluster of reports published on April 24-25 points to Apple making two notable iPhone design shifts over the next two years: a first foldable iPhone in 2026 and a 20th-anniversary iPhone redesign in 2027 [2][3][6][4][7]. Across the coverage, the foldable model is widely described as a book-style device with a roughly 7.7- to 7.8-inch inner display and a smaller outer screen. The 2027 model, meanwhile, is described as a more radical redesign that could use a custom micro-curved OLED display reportedly made by Samsung [3][5][6][8][10][9]. These reports still rest on leaks, supply-chain claims, dummy units, and analyst expectations rather than Apple announcements, and several outlets explicitly urge caution because foldable iPhone rumors have slipped for years [3][2][4]. Even so, the emerging picture is encouraging for device enthusiasts: Apple may finally be nearing its foldable debut after sitting out the category, while its 2027 anniversary model could pair a thinner, brighter display approach with a more immersive edge-to-edge design if the current supply-chain information holds up [2][6][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Macworld says solid-looking predictions of a foldable iPhone being just two years away have surfaced repeatedly since 2018, with expected launch years slipping from 2020 to 2026 [3].

Key Points

  • Price tradeoff: The foldable iPhone is rumored to start around $2,000, and MacRumors says dummy units suggest it may omit features such as MagSafe and the Action button despite that premium price [5][13].
  • Display supplier: Several reports say Apple is tapping Samsung to make the 2027 phone's quad-curved or micro-curved OLED panel, underscoring Samsung Display's likely role in a flagship anniversary component [8][10][9][11].
  • Release window: Most foldable-iPhone reports point to a fall 2026 debut, though Mashable notes some claims of later shipments in December and warns that delays remain possible amid memory-supply concerns [2][3][6].
  • Internal layout: MacRumors reports that dummy models place the foldable phone's volume buttons on the top edge, a design choice linked to preserving internal space because the motherboard is said to sit on the right side [13].

Perspectives

Macworld: Macworld urges more skepticism than usual because reports of a foldable iPhone being two years away have circulated repeatedly since at least 2018 without leading to a launch.

Technical Details

  • Pol-less OLED approach: MacRumors and Android Headlines say Apple is seeking a Samsung display design that removes the polarizer layer to reduce panel thickness, increase brightness, and lower power draw, while making reflection control more challenging [10][9].
  • Micro-curved panel: Reports describe the 2027 display as an equal-depth quad-curved OLED with shallow 'micro-curves' on all four sides, meant to preserve edge-swipe usability and reduce the image distortion associated with deeper waterfall-style screens [8][10][9].
  • Book-style fold: Current leak roundups describe Apple's foldable as a book-style device that opens to an iPad mini-like form factor rather than a clamshell, with an inner display around 7.7 to 7.8 inches and an outer display around 5.3 to 5.5 inches [3][5][6].

Industry Impact

  • Foldables market: Apple's expected 2026 entry would bring one of the smartphone industry's biggest brands into foldables after rivals already embraced the format, potentially broadening mainstream attention to the category [2][6].
  • Display supply chain: Reports that Samsung would supply the anniversary iPhone's custom panel highlight how advanced OLED innovation remains concentrated among a small number of specialist manufacturers [8][10][9].
  • Accessories sector: Dummy units are already circulating for testing by accessory makers, showing how case and hardware partners prepare ahead of launch using highly detailed physical stand-ins [13].

Historical Background

Apple introduced the original iPhone in 2007 [common]. The iPhone X, released in 2017 for the product line's 10th anniversary, marked a major redesign by removing the Home button and shrinking bezels. Several current reports explicitly compare the expected 2027 model to that kind of anniversary-style reset [common][8][4]. Meanwhile, foldable-iPhone rumors have circulated for years, with Macworld noting predictions dating back to 2018 that repeatedly pushed the launch further out [3].

Action Items

  • Delay accessory purchases for rumored form factors: If you are considering cases, chargers, or mounts for a future foldable iPhone, wait for Apple specifications because dummy-model reports suggest unusual button placement and possible MagSafe changes [13].
  • Compare upgrade timing: If you are planning a premium iPhone upgrade, weigh whether a 2026 foldable or the 2027 anniversary redesign better fits your needs, since current reports describe them as different design bets rather than the same device line [2][4][7].
