Austrian Airlines flight diverts to Sofia after bomb threat; checks find no danger

Category: World

An Austrian Airlines Airbus A320 flying from Tel Aviv to Vienna diverted to Sofia on Sunday, Oct. 18, after its crew reported a bomb threat near the Romanian border. A Bulgarian MiG-29 escorted the aircraft to the airport, where 51 passengers and six crew members were safely evacuated [1][4][6][16]. Bulgarian authorities and airline staff searched the plane, passengers and crew. Austrian Airlines said Monday that the checks found no danger. Passengers reboarded, and the flight continued to Vienna [1][11][12]. Bulgaria set up a crisis response team and opened an investigation. Haaretz reported that the airline received the warning from an Austrian citizen who was not aboard. Authorities have not publicly identified the person who issued the warning or announced the investigation's findings [4][18][31].

Key Points

  • Emergency signal: Flight-tracking data showed the aircraft using squawk code 7700, the general-emergency signal, as it changed course toward Sofia [1][16][10].
  • Air defense coordination: Bulgaria’s Defense Ministry said it coordinated the fighter escort with NATO’s Combined Air Operations Centre in Spain [31].
  • Passengers aboard included a youth delegation expected to address the United Nations, according to Israeli outlet Ynet [31].
