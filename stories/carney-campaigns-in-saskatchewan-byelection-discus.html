Carney campaigns in Saskatchewan byelection, discusses trade and electricity

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney campaigned Thursday in Yorkton for Liberal candidate Aaron Kienle ahead of the Dec. 7 Yorkton—Melville byelection, then met Saskatchewan Premier Scott Moe in Regina to discuss the economy, electricity and trade [2][3]. The Liberals have never won the riding, where advance voting began Friday [2]. Moe said Saskatchewan could help expand the electricity grid to serve new projects, while Carney said Ottawa still needs to secure a new trade deal with the United States [3]. Carney also credited Moe with helping extend a trade agreement with China that lowers tariffs on many Canadian agricultural and food exports; Moe said U.S. tariffs have hit Saskatchewan’s forestry, manufacturing and honey sectors [3].

Key Points

  • Conservative edge: Conservative Cathay Wagantall won nearly 80% of the Yorkton—Melville vote in 2025, while the Liberals received less than 15% [2]
  • Rural challenge: Yorkton accounts for about one-fifth of the mostly rural riding’s population, and Conservative MP Kevin Waugh said the Liberals had focused their campaign on the city [2]
  • Export products: The extended China agreement covers lower tariffs on lentils and other pulses, pork, beef and canola, while giving China greater access for steel and aluminum [3]
