Peru authorities investigate Dalí nightclub bombing that injured 33

Category: World

A pre-dawn explosion struck the Dalí nightclub in Trujillo on Peru’s northern coast, injuring at least 33 people, including three minors, according to local authorities and emergency officials [2][3][4][7][8][1]. Officials said at least five people were seriously injured, and media reports described a chaotic scene as people scrambled for help after the blast [3][6][8]. Investigators had not identified a suspect, and no group had claimed responsibility as of the reporting [1][8]. The blast comes as Peru has seen a rise in organized crime and extortion in recent years—a trend some analysts tie to government corruption—though the motive in this case remained unclear [5].

Key Points

  • Ages of minors: Officials said the injured minors were a 16-year-old and two 17-year-olds [3][8].
  • Conflicting early injury tallies: Early reports put the number of injured at at least 31, while authorities later said 33 [6][8].
  • Administrative location detail: AP described the site as Trujillo in Peru’s La Libertad Department [8].
