Samsung foldables set 1.44 million preorder record

Category: Technology

Samsung Electronics said its Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold8 and Galaxy Z Flip8 logged 1.44 million preorders in South Korea from July 28 to Aug. 3, the highest preorder total for any Galaxy smartphone line [21][3][28][31]. The total surpassed the previous Galaxy record of 1.38 million preorders for the Galaxy Note10 over 11 days in 2019, and exceeded the Galaxy S26 series’ 1.35 million seven-day preorder tally earlier this year [21][3][28]. The standard Galaxy Z Fold8 led the launch, accounting for about 70% of South Korean preorders as Samsung promoted its wider, passport-like body and 4:3 inner display for reading, video, games and multitasking [21][3][28][31]. Several reviews described the Fold8 as a shift away from the tall, narrow foldable format, with a 5.5-inch 16:10 outer display and a 7.6-inch AMOLED 4:3 inner screen, while noting that its high price remains a barrier for buyers [4][18][22][14][1]. Samsung and retailers scheduled general availability for Aug. 7, while preorder promotions and carrier financing offers continued in several markets [2][7][32][26]. In the United States, some Galaxy Z Fold8 configurations showed delayed shipping dates after early demand [17].

Key Points

  • Carrier demand: LG Uplus said the Fold8 made up 67.4% of its preorder customers, while KT and SK Telecom each said the model accounted for more than half of reservations [3][24][28].
  • Battery rating: EPREL listings rated the Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra and Flip8 batteries at 1,200 charge cycles before falling below 80% capacity, compared with 2,000 cycles for the Fold7 and Flip7 [11].
  • Launch software: US review units of the Fold8 and Fold8 Ultra received first updates labeled F971U1UEU1AZGI and F976U1UEU1AZGI, with roughly 637MB downloads focused on stability and reliability [5].
  • India financing: Samsung introduced 30-month no-cost EMI plans in India with zero down payment, starting at Rs 6,667 for Fold8 Ultra, Rs 6,000 for Fold8 and Rs 4,167 for Flip8 [26].

Perspectives

Samsung mobile design team: Hubert Lee said Samsung expanded the Galaxy Z lineup after finding that some foldable users prioritize productivity and multitasking while others prioritize portability.

Technical Details

  • Camera system: The standard Fold8 omits a telephoto lens, instead using a 50-megapixel wide and a 50-megapixel ultra-wide rear camera, which reviewers noted as a trade-off for the new form factor [4][18].
  • Chip and memory: The Fold8 pairs a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor with 12GB or 16GB of RAM and 256GB, 512GB or 1TB of storage [4].
  • Battery technology: The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra uses a silicon-carbide battery architecture, which Samsung says improves energy density and longevity compared with conventional lithium-ion batteries [20].

Industry Impact

  • Early demand indicators: The Galaxy Z Fold8 is reportedly seeing roughly three times the preorder demand of the Galaxy Z Fold7 [6][17].
  • Retail logistics: Samsung.com showed delayed US shipping for popular Fold8 and Fold8 Ultra configurations, with some base Fold8 variants pushed back to October despite an Aug. 7 availability date [17].

Historical Background

Samsung introduced the original Galaxy Fold in 2019, making foldable phones a commercial category after years of prototypes and trade-show concepts [common].

Business Angle

  • Post-preorder Fold8 sales will show whether demand persists after credits and carrier subsidies expire [16][32][common].
  • The more expensive Fold8 line accounted for 70% of preorders, making component costs and promotional spending important for Samsung’s mobile profitability [3][28][common].
  • Reported battery-cycle downgrades create a potential warranty and customer-satisfaction risk if real-world degradation tracks EPREL’s lower rating [11][common].
  • Financing plans in India lower the monthly ownership hurdle, but installment-driven demand can make full-price consumer acceptance harder to measure [26][common].

Action Items

  • Check delivery dates before ordering: Review the exact color, storage and carrier configuration at checkout because some US Fold8 combinations showed delivery delays extending well beyond the Aug. 7 launch date [17].
  • Compare subsidies against plan costs: For carrier deals, calculate the total service-plan cost and trade-in requirements, not just the headline discount or free-device offer [32][common].
  • Review battery-care settings: If buying a Fold8, Fold8 Ultra or Flip8, consider enabling Samsung’s battery protection settings because EPREL lists the new foldables at 1,200 charge cycles before dropping below 80% capacity [11][common].
