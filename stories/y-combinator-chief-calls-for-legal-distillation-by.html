Y Combinator chief calls for legal distillation by U.S. AI labs

Category: AI

Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan said U.S. regulators should not prohibit American open-weight AI developers from distilling frontier models, arguing that smaller labs should be able to access model outputs through authorized channels and build non-Chinese alternatives [1]. Tan told CNBC, “I would do nothing,” and later clarified that he was not endorsing stolen credentials or concealed access; he wants companies to be allowed to use models through the front door [1]. The proposal contrasts with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei’s call for U.S. regulators to restrict unauthorized distillation, after Anthropic published a second report alleging that Chinese AI labs used concealed identities, fraud and stolen credentials to extract knowledge from its models [1]. Mistral is pursuing sovereign, open-weight AI through enterprise partnerships and a reported €3 billion funding round for computing, research, infrastructure and international operations [4][15].
