Six of seven new Northeast Region fires declared out

Category: Canada

Ontario’s Northeast Region confirmed seven new wildland fires between Oct. 9 and Oct. 12; six have been declared out, while the seventh, Wawa 35, is under observation [1]. The region has two active fires, both under observation [1]. The fires were reported in several locations, including Algonquin Park, Parry Island First Nation and areas near Highway 17 [1]. Wawa 35, confirmed on Oct. 11 northeast of Montreal River Harbour, is the largest of the seven at 6.5 hectares [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Small fires: The six fires declared out ranged from 0.1 to 0.3 hectares each [1].
  • Fire identifiers: The extinguished fires were Sudbury 65 and 66, Haliburton 30, 31 and 32, and Wawa 34 [1].
