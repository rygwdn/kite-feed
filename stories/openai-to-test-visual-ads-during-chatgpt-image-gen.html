OpenAI to test visual ads during ChatGPT image generation this month

Category: Technology

OpenAI will begin testing image-based ads in ChatGPT in the United States later in October. The ads will appear while users generate images, with the trial initially limited to a small group of advertisers. The ads will be clearly labeled and remain separate from generated images; OpenAI says advertising will not affect ChatGPT’s answers [1][9]. The company says the format will let brands show products, ways to use them or related experiences. OpenAI’s safeguards are intended to keep ads out of conversations involving emotional vulnerability, sensitive topics or other unsuitable content. It is also working with DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science on brand-suitability tests that the company says will not give those firms access to real user conversations [1][6][5]. OpenAI is also expanding ad measurement, adding conversion-data and attribution integrations so advertisers can assess whether campaigns lead to purchases. The company says ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion people weekly and describes advertising as a way to support free and lower-cost access. It has not announced when visual ads might expand beyond the US test [1][11][18].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Measurement partners: OpenAI is adding conversion-data integrations with Hightouch, Tealium and LiveRamp, and attribution services including AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch and Kochava [1][5][11].
  • Placement controls: Advertisers can use a feature called Negative Phrases to exclude specified contexts, while DoubleVerify and IAS will assess brand suitability in controlled pilots [6][1].
  • Ad mockups: OpenAI’s examples show either a labeled banner with a product description and “Learn more” button or a horizontally scrollable row of product photos [6].
  • Effectiveness tests: OpenAI is working with Haus, Measured and WorkMagic on geographic experiments to test whether ads generate additional purchases rather than merely claim credit for existing sales [11][15].
  • Plan distinctions: ChatGPT ad availability varies by subscription: reports say Free and Go users can see ads, while Plus, Pro and business plans remain ad-free. OpenAI has not specified which plans will be included in the new visual-ad test [9][18][3].

Perspectives

Donatas Smailys, Billo CEO: Smailys questioned whether ads shown during image creation will perform well, arguing that image generation does not necessarily indicate shopping intent [8].
