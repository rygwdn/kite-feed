Alcohol found at scene as Ontario police investigate fatal Puslinch crash

Category: Canada

Ontario Provincial Police (OPP) are investigating whether alcohol, drugs or speed contributed to a Puslinch crash that killed four teenagers and injured two others; alcohol was found at the scene, but no connection to the collision has been established [1][4]. In the Oct. 31 crash, a vehicle carrying six young people left Watson Road South, struck a guardrail and entered a ravine [1][2]. Those killed were two 19-year-old men, a 17-year-old girl and a 15-year-old girl; the two survivors were girls ages 16 and 17, with the older girl suffering life-altering injuries [1][2]. Police have not determined whether the driver was among those killed, and Wellington County OPP says it is examining evidence to establish what happened before the crash [2][1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Road closure: Watson Road South was to remain closed while crews replaced the damaged guardrail [1].
  • Vehicle description: OPP did not identify the type of vehicle involved [2].
  • Recent collisions: OPP officials cited five children killed in a Mapleton Township crash in June 2026, five deaths in a Norfolk collision in March 2026 and five deaths near London in May 2025 [1].
