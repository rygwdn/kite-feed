Vancouver weighs World Cup legacy before final match

Category: Canada

Vancouver is preparing to host Switzerland and Colombia in a World Cup Round of 16 match at B.C. Place on Tuesday, the seventh and final tournament game scheduled for the city [1][2][3][4]. Fans gathered on Granville Street on Monday before the match, while Colombian supporters planned a banderazo outside the Hyatt Regency Hotel on Burrard Street, where Colombia’s national team was staying [2][3][4]. Vancouver extended Granville Street’s pedestrian zone through the Labour Day weekend after heavy World Cup use. Mayor Ken Sim said the city will explore bringing it back temporarily in November for a Christmas market [2][3]. Coun. Pete Fry voted against the extension, saying the special-meeting process did not allow enough consultation and that the estimated cost had risen to $4.75 million from $3.5 million [2].

Key Points

  • Co-host exits: Belgium beat the United States 4-1 in Seattle, leaving all three 2026 co-host countries eliminated from the tournament [5][13].
  • Tournament scale: The 2026 World Cup expanded to 48 teams, 104 matches and a new Round of 32 before the Round of 16 [7][11].
  • Colombian gathering: Colombian fan group Fiebre Amarilla urged supporters in Vancouver to represent the country well, leave the area clean and sing loudly [4].
  • Next stages: The quarterfinals begin after the Round of 16, with the World Cup final scheduled for July 19 at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey [13][7].
