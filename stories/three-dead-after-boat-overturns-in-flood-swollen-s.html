Three dead after boat overturns in flood-swollen Squamish River

Category: Canada

Three people died and five survived after a boat carrying eight people overturned Thursday on the flood-swollen Squamish River near Squamish, British Columbia, police said Friday [2][5][3]. Investigators believe the boat struck something, but have not established what caused it to overturn or why it was on the river [5][3]. Search crews recovered the third victim Friday after dangerous water and weather delayed the search; a washout on Squamish Valley Road also hindered responders’ access to the remote area [1][3]. Police said all others aboard were accounted for and urged the public to stay off the water because of fast currents and debris [2][5]. The investigation is continuing, and police have not released the victims’ identities [5][3].

  • Flood rescues: A woman whose vehicle became trapped in floodwater on Squamish Valley Road spent more than an hour in waist-high water before a passerby rescued her [7].
  • Evacuation alerts: Pemberton remained under an evacuation alert, while evacuation orders for some Lil’wat Nation properties and Zeballos remained in effect after the storm [3][4].
  • Coastal surge: Environment Canada warned high winds and wave activity could push water above predicted tides in low-lying parts of Metro Vancouver, southeast Vancouver Island and the southern Gulf Islands into the weekend [3][6].
