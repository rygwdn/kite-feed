Bangkok declares disaster across 50 districts after flooding

Category: World

Bangkok authorities declared a disaster across all 50 districts on Saturday, Sept. 26, after nearly 300 millimeters (11.8 inches) of rain had fallen in the Thai capital since Thursday afternoon. The designation allows local organizations to take emergency action. Flooding submerged roads, filled canals and inundated homes and restaurants, with eastern Bangkok among the hardest-hit areas [1][6][17]. At least two people died, and flooding has affected more than 84,000 people in 21 provinces nationwide [8][25][10]. Thailand’s Meteorological Department warned that heavy rain would continue across much of the country through Sunday, Sept. 27 [25][35].

Key Points

  • Emergency support: Bangkok opened shelters for evacuees and made some government facilities available for vehicle parking [25][27].
  • Medical warning: Governor Chadchart Sittipunt urged bedridden patients dependent on life-support equipment to consider temporary hospital stays [59].
  • Weather system: Forecasters linked the continuing rain to a low-pressure system over central Thailand and strengthening southwest monsoon conditions [25][10].

Perspectives

Bangkok Governor Chadchart Sittipunt: He warned that flooding near canals posed a threat to life and property and advised residents in canal-side risk areas to move possessions to higher ground [6].

  • 25 September 2026: The disaster designation initially covered three districts before authorities expanded it citywide [66].

Technical Details

  • Localized rainfall totals: Khlong Sam Wa recorded 190.5 millimeters in 24 hours, while several other eastern districts exceeded 150 millimeters [39].
  • Drainage bottleneck: Canals including Saen Saep, Prawet and Lat Phrao reached capacity; inflows from Nonthaburi and Pathum Thani added water that could not drain away [39].

Historical Background

Nationwide floods in Thailand in 2011 killed more than 500 people and damaged millions of homes [1][6]. Residents interviewed during the current flooding compared the water levels to the 2011 disaster [1].

Travel Advisory

  • Road safety: Authorities warned motorists to avoid more than 20 flood-affected roads, and some routes had deep standing water [38][53]. Check local traffic and emergency notices before traveling, and avoid roads reported as flooded.
