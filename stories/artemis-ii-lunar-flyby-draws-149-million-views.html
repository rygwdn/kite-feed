Artemis II lunar flyby draws 149 million views

Category: Science

NASA’s Artemis II mission drew 149 million views, breaking the agency’s streaming record, Space.com reported, as live coverage tracked the first crewed Orion lunar flyby [3]. In a Live Science interview, Chris White, Artemis II’s lead integrated communications officer, described Mission Control’s work during the April 6 flyby. Orion passed behind the moon, lost contact with Earth for roughly 40 minutes and remained about 250,000 miles, or 400,000 kilometers, from Earth [2]. White’s INCO team could not communicate with Orion while the moon blocked signals, but it operated exterior cameras that captured images during the close lunar pass, including a view of the end of a total solar eclipse behind the moon [2].
