Trump invites Colombia’s Petro to White House meeting

Category: World

President Trump and Colombian President Gustavo Petro held what both sides described as a cordial phone call, after a period of sharp tensions that included President Trump publicly floating the idea of military action against Colombia [4][1][11][19]. Trump said the leaders discussed drugs and “other disagreements,” and he announced that arrangements were being made for Petro to visit the White House in the near future [4][14][6][19]. Colombian officials signaled the call could ease strains and strengthen cooperation on drug trafficking, framing the outreach as a chance to reset day-to-day coordination [3][2]. The call comes amid wider regional friction after a US operation in Venezuela that Petro condemned as a violation of sovereignty, a dispute that had helped drive the latest escalation in rhetoric [1][8][17].

Primary Source: View original article

It was a Great Honor to speak with the President of Colombia, Gustavo Petro, who called to explain the situation of drugs and other disagreements that we have had.

— Donald Trump (South China Morning Post)

Did you know: Petro said he wanted direct communication restored between presidents and foreign ministers, warning that without dialogue, conflict becomes more likely [15].

Key Points

  • Street mobilization: Thousands demonstrated in Colombia as Petro’s government leaned into a message of sovereignty, fueling visible anti-American sentiment even while diplomatic contacts resumed [16][8].
  • ‘First’ conversation: Petro told supporters it was his first direct conversation with Trump since Petro took office in 2022, and he urged reestablishing direct channels between presidents and foreign ministers [15].
  • Dispute specifics: Trump said Petro called to explain “the situation of drugs,” after Trump had accused Petro of “making cocaine and selling it to the United States,” language Colombian officials have rejected [9][5].
  • Energy pitch: Petro said he raised disagreements over Washington’s approach to Latin America and proposed US investment in clean-energy projects in the region rather than a focus on oil [15].
  • Regional backdrop: Le Monde linked Trump’s earlier threats toward Petro to allegations tied to drug trafficking and to the aftermath of US action that removed Venezuela’s Nicolás Maduro, which intensified regional tensions [17].

Perspectives

White House (President Trump): Trump portrayed the call as positive, praising Petro’s “tone” and saying his team was arranging an in-person White House meeting soon after discussions on drugs and other disputes.

Timeline

  • over the weekend: US commandos raided Caracas, abducting Nicolás Maduro and Cilia Flores [15].
  • Wednesday: Trump and Petro held a cordial phone call; meeting planning began [2][6].

Industry Impact

  • Security cooperation: A diplomatic reset could affect operational coordination on counter-narcotics, which Colombian officials say they want to strengthen after the call [3].
  • Energy investment agenda: Petro’s pitch for US-backed clean-energy investment highlights how diplomacy can steer cross-border capital toward renewables versus fossil-fuel projects [15].

Historical Background

Colombia has long partnered with the US on counter-narcotics efforts, but relations can swing sharply when Washington emphasizes enforcement while Colombian leaders stress sovereignty and development priorities [3][8]. In this episode, several outlets trace the most recent rupture to the fallout from a US operation in Venezuela and to Trump’s subsequent threats toward Colombia, which Petro denounced as an affront to Latin American sovereignty [1][17][8].

Q&A

Q: What concrete steps will the two governments take next to restore day-to-day channels?

A: Petro said he asked to reestablish direct communication between foreign ministers and presidents, suggesting a return to regular high-level contacts [15].

Q: What issues besides drugs were included in the leaders’ “other disagreements”?

A: Trump referred broadly to “other disagreements,” while Petro said they have differences over Washington’s vision for US–Latin America relations and he raised clean-energy investment ideas [9][15].

Q: How did Petro describe the US action in Venezuela that helped trigger the dispute?

A: Petro called the US operation in Venezuela an “abhorrent” violation of Latin American sovereignty, according to DW’s account of his reaction [1].
