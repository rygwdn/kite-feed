Quebec man dies after rip current incident in Florida

Category: Canada

A 44-year-old man from Quebec died after he was found in the water off a Florida beach in Volusia County, northeast of Orlando [1][3][4]. Authorities say he was caught in a rip current, and responders attempted life-saving measures before he was later pronounced dead [1]. Canadian readers with loved ones travelling south are being reminded that rip currents can turn routine beach days into emergencies, especially along ocean-facing shores [1][3].
