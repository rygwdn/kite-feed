Snap launches $2,195 standalone Specs AR glasses

Category: Technology

Snap has opened preorders for Specs, a $2,195 pair of standalone augmented-reality glasses that puts computing, apps and digital overlays directly in the wearer’s view without a separate puck, controller or tether. The glasses include a full-color display, navigation, real-time translation, entertainment, fitness and educational experiences; Snap says they are scheduled to ship in the United States, United Kingdom and France later this fall [3][19][15][16]. Snap also introduced Specs Intelligence, an anticipatory AI service that works with Specs, iPhone and Mac. Users can connect external accounts and apps so the system can organize information related to meetings, travel, tasks and routines. Snap says users control which services they connect and that personal content will neither train its AI models nor support personalized advertising [4][19]. Reviewers found the glasses capable but large and expensive; CEO Evan Spiegel described them as a session-based computer rather than an all-day replacement for ordinary eyewear [11].

I’d still think of them today as a computer, and it’s going to be more session-based

— Evan Spiegel (CNET)

Did you know: Snap’s Specs can display streamed content as a virtual screen described by the company as equivalent to a 115-inch cinema screen [15].

Key Points

  • Cellular option: A charging case with built-in cellular connectivity costs an additional $200. Verizon customers pay $10 monthly, while non-Verizon customers pay $20; Orange and EE will offer plans in France and the UK [16].
  • AR content: Launch experiences include HBO Max, YouTube, Spotify, Shopify 3D shopping, Apollo 11 education and NBA and WNBA basketball training tools [9][19].
  • Privacy design: Snap says Specs does not use facial recognition or record continuously, while its AI service uses on-device processing and secure cloud infrastructure [15][19].

Perspectives

Snap: The company is positioning Specs around a dedicated operating system and third-party applications rather than as a Snapchat accessory [5][4].

  • 16 September 2026: Snap detailed Specs software, AI service and launch partnerships [4][19].
  • 16 September 2026: Preorders opened, with shipments planned for later this fall [16][19].

Technical Details

  • Display field of view: Specs has a 51-degree field of view [1][3].

Historical Background

Snap introduced its first Spectacles nearly a decade ago and has developed AR-enabled frames for about five years [3][6].

Business Angle

  • Enterprise support could give Snap a route to higher-value deployments in productivity and training, where the hardware’s price may be less decisive than in consumer retail [15][18].
  • Carrier partnerships with Verizon, Orange and EE give Snap a distribution route for connectivity services alongside the hardware [16].
  • Third-party applications such as HBO Max, Spotify, Shopify and sports-training tools are important to proving the platform has uses beyond Snapchat [9][8].

Action Items

  • Check cellular costs: Compare the $200 case surcharge and Verizon’s monthly rates with a Wi-Fi-only setup before placing a preorder [16].
