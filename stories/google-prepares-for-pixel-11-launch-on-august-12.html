Google prepares for Pixel 11 launch on August 12

Category: Technology

Google is expected to unveil the Pixel 11 series at its Made by Google event in New York on August 12. Reports point to the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold, along with the Pixel Watch 5 and possibly a Bluetooth tracker for Google's Find Hub network [3][11][12]. ZDNET says the event stream is scheduled for August 12 at 3 p.m. ET, after product announcements earlier in the day [3]. Pre-launch reports describe the Pixel 11 line as an incremental update, with expected processor, camera and storage changes rather than a major redesign [3][1]. Several reports say Google may drop 128GB configurations, move the base Pixel 11 model to 256GB storage, raise prices on higher-end versions and expand AI features [2][3][10]. Pixel-focused publications have cited reliability and usability complaints from recent models, including battery life concerns raised by a Digital Trends writer after nearly a year with the Pixel 10 Pro [7]. Android Police wrote that the Pixel 11 may not address every complaint at launch [4].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: A Chinese long-term Pixel 10 Pro review noted that the U.S. model removed the physical SIM slot, while other regional versions kept a slot and used that space differently [9].

Key Points

  • Storage shift: Firstpost says the Pixel 11 lineup could drop new 128GB variants and add 16GB RAM to some models [2].
  • Design cycle: 9to5Google cites Google's earlier statement that Pixel designs will change every two to three years as context for another year of similar hardware design [1].

Perspectives

9to5Google: Ben Schoon wrote that the Pixel 11 looks like a hard sell for enthusiasts because leaks point to a similar design, few major new features, some minor downgrades and a price increase.

Sources: 9to5Google

Industry Impact

  • Premium Android phones: ZDNET says the Pixel 11 launch follows Samsung's recent foldable releases, putting Google's Pixel 11 Pro Fold in the premium Android segment [3].
  • Bluetooth trackers: A potential Google tracker with Find Hub support would place Google more directly against Apple's AirTag in consumer item-tracking hardware [3].
  • Android distribution: Firstpost says the Pixel 11 cycle may coincide with Android's move toward tighter app controls, which could affect developers and users who rely on less restricted installation paths [2].

Historical Background

Google shifted the Pixel line to its in-house Tensor chips with the Pixel 6 generation, a change that also introduced the modern camera-bar design language [9]. The company moved its Made by Google phone launch earlier in the year in 2025, continuing a summer schedule for the Pixel 11 cycle [9].

Action Items

  • Watch the launch stream: Tune in to Made by Google on August 12 at 3 p.m. ET, when ZDNET says Google's event stream begins [3].
  • Check storage needs before upgrading: If you normally buy the cheapest model, compare the expected 256GB base configuration and reported $899 starting price against your current phone's storage use [3][2].
