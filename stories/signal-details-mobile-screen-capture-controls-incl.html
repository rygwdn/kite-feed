Signal details mobile screen-capture controls, including timed opt-outs

Category: Technology

Signal has detailed mobile controls designed to stop operating-system AI agents from reading chats displayed in its encrypted messaging app. The protections are available on iPhones running iOS 27 or later with Signal 8.37 or later, and on Android with Signal 8.35.1 or later [8]. They address the risk that agents can inspect a conversation after it has been decrypted for display, beyond Signal’s control [12][5]. On iPhone, users can turn off the protection or pause it for 10 minutes; on Android, Signal activates Screen security when it detects an assistant trying to read the screen [8][13]. Signal says these screen-capture measures do not block other routes to on-device data, and it cannot reliably provide the same protection on macOS [1][8].

Primary Source: View original article

In order to gain access to information about you, these OS AI agents 'scan' the information that's on your screen.

— Meredith Whittaker, president of the Signal Foundation (The Hacker News)

Key Points

  • Developer requests: Signal wants Apple, Google and Microsoft to offer a documented opt-out covering screen reading, file access, notifications, APIs and other operating-system pathways [1][2].
  • Agent permissions: Meta’s Muse agent accessed Signal on Android through notification permissions and on a Mac through accessibility and screenshot tools, according to reported examples [1][7].
  • Android override: Signal says it will restore protection after 90 days if an Android user disables it through a Screen security prompt; disabling it directly in Privacy settings does not trigger reminders [8].

Perspectives

Signal: The company said it is discussing with Apple and Google how to make their systems work better with privacy-sensitive apps [2].

Sources: Coywolf

Meta: Meta said agents need to see and interact with users’ computers to act on their behalf, and that Muse accesses other apps only through permissions users explicitly grant [7].

Sources: The Economic Times

Timeline

  • 21 May 2025: Signal enabled default Windows 11 screen protection against Recall [8]
  • October 2026: Apple added an Excluded Apps option in iOS 27.2 [1]
  • 20 November 2026: Signal announced default mobile screen-capture protections [2][8]

Technical Details

  • Android detection limits: Signal says differences among Android manufacturers make attempted screen capture difficult to detect consistently, so its protection activates only when it recognizes an AI assistant reading the display [8][12].

Action Items

  • Exclude Signal from Siri access on a Mac: Open System Settings, go to Siri, and add Signal to “Excluded Apps,” as Signal recommends for macOS [4].
