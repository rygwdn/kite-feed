Trump pulls Vance from Iran talks, says deal no longer needed

Category: World

President Donald Trump said Nov. 12 that the United States no longer needed a deal with Iran and directed Vice President JD Vance to focus instead on alleged crime and corruption in Democratic-run cities and states. Vance had renewed efforts to end the eight-month war, with reported plans for direct talks with a senior Iranian security official [3][5][31]. Trump had earlier signaled that diplomacy could continue after the Nov. 3 midterm elections. His administration has said sanctions and a U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports are pressuring Tehran; Trump has argued those measures make both further strikes and a deal unnecessary. A proposed interim arrangement would reopen shipping through the Strait of Hormuz in return for lifting the blockade and easing restrictions on Iranian oil and frozen funds, but the sides remain apart over nuclear inspections, Iran’s enriched uranium and the order in which terms would take effect [3][24]. Vance had blamed the war’s costs for Republicans’ election losses, while Trump has defended the campaign as necessary to prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon. Iran’s parliament speaker and chief negotiator, Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, said Tehran was awaiting a formal U.S. response to its proposal. Iran’s military reported explosions affecting four tankers in the strait [5][9][4].

  • Market reaction: Oil prices rose and U.S. stock futures fell after Trump’s announcement [22][33].
