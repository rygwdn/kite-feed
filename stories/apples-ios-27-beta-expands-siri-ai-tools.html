Apple’s iOS 27 beta expands Siri AI tools

Category: Technology

Apple’s first iOS 27 developer beta is in testing after WWDC26, where Apple highlighted Siri AI and Apple Intelligence and announced upgrades to Wallet, Find My, Maps, Calendar, Reminders and dictation [4][2][7]. TechCrunch reported that iOS 27 adds Apple Wallet receipt scanning for itemized bill splitting through Apple Cash, custom-duration location sharing in Find My, refreshed Flyover visuals and Local Lists in Apple Maps [4]. Digital Trends reported that iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPhone Air users on the iOS 27 beta can enable Advanced Dictation Preview, which runs on-device using Apple’s AFM Core Advanced model. The feature is off by default because Apple is gathering opt-in feedback during beta testing [1]. Huxiu reported that macOS 27 Golden Gate moves Mac support fully to Apple Silicon, makes this release the last full macOS version with complete Rosetta 2 support, and remains a developer preview not recommended for production machines [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Calendar input: Calendar can identify people, dates and places from typed natural language, but users must tap date suggestions for some entries instead of relying on full automatic parsing [7].
  • Reminder metadata: Reminders can autofill date, time and location from phrases such as “get the groceries at 6pm tonight,” though MacRumors said the beta behavior is inconsistent [7].
  • Mirroring changes: macOS 27 iPhone Mirroring adds multiple aspect ratios, Control Center access through Command-4 or the View menu, and DRM-protected video playback in the mirrored window [10].
  • Performance claim: Tom’s Guide tested Apple’s claim that iOS 27 loads apps up to 30% faster than iOS 26 in a June 21 hands-on review [11].
  • Mac display support: Huxiu said macOS 27 improves ultrawide display handling with support for higher specifications such as 5K at 120Hz and preserves display arrangements after reconnection [6].

Perspectives

Digital Trends: Rachit Agarwal said Advanced Dictation Preview is better than the standard dictation feature but easy to miss because Apple left it disabled by default in Settings during beta testing.

Sources: Digital Trends

Cult of Mac podcast: Leander Kahney and D. Griffin Jones described the first iOS 27 developer beta as a mixed hands-on experience, saying Siri AI seemed useful while the beta remained rough around the edges.

Sources: Cult of Mac

Huxiu reported that macOS 27 refines Liquid Glass visuals with a user-adjustable transparency slider in Settings > Appearance, and that window corners are less rounded than in macOS 26.

Sources: Huxiu

Tom’s Guide: John Velasco said his Siri AI review followed a week of daily use with the iOS 27 developer beta and listed practical advantages and drawbacks.

Sources: Tom’s Guide

Industry Impact

  • App developers: Third-party developers must update apps to use iPhone Mirroring’s new aspect-ratio behavior, while current adaptive layouts apply to iOS 27-compatible apps [10][6].
  • Mac software vendors: With macOS 28 set to limit Rosetta 2 to Intel-architecture games, developers without Apple Silicon-native apps must complete migration before the macOS 27 cycle ends [6].
  • Local discovery apps: TechCrunch said Apple Maps Local Lists could compete with third-party local-exploration services such as Google Maps, Instagram and TikTok by keeping restaurant and attraction recommendations inside Maps [4].

Historical Background

Apple began shifting the Mac from Intel processors to Apple Silicon in 2020 [common]. Huxiu reported that Apple had stated at WWDC25 that macOS 26 would be the final major macOS release supporting Intel Macs [6].

Business Angle

  • Apple’s Wallet, Maps and Find My changes add more everyday tasks to Apple’s own apps, including receipt scanning, Local Lists and custom-duration location sharing [4][common].

Action Items

  • Enable Advanced Dictation Preview: On a supported iPhone running the iOS 27 beta, open Settings > General > Keyboards and turn on Advanced Dictation Preview [1].
  • Avoid production Mac upgrades: If testing macOS 27, use a separate APFS volume, dual-system setup or external drive instead of upgrading your main work machine [6].
