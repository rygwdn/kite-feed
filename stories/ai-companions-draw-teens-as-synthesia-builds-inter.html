AI companions draw teens as Synthesia builds interactive avatars

Category: AI

NewsNation describes chatbot safety as a test as teenagers turn to bots for companionship, while The Wall Street Journal reports that middle-schoolers are seeking AI friends rather than human ones [2][3]. The articles provide no figures on how many young people use the bots or details of specific safety incidents. Separately, a TechCrunch reporter tried an interactive avatar of Synthesia executive Alexandru Voica that was designed to answer common press questions about the company [1]. The reporter created a personal avatar, and Synthesia asked at the opening of its New York office whether the reporter wanted one [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Company valuation: Synthesia reached a $4 billion valuation earlier in 2026, according to the TechCrunch article [1].
  • Annual revenue: Synthesia said it had passed $100 million in annual recurring revenue in 2025 [1].
  • Workplace training: Synthesia’s Roleplay Sessions product lets employees practice scenarios such as sales pitches with an AI avatar that responds and scores their answers [1].

Perspectives

TechCrunch reporter: The reporter called the interactive avatar the “final boss” of using AI in PR, contrasting it with AI-generated text pitches [1].
