Canadian Conservatives propose diesel tax cuts through Canada Day

Category: Canada

Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre proposed cutting federal taxes on diesel sales until at least July 1, 2027, by extending the full federal fuel excise-tax suspension and eliminating the GST on diesel purchases. He said Canada produces enough diesel to meet domestic demand, but transportation bottlenecks leave some regions reliant on U.S. imports [5][3][2]. Poilievre also called for permanently scrapping the federal industrial carbon tax and Clean Fuel Standard, arguing that they raise diesel production costs and discourage refinery investment. He outlined the proposal at an Ottawa-area gas station on Sept. 27 and said it would support refinery expansion and infrastructure to transport and store fuel [5][2][1].
