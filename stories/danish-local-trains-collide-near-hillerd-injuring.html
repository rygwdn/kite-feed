Danish local trains collide near Hillerød, injuring 18

Category: World

Two local passenger trains collided head-on early Thursday near Hillerød, north of Copenhagen, injuring 18 people. Five were reported in critical condition, according to emergency services and multiple news outlets [1][2][3][4][7][8][10]. Responders described heavy front-end damage and shattered glass inside the carriages [1][7]. Authorities said the cause was still unclear [2][7]. Emergency crews launched a large response, including helicopters to transport some of the injured, and police said all passengers were evacuated from the trains [6][4][8].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: A 2019 train crash in Denmark killed eight people and injured 16 [6].

Key Points

  • Emergency response: The alert went out at about 6:30 a.m., and authorities sent large numbers of police, fire crews, and medical teams to the crash scene [6][8].
  • Inside carriages: Rescue leaders described the train interiors as "chaotic," saying the head-on impact caused extensive damage and sent broken glass through the passenger areas [1].
  • Community use: Gribskov mayor Trine Egetved said local residents, workers, and students rely heavily on the line, underscoring the disruption to daily travel in the area [6].

Perspectives

Police: Police called the collision a serious accident, sealed off the area, and said emergency services had deployed in large numbers as crews secured the scene.
