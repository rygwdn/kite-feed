B.C. teachers ratify four-year deal with 3% raises

Category: Canada

British Columbia teachers have ratified a four-year collective agreement with the province, according to the B.C. Teachers’ Federation and the provincial government [1][2]. The contract includes 3% annual wage increases [1][3]. Both the union and the government said the settled contract should help keep schools focused on learning and the day-to-day supports students and families rely on [3][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Ratification result: The BCTF said 91% voted “yes” to approve the agreement [1][2].
  • Who it covers: The union said the deal applies to about 52,000 educators in B.C. [1].
  • How some outlets describe the wage language: Global News called the 3% annual increases a 12% wage boost over four years [4].
  • Status update: The province said the agreement is ratified and in effect following the vote [2].

Perspectives

BCTF union president: Union leadership said parents should see better supports for their school-aged children now that the agreement is ratified [3].

Sources: Toronto Star

B.C. government: The province cast the vote as the final step, saying the collective agreement is ratified and now in effect [2].
