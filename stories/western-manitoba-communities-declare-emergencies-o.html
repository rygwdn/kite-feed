Western Manitoba communities declare emergencies over rising rivers

Category: Canada

Brandon, Man., Sioux Valley Dakota Nation and Minnedosa have declared states of emergency after heavy rain pushed river levels higher across western Manitoba [2][4][5][3]. Brandon officials said provincial forecasts show the Assiniboine and Shellmouth river basins will keep rising this week, with levels expected to peak near those recorded during the 2014 flood [2]. Brandon Mayor Jeff Fawcett said the declaration gives the city authority to take protective steps and access support. No evacuation orders are in place [2]. The Assiniboine River was already spilling its banks in Brandon ahead of a forecast July 12 peak, and the city was raising some dikes to two feet above the forecast peak water level [5]. Sioux Valley Dakota Nation, about 40 kilometres west of Brandon, declared its state of emergency Saturday and said nearly 190 residents in flood-prone areas should prepare to leave if needed [4][1]. Chief Jennifer Bone said the community was monitoring water levels and weather forecasts, staying in contact with provincial and federal governments, and preparing for an expected crest on July 9 [4][1].
