Canada launches consultations for first men’s health strategy

Category: Canada

The federal government has launched a national consultation to hear from Canadians about men and boys’ health. It includes a countrywide survey and broader outreach under the #HealthyMen initiative [1][3][5][4]. Health Canada says the feedback will help shape Canada’s first Men and Boys’ Health Strategy, which the government expects to release later in 2026 [1]. Coverage of the launch has put mental health and stigma front and centre. The consultation is being framed as a practical chance to spot gaps and gather ideas, so future programs and services can better meet people where they are—across communities and life stages [1][3][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • How to weigh in: Ottawa is asking Canadians to fill out a countrywide survey as part of the consultation process [3][5].
  • What stood out at the launch: Speakers emphasized mental health and stigma, including remarks on why some men hesitate to seek support [2].
  • How it’s being presented: Health Canada is framing #HealthyMen as an ongoing “national conversation,” not just a one-time questionnaire [1].
  • What the minister said: The federal health minister struck an urgent tone—saying Canada needs to act—while urging people to share their feedback [5].
