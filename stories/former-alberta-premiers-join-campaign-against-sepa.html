Former Alberta premiers join campaign against separation

Category: Canada

Former Alberta premiers Rachel Notley and Jason Kenney, longtime political opponents, will co-chair a Canadian Chamber of Commerce campaign urging Alberta to remain in Canada [1][2][3][4][5][7]. The chamber calls the campaign Canada and Alberta: Stronger Together and is launching it ahead of an Oct. 19 referendum on whether Albertans want the province to stay in Canada or support a second binding referendum on leaving the country [4][5][7]. The chamber said the campaign will make an economic argument for national unity, saying uncertainty over constitutional or political separation risks investor confidence, economic growth and Canada’s competitiveness during an escalating trade war with the United States [4][5]. Notley said the debate distracts from building Alberta, creating jobs, strengthening health care and protecting provincial programs, while Kenney said Alberta’s role is central to Canada’s success [4][7].
