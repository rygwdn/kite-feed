Meteor fireball streaks across Great Lakes during Indiana drone show

Category: Science

A bright meteor flashed across the sky over the Great Lakes region Wednesday. The light was visible over eastern Michigan around 7:40 p.m., with sightings reported in neighboring states [2]. In Fort Wayne, Indiana, it crossed the sky during the city’s Thanksgiving Eve Night of Lights drone display, where spectators captured it on video [3][4]. Michigan meteorologist Blake Hansen identified the light as a meteor, and a NASA satellite detected it as though it were a burst of lightning, according to WZZM [2]. A separate local report described a bright green fireball over Northeast Ohio, while Cleveland television station WKYC also published footage of what it identified as a fireball meteor [1][5].
