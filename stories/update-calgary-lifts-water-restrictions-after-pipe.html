Update: Calgary lifts water restrictions after pipe tests pass

Category: Canada

Calgary has lifted all water-use restrictions for the city and surrounding communities after officials said testing showed a ruptured water main has been successfully repaired and is stable [3][4][6]. The restrictions had limited everyday activities like flushing and longer showers for residents while crews worked on the line [1][2]. Officials framed the news as a welcome milestone, while also emphasizing that more infrastructure work remains ahead [3][6].

Key Points

  • Supply importance: The repaired pipe is described as supplying about 60% of Calgary’s treated water, which is why the rupture triggered wide-reaching conservation measures [6].
  • Replacement ahead: Mayor Jeromy Farkas said the city still needs to replace the entire line even though the immediate repair tested successfully [3][6].
  • Repair progress: CTV reported the city has “completed two phases” of what it called a catastrophic water-main repair, with the overall work nearing completion [5].
  • Holiday disruption: A Globe and Mail photo caption notes emergency crews checked damage near the Trans-Canada Highway after the break on Dec. 31, 2025 [6].
