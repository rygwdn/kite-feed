Trump floats Kim meeting in China as Xi summit nears

Category: World

President Donald Trump said on Nov. 16 that he might meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un during his China visit this week, adding that Kim’s attendance depended on Chinese President Xi Jinping. No meeting has been confirmed [4][22][5]. The comments came after a White House official said there were no plans for talks. North Korea has previously signaled it sees no need to meet with Trump [24][4]. Trump is due to meet Xi and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday, Nov. 19, for talks expected to address nuclear weapons. Trump said he also wants to discuss Iran, Ukraine, trade and the economy, although U.S. officials had earlier indicated Ukraine would not be on the agenda [39][6][43]. Whether Kim will join the gathering remains uncertain. Trump and Kim held three meetings during Trump’s first term, but those encounters did not secure North Korean denuclearization. Analysts and U.S. officials have cautioned against expecting a substantive agreement from the planned trilateral talks [4][12].

Key Points

  • Russian outreach: Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Putin could also meet Kim if the North Korean leader attends the APEC summit; Putin met a delegation from North Korea’s ruling Workers’ Party on Nov. 16 [28][27].
  • Seoul’s position: South Korea’s foreign ministry said Seoul and Washington were in close contact and that South Korea was ready to assist dialogue between the United States and North Korea [24].
  • Troop deployment: Ukrainian and South Korean estimates put North Korea’s troop contribution to Russia’s war at about 14,000 to 15,000 since 2024, with most deployed in Russia’s Kursk region [4].

Perspectives

Edgard Kagan, former U.S. ambassador to Malaysia: Kagan said China does not want to join a trilateral nuclear-arms framework because it could be treated as a subordinate participant and face expectations of future negotiations [37].

Ukraine: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy warned against a Yalta-style redistribution of influence without affected countries at the table, while Foreign Minister Andriy Sybiha said the meeting could encourage Russia to continue its war [43][30].

Timeline

  • February 2019: Trump and Kim met in Hanoi but failed to reach agreement [4]
  • June 2019: Trump met Kim at the inter-Korean border in Panmunjom [13]
  • 16 November 2026: Trump raised the possibility of meeting Kim during his China visit [22]
  • 19 November 2026: Trump, Xi and Putin are scheduled to meet in Shenzhen [6][39]

International Reactions

  • 🇰🇷 South Korea: A foreign ministry official said it was difficult to predict whether or when a Trump-Kim meeting would take place [24].
  • 🇷🇺 Russia: Putin said Russia would not forget the heroism of North Korean fighters who fought alongside Russian servicemen [28].

Q&A

Q: Will North Korea’s nuclear program feature in the Trump-Xi-Putin talks?

A: U.S. experts predicted the issue would not feature prominently. The White House had not responded to a Yonhap inquiry about whether North Korea would be discussed [13][22].
