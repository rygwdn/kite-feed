Barrie mother charged with first-degree murder of son

Category: Canada

Police in Barrie, Ont., have charged a 47-year-old woman with first-degree murder in the death of her 10-year-old son after officers found the child during a wellness check on New Year’s Eve [1][3][4]. Barrie police said officers arrived around 3 p.m., found the woman inside the home, and took her to hospital [1]. Investigators laid the charge after she was medically cleared, and she was then remanded into custody, according to reporting cited by the National Post [1]. Police say the Major Crime Unit and Forensic Identification Unit continue to investigate, while limiting further updates to protect the family’s privacy [1].
