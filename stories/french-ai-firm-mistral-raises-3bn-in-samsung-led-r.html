French AI firm Mistral raises €3bn in Samsung-led round

Category: AI

French artificial intelligence company Mistral said on Sept. 8 that it raised €3 billion in a Series D round led by Samsung Electronics, giving the Paris-based company a post-money valuation of more than €21 billion, or about $24 billion [6][4][5][60]. Mistral described the financing as the largest equity fundraising round ever completed by a European technology company, three years after its launch [6][46][25]. The round included co-leads Scaleup Europe Fund, managed by EQT, and existing investor PSG Equity, while new backers included Advent, funds and accounts managed by BlackRock, and Luxembourg as a state investor; existing investors ASML, Nvidia, Andreessen Horowitz, Bpifrance, General Catalyst and others also participated [6][40][54][64]. Mistral said the money will expand frontier research, compute capacity, infrastructure, products, commercial growth and its international footprint [6][8][55]. Mistral is pitching customers on "sovereign" and open-weight AI, saying organizations and governments want performance while keeping control over infrastructure, data governance and deployment choices [6][5][60]. Chief executive Arthur Mensch told CNBC the company plans to build more infrastructure, including its own data centers, rent additional compute capacity and train "bigger and faster models" [60][66].

Key Points

  • Valuation jump: Mistral's valuation rose from €11.7 billion after a September 2025 round to more than €21 billion after the new financing [25][60][58].
  • Industrial backers: The last two rounds were led by major hardware manufacturers, with ASML leading the 2025 Series C and Samsung leading the latest Series D [25][47][1].
  • Compute strategy: Mensch told CNBC that Mistral aims to roughly double the amount of compute it owns over the next five years while using leased capacity during the buildout [60][66][35].
  • Commercial base: Mistral says it operates in 20 countries and supports more than 125 global enterprises, including Airbus, ASML and HSBC [6][54][55].

Industry Impact

  • Public capital: The Scaleup Europe Fund, a roughly €5 billion European Commission-backed vehicle managed by EQT, co-led the round, marking the first time a publicly backed EU fund has taken a lead role in a private European tech financing of this size [25][51].

Historical Background

Mistral was founded in France in 2023 by Arthur Mensch, who had worked at Google DeepMind, and former Meta researchers Timothée Lacroix and Guillaume Lample [1][55]. Its previous major financing was a €1.7 billion Series C in September 2025 at an €11.7 billion valuation, led by ASML [25]. ASML invested €1.3 billion in that round and took an 11% stake, according to Etoday [1].

Business Angle

  • The valuation has nearly doubled in a year, so future investors need evidence of durable revenue growth and margins, not only strategic backing [25][60][common].
  • Mistral's data-center push could deepen customer control but may also increase capital intensity, a risk for long-term free cash flow [60][66][common].
  • Samsung and ASML's participation links Mistral to chip and infrastructure supply chains, but the provided sources do not report binding procurement volumes [6][55].
  • The enterprise-sovereignty positioning targets customers with compliance and data-control needs, which can support stickier contracts if Mistral proves performance and reliability [6][common].
