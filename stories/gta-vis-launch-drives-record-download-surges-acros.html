GTA VI’s launch drives record download surges across networks

Category: Technology

The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI sent gaming and internet traffic surging: Cloudflare recorded UK PlayStation 5 downloads peaking at 8.6 times normal levels, while traffic to Rockstar’s online services reached 11.2 times normal in Australia and 9.3 times normal on the U.S. East Coast [9]. Virgin Media O2 also said the release broke its evening traffic record as players installed the game [9]. Rockstar Games released the game worldwide on Nov. 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version announced. Its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, estimated worldwide first-day retail sales at $3.79 billion and said players had bought 32.5 million copies before launch [8][1][7]. More than 1,600 Rockstar workers in the United Kingdom are voting on union recognition from Nov. 12 to Nov. 26 [13][12]. The vote follows Rockstar’s dismissal of 31 developers in 2025; the company says they shared confidential information, and the dispute is before an employment tribunal [12].

It says a lot about the industry that even my colleagues and I, working on the biggest game of all time, are worried about getting laid off. But the truth is that workers across the sector are helpless to resist the waves of redundancies crashing around us, until we get organised.

— Nestor Uriarte, Rockstar UK employee involved in union organizing (Gizmodo)

Did you know: The game’s radio programming includes nearly 750 songs spanning nine decades across 20 in-game stations [31].

Key Points

  • Preload period: PlayStation and Rockstar let players download the game nine days before launch so their consoles would be ready at release [17][19].
  • Platform split: Sensor Tower estimated that about three-quarters of the game’s sales were on PlayStation 5, with the rest on Xbox [9].
  • Multiplayer: Rockstar has not given a date for the multiplayer expansion, which could let players interact and go on missions together online [9].

Perspectives

Rockstar leadership: Publishing head Jennifer Kolbe told Bloomberg that the company would recognize a union if staff voted for one [12].

Industry Impact

  • Other single-player games: Sensor Tower estimated that Grand Theft Auto VI could reduce the time players spend on other single-player titles by 10% [9].
