Observatories release new images of cosmic structures

Category: Science

NASA released a James Webb Space Telescope image of the young galaxy cluster MACS J0553.4-3342, in the constellation Columba, as its Picture of the Month [1]. NASA said the July 3, 2026, image shows a large-scale cosmic “building site” and credited it to ESA/Webb, NASA & CSA, and S. Fujimoto [1]. Separately, astronomy image features highlighted ground-based views from Chile. Space.com featured a laser guide star beam from Gemini South Observatory on Cerro Pachón pointing into the Milky Way, with credit to the International Gemini Observatory/NOIRLab/NSF/AURA/P. Horálek [2][4]. Phys.org reported that the 570-megapixel Dark Energy Camera captured a star-rich scene containing the nebula NGC 6729 and the globular cluster NGC 6723; DECam is mounted on the U.S. National Science Foundation Víctor M. Blanco 4-meter Telescope at Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory in Chile, a program of NSF NOIRLab [5][3].
