Indonesia earthquake kills at least 54 on Flores

Category: World

A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck near Flores island in Indonesia’s East Nusa Tenggara province shortly before 6 a.m. local time Saturday, killing at least 54 people, injuring 135 and damaging more than 1,300 homes and other buildings, according to Associated Press reporting and Indonesia’s National Disaster Management Agency [6][14]. The U.S. Geological Survey put the quake at a depth of 10 kilometers, and authorities lifted an initial tsunami warning after monitoring showed no significant sea-level changes [2][6]. Rescuers searched collapsed buildings and landslide-hit areas Monday as many communities waited for aid on Indonesia’s 81st Independence Day [6][10]. A later BNPB infographic cited by Mothership listed at least 54 deaths, 199 injuries, 9,963 displaced people and 2,403 damaged homes; AFP, citing BNPB spokesperson Berton Suar Pelita Panjaitan, separately reported that 12,800 people could not return home by Monday morning [61][3][5][39].

Key Points

  • Aftershock threat: Authorities recorded at least 995 aftershocks by Sunday, 46 of them felt by residents, while a magnitude 5.9 aftershock struck Flores on Monday at a depth of 10 kilometers [2][4][61].
  • Blocked access: Search teams focused on Manggarai, East Manggarai and Nagekeo after landslides blocked access [1][7][24].
  • Emergency response: The provincial governor declared a 14-day state of emergency Sunday, and Indonesia’s Air Force began airlifting 13 tonnes of emergency aid aboard two Hercules transport planes and a Boeing [6][5][41].
  • Medical needs: Health officials said emergency field hospitals were being prepared and neurosurgeons and orthopedic specialists were urgently needed for patients with head injuries and broken bones [20].

Perspectives

Junaidin, Mbay resident: Junaidin told AFP that people in his tent returned home only to collect water, clothes and pillows. He criticized delays in government aid while national celebrations continued.

Industry Impact

  • Fuel access: State energy firm Pertamina said power outages left 20 petrol stations inoperative after the quake [44][35].
  • Tourism gateway: Aftershocks shook homes in Labuan Bajo, the gateway to Komodo National Park, disrupting tourism [1][61].

Historical Background

Flores was struck by another magnitude 7.7 earthquake in 1992 that triggered a tsunami and killed about 2,500 people, a disaster several survivors cited when explaining why they were afraid to return indoors [16][39][63]. Indonesia also suffered a 2004 magnitude 9.1 earthquake off Sumatra that generated an Indian Ocean tsunami, killing about 170,000 people in Indonesia and about 50,000 in neighboring countries [63].

Humanitarian Impact

Survivors in cut-off communities needed tents, food, medicines, blankets, beds, sleeping mats, drinking water and generators, while residents also requested diapers for children [40][16][63]. Some evacuees slept on hillsides or under tarpaulins because aftershocks and cracked homes made indoor shelter unsafe [16][39].
