Unions say nearly 10,000 federal workers get notices

Category: Canada

Canadian public-sector unions say close to 10,000 federal employees received workforce adjustment notices in the past week, signalling their positions could be cut across departments including Global Affairs Canada [1][2][3]. The notices are warnings rather than immediate layoffs, but they raise near-term uncertainty for affected workers and teams [2][3]. Unions are pressing the federal government for clearer, department-by-department disclosure so employees and the public can understand the scope and rationale of the reductions [1][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Union breakdown: The Public Service Alliance of Canada said more than 5,000 of its members received workforce adjustment notices this week [2].
  • Transparency push: Union leaders argue the running tally lacks detail and have called for a clearer breakdown of which roles and units are being affected [1].
  • Rising count: As more departments report impacts, unions say the number of workers flagged by notices has continued to climb over the week [1][3].
  • Departments named: Reporting says notices reached staff in multiple parts of the federal public service, including Global Affairs [1].
