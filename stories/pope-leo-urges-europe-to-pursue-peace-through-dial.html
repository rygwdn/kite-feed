Pope Leo urges Europe to pursue peace through dialogue

Category: World

Pope Leo XIV concluded a four-day visit to France in Metz on Sept. 28, urging European leaders to use patient dialogue, negotiation and compromise to counter war and what he called a “desire for domination.” He said Europe’s postwar experience showed that competing interests could be handled through dialogue, and called for the defense of international law and multilateralism. He did not name Russia or Ukraine, though coverage linked his remarks to the war in Ukraine [9][31][13][20]. Speaking at an event honoring Robert Schuman, one of the architects of European integration, Leo said Europe should draw on its Christian heritage while engaging with people of other cultures. He urged European countries to help migrants enter safely and legally and integrate into their communities [11][21][65]. On the visit’s final day, he met religious leaders in Metz and celebrated Mass at the city’s Cathedral of St. Stephen before returning to Rome [11][21][48].
