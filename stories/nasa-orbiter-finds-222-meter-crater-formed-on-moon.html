NASA orbiter finds 222-meter crater formed on Moon in 2024

Category: Science

Scientists using images from NASA’s Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter have identified McGetchin crater, a fresh lunar impact crater formed when a building-sized meteorite struck the surface in 2024. The crater measures about 222 meters across and 43 meters deep, making it more than two football fields wide. Researchers describe it as the largest recent impact crater observed on the Moon since at least 2009 [2][1][3][4]. Researchers named the structure for geologist and lunar scientist Tom McGetchin. They estimate that an impact large enough to create it occurs roughly once every 130 years and say the discovery could inform assessments of impact hazards for planned lunar activity [2][4]. The crater was identified in orbiter photographs, including an image taken Dec. 5, 2025 [2].
