Sony revives WH-1000XM4 headphones as $300 XM4C

Category: Technology

Sony introduced the WH-1000XM4C, also called the M4 2nd Gen, as a refreshed version of its WH-1000XM4 wireless noise-canceling headphones, which launched in 2020 [1][4][7][10]. The new model keeps the older foldable over-ear design and its place in the 1000X line, launching at about $299 to $300 in the U.S., below Sony’s current WH-1000XM6 flagship, which CNET and 9to5Google list at about $459 to $460 [1][2][7][10]. Sony also announced the WH-CH730N and WH-CH530 as lower-priced over-ear options, with U.S. prices reported at $179 to $180 and $69 to $80, depending on the publication and model listing [2][7][10]. Sony described the XM4C as an update to a still-popular older design, with sources citing the original XM4’s compact foldability, strong customer reception and continued use after two successor generations [3][6][7][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Updated sound tuning designed for today’s listening preferences

— Sony (CNET)

Key Points

  • Battery trade-off: Battery life is rated at 27 hours with ANC and 34 hours without it [1][4][11].
  • Repairability shift: Digital Trends says the XM4C includes a replaceable battery, a change it linked to the EU Batteries Regulation requirement for user-removable portable batteries from February 2027 [4].
  • Software upgrades: The XM4C adds Sony’s newer sound tuning plus 10-band and gaming EQ controls, replacing the older five-band equalizer noted by CNET and Popular Science [1][7][10].
  • Regional pricing: European reports list the XM4C at €249 or €249.99 in Germany and France, while 9to5Google says U.S. preorders showed shipping estimates around September 30 [11][13][12][2].

Perspectives

Sony: Sony told CNET the XM4 had been popular among younger female users and said the new lavender color targets that audience.

Sources: CNET

Popular Science: Popular Science reported from a Sony briefing that the company chose sequels over new concepts after young buyers and TikTok unboxing interest helped drive high customer ratings for the XM4.

Sources: Popular Science

Les Numériques: Les Numériques described the hardware update as minimal, noting the unchanged structure and the removal of NFC pairing.

Sources: Les Numériques

Timeline

  • August 2020: Original WH-1000XM4 reached the market [4][7]
  • 2022: Sony released WH-1000XM5 with a non-folding design [3]
  • July 2026: Original XM4 sales ended, according to Les Numériques [12]
  • 7 September 2026: Sony announced XM4C, CH730N and CH530 headphones [1][7]
  • 30 September 2026: U.S. preorder estimates showed XM4C shipping around this date [2]
  • February 2027: EU replaceable-battery rule starts applying to portable devices [4]

Technical Details

  • Wireless standard: The XM4C uses Bluetooth 6.0, while Popular Science lists SBC, AAC and LDAC codec support [7][8][10].
  • Audio hardware: Reports list the same HD Noise Cancelling Processor QN1, 40mm drivers and four-microphone array used for noise cancellation and calls [1][8][11].
  • Quick charging: Engadget says Sony revised quick charging so three minutes of charging provides one hour of listening time [1].

Industry Impact

  • Midrange competition: 9to5Google says the $299 XM4C price aligns it with competitors such as Nothing Headphone (1), while placing it far below Sony's current XM6 flagship [2].

Historical Background

The original WH-1000XM4 launched in competition with Bose noise-canceling headphones, earning wide acclaim and a CNET Editors' Choice award [7][3]. Its compact folding design distinguished it from later models, and it stayed on sale for years, outlasting two successor generations, before Sony repositioned it as a midrange offering [12].

Action Items

  • Check preorder timing before buying: U.S. buyers can compare retailer listings because 9to5Google reported preorder shipping estimates around September 30 [2].
  • Verify included accessories: HardwareZone says the XM4C includes a 3.5mm headphone cable but not a flight adapter or USB-C cable [8].
  • Compare lower-cost models: Look at the WH-CH730N and WH-CH530 if price matters more than flagship-level ANC, since Sony launched them below the XM4C price tier [2][7].
