JWST detects changes in Chariklo’s rings

Category: Science

The James Webb Space Telescope has detected changes in the two rings around Chariklo, a roughly 250-kilometer-wide Centaur orbiting the Sun between Saturn and Uranus. Observations found that the inner ring has become denser while the outer ring has faded [1][2][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Object classification: Chariklo is the largest known nonplanet Centaur [4].
  • Ring structure: Chariklo has two dense rings [3].

Perspectives

Research team at the Institute of Astrophysics of Andalusia: The measurements could test models of how ring systems evolve [3][4].

Sources: Naftemporiki

Astronomers cited by Space.com: The occultation-based approach could be extended to study other ring systems in the solar system [1].

Sources: Space.com

Historical Background

Astronomers reported the discovery of rings around Chariklo in 2014. The finding showed that narrow ring systems can exist around small bodies, not only around Saturn, Jupiter, Uranus and Neptune [4].
