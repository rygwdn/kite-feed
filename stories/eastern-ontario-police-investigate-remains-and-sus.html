Eastern Ontario police investigate remains and suspicious deaths

Category: Canada

Ontario Provincial Police found the remains of a third missing man, Lawrence Bertrim, on a rural property off Kelly Jordan Road in Montague, north of Smiths Falls [1][4]. CBC reported that police have not linked the deaths of the three men whose remains were found there, as the men's families and the Smiths Falls-area community await further information [1]. In a separate eastern Ontario case, Ottawa police and the Sûreté du Québec were investigating the suspicious deaths of two Ottawa residents after an early Saturday vehicle crash near Papineauville, Que., led later that morning to the discovery of a deceased woman in Lowertown [2][3]. The Ottawa Citizen reported that the investigation involved the Ottawa Police Service and Sûreté du Québec [2].
