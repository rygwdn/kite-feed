ESPN to raise streaming prices starting September 17

Category: Technology

ESPN will raise prices for its direct-to-consumer streaming plans on September 17, 2026. ESPN Select will increase to $13.99 a month from $12.99, and ESPN Unlimited will increase to $31.99 a month from $29.99 [3][4][5][6][7][9]. Annual prices will rise to $139.99 for Select from $129.99 and to $319.99 for Unlimited from $299.99 [4][5][6][7][9]. The increases also affect several Disney+, Hulu and ESPN Select bundles. The ad-supported Disney+, Hulu and ESPN Select bundle rises to $21.99 a month from $19.99, while the ad-free Disney+, Hulu and ESPN Select bundle rises to $32.99 a month from $29.99 [5][6][7][9][10]. A legacy Disney+, Hulu and ESPN Select bundle that is closed to new subscribers will rise to $27.99 a month from $24.99 [3][5][6][7][10]. The changes were listed on an ESPN support page identified by Sports Media Watch and mark the first price increase for the current ESPN streaming platform since Disney overhauled it in 2025 [2][5][6][7][9]. Business Insider reported that the new rates appeared on ESPN's sign-up page for new customers and inside MyDisney account add-ons and bundles via Hulu on August 24 [9].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Plan differences: ESPN Select includes ESPN+ content, many ad-supported live events, overflow programming, documentaries and studio shows; ESPN Unlimited adds ESPN's linear network feeds [4][5][8].
  • Unaffected bundles: Disney+ bundles that include ESPN Unlimited are unchanged, and the Fox One and ESPN Unlimited bundle also stayed unchanged [6][9][10].
  • NFL bundle: Business Insider reported that the ESPN Unlimited and NFL Plus Premium bundle rose to $41.99 a month from $39.99 [9].
  • Sports calendar: MMA Fighting linked the timing to the NFL season, which starts about two weeks later, and ESPN's recent integration of NFL Network programming into the subscription service [8].

Industry Impact

  • Combat sports: MMA Fighting reported that PFL is the only major MMA promotion airing fights on ESPN after UFC programming moved to Paramount following the end of its seven-year deal with the Disney-owned network [8].
  • WWE viewers: Sports Illustrated reported that WWE premium live events on ESPN Unlimited will cost more once the plan increase takes effect [11][8].

Historical Background

Disney overhauled ESPN's streaming strategy in 2025, creating the current ESPN Select and ESPN Unlimited structure [6][9]. Business Insider reported that, before the launch, cord-cutters could not livestream all ESPN-owned broadcast coverage without a live TV streaming service [9]. Technology publications also noted recent price increases by Peacock and Apple services that week [5][6].

Business Angle

  • The higher monthly and annual rates can lift average revenue per user if subscriber churn remains controlled after September 17 [3][9][common].
  • Disney+ bundles with ESPN Unlimited are unchanged, while lower-tier Select packages face increases [6][9][common].
  • Repeated streaming price increases can lead customers to rotate subscriptions rather than keep year-round plans [common].

Action Items

  • Review annual billing before September 17: Compare your current ESPN monthly plan with the new annual rates. Select rises to $139.99 a year and Unlimited rises to $319.99 a year on September 17 [5][7][9].
  • Check bundle type: Look in your Disney, Hulu or MyDisney account to see whether your bundle includes ESPN Select or ESPN Unlimited, because the changes apply differently by bundle type [6][9][10].
