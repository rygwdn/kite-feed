Apple leak points to camera-equipped AirPods for 2027

Category: Technology

Code and demo material in Apple's macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate show work on AirPods with built-in cameras tied to Visual Intelligence and Siri, according to MacRumors and other technology sites that examined the leak [2][6][1][4]. The demo shows a wearer holding up a book so the AirPods can identify its title, while setup text tells users to keep the earbuds uncovered for accurate information about their environment [6][7]. Apple has not announced the product, and reports differ on timing: MacRumors cited Bloomberg's Mark Gurman as saying the camera-equipped AirPods are now expected next year, while another MacRumors roundup said rumors have referenced two codenames, B790 and B798, with launch expectations shifting from a possible near-term debut to 2027 [2][7][3]. The leak prompted privacy concerns before any launch because earbuds are small, frequently worn and harder for nearby people to identify as camera-equipped than phones or smart glasses [5][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: The same macOS Tahoe 26.7 build that leaked the AirPods video also contained references to more than 20 other unreleased Apple devices across Mac, iPhone, iPad, and home-focused products [2].

Key Points

  • Sensor framework: Digital Trends said a forum member found a hidden macOS framework called “AccessorySensorManager” with references to sensors that could collect visual and environmental information from connected AirPods [5].
  • Image capture: TechRadar and Stadt Bremerhaven reported that the leaked code points to synchronized color images of up to 1 megapixel, apparently for AI sensing rather than normal photo or video capture [4][8].
  • Privacy indicator: Stadt Bremerhaven and Asia Economy said code or reports point to a hardware status LED intended to signal when the cameras are actively capturing visual information [8][9].
  • Use cases: Asia Economy said reported uses include recognizing nearby objects or food and helping users navigate unfamiliar areas through low-resolution visual information processed for Siri's AI features [9].

Perspectives

MacRumors: MacRumors said the leaked video and direct setup references indicate Apple is working on camera-equipped AirPods that feed data to Visual Intelligence and let Siri answer questions about the wearer's surroundings.

Sources: MacRumors

Digital Trends: Digital Trends framed the device as a privacy challenge for Apple because earbuds can be worn for long periods and are less visible as cameras than smartphones or smart glasses.

Sources: Digital Trends

PogoWasRight.org: PogoWasRight.org highlighted workplace surveillance implications and said the privacy risks extend beyond offices.

Sources: PogoWasRight.org

Technical Details

  • Dual-camera sensing: Stadt Bremerhaven reported that each earbud appears to have its own camera sensor, giving Visual Intelligence two slightly different viewpoints for environmental analysis [8].
  • Active and passive modes: The reported framework distinguishes an active mode capturing 640 × 640 images upscaled to 1,024 × 1,024 from a passive background mode using 320 × 320 or 512 × 512 images [8].
  • Peripheral inference: Initial analysis reportedly runs directly on the AirPods to detect conditions such as whether a person is in view before sending broader context to Apple's AI systems [8].

Historical Background

Meta's camera-enabled smart glasses previously drew scrutiny around camera-equipped wearables, and Asia Economy cited that history as context for the AirPods leak [9]. Apple introduced Visual Intelligence to interpret what a device can see, and the leaked AirPods material shows Apple testing a way to extend that function from a handheld device to earbuds [6][7].
