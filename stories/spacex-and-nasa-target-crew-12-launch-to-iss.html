SpaceX and NASA target Crew-12 launch to ISS

Category: Science

SpaceX and NASA plan to launch the Crew-12 astronaut mission to the International Space Station on Feb. 13, sending a Dragon capsule atop a Falcon 9 from Florida after weather-driven schedule slips [3][4][5]. With the ISS operating with a reduced crew after a NASA medical evacuation, several outlets said Crew-12’s arrival should ease the load on the team already on board [1][2]. The countdown has been shaped by repeated weather delays, several outlets noted [5][4]. If conditions cooperate, live coverage will follow the mission as it lifts off and begins its rendezvous with the station [3][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Live viewing: Space.com said Crew-12 is set to lift off early Friday (Feb. 13) and shared a live stream so viewers can watch in real time [3].
  • Weather holds: NASA delayed the launch again due to weather but kept Feb. 13 as the target date for the SpaceX Falcon 9 carrying Crew-12 [5].
  • Station staffing: CNN said the ISS is down to a bare-bones, three-person crew ahead of Crew-12’s arrival, and that the new mission is meant to relieve the reduced team [2].
  • Pad readiness: Space.com reported the Falcon 9 and Dragon for Crew-12 have been standing on the pad at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station for days ahead of the planned launch window [3].
  • Operational updates: In its live briefing, Space.com leaned on hour-by-hour readiness updates as NASA and SpaceX worked through final checks before liftoff [4].
