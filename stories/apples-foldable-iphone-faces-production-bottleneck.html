Apple’s foldable iPhone faces production bottlenecks ahead of October launch

Category: Technology

Apple’s first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, faces manufacturing constraints before its planned October release. A supply-chain source cited by China’s Jiemian News said Foxconn’s final-assembly yield was slightly above 60% on September 17, while the assembler continued adjusting equipment and processes. Samsung Display’s foldable OLED panels and hinge parts also reportedly had low yields, delayed deliveries and design changes, giving Foxconn less time to refine production [4][7][41]. Preorders are scheduled to begin October 16, and the phone is set to launch October 23. The phone starts at $1,999 in the United States, and buyers can begin preparing orders on October 12 [3][13].
