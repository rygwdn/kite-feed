OpenAI weighs funding round at valuation above $1.2 trillion

Category: AI

OpenAI is holding early discussions with investors about a private funding round that could value the ChatGPT maker at more than $1.2 trillion ahead of a possible initial public offering, according to people cited by the Financial Times and Bloomberg [2][6][7]. The valuation remains preliminary and could change during negotiations; a New York Times report put the possible valuation at $1.5 trillion [4]. Investors, not OpenAI, initiated the discussions, and any decision to proceed would depend on the timing of a public listing [11][13]. The financing could fund acquisitions and extend the period before an IPO. Chief Executive Sam Altman has said OpenAI is unlikely to go public in 2026 and linked the delay to work on AI safety and model alignment [1][12]. OpenAI raised $122 billion at an $852 billion valuation in March, while rival Anthropic was valued at $965 billion in its latest reported financing [10][16].

Did you know: OpenAI’s active user count was reported to exceed 1 billion [17].

Key Points

  • Revenue growth: OpenAI’s annualized revenue exceeded $40 billion in August after rising 20% following the GPT-5.6 launch [10][12].
  • Capital intensity: A source said OpenAI spent $34 billion last year, largely on model training, while the company said its March financing left it adequately funded [10][12].
  • Acquisition history: OpenAI has bought Jony Ive’s AI-device venture io and developer-tools startup Astral [6][10].
  • Rival listing: Anthropic is also preparing for a possible public offering, with one report saying it could begin IPO activity as early as October and list in November [13].

Perspectives

OpenAI: The company declined to comment on the reported financing discussions [13].
