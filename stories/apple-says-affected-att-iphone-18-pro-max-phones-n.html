Apple says affected AT&T iPhone 18 Pro Max phones need replacement

Category: Technology

Apple said a small number of iPhone 18 Pro Max phones on AT&T in the United States can lose cellular service, leaving owners unable to call, text or use mobile data. AT&T warned that emergency calls to 911 may also fail [1][3]. Apple says iOS 27.0.1 and a separate AT&T carrier-settings update are intended to prevent further cases, but neither can restore service on phones already affected. Apple says it will replace those phones free of charge. Customers can contact Apple Support or AT&T, or visit an Apple or AT&T store [9][10]. AT&T initially urged customers to install iOS 27.0.1, although affected users reported that the update did not restore service. The carrier-settings update will download automatically to AT&T users over 14 days. Users can also check for it manually in Settings. Apple has not disclosed the cause or the number of affected devices; its statement describes the group as “small” [20][1]. The problem has been reported only on AT&T-connected iPhone 18 Pro Max phones. No link to the U.S. model’s Qualcomm modem has been established. Apple’s preventive updates are now available, while owners whose phones have already lost service need to arrange a replacement [2][10].

Key Points

  • Earlier complaints: Some customers said their initial warranty replacement requests were declined before Apple confirmed that it would replace affected phones free of charge [12][9].
  • Update notes: The published release notes for iOS 27.0.1 listed Face ID, camera and touchscreen fixes, but no specific fix for AT&T cellular service [18].

Timeline

  • 18 September 2026: iPhone 18 Pro Max launches; AT&T users later report connectivity failures [12]
  • 25 September 2026: Reports of the SOS-mode issue begin appearing online [15]
  • 1 October 2026: AT&T texts customers urging an iOS 27.0.1 update [20]
  • 2 October 2026: Apple confirms service-loss issue and announces preventive updates [1]

Technical Details

  • Carrier settings version: Apple’s carrier-settings update changes AT&T’s bundle from version 72.0.2 to 72.1; users can check for the update in Settings > General > About [5].
  • A teardown cited by CNET found a new millimeter-wave antenna in the iPhone 18 Pro Max; Apple and AT&T have not linked it to the outages [10].

Action Items

  • To install iOS 27.0.1, open Settings > General > Software Update; to check for a carrier-settings update, open Settings > General > About [1].
