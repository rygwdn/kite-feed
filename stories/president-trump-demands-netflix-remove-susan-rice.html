President Trump demands Netflix remove Susan Rice from board

Category: Business

President Trump urged Netflix to remove Susan Rice from its board, telling the company to do so “immediately” or “pay the consequences,” according to multiple reports [1][2][3][4]. The demand focuses on Rice’s past role as a national security official in the Obama administration [1][3]. The episode shows how board appointments — and directors’ public political comments — can quickly become flashpoints that draw direct attention from elected officials, raising reputational pressure on consumer-facing companies [2][3][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Triggering remarks: Bloomberg said the clash followed Rice’s comments that Democrats would take action against corporations that “take a knee” to the President [2].
  • Deal backdrop: Financial Times cast the President’s comments as a return to a fight between Netflix and Paramount Skydance over acquiring Warner Bros. Discovery, while Business Insider said his intervention came amid negotiations around that Warner Bros. deal [3][1].
  • Board pressure: Benzinga reported Trump called Rice a “political hack” and urged Netflix to act “immediately,” a reminder that political rhetoric can quickly turn into direct pressure on public companies [4].

Perspectives

President Donald Trump: He demanded that Netflix remove board member Susan Rice, with reports saying he warned the company to act “immediately” or “pay the consequences,” and one account saying he called her a “political hack” [1][2][4].
