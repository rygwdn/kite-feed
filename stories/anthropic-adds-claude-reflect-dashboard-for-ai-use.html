Anthropic adds Claude Reflect dashboard for AI use

Category: AI

Anthropic introduced Reflect, a beta dashboard in Claude that lets users review their chatbot use over selected one-, three-, six- or 12-month periods [8][9][18][21]. The tool summarizes recurring topics, task types and usage patterns, and adds controls for quiet hours and break nudges after a chosen amount of Claude use [9][18][26]. Reflect is available to Free, Pro and Max users with Memory turned on, through Claude's web and desktop apps [9][18][20]. Anthropic said future updates will add a view of total time spent using Claude [9]. MacRumors reported that Reflect excludes incognito chats, underlying files from connected tools and conversations tied to health integrations, and that the displayed information is not used for other purposes [18].

Did you know: ITC reported that Anthropic developed Reflect with MIT Media Lab, the Digital Wellness Lab at Boston Children's Hospital and the Family Online Safety Institute [20].

Key Points

  • Access limits: TestingCatalog reported that cowork conversation support is not yet available and is listed as coming soon [9].
  • AI fluency: The dashboard maps usage to Anthropic's 4D AI Fluency Framework: Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence [18][9][20].
  • Privacy limits: Reflect does not draw from incognito chats, underlying files from connected tools or conversations tied to health integration tools, and Anthropic says it does not use Reflect information for other purposes [18][20].
  • Reflection prompt: Anthropic's example prompt asks, "What's one thing you want to keep doing yourself, even if Claude could do it faster?" [8][18][20].
  • Coming support: TestingCatalog said support for cowork conversations is not live yet and is listed as coming soon [9].
  • Interface metrics: Engadget reported that the dashboard shows a user's most active day, peak hour and total chats for the selected period, plus topic breakdowns with percentages [26].
  • Availability gap: Engadget said Reflect is limited to Claude's web client and desktop app at launch and did not describe mobile availability [26].
  • Fluency framework: Reflect maps activity to Anthropic's 4D AI Fluency Framework: delegation, description, discernment and diligence [9][18][27].

Industry Impact

  • AI product design: Reflect adds user-facing analytics and self-management controls to a chatbot. Several technology publications compared it with Spotify Wrapped, Digital Wellbeing and Screen Time [26][19][18][22].
  • Digital wellness: ITC reported that Anthropic developed the feature with MIT Media Lab, the Digital Wellness Lab at Boston Children's Hospital and the Family Online Safety Institute, giving Claude's usage controls an explicit digital-wellness framing [20].

Historical Background

Spotify Wrapped popularized annual activity recaps, and similar summaries have spread to services including YouTube and Uber [22]. Apple introduced Screen Time in iOS 12 in 2018, giving users device-use tracking and app-limit tools [common]. Engadget compared Reflect with Spotify Wrapped and Apple's Screen Time [26].
