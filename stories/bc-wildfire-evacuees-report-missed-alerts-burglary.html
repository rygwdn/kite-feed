B.C. wildfire evacuees report missed alerts, burglary

Category: Canada

Residents who fled the Bald Range wildfire near Summerland, B.C., reported missed emergency alerts, delays registering for support and a burglary after an Aug. 7 evacuation order emptied the community [1][4][2]. CBC reported that hundreds of evacuees stayed at the Penticton Trade and Convention Centre, while retired 911 dispatcher Dawne Young said her neighbour’s home was lost in the fire [1]. Mira Boucher said her family returned to find their Summerland home ransacked after spending weeks on Vancouver Island during the evacuation; she said the family had moved in only weeks before the fire and fled in the middle of the night with little notice as flames crested a nearby mountain on Aug. 7 [4]. A separate report said some B.C. residents whose homes were destroyed by wildfire were more worried about missing pets, while more evacuation orders and alerts were issued south of the Pear Lake wildfire [3].
