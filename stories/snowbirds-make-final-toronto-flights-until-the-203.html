Snowbirds make final Toronto flights until the 2030s

Category: Canada

The Canadian Forces Snowbirds flew over downtown Toronto and Lake Ontario on Saturday during the 77th Canadian International Air Show, part of the Labour Day weekend program along the city’s lakeshore [2][5]. The performances are among the team’s last Toronto appearances until at least the 2030s, the earliest a replacement fleet is expected [2][5][3]. The Toronto pause is tied to plans to replace the Snowbirds’ aging aircraft, which CBC reported have far exceeded their intended lifespan [5]. The Globe and Mail reported that Defence Minister David McGuinty said in May that negotiations were underway with the manufacturer of the team’s new jets [3].
