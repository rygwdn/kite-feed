Apple tests smaller Pencil for foldable iPhone Ultra

Category: Technology

Apple prototyped, tested and considered a modified Apple Pencil for its first foldable iPhone, expected to be called iPhone Ultra, but Bloomberg's Mark Gurman said the accessory is unlikely to ship with the device [2][8][1][13][15]. Apple is expected to introduce the foldable iPhone at its Sept. 9 event, and the iPhone Ultra name has not been officially confirmed [2][14][15]. The tested stylus was shorter and stubbier than iPad Apple Pencil models and was designed to attach magnetically to the side of the phone for storage and charging [2][1][8][7][12]. Reports citing Gurman said Apple found the side attachment awkward when opening or holding the foldable device, and the company also weighed concerns that stylus use could leave visible marks on the inner folding display [1][8][18][14][11].

Key Points

  • Screen size: Cult of Mac said reports have put the foldable iPhone's display at roughly 7.8 inches, large enough to compare the opened device to a small iPad [3].

Perspectives

9to5Mac: 9to5Mac's Michael Burkhardt wrote that an Apple Pencil would be useful on an iPhone Ultra, while saying Gurman's reporting indicates buyers should not expect one soon.

Sources: 9to5Mac

Timeline

  • 2007: Jobs introduced the iPhone while dismissing stylus-based smartphone input [2]
  • 2015: Apple launched the first Apple Pencil for iPad [3]
  • 30 August 2026: Gurman reported Apple tested a Pencil for its foldable iPhone [2]
  • 9 September 2026: Apple is expected to unveil its foldable iPhone [14]

Industry Impact

  • Accessory ecosystem: Apple's tested design would have required a new, shorter Pencil rather than compatibility with current iPad Pencil models, limiting immediate cross-device accessory reuse [2][12].
  • Foldable competition: Samsung already uses foldable-specific S Pen compatibility limits, giving Apple a direct example of how pen input on folding phones can require model-specific hardware restrictions [8][7].

Historical Background

Apple positioned the original 2007 iPhone around finger-based multitouch rather than stylus input, with Steve Jobs using the line “Who wants a stylus?” during the launch [2][19]. The company released the Apple Pencil for iPad in 2015, keeping optional pen input on tablets separate from the iPhone's original finger-first interface [3]. Apple has not offered an Apple Pencil for iPhone models to date [15].

Business Angle

  • Skipping a day-one Pencil could reduce the chance that the foldable iPhone launch is tied to display-wear complaints that might raise service costs or weaken premium positioning [11][common].
  • If Apple resolves the attachment and display-pressure issues later, a foldable iPhone Pencil could add accessory revenue tied to a larger-screen iPhone segment [3][common].
  • The key risk for investors is not the missing accessory; it is whether Apple's first foldable iPhone can justify premium pricing without avoidable hardware compromises [15][common].

Action Items

  • Do not buy an Apple Pencil for the foldable iPhone yet: Wait for Apple's Sept. 9 announcement because current reports say a dedicated foldable-iPhone Pencil and compatibility with existing Apple Pencil models are not expected [2][12].
