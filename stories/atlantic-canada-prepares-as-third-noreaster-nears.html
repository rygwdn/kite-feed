Atlantic Canada prepares as third nor’easter nears region

Category: Canada

Atlantic Canada is preparing for another round of wintry weather as a powerful nor’easter approaches Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador, the third such storm in about three weeks [1][2]. Forecasts point to more snow and difficult conditions, with Environment Canada issuing warnings in parts of the region [2]. The latest updates indicate eastern Nova Scotia is expected to take the brunt of the system, a reminder for residents to plan ahead and check local alerts before heading out [3].
