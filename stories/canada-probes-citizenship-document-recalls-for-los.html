Canada probes citizenship document recalls for ‘lost Canadians’

Category: Canada

Immigration Minister Lena Diab said she ordered an investigation after learning that “something” had triggered emails last week telling an unknown number of people to surrender their proof of Canadian citizenship [2][6][5][7]. The emails went to people described as “lost Canadians,” including applicants who had already been approved for citizenship documents under rules tied to citizenship by descent [2][6][5]. Diab did not identify the problem that prompted the recalls, but said Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada is not finalizing new applications while it reviews files. Applicants whose cases are deemed acceptable are being told their documents are valid [2][6][5]. The department told La Presse that the emails went to “a few dozen” people who obtained citizenship through descent under a new law allowing people born before Dec. 15, 2025, to claim Canadian citizenship if they can prove a direct Canadian ancestor [5]. Diab said she understands people are worried about their citizenship status and that the question of who receives proof of citizenship matters to Canadians [2][6][5]. Global News reported that Canada had begun returning citizenship certificates it had demanded days earlier from some lost Canadians [1].
