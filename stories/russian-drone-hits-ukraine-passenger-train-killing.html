Russian drone hits Ukraine passenger train, killing five

Category: World

Russian strikes across Ukraine hit a passenger train in the Kharkiv region and also damaged energy infrastructure overnight, according to Ukrainian officials [5][6][3][7]. Prosecutors said remains of five people were found at the train strike site, and multiple outlets reported at least five fatalities from the train attack [1][2][4][5]. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy condemned the strike on the civilian train as “an act of terrorism,” as reporting noted the attack came only days after direct talks aimed at ending the war [2][4][3].
