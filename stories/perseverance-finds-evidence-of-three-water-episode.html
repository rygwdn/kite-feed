Perseverance finds evidence of three water episodes on Mars

Category: Science

NASA’s Perseverance rover found evidence that water altered igneous rocks in the Margin Unit, along Jezero Crater’s inner edge, during at least three separate episodes billions of years ago. Mineral and structural traces preserved in the rocks indicate that the area’s water history involved more than one system or process [6][4]. Scientists had expected the carbonate-rich rocks seen from orbit to mark an ancient lake shoreline, but Perseverance found igneous rocks bearing signs of later water-related alteration. Researchers said crystals in the rocks preserved traces of changes caused by water [6][4].
