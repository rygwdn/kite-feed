Israel strikes southern Lebanon after two soldiers killed

Category: World

Israel struck southern Lebanon after saying Hezbollah had committed a “blatant violation” of the ceasefire. The Israeli military said two reservists were killed and four others were seriously wounded during operations in southern Lebanon [2][4][10][24][40][49]. Lebanon’s Health Ministry said an Israeli strike on Tibnin killed one person and wounded 12, including in a hit on the roof of a prayer hall at a cemetery. Strikes or shelling were also reported around Mansouri, Burj al-Shemali and the Tyre area [3][5][6][7][48]. The strikes took place as Israeli and Lebanese negotiators met in Rome in US-facilitated talks on implementing a June 26 arrangement. The arrangement calls for Israeli forces to withdraw from areas they occupy in southern Lebanon and for the Lebanese army to take control in exchange for Hezbollah’s disarmament [1][10][23][48]. Wednesday’s session ended early, with sources in the negotiations saying Israel requested the halt. Israeli delegation head Yechiel Leiter blamed alleged misleading leaks by the Lebanese side. Talks were expected to resume Thursday for a third and final day [9][al-monitor.com#3][24][25][31]. Israel issued an evacuation warning for Mansouri before the strikes, telling residents to leave the village and move at least 1,000 meters north. It was the first such warning for Lebanon in weeks; some reports said it was the first in more than a month [5][7][18][21][30]. Hezbollah had not immediately commented on the latest events. Earlier, it rejected provisions linking Israel’s withdrawal to its disarmament and opposed the direct negotiations [7][10][25][34].

Key Points

  • Blast details: An initial Israeli probe indicated the explosive detonated when troops entered a building in Majdal Zoun, while an Israeli military official said the nature of the explosive remained under review [4][48][33].
  • Soldiers named: The Israeli military identified the dead reservists as Maj. Harel Birenstock, 34, and Chief Master Sgt. Tamir Vaknin, 33, of the 2855th Battalion, 55th Brigade [24][25][31][38].
  • First fatalities: The soldiers were the first Israeli military fatalities reported in southern Lebanon since June 28 and the first Israeli deaths since the June truce took effect [10][12][40][44][31].
  • Pilot zones: The Rome talks focused partly on “pilot zones,” where Israeli withdrawal would be paired with Lebanese army deployment and verification of Hezbollah disarmament [7][16][25][9].
  • Further strikes: Middle East Eye cited Israeli media reports saying the army was considering additional attacks and seeking approval for a stronger response in Lebanon [32].

Historical Background

The latest round of Hezbollah-Israel fighting began March 2, when Hezbollah fired rockets at Israel in support of Iran and Israel responded with extensive strikes in Lebanon [9][25][22]. The June 26 arrangement followed earlier ceasefire extensions and left Israel maintaining what it described as a security zone in southern Lebanon [21][27][29].

Humanitarian Impact

More than 1 million people were displaced in Lebanon during the latest round of Hezbollah-Israel fighting that began March 2, and RT cited a UN figure saying more than 820,000 displaced Lebanese were returning home as the escalation unfolded [5][3]. Immediate concerns in the affected southern towns included evacuation, medical treatment for the wounded and access to homes and fields near Israeli positions [21][28].
