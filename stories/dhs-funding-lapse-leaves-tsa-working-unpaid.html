DHS funding lapse leaves TSA working unpaid

Category: Business

A limited funding lapse at the Department of Homeland Security began over the weekend, leaving Transportation Security Administration (TSA) officers screening passengers at U.S. airports without paychecks during the shutdown [2][1][3][4]. Coverage describes frontline airport workers bracing for another round of financial strain and wondering how long they can keep showing up under these conditions [1][2]. The lapse follows failed negotiations between the White House and congressional Democrats over proposed new restrictions for federal immigration agents, and the Guardian says Congress is taking a break as the impasse drags on [4]. The Guardian also calls the shutdown limited in scope—affecting about 13% of the federal civilian workforce—and says it could last at least 10 days [4].
