Pixel users report Android 17 touchscreen and connectivity bugs

Category: Technology

After Google released the stable Android 17 update to supported Pixel phones this week, some users reported touchscreen failures, lost 5G access, Wi-Fi problems and disappearing home-screen widgets [6][7][14][2][3][12][13]. The touchscreen issue includes temporarily unresponsive displays and vertical swipes registering in the opposite direction, with reports citing Pixel 10, Pixel 9, Pixel 8 and Pixel 7 models [3][6][12][13]. Connectivity complaints include 5G disappearing after the update on Pixel 9 Pro, Pixel 9 Pro XL, Pixel 8 Pro, Pixel 8a and Pixel 6a devices, along with missing eSIMs and saved Wi-Fi networks that no longer reconnect automatically [2][13]. Separate Wi-Fi reports describe Google apps, including YouTube, Gmail, Google Photos, Google Messages, the Play Store and Google Keep, failing or falling back to mobile data while the phone still shows a Wi-Fi connection [7][18]. Google has acknowledged at least the touchscreen and widget issues through Pixel support channels cited by Android-focused publications. Suggested steps include clearing the Pixel Launcher cache for touch problems, and Android Central said Google is developing a software fix for a widget bug that appears limited to phones with a work profile configured [3][6][14][12]. Android 17 is available first on Pixel phones; Samsung and other Android device owners must wait for separate manufacturer rollout schedules [8][15][20].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Security changes: Android 17 adds app memory limits, local network permissions, dynamic code-loading restrictions and Certificate Transparency by default as lower-level security changes [4].
  • Switching tools: The updated Android Switch tool can transfer more data from iPhones, including passwords, RCS messages and third-party app data when developers opt in [16][10].
  • Enterprise timing: TechRepublic said IT teams outside the Pixel ecosystem still face OEM rollout schedules, beta programs and app-testing considerations before Android 17 reaches managed fleets [8].

Perspectives

Google PixelCommunity: The official PixelCommunity Reddit account replied to a touchscreen-bug thread and suggested affected users clear the Pixel Launcher app cache, but Android Authority said Google had not committed to a fix for that issue.

Sources: Android Authority

Pixel users: One user described touch input registering several swipes in YouTube Shorts before the phone stopped registering touch for a few seconds, illustrating how the bug can interrupt normal app use.

Sources: Android Headlines

Droid-Life readers: Reader comments collected by Droid-Life showed split reactions: some users noticed better battery life, while others said the update felt indistinguishable from a monthly patch.

Sources: Droid-Life

Timeline

  • 16 June 2026: Android 17 stable rollout started for supported Pixel phones [6][7]
  • 19 June 2026: Android Central reported Google developing widget-bug software fix [14]
  • 19 June 2026: Android Authority described 5G-loss reports across several Pixel models [2]
  • 20 June 2026: Frandroid reported touchscreen complaints across Pixel generations 7 through 10 [12]

Action Items

  • Delay updating critical Pixel devices: If your phone is essential for work or travel, wait until Google ships fixes for acknowledged Android 17 bugs affecting touch input or widgets [3][14].
  • Try the 5G workaround: If 5G disappears after updating, use Settings > System > Reset options > Reset mobile network settings; this clears Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and mobile network configuration without deleting personal data, then reconnect Wi‑Fi and reconfigure eSIM if needed [13].
  • Remove work profile temporarily: If home-screen widgets vanish after Android 17 and a work profile is active, temporarily removing the work profile may restore the widgets until Google ships its fix [14].
