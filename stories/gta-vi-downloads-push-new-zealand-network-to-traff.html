GTA VI downloads push New Zealand network to traffic record

Category: Technology

New Zealand internet provider Chorus recorded peak network traffic of 5.6 terabits per second at 9:30 p.m. Wednesday as players downloaded Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI), surpassing its previous record of 5.4Tbps set during a Fortnite release. Chorus had forecast about 35 petabytes of extra traffic. The game’s download file is 212 gigabytes [24][4][84]. Rockstar Games began unlocking GTA VI at midnight local time on Nov. 19, starting in New Zealand and continuing market by market for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S consoles [4][15]. The staggered release spread download demand across time zones rather than making the game available worldwide at once [19]. The launch version targets 30 frames per second on consoles, and PC players will have to wait until 2027, Engadget reported [7][2]. Streams Charts reported that GTA VI reached a combined peak of 7,886,402 concurrent viewers across livestreaming platforms [24].

Key Points

  • Indian developers: Firstpost reported that 1,600 developers in Bengaluru helped make the game [45].
  • Sales outlook: Omdia estimated GTA VI could generate $3.2 billion in sales by the end of the year [15].
  • Hardware upgrades: A PCMag survey estimated that 7% of gamers planned to upgrade their machines for GTA VI or another major release [50].
  • Cloud access: Xbox Cloud Gaming was listed as an option on Xbox One from 1 a.m. Eastern time on launch day, extending access beyond the Series X/S generation [26].

Technical Details

  • Console rendering: The game upscales 1440p output to 4K on base PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X; Xbox Series S scales 720p to 1080p using AMD FSR 4, while PlayStation 5 Pro uses PSSR2 [76].
  • Console performance trade-offs: Digital Foundry's analysis examined how input lag factored into resolution and upscaling choices and compared performance across platforms [76].

Industry Impact

  • Internet providers: Chorus recorded 34.7 petabytes of total network data usage on Wednesday, exceeding the 34 petabytes recorded on election day [24].
  • Livestreaming platforms: In an early snapshot at 12:30 UTC on Nov. 18, YouTube had 2.66 million GTA VI viewers, Twitch 2.50 million and Kick 1.31 million, according to preliminary Streams Charts data [54].

Historical Background

Grand Theft Auto V, released in 2013, sold more than 230 million copies, and the franchise has sold more than 470 million worldwide [4]. The previous installment generated $1 billion in retail sales in its first three days [4].
