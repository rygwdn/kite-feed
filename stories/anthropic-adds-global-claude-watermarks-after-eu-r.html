Anthropic adds global Claude watermarks after EU rules

Category: AI

Anthropic said it will embed invisible, machine-readable watermarks in supported Claude-generated text worldwide as it moves to meet European Union AI Act transparency requirements for identifying AI-generated content [18][20][32]. The company said it will apply the system globally at launch [18][20]. Anthropic described the watermark as an imperceptible pattern created through subtle word-choice changes; it said the mark is not visible to readers and does not change a Claude response’s meaning, quality or readability [12][20][26]. Anthropic also said the watermark cannot be traced to a specific person, organization or chat [12]. The rollout drew objections from some Claude users, including people who told Business Insider they canceled subscriptions because they worried persistent marks could follow client work or code and raise authorship questions [26].

Key Points

  • Model rollout: Claude models launched before Aug. 2, 2026, are covered by an EU transition period, and Anthropic said it is working to add watermarking support to those models over the coming months [20][21].
  • Product reach: The marking system will cover Claude Platform API, consumer chat, Claude Code, Claude Cowork and Claude Tag, including Claude access through AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry [21].
  • Detection access: Anthropic said the pattern is detectable only with a key, while outside access remains limited as the company works to enable users and third parties to detect Claude watermarks [12][29].
  • File metadata: Supported SVG, PNG and JPG files will carry digitally signed provenance metadata based on the C2PA standard rather than relying only on text-pattern watermarking [21][29].

Perspectives

Anthropic: The company framed the move as an EU AI Act compliance requirement and said other AI providers will face the same transparency obligations.
