Two Thinking Machines Lab cofounders rejoin OpenAI

Category: AI

Two cofounders of Thinking Machines Lab, the AI startup founded by former OpenAI executive Mira Murati, are leaving the company to return to OpenAI [2][3]. TechCrunch reports the change had been developing for several weeks, according to an OpenAI executive, while WIRED describes the departures as a setback for the young startup and says competing narratives have emerged about why it happened [2][3]. The moves underscore how competitive—and fluid—the market is for senior AI talent, even among closely linked organizations [3].
