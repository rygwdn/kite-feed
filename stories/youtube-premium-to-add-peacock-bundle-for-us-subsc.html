YouTube Premium to add Peacock bundle for US subscribers

Category: Technology

NBCUniversal and YouTube announced a multiyear global strategic partnership that will add ad-supported Peacock Premium to YouTube Premium for eligible U.S. subscribers beginning in early 2027 [10][2][5][11][9]. NBCUniversal said subscribers will access Peacock within the YouTube experience, giving them Peacock’s shows, films and live sports without requiring a separate Peacock subscription [10][8][15][17]. The included Peacock tier is the ad-supported Premium plan, which costs $10.99 a month by itself, while YouTube Premium costs $15.99 a month for an individual U.S. plan after a recent price increase [1][2][6][11][8]. The bundle will add NBCUniversal programming, including Saturday Night Live, Law & Order: SVU, Love Island USA, Universal films and live sports such as the NFL, NBA and Olympics [10][2][15][18]. The agreement also extends NBCUniversal’s distribution on YouTube TV, creates sports production ties with NBC Sports, brings some live sports to the NBC Sports YouTube channel, and includes planned international bundles for Universal+ and Hayu in select markets [10][2][6][11][12]. YouTube will also offer Peacock Premium as a paid add-on through YouTube Primetime Channels later this summer, after Peacock Premium Plus launched there in June [2][11][9].

We’re incredibly excited to expand our partnership with NBCUniversal to redefine what a modern entertainment subscription can be for consumers

— Neal Mohan (CNET)

Did you know: Peacock reported its first-ever quarterly profit in the second quarter of 2026, six years after its debut [9][11].

Key Points

  • Subscriber scale: Peacock has 48 million paid subscribers, while YouTube Premium and YouTube Music have a combined 125 million subscribers worldwide [9][12][4].

Perspectives

YouTube: CEO Neal Mohan said the partnership pairs YouTube Premium’s uninterrupted creator and music experience with Peacock’s sports, films and original series.

Sources: CNET

NBCUniversal: Media Group Chairman Matt Strauss said the agreement accelerates Peacock’s next growth phase by bringing the service to millions of YouTube Premium subscribers and driving long-term business value.

Sources: Gizmodo

TechCrunch: TechCrunch said Peacock is pursuing distribution partnerships while other streaming companies pursue deals such as Paramount Skydance’s planned Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition and Fox’s Roku purchase.

Sources: TechCrunch

Timeline

  • June 2026: Peacock Premium Plus launched as a YouTube add-on [2][11].
  • 27 July 2026: NBCUniversal and YouTube announced the multiyear partnership [10].
  • Summer 2026: Peacock Premium add-on is set to arrive through YouTube Primetime Channels [2][11].
  • 2027: Peacock Premium joins YouTube Premium for eligible U.S. subscribers [10][5].

Industry Impact

  • Streaming distribution: NBCUniversal said the agreement is Peacock’s largest wholesale distribution partnership to date, putting Peacock Premium in front of millions of existing and future YouTube Premium subscribers in the U.S. [10][6][19].
  • Pay TV carriage: The partnership renews NBCUniversal’s multiyear distribution deal with YouTube TV, which The Next Web described as the largest and fastest-growing pay TV provider in the U.S. [10][6].
  • Advertising technology: NBCUniversal said the companies will deepen advertising and technology collaboration, and The Next Web identified FreeWheel as part of that advertising technology relationship [10][6].

Historical Background

Peacock launched in 2020. Before the YouTube deal, NBCUniversal had bundled Peacock with platforms including Apple TV, Amazon, Instacart+ and Walmart+ [9][19].

Action Items

  • Check your YouTube plan before 2027: Confirm you have full YouTube Premium, because reports say Premium Lite and YouTube Music-only plans will not receive the Peacock bundle [16][13][17].
  • Wait before buying Peacock separately: If you already pay for YouTube Premium in the U.S. and only need ad-supported Peacock, compare your current Peacock billing against the early-2027 included benefit before renewing a long-term Peacock plan [10][11].
