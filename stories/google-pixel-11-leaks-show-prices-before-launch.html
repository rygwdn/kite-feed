Google Pixel 11 leaks show prices before launch

Category: Technology

Details of Google’s Pixel 11 lineup leaked before the company’s Made by Google event, scheduled for Aug. 12 in New York City, where Google is expected to show its next Pixel phones [1][9]. Android Headlines said it obtained full specifications and US and European pricing for the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. The Verge said the leak broadly matches earlier pre-launch information [6][8][3]. The leak puts the base Pixel 11 at a starting price of $899, up $100 from the prior model, with base storage rising to 256GB from 128GB [8][3]. Android Headlines lists the base Pixel 11 with a 6.3-inch Actua OLED display, Tensor G6 chip, Titan M3 coprocessor, 12GB or 16GB of RAM, 256GB or 512GB of storage, a 4,985mAh typical battery, 30W USB-C PPS charging, Qi2 wireless charging, a 48-megapixel main camera, a 13-megapixel ultrawide camera and a 10.5-megapixel front camera [6]. Separate leaked Pixel 11 Pro Fold marketing renders show an 8-inch inner display, Pixel Glow LEDs and Gemini Intelligence branding. NextApple said Google had not confirmed the feature uses, battery capacity, camera specifications, pricing or launch timing for that model [10][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Pro pricing: Mynet, citing The Verge and Android Headlines, said Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL base models may use 12GB of RAM, with Pixel 11 Pro starting at $1,099 [3].
  • Display specs: Android Headlines lists the Pixel 11 display at 2424 x 1080 resolution, 60Hz-120Hz adaptive refresh, 3,000 nits brightness and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection [6].
  • Fold layout: NextApple said leaked Fold renders retain a book-style design and vertical outer screen while moving the flash and microphone into the black camera-glass area [10].

Perspectives

Android Police reviewer Rajesh Pandey: Pandey argued that a colored light in the camera bar is less useful than improving Pixel face unlock performance in low light.

Industry Impact

  • Flagship competition: Mashable noted Samsung had just announced new phones shortly before Google’s scheduled Pixel launch, while Memeburn said the Pixel 11 Pro Fold faces Samsung and Apple in the same foldable-phone market [1][2].
  • AI positioning: NextApple said repeated Gemini Intelligence branding in leaked Fold materials points to Google emphasizing AI use cases such as photography, information organization, real-time assistance and cross-app operation [10].
  • Accessory ecosystem: The Qi2 support listed in the leaked Pixel 11 specifications would add magnetic alignment for more consistent wireless-charging placement [6][common].
