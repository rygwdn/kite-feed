Pakistani rescuers find four bodies after Broad Peak avalanche

Category: World

Pakistani rescue teams recovered or located four bodies on Friday among 10 international climbers missing after an avalanche struck Broad Peak in the Karakoram range, officials said [29][60][53][64]. The expedition was led by Nepal-born British mountaineer Nirmal “Nims” Purja, and authorities had not publicly confirmed the identities of the recovered bodies in initial updates [50][66]. The Alpine Club of Pakistan said the avalanche hit around midday Thursday on the 8,047-metre peak and the team lost communication after the incident [6][18][29]. The reported expedition roster included Purja; Nepali climbers Pur Bahadur Gurung “Yukta,” Kili Pemba Sherpa, Nima Sherpa, Nawang Thindu Sherpa and Gyalu Sherpa; Pakistani climber Sohail Sakhi; Omani climber Nadhira Ahmed Abdullah Al Harthy; American climber Mallory Geis; and Chinese climber Wang Zhong [18][43][29]. The Alpine Club said two rescue helicopters carrying personnel and specialised equipment were dispatched Friday morning to support ground teams, while rescue efforts remained subject to weather and operational conditions at high altitude [18]. Karrar Haidri, the club’s senior vice president, said the club was “leaving no stone unturned” in the search operation [18].

Key Points

  • Summit route: The avalanche struck while the team was climbing from Camp II to Camp III during a summit push, expedition officials and the BBC reported [26][43].
  • Rescue assets: Pakistan Army helicopters joined the operation, and experienced rescuers reached the area where the climbers were believed to have gone missing [3].
  • Expedition teams: The missing climbers were linked to Seven Summit Treks, Elite Exped, Imagine Nepal Treks and Pakistani high-altitude guide teams [26][43].
  • Peak history: Before this avalanche, Broad Peak had seen reported fatalities or disappearances involving Kim Hong-bin in 2021, Gordon Henderson and Sharif Sadpara in 2022, and Murad Sadpara in 2024 [13].

Historical Background

Purja served with Britain’s Brigade of Gurkhas before joining the Royal Marines’ Special Boat Service, then became a full-time mountaineer and guide after leaving the military [17][21]. In 2021, he was part of an all-Nepalese team that completed the first winter ascent of K2 [1][17]. Broad Peak sits near K2 in northern Pakistan’s Karakoram range, a region that draws international high-altitude expeditions each climbing season [15][44].
