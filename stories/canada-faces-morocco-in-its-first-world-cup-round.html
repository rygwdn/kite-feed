Canada faces Morocco in its first World Cup round of 16

Category: Canada

Canada faces Morocco at Houston Stadium on Saturday, July 4, in the men’s World Cup round of 16, the Canadian men’s team’s first appearance in the tournament’s last 16 [10][20][14][30]. The match is scheduled for 1 p.m. ET, noon local time in Houston, and opens the tournament’s round-of-16 slate [20][24][31]. Canada advanced by beating South Africa 1-0 in the round of 32, with Stephen Eustáquio scoring in stoppage time [2][10][14][19]. Morocco advanced after drawing 2-2 with the Netherlands and winning 3-2 on penalties, according to match previews [10][19]. The teams last met at the 2022 World Cup in Qatar, where Morocco beat Canada 2-1 [10][20][31][27]. Saturday’s winner faces France or Paraguay in the quarterfinals [10][20][23].

Key Points

  • Selection calls: Ismaël Koné is out for the rest of the tournament with a broken ankle, while Alphonso Davies returned against South Africa in the 75th minute for his first international appearance since March 2025 [10][11][23].
  • Morocco form: Sportsnet described Morocco as world No. 6, the African Cup of Nations holder and a team that took seven of nine points in the group stage before eliminating the No. 8 Netherlands [19].
  • Houston support: Canadian supporters in Houston planned Voyageurs events on Friday and Saturday, including gatherings before the match [7][17].
  • Viewing demand: A live Ontario watch-party map listed more than 300 daily screening locations, including 51 Canada-specific supporter locations for the match [8].
