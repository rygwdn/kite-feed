Netanyahu condemns West Bank settler attacks in Qusra

Category: World

Dozens of masked Israeli settlers attacked homes around Qusra in the occupied West Bank on Saturday, surrounding a Palestinian house and throwing stones while seven Palestinians were trapped inside, witnesses told Reuters [3][6][17][18][19][35]. The Israeli military said it sent soldiers and border police after reports that Israeli rioters had arrived and that clashes had broken out, including stone-throwing at Palestinians and other civilians; it said forces removed rioters, separated the groups and confiscated suspected vehicles for a police investigation [1][15][41][47][48]. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu condemned what he called “criminal acts of violence” in Qusra and nearby Jalud and blamed “a handful of rioters,” in his first public statement on the Qusra violence [17][30][44][60]. In Jalud, NBC News said masked settlers attacked and injured members of its reporting team and a Palestinian woman they were interviewing [11][13][22][42].

Primary Source: View original article

I strongly condemn the criminal acts of violence committed in the villages of Qusra and Jalud by a handful of rioters who are violating the law, causing a grave injustice to the law-abiding Jewish community, interfering with the IDF in carrying out its missions and harming Israel’s standing in the world.

— Benjamin Netanyahu (Newstalk ZB)

Key Points

  • Residents’ allegations: Homeowner Luay Bayruti said Israeli soldiers handcuffed, blindfolded and beat people inside the house, while Saddam Oday said troops fired tear gas into the home and did not detain settlers [17][18][19][35][42].
  • Injury reports: Oday said four people were injured by tear gas and two by stones, while Qusra municipal official Ziad Odeh told The New Arab that four people were injured and bruised after being beaten [19][39][12].
  • Fields burned: Palestinian media and local officials said settlers set fire to nearby Palestinian-owned fields, trees and land during the Qusra violence [9][12][39][48].
  • U.S. pressure: U.S. and Israeli officials told Reuters that Washington had pressed Netanyahu about two weeks earlier to publicly condemn settlers who besieged Palestinians in their homes [17][35][53][42].
  • Investigation: Netanyahu said the IDF, Shin Bet and police were conducting an intensive investigation [44][60].

Historical Background

Netanyahu previously described violent settlers as a small group, saying in December 2025 that they numbered about 70 youths and revising that figure in July 2026 to 150 “juvenile delinquents” [60]. Rights groups dispute Israel’s description of settler violence as a fringe-minority issue, citing increased attacks alongside settlement expansion [30].

Humanitarian Impact

U.N. data cited by Sky TG24 said settler violence and access restrictions displaced more than 2,200 Palestinians in the West Bank in 2026 [29]. Palestinian Health Ministry figures cited by CNA said Israeli forces and settlers had killed at least 1,103 Palestinians in the West Bank since the Gaza war began in October 2023, while Israeli figures showed at least 48 Israelis killed in Palestinian attacks or Israeli military operations over the same period [42].
