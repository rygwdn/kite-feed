September Harvest Moon rose beside Saturn on Saturday

Category: Science

The September 2026 full moon, known as the Harvest Moon, reached full phase at 12:49 p.m. EDT Saturday, Sept. 26, and rose around sunset [2][3]. It was the full moon closest to the Sept. 22 autumnal equinox and therefore designated the Harvest Moon [2][7]. Observers had another chance to see it rise Sunday, Sept. 27 [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • On Sept. 27, the Moon was in its waning gibbous phase and remained 99% illuminated, according to NASA’s Daily Moon Guide tracker [6].
