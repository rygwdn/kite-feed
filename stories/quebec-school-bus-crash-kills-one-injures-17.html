Quebec school bus crash kills one, injures 17

Category: Canada

One youth died after a school bus left the road and tipped over in Sainte-Rose-de-Watford, in Quebec’s Chaudière-Appalaches region [2][4]. Police and emergency crews rushed to the scene and took multiple passengers to hospital [4][5]. Authorities said 17 people were injured. Investigators were still working to determine what caused the crash [2][4][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Injury breakdown: Police said four people suffered serious injuries, while the rest had minor injuries. Early reports said the serious injuries were not immediately life-threatening [5][6].
  • Who was aboard (varying descriptions): La Presse described the passengers as cadets from the Chaudière-Appalaches area, while other reports referred more generally to local children [2][4].
  • Small community: La Presse described Sainte-Rose-de-Watford as a municipality of 737 residents in Chaudière-Appalaches [2].
  • Severity reports: One account said one person was critically injured as responders assessed victims at the scene [3].

Perspectives

Quebec provincial police (Sûreté du Québec): Police said the bus was travelling south on a local road when it slipped to the left before tipping over. They were working to determine the cause [4].
