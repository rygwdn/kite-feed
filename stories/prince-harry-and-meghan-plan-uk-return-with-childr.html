Prince Harry and Meghan plan UK return with children

Category: World

Prince Harry and Meghan, the Duke and Duchess of Sussex, plan to move back to the United Kingdom after more than six years in the United States. The relocation is expected later in August, and the family is set to live in a private, non-royal residence outside London [3][8][18][15][4]. Their children, Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5, have enrolled at British schools and are expected to start classes in September [8][18][15][9][11]. The couple will not resume working royal roles, and their status as private individuals and non-working members of the royal family will remain unchanged [3][7][8][24][20]. King Charles III was told of the plan on Sunday, while Prince William and Princess Catherine were also informed [3][44][35][29]. The move follows the couple’s 2020 departure from senior royal duties and their subsequent move to California, where they settled in Montecito [3][8][18][20]. A person close to the Sussexes said the family also plans to keep its Montecito home and vacation property in Portugal [8][18][26][42].

  • Public response: Buckingham Palace and representatives for Harry and Meghan declined to comment on the reports [17][55][50].
