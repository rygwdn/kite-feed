Instagram limits reach for undisclosed AI-generated profiles

Category: AI

Instagram said on Aug. 31 that it will rename its existing “AI creator” label to “AI-generated profile” and limit the reach of accounts that feature AI-generated people without disclosing that identity [2][3][7][9][18]. The label tells users when a profile features a person generated or substantially created with AI. Instagram said users had complained that some profiles appeared to show real people until users later learned the person did not exist [2][3][16]. Under the updated policy, creators who do not apply the label can lose distribution in recommendations, including visibility to non-followers through Reels, Explore and in-feed suggestions. Creators who use the label will not be penalized simply for centering an account on an AI-generated person [7][9][3]. Instagram said real creators do not need the label when they use AI to edit photos, polish captions, create graphics or make other creative changes [2][13][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Old labels: Accounts that previously used the “AI creator” label can review the new label, confirm whether it still applies or remove it if it no longer fits [2][3][9].
  • Appeal route: Creators who believe Instagram incorrectly limited their account can appeal, and adding the label or winning an appeal can restore recommendation eligibility [9][13][10].
  • Profile placement: CNET reported that the “AI-generated profile” badge appears beneath the handle and display name in the bio header, and also appears on Feed posts, Reels and Story overlays [7].
  • Recent scrutiny: TechCrunch cited a Wired investigation into more than two dozen apparently AI-generated male influencer accounts promoting the dating app Goose, while Android Headlines cited reports on synthetic wellness accounts and fake doctor personas [2][6].

Perspectives

Meta representative: A Meta representative told CNET by email that the ability to label profiles had already begun rolling out, giving creators a way to apply the disclosure themselves.

Sources: CNET

Digital Trends: Digital Trends noted that the policy creates a narrower rule for synthetic personas rather than a platformwide filter for all AI-made images, captions or videos.

Sources: Digital Trends

Historical Background

Instagram already used an “AI Info” notice to identify individual pieces of content made or edited with AI before this update, which focuses on whole profiles built around synthetic people [12]. Habr reported that the announcement followed criticism of an Instagram AI tool that let users create images using other people’s photos, after which Meta removed that feature [17].

Action Items

  • Check AI persona accounts: If you run a profile centered on an AI-generated person, edit the profile and toggle on “AI-generated profile” to avoid recommendation limits [9].
  • Review account status: If Instagram limits your profile, check Account Status and use the appeal process if you believe the label decision is wrong [13].
