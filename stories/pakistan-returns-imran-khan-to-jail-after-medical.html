Pakistan returns Imran Khan to jail after medical exam

Category: World

Pakistani authorities returned jailed former prime minister Imran Khan to Adiala Jail early Friday after a court-ordered medical examination in Islamabad, ending a hospital transfer that lasted only a few hours, according to Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf and government statements [2][7][9][10][13][27][31][44]. The Supreme Court had ordered authorities on Tuesday to take the 73-year-old to Shifa International Hospital within 48 hours after his lawyers raised health concerns, including claims about deteriorating eyesight [7][9][16][19][21][51]. Information Minister Attaullah Tarar said a team that included an ophthalmologist, a cardiologist and a physician examined Khan and declared him medically fit before he was sent back to prison [2][7][19][21][31][44]. Reports differed on where the examination took place: several accounts said Khan was examined at Shifa International Hospital, while Al Jazeera and PerthNow, carrying Reuters, reported Tarar as saying he was instead taken to the state-run Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences because of a “security situation” involving PTI workers near Shifa [14][54]. Khan has been imprisoned since August 2023 and says the cases against him are politically motivated, a claim Pakistan’s government denies [10][16][37][58]. His party said it would proceed with a nationwide march planned for September 27 to demand access to Khan, faster hearings in his cases and his release [16][31][41].

Key Points

  • Security operation: Authorities sealed roads around Shifa and deployed hundreds of police; Firstpost and Free Press Journal reported that more than 800 personnel were used during the transfer [7][9][4][43].
  • Family presence: Tarar said Khan’s sister, Uzma Khan, a medical doctor, was present throughout the examination [7][19][21][29].
  • Government challenge: The government challenged the Supreme Court’s hospital-transfer order as discriminatory, but the registrar’s office returned the review petition after procedural objections [31][41][48].
