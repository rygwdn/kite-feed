Cracked screens reported on Apple's first foldable iPhone

Category: Technology

Owners of Apple’s first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, reported cracked inner screens along the hinge after it went on sale Oct. 23, with some saying the damage appeared within hours [8][1][2]. One Australian buyer said the display broke after fewer than 20 openings; Apple replaced the phone at a store, while the cause and number of affected devices remain unknown [1][3]. Photos shared online show cracks near the fold and bright lines or dead pixels, but the circumstances behind some images, including those said to show Chinese store demonstration units, are unclear [6][17]. Apple has not publicly explained the failures [7][3]. The National reported that Apple planned to send damaged units to engineering teams in California for examination; the company has not announced a conclusion or a fix [8]. Two reviewers reported thousands of successful folds in durability tests, while a separate stress test damaged the screen when force was applied against its normal folding direction [6][4][11].

Key Points

  • Stress testing: Zack Nelson, host of the JerryRigEverything YouTube channel, found that dirt and sand jammed the hinge and prevented the phone from closing fully [4][9].

Perspectives

Affected iPhone Duo owner in Australia: The owner said phone support offered an express replacement that required a hold for the phone’s full price, with no delivery estimate. He went to an Apple Store, where staff replaced the phone immediately [1].

Sources: TechSpot

Zack Nelson, JerryRigEverything host: Nelson said the Galaxy Z Fold 8’s frame flexed and its back glass briefly lifted in his earlier bend test, but its inner display kept working [4].

Sources: SamMobile

AppleInsider: Sources at seven Apple Genius Bar stores, including four flagship locations, had not seen any Duo service visits or returns for broken screens, AppleInsider reported [5].

Sources: AppleInsider

Timeline

  • 9 September 2026: Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo, according to VnExpress [18].
  • 16 October 2026: Apple began accepting preorders for the foldable phone [18].
  • 23 October 2026: The iPhone Duo went on sale and customer damage reports surfaced [3][17].

Technical Details

  • Inner display construction: The screen is described as ten ultrathin layers combining glass, polymers, titanium and adhesives to spread mechanical stress; the reported failures have not been linked to a specific layer [6].
  • Fold-cycle tests: In published tests, Joanna Stern’s team folded the device 32,640 times and Tim Pan folded it 11,500 times; the hinge remained usable, though Pan saw a more pronounced crease [6].

Historical Background

Samsung postponed the first Galaxy Fold’s release from April to September 2019 after screens on review units failed within days, Frandroid reported [6]. Its account notes that Samsung’s subsequent foldable models have had more years of use and product development behind them than Apple’s first foldable [6].

Q&A

Q: What should owners do if they hear unusual sounds near the hinge?

A: MacObserver advised owners to watch the display and visit a retail store promptly if they hear unusual sounds near the hinge [2].

Q: What may a screen repair cost without coverage?

A: MacObserver reported a $999 charge to repair a broken inner screen without warranty coverage [2].

Action Items

  • Check warranty coverage if a Duo screen or hinge fails: Apple’s standard warranty covers failures resulting from normal use, while buyers have a 14-day return period, Tom’s Guide reported [11].
