Spotify adds kids’ music filter for Wrapped and recommendations

Category: Technology

Spotify is rolling out a setting that lets users exclude children’s and family music from their taste profile, preventing those streams from influencing recommendations such as Discover Weekly and Daily Mix, as well as the annual Wrapped recap. The feature began rolling out globally on mobile Sept. 15 for Free and Premium users, and the setting is enabled by default [18][6][7][1][12]. After users switch it off, they can still search for, play and save children’s and family music in playlists. Previously played tracks are also retroactively excluded from personalized listening data, including Wrapped’s top-song and top-artist lists. Spotify says the category includes music from children’s films and television shows, nursery rhymes and similar content [18][3][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Parents love using Spotify to play music for their families. But parents don’t always share the same taste as their young children—and playing music for your kids shouldn’t have to affect your own recommendations.

— Spotify (Spotify Newsroom)

Key Points

  • Platform availability: The toggle is available in Spotify’s iOS and Android mobile apps [6].
  • Processing delay: Spotify says personalized recommendations may take up to 48 hours to reflect the change [12][16].
  • Account coverage: The setting applies across devices signed in to the account, not just the phone where it is changed [8].
  • Separate accounts: Spotify’s Managed Accounts give children separate listening histories and recommendations, but the new toggle applies to children’s music played through an adult profile [7][12].

Perspectives

Spotify: The company described the control as part of its effort to give parents and guardians more control over how they and their children listen, alongside tools that exclude individual tracks or playlists from a taste profile [18].

Sources: Spotify Newsroom

Apple Music users: A 9to5Mac writer with a toddler said the feature addresses a problem also affecting Apple Music activity and wished Apple would introduce a comparable control [3].

Sources: 9to5Mac

Action Items

  • Exclude children’s music from your Spotify profile: In the mobile app, tap your profile picture, open Settings and privacy, select Content and display, and turn off “Include kids and family music in taste profile” [1][6].
