Anthropic adds read-only bank-account analysis to Claude for US subscribers

Category: AI

Anthropic began rolling out its Personal Finance feature in Claude on Nov. 5, letting U.S. subscribers on its Pro, Max and Ultra plans connect selected bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid, a financial-data connection service [1][3][4]. Claude can analyze balances, bills and up to 24 months of transactions, but its access is read-only: it cannot move or invest money, and Anthropic says the tool is not for investment advice [2][3][5]. The feature can answer questions about spending and payments and create financial summaries. Anthropic says it does not use conversations in the Personal Finance hub to train its models. The feature is available only in the United States, and the rollout is gradual [1][3]. OpenAI’s ChatGPT Finances uses users’ standard model-training settings, which allow them to opt out of having their conversations used for training [2].

Key Points

  • ChatGPT comparison: OpenAI’s ChatGPT Finances debuted in May and uses the user’s standard model-training settings; users must turn off “Improve the model for everyone” to opt out [2].
  • Plaid network: Plaid’s system connects to more than 12,000 financial institutions, according to Kursors.lv [5].
  • Specific tools: Claude can produce weekly spending charts and expense calendars, and explain financial terms in plain language [5].

Perspectives

Anthropic: The company says Personal Finance has no ads or sponsored recommendations and does not offer its own credit cards, loans or savings accounts; it says it wants feedback from banks, wealth managers and financial-services companies [2].

Sources: Engadget

OpenAI: The company says synced account data is deleted from its systems within 30 days after disconnection [2].

Sources: Engadget

Q&A

Q: What happens to Claude’s access if a user disconnects an account?

A: Anthropic says Claude will no longer see data previously provided through Personal Finance after the bank account is unlinked [2].
