Oil falls 3% as Trump reportedly gives Iran diplomacy more time

Category: Business

Oil prices fell more than 3% on Monday after reports that President Donald Trump wants to give diplomacy with Iran more time before deciding whether to resume military action after the U.S. midterm elections on Tuesday, Nov. 3. Brent crude dropped below $110 a barrel and West Texas Intermediate fell below $101 [2][7][8]. Trump told Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in a Saturday call that he preferred diplomacy, according to an Axios report cited by Bloomberg. U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said no decision had been made on new strikes [4]. Iranian and Qatari officials were holding a third consecutive day of discussions. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said a proposal could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days if Washington meets conditions. Attacks on vessels continued, and oil-product shipments remained constrained even as crude exports from the Persian Gulf neared prewar levels [7][4][5]. OPEC+ agreed to keep December output targets unchanged [4].

Key Points

  • Diesel squeeze: Diesel prices have surged in the United States and Europe, while spare refining capacity is concentrated in China, whose authorities tightly control fuel exports [4].
  • Forecast revision: Goldman Sachs raised its Brent forecast to $105 a barrel for December and an average of $88 next year, citing geopolitical supply risks [4].
  • Vessel damage: Qatari LNG shipper Nakilat said its Al Mayeda was damaged by unidentified objects in the Persian Gulf on Friday night; the vessel remained operational and no crew members were injured [5][8].
  • Market risk: ING analysts said investors may reassess the geopolitical risk premium if the midterms pass without renewed strikes or a diplomatic agreement [7].
