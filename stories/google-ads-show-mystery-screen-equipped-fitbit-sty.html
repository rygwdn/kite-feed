Google ads show mystery screen-equipped Fitbit-style tracker

Category: Technology

Google Pixel 11 ads on YouTube appear to show an unannounced fitness tracker with a screen that is not the Pixel Watch 5 or Fitbit Air, according to 9to5Google and Digital Trends [1][2][3]. The ads, spotted by Reddit users over the past day, promote the refreshed Pixel lineup and show the mystery wearable alongside Pixel 11 phones and Google devices in matching colors [1][3]. The device appears to have a small, oblong display with what 9to5Google described as a condensed version of the Pixel Watch 5 interface, while its body resembles the pebble-like Fitbit Air and the overall look of a Fitbit Charge-style tracker [1][3]. Neither outlet said Google had confirmed the device, and 9to5Google said it is hard to know whether the tracker is real at all [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • User sightings: 9to5Google said a Reddit user posted screenshots of multiple ads and that it confirmed at least one other user had seen the same ads [3].
  • Ad variants: One ad showed two Pixel 11 phones and a Pixel Watch 5 in “Fog” colors, while another focused on green-colored devices in the 2026 Pixel lineup [3].

Perspectives

9to5Google: Ben Schoon wrote that his first reaction was that the device might be AI-generated, but he said seeing the same device in two ads made that explanation less certain.

Sources: 9to5Google

Digital Trends: Digital Trends said the appearance could be significant but cautioned that the ad placement could simply be a mistake.
