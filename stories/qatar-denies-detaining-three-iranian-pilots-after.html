Qatar denies detaining three Iranian pilots after March downing

Category: World

Qatar rejected Iran’s claim that Qatari forces have held three Iranian pilots since March, after Tehran asked the International Committee of the Red Cross to intervene in the case [4][2][1][5][11][29]. Iranian Brig. Gen. Mohammad Bagherzadeh identified the pilots as Javad Salehi, Abdolmajid Dashtian and Omran Behraveshian, saying they were captured alive after Su-24 fighter jets crashed or were downed during a March 2 mission targeting a military base in Qatar [3][1][26][38][55]. Qatari Foreign Ministry spokesperson Majed Al Ansari said Doha “categorically” denied detaining Iranian pilots, said the aircraft had violated Qatari airspace and said Qatari forces tried to communicate with the pilots before taking measures to defend the country under international law [4][1][19][31][24]. Qatar said search-and-rescue teams later found the remains of one pilot, contacted Iran to coordinate a handover and invited an Iranian team in April to review the operation, but said Tehran did not respond [5][19][39][37][29].

We categorically deny the claims circulating regarding the detention of Iranian pilots & are surprised by these misleading statements at this particular time, amid the ongoing diplomatic efforts & initiatives aimed at de-escalating tensions in the region.

Key Points

  • Family contact: Iran alleged the three pilots had not been allowed to meet or contact their families or Iranian officials handling the case [3][5][23][48].
  • Base target: The March 2 operation involved Al Udeid Air Base, which hosts forward components of US Central Command and is described as the largest US base in the region [3][13][33].
  • Public disclosure: Iran had previously reported one pilot killed and others missing, but had not said publicly that the missing pilots were captured until this August claim [2][18][5][57].

Historical Background

The Middle East war began on February 28, when the United States and Israel launched strikes on Iran, and Tehran retaliated with attacks across the region [3][13][48]. Diplomatic efforts produced an April ceasefire after nearly 40 days of fighting, followed by a June framework for peace talks that later collapsed [3][13]. Qatar has maintained ties with Iran while also hosting major US military facilities [30][3].
