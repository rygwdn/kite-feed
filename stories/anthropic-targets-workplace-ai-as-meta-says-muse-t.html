Anthropic targets workplace AI as Meta says Muse tops 10 million

Category: AI

Anthropic introduced Claude Duet, a work-focused AI agent for professionals and small businesses, while Meta chief executive Mark Zuckerberg said more than 10 million people in the United States and Canada had set up its Muse agent [14][13]. Duet is available as a research preview for U.S. Pro and Max subscribers. OpenAI’s Dots agent also targets ongoing work [14][10]. OpenAI, PerturbAI and Scripps Research said they deployed nearly 2,000 GPT-Rosalind agents to analyze a mouse-brain CRISPR atlas and generate research leads in 32 minutes [13]. Anthropic acknowledges Duet can misread conversations or misjudge urgency, and users cannot yet watch it work in a browser [8]. A GeekPark analysis described the risk of “personal agent fatigue,” arguing that users may abandon a tool if opening another app and approving tasks adds friction [47].

Key Points

  • Meta said shopping was Muse’s biggest use case: more than a quarter of active users used it for shopping, with average purchases above $200 [6].
  • OpenAI said Dots agents can maintain software, revise projects and update scientific analyses when new data arrives [13].
  • Workplace focus: Anthropic’s examples center on business operations, including checking unpaid invoices, preparing payroll and drafting hiring posts, rather than everyday errands [8][21].
  • Trust and control: Anthropic says Duet can refuse to share credit-card or government-ID data and requires an explicit instruction before sending health or personal-finance details [12][44].

Perspectives

Meta: Zuckerberg said the company plans a “Muse Confidential VM” intended to prevent Meta itself from seeing users’ content [6].

Sources: Quartz

TechRepublic: The publication’s analysis says businesses connecting agents to internal apps need to decide what data the agents can access and establish procedures for oversight and recording actions [31].

Industry Impact

  • Advertising and commerce: Yahoo said shopping agents could threaten search and display ads by directing consumers to price comparisons. Meta is pursuing transaction fees through a commerce protocol with partners including Walmart, Stripe and Shopify [6].

Historical Background

The current agent launches follow the rise of OpenClaw, an open-source personal agent built by programmer Peter Steinberger, according to R&D World [13]. Steinberger joined OpenAI in February with the stated goal of building an agent “that even my mum can use,” while OpenClaw moved to an independent foundation [13].

Business Angle

  • Agent usage that counts against existing subscription limits may increase engagement but could also limit how much background work subscribers can afford to delegate [9][31].
  • Meta’s reported third-quarter advertising revenue reached $63.06 billion, with ad pricing up 14%, providing a funding source Anthropic lacks [6].
  • Yahoo argued that agents’ access to accounts and ability to spend raise questions about who bears the cost when an agent buys the wrong item [6].

Q&A

Q: Will Duet constantly monitor connected accounts?

A: No. Anthropic says Duet checks them every few hours; more frequent checks would use up subscribers’ existing plan limits too quickly [2].
