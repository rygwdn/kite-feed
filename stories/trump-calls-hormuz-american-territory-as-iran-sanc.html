Trump calls Hormuz ‘American territory’ as Iran sanctions loom

Category: World

President Trump said Iran “would love to make a deal” but was “not ready to make the right deal,” and called the Strait of Hormuz “American territory” in remarks tied to a South Carolina rally and earlier comments to reporters at Joint Base Andrews [5][6][19][37]. The comments followed Washington’s threat to impose what Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called the “toughest sanctions in history,” with details expected on Monday, and the United States’ continuing blockade of Iranian shipping and ports [15][22][12]. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian said it was better to end the war while Tehran was in a “position of power and dignity,” defended a June memorandum of understanding with Washington and rejected criticism that Iran had surrendered concessions [1][18][21][28]. Major General Ali Abdollahi, Iran’s military chief, warned of “crushing, punishing and devastating responses” to new U.S. threats. The two countries were not exchanging fire or pursuing active peace talks as the conflict neared six months [9][22][12].

Key Points

  • Military options: Trump said sanctions did not limit U.S. military options, telling reporters Washington was “seeing what happens” [17][34][45].
  • Regional trade: Iran said it finalized a preferential trade agreement with Oman as Tehran sought stronger regional commercial ties under U.S. pressure [3].
  • China exposure: China buys more than 80% of Iran’s shipped oil, according to 2025 Kpler data cited by Reuters-based reports [38][47].
