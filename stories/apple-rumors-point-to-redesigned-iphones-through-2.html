Apple rumors point to redesigned iPhones through 2028

Category: Technology

Recent reports and commentary describe a possible change in Apple’s iPhone roadmap, centered on a 20th-anniversary model in 2027, a revised iPhone 18 launch cadence, and a follow-up 2028 device that may refine the same design direction [5][7][6]. Apple has not announced these products, and several accounts frame the details as leaks, rumors or analyst expectations rather than confirmed plans [4][6]. The rumored anniversary iPhone is described as moving closer to Jony Ive’s long-discussed “single slab of glass” concept, with a near-borderless display that curves over the sides, an all-glass body, a roughly 7-inch screen, and possible under-display Face ID and front-camera technology that would remove the Dynamic Island cutout [5][8]. 9to5Mac said the device may not arrive as a direct Pro-series successor because the iPhone 17 Pro reportedly shifted toward a more utilitarian aluminum design for thermal performance while the iPhone Air occupied the thinner, more premium glass-and-titanium position [5][8]. Digital Trends reported that Apple may split future iPhone launches across fall and spring, with the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and a long-rumored foldable model taking the fall 2027 slot while the standard iPhone shifts later [7]. MacRumors reported that a 2028 iPhone may keep the curved all-glass direction while addressing visible compromises in the 2027 version, including display brightness or distortion in curved areas [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Launch structure: Digital Trends said a split schedule would prevent a regular iPhone from competing for attention with Pro models, the foldable iPhone, the “e” series and the Air line at one keynote [7].
  • Foldable focus: NextApple reported that Apple’s first foldable phone may use an inward book-style design, launch as “iPhone Ultra” or “iPhone Fold,” and emphasize a reduced crease and more durable hinge [9].
  • Price talk: Digitech Bytes said leaks point to possible iPhone 18 price increases tied to higher manufacturing costs and new features, while India Today separately reported that the iPhone 18 Pro may receive Apple’s biggest price hike in years [4][10].

Perspectives

NextApple: NextApple framed the foldable iPhone, touchscreen MacBook, Apple TV 4K, HomePod mini 2 and a smart-home hub as part of a wider fall hardware refresh tied partly to Siri AI and home devices.

Sources: NextApple

Technical Details

  • Indium zinc oxide cathodes: MacRumors reported that a 2028 switch to more transparent indium zinc oxide cathodes could help preserve brightness and image quality where a display curves around the phone’s edges [6].
  • Solid-state haptic buttons: MacRumors said the 2028 device may adopt solid-state haptic buttons integrated into the frame, replacing moving physical buttons with pressure-sensitive controls that simulate clicks [6].

Industry Impact

  • Smartphone competition: NextApple noted that Samsung launched the Galaxy Fold in 2019, while Apple has yet to enter foldables, leaving Apple’s rumored model aimed at a high-end category that rivals have already commercialized [9].
  • Smart-home hardware: NextApple said three of the five rumored fall products are tied to the home, including a new Apple TV 4K, HomePod mini 2 and a screen-equipped HomePad-style hub [9].
  • Mac lineup: NextApple reported that a touchscreen MacBook could use an OLED touch display and macOS interface changes such as pull-to-refresh, direct touch input and finger-friendly controls [9].

Historical Background

Apple introduced the original iPhone in 2007, making 2027 the product line’s 20th anniversary [common]. The Dynamic Island arrived on the iPhone 14 Pro generation in 2022, replacing the notch with an interactive display cutout on supported models [common].

Business Angle

  • A split launch schedule could smooth Apple’s product marketing calendar, but it may also make year-over-year iPhone comparisons harder for investors to interpret [7][common].
  • New display materials and under-screen components could protect differentiation, but added complexity can pressure margins if yields are poor [6][common].

Action Items

  • Avoid timing assumptions: If you plan to upgrade, compare current devices against announced products rather than relying on rumored 2027 or 2028 release windows [7][6].
