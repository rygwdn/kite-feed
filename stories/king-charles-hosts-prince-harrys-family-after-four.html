King Charles hosts Prince Harry's family after four-year gap

Category: World

King Charles III and Queen Camilla hosted Prince Harry, Meghan, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet at Highgrove House on Friday, July 10, Buckingham Palace confirmed, marking the first meeting between the king and Harry’s full family in about four years [1][2][6][9][20][21][62]. The palace described the meeting at the king’s private country residence in Gloucestershire as a private family occasion and said it would release no photos or further details [2][29][31][62][75]. Harry arrived in the United Kingdom on Monday for charity engagements and events tied to the one-year countdown to the 2027 Invictus Games in Birmingham, the sporting competition for wounded veterans that he founded [2][21][22][42][75]. Before the visit, British authorities rejected Harry’s request for taxpayer-funded police protection for his family, and royal officials withdrew an offer for him to stay at Buckingham Palace after he did not accept it in time [2][12][13][34][76]. Harry and Meghan stepped back from royal duties in 2020 and moved to California. Their children, Archie, 7, and Lilibet, 5, had not seen their paternal grandfather in person since 2022 [5][15][21][22][62]. Harry’s trip also coincided with a court defeat in his privacy case against the publisher of the Daily Mail; a judge ruled that he had failed to prove his claims, and a further hearing on possible legal costs is scheduled for July 29-30 [12][13][36][22].

Key Points

  • Travel route: Press Association said Meghan and the children traveled to the United Kingdom from an unspecified European destination; CNN cited British media reports that the family had been vacationing in Portugal before the Highgrove meeting [21][32][36][43][75].
  • William absent: Reports said Prince William was at a charity polo match in Windsor during the Highgrove gathering, and it was unclear whether Harry would meet his brother [37][45][23][50].
  • Public schedule: Harry visited Birmingham Children’s Hospital and took part in Invictus-related events before the family meeting, while Meghan was not expected to appear publicly during the trip [42][77][62].
  • Legal battle: Harry previously lost a legal challenge against the Home Office over a downgrade in his police protection, a change he said prevented him from safely bringing his family [2][46].

Perspectives

Sussex representatives: Representatives for Harry said he was still considering options to make the visit safe and allow his children to spend time in the United Kingdom.
