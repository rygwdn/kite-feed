Ontario Progressive Conservatives allow media at Ford speech

Category: Canada

Ontario’s Progressive Conservative Party says it will allow journalists to attend and report on Premier Doug Ford’s speech at the party’s upcoming convention, reversing an earlier plan that would have restricted media access [1][2][3][4]. The change restores a level of openness that reporters have had at these events during Ford’s time as premier, after the party initially signalled a different approach for this convention [1][3]. The party attributed the planned restriction to internal confusion, describing it as a “miscommunication,” and confirmed it would proceed with on-the-record media coverage of the speech [2][1]. For Ontarians following provincial politics, the reversal means the convention’s marquee address will again be subject to direct questioning and public scrutiny, a small but meaningful win for transparency in the day-to-day of democracy [1][2].

Key Points

  • First since 2018: The proposed restriction would have been the first time since Ford became premier in 2018 that journalists were barred from accessing the convention speech, according to coverage of the party’s initial plan [1][3].
  • Party explanation: The Globe and Mail reported that Ford’s campaign manager characterized the episode as a “miscommunication” by the party, as it moved to reverse the decision [2].
  • Reporter access: Global News reported the party decided reporters will be permitted to cover the speech after it had initially planned to prevent journalists from accessing the event [1].
