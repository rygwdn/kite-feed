OpenAI plans screenless ChatGPT speaker for 2027 launch

Category: Technology

OpenAI is developing a mobile, screen-free smart speaker as its first consumer hardware device, Bloomberg reported, with the product designed as a home AI companion and a physical version of ChatGPT [20][2][4][5][14]. The device remains under development and is expected to be unveiled in 2026 and released in 2027, though several reports said the timing could change [5][17][26][38]. The speaker is expected to control smart-home appliances, play media, answer questions, respond to messages and use ChatGPT capabilities [3][10][14][29]. Reports said OpenAI wants the device to learn about its owner over time, become more personalized and proactive, and draw on personal information such as emails to better understand the user [2][5][17][26]. Apple filed a trade-secret lawsuit against OpenAI last week, accusing the company of stealing information tied to its consumer-hardware push; OpenAI has denied wrongdoing [2][10][5][1]. The project follows OpenAI’s acquisition of Jony Ive’s io Products [30][32][23].

Key Points

  • Legal claims: Apple’s complaint also names two former Apple employees and alleges OpenAI used former staff, recruiting and supplier relationships to obtain confidential information for consumer hardware [30][32].
  • Internal framing: OpenAI internally describes the speaker-like product as an AI-built computer intended to help busy people be more productive, not simply as a conventional smart speaker [12][29][38].

Technical Details

  • GPT-Live voice mode: Reports describe GPT-Live as a new or upgraded ChatGPT voice mode that can listen and speak at the same time, allowing interruptions and faster conversational turn-taking [33][42][26][36].
  • Environmental sensing: The speaker reportedly combines a camera with other sensors to recognize surroundings and context, including nearby objects or people, so responses can account for the room rather than only the user’s words [4][12][38].
  • Mechanical motion: Reports describe self-moving mechanical components, but do not specify whether the motion involves full locomotion, directional movement or expressive parts within the enclosure [2][7][5].

Industry Impact

  • Smart-home platforms: The device would place OpenAI in direct competition with Apple HomePod, Amazon Alexa devices and Google smart-home products in the home-assistant category [3][29][31].
  • AI hardware teams: OpenAI’s hardware effort includes Jony Ive’s LoveFrom and many former Apple designers and engineers who worked on products such as the iPhone and Mac [2][30][32].
  • Product roadmap: OpenAI’s hardware unit is reportedly working on about five products, starting with the speaker and later exploring a mobile AI device that could replace smartphones, wearables such as a pendant, and home robotics [29][38][31].

Historical Background

Amazon introduced the Echo and Alexa in 2014, Google followed with Google Home in 2016, and Apple entered the smart-speaker market with HomePod in 2018 [28].
