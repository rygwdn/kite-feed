B.C. officials urge caution on ICE property deals

Category: Canada

A Vancouver-based company linked to billionaire Jim Pattison is facing public pressure over a proposed sale of a Virginia warehouse to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), which the agency wants to use as a holding and “processing centre” [1][3]. The plan has prompted boycott calls in Canada and local pushback in Virginia, where residents are organizing demonstrations and a suburban county is exploring legal options [3]. In British Columbia, the attorney general has publicly urged Canadian business leaders to think carefully about the implications of supporting ICE operations as scrutiny grows over the agency’s enforcement practices [2][3].
