Avi Lewis leads NDP leadership race fundraising

Category: Canada

Federal NDP leadership candidate Avi Lewis has emerged as the top fundraiser in the contest to choose the party’s next leader, according to Elections Canada filings reported by multiple outlets [1][2][3]. The reporting says Lewis is ahead of Edmonton MP Heather McPherson, described as the only sitting New Democrat MP running in the race so far [1][2][3]. Fundraising totals can signal early organizational strength, but they do not necessarily predict the final outcome of a leadership vote [common]. Still, the numbers in the filings suggest Lewis has built a strong donor network early, while McPherson’s second-place showing indicates a meaningful base of support among donors, too [2][1].
