Anthropic opens Claude Duet agent preview to U.S. subscribers

Category: AI

Anthropic has opened a waitlist-based research preview of Claude Duet, a proactive AI agent for U.S. Claude subscribers that runs in a virtual machine and can use connected apps while users are away [1][3]. Pro and Max subscribers can access the preview through mobile apps; Anthropic says Duet is included in plans starting at $20 a month and shares usage limits with other Claude tools [1]. Duet can prepare daily briefings, draft replies, check invoices and prepare payroll, but requires user approval before sending messages, making payments or signing documents [3]. The preview remains limited to the United States and excludes Team and Enterprise customers [1].

Key Points

  • Approval controls: Anthropic says Duet cannot trade securities or move money between accounts, even with user approval [3].
  • App connections: The agent can draw on more than 6,000 connectors and plugins in Anthropic’s Claude Marketplace [3].
  • Check frequency: Anthropic designed Duet to check connected apps every few hours rather than continuously to conserve shared usage limits [3][1].

Perspectives

Amin Ebrahimi, owner of Shahs of Kabob: Ebrahimi said he used the preview for accounting, customer support, marketing and store-by-store invoices at his South Florida restaurant [3].
