B.C. wildfires prompt alerts near Osoyoos, Boston Bar

Category: Canada

An evacuation alert was issued Monday for dozens of homes and properties on Cougar Court and Sasquatch Trail on Anarchist Mountain, east of Osoyoos, B.C., while local firefighters, the BC Wildfire Service and the South Okanagan Task Force fought a blaze near the Crowsnest Highway. The Anarchist Mountain Fire Department said the fire was being held as of 4 p.m. and asked people to avoid the area. The cause remained under investigation [2][3]. Near Boston Bar, crews took advantage of rain and cooler weekend weather to attack the Brunswick Creek and Ainslie Creek fires directly before fire activity was expected to increase. The Brunswick complex has changed little since last week and covers more than 188 square kilometres [1][2][4][5]. The fires have triggered evacuation orders and alerts for hundreds of properties, destroyed some structures and forced Highway 1 in the Fraser Canyon to close last week before it reopened [1][4][5].

Key Points

  • Osoyoos timing: Postmedia reported the Anarchist Mountain fire was discovered Monday morning north of Highway 3, with an evacuation alert issued at 3:30 p.m. for homes along Sasquatch Trail and Cougar Court [8].
  • Firefighting priorities: The BC Wildfire Service said crews near Boston Bar were working to keep the Highway 1 corridor open, protect structures near Boothroyd and build fire lines on the southwest flank of the Ainslie Creek blaze [1][2][5].
  • Fire sizes: Postmedia listed two wildfires of note near Boston Bar: Brunswick Creek at more than 30 square kilometres and Ainslie Creek at almost 160 square kilometres, both out of control [8].

Timeline

  • 2 July 2026: Brunswick Creek fire discovered west of Highway 1 [8]
  • 4 July 2026: Ainslie Creek fire discovered east of Highway 1 [8]

  • 13 July 2026: Osoyoos-area evacuation alert issued for Sasquatch Trail, Cougar Court [8]

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: Residents on Cougar Court and Sasquatch Trail were told to be ready to leave as crews fought the Anarchist Mountain blaze [2][3]. People under evacuation alert should be prepared to leave immediately.
  • Roads: Crews are working to keep the Highway 1 corridor open; check conditions before travelling through the Fraser Canyon [1][5].
