Ottawa unveils official portrait of former PM Harper

Category: Canada

Former prime minister Stephen Harper’s official portrait is being unveiled today at a ceremony on Parliament Hill, timed to coincide with the 20th anniversary of the election that first brought his Conservative government to power in 2006 [1][3][4]. The event takes place in the Sir John A. Macdonald Building in downtown Ottawa, with Prime Minister Mark Carney scheduled to deliver remarks highlighting Harper’s contributions to public life and to the country [1][4]. The painting, which will join the existing series of prime ministerial portraits in the West Block foyer of the House of Commons, was completed in 2023 by Canadian artist Phil Richards, known for creating the official Canadian Diamond Jubilee portrait of Queen Elizabeth II in 2012 [4]. This unveiling is part of a trio of commemorative events in Ottawa this week celebrating Harper’s legacy, including a medal from the Royal Canadian Geographical Society and a separate anniversary gala [4].

Key Points

  • Carney’s remarks: Ahead of the ceremony, Prime Minister Mark Carney called it “a very important day” and described the unveiling as “a chance to reflect on [Harper’s] contributions and how great this country is” [4].
  • Artist background: Portrait artist Phil Richards began work on Harper’s painting in 2022 and delivered it in 2023, adding to a portfolio that includes the 2012 Canadian Diamond Jubilee portrait of Queen Elizabeth II commissioned for the monarch’s 60th year on the throne [4].
  • Series of portraits: Harper’s painting becomes the 22nd official portrait of a former Canadian prime minister, the first addition to the collection since Paul Martin’s portrait was unveiled in 2015, and will hang in the West Block foyer of the House of Commons [4].
  • Legacy celebrations: The portrait unveiling forms one of three Ottawa events marking Harper’s time in office, alongside a medal from the Royal Canadian Geographical Society recognizing his public service and a gala celebrating the 20th anniversary of his government’s election, scheduled for Wednesday evening [4].
  • Political milestone: Harper first became prime minister after the 2006 federal election, two years after he helped create the modern unified Conservative Party, and he went on to lead it through additional victories in 2008 and 2011 [4].
