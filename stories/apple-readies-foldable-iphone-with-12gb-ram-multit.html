Apple readies foldable iPhone with 12GB RAM, multitasking

Category: Technology

A wave of supply-chain and software reports suggests Apple is preparing its first book-style foldable iPhone for a launch later in 2026, with a design that shifts from a phone-like exterior to a tablet-like experience when unfolded [1][7][16]. Several outlets say Apple is tuning iOS for the larger inner screen, including iPad-style layouts and side-by-side app viewing, so the unfolded device feels more like a small-tablet workspace than a stretched phone interface [7][16][15]. On the hardware and pricing front, reports say the device could ship with 12GB of RAM, with Samsung supplying the memory modules as DRAM prices rise amid tight global supply and strong AI-server demand [1][14][17]. Separate leaks point to three storage tiers—256GB, 512GB, and 1TB—along with rough, region-dependent pricing estimates that often land above $2,000, putting the foldable in premium territory that can rival high-end laptops on cost [2][3][6][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Memory costs: The Bell’s report, cited by multiple outlets, says the price for a 12GB LPDDR5X module used in iPhones rose from about $30 in early 2025 to roughly $70 in early 2026, as AI-server build-outs tighten memory supply [1][14].
  • Panel orders: Display-industry reporting says Apple ordered 11 million foldable OLED panels from Samsung Display late in 2025, then increased the order by about 20% (roughly 2 million more). The report says the boost is welcome as China’s smartphone display market weakens [10].
  • Name debate: AppleInsider cites a separate Weibo rumor claiming Apple may brand the foldable as “iPhone Ultra,” with suggested pricing starting above 10,000 yuan and top configurations exceeding 20,000 yuan [4].
  • CAD leak: Another leak claims CAD images show what could be a near-final design for a fall 2026 device, reinforcing the broader picture of a book-style foldable form factor [8].

Perspectives

Bloomberg (Mark Gurman): Cast Apple’s foldable work as an effort to refine screen ratios and optimize iOS so the unfolded view behaves more like an iPad (including split-view-style multitasking) than a scaled-up phone UI.

Sources: PCMag

Apple supply chain watchers (The Bell report): Said Samsung will ship 12GB DRAM modules starting in Q2 2026, after negotiating higher pricing as memory supply tightens.

Sources: MacRumors

Weibo leaker Instant Digital: Claimed Apple is planning a three-tier storage lineup for the foldable, with yuan-denominated pricing that (via rough conversion) puts the top configuration near the high-$2,000 range, depending on exchange assumptions.

Sources: MacRumors

Macworld: Argued Apple’s U.S. pricing could land closer to $1,999–$2,399 if it follows its usual China-to-U.S. pricing pattern rather than strict currency conversion.

Sources: Macworld

MobileSyrup: Suggested Apple’s approach could make foldables easier to use by avoiding stretched interfaces and leaning into tablet-like layouts that many apps already support.

Sources: MobileSyrup

Timeline

  • February 2025: Early 2025: 12GB LPDDR5X module priced around $30 [1].
  • December 2025: Late 2025: Apple reportedly ordered 11 million foldable OLED panels [10].
  • 12 March 2026: Reports detail 12GB RAM sourcing and storage tiers [1][2].
  • April 2026: Q2 2026: Samsung DRAM shipments expected to begin [1].
  • Fall 2026: Later in 2026: launch timing is repeatedly rumored for the second half of 2026 [1][2].

Technical Details

  • Foldable display economics: Industry reporting pegs the foldable inner AMOLED panel at around $250 each, and notes Apple’s ramp would also require additional AMOLED panels for the external display [10].
  • Foldable UI behaviors: Bloomberg-reported software work centers on iOS adapting to the unfolded, wider display with iPad-like layouts and split-view/side-by-side app behavior, while remaining iOS (not iPadOS) and not positioning the device as an iPad-app platform [7][16].

Industry Impact

  • Memory supply chain: Reporting says Apple is leaning more heavily on Samsung for iPhone DRAM to steady deliveries as prices rise, while other DRAM vendors (including SK hynix and Micron) are expected to join the foldable’s supply chain later [1].
  • Display market: Apple’s reported 20% increase in foldable OLED panel orders would add roughly 2 million more panels, which OLED industry reporting says is a positive for Samsung Display during a weaker smartphone display market in China [10].
  • Foldable software competition: Coverage emphasizes Apple tuning iOS—and the device’s unfolded aspect ratio—toward a more tablet-like layout to address the “stretched UI” critique of many foldables, while still keeping it an iPhone-class OS experience rather than turning it into an iPad app device [7][16][11].
