Anthropic's Claude agent enters workplace AI race with 6,000 connectors and plugins

Category: AI

Anthropic opened a U.S. research preview of Claude Duet, an AI agent designed to monitor work apps and carry out tasks for professionals and small businesses, competing with Meta and OpenAI [4][13]. Duet uses Claude Opus 5.5 to initiate and coordinate work across services, drawing on more than 6,000 connectors. It checks connected accounts every few hours rather than continuously because background activity counts toward subscribers’ usage limits [11][21]. The product is still an early test. Anthropic says it can misread conversations, misjudge urgency or miss updates arriving between checks; users cannot yet see the agent’s cloud browser as it works [11][34][43]. Anthropic also says Duet is less capable at personal errands such as booking tickets [13][17]. Meta chief executive Mark Zuckerberg said more than 10 million people in the United States and Canada had set up Muse agents in the preceding six weeks [13]. Duet’s preview is limited to U.S. Claude Pro and Max subscribers admitted through a waitlist; Anthropic has not announced when Team or Enterprise customers will get access [1][8].

Key Points

  • Security design: Anthropic says a dedicated classifier and safety prompt screen messages, block attempts to disclose credit-card numbers and government IDs, and release health or personal-finance information only on explicit user instructions [11][20].
  • Early-user example: Real-estate agent Jess Provencher said Duet caught an incorrect home-viewing date in her schedule [46].
  • Agent commerce: Stripe’s Link uses a virtual card for Duet purchases so users do not have to share their actual card numbers [21][20].
  • Price comparison: Engadget reported that Anthropic plans to include Duet in its $20-a-month Pro plan after general release, while access to OpenAI’s Dots starts with a $100-a-month Pro tier [16].

Technical Details

  • Prompt-injection defense: Emails and webpages can contain hidden instructions intended to redirect an agent. Anthropic says Duet treats viewed content as information, screens actions and requires approval for consequential steps, while acknowledging that no defense is perfect [11][52].
  • Credential separation: Anthropic says Duet can access connected services without directly seeing passwords or sign-in tokens [11][37].

Industry Impact

  • Business software integrations: Duet connects with Marketplace tools for email, accounting, payroll, customer management and e-commerce to carry out tasks in existing business software [11][21].
  • Online commerce: Stripe Link and Shopify Shop Pay let Duet complete purchases after user approval [20].

Historical Background

At the turn of 2026, independent developer Peter Steinberger’s OpenClaw drew attention to multifunctional agents — software able to work across email, spreadsheets, calendars and other services. Unlike later consumer-facing apps, OpenClaw required technical expertise to install [39].
