Paramount names Mattel’s Ynon Kreiz co-CEO ahead of Warner Bros. Discovery deal

Category: Business

Paramount Skydance named outgoing Mattel chief executive Ynon Kreiz co-CEO of the combined Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, alongside David Ellison. Kreiz is due to start on October 5, and Paramount expects to close its $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery on October 6 [3][7]. On September 30, a U.S. judge approved a settlement with 12 states that had sued to block the merger, clearing the way for the deal to close. Under the announced division of duties, Ellison will lead strategy, creative direction and technology, while Kreiz will oversee daily operations and integration [11][9].
