Canadian artillery gunner dies during Latvia deployment

Category: Canada

Canada’s military says Gunner Sebastian Halmagean has died near Riga, Latvia, while deployed with the Canadian-led NATO Multinational Brigade Latvia on Operation REASSURANCE [4][2][3][5][1]. The Department of National Defence said the death occurred on Jan. 29, 2026, during Halmagean’s first overseas deployment [4][1]. Officials have not released the circumstances of his death, and the military says an investigation is underway [4][3][5]. The announcement has prompted condolences and renewed attention in Canada to the role Canadian troops play in NATO’s forward presence in Latvia [4][2].
