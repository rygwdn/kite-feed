Update: Ontario and Quebec face more storms, outages

Category: Canada

A new round of winter weather brought blizzard-like conditions in parts of Ontario and Quebec, making driving and road conditions hazardous as storm fronts continued to move through the region [1][4]. In Ontario, Hydro One reported thousands of customers still without electricity after snow, freezing rain and strong winds, while warning that additional high winds could trigger more outages [7]. Environment Canada issued advisories for blowing snow and snow squalls in southwestern Ontario and northern parts of the province [7]. Officials in Springwater, Ont., also urged residents to stay off roads during the significant weather event [5].

Did you know: Environment Canada advisories for snow squalls and blowing snow are intended to warn of rapidly changing visibility and road conditions over short distances [7].

Key Points

  • Closed highways: Ontario Provincial Police urged drivers to avoid closed highways as some routes remained shut following the storm’s impacts in parts of the province [2].
  • Slow restorations: Hydro One said challenging road conditions have slowed repair crews in some areas as they work to restore service [7].
  • Mixed hazards: Storm impacts varied across Ontario, with freezing rain, rain and wind affecting southern areas while northeastern Ontario faced heavy snow and near-blizzard conditions [6].
  • Local emergency alert: The Township of Springwater issued an emergency alert tied to the weather event and road closures, reinforcing calls to limit travel [2].
