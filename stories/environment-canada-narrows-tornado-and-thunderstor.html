Environment Canada narrows tornado and thunderstorm alerts nationwide

Category: Canada

Environment and Climate Change Canada this week began using more localized tornado and severe thunderstorm warnings for people in Canada during severe weather events [2][3][7]. The upgraded system uses GPS-based polygon targeting to send alerts to people inside a specific outline of a storm’s expected path, instead of entire regions [1][5][6]. The IFP reported that the system launched Tuesday, Aug. 11, and will notify people within 30 minutes of a storm’s direct path [1]. Environment Canada and other reports said the change is intended to reduce “alert fatigue” from repeated phone warnings and provide clearer, more timely information about tornado and severe thunderstorm threats [2][3][6].

Key Points

  • Alert scope: The initial rollout applies to tornado and severe thunderstorm warnings, not every type of weather alert [1][2].
  • Hazard priority: Environment Canada senior meteorologist Jonathan Bau said officials chose these warnings first because wind gusts, heavy rain and related hazards can have high impacts [1].
