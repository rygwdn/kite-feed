China restricts trade with 56 U.S. companies

Category: Business

China imposed new trade restrictions on Monday targeting 56 U.S. companies, adding 10 defense, drone, aerospace and rare earth-related firms to its export control list and barring Chinese government procurement from 46 other U.S. companies [3][2][32][13][6][12]. The Commerce Ministry said Chinese exporters may no longer supply dual-use items to the 10 listed entities; the Finance Ministry blocked government entities from buying products from companies including units of Lockheed Martin, Raytheon and General Dynamics [13][15][23][70][12]. Beijing said the export controls respond to the U.S. Defense Department's expansion of its list of companies it says are linked to China's military, which added firms including Alibaba, Baidu and BYD earlier this month [3][6][13][16][12]. The 10 companies named by China include Aveox, Red Cat Holdings, Teal Drones, Jaia Robotics, Ball Aerospace & Technologies, Oshkosh Defense, L3Harris Maritime Services, MP Materials and USA Rare Earth [2][23][70][57]. The measures took effect immediately on June 22. China said ongoing covered export activity must stop unless the Commerce Ministry approves a special export application [23][70][58][73]. Reports placed the move about a month after President Trump visited Beijing and met Chinese President Xi Jinping in May, with later U.S. and Chinese actions focused on technology, defense procurement and critical minerals [19][18][22][39].

Key Points

  • Rare earth targets: MP Materials and USA Rare Earth were among the listed firms, and Bloomberg said both are part of Washington's effort to build critical-minerals supply chains outside China [5][2][23][41].
  • Extraterritorial reach: China's export order also bars organizations or individuals in any country or region from transferring China-origin dual-use items to the 10 listed U.S. entities [23][70][72][44].
  • Procurement carveout: China's procurement ban does not apply to U.S.-invested companies operating in China, according to finance ministry details cited by several reports [8][19][59][74].
  • U.S. list effects: The Pentagon's 1260H listing does not impose immediate sanctions, but Bol News and Dünya said it restricts U.S. defense contracts from June 30 and expands further limits in 2027 [29][50].

Perspectives

China Commerce Ministry: The ministry said the controls were adopted to safeguard national security and interests, meet non-proliferation obligations and respond to the U.S. government's expansion of its Chinese military enterprise list.

Industry Impact

  • Defense contractors: China's procurement restrictions name major U.S. defense groups and units including Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, Boeing Defense, General Dynamics and General Atomics [13][8][60][57].

Historical Background

The 1260H list was created under the U.S. National Defense Authorization Act, and the Pentagon's latest update on June 8 added more than 80 companies and their subsidiaries [18][44]. Trump and Xi agreed in May to work toward reducing tariffs; since then, U.S. and Chinese actions have included technology and critical-minerals restrictions [18][19][common].
