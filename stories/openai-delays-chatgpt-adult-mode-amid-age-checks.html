OpenAI delays ChatGPT adult mode amid age checks

Category: AI

OpenAI has again delayed a planned “adult mode” for ChatGPT—an option meant to let verified adult users access adult content—according to reporting by Axios, PCMag, and TechCrunch [1][2][3]. OpenAI says it needs more time before rolling out the experience more broadly [3][2]. Across the coverage, the main sticking point is age gating. OpenAI is focusing on age prediction and verification, plus safeguards meant to ensure adult-only content stays restricted to adults [2]. The pause reflects a careful approach to higher-risk capabilities in a product used at massive scale [2][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Still believes in the principle of treating adults like adults, but says getting the experience right will take more time.

— An OpenAI spokesperson (Axios)

Key Points

  • Scope: TechCrunch says the feature is meant to let verified adult users access erotica and other adult content [3].
  • Timing: TechCrunch reports this is another postponement after an earlier target that included a December timeline [3].
  • Gating mechanics: PCMag reports OpenAI is holding the launch until it “sorts out age prediction,” linking the release to stronger access controls [2].
  • Messaging: An OpenAI spokesperson told Axios the company still believes in “treating adults like adults,” but says “getting the experience right will take more time” [2][1].

Perspectives

OpenAI/PCMag: OpenAI says the holdup is practical: it wants age prediction in place before enabling adult-only responses, which suggests the delay is about meeting a reliable standard for gating and safeguards.

Historical Background

OpenAI’s adult-mode concept traces back to Sam Altman’s “treat adults like adults” push for tighter age-gating, including allowing “erotica for verified adults” [4]. Later reporting highlighted age prediction as a safeguard OpenAI wants in place before it expands access to explicit content [2][4].
