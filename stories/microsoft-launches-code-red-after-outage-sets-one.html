Microsoft launches Code Red after outage, sets one-hour recovery goal

Category: Technology

Microsoft has launched an internal “Code Red” overhaul after a Nov. 12-13 failure disrupted Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 services for about 24 hours. The company aims to resolve future global incidents within one hour [15][6]. Microsoft attributed the outage to a Windows scalability defect introduced in July and said neither cyber activity nor AI agents caused it. The disruption affected government agencies, trading firms and airline communications across several countries [15][23]. The company’s review found that diagnostic information scattered across internal systems hampered its response: engineers exchanged more than 11,000 messages in 11 Teams chats without maintaining a single shared view of the incident [15][4]. Microsoft plans to limit system load, separate traffic by region and speed recovery, with AI agents intended to help engineers interpret system-health data [15]. The company has not said when it expects to meet the one-hour target. Jason Henderson, president of Microsoft 365 Core, will hold daily meetings with the Substrate leadership team and weekly reviews. In a separate recent incident, an urgent fix took five to six days to reach users worldwide [15][6].

Key Points

  • Failure history: Microsoft executive Charles Lamanna said the affected component had accumulated “roughly ten years’ worth of acceptable failures,” according to a memo by Amanda Silver [15].
  • Patch goal: Jason Henderson said Microsoft wants its most urgent security fixes safely deployed worldwide within an hour of detection [15][10].

Perspectives

Satya Nadella, Microsoft's chief executive, said better software testing and stronger system isolation could have prevented the disruption [23].

Sources: To Vima

Brad Smith, Microsoft president, warned that more technology outages could occur in the coming months, saying AI has accelerated the discovery of weaknesses in computer code and that the infrastructure update will take more than weeks to complete [11].

Technical Details

  • Gateway lock contention: Microsoft's post-incident account traced the outage to a scalability flaw that caused lock contention in gateway processes. About 15 hours before the outage, a Teams failover shifted traffic to a gateway shared with Exchange Online, while maintenance had taken nearly 14% of gateway capacity offline [6][23].
  • Regional failover limits: The primary gateway's failure left more than 80% of its servers unresponsive, and Exchange Online could not switch to another gateway as some other services did [23].
  • Recovery sequence: Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive partially recovered before Exchange Online, but features that depended on Exchange could still be affected [7].

Industry Impact

  • Public services: Immigration New Zealand halted processing of all visa applications during the outage; police, health services, courts, businesses and schools in the UK were also affected [23].

Action Items

  • Test essential workflows without Microsoft 365: Have your technology team rehearse a critical task—such as payroll approval—without Outlook or Teams, and confirm that staff can access contact lists, runbooks and status updates through separate channels [7].
  • Check eligibility for service credits: Review the Microsoft 365 service-level agreement for your subscription to see whether availability fell below its targets [8].
