WhatsApp adds scheduled messages with two-week delivery window

Category: Technology

Meta-owned messaging service WhatsApp began rolling out scheduled messages worldwide on Nov. 25, letting users queue text, photos and videos for delivery 10 minutes to 14 days in advance in individual and group chats [13][7][9]. The feature is among WhatsApp’s most requested additions, the company said [3]. Users schedule a message by composing it, then pressing and holding the send button to choose a date and time. Recipients see it as an ordinary message, while senders can review and delete pending messages. WhatsApp says delivery does not depend on the sender’s phone being on or connected, and scheduled messages retain end-to-end encryption [7][10][29]. The rollout will reach accounts gradually over the coming weeks, with availability reported on WhatsApp’s official platforms, including Android, iOS, macOS and Windows [1][13]. The launch follows beta tests on Android and iOS in October [35].

Key Points

  • Group membership: A scheduled group message goes only to people who belonged to the group when it was queued; later additions will not receive it [15][12].
  • Media limits: Reports list text, photos and videos as supported message types, but not documents or voice notes [16][5].
  • Competitor differences: Telegram allows scheduling up to 365 days ahead and can send a message when a contact comes online; Signal queues messages locally, so delivery may wait until the sender’s phone reconnects [6].
  • User controls: A dedicated scheduled-message list lets senders manage queued items in one place [18].

Timeline

  • February 2026: A beta version of WhatsApp first showed work on message scheduling [30]
  • 12 October 2026: WhatsApp began testing the feature in an Android beta [35]
  • 22 October 2026: WhatsApp began testing scheduled messages in an iOS beta [35]
  • 25 November 2026: WhatsApp announced the global rollout of scheduled messages [13]

Historical Background

Telegram has offered scheduled messages since September 2019 and Signal since early 2023. Apple’s iMessage also offered a Send Later feature before WhatsApp introduced its option [6][4].
