Health Canada approves Eli Lilly’s daily weight-loss pill Foundayo

Category: Canada

Health Canada authorized Eli Lilly’s Foundayo, a daily GLP-1 pill for weight loss, on Nov. 13. It is approved for adults with obesity or adults who are overweight and have a related medical condition such as high blood pressure or sleep apnea. It is also authorized to help treat Type 2 diabetes [1][2][3]. The pill offers an alternative to weekly injections, and the company expects it to reach pharmacies in January [1][2][3]. Novo Nordisk’s Wegovy pill is still awaiting Health Canada approval for weight loss; its Rybelsus pill is already sold in Canada for Type 2 diabetes, not weight loss [1][2][3].

It seems to be just as good as if not better

— Dr. Ehud Ur, endocrinologist (Global News)

Key Points

  • Side effects: GLP-1 medications, whether injectable or oral, can cause gastrointestinal issues such as nausea or vomiting [1][2][3].
  • Wegovy pill formulation: Novo Nordisk uses salcaprozate sodium (SNAC) to protect semaglutide from digestive enzymes; it temporarily reduces acidity around the pill and helps it pass through the stomach lining into the bloodstream [1][2][3].

Perspectives

Dr. Ehud Ur, endocrinologist: Ur said his decision to prescribe Foundayo would depend on the patient. He is awaiting clinical-trial findings on whether it provides cardiovascular benefits comparable to those of semaglutide and tirzepatide.
