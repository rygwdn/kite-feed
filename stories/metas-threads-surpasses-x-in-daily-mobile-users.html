Meta’s Threads surpasses X in daily mobile users

Category: Technology

New third-party analytics reported that Meta’s Threads is now getting more daily active users on iOS and Android than Elon Musk’s X, marking a notable shift in the mobile social networking race [1][3][2]. The data cited puts Threads at 141.5 million daily active mobile users versus X at 125 million as of January 7 [3][1]. Coverage attributes Threads’ momentum to Meta’s steady product updates plus the advantage of Instagram-driven promotion and cross-pollination that makes trying the app easy [1][3]. At the same time, the reporting says X still holds the lead for usage in web browsers, suggesting the two services are winning on different platforms—for now [2][3].
