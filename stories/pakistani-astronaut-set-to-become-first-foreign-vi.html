Pakistani astronaut set to become first foreign visitor to China’s station

Category: Science

A Pakistani astronaut will fly as a payload specialist on China’s Shenzhou-24 mission, becoming the first foreign national to visit the Tiangong space station, China’s Manned Space Agency said on Nov. 7. The agency has not announced the astronaut’s identity or an official launch date. The mission is expected by year’s end, while Pakistan’s Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal said in October it was scheduled for Nov. 25 [1][5][4]. The visitor is due to stay briefly in orbit and return to Earth aboard Shenzhou-23, which docked with Tiangong in May. Pakistani candidates Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud trained in China; one will be selected for the flight and the other will serve as backup [5][6]. The mission follows a 2025 Pakistan-China agreement to train Pakistani astronauts for space-station flights [2][5].
