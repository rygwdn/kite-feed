Samsung foldable phones set preorder records before launch

Category: Technology

Samsung said its latest foldable lineup — the Galaxy Z Flip 8, Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra — drew record early demand before retail availability on Aug. 7. The company said global preorders for the newest Galaxy Z series rose 30% from the previous generation, while combined preorders for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra rose 70% from the Galaxy Z Fold 7 generation [10][13][3]. In Europe, Samsung said the new Z series topped its previous foldable preorder record by more than 17%, and combined preorders for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra were 70% higher year over year than for the Galaxy Z Fold 7 [4][10][9]. The wider Galaxy Z Fold 8 made up more than 40% of European preorders, while TechCentral reported that the wider, more compact Fold 8 represented almost 50% of reservations in Samsung's broader preorder data [4][10][3]. Samsung has marketed the wider Fold 8 screen for reading digital books and watching media, while the more expensive Fold 8 Ultra targets users seeking a larger workspace, stronger cameras and flagship performance [3][7]. The launch precedes Google's scheduled Pixel 11 Pro Fold introduction on Aug. 12 and Apple's expected September unveiling of its first foldable iPhone [3].

The Fold 8 Ultra, which I’m going to call the flagship model of the three — this is going to be the most capable foldable experience, having the 8-inch workspace experience, the most advanced camera system, and the flagship performance.

— Tarik Akad (MobileSyrup)

Key Points

  • Korea record: In South Korea, Samsung received 1.44 million preorders during a seven-day reservation period, compared with 1.04 million for the 2025 Fold 7 and Flip 7 generation [17][18].
  • Premium pricing: Samsung's unlocked US pricing starts at $1,199.99 for the Flip 8, $1,899.99 for the Fold 8 and $2,099.99 for the Fold 8 Ultra [33][2].
  • Launch incentives: Retailers and carriers promoted preorders with offers including $350 Amazon gift cards, up to $1,200 Samsung trade-in credit and up to $1,900 in carrier bill credits [33][42][40].

Perspectives

Samsung Canada: Tarik Akad described the Fold 8 Ultra as the flagship productivity and creation device, while saying the wider Fold 8 takes a different approach with a passport-style form factor.

  • 28 July 2026: South Korea's seven-day reservation period began [17].

  • 6 August 2026: Samsung said European preorders remained open one more day [4].
  • 7 August 2026: New foldables reached retail availability in global markets [16][3].
  • 12 August 2026: Google scheduled its Pixel 11 Pro Fold launch [3].
  • September 2026: Apple is expected to unveil its first foldable iPhone [3].

Technical Details

  • Silicon-carbon batteries: Samsung introduced silicon-carbon battery technology in the new foldables, a chemistry intended to raise energy density; the Flip 8 has a 4,300mAh battery despite being thinner than before [21][1].
  • Fold 8 aspect ratios: The standard Fold 8 uses a 5.5-inch 10:16 outer display and a 7.6-inch 4:3 inner display, a shift toward a shorter, wider phone shape when closed [27][19].
  • Ultra imaging stack: The Fold 8 Ultra combines a 200MP main camera, 50MP ultrawide camera and 10MP telephoto camera, with 3x optical zoom and 30x digital zoom cited in product coverage [6][27].

Industry Impact

  • Smartphone competition: Samsung's wider Fold 8 reached market before upcoming foldable releases expected from Google and Apple [3].
  • Carrier promotions: US carriers and Samsung offered aggressive preorder subsidies, with some deals making the devices effectively free with trade-in [31][42].
  • Accessories: Business Insider said new foldables have fewer case options at launch because hinges and external screens complicate designs, with early options from Samsung, Spigen, OtterBox and Dbrand [22][26].
  • Retail bundling: Amazon bundled substantial gift cards with preorders, while Samsung.com offered its own credits [34][33].

Historical Background

Samsung's 2025 foldable generation set a preorder record in South Korea at the time, but the new lineup surpassed it [17]. HardwareZone reported that the Galaxy Note10 drew 1.38 million preorders over 11 days in 2019, while Samsung's Galaxy S26 series recorded 1.35 million preorders earlier in 2026 [18]. Samsung's 2026 foldable lineup also changed the year-over-year comparison by adding a third, higher-positioned Ultra model for the first time [17].
