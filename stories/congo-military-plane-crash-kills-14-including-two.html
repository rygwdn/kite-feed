Congo military plane crash kills 14, including two generals

Category: World

At least 14 people died when a Congolese military aircraft crashed on Friday, September 25, near Kenge in Kwango province, while returning to Kinshasa from Kikwit. The Congolese military identified two of the dead as Lieutenant General Mutombo Katalayi Tiende, head of the military court, and Lieutenant General Bakumi Likulia, auditor general of the armed forces. Officials said all aboard died; early accounts citing a provincial official put the toll at 13, but the military later reported at least 14 [4][6][2][3][25]. Local officials said the aircraft developed a technical fault during the flight, but the cause has not been established. The military opened an investigation, and Civil Aviation Authority board chairman Tryphon Kin-Kiey Mulumba said the cause remained unknown [4][9][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Crash site: Kwango Deputy Governor Remy Saki Bokinda said the plane attempted to land in Kenge, where no landing strip was available, and struck a residential plot [6][25].
  • Recovery details: Bokinda said 12 bodies were burned beyond recognition and the pilot’s body was found outside the aircraft [6][25].
  • Aircraft operator: The aircraft was a Let L-410 operated by Tracep Aviation Congo for the Congolese armed forces [7].
