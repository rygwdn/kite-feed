NASA selects GIMLI mission to probe suspected lunar cave

Category: Science

NASA has selected the GIMLI project to investigate a suspected underground cave at the Moon’s Marius pit using radar and seismic tests [2][3]. The project aims to determine whether the feature could provide natural shelter for future astronauts [2][3]. The target is described as a possible lava tube and a candidate site for a future lunar base, but its structure and suitability remain unknown [2].
