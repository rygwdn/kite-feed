Nunavut NDP MP Lori Idlout joins Liberals

Category: Canada

Nunavut MP Lori Idlout has left the NDP to sit with Prime Minister Mark Carney’s Liberals — a “crossing the floor” move that boosts the government’s numbers in the House of Commons [1][2][3][5][6]. Multiple outlets say the switch leaves Carney’s government two seats short of a majority [5][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Fourth defection: La Presse says Idlout is the fourth opposition MP to join Carney’s government, a sign of how quickly the parliamentary math has changed [1].
  • Recent pattern: The NDP says Idlout’s decision follows three Conservative MPs who crossed to the Liberals in recent months [2][5][6][3].
