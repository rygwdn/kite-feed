285,000-signature petition urges Canada to remove U.S. ambassador

Category: Canada

A House of Commons petition calling for the removal of U.S. Ambassador Pete Hoekstra from Canada closed after drawing 285,921 signatures, and former Green Party leader Elizabeth May can now present it to Parliament [6][3]. The federal government must respond within 45 calendar days after it is tabled [1][2]. The petition says Hoekstra’s public comments have harmed Canada-U.S. relations, including his description of the 2025 Canadian election campaign as “anti-American.” It also accuses him of criticizing Canadian involvement in U.S. politics while making comments about Canadian affairs that the petition calls inconsistent with diplomatic protocol [4][6]. Hoekstra said Ontario political ads could be interpreted as political interference and has denied claims that the Trump administration engaged in political interference [4][6]. The U.S. Embassy in Ottawa said it was aware of the petition and declined further comment [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Keep your nose out of American politics, four weeks before an election.

— Pete Hoekstra, U.S. Ambassador to Canada (Toronto Sun)

Key Points

  • Regional support: Ontario accounted for 123,386 signatures, close to half the total; British Columbia contributed more than 57,000 and Alberta more than 31,000 [6].
  • Petition demand: The petition asks Ottawa to declare Hoekstra “persona non grata” and accuses him of normalizing the Trump administration’s threats to make Canada the 51st state [6].
  • Alberta allegations: The petition cites reports linking Hoekstra to Republican political operatives who met with Alberta separatists [6].

Historical Background

In June 2025, Hoekstra described President Donald Trump’s comments about Canada becoming the 51st state as a “term of endearment” [6].
