CFL's B.C. Lions rally past Roughriders to reach West final

Category: Canada

The B.C. Lions rallied from a 17-3 second-quarter deficit to beat the Saskatchewan Roughriders 33-26 in Saturday’s Canadian Football League West Division semifinal in Vancouver [2][3]. The win eliminated the defending Grey Cup champions and sent the Lions into the West final [2]. Quarterback Nathan Rourke threw for 366 yards and three touchdowns as B.C. overcame a halftime deficit, and Jevon Cottoy scored twice. The Lions, who started the season 1-5, visit the Edmonton Elks on Nov. 7. Calgary hosts the Grey Cup on Nov. 15 [2][5].

And so when you come up short it sucks, it hurts. You can't lie about that.

— Trevor Harris, Saskatchewan Roughriders quarterback (CBC News)

Key Points

  • Cottoy’s night: His 102 receiving yards surpassed his previous playoff high of 82, set in 2024 [5].
  • Defensive stops: After Saskatchewan’s second-quarter touchdown, B.C. forced the Roughriders to settle for three Brett Lauther field goals in the second half [5].
  • Home-field edge: The Lions and Roughriders both finished the regular season 10-8, but B.C. hosted the semifinal after winning their season series 2-1 [2].

Perspectives

Trevor Harris, Saskatchewan Roughriders quarterback: Harris said the loss would motivate the team to improve. He declined to decide immediately whether to continue playing and said he would announce his plans soon [5].
