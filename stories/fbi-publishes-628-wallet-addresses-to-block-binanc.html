FBI publishes 628 wallet addresses to block Binance hack laundering

Category: Technology

The FBI has published 628 cryptocurrency addresses linked to laundering proceeds from the $6.3 billion theft from cryptocurrency exchange Binance and urged exchanges and other virtual-asset services to block related transactions. The bureau attributes the Oct. 29 breach to the North Korean state-backed group TraderTraitor [14][13]. Investigators have seen the stolen assets converted mainly into ether and expect further transfers through decentralized-finance platforms and instant-swap services [13][19]. The list includes 608 Ethereum addresses, 19 Bitcoin addresses and one Solana address. Blockchain analysis indicated that Tether, Circle, Ripple, Arbitrum and Optimism had frozen about $2.85 billion of the stolen assets on-chain [14][13]. Binance has reopened withdrawals in stages while checking affected networks and assets. The exchange’s initial inquiry pointed to an engineer’s compromised laptop and access to a service container used to submit unauthorized withdrawals. It later identified additional points of intrusion and said the investigation is continuing [14][35][19].

A hack at a financial institution means stolen usernames and passwords. A hack at a crypto exchange is bank robbery at the speed of the internet, which creates an unstable risk profile for anyone responsible for other people’s capital.

— Ari Redbord, global head of policy at TRM Labs (The Indian Express)

Did you know: Blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs found indications that preparations for the Binance attack began about 36 hours before the theft [57].

Key Points

  • Market effects: Bitcoin fell to a six-week low on Friday before recovering above $78,000 on Monday; USDT and USDC together lost about $1.2 billion in market capitalization since Friday [37][21].
  • Customer withdrawals: Customers withdrew a net $3.64 billion, about 2.1% of Binance’s assets, after withdrawals resumed [4][24].
  • Recruiter-themed targeting: Microsoft described attackers posing as recruiters for an AI-powered blockchain intelligence company and using interview lures to target software developers [13].
  • Recovery funding: Binance said replacement tokens were funded through its SAFU reserve, futures insurance fund and corporate treasury, while co-CEO Yi He said it would replenish SAFU to about $1 billion [42][31].
  • Customer reimbursement: Binance said it had replaced about 95% of the stolen assets and would absorb the losses; the remaining tokens were less liquid and could take longer to source [14][19].
  • Investigation: Binance said an engineer’s laptop had been compromised and that investigators later found additional points of entry [35][56].
  • Frozen assets: Binance said about $3 billion had been located by Friday, but asset freezes do not necessarily mean funds have been recovered or returned [55][57].
  • Withdrawal rollout: Deposits remained open as Binance gradually restored withdrawals after security and readiness checks [19][5].
  • Market spillover: Spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded $643 million in outflows on Friday, while more than $1.3 billion in leveraged positions were liquidated, TRM Labs policy chief Ari Redbord said [4][24].
  • Exchange safeguards: Coinbase said it adjusted cold-storage systems, while Kraken monitored its platform and Gemini added alerts and restrictions on fund movements [56].

Technical Details

  • Breach investigation: Binance initially identified an engineer’s compromised laptop, then reported additional breach points. Co-CEO Richard Teng said malicious software used a self-destruct mechanism to hide activity [69][71].
  • Delegated transaction authorization: An investigation cited by the Financial Times said attackers exploited a feature that lets automated software receive transaction-authorization privileges, using it to steal about $3.8 billion in Ether [57].
  • On-chain laundering: The FBI said it observed TraderTraitor converting stolen assets primarily into ether and expected further laundering through decentralized-finance and instant-swap services [56][19].
  • Developer malware chain: Research cited by The Hacker News says a trojanized Terraform provider can deliver a Bash loader and the cross-platform information stealer FLATROOF, followed by the ROOFDECK backdoor [13].
  • Theft pattern: Attackers stole approximately $6.36 billion in cryptocurrency across ten blockchains, transferring most of the funds in about 20 minutes [14].
  • Automated fund movement: Chainalysis said the attackers used automation to move stolen funds around the Tron blockchain after withdrawing them from the exchange [56].

Industry Impact

  • Limits of intervention: TRM Labs said about $2.4 billion in crypto had reportedly moved beyond issuers’ reach, despite freezes by Tether and Circle [3].
  • Crypto market liquidity: The incident coincided with $643 million in spot bitcoin ETF outflows and more than $1.3 billion in liquidations of leveraged positions on Friday, figures cited by TRM Labs policy chief Ari Redbord [4][24].
  • Exchange security: Coinbase made changes to cold-storage systems, Gemini added alerts and restrictions on fund movements, and Kraken said its services remained normal [56].
  • Industry coordination: OKX CEO Star Xu urged exchanges to establish real-time security incident notifications, coordinated monitoring of suspicious funds and rapid responses to attacks [56].

Historical Background

The FBI previously attributed the $1.5 billion theft from crypto exchange Bybit in February 2025 to TraderTraitor, according to reports on the current case [7][23]. Security reporting says the group has been active since at least 2020 and has targeted cryptocurrency firms and their employees [13].

Q&A

Q: Which customer balances did Binance say were fully backed?

A: Binance said BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, USDC, XRP, USD1, RLUSD and ZEC balances were backed at 100% [14][17].

Q: When can users independently check Binance’s reserve holdings?

A: Binance said users would be able to independently verify its reserve holdings starting Tuesday, Nov. 3, after zero-knowledge proof generation, which was expected to take 40 to 50 hours [57].

Q: Was Binance.US affected by the breach?

A: The hack affected Binance’s global centralized exchange. Its U.S.-facing platform said it was not affected [12].
