B.C. helicopter crash kills two during forestry work

Category: Canada

A West Coast Helicopters Hughes 500 went down Tuesday morning during forestry operations in the Loughborough Inlet area on British Columbia’s central coast, killing two people and injuring one [1][5][3]. West Coast Helicopters said the aircraft was carrying a pilot and two passengers; one person survived and two died. The company identified one of those killed as pilot Riley Browne and said the other was a CFMG crew member [1][5]. Sean Smyth, general manager of West Coast Helicopters, told CBC the details of the crash remained unknown [3]. The Transportation Safety Board of Canada said it was deploying investigators to the site, with The Globe and Mail reporting they were expected to arrive Wednesday morning [2].
