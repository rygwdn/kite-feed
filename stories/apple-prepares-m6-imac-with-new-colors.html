Apple prepares M6 iMac with new colors

Category: Technology

Apple is reportedly preparing an updated iMac with an M6 chip and revised color options, with sales expected before the end of 2026, according to articles citing Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman [2][3][4][5][6]. The model is expected to remain a 24-inch all-in-one desktop and replace the current M4 iMac, which Apple introduced in October 2024 [4][9][11]. Reports do not point to a larger display, OLED panel or major chassis redesign, instead describing a chip update and color changes [4][5][6][9]. Several reports said Apple skipped M2 and M5 iMac generations, giving the lineup less frequent processor updates than some other Macs [2][8][11].

Primary Source: View original article

All signs point to the M6 models being close to launch, and you should be able to buy one before the end of the year

— Mark Gurman (Cult of Mac)

Key Points

  • Color lineup: Current iMac models are sold in green, yellow, orange, pink, purple, blue and silver, while Gurman has not identified which new colors Apple plans [4][9][13].
  • Chip process: Reports described the M6 as Apple’s first Mac chip expected to use a 2-nanometer manufacturing process. They said Apple is not expected to release M6 Pro, Max or Ultra variants in that generation [2][8][11][12].
  • Mac schedule: The iMac update could be part of a broader Mac refresh that includes redesigned MacBook Pro models, an M6 base 14-inch MacBook Pro and updated Mac Studio configurations [2][8][6].
  • OLED timing: Digital Trends cited current timelines around 2029 for a future OLED iMac, and MacRumors said an OLED model will not arrive for several years [5][4].

Perspectives

Mark Gurman: He said the iMac refresh appears close to launch and identified the main expected changes as an M6 chip and new color options, without specifying the colors.

Sources: MacRumors

Digital Trends: The outlet said the baseline M6 is a natural fit for the iMac because Apple’s 24-inch all-in-one has not relied on Pro or Max-class silicon.

Sources: Digital Trends

ComputerBase: The site reported that the M6 is expected to be Apple’s first 2nm chip, alongside the A20 Pro in the iPhone 18 Pro.

Sources: ComputerBase

Timeline

  • May 2021: Apple revived the iMac with an M1 redesign [6]
  • October 2024: Apple introduced the current M4 iMac model [4][9]
  • 23 August 2026: Gurman’s reported M6 iMac plans circulated in Mac publications [2][4]
  • 2026: Apple reportedly plans sales before year-end 2026 [2][6]

Industry Impact

  • Display suppliers: Digital Trends said Samsung Display and LG Display are developing 24-inch OLED panels for a future iMac; MacRumors reported LG Display is working on a five-layer OLED structure aimed at burn-in resistance and brightness [5][4].

Historical Background

Apple moved the iMac to Apple silicon with the M1 redesign in May 2021, then updated the line to M3 in 2023 and M4 in 2024 [6][11]. ComputerBase’s model table lists the Apple silicon iMac progression as M1 in 2021, M3 in 2023, M4 in 2024 and the expected M6 model in 2026 [11].

Business Angle

  • Incremental iMac updates can protect margins if Apple reuses the 24-inch design while upgrading internal silicon [4][5].
  • Skipping M5 could simplify inventory and marketing, but it may also stretch refresh intervals for iMac buyers waiting for larger displays or OLED [2][4].

Action Items

  • Delay an iMac purchase if timing matters: If you specifically want the newest iMac, consider waiting to see whether Apple announces the reported M6 model before the end of 2026 [2][4].
  • Choose another Mac for larger-screen needs: If you need a bigger display or higher-end desktop performance now, compare Mac mini or Mac Studio configurations with an external display because the reported iMac remains 24 inches [2][5].
