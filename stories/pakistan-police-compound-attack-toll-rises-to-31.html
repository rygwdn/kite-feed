Pakistan police compound attack toll rises to 31

Category: World

A suicide attacker drove an explosives-laden car into the Old Police Lines complex in Kohat, Pakistan’s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, during Friday prayers at a mosque used by police officers, their families and civilians. Gunmen then tried to enter the compound, triggering a firefight with security forces. By Saturday, officials said at least 31 people had been killed, including 16 police personnel, and more than 100 had been injured [2][3][5][11]. Several attackers remained at large as fighting continued into Saturday, while the number of militants involved remained unclear [10][11]. Ittehad-ul-Mujahideen Pakistan, a local group linked to Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan, claimed responsibility in some accounts, while the Pakistani Taliban denied involvement in another [10][4]. President Asif Ali Zardari called for the complete elimination of militants, and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif ordered authorities to accelerate rescue operations [3][23].

  • Attacker casualties: Police said six gunmen were killed in the explosion and ensuing gun battle [3][42].
  • Earlier toll: Initial reports on Friday counted 21 dead and 80 wounded before later updates [42][10].
