Apple replaces iPhone 18 Pro Max phones affected by AT&T service failures

Category: Technology

Apple confirmed that a small number of iPhone 18 Pro Max devices on AT&T have lost cellular service, leaving owners unable to make calls, send texts or use mobile data [15][2][9]. Affected phones may show “SOS” in the status bar, and AT&T warned customers that the disruption could also interfere with calls to 911 [12][7]. Apple says iOS 27.0.1 and a separate AT&T carrier-settings update are intended to prevent additional failures. Customers should receive the carrier update automatically within 14 days, or they can check for it under Settings > General > About [15][1]. Those updates will not restore service on a phone that has already failed. Apple tells affected owners to contact Apple or AT&T or visit one of their stores for a hardware replacement. ZDNET reported that replacements are free [15][20]. AT&T has not publicly explained the cause, and Apple has not said how many devices are affected [20][4][2].

Primary Source: View original article

We have identified an issue affecting a small number of iPhone 18 Pro Max users on the AT&T network that may cause a device to lose service and be unable to make calls.

— Apple spokesperson (9to5Mac)

Key Points

  • Modem difference: U.S. iPhone 18 Pro Max models use Qualcomm’s Snapdragon X80 modem, while the iPhone 18 Pro uses Apple’s C2. Apple and AT&T have not linked that hardware difference to the failures [2][15][20].
  • Canadian models: iPhone 18 Pro Max models sold in Canada use Apple’s C2 modem. Apple’s reported cases have been limited to AT&T users in the United States [21].
  • Settings update: Carrier-settings updates can change network configurations without a full iOS release and can be delivered separately from iOS 27.0.1 [2][4].

Perspectives

AT&T: The carrier said its priority was keeping customers connected and that it was working with Apple to address the issue [20].

Sources: ZDNET

Affected customer Lindsay Robertson: She told PCMag that neither her new phone nor her old phone could make or receive cellular calls. She said she planned to reactivate the old device before seeking a replacement [12].
