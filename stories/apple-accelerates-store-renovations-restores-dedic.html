Apple accelerates store renovations, restores dedicated Genius Bars

Category: Technology

Apple is accelerating renovations across its retail-store network. Redesigned locations are expected to reorganize online order pickups and Today at Apple sessions, restore dedicated Genius Bars and remove the large video walls found in many stores, according to Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman’s Power On newsletter as cited by several reports [3][5][2]. The changes would move pickup and workshop functions to dedicated sections along the sides of stores and return customer support to a defined counter at the rear. The plan revises elements of Apple’s previous retail design, which emphasized open spaces, video walls and community programming. Apple also reportedly plans to modernize, relocate or close selected stores while selecting new locations more selectively. In Germany, Apple operates 16 stores and has not opened a new one in five years; Munich has been identified as a possible location for a store next year [4][7].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Pickup bays: Online pickups are planned for roughly 10-foot-wide wooden bays with an Apple logo, secure drawers and a handoff table [3][6].
  • Service counters: The returning Genius Bars are expected to feature all-wood construction, two counter heights for accessibility, storage drawers and product posters [5][1].
  • Workshop layout: Today at Apple sections will include a large display, equipment storage, an instructor workspace and stools within the Avenue areas [3][1].
  • Staff procedures: Renovated stores will reportedly impose tighter rules for pre-opening meetings, requiring staff to stand and restricting drinks and personal devices [5].

Timeline

  • 2017: Apple redesigned stores around open spaces and community programming [5]
  • 13 September 2026: Reports detailed Apple’s accelerated retail renovation plans [3][5]
  • 14 September 2026: German report assessed expansion prospects and possible Munich location [7]

Historical Background

In its 2017 retail redesign, Apple replaced the traditional Genius Bar with the Genius Grove concept [5].
