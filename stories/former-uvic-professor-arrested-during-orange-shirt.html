Former UVic professor arrested during Orange Shirt Day activities

Category: Canada

Communities across Canada held events on Sept. 30 for the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation and Orange Shirt Day [2][3]. Members of the Lower Similkameen Indian Band held a walk near Cawston a day earlier [1]. Former University of Victoria professor Frances Widdowson was arrested during Orange Shirt Day activities at the university [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Toronto events: NOW Toronto listed four events in the city for Orange Shirt Day and the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation [2].
  • Edmonton events: The Edmonton Journal invited residents to participate in local events throughout Sept. 30, but its accessible article excerpt did not provide further event details [3].

Perspectives

Frances Widdowson criticizes what she calls the “residential school narrative.” The Times Colonist reported that she wore a wig and hat to conceal her identity, but protesters quickly identified her at the UVic event [6].

Sources: Times Colonist

Historical Background

Orange Shirt Day grew from the story of Phyllis Webstad, whose orange shirt was taken from her on her first day at a residential school in 1973 [common]. The National Day for Truth and Reconciliation became a federal statutory holiday in 2021, following the Truth and Reconciliation Commission’s Call to Action 80 to establish a day honouring survivors, their families and communities [common].
