Alphabet’s Wing expands Walmart drone delivery to 150 stores

Category: Technology

Alphabet-owned Wing and Walmart say they will expand drone delivery to 150 additional Walmart locations across the US over the next year, broadening service beyond earlier rollouts that started in the Dallas–Fort Worth area [1][2][5]. The companies frame the push as a scale-up toward a larger nationwide network, with service planned for major metro areas including Los Angeles, St. Louis, Cincinnati, and Miami (with additional metros to be announced) [1][5][3]. The expansion aims to make fast, small-item deliveries more widely available and to normalize drones as another convenient fulfillment option for everyday shopping [4][3].

Whether it’s a last-minute ingredient for dinner, a must-have charger for a phone, or a late-night essential for a busy family, the strong adoption we’ve seen confirms that this is the future of convenience,”

— Greg Cathey (Mashable)

Did you know: Walmart says it has completed more than 150,000 successful drone deliveries since launching the service [4].

Key Points

  • Customer usage: Wing says its top quartile of customers order drone deliveries as often as three times a week, suggesting repeat-use behavior rather than one-off trials [1].
  • Operational setup: Axios-reported details cited by Slashdot describe drones being loaded in a fenced parking-lot area, flying autonomously to addresses while human pilots monitor flights from a central hub [3].
  • Free for now: Wing’s chief business officer Heather Rivera said deliveries are currently free to help people experience drone delivery, with the company expecting fees later that are comparable to other options [3].
  • Bigger drone: Wing says it is developing a larger delivery drone designed to fly up to 65 mph and carry up to five pounds, doubling its current capacity [1].
  • AI shopping tie-in: Slashdot reports Walmart also announced a deal that lets customers buy Walmart products from within Google’s Gemini, following a similar arrangement Walmart announced for ChatGPT in October [3].

Perspectives

Wing: Wing presents the rollout as a way to introduce more households to drone delivery, keeping deliveries free initially and expecting pricing later that is in line with other delivery methods [3].

Timeline

  • April 2022: Wing begins US deliveries with Walgreens health products [1].
  • August 2023: Walmart and Wing launch initial service in Dallas–Fort Worth [1].
  • June 2025: Partnership expands drone coverage to 100 more stores [1].
  • January 15: Wing and Walmart plan to kick off deliveries in Houston [1].

Technical Details

  • Autonomous flight with oversight: The drones fly autonomously to the delivery address, while human pilots monitor each flight from a central operations hub, combining automation with operational supervision [3].
  • Payload and speed targets: Wing says it is working on a larger drone designed for up to 65 mph flight and a five-pound payload, which would expand the range of deliverable items [1].
  • Parking-lot launch sites: According to details cited by Slashdot, items get loaded into a small cardboard basket and launched from a fenced area in a Walmart parking lot, indicating a store-adjacent micro-fulfillment workflow [3].

Industry Impact

  • Urban logistics: Expanding into large metro regions (including Los Angeles, Miami, St. Louis, and Cincinnati) could push competitors and local delivery services to differentiate on speed, price, or assortment as drones target small, urgent baskets [5][1].
  • Retail automation: Wing’s described workflow—store picking, a parking-lot loading area, and centrally monitored autonomous flights—signals more automation “around” the store, not just in warehouses [3].
  • Conversational commerce: Walmart’s Gemini integration reflects a broader shift where retailers try to place purchasing inside AI assistants, potentially reshaping how brands compete for visibility before a shopper ever opens a retail app [3].

Historical Background

Retail drone delivery in the US has been tested for years, but most programs have stayed limited in geography and order types due to operational and regulatory complexity [common]. Wing entered the US with deliveries of health and wellness products through Walgreens in April 2022, before scaling retail partnerships further [1]. Walmart and Wing launched their joint drone delivery service in August 2023 in the Dallas–Fort Worth area, then expanded again in June 2025 to more than 100 additional stores across multiple cities [1].
