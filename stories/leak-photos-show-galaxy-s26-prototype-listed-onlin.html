Leak photos show Galaxy S26+ prototype listed online

Category: Technology

Images claimed to show a real Samsung Galaxy S26+ prototype have surfaced online. SamMobile and Android Headlines point to photos shared on X and a Craigslist listing that appears to offer the unreleased phone for sale ahead of Samsung’s expected February 25 Unpacked event [1][4]. Both outlets say markings on the device suggest it’s an internal testing unit, and the photos offer an early real-world look at the handset’s design [1][4]. Other reporting pulls back to the bigger picture beyond the S26 leak: Android Police says early One UI 9 animations hint at a wider-screen Galaxy Z Fold variant as attention builds toward Samsung’s next foldables expected this summer, while an Android Police opinion piece argues some enthusiasts are already more interested in phones launching after the S26 [3][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Reservations vs. resale: Android Headlines notes Samsung’s Galaxy S26 reservation page is already live, even as an unofficial Craigslist listing surfaced offering an unreleased S26+ [4].
  • Craigslist price tag: Android Headlines says the post asked $1,650 for what looked like a functional Galaxy S26+ and warned that buying unreleased phones from strangers can bring scam and security risks [4].
  • Design cue: The leaked photos point to a new “camera pill” layout, replacing the separate camera-ring look used on recent models; otherwise, the hardware looks similar to the Galaxy S25+, according to Android Headlines [4].
  • Software/branding hint: Android Headlines says the photos show the official Galaxy S26 lineup wallpaper on the device, supporting the claim that it’s a real unit rather than a mockup [4].
  • Where the hype is going: In its commentary, Android Police says the Nothing Phone (4a) sits at the top of the writer’s wishlist, a reminder that enthusiast attention can shift quickly beyond the Galaxy S26 cycle [2].
