Ottawa weighs guardrails for Canada Labour Code tool

Category: Canada

Ottawa opened a second round of consultations on Canada’s labour relations framework, including possible guardrails and alternatives to a contentious Canada Labour Code tool, Jobs Minister Patty Hajdu said [4][1][2][3]. Hajdu said the review aims to reduce prolonged work stoppages before they reach the point where Ottawa considers federal intervention [2]. The federal government announced the consultation on July 3, and Canadian Press coverage appeared the same day in The Globe and Mail and Winnipeg Free Press before Castanet published its version on July 4 [4][2][3][1].
