Toronto Caribbean Carnival parade fills waterfront with revellers

Category: Canada

Toronto Caribbean Carnival’s 59th annual Grand Parade moved along the city’s waterfront on Saturday, Aug. 1, with masqueraders, colourful costumes and music drawing participants and spectators to one of Toronto’s major summer cultural events [1][4]. Road closures were in effect, and police increased their presence for the parade and Carnival weekend [1][4]. Revellers also travelled into Toronto by GO train from communities including Ajax and Pickering, carrying flags of Trinidad and Tobago, Grenada, Jamaica and other Caribbean nations [5]. NOW Toronto reported that residents and tourists described Caribana as a celebration of “Unity, community, love,” while a separate feature highlighted former NBA player Jamaal Magloire’s 20 years with Toronto Revellers [2][3].
