OpenAI investor slides put July revenue run rate near $30 billion

Category: AI

OpenAI’s latest investor presentation put its July annualized revenue near $30 billion, below earlier reports of about $40 billion for that month. Its September figure was close to $50 billion [11]. Investors had adjusted OpenAI’s numbers to compare them with Anthropic, producing the roughly $70 billion figure circulated last month. Anthropic counts sales through cloud platforms such as AWS and Google Cloud, while OpenAI excludes those partner sales [6][18]. Annualized revenue projects a recent sales pace across a year; it is not revenue earned over a full year [7]. After the figures became public, the Nasdaq 100 fell 1.4%, Nvidia lost 2.9%, Oracle dropped 5.5% and Micron fell 4.8%. U.S. stock futures rose the following morning [11][18]. OpenAI remains loss-making and has forecast cumulative cash burn close to $280 billion through 2030 [11]. ASML is scheduled to report results on Oct. 14 and TSMC on Oct. 15. Anthropic’s possible November listing could make more of its financial information public [18].

Key Points

  • Funding needs: OpenAI is negotiating a private financing round at a potential $1.4 trillion valuation, according to the Financial Times [6][7].

  • 15 October 2026: TSMC is scheduled to report results [18]

Industry Impact

  • Semiconductor makers: Samsung estimated third-quarter operating profit at 107.4 trillion won, while TSMC reported quarterly sales of 1.49 trillion Taiwan dollars, up 54.6% from a year earlier [18].
  • Infrastructure suppliers: Oracle provides substantial computing capacity to OpenAI and has multibillion-dollar contracts with the company [18].

Business Angle

  • The broader AI industry would need to reach $6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031 to justify projected data-center spending, according to a Bain report [1].

Q&A

Q: What has changed about OpenAI’s IPO plans?

A: OpenAI confidentially filed IPO paperwork in June and had been expected to list in the fall, but has since delayed its listing plans. Sam Altman said the company would not rush toward an IPO as concerns about risks from powerful AI grow [7][11].
