Sword attack at Swedish school kills one, injures three

Category: World

An 18-year-old man armed with a sword attacked Brinell School in Fagersta, central Sweden, on Friday afternoon, killing one person and injuring three others before police arrested him, authorities said [12][2][4][7][18]. Police Chief Tommy Alriksson said officers shot the suspect during the arrest, and police said there was no longer any danger to the public [3][4][18][28]. The attack happened while classes were in session at the upper secondary school, which officials and local reports described as having about 400 to 500 students, including accounts that put enrollment at about 450 students ages 16 to 20 [3][10][7][1]. Authorities said the motive was not immediately known, and Alriksson said police had no reason to believe more than one perpetrator was involved [3][11][18][25]. The school had just reopened after the summer break, and for some pupils it was the first day back [7][14][1].

Key Points

  • Victim details: Västmanland County said two seriously injured boys were between 12 and 17 years old, while another injured person had minor injuries or was released from hospital [3][10][1][14].
  • Local lockdowns: Authorities locked down eight nearby schools before later lifting the measures, while the Västmanland region declared a state of high alert after the attack [1][28].
  • Suspect reports: SVT and other local media reported the suspect wore a helmet, was a former Brinell student and had a previous assault conviction [7][1][23][31].

Historical Background

Sweden experienced a deadly sword attack at a school in Trollhättan in October 2015, when a masked attacker killed a teacher and two students before police shot him [23][39]. In February 2025, a gunman killed 10 people at the Campus Risbergska adult education center in Örebro, in what sources described as Sweden’s deadliest gun attack [22][39][44].
