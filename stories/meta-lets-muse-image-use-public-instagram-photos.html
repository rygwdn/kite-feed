Meta lets Muse Image use public Instagram photos

Category: AI

Meta launched Muse Image, its first image-generation model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, on July 7 and made it available through the Meta AI app, Instagram Stories and WhatsApp, with planned expansion to Facebook, Messenger and advertiser tools [13][2][31]. The tool can create or edit images from prompts, uploaded photos and sketches, and it can use public Instagram accounts as image references when a user @-mentions a profile [2][24][37]. Public Instagram accounts are eligible for this reuse by default unless users change settings or make accounts private. Meta says users will not receive notices when AI features create content using their Instagram material [24][25][31][37]. Meta says users can opt out in Instagram’s “Sharing and reuse” settings by disabling permissions for posts and reels; several reports said images already generated with a user’s content will not be removed after an opt-out [2][25][20][31].

Did you know: Meta says Muse Image can generate QR codes with coding tools, a less common feature for consumer image generators [2][13].

Key Points

  • Public accounts: Instagram’s help text says content from public accounts may be reused in remix, sequence, templates and stickers, and may also support content creation through Meta AI features [37][44].
  • Minors limited: Instagram accounts belonging to users under 18 restrict AI access to accounts they follow, according to Scripps News and RT accounts of Instagram’s policy [36][44].
  • Product features: Muse Image can handle room redesigns, personalized presets and mark-up-based editing, where users draw over an image area to guide changes, according to Meta’s blog post and early coverage [13][2].
  • Creator pushback: Creative Artists Agency urged Meta to replace the default opt-out policy with clear, documented consent for name, image, likeness, voice and creative works [16][26][27].

Industry Impact

  • Creator economy: Influencers and public creators face potential reputational and commercial exposure because public profiles can be used as AI references unless users disable reuse; Rediff noted particular risks for those whose public profiles are integral to their commercial value [14][16].
  • Advertising tools: Meta plans to bring Muse Image to advertisers through Advantage+ creative, extending the consent debate to its commercial AI tools [13][15].
  • Platform moderation: The BBC noted the feature may face scrutiny while Ofcom investigates X over Grok’s role in creating and sharing non-consensual AI-altered images of real people [24].

Historical Background

Meta created Meta Superintelligence Labs in 2025, and reports describe Muse Image as the first image-generation model from that unit [41][13]. The launch follows OpenAI shutting down its Sora app in March 2026 and backlash against X over non-consensual AI images generated through Grok, according to Gizmodo and BBC reporting [10][24].

Business Angle

  • Muse Image connects generation tools to Instagram’s public content and social graph [13][31].
  • The opt-out design has drawn objections from creator representatives before Meta expands Muse Image to Facebook, Messenger and advertiser workflows [16][13].
  • If users make accounts private or disable reuse broadly, Meta’s social-context advantage for AI generation may narrow [2][25].

Action Items

  • Disable Instagram AI reuse: Open Instagram, go to Settings, choose “Sharing and reuse,” then turn off the permissions under “Allow people to reuse your content on Instagram and with AI features at Meta” for posts and reels [2][31].
  • Make an Instagram account private: Switching an account to private prevents other users from generating AI images with its public photos, according to TechSpot and PCMag [25][2].
