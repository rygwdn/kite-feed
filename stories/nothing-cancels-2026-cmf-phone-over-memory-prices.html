Nothing cancels 2026 CMF phone over memory prices

Category: Technology

Nothing’s budget-focused CMF brand will not launch a successor to the CMF Phone 2 Pro in 2026 after co-founder Akis Evangelidis said current memory prices made the project uneconomic at CMF’s target price point [2][3][4][5][7][8]. Evangelidis said the company had been working on a follow-up, but could not build a phone that felt like a genuine step forward while keeping a price that made sense for CMF [1][2][3][5]. RAM prices have risen sharply over the past year as manufacturers redirected production toward large AI infrastructure orders, tightening supply for consumer electronics [3][4][7]. The CMF Phone 2 Pro launched in April 2025, and Evangelidis said CMF will still introduce several new products and some new categories this year while Nothing’s broader smartphone launch plans continue [2][3][8].

Primary Source: View original article

We were working on a successor but with memory prices where they are right now, we can't build a phone that feels like a genuine step forward at a price that makes sense for CMF. As a result, we've decided not to launch a new CMF phone this year.

— Akis Evangelidis (TechSpot)

Key Points

  • RAM price spike: A basic 2x8GB kit of DDR4 RAM that cost about $50 in early 2025 now sells for significantly more, one example of the component cost surge affecting budget devices [1].

Perspectives

CMF product team: Evangelidis said CMF still plans several new products and some entirely new categories this year, even without a new phone.

Sources: Engadget

Nothing management: Evangelidis said in a follow-up post that launching the CMF Phone 2 Pro hardware today would make it cost roughly 50% more.

Sources: PCMag

Timeline

  • 2024: CMF Phone 1 launched at a $200 retail price [5].
  • April 2025: CMF Phone 2 Pro debuted as CMF’s latest flagship device [3][8].

Industry Impact

  • PC market: IDC predicted PC shipments could shrink by almost 10% this year as higher memory costs lift device prices [3][4].
  • Consumer electronics: Apple and Samsung have warned customers that price increases are coming because of rising RAM costs [3][4].

Historical Background

Nothing launched CMF as its lower-cost hardware brand, later spinning it into an independent subsidiary headquartered in India, described as the company’s strongest market [3].

Business Angle

  • Cost inflation: Evangelidis said building the CMF Phone 2 Pro hardware today would cost roughly 50% more, putting a direct follow-up outside the brand's price point [5].
  • Nothing will skip a CMF phone launch in 2026, reducing the brand’s handset visibility for the year [2][3].
