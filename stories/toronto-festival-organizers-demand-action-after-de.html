Toronto festival organizers demand action after deadly shooting

Category: Canada

Organizers of Toronto’s Salsa on St. Clair festival threatened to withdraw from the annual event unless the City of Toronto and police make concrete commitments to improve policing and security on and around St. Clair Avenue, after gunfire erupted Saturday near St. Clair Avenue West and Arlington Avenue [2]. CBC reported that two men were killed and five others were injured; The Canadian Press reported that four people were sent to hospital after the shooting at a festival where police said 13,000 people had gathered [2][4]. Toronto police Chief Myron Demkiw described the shooting as a “targeted event” and called for legal reforms to impose tougher consequences for firing a gun in a public space or at a large gathering [4]. The Globe and Mail reported that Demkiw and a deputy mayor called for stricter punishments, while experts said that approach was not supported by evidence [1]. Police continued their investigation Sunday, and organizers cancelled the second day of Salsa on St. Clair [4].

Key Points

  • Victims named: Police identified the two men killed as 25-year-old Shaquan Quashie and 20-year-old Cesar Vernaza, and said the two knew each other [4].
  • Organizer warning: TLN Media Group called officials’ response “maddening” and said it refused to expose staff, friends, clients and community supporters to unsafe conditions [2].
  • Police probe: Demkiw said investigators were examining whether two other Toronto shootings from the same weekend had any potential links [4].
  • Election issue: Councillor Brad Bradford, who is challenging Mayor Olivia Chow in the Oct. 26 mayoral election, criticized police comments that Toronto is “extremely safe” and called for bail reform and gun enforcement [6].
