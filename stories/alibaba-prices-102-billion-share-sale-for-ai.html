Alibaba prices $10.2 billion share sale for AI

Category: Technology

Alibaba Group priced a Hong Kong placement of 710 million new shares at HK$112.70 each, raising HK$80 billion, or about $10.2 billion, for artificial intelligence development and infrastructure [3][8][40][6]. The company said all net proceeds would go toward its “full-stack” AI capabilities, including infrastructure, chips and the development and deployment of AI models [11][13][26][31]. The sale is the largest primary follow-on offering by a Hong Kong-listed company and the third-largest such offering globally this year, behind Alphabet and Intel [2][11][40][26]. The pricing represented an 8.4% discount to Alibaba’s Friday closing price in Hong Kong and a 3.6% discount to its New York-listed shares, according to a company filing cited by the South China Morning Post [3]. Alibaba’s Hong Kong shares fell after the deal; several reports put the early decline at about 8% to 10% [6][21][40][30]. The placement followed quarterly results in which Alibaba’s net profit fell about 75% from a year earlier as AI-related capital expenditure rose sharply, while the company said demand for AI services shortened its expected payback period on those investments to about 2.5 years [2][11][40].

Did you know: Alibaba’s latest placement is its first new-share placement in Hong Kong since its 2019 Hong Kong listing [28][24].

Key Points

  • Investor demand: The placement was about three times oversubscribed, with sources saying orders exceeded HK$200 billion and sovereign and long-term funds received more than 40% of the final allocation [3][33][35].
  • Spending scale: Alibaba’s capital expenditure rose 75% year on year to 67.68 billion yuan in the April-to-June quarter, and the company said it had already spent nearly half of its three-year capex plan [2][7][34][11].
  • Cloud growth: Cloud and AI revenue rose 45% to 48.44 billion yuan, while AI model services exceeded 16 billion yuan in annual recurring revenue [2].
  • Deal timing: TechNode reported that the transaction is expected to close on Aug. 26, two days after Alibaba announced the final pricing [8].

Perspectives

Alibaba management said on the earnings call that AI spending was a high-certainty investment, with CEO Eddie Wu saying AI-related capex could break even in about three years at current margins and sooner if proprietary chip use rises. Wu said older accelerators remain heavily used and inference demand has turned compute from a cost center into a revenue driver.

Technical Details

  • Cloud footprint: Alibaba Cloud launched a third South Korea data center last week, bringing its network to 104 availability zones across 30 regions [40].
  • Qwen models: Alibaba’s Qwen open-source AI models have gained traction among developers, and the latest equity issuance followed a strong reception for its Qwen 3.8-Max model [16][23][1].

Industry Impact

  • Chinese tech shares: Central News Agency reported that Alibaba’s drop weighed on other Hong Kong technology names, including Baidu, the Hang Seng Tech ETF and AI stock MiniMax in early trading [37].
  • AI infrastructure financing: Valor reported that large hyperscalers including Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft and Oracle are expected to spend more than $690 billion in fiscal 2026, while ByteDance attracted more than $30 billion in orders for a syndicated loan tied to AI investment [15].

Business Angle

  • AI infrastructure spending must generate enough cash after depreciation, chip replacement and data-center operating costs to offset the capital outlay [12][common].
  • External capital reduces near-term balance-sheet strain, but new shares dilute existing holders unless the funded AI assets lift per-share earnings over time [3][common].
  • Alibaba’s reported cloud and AI revenue growth provides a demand signal, but free cash flow will show whether that revenue turns into cash generation [2][15][common].
  • Management’s shorter payback period depends on future quarters confirming high utilization and renewal pricing for AI compute capacity [40][34].
