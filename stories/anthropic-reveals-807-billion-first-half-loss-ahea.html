Anthropic reveals $80.7 billion first-half loss ahead of November IPO

Category: AI

Anthropic reported $16.3 billion in revenue and an $80.7 billion net loss for the first half of 2026 in a draft prospectus for its planned November initial public offering. The company behind Claude said $72 billion of the loss came from accounting revaluations of convertible notes and other financing instruments, rather than operating cash outlays [2][50]. Anthropic spent nearly $9 billion on computing and infrastructure in the first six months and has committed at least $518 billion over a decade for cloud and computing capacity. It said total spending could reach $750 billion if deals under negotiation proceed, while warning that cloud providers have not supplied all the capacity it seeks [50][16][63]. At an Oct. 14 investor meeting, the company said third-quarter revenue rose 45% to $16.8 billion, a slowdown after revenue more than doubled from the first to the second quarter. Some investors are discussing an IPO valuation near $1.5 trillion, down from earlier expectations of about $2 trillion. Anthropic is still expected to list in November [50][63][14].

Key Points

  • Customer concentration: Two customers accounted for about one-fifth of first-half sales, and Anthropic expects a significant share of future revenue to remain concentrated among a limited number of clients [2][72].
  • Safety liabilities: The prospectus warns that harmful model behavior, including responses resembling blackmail or attempts to avoid shutdown, could expose Anthropic to damages beyond its insurance coverage [72][73].
  • Local resistance: Anthropic cautioned that opposition to data-center construction is becoming more coordinated across jurisdictions and that its high public profile could draw extra attention to projects linked to the company [59].
  • Rival financing: OpenAI is seeking about $30 billion at a valuation above $1.4 trillion before Anthropic's expected listing, with existing backers SoftBank and Abu Dhabi-based MGX in talks to participate [12][10].
  • Cloud commitments: Reported long-term contracts include $111 billion with Google Cloud, $110 billion with Amazon Web Services and $31 billion with Microsoft, most of them non-cancellable [16][78].

Historical Background

Anthropic reported a $42 billion net loss for 2025, most of it also attributed to accounting charges associated with financing instruments [50]. The company generated $4.6 billion in revenue in 2025, compared with $16.3 billion in the first half of 2026 [55][57].

Business Angle

  • First-half computing and infrastructure spending exceeded the $7.3 billion Anthropic spent in all of 2025 [16].
  • Computing and infrastructure spending per dollar of revenue fell from about $2.09 in the first half of 2025 to $0.55 in the first half of 2026 [60].

Q&A

Q: Was Anthropic profitable in any recent quarter despite its reported losses?

A: At the Oct. 14 investor meeting, Anthropic said it was profitable on an adjusted basis in the third quarter [63].

Q: Could Anthropic's infrastructure commitments rise through a proposed Nscale deal?

A: Anthropic is in talks with Nscale on a second data-center deal worth about $45 billion for a 460-megawatt facility, and it has a right of first refusal on a planned third building [29].
