Pentagon adds ChatGPT and Grok for 3 million personnel

Category: AI

The Pentagon launched OpenAI’s ChatGPT Mil and Starshield AI’s Grok for Government on GenAI.mil on Aug. 31, expanding its secure generative AI portal for military and civilian personnel working on unclassified tasks [2][3][5][6][8]. The tools join Google Gemini on a platform that routes Defense Department use of commercial AI models through approved government channels instead of consumer services [2][6][9]. The rollout gives roughly 3 million Defense Department personnel access to customized AI assistants for administration, logistics, planning, policy and collaboration, with the tools limited to sensitive but unclassified workloads [2][4][6][9]. The Defense Department said GenAI.mil has added more than 1.7 million unique users since launch [2][6][9].

Perspectives

Defense Department official: The official described a practical division of labor among models, saying Gemini was suited to search or source scraping and ChatGPT to text-based work.

Defense Department: The department said Grok for Government would provide “immediate productivity gains, stronger knowledge continuity and more secure and efficient collaboration.”

Sources: The Hill

OpenAI: ChatGPT Mil was offered through OpenAI for Government and is designed to resemble the commercial ChatGPT experience while operating inside the department’s secure environment.
