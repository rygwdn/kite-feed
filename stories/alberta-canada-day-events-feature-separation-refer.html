Alberta Canada Day events feature separation referendum debate

Category: Canada

Albertans marked Canada Day with patriotic celebrations and unease as the province heads toward an Oct. 19 referendum on whether Alberta should remain in Canada or start a process for a second, binding vote on leaving Confederation [2][5]. The referendum campaign began in May, and several groups have formed to influence public opinion before the vote [5]. The Globe and Mail reported that pro-independence groups used Canada Day to emphasize Alberta's blue over Canada's red while increasing fundraising and support ahead of Oct. 19 [3].
