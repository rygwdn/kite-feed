Montreal father wanted to testify before murder conviction

Category: Canada

Luciano Frattolin, convicted on Aug. 10 of second-degree murder and concealment of a human corpse in the death of his nine-year-old daughter Melina Frattolin, told a New York judge outside the jury’s presence during his trial that he wanted to testify in his own defence [3][4][1]. His lawyers had advised him not to do so [3][4][5]. A New York jury found that Frattolin killed Melina on July 19, 2025, the last day of a weeklong trip to the United States [3][4]. Her body was found the next day near Ticonderoga, N.Y., about 400 kilometres north of New York City, and an autopsy found she died from asphyxia due to drowning [3][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Courtroom exchange: After the prosecution closed its case, the judge told Frattolin he had no obligation to testify because the presumption of innocence still applied [3].
  • Montreal connection: Melina Frattolin lived with her mother in Montreal before the U.S. trip during which prosecutors said she was killed [3].
