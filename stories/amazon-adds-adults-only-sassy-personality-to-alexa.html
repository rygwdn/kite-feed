Amazon adds adults-only ‘Sassy’ personality to Alexa+

Category: AI

Amazon has added a new, adults-only “Sassy” personality option to Alexa+, expanding the assistant’s selectable styles beyond Brief, Sweet and Chill [1][3]. The new mode is built to be more irreverent, leaning on sarcasm and occasional profanity, including playful “roasts” [1][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Access controls: PCMag says adults must complete a security check to turn on Sassy, and it’s unavailable when Amazon Kids is enabled [1].
  • Content boundary: TechCrunch says the mode can curse and “roast” users, but it won’t engage with NSFW content [3].
  • Where it fits: Sassy is the fourth Alexa+ personality type, joining Brief, Sweet, and Chill [1].

Action Items

  • Turn on the Sassy personality (adults only): You’ll need to complete the required security check to use it [1].
  • Keep shared devices comfortable for everyone: On a shared Echo, you may want to leave Sassy off (or use Amazon Kids profiles/settings where appropriate) to avoid profanity during everyday interactions [1].
