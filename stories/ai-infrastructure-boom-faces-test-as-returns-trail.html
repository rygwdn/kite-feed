AI infrastructure boom faces test as returns trail spending

Category: AI

The artificial intelligence industry’s infrastructure buildout is outpacing clearly measurable productivity gains, economists say, as companies seek new revenue to cover heavy investment [9][11][13]. PwC estimates cumulative global data-center spending could exceed $30 trillion by 2050, while Anthropic plans to spend $518 billion in coming years, more than 100 times its 2025 revenue [9][11]. Bain & Company says leading U.S. cloud companies and other AI firms need more than $4.2 trillion in new revenue over five years to finance infrastructure expansion; productivity gains in existing markets alone will not close the gap [9][13]. Columbia Business School economist Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh estimates U.S. AI investment could reach about $9 trillion from 2025 to 2032. He says the sector would need $3.55 trillion in annual revenue by 2032 to deliver a 10% return [9][11]. Economist Diane Coyle says productivity effects from earlier transformative technologies typically took 10 to 50 years to spread through the economy. AI infrastructure financing often relies on debt, which can amplify losses if demand or asset values fall [9][13].

As long as one is left with the infrastructure that's needed to support all the productivity effects down the road, that's okay.

— Diane Coyle (Tuoi Tre)

Key Points

  • Productivity hurdle: JPMorgan estimates U.S. productivity would need to grow 3% to 5% annually over the next decade to justify Nvidia’s valuation, compared with the Congressional Budget Office’s 1.75% baseline [9][13].
  • Early-career jobs: A Stanford study cited in the reporting found employment among 22-to-25-year-olds in AI-exposed industries, including accounting and paralegal work, was 19% lower than in less-exposed occupations [9][11].
  • Consumer subscriptions: A PNC-based estimate cited by TechCrunch found that 2.2% of consumers paid for AI services in May, spending an average of $31 a month [2].
  • New markets: Bain says new applications may be needed to bridge the investment gap, including AI-guided robotics and the development of materials for batteries and semiconductors [9][aawsad.com#1].
  • Cash generation: Microsoft reported $115.9 billion in additions to property and equipment in fiscal 2026, compared with $182.9 billion in operating cash flow [10].

Perspectives

Dario Amodei, Anthropic chief executive: Amodei has argued that AI could eliminate half of entry-level white-collar jobs within five years. Research cited in the report describes a narrower effect on hiring for some early-career roles.

Sources: Tuoi Tre

Industry Impact

  • Cloud services: Microsoft said revenue from Azure and other cloud services rose 43% in its fiscal fourth quarter, and annual Azure revenue topped $100 billion for the first time [10].
  • Memory manufacturing: Micron's net capital expenditures rose to $27.37 billion in fiscal 2026 from $13.80 billion a year earlier as it expanded capacity to meet AI-related memory demand [10].

Historical Background

The article compares the current investment cycle with the railroad crisis of 1873, which bankrupted railroad magnates while railways continued operating, and the collapse of the dot-com boom in the 1990s, after which the internet remained in use [9][11][aawsad.com#1].

Business Angle

  • Microsoft said about two-thirds of quarterly capital spending went to short-lived assets, primarily CPUs and GPUs [10].
  • OpenAI's enterprise bookings reportedly doubled since July [2].
  • With 325 million subscribers, comparable to Netflix's scale, $34 per customer would yield about $11 billion in annual revenue, less than a third of OpenAI's operating costs, according to the article's comparison [2].

Q&A

Q: What growth scenarios did Anthropic model for AI’s effect on the economy?

A: Its economics team modeled annual growth in 2030 at 2.4% with a modest AI impact, 5.4% with a substantial impact and 15.4% with an extreme impact, compared with a 2% baseline without AI. It did not assign probabilities to the scenarios [13].
