Surrey, B.C., house fire injures one, police investigate

Category: Canada

Police and fire crews responded to a house fire in Surrey, B.C., that seriously injured one person, who was transported to hospital by helicopter [1][2]. Investigators have not determined the cause, but police said they do not believe the incident was related to arson or extortion [2]. Authorities said the injured person is expected to survive, offering a note of hope amid a frightening incident [2].

Key Points

  • Where it happened: The fire followed an explosion at a home in Surrey’s Cloverdale area, in the 16900-block of Jersey Drive, with calls coming in shortly after 4:30 p.m. Friday [2].
  • What crews saw: Surrey’s assistant fire chief said firefighters arrived to flames coming from the ground floor of a two-storey, older wood-frame house, and found a man outside with significant burns [2].
  • Containment success: Fire officials said crews contained the blaze to the involved home and it did not spread to nearby residences [2].
  • Investigation lead: The RCMP’s provincial operations support unit has taken over the investigation into the explosion and fire [2].
  • Public help requested: Investigators asked anyone with information to contact Surrey Police Service non-emergency at 604-599-0502 or Crime Stoppers at 1-800-222-8477 [2].
