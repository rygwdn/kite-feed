Workers need $176,100 earnings history for maximum Social Security

Category: Business

A worker needed an earnings record showing at least $176,100 in 2025—the annual taxable wage cap—to qualify for the maximum Social Security retirement benefit, according to a GoBankingRates explainer published Oct. 10. To receive the highest monthly amount cited, $5,108, a claimant also had to wait until age 70 to start benefits [1]. The Social Security Administration calculates benefits using a worker’s 35 highest-earning years, adjusted for wage changes, rather than just the final years of employment. The article lists maximum monthly payments of $2,831 at age 62 and $4,043 at full retirement age. It puts full retirement age at 66 to 67, depending on birth year [1].
