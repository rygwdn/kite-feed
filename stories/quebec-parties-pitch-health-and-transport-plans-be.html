Quebec parties pitch health and transport plans before debate

Category: Canada

Quebec’s five major party leaders campaigned on transport, public safety and health care on Saturday as they prepared for the first of three televised debates. The first debate is scheduled for Tuesday, Sept. 15, ahead of the province’s Oct. 5 election [1][2][3]. St-Pierre Plamondon proposed investing $550 million over four years in prevention, including measures to promote physical activity and expand mental-health care, and said the PQ would eliminate the Santé Québec agency [3].
