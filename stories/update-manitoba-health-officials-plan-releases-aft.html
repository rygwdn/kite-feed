Update: Manitoba health officials plan releases after bus rollover

Category: Canada

Health officials in Manitoba say three people who were airlifted to Winnipeg after a school bus rollover near Mafeking are expected to be released before the end of the day, while a fourth patient remains in hospital in stable condition [1][3]. The crash happened Tuesday when the bus lost control and rolled off a highway while attempting to pass another bus, according to reporting and officials [3][4]. Police said multiple students and the driver were taken to hospital, and the incident has drawn attention to winter travel risks on rural highways [4].
