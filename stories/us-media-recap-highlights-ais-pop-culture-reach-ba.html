US media recap highlights AI’s pop culture reach, backlash

Category: AI

A year-end look at 2025 argues that AI is no longer a distant concept in entertainment, showing up across pop culture and on everyday screens [1]. At the same time, another 2025 recap describes growing pushback against generative AI—from classrooms and workplaces to local fights over data-center development and its side effects [3]. Together, the pieces frame 2025 as a year when AI felt more culturally present while also triggering sharper public debates about costs, consent, and governance [1][3].
