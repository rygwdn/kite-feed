Jersey Mike’s files for IPO, plans NYSE listing under JMKE

Category: Business

Jersey Mike’s filed for an initial public offering and plans to list on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker JMKE, Quartz reported. Bloomberg also covered the sandwich chain’s IPO plans in its Businessweek Daily podcast [2][1]. The Financial Times reported that Blackstone is seeking a windfall and a valuation of up to $12 billion [5]. The sandwich chain reported net income of $55 million on revenue of $724 million in 2025, up sharply from a year earlier, according to Quartz [2]. CNBC reported that Jersey Mike’s has nearly 3,300 locations, making it the second-largest U.S. hoagie sandwich chain behind Subway [3].

Key Points

  • Sales growth: CNBC reported that Jersey Mike’s same-store sales increased 50% in recent years. Same-store sales measure performance at existing restaurants, apart from growth from new locations [3].
  • IPO disclosures: MarketWatch said the IPO filing includes details on sales gains and family compensation, two areas public-market investors typically examine for governance and related-party risk [4][common].

  • Sandwich chains: CNBC identified Jersey Mike’s as the second-largest U.S. hoagie sandwich chain behind Subway, placing its public disclosures alongside the category leader [3].
  • Private equity exits: The Financial Times reported that Blackstone is targeting a valuation of up to $12 billion, making the offering a private equity exit in consumer dining [5].
