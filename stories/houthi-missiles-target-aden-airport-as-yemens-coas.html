Houthi missiles target Aden airport as Yemen’s coastal fighting intensifies

Category: World

Yemen’s Transport Ministry said Houthi missiles and explosive-laden drones targeted Aden International Airport early Wednesday, with one missile landing near the runway minutes before a flight from Cairo was due to arrive. Authorities diverted the plane to Jeddah, evacuated the terminal and moved two aircraft. No casualties were reported. The Houthis said they targeted military supplies at a nearby camp. The ministry warned that the near miss could have caused a civilian aviation disaster. Aden airport is Yemen’s main international gateway outside Houthi control [5][8][27][46]. The Houthis also claimed drone and missile strikes on King Khalid Airport in Riyadh and attacks on Abha airport and Khamis Mushait air base. Saudi authorities did not immediately confirm the claims of airport attacks. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile north of Riyadh [9][10][3]. Saudi-backed Yemeni forces pressed an offensive to retake Red Sea coastal areas after Houthi advances. Control of territory remains disputed, and fighting has intensified around Taiz and the Bab al-Mandeb approaches [8][16][19].
