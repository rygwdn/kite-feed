Winter storm brings freezing rain to Ontario, Quebec

Category: Canada

A late-season winter storm swept across Central Canada on Wednesday, bringing a mix of heavy rain, freezing rain, and heavy snow to parts of Ontario and Quebec, including the Ottawa–Québec City corridor [2][3][1]. Environment Canada warned that ice buildup could disrupt travel, damage property, and trigger power outages [3][1]. La Presse reported that the worst-case freezing-rain scenario ultimately spared most of Quebec. Still, Environment Canada said Thursday could bring localized trouble, including broken branches and slick roads [4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Warnings across the region: Environment Canada issued freezing-rain and winter-storm warnings for much of southern Québec as the system moved toward the Ottawa–Québec City corridor [1].
  • Live updates: La Presse followed the storm with a rolling live file as conditions shifted through the day [5].
