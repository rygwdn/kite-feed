Nvidia urges Europe to build AI capacity as compute lags

Category: AI

Nvidia Chief Executive Jensen Huang urged European companies to become AI producers at the company’s Oct. 21 GTC conference in Berlin, where speakers described computing capacity as a major constraint on the region’s AI development. Europe has about 5% of the world’s AI computing capacity, compared with 75% in the United States, according to the Associated Press. Jeannette zu Fürstenberg, president and managing director of AI investor General Catalyst, said, “The big bottleneck is compute” [32][29][13]. Mistral and Nvidia separately announced plans to extend their partnership into engineering simulation. The companies plan to combine Mistral’s AI models and physics-modeling tools with Nvidia software for aerospace, automotive and semiconductor design, and to publish datasets and benchmarks for evaluating physics models and engineering agents [2][5]. Google Cloud said its Vera Rubin-based A5X computing instances are due to become generally available next month, with expansion into European regions to support local data-residency requirements [33][19]. Mistral and Nvidia have not announced release dates for their engineering integrations or benchmarks [2].

Key Points

  • Larger clusters: Google Cloud says its planned networking can link up to 80,000 Rubin GPUs at one site and 960,000 across multiple sites [33].
  • Simulation assets: Mistral and Nvidia plan to link Mistral models with Omniverse libraries to generate 3D scenes and simulation-ready assets for engineering workflows [2][5].
  • Independent safeguards: Nvidia’s Sentry security framework runs on BlueField-4 outside the host software, inspecting agent traffic and enforcing access rules without relying on agents to report their own actions [14][17].
  • Cooling claims: Nvidia says its closed-loop liquid cooling and dry coolers could save 54 million liters of water a day at a 2-gigawatt scale; it cautions that extreme heat may require additional cooling [31].

Technical Details

  • Runtime policy enforcement: Nvidia Sentry places a BlueField-4-based security layer outside host software to inspect agent communications. DOCA Flow applies layer-four network filtering, and DOCA Tower performs application-layer checks [14].
  • Physics-model evaluation: The planned Mistral-Nvidia benchmarks will compare model and agent accuracy, task completion time and computing cost, rather than output quality alone [2][3].

Industry Impact

  • Regional investment: Mistral’s infrastructure plan targets 1 GW of capacity by 2030, extending beyond the engineering collaboration [3][5].
  • Engineering workflows: Physics AI could increase the number of simulations engineers can run, while conventional simulation tools remain important for validation and unusual cases [2].
