Samsung launches Galaxy Z Fold 8 lineup

Category: Technology

Samsung introduced its 2026 foldable lineup at Galaxy Unpacked in London, adding the Galaxy Z Fold 8, Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Flip 8 to its smartphone range [5][45][38]. The company split its book-style foldable line for the first time: the standard Galaxy Z Fold 8 uses a shorter, wider “passport-style” shape, while the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra keeps the taller Fold design and adds higher-end hardware [8][15][1]. U.S. pricing starts at $1,899.99 for the Galaxy Z Fold 8, $2,099.99 for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and $1,199.99 for the Galaxy Z Flip 8, with preorders available ahead of an Aug. 7 release [32][31][46][48]. Samsung announced the phones before Apple’s rumored foldable iPhone announcement in September, a comparison several reviewers made with Samsung’s established foldable line [2][18].

Key Points

  • Display split: The Galaxy Z Fold 8 has a 7.6-inch inner display and a 5.5-inch outside display in a wider 16:10 shape, while the Ultra model has an 8.0-inch main display [15][19][33].
  • Camera hierarchy: The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra has a 200MP main camera, while coverage of the standard Fold 8 describes a simpler camera system and no telephoto camera [33][16][19][30].
  • Multitasking software: One UI 9 adds an Open Canvas-style three-app split-screen mode with a 90:10 layout on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 series [9].
  • Battery and charging: The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra uses a 5,000mAh battery, while the Galaxy Z Flip 8 keeps a 4,300mAh battery with 25W wired charging [33][24][45][41].
  • Preorder incentives: Samsung and major retailers advertised trade-in credits, carrier bill-credit offers and gift cards, including up to $1,900 off through carrier deals for Fold models and a $200 Amazon gift card for Flip 8 preorders [31][46][32].

Technical Details

  • Flex Titanium display support: Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra uses Flex Titanium technology to make the folding display stronger, thinner and flatter, with reviewers focusing on reduced crease visibility [3][24].
  • Silicon-carbon battery: The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra introduces a silicon-carbon battery in a Galaxy phone, enabling a 5,000mAh capacity inside a 4.1mm unfolded body, according to product coverage [24].
  • APV codec and LUT presets: The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra adds APV codec support and Cine LUT tools with four cinema presets for video workflows [42].

Industry Impact

  • Carrier and retail channels: Reliance Digital made the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold8 and Galaxy Z Flip8 available online and in stores in India, while KT opened experience zones at flagship and regional stores in South Korea [33][34][44].
  • Accessory market: Samsung published official case videos for the Galaxy Z Fold 8, Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Flip 8, including magnetic carbon, silicone and clear cases for the Fold models [4].
  • Foldable competition: Motorola’s Razr Ultra launched at $1,499.99 before Samsung’s $1,199.99 Galaxy Z Flip 8, leaving Samsung’s flip phone $300 cheaper at launch in the U.S. [48].

Historical Background

Samsung launched the original Galaxy Fold in 2019, helping establish the modern book-style foldable smartphone category [common]. The Galaxy Z Flip line arrived in 2020 with a clamshell design focused more on compactness and style than tablet-like multitasking [6]. Until the 2026 lineup, Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold series generally followed a tall, narrow cover-screen format that reviewers often described as less comfortable for ordinary phone use [7][15].

Action Items

  • Compare preorder offers before buying: Check Samsung, Amazon and carrier offers because incentives vary by trade-in status, gift card value and bill-credit requirements [31][46][32].
  • Test the form factor in person: If possible, handle both Fold designs before ordering because the Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra use different aspect ratios and target different viewing and productivity preferences [15][19].
