Alberta premier rejects Crown gas corporation after report leak

Category: Canada

Alberta Premier Danielle Smith said Sept. 12 that her government is unlikely to create a Crown corporation to build natural-gas pipelines after a leaked cabinet report proposed public entities and legislative changes to expand infrastructure for rising demand from data centres, oilsands production and other industries [1][5][2]. Smith said the government is examining ways to increase competition and accelerate construction, arguing that private-sector interest may make public intervention unnecessary [5]. Energy Minister Brian Jean presented the 46-page document at a Sept. 10 cabinet meeting. It outlined options including giving government-owned corporations powers to plan, finance, backstop or require pipeline expansions [3][2]. It also proposed transferring regulatory control of TC Energy’s Nova Gas Transmission system from the federal Canada Energy Regulator, according to The Narwhal [4]. Government press secretary Josh Aldrich said internal materials represent policy options rather than decisions [3].
