UAE says Flydubai co-pilot planned crash into Ben Gurion terminal

Category: World

United Arab Emirates prosecutors said on Oct. 9 that an Omani Flydubai co-pilot planned to seize a Dubai-to-Tel Aviv flight and crash it into Ben Gurion Airport’s passenger terminal to cause mass casualties. The 29-year-old allegedly attacked the captain with an emergency axe aboard the flight on Sept. 30; investigators said the Sept. 11, 2001, attacks inspired him [3][5][12]. Prosecutors said the suspect’s radicalization began in 2017 while he was studying in Australia and that he used two earlier Tel Aviv flights to learn the route and airport. He joined Flydubai in January 2026 after working for other airlines. The UAE attorney general said the co-pilot met Flydubai’s stated professional and security requirements and that checks found no indication of extremist views or an attack plan [3][5][10]. The injured captain opened the cockpit door, allowing passengers and crew to restrain the co-pilot. Another pilot aboard helped take control before the plane landed in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia. The suspect said he acted alone, but prosecutors are verifying that claim and examining statements, recordings and technical reports [23][36].
