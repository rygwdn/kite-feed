NASA’s Curiosity marks 5,000th Martian day with panorama

Category: Science

NASA’s Curiosity rover marked its 5,000th Martian day on Aug. 30, 2026, while stopped at a sandy ridge nicknamed “Chocolatal” in Gale Crater. It used its navigation cameras to photograph the landscape that morning, then took a second panorama on Sept. 2, its 5,003rd sol [1][3][4]. NASA combined the morning and afternoon images into a single scene, adding blue tones for morning and yellow for afternoon as an artistic interpretation. The panorama shows Curiosity and its tracks against the landscape around Mount Sharp and Gale Crater [1][3][5].
