Anthropic opens Claude Duet research preview

Category: AI

Anthropic has opened Claude Duet, an AI assistant that works in the background, as a research preview for U.S. Pro and Max subscribers through a waitlist. Running on its own cloud computer, Duet can monitor connected apps, prepare a morning briefing, schedule meetings, draft follow-ups and build dashboards [1][4]. Duet’s background checks and longer tasks count toward the same usage limits as regular Claude chats. Users must approve sensitive actions, and some financial actions are prohibited even with approval [1][4]. Separately, Anthropic made its Cowork agent generally available to Enterprise customers on Oct. 23, adding spending caps and other controls for usage-based billing [2].

Key Points

  • Account safeguards: Approval prompts show the proposed action and destination. Sign-in credentials are kept separate from the model, and outgoing messages are screened for sensitive information, though Anthropic says the checks can miss content or flag harmless material [4].
  • Enterprise controls: Claude Enterprise administrators can limit models by role, set spending caps at the organization, group or individual level, and pool monthly budgets. Outbound network access is disabled by default for its cloud agent sessions [2].
  • Competitive field: Meta's Muse attracted more than 10 million users in the U.S. and Canada within six weeks of launch, according to CEO Mark Zuckerberg [3].

Timeline

  • 8 September 2026: Meta launched Muse, its assistant that works in the background [3].
  • 29 September 2026: OpenAI launched Dots, another assistant that works in the background [3].
  • 21 October 2026: Anthropic announced Duet as a research preview [3][4].
  • 23 October 2026: Anthropic made Claude Cowork integration generally available to Enterprise customers [2].

Technical Details

  • Cloud session: Duet runs on its own virtual machine and browser, so it can keep working while a user's computer is off. Each Cowork session uses an isolated virtual machine [1][2].
  • Retained memory: Disconnecting an app prevents future access, but Duet retains information it has already remembered until its memory is reset [4].

Industry Impact

  • Business adoption: Anthropic reported in April that more than 1,000 business customers were spending over $1 million annually, twice the number reported two months earlier [3].
