Barclays gives half its developers access to Anthropic’s Claude

Category: AI

Barclays is expanding its use of Anthropic’s Claude, giving it to half its developers, according to The Wealth Advisor [4]. PYMNTS identifies Claude Code, Anthropic’s coding tool, as part of the rollout [1]. Bloomberg reports that the expansion aims to improve efficiency [3].
