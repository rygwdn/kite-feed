Montreal roadwork closes Highway 40, tunnel routes

Category: Canada

Montreal-area drivers face major weekend road closures from July 10 to July 13, including a full eastbound closure of Highway 40, the Metropolitan, between Exit 65 near Montreal-Trudeau airport and the Highway 15 southbound access [2][1][3]. The closure runs from Friday at 11:30 p.m. to Monday at 5 a.m. Quebec’s Transport Ministry said it is needed for asphalt work [2]. Mobilité Montréal also listed closures around the Louis-Hippolyte-La Fontaine tunnel from July 10 to July 13 [1][3]. Authorities warned of significant congestion in the affected sectors and advised drivers to plan ahead [1][2][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Ramp closures: Accesses from Côte-Vertu Boulevard and Highway 520 eastbound were scheduled to close at 11 p.m., along with Décarie interchange ramps from Highway 15 northbound and Marcel-Laurin and Décarie boulevards [1].
  • Suggested detours: Mobilité Montréal suggested drivers use highways 13, 20, 30 and 440 as alternate routes around the Highway 40 work zone [1][3].
  • Fireworks closures: The Jacques-Cartier Bridge was scheduled to close in both directions on July 9 and July 12 because of the Loto-Québec International Fireworks Competition [3].
  • South Shore work: On Highway 30 eastbound in Beauharnois, one of two lanes was scheduled to close Sunday at the Saint-Louis River bridge from 6 a.m. to 2 a.m. [3].

Perspectives

Quebec Transport Ministry: The ministry said the highway work could be postponed, extended or cancelled because of adverse weather or operational constraints.
