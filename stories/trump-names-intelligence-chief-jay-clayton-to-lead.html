Trump names intelligence chief Jay Clayton to lead AI task force

Category: AI

President Trump named national intelligence director Jay Clayton to lead a new federal AI task force, described as the “Super Intelligence Force.” Its remit includes reviewing AI risks, security threats and the federal government’s role [50][75][74]. The task force will conduct a White House-led review, but its co-chair said it would not rush regulatory rules, Axios reported [36]. The task force has a published charter, though available reporting describes its broad remit rather than specific proposed regulations [74][75]. Separately, the Justice Department instructed staff to refer to artificial intelligence as “super intelligence” under a Trump order, Reuters reported [61]. Former Federal Trade Commission Chair Lina Khan has called for Congress to legislate on AI [54].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • State pressure: North Carolina joined 25 other states urging Congress to regulate AI [68].
  • Utah directive: Utah Gov. Spencer Cox signed an order directing state government to use what it calls “responsible” AI [67][51].
  • California laws: California enacted AI laws covering employment, health care and biosecurity, according to Distilled Post [30].
  • New York City hearing: The City Council held a hearing on AI safety risks [23].

Perspectives

Experts cited by Nextgov said the new AI czar’s leadership could help advance regulation [35].
