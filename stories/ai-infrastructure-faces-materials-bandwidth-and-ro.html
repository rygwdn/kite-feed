AI infrastructure faces materials, bandwidth and robotics constraints

Category: AI

The expansion of artificial intelligence is creating engineering demands beyond model design, with semiconductors and data centers approaching physical limits in performance, heat management, electrical efficiency and reliability [1]. Hardware-focused startups are attracting renewed venture-capital interest as investors back companies developing robots, semiconductors and other “deep tech” systems [2]. As accelerator counts grow, AI clusters face networking constraints [4].
