OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 Astra across paid products

Category: AI

OpenAI has begun a gradual rollout of GPT-6 Astra. It is available in regular ChatGPT as GPT-6 Pro to $100 and $200 Pro subscribers and Business and Enterprise customers. Plus subscribers can access Astra through ChatGPT Work and Codex rather than standard ChatGPT, while Free and Go users are excluded. OpenAI has not said when eligible accounts will receive access [5]. OpenAI said Perplexity is using Astra for end-to-end work including drafting communications, modifying software and monitoring production systems, with fewer check-ins than the company required with earlier models [2]. In a separate test, Simon Willison had Astra generate 5K and 10K running routes from OpenStreetMap data, display an interactive map and produce GPX and GeoJSON files. He said the interface did not expose the executed code after the conversation was compacted [1].

Primary Source: View original article

I think any LLM system that uses compaction needs to both preserve the pre-compacted text and make that text available via agent tool calls, to protect against this kind of problem.

— Simon Willison (Simon Willison’s Weblog)

Did you know: The route-generation task took 27 minutes, according to Willison [1].

  • Usage limits: Engadget reported that Astra access comes with usage limits, although the article did not specify a single limit applying to every eligible plan [5].
  • Product paths: Enterprise access may depend on workspace model permissions, and Codex CLI users need version 0.153.0 or later to select Astra [5].
  • Route setup: Willison asked Astra to create loops from his house [1].
