Canadiens beat Red Wings 1-0 in six-round shootout

Category: Canada

The Montreal Canadiens snapped a three-game losing streak Saturday, beating the Detroit Red Wings 1-0 in a shootout at the Bell Centre. Zachary Bolduc scored in the sixth round, while Jacob Fowler stopped 30 shots in regulation and overtime and all six Detroit shootout attempts. Daniil Tarasov also made 30 saves for Detroit [1][4][6]. Montreal had three goals overturned: Cole Caufield’s first-period shot did not cross the goal line, Bolduc’s second-period goal was disallowed for goaltender interference, and Alexandre Texier’s overtime goal was ruled offside [1][9]. Canadiens fans also chanted for Detroit forward Dylan Larkin, who requested a trade in June; he said he was surprised by the reception [3]. Detroit hosts New Jersey and Montreal hosts Buffalo on Tuesday [1].

Primary Source: View original article

I never would have imagined that in this building and in my career.

— Dylan Larkin (The Canadian Press, published by Times Colonist)

Key Points

  • Power-play trouble: Montreal went 0-for-4 with the man advantage and is 1-for-17 (5.9%) on the power play this season [1].
  • Fowler’s call-up: The 21-year-old was recalled from the Laval Rocket on Wednesday and is filling in for Samuel Montembeault, who is expected to miss 10 to 14 days with a lower-body injury [1].
  • Trade control: Larkin’s eight-year, $69.6-million contract includes a full no-trade clause, and his reported list of acceptable destinations does not include a Canadian team [3].

Perspectives

Dylan Larkin: Larkin said he had not yet processed the crowd’s reaction and was focused on a tight-checking game. He cited a league poll that ranked the Bell Centre highly and declined to speculate about Montreal as a trade destination [3].

Sources: The Canadian Press, published by Times Colonist

Todd McLellan: Detroit’s coach acknowledged that the trade uncertainty was a distraction but said players and staff had worked to keep the team focused on preparation [3].
