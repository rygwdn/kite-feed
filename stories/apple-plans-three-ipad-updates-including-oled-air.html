Apple plans three iPad updates, including OLED Air, for first half of 2027: report

Category: Technology

Apple is preparing new iPad Air, iPad Pro and entry-level iPad models for the first half of 2027, according to Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman’s Power On newsletter [4][2][3]. The Air is expected to gain an OLED display, while the Pro is expected to add vapor-chamber cooling. Apple has not announced the products or confirmed their specifications [4][7]. The reported updates would follow October’s OLED iPad mini update, bring OLED to the Air, which currently uses an LCD screen, and improve heat management on the Pro during demanding workloads [1][2]. Reports suggest the entry-level model will gain hardware to support Apple Intelligence and newer wireless connectivity, while details on its screen and design remain limited [5][7]. Gurman’s reported schedule puts all three updates before July 2027; Apple has not announced launch dates or prices [8][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Base model: Reports point to an A19 processor and 8GB of RAM for the entry-level iPad, plus Apple’s N1 wireless chip and C1X modem in cellular versions [5][7].
  • Air display: One report says the Air’s OLED screen is expected to retain a 60Hz refresh rate and that the model is unlikely to switch from Touch ID to Face ID [5].
  • Pro chip: The next Pro is expected to use an M6 chip, although some reports say M7 is also possible. The current 11-inch and 13-inch Pro models start at $1,199 and $1,499, respectively [4][10].
  • Air pricing: One report estimates the next 11-inch Air could start at $799 to $899, but Apple has not announced pricing [5].
