Canadian advocates press RCMP for Tumbler Ridge gun details

Category: Canada

Canadian gun-control advocacy groups are urging the RCMP to share more information about the firearms used in the Feb. 10 mass shooting in Tumbler Ridge, B.C. [1][2][3]. The shootings left nine people dead, including the young assailant, and injured others, according to incident details cited in reporting [2]. The RCMP says it can’t release the requested information while the investigation is still active [3]. Separately, the local MP has voiced support for a lawsuit filed by the mother of a girl who was critically wounded. The suit alleges OpenAI bears some responsibility connected to the attack [5][6]. Another report notes that lawsuits targeting AI companies could take years to move through the courts [7].

Perspectives

Gun-control advocacy groups: Five advocacy organizations told the RCMP they are “alarmed and disappointed” that the force hasn’t released basic information about the firearms used, including the models and whether they were legally obtained.
