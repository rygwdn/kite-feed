Trump announces AI Force and AI czar role

Category: AI

President Trump said on September 19 that his administration would create an “AI Force” modeled on the Space Force and appoint an artificial intelligence czar. He provided no details on either initiative’s structure, membership, authority or timetable [1][51][58]. He said the administration would support the industry’s growth rather than slow it and would use existing criminal and civil law to address harmful conduct [51][47][48]. Trump announced the plans while rejecting calls for new federal safeguards and describing concerns about AI’s potential harms as a “hoax.” He said the United States must maintain its lead over China and proposed replacing the term “artificial intelligence” with alternatives including “Superior Intelligence,” “Extreme Intelligence” or “Supreme Intelligence” [46][51][58]. AI is expected to feature in Trump’s planned meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping next week [50][51].
