Anthropic takes over $3.4 billion in gas generator orders for AI data centers

Category: AI

AI company Anthropic has committed $3.4 billion to secure 1.7 gigawatts of gas-fired generators for its data centers by taking over existing equipment orders from San Antonio-based supplier OnePWR Solutions, the Financial Times reported. The newspaper said the companies agreed to the deal in late September and that it includes engineering and construction capabilities to install the equipment. Anthropic did not buy OnePWR, and Reuters said it could not independently verify the report [12][2]. The deal gives Anthropic direct access to generator orders OnePWR placed with Caterpillar about two years earlier, as demand for large power equipment stretches manufacturing lead times. Anthropic initially planned to buy electricity from OnePWR, the Financial Times reported. Strong demand for its Claude models has prompted the company to seek more computing and power capacity [12][7][2]. Anthropic and OnePWR declined to comment to the Financial Times and did not respond to Reuters’ requests for comment. The report does not identify where the generators will be installed or when they will begin operating [12][2][21].

Key Points

  • Long-term commitments: Anthropic’s amended public-listing filing disclosed about $895.4 billion in computing and infrastructure commitments through 2045, with roughly $447.5 billion expected before the end of 2030 [5][22].
  • Separate corroboration: Investment bank Piper Sandler listed a September 2026 sale of OnePWR’s power-generation platform for $3.4 billion to an unnamed frontier AI lab, but did not identify the buyer [18][1].

Perspectives

Anthropic: In its amended listing filing, the company identified electricity availability as an obstacle to data-center development and warned that natural-gas generation exposes it to fuel-price swings, supply disruptions and pipeline limits [5].

Timeline

  • About two years before September 2026: OnePWR placed its Caterpillar generator orders about two years before the reported transfer [12].
  • September 2026: Anthropic agreed to take over OnePWR’s 1.7-gigawatt generator commitments [12][2].
  • 2 November 2026: Anthropic’s amended public-listing filing detailed energy and infrastructure risks [5].
  • 8 November 2026: Financial Times disclosed the generator transaction; Reuters said it could not verify it [12][2].

Industry Impact

  • Power equipment: AI developers are taking a more direct role in securing generation equipment as demand for generators and gas turbines rises and manufacturing lead times stretch; xAI also acquired mobile gas turbines for $2 billion this year [12][2].
  • Data-center energy: Anthropic’s filing described electricity availability as an obstacle to data-center expansion and noted that new generation, transmission and grid connections can take years to build [5].

Historical Background

In February 2026, Anthropic said it would pay the full cost of grid upgrades needed for data centers it co-develops [18].

Business Angle

  • Anthropic’s filing says about $213.8 billion of its commitments include termination rights or depend on financing, site, facility or regulatory conditions [18].

Q&A

Q: Does Anthropic’s grid-upgrade pledge cover every data center it uses?

A: No. Its February pledge applies to data centers it co-develops for its own workloads. For leased capacity, Anthropic said it was still exploring ways to address the effect of its demand on electricity prices [18].

Q: How soon could the generators be available?

A: The Financial Times reported that the equipment may become available in the near term and over the next two years [12][6].
