Delta restores Atlanta flight system after outage delays 1,000 flights

Category: Technology

Delta Air Lines restored its automated aircraft weight-and-balance system on Sunday after an outage delayed about 1,000 flights, or 67% of its schedule, as Thanksgiving travel approached. Flight operations were still recovering after automation returned. Delta attributed roughly 70% of delays and cancellations tracked by FlightAware to the outage and the rest to weather [9][8]. The system calculates aircraft weight and balance before departure. Crews use that information to confirm a plane can fly safely. Delta used manual procedures while working to restore the system, and the Federal Aviation Administration said its air-traffic systems were not involved. The agency issued a roughly hour-long ground stop for some flights into Atlanta, Delta’s largest hub. Delta had about 5,000 flights scheduled Sunday, according to the airline [1][9][8].

  • Traffic control: The FAA advisory covered Delta flights departing for Atlanta from within the contiguous United States, as well as flights leaving the hub [10].
