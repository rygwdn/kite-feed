Trump evacuated after shooting at Washington press dinner

Category: World

President Trump, First Lady Melania Trump, Vice-President J.D. Vance, and other senior US officials were evacuated from the White House Correspondents’ Association dinner at the Washington Hilton in Washington on April 25 after a gunman fired at a security checkpoint outside the ballroom [6][23][15][40]. Authorities took the suspect into custody before he reached the reception hall, and multiple outlets said President Trump and other protectees were unharmed [1][3][46][51]. Officials said the suspect was carrying multiple weapons. President Trump later said a Secret Service officer was shot but protected by a bulletproof vest [16][20][14][37]. US Attorney Jeanine Pirro said the suspect would face charges including assaulting a federal officer with a dangerous weapon and using a firearm during a crime of violence. Investigators were still examining the motive and whether additional charges, including possible terrorism counts, could follow [41][25][46]. The shooting turned a high-profile evening celebrating the Washington press corps into a scene of confusion, with hundreds of guests diving under tables as armed agents entered the room [2][47][19]. One reassuring note in an otherwise tense night was the speed of the security response: officers stopped the suspect before he entered the ballroom, the injured officer was reported to be doing well, and the event is expected to be rescheduled [1][41][42].
