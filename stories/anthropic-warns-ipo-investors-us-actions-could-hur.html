Anthropic warns IPO investors U.S. actions could hurt customer ties

Category: AI

Anthropic is preparing a public offering as early as mid-November, Bloomberg reported. Formal marketing could begin the week of Nov. 9, with the company seeking to list before the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday on Nov. 26. Investor estimates put its potential valuation at $1.8 trillion to $2 trillion [27][35][34]. A draft IPO prospectus warns that U.S. government actions and perceptions of Anthropic could harm its relationships with commercial customers and partners, not just government sales. The filing cites President Donald Trump’s February order for federal agencies to stop using the company’s models, a Defense Department supply-chain-risk designation and worldwide export restrictions imposed in June on its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models. The Commerce Department later lifted the restrictions, and Anthropic restored access [24]. Anthropic said those actions could cause revenue losses, business disruption and reputational damage even if the company ultimately prevails. Government agency contracts account for less than 1% of annual revenue, the filing said, but government views could affect customers, partners, employees and investors. The filing also disclosed that advanced AI could pose “catastrophic or existential risks to humanity” [24].

  • Offering calendar: Anthropic is seeking to begin investor marketing in the week starting Nov. 9, ahead of the Nov. 26 Thanksgiving holiday [27][37].
