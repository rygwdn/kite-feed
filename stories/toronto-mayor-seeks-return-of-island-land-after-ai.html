Toronto mayor seeks return of island land after airport rejection

Category: Canada

Toronto Mayor Olivia Chow filed an urgent motion on Monday asking Ontario to return waterfront and island lands after the federal government rejected Premier Doug Ford’s proposal to expand Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport for jet service [5][6][4]. Chow said she wants Little Norway Park and Toronto Islands land back under municipal control [5][6]. Ontario passed legislation in early June giving the province power to take over the City of Toronto’s share in the airport’s governing agreement and lands owned by the city [3][7]. Federal involvement is required because the Toronto Port Authority, an arm’s-length federal agency, operates Billy Bishop, while the city owns 20 per cent of the land and the Toronto Port Authority and Transport Canada hold the rest [3]. The federal government announced on Friday that Ottawa would focus “exclusively” on approved safety upgrades already underway at Billy Bishop instead of the airport expansion plan [5]. Canada’s National Observer reported that the Toronto Port Authority estimated the expansion could cost up to $5 billion [3].

Key Points

  • Council timing: Chow described the motion as urgent because Monday marked the last city council sitting this term before the municipal election [6].
  • Public feedback: A federal online survey drew more than 87,000 respondents, and PressProgress reported that 87 per cent opposed the expansion plan [7].
  • Expansion scope: PressProgress reported the provincial plan would double airport capacity, extend the runway to nearly two kilometres and allow Boeing 737s to land downtown [7].
