China expands AI from classrooms to logistics hubs

Category: AI

China’s AI expansion reaches daily life, infrastructure, education and robotics. The New York Times described everyday applications in Beijing as useful, gimmicky or unexpectedly funny [4]. WIRED reported that a city in Inner Mongolia has become a crucial data-center hub because of cheap energy, abundant land and proximity to Beijing [5]. At Beijing’s Global Smart Education Conference 2026, officials and educators discussed AI-driven schooling as China moves from isolated pilots toward system-wide deployment through an “AI Plus Education” action plan [6]. At the 2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing, Xu Xiaolan said embodied AI products are shifting from small-batch trials toward broader deployment. More than 1,000 Chinese and overseas guests attended, and more than 300 companies showed over 3,000 products [7]. In Hong Kong, Financial Secretary Paul Chan opened the four-day Computer and Communications Festival by encouraging residents to learn how to use AI. Humanoid robots, 3D printers and AI hubs were among the technologies on display at the Convention and Exhibition Centre in Wan Chai [9].

Key Points

  • Education rollout: China’s AI Plus Education plan covers talent cultivation, application innovation, infrastructure development and ecosystem building [6].
  • Hong Kong showcase: Hundreds of visitors attended the 22nd Hong Kong Computer and Communications Festival on its opening day, exploring AI experience zones and exhibitor booths [9].

Perspectives

Ren Youqun, China’s vice minister of education: Ren said China has made AI integration in education a national priority and is embedding intelligent technologies across teaching elements, processes and scenarios.

Xu Xiaolan, Chinese Institute of Electronics: Xu described embodied AI as a potential trillion-yuan-level industry and an important engine for new forms of the intelligent economy.
