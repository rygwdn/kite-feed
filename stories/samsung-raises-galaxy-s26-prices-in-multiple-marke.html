Samsung raises Galaxy S26 prices in multiple markets as memory shortage drives costs

Category: Technology

Samsung raised prices for most Galaxy S26 models in the United States and several other markets, adding $100 to most U.S. configurations and $200 to the 1TB S26 Ultra, which now costs $1,999.99 [1][15][5]. The base S26 now starts at $999.99, $200 more than the Galaxy S25’s launch price. It had launched at a $100 premium over the S25 [6][14]. Prices also rose in Canada, Europe, the United Kingdom, South Korea, India, China and Brazil, though the increases vary by market [15][18][23][27]. Samsung attributed the German increases to higher RAM and storage-chip costs. Reports on its earnings call and the wider market point to AI data centers absorbing large amounts of memory supply [28][2][5]. Samsung’s S26 FE and its newest foldable phones were not included in the latest U.S. increases [4][29]. A Korean technology leaker says Samsung may raise S27 prices further, but the company has not confirmed those plans [30].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Regional changes: Canadian prices rose by C$150 for most configurations and C$250 for the 1TB Ultra; in China, listed increases ranged from 800 to 1,800 yuan, while Brazilian increases ranged from R$300 to R$1,300 [12][23][27].
  • Retailer pricing: Samsung’s updated prices were live on its own site while Amazon and Best Buy still showed older prices at the time of reporting [4].
  • Industry pricing: Apple has also raised prices on several iPhone models by about $100, and Google’s Pixel 11 lineup costs more than its predecessor [5][2].
  • Supply outlook: Samsung told investors it expected the memory shortage to worsen in 2027 and potentially persist through 2028 [2].

Perspectives

Samsung Electronics: Prices in Germany rose by €100 for each of the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra, to €1,099, €1,349 and €1,549, respectively [28].

Sources: GIGA

Lanzuk, Korean technology leaker: Lanzuk said an initial S27 price structure had been set, but a possible additional increase could reach the high-130,000 won range, with larger jumps for higher-storage versions [30].

Sources: The Korea Herald

Technical Details

  • AI memory demand: AI data centers require substantial memory capacity, including high-bandwidth memory, increasing demand for components used in consumer devices [5][2].
  • Component price forecasts: TrendForce projected fourth-quarter 2026 contract-price increases of 10% to 15% for conventional DRAM and 15% to 20% for NAND flash [30].

Historical Background

Earlier in 2026, Samsung co-CEO TM Roh said the global memory shortage would have an “inevitable” effect on phone prices [14].

A: Samsung’s China website offered coupons that lowered some Galaxy S26-series prices to as little as 5,999 yuan and advertised price protection for the Double 11 shopping period [23].
