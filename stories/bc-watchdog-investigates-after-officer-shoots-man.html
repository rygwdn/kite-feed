B.C. watchdog investigates after officer shoots man in Burnaby

Category: Canada

British Columbia’s Independent Investigations Office is investigating after an RCMP officer shot and seriously injured a man at a Burnaby residence on Sunday morning [1][3]. Police said officers responded at about 10:15 a.m. to a disturbance on Kingsway Avenue and that the man approached them with an edged weapon before an officer fired [1][3]. The man received first aid at the scene and was taken to hospital; no one else was injured, the Vancouver Sun reported [3]. The watchdog says it has opened an investigation into the shooting [1].

Key Points

  • Witness appeal: The Independent Investigations Office is seeking witnesses to the shooting [1].
  • Call details: The watchdog said police were responding to a possible domestic dispute [1].
