UK proposes midnight social media curfew for teens

Category: Technology

The UK government proposed making social media apps unavailable by default for 16- and 17-year-olds between midnight and 6 a.m., while allowing teenagers to override the setting in their accounts [8][6][5][11]. The plan would apply to services including Instagram, TikTok and YouTube and would turn off autoplay, infinite scroll and personalised feeds by default for older teenagers [8][7][17]. Ministers said the measures are intended to improve sleep, concentration and family time, and the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology plans to put the proposals before Parliament by the end of 2026 so they can begin in spring 2027 [7][20][17]. The curfew would sit alongside a separate under-16 social media ban announced in June, expected to cover platforms including Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X while excluding messaging services such as WhatsApp and Signal [6][9][51]. The government proposed default settings rather than a hard ban for older teenagers, and Online Safety Minister Kanishka Narayan rejected claims that teenagers would simply switch the protections off [6][51]. Critics including campaigner Ellen Roome, social media analyst Matt Navarra and opposition Conservative education spokesperson Laura Trott questioned whether optional curbs would have much effect [8][4][27].

  • The proposal targets engagement mechanics rather than only content moderation, which could affect time spent and ad inventory if other countries copy it [8][17].
