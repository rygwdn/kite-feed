Xiaomi takes dual-screen 18 Pro phones global at €1,399

Category: Technology

Xiaomi launched its 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max smartphones internationally at a Paris event on Nov. 17, bringing the rear-screen lineup beyond China for the first time. European prices start at €1,399 for the Pro and €1,599 for the Pro Max; the top Max configuration, with 16GB of memory and 1TB of storage, costs €2,099 [8][5]. The company has not announced U.S. availability and is not selling the phones there at launch [6][3]. Both models have a rear touchscreen beside their cameras for framing selfies and vlogs with the rear cameras and displaying notifications and app information. Their front screens also include a hardware-based privacy mode that narrows side viewing angles. The camera systems combine 200-megapixel main and telephoto cameras with a 50-megapixel ultrawide camera; the Pro Max adds a sensor feature intended to retain detail in bright and dark areas [2][6]. International models have smaller batteries than their Chinese versions: 6,000mAh versus 7,000mAh in the Pro, and 7,050mAh versus 8,500mAh in the Pro Max. Both run Android 17 with Xiaomi’s HyperOS 4 [2][22][3]. Preorders in Singapore begin Nov. 18, while Xiaomi has announced pricing and sales in European markets [2][22].

Key Points

  • Privacy limits: Xiaomi says dedicated privacy pixels narrow the viewing angle, but Android Headlines found the screen remained readable from an angle and the feature worked in only some apps [2][3].
  • AI app trials: CNET found that generating mini-apps could take about five minutes and that the feature struggled with requests requiring access to phone data, offering little explanation when it failed [7].
  • Support period: Android Headlines said the Pro Max receives four years of operating-system updates, less than the support periods it cited for OPPO, Samsung and Google phones [3].
  • Memory costs: Xiaomi’s European launch prices are substantially higher than its starting prices in China; El Mundo linked the pricing pressure to memory costs it said had risen about 350% year over year [19].

Perspectives

Xiaomi promotes its GenAI Apps feature as a way to create mini-apps for the rear screen, called the Back Display, from natural-language prompts [11].

Android Headlines’ reviewer rated the Pro Max 4.5 out of 5, praising its speed and thermal performance in testing, but said the OPPO Find X10 Pro Max was a better choice for buyers prioritizing battery life or cameras [3].

Technical Details

  • LOFIC imaging: Lateral Overflow Integration Capacitor technology redirects excess photocharge when bright pixels approach capacity, helping prevent highlights from clipping; the Pro Max sensor is rated at 16.2 stops of dynamic range [28][6].
  • Privacy pixel layout: Xiaomi’s system switches off standard pixels while keeping specialized pixels with narrower viewing angles active; it can apply to selected apps and notifications rather than the whole display [2][28].
  • Chip manufacturing: The two Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 processors use a 2-nanometer manufacturing process and reach CPU clock speeds of up to 5GHz, according to Xiaomi launch coverage [4][19].

Historical Background

Xiaomi also experimented with a small rear screen on the Mi 11 Ultra in 2021, according to El Mundo [19].
