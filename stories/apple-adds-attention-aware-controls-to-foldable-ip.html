Apple adds attention-aware controls to foldable iPhone Duo

Category: Technology

Apple’s second iOS 27.3 developer beta adds Attention Aware features to the iPhone Duo, the company’s foldable phone, which launched without Face ID. The same beta also adds limited face unlocking through the front camera. The new attention controls use the outer display’s camera to check whether someone is looking at the screen before dimming it, expanding a lock-screen notification or lowering some alerts. Users do not need to set up face unlocking to use these controls [27][36]. Hands-on testing found that camera recognition could be inconsistent: one reviewer reported mixed results with glasses, failure to recognize a face behind a mask and occasional trouble in dim light [32]. The Verge news editor Dominic Preston said he managed to fool it with a photo once in more than 20 attempts; after a failed spoof, the phone required Touch ID for the next unlock [23]. Camera-based unlocking remains distinct from Face ID’s dedicated depth-sensing hardware [23]. Apple is also testing a setting to lock the phone when it is folded shut [12][36]. Reports say the company is prototyping Face ID for the Duo’s successor. The design is not finalized, and Apple is reportedly targeting a fall 2027 release for that model; iOS 27.3 is expected to reach the public in December 2026 [9][34].

Key Points

  • App support: Apple said more than 200,000 apps are now optimized for the Duo, up from more than 100,000 earlier in November [2][19].
  • Fold-to-lock: The new Close to Lock option lets owners lock the Duo when they close it instead of moving the inner screen’s content to the outer display; it is off by default [12][15].
  • Siri and video: The beta lets Siri answer questions using an entire Safari webpage, including content below the visible screen, and adds video podcasts to Apple TV [10][36].
  • Later models: Apple’s reported priorities for models beyond the Duo 2 include a telephoto camera and a thinner, lighter build; those upgrades may not arrive before 2028 [9].

Perspectives

Dominic Preston, The Verge news editor: Preston said the feature made routine unlocking quicker and more comfortable than using Touch ID [23].

Soha reviewer: The reviewer said waiting for a stalled scan can be slower than using Touch ID [32].

Historical Background

Apple introduced Face ID with the iPhone X in 2017, using infrared hardware to create a three-dimensional facial map rather than relying on a standard selfie camera [11].

Q&A

A: Owners can use an Apple Watch to unlock the iPhone Duo when the camera does not recognize their face [32].
