Pope Leo urges Putin to allow aid and evacuations in Oleshky

Category: World

Pope Leo XIV called Russian President Vladimir Putin from a Rome hospital on Oct. 16 and urged a ceasefire in Russian-occupied Oleshky, in Ukraine’s Kherson region, to allow food and medical aid to reach civilians and residents to leave safely [18][17]. The Vatican said the pope was deeply concerned about conditions in the city, where about 2,000 civilians remain trapped amid shortages of food and medicine [3][21]. The Kremlin said Putin supported the Vatican’s efforts to secure residents’ safety and essential supplies, and that the two sides agreed to cooperate. It also blamed Kyiv for the crisis, contradicting Ukrainian accounts of Russian obstruction [10][11]. The Kremlin statement did not confirm that Putin had agreed to the requested ceasefire or evacuation routes [23]. The call came three days after the pope underwent surgery to remove the lower lobe of his right lung. The Vatican reported that his recovery was steady [34][12]. Cardinal Pietro Parolin, the Vatican secretary of state, said the pope initiated the call. Ukrainian officials say a planned evacuation effort is ready, but Russia has not confirmed its participation [17][21].

It was the Holy Father who took the initiative to plead the case of this besieged city, where some 2,000 civilians need humanitarian routes through which they can leave.

— Cardinal Pietro Parolin (Euronews)

Key Points

  • Food deprivation: Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said residents were scavenging for food scraps and gathering grass because no other food was available [11].
  • Broader affected area: The Ukrainian Council of Churches and Religious Organizations estimated that more than 6,000 civilians, including about 200 children, remained in Oleshky and nearby communities [11].
  • Earlier appeal: The Ukrainian Council of Churches and Religious Organizations appealed to Pope Leo and other religious leaders on Sept. 19 to intervene over Oleshky [11][15].
  • Papal recovery: Doctors said a chest X-ray showed good recovery of the operated lung, while tests to determine whether the cancer had spread were still pending [12].
  • Humanitarian law: The International Committee of the Red Cross says international humanitarian law requires rapid and unimpeded passage of relief for civilians in need [11].

Perspectives

United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights: Volker Türk called the situation in Oleshky desperate and urged Russia to enable civilians to evacuate [8][12].

Sources: Dawn

Timeline

  • 26 May 2026: An Oct. 2 assessment identified this as the date of the last food delivery to Oleshky [11].
  • 21 September 2026: Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha described residents scavenging for food [11].
  • 16 October 2026: Pope Leo asked Putin for a ceasefire, aid access and safe evacuation [18].

Historical Background

Russian forces occupied Oleshky in 2022. The destruction of the Kakhovka Hydroelectric Power Plant in 2023 flooded the city and other communities in Kherson [1][11]. Pope Leo and Putin previously spoke by phone on June 4, 2025; the Oct. 16 call was their second known conversation [17][18].

Humanitarian Impact

Mines and constant drone attacks make it dangerous to leave. Residents also lack electricity and adequate medical care, and some have reportedly been unable to bury those killed [3][21][1].
