Gunmen kill at least 30 in Niger state raids

Category: World

Gunmen launched coordinated dawn raids on three rural communities in Nigeria’s north-central Niger state—Konkoso, Tungan Makeri and Pissa—killing at least 30 people and leaving others missing, according to police, residents and local officials [1][3][4][5][6][7][8][9][10]. The attacks are the latest in Nigeria’s wider security crisis and have drawn international attention after US President Donald Trump publicly questioned the country’s security situation [1][5][9][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Toll estimates and breakdown: A humanitarian source cited by Dawn put the toll at “more than 45,” including 38 killed in Konkoso, seven in Tungan Makeri and one in Pissa, while police reported a lower confirmed count in Tungan Makeri as of Saturday [2][3][10].
  • Attack tactics: Several reports said the attackers arrived on motorbikes, set homes and shops on fire, and in some cases shot people or cut their throats [6][7][9][2].
  • Extent of destruction: The same humanitarian source said nearly all homes in Konkoso burned and warned that search teams could still find more bodies as they comb the area [2].
  • Kidnappings/missing persons: Police said the gunmen abducted some villagers during the raids, and witnesses said others were still unaccounted for afterward [3][1][5][2].
  • None

Perspectives

Niger state police: Police confirmed deaths in Tungan Makeri and said the attackers kidnapped some villagers during the dawn raid. They said the overall casualty figure was still being verified.

Sources: ABC News, Dawn

Residents and local officials: Survivors said the assailants rode in on motorbikes, set buildings ablaze, and fired indiscriminately as people tried to escape.

Sources: The Guardian

Humanitarian source: A contact cited by Dawn gave a higher death toll than official figures, said Konkoso was largely burned down, and warned that more bodies could still be found as searches continue.

Sources: Dawn
