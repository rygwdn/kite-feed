Newcastle car crash injures 10 fans before rugby league final

Category: World

A car struck a crowd of Newcastle Knights supporters in Wallsend, near Newcastle, on Saturday morning, injuring 10 people as they waited to farewell the team bus before Sunday’s National Rugby League grand final in Sydney. A five-year-old girl and a 67-year-old man were in critical condition; eight others, including two young children, were reported to be stable at John Hunter Hospital [12][13][8]. Police arrested the 18-year-old driver at the scene. New South Wales Police Assistant Commissioner David Waddell said the driver had allegedly been driving erratically before losing control. Police said early inquiries did not indicate a terrorist motive and that investigators expected to charge the driver later Saturday, after mandatory testing [12][11][21]. The crash happened at about 9:30 a.m. on Newcastle Link Road, where more than 1,000 people had gathered. Emergency crews treated the injured and took them to hospital. The Knights bus was diverted, and the road reopened later Saturday. The team was due to play the Sydney Roosters in the grand final on Sunday [13][12][27].

Key Points

  • Crowd assistance: Bystanders gave injured fans first aid, including CPR, before emergency responders arrived and alongside them afterward. NSW Ambulance said their help made its work easier [11][12].
  • Emergency response: Ten ambulances and three helicopters responded, with paramedics treating people at the scene before taking them to hospital [27][16].
  • Final stakes: Newcastle were seeking their first NRL premiership in 25 years, while the Sydney Roosters were pursuing a third title after consecutive wins in 2018 and 2019 [27].
  • Road disruption: Police asked fans to leave the area and keep roads clear to help emergency services and traffic management [13].

Perspectives

NSW Police: Assistant Commissioner David Waddell said officers removed the driver and two passengers from the scene after the crowd turned on them [12][11].
