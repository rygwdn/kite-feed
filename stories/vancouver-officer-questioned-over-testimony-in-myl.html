Vancouver officer questioned over testimony in Myles Gray death

Category: Canada

A public hearing into the 2015 death of Myles Gray heard testimony that drew scrutiny over apparent inconsistencies, including detailed questions for a Vancouver police officer about what he saw and did during an encounter involving several officers [1][3]. One point of dispute surfaced when a witness said Vancouver officers were not on Gray’s back before he died — a claim that conflicts with other accounts discussed at the hearing [2][3].
