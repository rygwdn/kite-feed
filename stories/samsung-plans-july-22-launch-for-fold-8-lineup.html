Samsung plans July 22 launch for Fold 8 lineup

Category: Technology

Samsung has scheduled its next Galaxy Unpacked event for July 22, 2026, in London. The presentation starts at 2 p.m. BST and is expected to feature the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold 8, Galaxy Z Flip 8, updated wearables and Galaxy Glasses [3][11][15]. Reports say the Fold 8 Ultra would succeed last year’s book-style Fold, while the standard Galaxy Z Fold 8 name would go to a shorter, wider passport-style device; Samsung has not explained the reported naming change [3][11]. ETNews, cited by Android Authority and 9to5Google, said Samsung set initial production at 2.8 million units for the wide Galaxy Z Fold 8, 2 million for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and 1.5 million for the Galaxy Z Flip 8 [4][6][2][14].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Samsung’s London event would be its first Galaxy Unpacked in the city since the Galaxy S3 launch nearly 14 years earlier [3].

Key Points

  • Leaked colors: Images attributed to Roland Quandt showed the Galaxy Z Fold 8 in Graphite, Cream and Lavender, with a dual-camera rear system and a new cover screen [1].
  • Screen shape: The wide Fold 8 is reported to use a 16:10 aspect ratio when closed and a 4:3 aspect ratio when opened [1].
  • Flip pricing: A deleted PCDiga listing put the Galaxy Z Flip 8 at €1,349 for 256GB and €1,549 for 512GB, both €50 above a previous leak [10].
  • Market data: SAG said global foldable smartphone shipments declined 2% year over year to 3.4 million units in Q2 2026, compared with an 8% decline in Q2 2025 [9].
  • Hardware leaks: Techgenyz said the Galaxy Z Fold 8 may use two 50-megapixel rear cameras for the primary and ultrawide sensors, while the Flip 8 may have a redesigned hinge to reduce crease visibility [11].

Perspectives

ETNews supply-chain sources: Sources cited by ETNews said Samsung raised the wide Galaxy Z Fold 8 production target after initially planning similar volumes for the Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra.

Sources: Android Authority

9to5Google: 9to5Google said Samsung’s initial production mix marks a shift from past years, when the company put more emphasis on the Galaxy Z Flip series than on Fold models.

Sources: 9to5Google

Technical Details

  • 4:3 inner display: The reported 4:3 opened aspect ratio would give the inner screen a squarer layout than narrower book-style foldables, which can better support tablet-style apps and reduce letterboxing for some content [1][common].
  • Dual-display sizing: Digital Trends said the rumored Fold 8 design may use a 5.5-inch cover display and a 7.6-inch inner screen, while sitting below the Fold 8 Ultra in the lineup [10].
  • Ultra upgrades: Memeburn said the Fold 8 Ultra would increase battery capacity to 5,000mAh and ultrawide resolution to 50MP compared with the Fold 7 [13].

Industry Impact

  • Foldable competition: SAG placed Huawei first in global foldables with 47.7% share in Q2 2026, followed by Samsung at 15.2% and Lenovo at 12.9% [9].
  • Form-factor direction: Android Authority said industry analysts expect the 4:3 aspect ratio to become the new industry standard for foldables [4].
  • Retail pricing: A deleted Portuguese retailer listing showed the Galaxy Z Fold 8 at €2,069 for 256GB, €2,269 for 512GB and €2,669 for 1TB, each €70 above an earlier European leak [10].

Historical Background

Samsung introduced the original Galaxy Fold in 2019 after delaying the launch to address early review-unit display failures [common].

Business Angle

  • ETNews, cited by 9to5Google and Android Authority, said Samsung set initial production at 2.8 million units for the wide Galaxy Z Fold 8, 2 million for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and 1.5 million for the Galaxy Z Flip 8; 9to5Google said total foldable production of about 5.5 million to 6 million units would remain near last year’s level [6][4].
  • A deleted European listing put the Galaxy Z Flip 8 at €1,349 for 256GB and €1,549 for 512GB, while SAG said global foldable smartphone shipments totaled 3.4 million units in Q2 2026 [10][9].
  • SAG put Huawei’s foldable share at 47.7% in Q2 2026 and Samsung’s at 15.2%, while Samsung’s foldable shipments rose 24.9% year over year [9].

Action Items

  • Watch Galaxy Unpacked: Stream the event through Samsung.com, Samsung Newsroom or Samsung’s official YouTube channel at 2 p.m. BST on July 22 [3][15].
  • Confirm prices before buying: Wait for Samsung’s July 22 announcement before relying on leaked European retailer prices or unverified specifications [10][11].
