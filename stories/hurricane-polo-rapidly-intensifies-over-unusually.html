Hurricane Polo rapidly intensifies over unusually warm Pacific waters

Category: Science

Polo fluctuated between Category 4 and Category 5 while tracking offshore, with heavy rain, flooding and landslides forecast along southwestern Mexico [14][7]. Hurricane Nolo strengthened as it approached Hawaii’s Big Island, where forecasters warned of dangerous rainfall and flooding. The Associated Press reported that the Pacific hurricane season was very active and that El Niño conditions that emerged in June had warmed ocean waters above normal [4][7][19].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Hawaii rainfall: Forecasters projected 15 to 30 inches of rain on the Big Island through Saturday, with higher totals possible; Maui also faced life-threatening flood risk [7].
  • Mexico impacts: In Lázaro Cárdenas, seawater entered homes and left furniture and planks strewn about, while residents reinforced businesses and evacuated some homes [7].

Perspectives

Brian McNoldy, University of Miami hurricane expert: He said El Niño was ramping up to perhaps become the strongest on record [19].
