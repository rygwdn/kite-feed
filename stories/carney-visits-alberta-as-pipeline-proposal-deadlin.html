Carney visits Alberta as pipeline proposal deadline arrives

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney said he would travel to Alberta on the evening of July 1 for Canada Day, one day before an expected provincial announcement on Alberta’s submission to Ottawa’s new major projects office for a proposed oil pipeline [1][6][2][7]. Speaking in Kuujjuaq, Que., Carney said he had been in close contact with Smith and that, from the province’s perspective, “things are moving well.” He said he would wait for Alberta’s official announcement before commenting further [1][6]. Alberta faced a July 1 deadline to submit a proposal to the federal office, which Ottawa created to speed approval of major projects, but Carney said Ottawa would still take several months to decide whether to refer the province’s proposal to that office [1][6]. The visit also takes place before an autumn vote in which Albertans will decide whether the province should hold another referendum on leaving Canada [1][6][2]. Ahead of the trip, Carney released a 17-minute YouTube video on Ottawa-Alberta tensions, from Pierre Elliott Trudeau’s National Energy Program to more recent federal energy policies under Justin Trudeau. He said Ottawa had made Albertans feel their resources were not their own and, later, that their energy contributions were “running against the tides of history” [3]. He said he wanted to “help put those divisions in the past” and would take part in Canada Day events in Ottawa before flying to Edmonton [3].
