CES 2026 sets AI-heavy agenda as show opens

Category: Technology

CES 2026 gets underway in Las Vegas this week, with show-floor dates running January 6–9 and a wave of press events beginning today and continuing into Monday [2]. Preview coverage across outlets says “AI” will be the dominant theme, shaping everything from TVs and chips to cars, robots, and smart-home gear [2][4][11][8]. Alongside the expected big-tech keynotes, LEGO is also stepping into the CES spotlight with its first-ever press conference on Monday—an unusual (and very on-brand) crossover of play and technology [9][5].

Did you know: LEGO is holding its first-ever CES press conference in Las Vegas on Monday, Jan. 5 [9].

Key Points

  • Samsung kickoff: Samsung is scheduled to open CES programming on Sunday with “The First Look,” hosted by TM Roh, focused on its 2026 “vision” and new “AI-driven customer experiences” for the DX (Device eXperience) division [2].
  • Bosch livestream: Bosch says it will emphasize “mobility, smart home integrations and manufacturing,” including an “AI-powered cockpit” with Microsoft Teams and NVIDIA-related software integrations; its CES presentation is slated to stream Monday at 12PM ET via Bosch’s press page and YouTube [3].
  • TV tech shift: One hardware trend to watch is RGB TV backlighting: Mashable reports Samsung, LG, and Hisense plan to showcase RGB TVs designed for improved color accuracy, higher brightness, and better contrast compared with more typical backlight approaches [10].
  • LEGO enters CES: LEGO’s first CES press conference is set for 1 p.m. Monday, Jan. 5; Mashable notes the company has several “tech-y” tie-ins, including a new LEGO Batman video game and an F1 collaboration for a LEGO car [9].
  • Live roundup coverage: Several outlets are planning rolling, on-the-ground coverage and “live updates” spanning categories like TVs, smart glasses, phones, and other early announcements as companies pitch their 2026 direction [6][7].

Perspectives

Consumer Technology Association (CTA): The organization running CES provides a published schedule and exhibitor directory that frame the event’s official press conferences, panels, and presentations [2].

Timeline

  • Sunday, January 4: Samsung scheduled to kick off CES with “The First Look” [2].
  • Monday, January 5: Bosch set to livestream CES press conference at 12PM ET [3].
  • Monday, January 5: LEGO plans first-ever CES press conference at 1 p.m. [9].
  • January 6 through 9: CES 2026 show floor officially open at Las Vegas venues [2].

Technical Details

  • RGB TV backlighting: RGB TVs use many small red, green, and blue lights to create the picture, rather than relying on more typical white/blue backlighting; preview coverage says this can improve color accuracy, brightness, and contrast [10].
  • AI in the car cockpit: Bosch describes “AI in the car” as cockpit software that can tie functions together—such as using voice commands to join a Microsoft Teams call while the system activates adaptive cruise control [3].
  • Licensing-focused exhibitors: Bosch positions much of its CES work as underlying technology meant to be licensed to other companies, not only sold as Bosch-branded products [3].

Industry Impact

  • Automotive software ecosystems: Bosch’s plan to integrate cockpit experiences with Microsoft and NVIDIA highlights how in-car UX is increasingly shaped by big software platforms and AI tooling partnerships [3].
  • Display manufacturing priorities: The push toward RGB TV designs indicates continued investment in display backlight and color technologies as brands compete on perceived picture quality [10].
  • Consumer tech storytelling: LEGO’s first CES press conference signals CES’s widening tent—where entertainment, games, and playful physical products can share stage time with chips and cars [9][5].

Historical Background

CES is an annual Consumer Technology Association trade show that companies use to preview product and platform directions for the year ahead [common]. Recent CES editions have increasingly centered on AI features and integrations across devices, which preview coverage expects to continue into 2026 [2][11].

Q&A

Q: Where will Bosch’s booth be located on the CES show floor?

A: Engadget reports Bosch will be in Central Hall at the Las Vegas Convention Center, booth 16203 [3].

Q: What venue footprint will CES use around Las Vegas this year?

A: Engadget says demos and events will run at the Las Vegas Convention Center and other hotels across the city [2].

Q: What should viewers do if LEGO doesn’t publish an official livestream?

A: Mashable says it will update with a link if one appears, and otherwise will report the announcements from the press conference as best it can [9].

  • Track CES coverage by product category: Use live update hubs from outlets covering the show floor (for example, TVs vs. smart glasses vs. phones) to focus on the gear you care about [7][6].
