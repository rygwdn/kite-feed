Microsoft exits Nscale's delayed UK data centre deal before IPO

Category: Technology

Microsoft has withdrawn from a deal to anchor Nscale’s data centre in Loughton, Essex, where a power connection is not expected until the 2030s, The Telegraph reported [1][8]. Nscale, a cloud provider focused on AI computing, is preparing for a possible US initial public offering this month to raise as much as $3 billion [1][2]. The site had been expected to receive power by the end of this year, but grid delays have pushed the connection back by years [3]. Nscale said it is exploring other power sources and UK locations, and that its other Microsoft contracts remain unaffected [1]. Microsoft declined to comment to The Telegraph, according to TNW [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Our relationship with Microsoft remains strong and long-term, and this unfortunate London-wide grid issue has no impact on our other contracts

— Nscale spokesperson (The Telegraph)

Key Points

  • Customer concentration: Microsoft and Anthropic account for about 85% of Nscale’s contracts [1].
  • Norway delays: Nscale’s flagship Norwegian data centre is six months behind schedule, with 6.4 megawatts of a planned 30 megawatts operating [1].
  • Valuation pushback: Some investors have urged Nscale to target a valuation of $16 billion to $19 billion rather than roughly $30 billion, Bloomberg reported [5].
  • UK grid backlog: More than a quarter of planned data-centre capacity slipped in 2025, while some UK projects face waits approaching a decade for power connections [3].

Timeline

  • July 2026: TNW reported that grid delays threatened the site’s planned connection [1]
  • August 2026: Anthropic agreed to spend $45 billion on Nscale computing [1]
  • 18 October 2026: The Telegraph reported Microsoft’s exit from the Essex project [1][8]

Industry Impact

  • Neocloud providers: Gartner predicts that cost pressures and a lack of differentiated business models could force more than 80% of providers to cease independent operations by 2030 [3].

Business Angle

  • Microsoft had agreed to rent 23,000 Nvidia AI chips at the Essex facility [3].
  • Heavily indebted AI cloud providers face concerns about their ability to repay debt. Shares in Nscale peer CoreWeave fell 25% last week [1].

Q&A

Q: Who else was signed up for the Loughton site?

A: Before withdrawing, Microsoft was the site's only announced customer, with an agreement to rent 23,000 Nvidia AI chips there [3].

Q: What other Microsoft project has Nscale disclosed?

A: Microsoft has also signed a letter of intent for a campus in West Virginia [1].
