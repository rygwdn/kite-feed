NASA-backed mission to land on Phobos and return samples to Earth

Category: Science

A NASA-backed mission to Mars’ moon Phobos is scheduled to launch on Oct. 19, aiming to land on the moon, study it and return samples to Earth [1][2]. The mission aims to investigate Mars’ moons [1]. Built in Germany and France, the Idefix rover is set to explore Phobos [3]. The planned sample return would bring material from the Mars system to Earth for the first time [3].
