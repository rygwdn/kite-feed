Alberta premier rejects alcohol ban over Trump's tariff threat

Category: Canada

Alberta Premier Danielle Smith said Saturday she does not plan to respond to President Trump's threat of a 50% tariff on Canadian goods by banning sales of U.S. alcohol in Alberta [2][1][6]. Smith made the comments on her provincewide call-in radio show, saying an alcohol ban would be an extraordinary measure and that retailers and consumers should decide what to stock and buy [2][1]. Smith said Alberta and Canada should stay calm and try to win support among Americans rather than remove U.S. products from shelves [2][5]. The Canadian Press reported that several provincial liquor boards stopped buying American alcohol last year after earlier tariff threats and annexation comments from Trump; Alberta and Saskatchewan later returned U.S. alcohol to shelves, while Ontario and Quebec have not resumed stocking it [2][6].
