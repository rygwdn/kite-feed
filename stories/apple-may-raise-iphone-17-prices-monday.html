Apple may raise iPhone 17 prices Monday

Category: Technology

Apple could raise prices for the iPhone 17 lineup as soon as Monday, August 10, according to a Weibo post by leaker Fixed Focus Digital. Apple has not confirmed a change, and the post did not say which models, storage tiers or markets would be affected [2][1][4][6][7][9][10][11][12][13][14]. The possible increase would come before Apple’s expected September launch of the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and first foldable iPhone, while the standard iPhone 18 and iPhone 18e are expected in spring 2027 [4][9][11][12][14]. The reports cite a sharp rise in DRAM and NAND memory costs, which Apple used to justify June price increases for Macs, iPads and other products while leaving iPhone prices unchanged [2][4][6][9][10][11][13][14]. Fixed Focus Digital also claimed Apple had scrapped plans to expand production capacity on some lines from 15% to 30%, but the post did not identify the affected products or components [4][10][14].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: In Taiwan, Apple's June price increases raised the iPad 11 from NT$11,900 to NT$14,900, a roughly 25% jump [7].

Key Points

  • Pro rumors: 9to5Mac said rumored iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max starting prices are $200 to $300 above current Pro models, at $1,299-$1,399 and $1,399-$1,499, respectively [3].
  • Taiwan math: NextApple calculated that a 20% Taiwan increase would lift the iPhone 17 Pro Max from NT$44,900 to about NT$53,880, but described the figure as a calculation rather than official pricing [7].
  • Japan precedent: Les Numériques and UNIAN said iPhone prices in Japan had risen by roughly 8% to 11%, while other markets had not yet seen the same adjustment [13][14].

Perspectives

9to5Mac: Chance Miller advised buyers considering a Pro model to buy an iPhone 17 Pro now, saying rumored iPhone 18 Pro changes may not justify an extra $200 to $300 for many upgraders.

Sources: 9to5Mac

Digital Trends: Digital Trends said the key unknown for current buyers is whether any increase would hit every iPhone 17 model or only selected versions.

Industry Impact

  • Consumer electronics: Cult of Mac and Redmond Pie said higher RAM and storage costs have pushed up prices across multiple device categories and manufacturers [2][6].
  • Gaming devices: Les Numériques said the memory-chip crisis has also affected gaming hardware prices, disrupting the usual pattern of lower prices as devices age [13].
  • Retail channel: Macerkopf noted that Amazon prices for some iPhone 17 models in Germany were below Apple’s official prices, making third-party discounts relevant if Apple changes list prices [9].

Business Angle

  • Any revenue effect from higher list prices would depend on whether buyers accept the increases, trade down or delay upgrades [common].
  • A $200-$300 Pro-model increase would lift revenue per device only if customers do not trade down to lower-priced models or delay upgrades [3][common].
  • Memory shortages can create a margin squeeze when component costs rise before retail prices adjust [2][common].
  • Keeping the iPhone 17 in the lineup into 2027 could let Apple reprice older models instead of relying only on the iPhone 18 Pro launch [4][11].

Action Items

  • Compare current iPhone 17 prices before Monday: Check Apple and major third-party retailers now because the rumored August 10 change has not been confirmed and affected models remain unknown [1][12].
  • Avoid relying on resale availability: If you want an iPhone 17 Pro specifically, note that 9to5Mac expects Apple to discontinue it when the iPhone 18 Pro is announced next month [3].
