Denny’s restores Nvidia founder plaque as Huang returns to CEO role

Category: Business

Denny’s publicly welcomed Jensen Huang back as Nvidia’s chief executive and said it had restored a plaque marking the San Jose diner booth where Huang and co-founders Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem discussed starting the chipmaker [2][3]. The restaurant chain said it learned the plaque had been removed on Nov. 13, one day after Huang resigned, and put it back shortly afterward. It has not said who removed it or why [1][8]. Nvidia’s board reinstated Huang on Nov. 23, 11 days after accepting his resignation following reports of an affair with a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a customer suspected of links to Chinese intelligence. Huang acknowledged infidelity; Nvidia’s independent board review is continuing [14][16][4]. The board said it considered input from senior U.S. government officials, while President Donald Trump has said he helped secure Huang’s return [14][11]. Denny’s posted its renewed tribute on Nov. 25. The endorsement drew online mockery and criticism. Huang’s reinstatement also renewed questions about Nvidia’s succession planning, board independence and oversight of export-control compliance [3][14][16].

Key Points

  • Online response: Business Insider reported that Denny’s post had drawn more than 3.9 million views on X, with commenters divided between jokes and criticism of the chain’s support for Huang [3].
  • Board scrutiny: Four Nvidia directors joined the board in 1993 or 1997, and governance experts questioned whether long-serving directors can remain independent [14].
  • Compliance changes: Nvidia’s board said it would appoint a chief compliance officer and created a committee to oversee national security and export controls [16].
  • Credit risk: S&P Global Ratings warned it could cut Nvidia’s AA issuer rating by one notch if the company does not address governance deficiencies [16].
  • Leadership structure: Huang defended a flat structure in which dozens of senior executives report directly to him [16].

Perspectives

Denny’s: The chain says Huang’s personal history with it began when he worked there as a teenager, and that Nvidia’s origin story is part of Denny’s history worth preserving [1][2].

Denny’s installed the plaque in 2023, describing the booth as the birthplace of a $1 trillion company; Nvidia was founded in 1993 [2][13].
