Trump rules out Iran strikes before midterms as Pentagon prepares options

Category: World

President Donald Trump said Oct. 8 that the United States would not attack Iran before the Nov. 3 midterm elections, citing what he called “productive discussions” with Tehran. He said the U.S. naval blockade of Iran would remain in force and reiterated that Iran would not be allowed to obtain a nuclear weapon [2][16]. The pledge does not rule out action after the election [53]. The Pentagon was developing options for renewed military action. The New York Times reported that planners had prepared a roughly three-day campaign targeting Iran’s missile and drone arsenal, energy facilities and military sites, while three U.S. aircraft carriers were expected in the region. Trump had rejected five earlier proposals, and officials said he had not made a final decision to strike [59][58]. The war began with U.S. and Israeli strikes on Feb. 28. A June ceasefire arrangement collapsed within weeks as fighting and disputes over the Strait of Hormuz resumed [9]. Iran is reviewing Washington’s response to its proposal to reopen the strait within seven days. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Tehran expected to respond within days [16][13].
