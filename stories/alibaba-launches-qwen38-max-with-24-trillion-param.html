Alibaba launches Qwen3.8-Max with 2.4 trillion parameters

Category: AI

Alibaba unveiled Qwen3.8-Max, its latest flagship large language model in the Qwen series, on Monday, saying it is designed for coding, professional work, research and multimodal tasks [6][2][5]. The model has 2.4 trillion total parameters, with TestingCatalog and The Currency Analytics describing 95 billion as active at a given time, and Alibaba said it supports a context window of up to about 1 million tokens [2][5][6][9][8]. Qwen3.8-Max is available through QwenCloud or Alibaba Cloud’s API, and TechNode said Alibaba has also integrated it into its Qwen Office agent product [2][5][7]. TestingCatalog, TechGenyz and TechNode said Alibaba planned to release open weights for Qwen3.8-Max the following week, while The Currency Analytics reported that the model was already available on Hugging Face and ModelScope [2][5][7][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Next week, the open weights of Qwen3.8-Max will be released, and Qwen3.8-27B is also going open-weights to meet you all!

— Qwen (TestingCatalog)

Key Points

  • Agent tests: In a company-run coding test, Qwen3.8-Max worked for about 16 days on the open-source oh-my-cli project, producing 265 commits, 127 pull requests and 151 issues [2].
  • Arena ranking: TechNode said Qwen ranked second only to Anthropic’s Claude series in recently released third-party Arena rankings, while iChongqing cited Arena in saying Qwen models rank among top-tier large language models [7][6].
  • API pricing: Latent Space listed Qwen API pricing at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens [4].

Perspectives

Alibaba: Alibaba said Qwen3.8-Max can operate independently over extended periods and described the model as capable of long-horizon coding execution without human intervention.

Sources: iChongqing

Latent Space: Latent Space said the launch dispelled doubts that arose after the 'Qwen Exodus' and a shift toward closed APIs, and said Qwen3.8-Max would have been the top open model except for the recent Kimi K3 release.

Sources: Latent Space

The Currency Analytics: The Currency Analytics said Alibaba was betting that broad availability could matter more than leading every benchmark, comparing the strategy to Google’s open-source Android push.

Sources: The Currency Analytics

Timeline

  • July 2026: Moonshot AI released the 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 model [6]
  • 3 August 2026: The Qwen account announced Qwen3.8-Max and planned open weights [2][4][8]
  • 4 August 2026: Alibaba unveiled Qwen3.8-Max in reports from China [6][7]

Historical Background

Alibaba’s Qwen line has been one of China’s prominent open-model families, and Latent Space said there had been doubt about whether the lab would keep releasing relevant open models after a management change and more closed API launches [4]. In July 2026, Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, which it said had 2.8 trillion parameters and was the largest-parameter open-source AI model globally [6].

Business Angle

  • Alibaba may use open weights to seed developer adoption while monetizing hosted access through Alibaba Cloud and Qwen Office integrations [7][common].
  • The reported $2 input and $6 output price per million tokens creates competitive pressure if rivals charge more for comparable coding or agentic workloads [4][common].
  • A model of this scale can carry high training and inference costs, so utilization and cloud attach rates matter more than headline parameter count [5][common].
