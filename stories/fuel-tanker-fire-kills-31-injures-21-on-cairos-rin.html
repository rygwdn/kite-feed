Fuel tanker fire kills 31, injures 21 on Cairo’s Ring Road

Category: World

A fuel tanker overturned and caught fire on Cairo’s Ring Road on Wednesday, killing at least 31 people and injuring 21 in the Al-Warraq district of Giza, Egyptian authorities said. The blaze spread to nearby traffic on the busy route around Greater Cairo, burning vehicles and prompting a large emergency response [4][20][7]. The Health Ministry said 39 ambulances responded and the injured were taken to nearby hospitals. Authorities later revised the injury count to 21. Prosecutors opened an investigation, ordered DNA testing to identify some victims and said casualty and damage assessments were continuing [4][20]. Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly ordered an inquiry into the crash. A government spokesperson said officials would wait for its findings before deciding whether to take further action on the transport of petroleum products [7][18].

Key Points

  • Vehicle damage: Prosecutors’ preliminary estimate counted 27 burned vehicles, including cars and motorcycles [20][17].
  • Road safety: Egypt recorded 5,829 road deaths in 2025, a 10.8% increase from 5,260 in 2024, according to state statistics [20][34].
  • Transit disruption: The fire disrupted traffic and Bus Rapid Transit services on the Ring Road; the operator said it activated emergency measures to manage service [32].
  • Rescue support: The Egyptian Red Crescent sent emergency teams to the scene and hospitals to assist civil defense and ambulance crews [25].

Perspectives

Egypt’s Financial Regulatory Authority: The regulator ordered the compulsory motor-insurance fund to speed up claims processing and said representatives would contact affected families to complete paperwork [25].

Humanitarian Impact

The Health Ministry initially said 15 injured people were taken to hospital, including four in critical condition. It later raised its emergency alert level, and Health Minister Khaled Abdel Ghaffar visited the scene and nearby hospitals [7].
