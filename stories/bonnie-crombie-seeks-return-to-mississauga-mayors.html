Bonnie Crombie seeks return to Mississauga mayor's office

Category: Canada

Former Ontario Liberal leader Bonnie Crombie plans to register on Tuesday to run in the upcoming Mississauga mayoral election, seeking to regain the job she left in 2024 [1][2][3]. Crombie told supporters and Mississauga councillors on Sunday that she would register as a candidate; Global News reported the registration was expected at Mississauga’s civic centre on Tuesday morning [1][3]. Crombie served as Mississauga mayor for a decade before resigning midway through her third term to lead the Ontario Liberals [2][3]. She is expected to face incumbent Mayor Carolyn Parrish and other candidates, including city councillors Alvin Tedjo and Dipika Damerla [2]. Crombie stepped down as Ontario Liberal leader early this year after a leadership review at the party’s 2025 annual general meeting and after failing to win a seat in the legislature in the 2025 provincial election [2].
