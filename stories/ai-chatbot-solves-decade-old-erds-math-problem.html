AI chatbot solves decade-old Erdős math problem

Category: AI

An AI system solved Erdős problem 1196 on March 13, 2026, according to Folha’s syndicated BBC report, which described the mathematics problem as previously unsolved [3]. A New Scientist headline said a free AI chatbot produced the solution in 13 minutes; a Mastodon post called it a major AI mathematics breakthrough [2][1]. Jared Duker Lichtman, a Stanford University mathematician who had worked on the problem for seven years, received an unexpected email with the proposed answer [3]. Lichtman said the raw AI output initially looked preliminary and disorganized, but after about an hour reading and checking it, he concluded that it was correct and that the idea was strong [3].
