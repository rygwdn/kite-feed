Amazon adds cameras, sleep sensing and AI to five Alexa devices

Category: Technology

Amazon unveiled five Alexa-powered home devices on Oct. 28: an upgraded Echo Dot, a camera-equipped Echo Studio, the bedside Echo Time, the Alexa Photo Frame and a redesigned 16-inch Echo Show 16 [49][60]. The lineup uses Amazon's Alexa+ assistant as Apple and other companies develop home devices [49][10]. The new Echo Dot costs $99.99 and adds smart-home hub functions and an eero Wi-Fi extender. The Echo Studio with Home Camera costs $249.99 and can show a live view of a room or answer questions about objects in view [74][6]. Echo Time, priced at $199.99, has no camera and can use optional sleep sensing to adjust wake-up timing [81][39]. The Photo Frame starts at $199.99, while Echo Show 16 starts at $499.99, or $569.99 with an anti-glare screen [2][11]. Most devices are available now. Echo Show 16 is available for preorder and is scheduled to ship Dec. 16. Amazon says it will begin rolling out a preview of Alexa Home Map, a 3D interface for locating and controlling connected devices, later this year [32][24].

Key Points

  • Dot connectivity: The Echo Dot supports Zigbee, Matter and Thread, and Amazon says its built-in eero extender can add up to 1,000 square feet of coverage to an existing eero network [74][28].
  • Sleep sensing: Echo Time offers hundreds of customizable clock faces; its display dims and reduces blue light at night, then brightens gradually in the morning [37][29].
  • Privacy controls: Amazon says the Echo Studio has a dedicated control to disable its camera and microphones, plus an indicator light when images or video are streamed to the cloud [6][22].
  • Alexa subscription: Some Echo Show 16 features require Alexa+, which Amazon includes with Prime; customers without an eligible membership may pay $20 a month for Alexa+ [26].
  • Price shift: The new Echo Dot starts at $99.99, double the $50 entry price cited for an earlier model by WIRED [7].

Technical Details

  • On-device processing: Amazon's AZ3 silicon includes an AI accelerator for local processing on the Echo Dot; the company says it improves conversational detection and filters background noise [74].
  • Room sensing: Echo Time's optional sleep-sensing system combines ultrasound-based breathing detection with temperature and presence sensing to estimate sleep timing without a wearable [37][39].
  • 3D home map: Alexa Home Map can generate a 3D home model during setup using an address and room scans made with a compatible iPhone, then place connected devices on the map for direct control [24].

Industry Impact

  • Smart-home platforms: The Echo Dot supports the open smart-home standards Zigbee, Matter and Thread, allowing Alexa to control compatible devices from different brands [74].
  • Connected-home screens: Amazon's 16-inch Echo Show combines Fire TV and household controls, while Apple's HomeView has a smaller, 7-inch screen [27][84].

Historical Background

Amazon devices chief Panos Panay said the company had sold more than 100 million Echo Dots, and WIRED reported that customers spent more than a trillion minutes listening to music on the Dot in 2026 [7]. Amazon says the Echo Show line grew more than 25% year over year, a figure it cited in explaining the importance of screens to Alexa [54].
