Argentina and Egypt set World Cup last-16 clash

Category: World

Defending champion Argentina reached the World Cup last 16 with a 3-2 extra-time win over Cape Verde in Miami Gardens on Friday. The winner came in the 111th minute, when Cristian Romero’s header from a Lionel Messi corner deflected off Diney Borges for an own goal [8][7][5][23][25]. Messi opened the scoring in the 29th minute, Deroy Duarte equalised after the break, Lisandro Martínez restored Argentina’s lead in extra time and Sidny Lopes Cabral levelled again before the decisive own goal [8][7][23][32]. Cape Verde, playing in its first World Cup, exited after twice coming from behind against the reigning champions [8][5][19][46]. Egypt advanced earlier by beating Australia 4-2 on penalties after a 1-1 extra-time draw in Texas, giving the Pharaohs their first World Cup knockout-stage win [1][9][11][52][53]. Emam Ashour headed Egypt in front in the 13th minute before a Mohamed Hany own goal brought Australia level, and Hossam Abdelmaguid converted the decisive penalty after Australia missed twice in the shootout [9][28][36][53]. Argentina will face Egypt in the Round of 16 in Atlanta on Tuesday [8][15][19][42].

Key Points

  • Messi milestone: Messi’s first-half goal was his seventh of the tournament and raised his career World Cup total to 20 [15][19][23][46].
  • Cape Verde run: Cape Verde reached the knockout stage after group-stage draws with Spain, Uruguay and Saudi Arabia, becoming the smallest country to get that far [4][5][19][46].
  • Goalkeeper impact: Vozinha, Cape Verde’s 40-year-old goalkeeper, made 10 saves against Argentina, including five against Messi [19][46].
  • Shootout gamble: Australia coach Tony Popovic sent on Mathew Ryan for the penalty shootout, but Harry Souttar missed Australia’s first attempt and 18-year-old Lucas Herrington hit the bar [1][9][28][10].

Perspectives

Mohamed Salah: Salah said he told Egypt’s players before the Australia match to enjoy the World Cup stage and not let the pressure affect them.
