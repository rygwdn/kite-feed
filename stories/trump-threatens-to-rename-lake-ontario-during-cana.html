Trump threatens to rename Lake Ontario during Canada trade fight

Category: Canada

President Trump said Tuesday that the United States was giving “serious consideration” to changing Lake Ontario’s name to “Lake America,” linking the threat to a trade fight with Canada and saying the U.S. did not expect to keep doing much business with Ontario [8][3][10]. Lake Ontario lies between Ontario and New York and forms part of the Canada-U.S. border [8][10][11]. After negotiators failed to resolve the standoff, Washington began imposing 50% tariffs on hundreds of Canadian goods, and Trump announced a 50% tariff on Canadian automotive and steel imports beginning in January, CBS News reported [8]. Canada has said it will impose retaliatory tariffs of 15% to 50% on about 700 U.S. products worth $20 billion, including lobster, plastics and cheese tied to politically important U.S. states [21].

The United States is giving serious consideration to changing the name of Lake Ontario to Lake America

Key Points

  • Naming authority: A U.S. president can direct U.S. federal bodies such as the Coast Guard and the U.S. Board on Geographic Names, but Canada and international cartographic bodies could keep using Lake Ontario [6][11][19].
  • Indigenous origin: First Nations representatives objected to the “Lake America” proposal because Indigenous peoples used the name Ontario centuries before Europeans adopted it [5].
  • Previous renaming order: Trump signed Executive Order 14172 on Jan. 20, 2025, directing U.S. federal use of “Gulf of America” for the Gulf of Mexico, and Google Maps now displays “Gulf of Mexico (Gulf of America)” [6].
  • Map post: Trump also posted a map showing “Lake Ontario” crossed out and replaced by “Lake America” in gold lettering with a U.S. flag [10][12].

Technical Details

  • Domestic Names Committee: The U.S. Board on Geographic Names’ Domestic Names Committee, housed in the Interior Department, handles U.S. federal geographic-name standardization and would be a key administrative channel for any American renaming order [6].

Historical Background

Most Great Lakes names are anglicized Indigenous names, including Ontario, Huron, Michigan and Erie, while Superior is an anglicized French name [6]. The name “America” itself derives from Amerigo Vespucci, the 16th-century Italian explorer [6]. Seoul Shinmun also reported that Trump has floated renaming the Strait of Hormuz as the “Trump Strait” or “America Strait” during tensions with Iran [12].

Business Angle

  • Auto and steel companies face tariff risk because Trump announced a 50% tariff on Canadian imports in those categories beginning in January [8].
  • Canadian retaliation aimed at lobster, plastics and cheese could pressure U.S. producers in Maine, Ohio and Wisconsin if buyers switch suppliers or delay orders [21].
  • Companies that can change suppliers or raise prices are less exposed than firms locked into single-border supply chains [common].
