GM Canada workers ratify three-year Unifor contracts

Category: Canada

Unionized General Motors workers in Ontario ratified new three-year collective agreements after Unifor and GM reached tentative labour contracts on Aug. 22 covering more than 4,600 autoworkers [1][2][3]. Unifor said members in Oshawa, St. Catharines and Woodstock voted 80.5% in favour, while members in Ingersoll backed the agreements by 96.5% [2]. The union said the agreements raise wages for full-rate production members to $50.20 an hour and skilled trades workers to $62.71 an hour. Unifor National President Lana Payne said they commit more than $1 billion in investments to Canadian GM facilities [2]. GM Canada president and managing director Jack Uppal said ratification supports employees, strengthens manufacturing operations and provides a foundation for GM’s future in Canada [2].
