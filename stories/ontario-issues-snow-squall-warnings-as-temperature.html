Ontario issues snow squall warnings as temperatures plunge

Category: Canada

A burst of winter weather is moving across Ontario on Thursday, bringing much colder temperatures and accumulating snow, with warnings posted for wide areas of the province [1][4]. Toronto is expected to pick up a fresh overnight snowfall that could be the city’s largest of the season so far, alongside gusty winds and bitter cold [2][3]. Forecasters say the timing could make for a messy morning commute, especially where snow squalls reduce visibility and quickly cover roads [1][3].

  • Blowing snow: Strong winds can lift and drift fresh snow, reducing visibility and creating uneven, hazardous patches for drivers and pedestrians [2].
