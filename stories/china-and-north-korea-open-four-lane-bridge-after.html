China and North Korea open four-lane bridge after 12-year delay

Category: World

China and North Korea opened the 3-kilometer New Yalu River Bridge on Thursday, creating a four-lane road link between Dandong and Sinuiju after a 12-year delay [11][8]. Trucks crossed in both directions after the opening ceremony, which Chinese Premier Li Qiang and North Korean Premier Pak Thae Song attended by video [28][15]. The bridge was completed in 2014 but remained unused because North Korea’s connecting roads and customs facilities were unfinished. Pandemic-era border restrictions further disrupted exchanges [2][11]. The nearby ageing Sino-Korean Friendship Bridge has carried more than 70% of official bilateral trade, but its road traffic can move only one way at a time [9][7]. Beijing says the new crossing will expand trade and people-to-people exchanges. Bilateral trade reached about $2.7 billion in 2025, but analysts say the bridge alone is unlikely to restore trade to pre-2017 levels while United Nations sanctions remain in place [11][23][20]. South Korea’s Unification Ministry called the opening the first tangible implementation of agreements reached at the leaders’ June summit and said it would monitor further developments [28].

Key Points

  • Trade imbalance: China’s exports to North Korea reached $2.3 billion in 2025, up 25% to a six-year high but still below levels before sanctions tightened in 2017 [13][20].
  • Russia factor: The opening followed the launch of North Korea’s first road bridge with Russia about a month earlier, as Pyongyang has expanded military cooperation with Moscow [7][8].
  • Expert assessment: Kyungnam University’s Lim Eul-chul said the crossing could ease shipments of heavy machinery, raw materials and energy supplies, while warning against expecting an immediate return to pre-sanctions trade [2][20].
  • Border links: South Korea counted at least 17 active road and rail connections along the roughly 1,400-kilometre China–North Korea border [9].

Perspectives

Chinese government: Premier Li Qiang called the bridge a new landmark symbolizing the traditional friendship between the two countries [7].

Timeline

  • 2009: China and North Korea agreed to build a new road bridge [23].
  • 2010: Construction began downstream from the existing Friendship Bridge [23][28].
  • October 2014: Construction finished, but the bridge remained unused because North Korea’s connecting roads were unfinished [28][11].
  • 2017: United Nations sanctions tightened, leaving trade below its earlier levels [13][23].
  • March 2026: Passenger trains between China and North Korea resumed after six years [11][8].
  • June 2026: Xi and Kim pledged to elevate ties during their Pyongyang summit [28][8].
  • 15 October 2026: The two countries officially opened the bridge, and trucks crossed in both directions [15][28].

Technical Details

  • Deck and lanes: The bridge has a 28.5-metre-wide deck designed for four lanes of two-way traffic [28].
  • Navigation clearance: The channel beneath the bridge is designed to accommodate vessels of up to 3,000 tonnes [28][25].
  • Old crossing constraints: The Friendship Bridge combines rail and road traffic and could accommodate trucks weighing no more than 20 tonnes [28].

Industry Impact

  • Dandong commerce: Residents and local businesses in Dandong expect the crossing to attract more commercial activity and visitors after years of delay [8][20].
  • Cargo forecast: Lim Eul-chul estimated the new crossing could triple or quadruple cargo traffic, particularly shipments of heavy machinery and construction materials [6].

Historical Background

The older Sino-Korean Friendship Bridge was completed in 1943 during Japan’s occupation of the region [11][28].

Q&A

Q: What air service between China and North Korea resumed in 2026?

A: Air China resumed direct flights between Beijing and Pyongyang in March after a six-year hiatus [11].

Q: Why do analysts expect trade to grow gradually rather than surge?

A: North Korea is wary that a sudden flood of Chinese imports could expand Beijing’s influence over its domestic market, and sanctions remain in effect [11].
