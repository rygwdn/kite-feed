US and Gulf states seek Ukraine anti-drone help

Category: World

Ukraine says the United States and several Middle Eastern partners have asked Kyiv to share expertise — and possibly equipment — to help counter Iranian-designed Shahed drones used in regional attacks [1][2][6][7][8][9][10]. President Volodymyr Zelensky said he has told Ukraine’s specialists to support those efforts, pointing to Kyiv’s experience building relatively cheap drone interceptors and other counter-drone methods developed during Russia’s invasion [1][2][10]. Zelensky also set boundaries: any help should not weaken Ukraine’s own air defenses, and Kyiv wants cooperation to strengthen its diplomatic push to end the war with Russia [10][3][6][9]. Several reports add that Ukraine worries rising needs in the Middle East could pull scarce U.S. air-defense systems and munitions away from the supplies Ukraine depends on — another complication as Russia-Ukraine war talks face delays [4][2][5]. More broadly, the moment underscores growing interest in cheaper, scalable ways to blunt drone threats, drawing on Ukraine’s hard-won experience under sustained drone attacks [2][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Regional outreach: Zelensky said he discussed possible cooperation with leaders of the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, and Kuwait as countries look for better defenses against Shahed drone attacks [9].
  • Cost pressure: Ukraine’s interceptor drones can cost about $1,000 — far less than relying mainly on high-end air-defense missiles, which several outlets describe as expensive and limited in supply [2][3][5].
  • Possible procurement: Semafor reported that the Pentagon is exploring buying Ukraine’s inexpensive drone interceptors as part of efforts to counter Iranian strikes [5].
  • Energy protection: Semafor said Gulf states could also draw on Ukraine’s experience protecting energy infrastructure after repeated long-range attacks during the Russia-Ukraine war [5].
  • Air-defense tradeoffs: The New York Times reported that Kyiv fears the U.S. could divert air-defense systems Ukraine needs to counter Russia toward the Iran-related conflict in the Middle East [4].

Perspectives

Ukraine (Volodymyr Zelensky): Zelensky said he ordered Ukrainian experts to help and framed the cooperation as reciprocal support. He also insisted it must not undercut Ukraine’s own air defenses and should strengthen Kyiv’s diplomacy to end Russia’s war [10][6][9].

Sources: BBC News

United States (reported): Semafor reported that the Pentagon is exploring purchases of Ukrainian-made, low-cost drone interceptors as it looks for more ways to defend against Iranian drone attacks [5].

Sources: Semafor

Ukraine (peace-talk context): PBS reported that Russia-Ukraine talks have been delayed even as other countries seek Kyiv’s drone expertise, highlighting how Ukraine’s defense innovation is advancing amid an unresolved war [2].

Sources: PBS NewsHour

Ukraine (resource concern): The New York Times reported that Ukrainian officials worry the U.S. could redirect air-defense systems away from Ukraine because of the Middle East conflict [4].

Sources: The New York Times
