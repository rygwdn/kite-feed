France praises Carney’s response to U.S. demands, seeks closer Canada ties

Category: Canada

France’s new ambassador to Canada, Hélène Farnaud-Defromont, said European leaders admire Prime Minister Mark Carney’s response to what she called “extremely radical” U.S. demands and urged Ottawa and Paris to move quickly to strengthen strategic ties [1][3][4]. France is seeking closer cooperation as Canada and the European Union prepare for a bilateral summit later this month [4]. Carney suspended trade negotiations with Washington in August, accusing the Trump administration of adding new demands at the last minute, the ambassador said [4]. She urged Canada and France to avoid years of negotiations over a new partnership and identified defence, aerospace, energy, artificial intelligence and quantum technology as possible areas for cooperation [4].

We have a bilateral relationship that is very good, comprehensive and substantial, but we believe we can achieve even more together, in areas that are truly strategic

— Hélène Farnaud-Defromont (The News International)

Key Points

  • Shared values: Farnaud-Defromont said Canada and France share democratic values [4].
  • Global uncertainty: She said France wants to strengthen its relationship with Canada amid growing global economic and political uncertainty [4].
