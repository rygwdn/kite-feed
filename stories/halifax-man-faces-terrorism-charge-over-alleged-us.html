Halifax man faces terrorism charge over alleged U.S. consulate threat

Category: Canada

Ali Assakereh, 54, of Halifax, faces a terrorism-related charge after an alleged bomb threat against the U.S. Consulate in Halifax [1][2][4][3]. The new count accuses him of threatening to endanger the life of an internationally protected person [1][2]. Assakereh already faced two other offences related to the alleged threat, which shut down a large section of downtown Halifax [4].
