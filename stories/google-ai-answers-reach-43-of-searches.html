Google AI answers reach 43% of searches

Category: AI

Google’s AI-generated search answers, called AI Overviews, appeared in 43% of searches, up from 15% a year earlier, according to a Similarweb analysis cited by TechCrunch [1]. Similarweb also found that visits to Google’s AI Mode rose from 126 million in June 2025 to 279 million by May 2026, as Google keeps more users inside AI-generated responses rather than sending them directly to lists of links [1]. The shift has drawn concern from publishers and commentators because AI citations may not produce referral traffic, and because a synthesized answer can flatten uncertainty, contradictions and source conflicts, according to NWT columnist Per-Ola Olsson [1][5]. Yahoo is promoting Yahoo Scout as an AI search chatbot that shows sources so users can double-check information, according to Fast Company [6].
