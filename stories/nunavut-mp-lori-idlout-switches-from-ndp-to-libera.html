Nunavut MP Lori Idlout switches from NDP to Liberals

Category: Canada

Nunavut MP Lori Idlout has crossed the floor from the NDP to join Prime Minister Mark Carney’s Liberals, leaving the government two seats short of a majority in the House of Commons [3][5][7][11]. National Observer said the Liberals now hold 170 seats, with three federal byelections set for April 13 that could determine whether the party reaches majority territory [2]. Idlout said she made the move after hearing from constituents who want Nunavut to have a stronger voice inside the governing caucus [4][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Recent defections: Le Devoir reported Idlout’s switch came after three Conservative MPs recently defected to the Liberals [11].
  • First interview as a Liberal: In an interview with The Canadian Press, Idlout said constituents had been urging her to join the government, and she said staying with the NDP made her feel she was “betraying” Nunavummiut [4][6].
  • History of crossings: The Winnipeg Free Press noted Idlout is one of more than 300 MPs who have crossed the floor in Canada’s parliamentary history [1].
  • NDP outlook: The Tyee framed Idlout’s move as the NDP heads toward a leadership change, and weighed what the defection could mean for the party’s electoral chances [12].
