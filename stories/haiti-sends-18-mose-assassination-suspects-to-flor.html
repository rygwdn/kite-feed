Haiti sends 18 Moïse assassination suspects to Florida trial

Category: World

Haiti transferred 18 suspects in the 2021 assassination of President Jovenel Moïse to the United States on Sunday, Sept. 20, aboard a military aircraft. They will face federal proceedings in Florida over their alleged roles in the plot. U.S. Attorney Jason Reding Quiñones said 30 people have been indicted in the case. Joseph Felix Badio, a former Haitian anti-corruption official identified by Haitian government and police sources as a central suspect, was among those transferred [5][12][1][15]. Moïse, 53, was shot at his private residence in Port-au-Prince on July 7, 2021, by an armed group that included about 20 military-trained Colombian mercenaries. His security detail did not intervene, the reports said [5][1][13]. The transfer followed five years without a trial in Haiti and came after four men were convicted in a Miami federal court in May 2026 in connection with the alleged plot [17][42][13][37].

Did you know: The Colombian ex-soldiers accused in the case said they believed they were joining a coup and did not know about a plan to kill Moïse [35].

Key Points

  • Prior convictions: Four men convicted in Miami in May faced charges including conspiracy to kill or kidnap Moïse, material-support conspiracy and violations of the U.S. Neutrality Act [13][37].
  • Florida planning: U.S. prosecutors argued that South Florida served as a staging ground where parts of the assassination plan and financing were organized [17][25].
  • Security firm link: The four men convicted in May were linked to Florida-based CTU and allegedly expected lucrative contracts if Moïse were removed from office [13][44].
