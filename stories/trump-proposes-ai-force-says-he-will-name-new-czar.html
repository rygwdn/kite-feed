Trump proposes AI Force, says he will name new czar

Category: AI

President Trump said on September 19 that his administration would create an “AI Force” modeled on the Space Force and appoint a new AI czar, but gave no details about the proposed body’s structure, authority, budget or launch date [1][3][69][30]. He said the administration would support AI growth rather than slow it and would use existing criminal and civil law to address harmful uses [15][17]. Trump framed the initiative as part of the U.S. effort to maintain an advantage over China, saying AI could account for up to 25% of U.S. gross domestic product [14][32]. The announcement followed warnings from technology executives and researchers about safety risks, including calls for slower development and independent oversight. Trump has dismissed those concerns as a hoax [18][36]. The White House had not provided further details by September 20 [62].
