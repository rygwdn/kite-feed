Quebec deputy premier Geneviève Guilbault to leave politics

Category: Canada

Quebec minister Geneviève Guilbault says she will leave politics at the end of her current term, describing the move as a personal family decision [1][2]. Guilbault has been a prominent Coalition Avenir Québec figure for about eight years and served as Quebec’s deputy premier from 2022 to 2025 [1]. Political figures across Quebec publicly saluted her career after the announcement, highlighting the respectful tone around her planned transition to life outside elected office [3].
