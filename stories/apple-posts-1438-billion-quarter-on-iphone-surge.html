Apple posts $143.8 billion quarter on iPhone surge

Category: Technology

Apple said its fiscal first quarter (ending December 27, 2025) was the biggest in the company’s history, fueled by a holiday-season jump in iPhone demand and updated phone designs [1][2][4][10]. Several outlets reported iPhone revenue reached an all-time high of about $85.2 billion, helping push total quarterly revenue to about $143.8 billion and net profit to about $42.1 billion [1][2][5][10][6]. Beyond hardware, Apple’s Services business also set a quarterly record, growing in the mid-teens year over year, underscoring how the company increasingly earns from subscriptions and digital offerings tied to its devices [1][2][10]. Apple also said its installed base topped 2.5 billion active devices worldwide, giving it a larger audience for software, support, and services—an upbeat milestone for customers who rely on Apple products across daily life and work [7][10][11][13].

The demand for iPhone was simply staggering,

Did you know: Apple said it no longer reports the number of iPhones sold, so outside analysts infer demand from revenue and other signals [1].

Key Points

  • Dividend declared: Apple’s board declared a cash dividend of $0.26 per share, payable February 12, 2026 to shareholders of record on February 9, 2026 [2].
  • Margins improved: Apple reported gross margin of 48.2% versus 46.9% a year earlier, while projecting March-quarter gross margin between 48% and 49% [10].
  • Mac mixed: Mac revenue declined 7% year over year, even as Tim Cook said the Mac installed base hit an all-time high and nearly half of quarterly Mac buyers switched from another platform [2].
  • Services momentum: Cook cited specific Services tailwinds including a 36% year-over-year increase in Apple TV viewership in December, alongside Apple Music reaching all-time highs in listenership and new subscriber growth [2].
  • Supply constraints: DigiTimes reported iPhone 17 demand outstripped chip supply, with rising chip costs and tightening memory supply influencing Apple’s margins and supply-chain strategy [3].

Technical Details

  • Installed base: Apple’s “installed base” refers to devices actively in use; the company said it now exceeds 2.5 billion active devices worldwide, up from more than 2.35 billion a year earlier [10][15].
  • Services revenue: Apple’s Services segment includes subscription and digital offerings; multiple reports said it hit a record quarter, growing around the mid-teens year over year [1][2][10].
  • Gross margin: Gross margin measures the share of revenue left after direct costs; Apple reported 48.2% for the quarter and guided to 48%–49% for the March quarter [10].

Industry Impact

  • Mobile competition: Reports of double-digit growth from users switching to iPhone suggest Apple continues to pull customers from other smartphone platforms, potentially intensifying competition for upgrades and ecosystem lock-in [2].
  • Semiconductor supply chain: DigiTimes said iPhone 17 demand outstripped chip supply and rising chip and memory costs are influencing Apple’s supply-chain strategy, a signal suppliers may face tighter negotiations and shifting order patterns [3].
  • Subscription economy: Record Services growth—supported by Apple TV and Apple Music engagement metrics cited by Cook—highlights how device ecosystems can drive recurring revenue for media and cloud services providers [2].

Historical Background

Apple’s December-quarter results have often been its biggest because they include holiday shopping [common]. Apple also stopped reporting unit sales for iPhones in recent years, shifting investor attention toward revenue, margins, and Services growth instead of device counts [1].

Q&A

Q: Did Apple say which iPhone 17 model sold best?

A: No—Apple did not break out iPhone revenue by model, only saying results were driven by the “iPhone 17 family” [9][14].

Q: What does the 2.5 billion figure actually count?

A: It counts active devices currently in use (installed base), not the cumulative number of devices Apple has ever sold [14][15].

Q: What near-term outlook did Apple provide for the next quarter?

A: Apple projected March-quarter year-over-year revenue growth of 13% to 16% and gross margin between 48% and 49% [10].

Action Items

  • Check your Apple subscription usage: Review Apple ID subscriptions (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions) and right-size plans like iCloud+ or Apple Music based on your actual use, since Services are a growing part of Apple’s ecosystem push [2][15].
  • If you are a Mac switcher, compare trade-in options: If you are moving from another platform, compare Apple Trade In values with third-party resale to reduce the cost of entry, reflecting Cook’s note that many Mac buyers are switching from elsewhere [2].
