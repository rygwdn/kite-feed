Apple advances some iPhone Duo deliveries, but dates remain uncertain

Category: Technology

Apple has moved up estimated delivery windows for some preorders of the iPhone Duo, its first foldable phone, in the United States, Japan and Germany ahead of its Oct. 23 launch [7][16]. Some Japanese customers reported dates as early as Oct. 23 or 24, but one later said an apparent Oct. 24 estimate was a software error and that the delivery date had reverted to December. Not all buyers have seen changes [9][20]. Delivery estimates for new orders reached Jan. 25, 2027, within about 30 minutes of preorders opening Oct. 16 [7]. Counterpoint Research analyst Liz Lee said manufacturing delays, including late certification of the foldable OLED display and extended hinge and durability testing, slowed the production ramp-up [34]. Apple has not explained the revised estimates. The company is scheduled to begin sales and deliveries Oct. 23 and has told customers seeking store pickup to check availability on Oct. 24. Apple’s localized websites say walk-in sales will not be offered in Japan and South Korea for a limited time [39].

Primary Source: View original article

The pre-order demand for iPhone Duo, our first foldable iPhone, has been nothing short of extraordinary. We are working hard to get this revolutionary new product into customers' hands as quickly as possible, and we will continue accepting orders online.

— Apple (AppleInsider)

Key Points

  • Uneven updates: A U.S. buyer’s December estimate shifted to Nov. 20–30, while two orders previously slated for Dec. 1–8 moved to Nov. 16–23 [7].
  • Carrier orders: A German customer who ordered a 1TB model through Deutsche Telekom was told it should arrive between Oct. 26 and 31 [16].
  • Demand caveat: Counterpoint Research estimates 2026 shipments at no more than 5 million to 6 million units and cautions that preorder waits alone do not establish end-user demand [34].
  • Market expansion: Apple has scheduled the iPhone Duo’s launch in 28 additional countries and territories for Oct. 30 [29].

Perspectives

Mark Gurman, Bloomberg journalist: Gurman argued that Apple should not have opened preorders knowing the phone faced severe supply constraints. He said its decision to launch simultaneously in more than 70 countries was a mistake [36].

Sources: MacLife

Timeline

  • 16 October 2026: Apple opened preorders; new-order delivery estimates soon extended into January 2027 [7].
  • 19 October 2026: Customers began reporting earlier delivery windows in the United States and Japan [7][9].
  • 23 October 2026: Apple is scheduled to begin deliveries and retail sales of the foldable phone [7].
  • 24 October 2026: Apple directed customers to check stores for possible pickup availability [39].

Technical Details

  • Foldable OLED qualification: Counterpoint Research said the foldable OLED module completed production certification in late June, later than the planned May start, delaying the manufacturing schedule [34].
  • Hinge and durability testing: Longer-than-planned validation of the hinge structure and screen durability, along with unstable hinge yields and display-backplane supply, slowed the production ramp [34].

Industry Impact

  • Contract manufacturing: Foxconn is handling Apple’s first mass-production run of a foldable iPhone, using a process different from the iPhone Pro line. Counterpoint says unstable component yields and a concentrated production base are hampering the initial production ramp-up [34].

Historical Background

Apple reportedly considered delaying the phone’s launch until November because of production difficulties but kept its planned schedule [36]. A report cited by MacLife said production had reached only several hundred units a day in late August [36].
