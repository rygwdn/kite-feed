Nvidia plans open-source AI agent platform launch

Category: AI

Nvidia is preparing to launch an open-source platform for building and running AI “agents,” according to reporting published ahead of the company’s annual developer conference [1][3]. The project aims to give developers a straightforward way to assemble agent-like systems—software that can plan tasks and call tools—at a time when “agentic” assistant platforms are rapidly proliferating [1][3]. Because it’s open source, the platform could make it easier for researchers, startups, and enterprise teams to inspect, adapt, and standardize how agents are orchestrated across workflows, instead of treating them as opaque, vendor-locked features [1]. The reporting also frames the move as a competitive response to platforms such as OpenClaw, highlighting how quickly this corner of the AI ecosystem is moving—and how much value companies see in the software layer that sits above models and hardware [1][3].
