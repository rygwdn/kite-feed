OpenAI adds ChatGPT age prediction to limit content

Category: AI

OpenAI has begun rolling out an age-prediction system in ChatGPT aimed at reducing the chances that users under 18 receive sexual or other mature content [1][2][3]. Instead of relying only on a user-entered birthday, the system estimates age and applies tighter restrictions when it believes someone is a minor [1][2]. OpenAI and the coverage note the approach can make mistakes, which could mean some adults get treated like teens and see more limited responses or access to certain topics [1][3].

Primary Source: View original article

The model looks at a combination of behavioral and account-level signals, including how long an account has existed, typical times of day when someone is active, usage patterns over time,and a user’s stated age

— OpenAI (Engadget)

Did you know: Many online age gates rely on self-reported birthdays, which are easy to falsify and therefore provide limited protection by themselves [common].

Key Points

  • Policy shift: PCMag reports the age system ties into additional restrictions for younger users beyond mature topics, signaling a more rules-driven experience for accounts the system flags as teens [1].
  • Broader pressure: Several outlets note the rollout arrives amid scrutiny over how chatbots affect young people, including lawsuits tied to teen harm allegations and regulatory attention such as FTC interest in children’s impacts [4][11][12].
  • Content guardrails: When ChatGPT flags an account as possibly under 18, it blocks access to categories OpenAI lists as higher risk, including graphic violence, depictions of self-harm, and sexual, romantic, or violent role-play [4][12][10].
  • Platform skepticism: OpenAI and reviewers caution that age prediction can be imperfect and that some underage users may try to circumvent gating, similar to challenges seen on other platforms [1][11][6].
  • Default safer: OpenAI says that if the system is not confident about a user’s age, the account will default to safer settings rather than broader access [4].
  • Misclassification risk: OpenAI acknowledges the predictor will not be correct for every user, raising the possibility of incorrect “under-18” classification that could limit what some adults can do in ChatGPT [1][3].
  • Selfie fallback: Adults who believe they were incorrectly placed into the under-18 experience can confirm their age by submitting a selfie through Persona, a third-party identity verification service [4][1][11].
  • Youth protections: TechCrunch describes the feature as part of a push to keep “problematic content” from reaching minors, reflecting broader industry pressure to add child-safety controls to generative AI products [2].
  • Product governance: Reuters’ report (via Google News) frames the rollout as an OpenAI safety and compliance measure, as AI providers face scrutiny over how their tools are used by children and teens [3].

  • Tuesday: OpenAI announced age prediction rollout on consumer plans [4][11].

Technical Details

  • Age prediction: A system that estimates a user’s age based on signals available to the service, used here to decide whether to apply under-18 restrictions in ChatGPT [1][2].
  • False positives: When an automated system incorrectly flags an adult as under 18, which can lead to unnecessary content or feature restrictions [1][3].
  • Third-party verification: To correct suspected misclassification, OpenAI routes age confirmation through Persona using a selfie-based process [4][1][12].
  • Age assurance: A broader set of methods to determine whether a user is above a threshold (like 18) without relying solely on self-reported information, often used to enforce content policies [common].
  • Behavioral signals: OpenAI says the model infers age from usage behavior and account signals like activity times, account age, long-term patterns, and stated age [4][11].
  • Confidence defaulting: When the system lacks confidence in an estimate, OpenAI says it errs toward safer settings rather than granting broader access [4].

Industry Impact

  • AI product design: The rollout reinforces a trend toward age-gated experiences in consumer AI, using inferred signals plus verification pathways instead of only self-reported ages [11][4].
  • Identity vendors: By using Persona for selfie-based verification, OpenAI adds demand for third-party age/identity tooling and integrates those services into mainstream AI products [4][12].
  • Privacy tradeoffs: Age estimation can increase attention on what signals are used to infer age and how providers balance safety goals with user privacy expectations [1].
  • Child safety tooling: The rollout adds momentum to a broader shift toward built-in youth protections in consumer AI chatbots, nudging competitors to offer comparable safety controls [2][3].
  • Platform governance: Publishers note that imperfect classification and potential circumvention will keep pressure on companies to improve transparency, appeals, and safety-by-default mechanisms [1][11].
  • Content policy design: More automated gating can push AI companies to create clearer policy tiers (adult vs. teen experiences) and better appeals or correction flows when systems make mistakes [1].
  • Ecosystem integrations: 9to5Mac notes uncertainty about how age prediction might interact with Apple’s Siri fallback to ChatGPT, spotlighting how downstream integrations may need to respect upstream safety settings [5].

Historical Background

Online platforms have long used age gates (often simple “enter your birthday” prompts) to restrict adult content, but these are easy to bypass and can fail to protect minors [common]. In recent years, regulators and child-safety advocates have increased pressure on internet services to demonstrate meaningful protections for young users, including stronger content controls and age-assurance measures [common].

Q&A

Q: How does ChatGPT handle edge cases like 18- and 19-year-olds?

A: The articles describe a system intended to distinguish under-18 users from adults, but they do not spell out how it treats users close to the cutoff or what confidence thresholds it uses [1][2].

Q: Is there an appeal or verification path if the system guesses wrong?

A: The coverage notes OpenAI expects errors, but it does not provide a detailed public process for users to contest a misclassification in the reporting provided here [1][3].

Q: What kinds of signals are used to infer age?

A: The articles report that ChatGPT will predict age, but they do not enumerate the specific signals or data inputs the model uses in making that prediction [2][3].

Q: What happens if the system is unsure about your age?

A: OpenAI says accounts will default to safer settings when the model is not confident about age [4].

Q: Will this change how Siri hands requests to ChatGPT?

A: 9to5Mac says it is not immediately clear how age prediction will impact Siri fallback to ChatGPT [5].

Q: Does OpenAI describe an ‘adult mode’ in this rollout?

A: Coverage says OpenAI is preparing for an “adult mode” that would allow NSFW content, making age gating more important [11].

Action Items

  • Check what age experience you are getting: In the ChatGPT app, review account/safety settings and any age-related notices; 9to5Mac outlines where users can look to see whether age prediction has affected their experience [5].
  • Set safer defaults for teens using shared devices: If a household shares a device, consider using separate accounts and enabling the strictest content controls available for teen users to reduce accidental exposure to mature topics [2].
  • Prepare for possible verification: If you get restricted unexpectedly, follow the in-product prompt to verify age via Persona with a selfie, which OpenAI says can restore the adult experience when misclassified [4][1].
  • Check your account settings if responses suddenly become more restricted: If ChatGPT begins refusing mature topics you previously could access, review any age or profile settings available in your account and follow any in-product prompts to correct details [1].
  • Set up a teen account correctly: If a teen uses ChatGPT, enter the correct under-18 age at signup so safeguards apply automatically, rather than relying on inference later [4].
