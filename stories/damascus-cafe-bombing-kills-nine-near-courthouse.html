Damascus cafe bombing kills nine near courthouse

Category: World

A bomb planted inside a crowded cafe in central Damascus killed nine people and wounded 20 on Thursday, Syrian authorities said [2][7][8][11][14][17][31]. The blast occurred near the Palace of Justice in the capital's Hejaz district, and Syria's Interior Ministry said the device detonated at about 3 p.m. local time around 70 metres west of the courthouse [22][31][58]. No group immediately claimed responsibility for the bombing [4][10][13][14][31]. Syrian authorities said investigators set up a security cordon, collected forensic evidence, reviewed surveillance footage and took witness statements as they sought to identify the attackers [22][58][61]. The bombing was the deadliest in Damascus since a June 2025 suicide attack on a church killed 25 people [4][16][31].

Key Points

  • Explosive device: Preliminary findings described a crude improvised explosive device weighing about one kilogram and packed with metal fragments, causing severe injuries and extensive damage [16][21][31][61].
  • Lawyer deaths: A Syrian lawyers' union official told dpa the cafe was a regular gathering place for lawyers, and lawyer groups identified six lawyers among those killed [56][59][39].
  • Security measures: Police dogs and engineering teams swept the site for additional devices while special units secured the area against further threats [49][58][61].
  • IS concern: Security officials had warned that Islamic State sought to exploit the post-Assad security vacuum by reactivating sleeper cells, recruiting fighters and moving weapons [16][37][63].

Historical Background

Rebel forces led by Ahmed al-Sharaa overthrew Bashar al-Assad in December 2024, ending more than 14 years of civil war in Syria [16][35][31]. Damascus has seen several security incidents since then, including a May 2026 car bomb outside the Defence Ministry that killed one Syrian soldier and wounded at least 18 people [16][68][63]. In June 2025, a suicide bombing at a Damascus church killed 25 people; Saraya Ansar al-Sunnah said it carried out that attack, while Syrian authorities blamed Islamic State [4][31].

Humanitarian Impact

SANA said hospitals in Damascus received casualties, while Syrian medical officials described injuries ranging from minor to critical and including abdominal, chest and head trauma [22][46][58].
