Trump hosts Anthropic chief Amodei for White House dinner

Category: AI

President Donald Trump hosted Anthropic Chief Executive Dario Amodei for a private dinner at the White House on Sunday, Sept. 27. It was their first one-on-one meeting. The gathering followed months of conflict over AI policy and Anthropic’s dispute with the administration [2][18][13][45]. Two days earlier, a federal appeals court upheld the Pentagon’s designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, allowing the military to bar Anthropic’s models. The dispute followed Anthropic’s refusal in February to permit its tools for fully autonomous weapons or domestic mass surveillance [17][14].
