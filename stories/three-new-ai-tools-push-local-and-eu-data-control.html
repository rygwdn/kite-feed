Three new AI tools push local and EU data control

Category: AI

Three small AI launches caught the community’s eye this week, each taking a different angle on how and where AI should run. EUrouter pitches itself as a way to plug in newer AI models while keeping data inside the European Union, aimed at teams that want privacy and compliance without sending information outside the region [1]. On Hugging Face, a project describes pure Rust, zero-dependency AI models that run locally and are offered free forever [2]. TattooForge, meanwhile, offers an AI tattoo design generator [3].
