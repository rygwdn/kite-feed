Carney meets Alberta remain campaigners ahead of referendum

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney met supporters of the Forever Canadian campaign in Banff, Alta., on Sept. 11, after the final day of a federal cabinet retreat. About 50 supporters cheered as Carney shook hands with them, sang “O Canada” and wrote “Canada is everyone and for everyone” on the group’s pink campaign vehicle [1][8][6][7]. The meeting took place before Alberta’s Oct. 19 referendum on whether to hold a second vote on leaving Canada. Carney has called for national unity but has not campaigned extensively for Alberta to remain in the federation; on Thursday, he urged Albertans to vote [3][6]. Former Alberta deputy premier Thomas Lukaszuk, who leads Forever Canadian, said Carney’s stance in trade negotiations with the White House showed that “there is someone at the helm” [3][6].
