OpenAI raises $110 billion as ChatGPT hits 900M

Category: AI

OpenAI said it secured $110 billion in new private funding at a $730 billion pre-money valuation. Amazon is investing $50 billion, with Nvidia and SoftBank putting in $30 billion each [11][5][9]. OpenAI described the financing as fuel for “scaling AI for everyone,” alongside plans to expand global AI infrastructure and enterprise offerings [11][13]. OpenAI also shared fresh adoption numbers: ChatGPT now has more than 900 million weekly active users and more than 50 million consumer subscribers, and the company said more than 9 million paying business users rely on ChatGPT for work [4][3]. OpenAI and Amazon, meanwhile, announced a strategic partnership to bring OpenAI’s Frontier platform to AWS and expand infrastructure for custom models and enterprise AI agents. OpenAI and Microsoft also issued a joint statement highlighting their continued work together across research, engineering, and product development [12][10]. Together, the announcements signal strong momentum—but they also raise the stakes. Several outlets described the round as a high-pressure bet that could intensify competition among cloud and chip providers and reshape industry partnerships [2][7][8].

Industry Impact

  • Cloud competition: Amazon’s investment and expanded partnership put AWS in a stronger position as a channel for OpenAI’s enterprise offerings. CNBC and Axios said this could lift Amazon’s AI and cloud businesses and shift competitive dynamics [5][6][8].
  • Chip and compute: Nvidia’s participation ties capital to access to large, dedicated compute capacity, reinforcing the trend of chipmakers pairing supply and financing to secure major AI workloads [3].
  • Enterprise adoption: OpenAI’s disclosed base of paying business users relying on ChatGPT points to growing workplace penetration, which can drive demand for adjacent tools such as agents, deployment platforms, and governance services [3][12].
