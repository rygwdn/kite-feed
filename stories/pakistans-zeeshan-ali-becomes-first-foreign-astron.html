Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali becomes first foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong station

Category: Science

Pakistan Air Force pilot and payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali has entered China’s Tiangong space station, becoming the first foreign astronaut to visit the outpost and the first Pakistani to reach orbit. Shenzhou-24 docked on Nov. 25 after launching from Jiuquan, China, carrying Ali, Chinese commander Tang Hongbo and flight engineer Wu Yi. The flight is the first joint mission with a foreign astronaut in China’s crewed space program [1][5][8][3]. Ali is assigned four China-Pakistan research projects in biotechnology and materials, including tests involving photosynthetic cyanobacteria, quinoa seeds and composite materials. The mission includes more than 90 experiments and technology tests in fields including life sciences, materials science, physics and space medicine [12][13][25]. Ali is expected to spend five days to a week aboard Tiangong, then return to Earth with two members of the Shenzhou-23 crew aboard their spacecraft. Chinese astronauts Tang and Wu are scheduled to remain for the usual six-month rotation, while Shenzhou-23 commander Zhu Yangzhu will stay for a year as researchers study how prolonged spaceflight affects the human body [2][19][18].

What comes after this flight matters more than the flight itself

— Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, Pakistani astronaut and payload specialist (The Nation)

Did you know: Pakistani adventurer Namira Salim flew on Virgin Galactic’s suborbital Galactic 04 mission in October 2023, before Ali became the first Pakistani to reach orbit [4][1].

Key Points

  • Research range: Fundamental-physics work will use optical-lattice quantum simulation to explore new states of matter and test a space cold-atom interferometer for inertial measurement [12].
  • Crew milestone: Tang Hongbo is set to become the first astronaut to visit Tiangong three times [12].
  • Training demands: Ali trained in Beijing for seven months, including intensive Chinese-language instruction because spacecraft controls, software and voice announcements operate in Chinese [2][11].
  • Station capacity: Tiangong can host up to six astronauts during crew handovers, allowing Shenzhou-24’s arrival to overlap with the departing crew [8].

Perspectives

Blaine Curcio, space industry consultant: Curcio said sending another country’s astronaut requires substantial trust and gives Pakistan detailed insight into China’s crewed space program. He said the mission could show other countries that Beijing is open to similar cooperation [2].

Sources: The Guardian

Ahsan Iqbal, Pakistan's planning minister: Iqbal said the flight could lead to expanded cooperation, including a possible joint lunar mission. He said it shows how developing countries can build capabilities in strategically important areas [8].

Scientific Significance

  • Life-support research: The microalgae experiment examines whether photosynthetic microorganisms could provide oxygen and nutrients for future long-duration missions. The flight tests the idea in orbit but will not establish its practical performance [13][16].
  • Seed response: Researchers will test how microgravity and cosmic radiation affect quinoa seeds to learn about seed longevity and traits that may withstand harsh environments. The articles describe planned experiments, not results [13][3].
  • Space medicine: Crew studies will include multi-omics research and studies of behavior and brain function, alongside tests of health-maintenance technologies [12].

Historical Background

China-Pakistan space cooperation dates to the early 1990s; China has launched Pakistani satellites, and a Pakistani-built rover is scheduled to fly on a Chinese lunar mission around 2028 [2][8].

Q&A

Q: Could other countries send astronauts to Tiangong?

A: Space industry consultant Blaine Curcio said Brazil and Egypt may seek opportunities, though future participation would depend on available partnerships. He also said European countries or others participating in the International Space Station could become candidates if no Western replacement station is available [12][2].
