Canada orders new appeal in Indigenous sisters’ murder convictions

Category: Canada

Federal Justice Minister Sean Fraser ordered a new appeal in the 1994 second-degree murder convictions of Odelia and Neressa Quewezance, sisters from Keeseekoose First Nation in Saskatchewan. A jury convicted them in the death of Anthony Joseph Dolff [3][5]. The Justice Department review took more than four years. It found reasonable grounds to conclude that a miscarriage of justice likely occurred, citing new information that was not available at trial or during the earlier appeal [3][5][7]. Fraser said the order returns the case to the courts for consideration of the new information and does not determine the sisters’ guilt or innocence [4][3].
