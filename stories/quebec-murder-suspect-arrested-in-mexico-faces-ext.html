Quebec murder suspect arrested in Mexico, faces extradition to Canada

Category: Canada

A 22-year-old Quebec man wanted in connection with a 2025 Montreal homicide was arrested Thursday in Playa del Carmen, Mexico, and is being held pending extradition proceedings, Montreal police announced Friday — more than a year after the killing [4][1][8]. The case concerns a 34-year-old man found with a lower-body stab wound near Ontario and Saint-Germain streets shortly after midnight on March 14, 2025. He died in hospital overnight. Police identified the arrested man as Gabriel Lavoie and said the investigation remains ongoing [4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Earlier arrests: Dave Lambert-Chouinard and Valdano Saint-Hillien were arrested shortly after the killing and charged with manslaughter [4].
  • Homicide count: The victim's death was Montreal's fifth homicide of 2025, Global News reported [4].
  • Third suspect: Police had been seeking a third suspect on a murder warrant before Lavoie's arrest in Mexico [4].
