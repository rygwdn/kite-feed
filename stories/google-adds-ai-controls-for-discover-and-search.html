Google adds AI controls for Discover and Search

Category: Technology

Google on Aug. 20 announced personalization updates for Search, Discover and Google News, including an embeddable “Preferred Sources” button that publishers can add to their sites and new controls for users to prioritize publishers, topics and content formats [9][14][7][8]. Google said preferred publishers can appear more often in Search features including Top Stories, AI Overviews and AI Mode, while Google News on Android now lets users customize daily audio briefings by topic where the feature is available [9][2][3][4]. Google also said Discover will soon allow users to type or speak natural-language requests about what they want to see more or less of, with the feed adjusting immediately and remembering those requests for later visits [1][5][7][6]. The Discover controls are rolling out through the Google app in the coming days, accessible from the three-dot menu next to a story or a dedicated card in the feed [1][7][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Audio choices: Google News audio briefings can be tuned across categories such as Headlines, Business, Entertainment and lifestyle, Sports, and Science and technology, with more granular topic choices under each category [7][2].
  • Follow-up prompts: Google can ask follow-up questions in Discover to refine a user’s stated interests before refreshing the feed [7][6][11][12].

Perspectives

Google: Google framed the changes as a way for users to tailor content, save preferred sources and customize audio briefings across its Search, Discover and News products.

Sources: Google Blog

TechCrunch: TechCrunch described the publisher button as a response to AI-driven traffic losses for publishers and said Google says preferred-source status can drive more traffic to publisher sites.

Sources: TechCrunch

Engadget: Engadget noted that Google referral traffic has declined in recent years because of products including AI Overviews and AI Mode, while Search remains an important revenue source for many media outlets.

Sources: Engadget

  • May 2026: Preferred Sources reached AI Mode and AI Overviews [14]

  • August 2026: Discover controls begin rolling out in coming days [1][7]

Historical Background

Google previously let users influence Discover mainly through signals such as searches, pages visited, followed topics and feedback controls for seeing less of a topic or source [5][12].

Business Angle

  • The button gives publishers a way to seek preference signals from readers, and Google said preferred sources receive higher click-through when available [14].
  • The tools add explicit preference signals inside Google products, rather than relying only on inferred behavior [5][common].
  • Preferred-source placement still operates inside Google-controlled Search, AI and News surfaces [3][common].

Action Items

  • Add a preferred publisher: Use a publisher’s embedded Preferred Sources button when available, or visit Google’s source preferences page and search by publisher name or website [14][3].
  • Tune Discover manually: Open the Google app, tap the three-dot menu next to a Discover story, and describe the topics or links you want to see more or less of when the rollout reaches your account [1][7].
  • Customize audio briefings: In Google News on Android, choose the categories and subtopics you want included in daily audio briefings where the feature is available [7][10].
