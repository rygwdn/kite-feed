Trump meets Putin’s envoy, hints at Europe-wide Ukraine deal before talks

Category: World

President Donald Trump said he met Russian President Vladimir Putin’s envoy Kirill Dmitriev at the White House on Nov. 5 and predicted an unspecified agreement on Ukraine that could extend across Europe. Trump disclosed no terms and said he did not meet Ukrainian officials who were in Washington for a separate event hosted by First Lady Melania Trump [9][15][57]. Melania Trump brought Russian and Ukrainian representatives together to discuss the six-day local ceasefire she helped broker in Russian-occupied Oleshky and urged the sides to seek a more lasting truce. The meeting preceded a new round of U.S.-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks scheduled for Abu Dhabi on Nov. 10-11 [10][8][13]. Separately, U.S. envoy Jared Kushner said American, Ukrainian, NATO and European representatives discussed security guarantees for Ukraine and a wider European security framework in Brussels. Trump did not explain how those talks relate to the agreement he described. Some European officials fear a wider deal could reduce the U.S. military presence in Europe and give Moscow greater influence on the continent [48][4].

Key Points

  • Security proposals: Kushner listed five areas under discussion in Brussels: ongoing security consultations, crisis communications, military transparency, reciprocal steps to reduce escalation risks and a long-term European security framework [13][48].
  • Evacuation claims: Melania Trump said nearly 800 people left Oleshky during the truce, but neither the Ukrainian nor Russian side confirmed that figure, BBC Russian reported [28].
  • Kyiv’s position: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said the Oleshky pause showed that fighting stops when Putin wants it to and that it saved hundreds of lives [15].
  • Congressional scrutiny: The incoming Democratic majorities in both chambers of Congress are expected to press for more Ukraine aid and examine the administration’s Russia policy, according to reports [14][21].

Perspectives
