Google tests Gemini phone-calling feature on Pixel 11

Category: Technology

Google began testing Call for Me on Sept. 24, a feature that lets Gemini place routine business calls for users, including checking store inventory, making restaurant reservations and changing appointments. The early preview is limited to U.S. users with a Pixel 11-series phone, a paid Gemini subscription and enrollment in the Phone by Google beta [2][4][5][9]. Users request a call through Gemini; the AI introduces itself, can navigate automated menus and wait on hold, and displays a live transcript that users can monitor and take over at any point. Gemini can share personal information approved by users [4][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Calls made by Gemini on behalf of the user represent real customers conducting real transactions.

— Google (9to5Google)

Key Points

  • Call safeguards: Before dialing, Gemini displays a summary of the task and the information it plans to share [12].
  • Additional eligibility rules require users to be adults, use English-language device settings and have a U.S. SIM [7][12][14].
  • Google's rationale: Google said it wants the system to respect local businesses' time [2].

Historical Background

Pixel phones already offered separate call-assistance tools, including Hold for Me and Direct My Call [5].

Q&A

Q: Will Call for Me make calls from a separate Google number?

A: No. It uses the user's personal phone number and is dialed directly from the user's phone [4].
