Trump defends Putin ties as Russian strikes kill eight in Ukraine

Category: World

President Donald Trump defended his personal relationships with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping after meeting them in Shenzhen, China, saying his ties with both leaders helped keep the United States safe. Trump said Putin wanted to end the war in Ukraine through diplomacy and that a deal had a “real good chance,” but he would not discuss security guarantees for Kyiv before an agreement. The three leaders issued a short peace declaration that affirmed nuclear war must never be fought but did not mention Ukraine or Iran [2][8][13][14]. Hours after the meeting, Russia launched one of its largest missile and drone attacks of the war, killing at least eight people and injuring nearly 30, according to Ukrainian officials. The strikes hit electricity facilities. Ukraine had earlier damaged the Crimean Bridge, which links occupied Crimea to Russia [8][19][23]. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky called Putin an enemy and “a killer,” saying that describing the Russian leader as a friend meant Putin did not feel enough pressure [1][52]. A Russian cruise missile and two other suspected objects also entered Polish airspace during the attack; NATO scrambled jets after what Poland called a provocation [1]. Trump deferred discussion of Ukraine’s postwar security, while Moscow rejected NATO-like guarantees [8][19].
