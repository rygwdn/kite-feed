Israeli attacks kill six in Gaza during ceasefire talks

Category: World

Israeli attacks killed at least six people in the Gaza Strip on Sunday, including 9-year-old Tala Abu Matar, Palestinian health officials and medics said, as mediators held talks over the U.S.-brokered ceasefire [4][7][8][10][11][12][14][38]. Medics said Israeli gunfire hit a tent encampment on the eastern side of Al-Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza, killing the girl; the Israeli military said it was not aware of the incident [4][8][10][12][14][38]. An Israeli airstrike on a metal foundry, described in some accounts as a blacksmith workshop, killed four people in Gaza City’s Sabra neighbourhood, and witnesses said three Israeli missiles hit the site [4][8][10][12][38][39]. Israel’s military said it struck “terrorist” infrastructure, later describing the target as a Hamas weapon production site, but gave limited details in several accounts [3][14][36]. Later Sunday, an Israeli strike on a tent encampment in the Mawasi area of Khan Younis killed at least one person and wounded several others, including children, medics said; accounts differed on Israel’s response, with some saying the military did not immediately comment and another quoting it as saying the strike was part of routine activities and that it was not aware of casualties [11][12][14][38]. The attacks took place as Hamas leaders visited Cairo for talks on implementing the second phase of President Trump’s Gaza peace plan, including Hamas disarmament and Israeli army withdrawals, according to sources close to the negotiations [17][23][26][35][38]. Those sources said the talks had not produced a breakthrough [17][23][26][35][38].

Key Points

  • Ceasefire toll: The October 2025 ceasefire halted major fighting, but Israeli attacks have killed more than 1,000 Palestinians and wounded more than 3,535 since it took effect, while four Israeli soldiers have been killed in Gaza in the same period [17][23][26][28][35].
  • Follow-up strikes: After the first strikes in Sabra, Palestinians received evacuation orders, and roughly an hour later intense airstrikes hit the same blacksmith shop, the Palestinian Red Crescent said [14][9][29].
  • Northern operations: Israel's military said its forces had killed at least two Hamas fighters in northern Gaza since Thursday who were planning attacks on Israeli troops [4][10][12][38].
  • Civilian displacement: Nearly all of Gaza's 2 million people, most displaced several times, now live on a narrow coastal strip, mainly in makeshift tents or damaged buildings [23][28][26][35].

Perspectives

Israel Defense Forces: The military said it struck a weapons production site in Gaza and that operatives were inside when it struck; it also said it was unaware of the reported incident in which a 9-year-old girl was killed.
