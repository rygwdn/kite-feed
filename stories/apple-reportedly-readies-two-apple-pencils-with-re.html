Apple reportedly readies two Apple Pencils with replaceable batteries for 2027

Category: Technology

Apple is developing two new Apple Pencil models for the first half of 2027, alongside updated iPad Pro hardware, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported [1][3][5][6][7][12]. The planned lineup includes a successor to the entry-level Apple Pencil with USB-C, internally codenamed B582, and a refreshed Apple Pencil Pro, internally codenamed B632 [3][7][12]. The redesign may focus on battery systems as Apple works to comply with European Union rules requiring portable batteries in consumer electronics to be easier for users to remove and replace [5][10][13]. Article 11 of the EU Batteries Regulation entered into force in 2023 and becomes legally applicable in February 2027, covering accessories including rechargeable styli, keyboards, mice and headphones [5][10][13]. Apple has not announced the products, and Gurman did not report specific new Apple Pencil features beyond possible battery-system changes [1][6][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Feature gap: The current Apple Pencil Pro has squeeze gestures, barrel-roll detection, haptic feedback and Find My support, while the USB-C model lacks pressure sensitivity and wireless charging [4].

Perspectives

iFixit: The repair site has given all Apple Pencil models failing repairability scores because users cannot replace the internal batteries when they die.

Sources: Engadget

Timeline

  • 2023: The EU Batteries Regulation entered into force [5][10]
  • November 2023: Apple released the USB-C Apple Pencil [6]
  • May 2024: Apple introduced the Apple Pencil Pro [6][4]
  • February 2027: Article 11 becomes legally applicable [5][10]
  • 2027-H1: New Apple Pencils are expected with iPad hardware [7][3]

Historical Background

Apple's Pencil lineup has had multiple tiers since Apple added the lower-priced USB-C model in 2023 and the premium Apple Pencil Pro in 2024 [6]. EU product rules have previously changed Apple's hardware decisions: Apple moved the iPhone 15 lineup to USB-C after EU charging-port legislation [7][3].

Business Angle

  • Accessory redesigns may add manufacturing complexity; Apple has greater scale than smaller hardware rivals [common].
  • A longer-lasting Apple Pencil could reduce replacement purchases among artists, students and professionals who depend on the stylus [4][common].
  • Sources cited limited internal space in AirPods as a barrier to user-replaceable batteries [5][13].
