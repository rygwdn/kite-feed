SpaceX IPO lifts company valuation above $2 trillion

Category: Business

SpaceX went public on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX on June 12 at $135 a share, in a flotation that valued the company at about $1.77 trillion and raised about $75 billion before additional underwriter shares were counted [8][12][16]. The IPO ranks as the largest on record, exceeding Saudi Aramco’s $29.4 billion listing, and SpaceX ended its first week of trading 37% above the IPO price [4][18][7]. The first-week rally lifted SpaceX’s market value above $2.4 trillion in some accounts and made Elon Musk the world’s first trillionaire, with most accounts attributing the milestone to the value of his SpaceX stake after the listing [7][12][15]. Several sources tied the valuation to investor expectations for Starlink, reusable rockets, defense work, artificial intelligence infrastructure and possible orbital data centers, while also noting that SpaceX is unprofitable and needs heavy investment [6][16][13]. The debut could also affect investors who did not directly buy SpaceX shares: NPR reported that broadly diversified funds can acquire newly public companies through index-tracking portfolios, while The Standard said possible index inclusion could drive passive-fund demand in the first days and weeks after listing [1][13]. Business Insider reported that stock awards at SpaceX, OpenAI and Anthropic have produced large paper gains for some employees, but said vesting, dilution, lockups and taxes can reduce realized proceeds [5].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: TMTPost said SpaceX launched 165 rockets in 2025, accounting for 85% of U.S. launches and 51% of global launches that year [14].

Key Points

  • Valuation math: The Standard calculated that SpaceX traded at about 95 times sales after its first trading day, based on 2025 revenue of roughly $18.7 billion and a market value above $2 trillion [13].
  • Profit question: Secretmag, citing CNBC and Bryce Tech data, said SpaceX reported a loss of almost $5 billion in the year before the offering, despite dominating launch payload volume in early 2026 [12].
  • Index flows: BLS Global Investing told The Standard that FTSE Russell and CRSP could consider SpaceX for index inclusion within about five trading days, followed by MSCI in about 10 trading days and Nasdaq-100 in about 15 trading days if requirements were met [13].
  • Capital markets: Goldman Sachs expects U.S. companies to raise $225 billion in 2026, a record level; Le Monde said the issuance is helping fund corporate investment that has risen 12% since the end of 2022 [10].
  • Employee wealth: Business Insider said an August 2022 SpaceX Software Engineer II offer with a $365,000 five-year equity grant could be worth more than $4 million at the IPO valuation if the employee held it in full [5].

Perspectives

Retail index investor Kelsea Jordan: Jordan told NPR she invests broadly for early retirement but worries that broad-market portfolios could expose her to SpaceX and other technology companies she considers overvalued.

  • December 2026: Remaining lockup tranche expected roughly 180 days after the IPO [13]

Technical Details

  • Reusable launch economics: TMTPost cited SpaceX cost estimates that put low-Earth-orbit launch costs at $2,700 per kilogram for Falcon 9 and $1,400 per kilogram for Falcon Heavy, down from a 1970-2000 global average of $18,500 per kilogram [14].
  • Starlink unit economics: TMTPost said Starlink subscription ARPU fell from $99 in 2023 to $81 in 2025 and $66 in the first quarter of 2026, while business, maritime and aviation plans can cost about $500 a month [14].
  • Orbital compute proposal: TMTPost described Musk’s orbital data-center concept as involving satellites with solar arrays longer than 170 meters and about 100 kilowatts of power each, while noting unresolved questions about radiative cooling in space [14].

Industry Impact

  • IPO pipeline: Bloomberg said SpaceX’s first-week performance increased anticipation for expected public listings by OpenAI and Anthropic [4].
  • Private tech compensation: Business Insider said equity grants at OpenAI and Anthropic have also created large paper gains, with some 2022 and 2023 OpenAI engineer grants valued above $50 million [5].

Business Angle

  • At a high sales multiple, missed expectations for Starlink growth, launch margins or AI infrastructure revenue could put pressure on the valuation [13][common].
  • Lockup expirations may begin after second-quarter results in July, with further unlocks after third-quarter results and the remaining shares about 180 days after the IPO in December, potentially turning long-held stakes into supply that tests demand [13][5].
  • SpaceX’s launch-cost advantage and Starlink scale could become a competitive advantage if they produce recurring cash flow rather than only higher capital spending [14][common].
  • Index inclusion can create forced buying by passive funds, but it does not by itself prove long-term intrinsic value [13][common].

Action Items

  • Check fund exposure: Review the holdings page for your index funds or target-date funds after quarterly updates to see whether SPCX has been added; NPR reported that broad funds can make investors SpaceX shareholders without a direct purchase [1].
  • Review lockup dates before trading: If considering SpaceX shares, compare your timing with the lockup schedule The Standard described for post-second-quarter results, post-third-quarter results and about 180 days after IPO [13].
