Apple plans Apple Upgrade device leases with Klarna

Category: Technology

Apple plans to launch Apple Upgrade in the U.S. on Tuesday, July 28, offering leases for most iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch models through Klarna, according to reports citing Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman [1][3][4][6][7]. Apple will stop accepting new enrollments in the iPhone Upgrade Program the same day, while existing participants can remain in that program “for the time being,” Gurman said [1][4][7]. The reported leases run 24 months for iPhones and Apple Watches and 36 months for iPads and Macs, with end-of-term options including returning the device, upgrading to a newer model or paying a fee to keep it [1][3][6][7]. Gurman said lower-priced devices, including Apple Watch SE, the entry-level iPad, iPhone 16 and MacBook Neo, will be excluded. Reports citing him said AppleCare+ will not be bundled into the monthly price, unlike the older iPhone Upgrade Program [1][3][6][7]. Apple plans to market the program as a lower-monthly-payment alternative to current financing options, pointing to a lease-focused model rather than traditional installment ownership, according to Gurman [1][7]. El CEO separately described Apple Upgrade as a direct-sales monthly-payment plan available from July 28 in Apple’s physical and online stores [8][1][3].
