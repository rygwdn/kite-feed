Oarfish fin rays could guide underwater robots designed to avoid startling fish

Category: Science

Roboticists are looking to the oarfish’s distinctive swimming movements as a possible model for underwater robots that move without startling fish [1][2]. The fish’s many fin rays help produce its unusual movement. The reports describe a potential approach to underwater robotics, not a deployed robot or completed technology [1][2].
