Canada's Conservative leader Poilievre urges Alberta to reject separation

Category: Canada

Federal Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre urged Albertans to vote to remain in Canada in a referendum less than three weeks away. He said Albertans’ grievances stem from federal government policies, not Canada itself, and that separation would not resolve them [1][4][5]. Alberta’s United Conservative Party government, led by Premier Danielle Smith, called the vote [1].
