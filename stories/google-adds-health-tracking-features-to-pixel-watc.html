Google adds health tracking features to Pixel Watch 3-5

Category: Technology

Google is rolling out Google Health version 5.09 for Android and iOS, adding Health Guardian features for Pixel Watch 3, 4 and 5 users: blood-pressure trends, insulin-resistance trends and sleep-breathing quality metrics. The features analyze longer-term patterns rather than provide clinical test results. Google’s first monthly Health Guardian summaries are due at the beginning of October [1][3][6][8]. The update also adds support for Cardio Load from third-party workouts [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Report thresholds: Blood-pressure trends require at least five consecutive days of watch wear in September, while insulin-resistance trends require seven days and nights; reports then appear in the Health app [3].
  • Emergency detection: Pixel Watch 4 and 5 are set to offer breathing-emergency detection in selected European markets, including France, Germany, Ireland and the UK [8][9].
  • Fitbit pricing: Fitbit Air is expected to get blood-pressure and insulin-resistance trends later in 2026, but those features will require a Google Health Premium subscription [6][11][8].

Technical Details

  • Model training data: Google trained its insulin-resistance trend model on data contributed by more than 5 million users and more than 1 trillion data points. Its blood-pressure model was built from billions of minutes of heart-rate and movement data [8].
  • Emergency call sequence: When a qualifying oxygen drop occurs, the watch alerts the wearer first. If there is no response, a 30-second countdown precedes an automatic emergency call that includes the wearer’s location [9].
