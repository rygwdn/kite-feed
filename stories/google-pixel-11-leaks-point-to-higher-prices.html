Google Pixel 11 leaks point to higher prices

Category: Technology

Several leaks ahead of Google’s Aug. 12 Made by Google event say the Pixel 11 lineup will include the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold, with U.S. starting prices of $899, $1,099, $1,299 and $1,899, respectively [4][5][12]. Google has not officially confirmed the leaked prices or specifications, and Techkrams reported that Google has confirmed only the Pixel 11 name, the Aug. 12 presentation date and preorders beginning the same day [8]. The leaked pricing would raise the starting price of the base Pixel 11 by $100 from the Pixel 10, keep the Pixel 11 Pro at last year’s $1,099 starting price, and raise the Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold by $100 each [4][5]. PCMag reported that Google devices executive Shakil Barkat told 9to5Google the Pixel family would see price “adjustments” because memory prices have risen sharply across the industry over the past year [4]. Several leaked specification lists include Google’s Tensor G6 processor, a Titan M3 co-processor, OLED displays, Qi2 wireless charging and storage tiers beginning at 256GB on the base model [5][9][8]. The Pro and Pro XL models are expected to start with 12GB of RAM, down from 16GB on their Pixel 10 counterparts, while some reports list the standard Pixel 11 with 12GB or 16GB RAM configurations [4][5][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: A leaked German specification table lists Bluetooth 6 for the Pixel 11, a standard that has only recently begun appearing in consumer-device spec sheets [8].

Key Points

  • Base hardware: The standard Pixel 11 is listed with a 6.3-inch OLED display, 2,424-by-1,080 resolution, 3,000-nit peak brightness and a 60Hz-to-120Hz adaptive refresh rate [5][9].
  • Battery setup: The base model is expected to carry a 4,985mAh typical battery, 30W wired charging and Qi2 wireless charging support, with no charger in the box in one German specification table [5][9].
  • Camera mix: Leaked base Pixel 11 camera details include a 48MP main camera, a 13MP ultrawide camera, no dedicated telephoto lens and up to 30x digital zoom [5][9][14].
  • Fold design: Leaked renders indicate the Pixel 11 Pro Fold may feature a new Pixel Glow RGB notification light, which is absent from the standard Pixel 11 [14].

Perspectives

9to5Google: Ben Schoon wrote that the Pixel 11 cycle looked like “bad timing,” citing leaked prices, few design changes and no headline feature comparable to last year’s Qi2 support.

Sources: 9to5Google

Digital Trends: Digital Trends described the standard Pixel 11 leak as a mixed set of upgrades and downgrades because it pairs doubled base storage and higher RAM options with higher pricing and uncertain camera changes.

Sources: Digital Trends

Industry Impact

  • Foldable competition: The leaked Pixel 11 Pro Fold price of $1,899 would sit $200 below Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra while listing similar display and zoom hardware, according to Digital Trends [3].

Historical Background

The Pixel 11 generation marks the 10-year anniversary of Google’s Pixel line, according to 9to5Google [6]. Google introduced Qi2 support with the Pixel 10 series last year, and 9to5Google said Google has stated that major Pixel design refreshes are planned every two to three generations [6].

Business Angle

  • Higher starting prices could offset component inflation, but carrier or retail discounts would reduce the effect [4][common].
  • Leaked Pro configurations list 12GB starting RAM, down from 16GB, even as storage and processor specifications improve [4][5].

Action Items

  • Compare RAM tiers before buying: Check the final Pro and Pro XL configurations, since leaks say those models may start at 12GB of RAM instead of last generation’s 16GB base [4].
