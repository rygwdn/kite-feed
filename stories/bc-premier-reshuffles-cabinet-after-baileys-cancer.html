B.C. premier reshuffles cabinet after Bailey's cancer diagnosis

Category: Canada

British Columbia Premier David Eby announced three cabinet changes on Aug. 14 after Finance Minister Brenda Bailey said she would take leave for cancer treatment and recovery [5][4][1]. Josie Osborne moved from health to finance, Ravi Kahlon moved from jobs and economic growth to health, and Bailey remained in cabinet as minister of jobs and economic growth [5][4][1]. Bailey said she has early-stage, treatable cancer; the province said she will begin treatment in late August 2026 and expects to return to work in early October 2026 [5][6][7]. Eby said budget preparations were already underway and that September is an intense month in the budget cycle, making the finance portfolio difficult to carry during cancer treatment [4]. Adrian Dix, minister of energy and climate solutions, will cover Bailey’s jobs portfolio while she is on leave [5][4].
