San Francisco orders Meta to halt AI-generated child sexual abuse ads

Category: AI

San Francisco’s City Attorney’s Office ordered Meta to stop allowing ads involving AI-generated child sexual abuse material and asked the company to explain how the ads repeatedly appeared on Facebook and Instagram [1]. Meta said the ads fall outside the city’s jurisdiction [1]. The action followed findings by the Tech Transparency Project that Meta ran hundreds of paid ads containing AI-generated child sexual abuse imagery over the past 10 months. The findings included 332 ads identified in a report cited by BBC and more than 250 additional ads researchers found since the beginning of August while checking whether Meta’s safeguards were working [4][2]. Several ads ran in India after the Indian government ordered Meta to remove ads and content promoting child sexual abuse material on Instagram, following a BBC Eye investigation [4][3]. Meta told BBC, “We don't tolerate nudify apps or any kind of child exploitation, whether real or AI-generated. These criminals constantly shift tactics to evade detection, which is why we keep strengthening our detection and enforcement” [4].
