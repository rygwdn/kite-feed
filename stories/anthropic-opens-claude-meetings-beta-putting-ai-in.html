Anthropic opens Claude Meetings beta, putting AI inside Google Meet

Category: AI

Anthropic, the AI company preparing a public listing, has opened beta access to Claude Meetings, which joins Google Meet calls as a visible participant and can draft plans and handle other tasks during meetings. Unlike tools that mainly transcribe calls for later summaries, Claude can answer questions in the meeting chat, retrieve information from connected work apps and draft timelines. Notes and action items remain available in Claude after the call [3][5][4]. The release also expands Claude’s workplace tools: Whiteboards organizes team ideas into editable plans, Sheets creates and edits collaborative spreadsheets, and Claude Data—the renamed Dashboards product—is generally available to paid subscribers. Updated interactive connectors let users open full versions of partner applications inside Claude, including tools from Adobe, Atlassian and HubSpot [6][5][23]. Meetings is initially available only for Google Meet and to customers on Claude Team and Enterprise plans. Anthropic says it plans to add other meeting platforms. The company is seeking to expand Claude’s use beyond software development, but product lead Nicholas Lin described Sheets as basic, with established spreadsheet programs better suited to advanced work [1][11][15].

Did you know: Anthropic had tested Claude Meetings internally for about two months, and product lead Nicholas Lin said it was participating in nearly all of the company’s meetings [1].

Key Points

  • Meeting safeguards: Claude identifies itself in the call, requires administrator approval for calls with outside guests and leaves automatically if an unapproved guest joins [5][25].
  • Spreadsheet limits: Claude Sheets supports up to 100,000 cells and 144 functions, but lacks pivot tables and protected ranges; mobile access is read-only [20].
  • Enterprise defaults: Claude Meetings is on by default for Team customers, while Enterprise administrators control access; Whiteboards and Sheets start disabled for Enterprise accounts [5][25].
  • Access exclusions: Organizations using zero data retention, HIPAA-ready configurations or customer-managed encryption keys cannot use Claude Meetings, according to Anthropic’s product information [25].
  • App integrations: The connector update initially supports 13 embedded partner services, including Adobe, Atlassian, Box, HubSpot and Notion [6].

Technical Details

  • Certified queries: Claude Data can display organization-approved query definitions as certified metrics; live-share viewers can see refreshed results without having access to the underlying data connector [6][25].
  • Connector permissions: Interactive connectors open partner applications within Claude, while access follows existing file permissions and administrators can limit the actions available to each connector [5].

Industry Impact

  • Office software: Claude Sheets and Whiteboards put Anthropic in competition with Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace for spreadsheet and collaborative-planning tasks [17].
  • Meeting assistants: Claude Meetings adds an in-call participant to a category that already includes tools from Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Webex, as well as specialist services such as Otter and Granola [5].
  • Enterprise applications: Anthropic’s updated connectors let users open and use partner apps within Claude [22][6].
