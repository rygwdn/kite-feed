Apple gives iOS 28 engineers two more weeks to develop features

Category: Technology

Apple has revised the first development milestone for iOS 28, iPadOS 28 and macOS 28 to give engineers six weeks to code and explore features, followed by two weeks for integration, according to Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman. Apple usually splits each eight-week milestone evenly between coding and “convergence,” when separate features are integrated into the operating system. The revised split keeps the first milestone at eight weeks overall [1][2][4][10]. The reported change applies only to the first milestone. Apple has not confirmed whether it will use the approach in later stages, and no iOS 28 features have been disclosed [5][4]. The next milestone is due to begin on Nov. 9, when Apple will start finalizing planned features. Apple is expected to preview iOS 28 in June 2027 and release it publicly in September [2][11][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Employees say they like the change, indicating that it creates more time for coming up with ideas and exploring them before needing to lock things in.

— Mark Gurman, Bloomberg reporter (MacRumors)
