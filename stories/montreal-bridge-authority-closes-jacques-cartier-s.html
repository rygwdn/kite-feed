Montreal bridge authority closes Jacques Cartier span after fire

Category: Canada

A fire in a vacant industrial building near the Montreal approach to the Jacques Cartier Bridge led officials to shut the bridge to traffic Wednesday morning, disrupting the commuter link between the city and the South Shore [3][4][1]. Firefighters were called late Tuesday night, and the blaze spread through the three-storey structure near De Lorimier Avenue and Logan Street [4][2]. Montreal police later took over the investigation as the cause remained unknown, while the response showcased significant coordination among emergency services [2][1].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: The fire site was reported at the intersection of De Lorimier Avenue and Logan Street, right by the Montreal-side approach to the bridge [4].

Key Points

  • Large response: More than 150 firefighters were deployed to battle the flames, underscoring the scale of the overnight operation [1].
  • How it spread: Authorities said the fire began on the first floor and moved upward to the top floors of the building [4].
  • Timing details: Fire crews received the call around 11:30 p.m. Tuesday, with the bridge closure implemented early Wednesday as officials assessed safety [2][3].
  • Investigation lead: Montreal police assumed responsibility for the investigation into what sparked the blaze [2].
  • Partial reopening: CTV reported the Jacques-Cartier Bridge partially reopened later, easing pressure on regional traffic routes [5].
