US jet disables Panama-flagged cargo ship near Iran blockade

Category: World

A U.S. fighter jet struck the stern of the Panama-flagged cargo vessel M/V Ocean Molica, also known as the Arika Sun, in the Gulf of Oman on Oct. 10, disabling its propulsion after the crew ignored repeated warnings and tried to breach the U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports, U.S. Central Command said. CENTCOM said the ship had left an Iranian port and its crew was unharmed [2][5][10][7]. The strike is part of a blockade Washington resumed on July 14. CENTCOM said that during three months of enforcement, U.S. forces had disabled four commercial vessels, turned back 135 ships and destroyed 10 tankers it linked to Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps oil network [5][10]. Separately, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency said an outbound tanker in the Strait of Hormuz was hit by an unidentified projectile and caught fire. Its crew were reported safe, but the extent of the damage and environmental effects remained unclear. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard also claimed it struck a vessel for using an unauthorized route. CENTCOM urged mariners to heed blockade warnings and maintain contact with U.S. naval forces [5][10][23].
