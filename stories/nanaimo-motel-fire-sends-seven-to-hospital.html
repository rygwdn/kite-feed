Nanaimo motel fire sends seven to hospital

Category: Canada

A fire at a motel in Nanaimo, B.C., sent seven people to hospital for smoke inhalation, according to reports from local and national outlets [1][2][3]. The blaze also displaced residents, with reporting indicating dozens were left without a place to stay immediately afterward [2][3]. City officials and responders focused on getting people treated, safely out of the building, and connected to temporary housing supports in the aftermath [2][3].
