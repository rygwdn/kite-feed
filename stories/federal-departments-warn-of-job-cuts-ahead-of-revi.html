Federal departments warn of job cuts ahead of reviews

Category: Canada

Federal departments have begun issuing layoff warning notices to public servants, including thousands at Global Affairs Canada, before the department finishes a foreign policy review meant to set updated priorities [1]. A union representing affected staff says the sequencing raises questions about how decisions on staffing can be made before the review’s conclusions are known [1]. Separately, a union says the Canadian Food Inspection Agency plans to cut more than 1,300 jobs, as broader notices warning of potential eliminations continue across the federal public service [3]. The situation has prompted calls for clearer public communication about the scope and rationale of potential reductions, while departments prepare for organizational changes heading into 2026 [2].
