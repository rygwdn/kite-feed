Update: Calgary plans to ease water limits next week

Category: Canada

In the latest development, Calgary officials say they expect to start easing citywide water-use restrictions early next week after crews repaired a major water main break [1][2]. Even with the repair done, the city says the overall system is still under strain while the replaced section is brought back online, so residents are being asked to keep conserving for now [4]. City staff also caution that conservation measures are expected to return in the spring, when additional fixes that can’t be completed during winter are scheduled to begin [1].

  • Refill underway: City infrastructure staff said the next step involves filling the replacement pipe with enough water to equal about nine Olympic-sized swimming pools, a process that takes time even after the break itself is fixed [2].
  • System still strained: Officials said Calgary is “not out of the woods yet,” describing ongoing pressure on the network as crews work through the broader restoration of a critical feeder main after the northwest break [4].
  • Spring work ahead: Calgary officials said some pipe repairs can’t be done in winter, and they plan to restart conservation efforts in spring when that deferred work begins [1].
