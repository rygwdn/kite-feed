Court records detail RCMP confusion after Tumbler Ridge killings

Category: Canada

Newly released search-warrant documents show RCMP officers were initially uncertain whether one or more shooters were involved in the Feb. 10 mass shooting at Tumbler Ridge Secondary School in British Columbia after police received different names connected with 18-year-old suspect Jesse Van Rootselaar [1][2][5][6]. The records were released this month after a court action by a media consortium and include heavily redacted information-to-obtain materials filed by police to seek search warrants [2][5]. Van Rootselaar is suspected of killing five high school students, a teacher and two family members in Tumbler Ridge, according to search-warrant materials described by the Vancouver Sun and Yahoo News Canada [2]. The documents describe the first police response at the school and the investigation at the family home, but redactions removed exact call and response times, making a precise minute-by-minute timeline difficult to reconstruct [2][5].

Key Points

  • Police arrival: The warrant materials say the suspect fired at police when they arrived, and RCMP Sgt. Wahnese Antonioni-Stevens entered the school and saw a young boy who appeared to be dead on the stairs [2].
  • Home scene: La Presse, citing the documents, reported that police found the suspect's mother and brother at the family home near shotgun shells scattered on the floor and an empty shotgun [5].
  • Redaction scale: The released package included hundreds of pages across 11 documents, including one as long as 110 pages, with more than half the information blacked out in some instances [2].
  • Search targets: RCMP sought warrants for Van Rootselaar's family home, a family vehicle and several electronic devices seized from the residence [2].
