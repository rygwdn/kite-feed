Samsung sets Feb. 25 event for Galaxy S26

Category: Technology

A fresh wave of leaks suggests Samsung will hold its first Galaxy Unpacked event of 2026 on February 25, where it’s expected to unveil the Galaxy S26 lineup (S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra) [1][2][14][15][12]. As of January 30, that date has not been formally announced by Samsung in the reporting cited here, but multiple outlets describe it as strongly indicated by an invite-style image shared by leaker Evan Blass [1][2][14]. On hardware, the clearest theme is continuity: several sources say the S26 phones look broadly similar to the prior generation, with flat fronts and a familiar overall silhouette, while changes are expected to focus on internals like chips and camera components rather than a dramatic exterior redesign [2][13][8]. That incremental approach can be good news for buyers who value refinement—especially if the rumored camera upgrades around zoom video translate into steadier, clearer clips for everyday moments like concerts and school performances [10][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Several leaked render sets emphasize very thin, uniform bezels and a centered hole-punch selfie camera, a layout that has become a standard design approach for many premium Android phones [11][common].

Key Points

  • Design cues: Leaked renders across outlets show a triple-camera stack with a raised camera island that some reports note could make the phone wobble on a table [11][7].
  • Chip split: Some leaks point to Samsung using Exynos 2600 in Europe while Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 appears in other markets [12][2].
  • Base specs: One leak set describes a 6.3-inch fullHD+ Dynamic AMOLED screen, a 256GB storage option, and a 4,300mAh battery for the Galaxy S26 [12].
  • Zoom video: Tipster Ice Universe says Samsung is targeting smoother 5x periscope zoom video, with one report citing a possible aperture change from f/3.4 to f/2.9 to bring in more light [10][6].
  • Color options: Android Headlines’ leaked Ultra renders depict two finishes labeled Cobalt Violet and Black, and they also show the S Pen alongside the device [11].

Technical Details

  • Periscope telephoto: A periscope telephoto camera uses folded optics to achieve longer zoom (here, 5x) while keeping the phone relatively thin; multiple leaks focus on this lens because zoom video has historically been harder to stabilize well [10][common].
  • Aperture (f-number): A lower f-number means a wider aperture that can capture more light; one report says the S26 Ultra’s 5x lens may move from f/3.4 to f/2.9 to improve low-light zoom video quality [10].
  • System-on-chip: Leaks describe a split between Samsung’s Exynos 2600 and Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 depending on region, which can influence performance, battery behavior, and modem features [12][2].

Industry Impact

  • Creator workflows: If the rumored 5x zoom video improvements hold up, phones could better cover distant subjects (concerts, sports, presentations) without as much jitter, potentially reducing reliance on dedicated cameras for casual creators [10][6].
  • Accessory ecosystem: Ongoing uncertainty around Qi2 support (not resolved in the leak roundups) could affect third-party charger and magnetic accessory planning until Samsung confirms final specs [9].
  • Chip supply: A reported Exynos/Snapdragon regional split implies Samsung will again balance in-house silicon and external supply, affecting component sourcing strategies and potentially regional performance comparisons [12][2].

Historical Background

Samsung has used its Galaxy Unpacked events to introduce new Galaxy S-series flagships on an annual cadence [common]. Several reports note Unpacked events have often happened in January in recent years, making a rumored shift to late February a notable scheduling change for 2026 coverage and buying timelines [1].

Q&A

Q: Will the Galaxy S26 Ultra still include an S Pen slot?

A: Leaked renders show an S Pen alongside the Galaxy S26 Ultra, suggesting continued stylus support, though Samsung has not confirmed it in the reporting here [11].

Q: What colors are rumored for the Galaxy S26 Ultra?

A: One set of leaked Ultra renders labels two finishes as Cobalt Violet and Black [11].

Q: What’s the main physical downside mentioned in the leaked designs?

A: Some reports note the raised camera island could cause tabletop wobble when the phone sits flat [11][7].

Action Items

  • Wait for the official Unpacked invite details: If you’re planning an upgrade, hold off on buying until Samsung confirms the event time/time zone and final specs for your region, since current details are leak-based [2][1].
  • Check your regional chipset before pre-ordering: When Samsung posts official product pages, verify whether your market gets Exynos 2600 or Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, as leaks suggest a regional split [12][2].
  • If you shoot zoom video, plan a quick store test: Bring a known “stress test” scenario (dim indoor zoom, moving subject) to compare 5x video stabilization and detail between your current phone and the S26 Ultra once demo units appear [10][common].
