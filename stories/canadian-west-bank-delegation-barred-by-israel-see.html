Canadian West Bank delegation barred by Israel seeks sanctions

Category: Canada

Organizers of a Canadian delegation say Israeli authorities denied their group entry to the West Bank earlier this month, and they are now urging the federal government to strengthen Canada’s sanctions aimed at newly established Israeli settlements in occupied territory [1][4][3]. The delegation’s organizers describe the trip as an effort to meet with displaced Palestinians and see conditions on the ground [4]. They argue that a tougher Canadian response would align with the United Nations’ view that these settlements are illegal under international law [3].

Key Points

  • MPs included: The group included six Canadian MPs who were turned away at the Allenby border crossing on Dec. 16, according to organizers [4].
