Anthropic co-founders disclosed $560 million in pre-IPO share sales

Category: AI

Anthropic co-founders Dario Amodei, the chief executive, and Daniela Amodei, the company’s president and board chair, each sold 3.3784 million shares at the $83 initial public offering price, worth about $560 million combined, according to filings cited by BigGo [6]. Shares in the Claude developer closed at $82.79 on Nov. 25, below the offer price five trading sessions after its record $81.3 billion IPO [11][12]. Anthropic’s third-quarter revenue reached $16.96 billion, up from $1.38 billion a year earlier, while the company recorded a $567 million operating loss [10]. Computing and infrastructure expenses rose $4.3 billion in the quarter as revenue increased $5.4 billion from the previous quarter [10]. The company has roughly $900 billion in long-term computing and infrastructure commitments and faces a series of share lockup expirations. Up to 303 million shares could become eligible for sale on Dec. 15, and Anthropic is expected to release its first results as a public company early next year [10][12][6].

Key Points

  • Customer growth: The number of Anthropic customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue rose from more than 950 to over 11,900 in a year; more than 1,900 customers were spending at least $1 million annually by the third quarter [10].
  • Usage-based sales: About 79% of Anthropic’s revenue in the first nine months of 2026 came from usage-based charges, leaving sales exposed if customers reduce token consumption or switch to cheaper models [10].
  • Discount pressure: Price cuts and customer discounts introduced in the second and third quarters reduced revenue by about $600 million, as Anthropic competed with ChatGPT, Gemini and other models [10][17].
  • IPO float: About 11% of Anthropic’s shares were freely tradable at listing, compared with less than 5% in SpaceX’s IPO, according to BigGo’s comparison [6].
  • Sector strain: The Nov. 26 selloff also hit major AI-linked firms: Nvidia fell 8.5%, while CoreWeave and Nebius each dropped more than 10% [15].

Perspectives

Chris Beauchamp, IG Group’s chief market analyst, said attention after an IPO typically shifts from hype to scrutiny of financial performance, growth prospects and valuation [6].

Industry Impact

  • Cloud and chip suppliers: Anthropic’s infrastructure relies on providers including Amazon, Google and Microsoft for computing capacity and on Nvidia and AMD for chips, while some of those firms also compete in AI or hold investments in the company [17].
  • AI infrastructure finance: CoreWeave’s 2031 bond yield approached 18% as its price fell near record lows, while credit-default swap spreads for Oracle, Alphabet, SpaceX and CoreWeave reached records [15].

Historical Background

Nine large U.S. technology listings that fell at least 10% below their first-day closing price within six sessions later fell at least 33.9% from that close within two years [12]. Only three of those nine were above their first-day close after one year, and the median return was a loss of 23.9% [12]. The comparison is limited: the cases span different market periods, and Anthropic’s roughly $1.11 trillion valuation at the time exceeded those of the other companies at listing [12].

Business Angle

  • Anthropic’s adjusted operating margin fell from 19% in the second quarter to 15% in the third, mainly due to $5 billion in costs associated with short-term computing-capacity deals [10].
  • The company reported net revenue retention of about 900% at the end of September, but the calculation includes customers who generated as little as $1 in revenue in the prior-year quarter [10].
  • Separate from scheduled lockup expirations, future secondary share issuances could reduce existing shareholders’ ownership stakes [10].

Q&A

Q: What will investors look for in Anthropic’s first public-company earnings report?

A: They will be watching whether sales are running above the $68 billion annualized pace recorded in September and whether revenue growth can outpace computing costs [8][11].
