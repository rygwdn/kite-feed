B.C. crews demolish school after Tumbler Ridge shooting

Category: Canada

Crews have begun demolishing Tumbler Ridge Secondary School in northern British Columbia, where a deadly mass shooting took place earlier in 2026 [1][2][4][5][6]. Residents gathered in Tumbler Ridge on Monday, Aug. 17, as crews tore down the first wing of the former school [4]. The attack was the Feb. 10 shooting at the school [6], which killed six people and injured 27 [5]. Le Devoir reported that those killed were five children and an educational assistant [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Site plan: CBC reported that demolition and removal are expected to be complete by fall, with the cleared site to become a grass field [4].
  • First wing: The first section being demolished housed the former school’s auto shop and trades area, according to CBC [4].
  • Attack sequence: The Edmonton Journal reported that Jesse Van Rootselaar killed his mother and brother before heading to the school [5].
