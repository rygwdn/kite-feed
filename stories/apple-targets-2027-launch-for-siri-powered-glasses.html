Apple targets 2027 launch for Siri-powered glasses tied to iPhones

Category: Technology

Apple is preparing its first smart glasses for a possible unveiling at its June 2027 developers’ conference, with shipments before year-end. The product would center on Apple’s Siri assistant and iPhone app integration [10][3][8]. The glasses, internally known as N50, are not expected to have an augmented-reality display or run third-party apps themselves; a paired recent iPhone would run those apps and handle many requests. Apple has not announced the product or confirmed its launch schedule [12][21]. Planned features include cameras that let Siri answer questions about the wearer’s surroundings, translate signs and conversations, and handle tasks in supported iPhone apps, such as booking a ride or restaurant table. Apple is expanding Siri’s App Intents framework in iOS 27.3 so developers can make app functions available to the assistant without building separate glasses apps [5][3]. Prototype reports describe frames slightly thinner than Meta’s second-generation Ray-Ban glasses, flush-mounted camera openings, Wi-Fi and a light indicating activity or charging. Software and AI development are reportedly behind schedule, putting the 2027 targets at risk. Apple plans to add support for its own apps in December and open third-party integrations in 2027 [15][21][6].

Key Points

  • Phone limitation: The glasses are expected to require a recent iPhone and will not support Android; Wi-Fi may preserve some functions, such as audio playback, when the phone is not nearby [3][23].
  • Audio features: Prototypes reportedly support calls, music playback and FaceTime audio, with employees describing the sound quality as “industry-leading” [3][8].
  • Related device: Apple is also developing camera-equipped AirPods for late 2027, another product intended to feed environmental information to Siri [3][7].

Timeline

  • 2024: Apple first demonstrated Siri’s expanded ability to act within apps [20]
  • 3 November 2026: Apple introduced the Siri App Intents developer preview in iOS 27.3 [5]
  • December 2026: Apple plans to expand Siri support for its own apps [6]
  • spring 2027: Apple plans to accept App Store submissions using expanded Siri integrations [5]
  • June 2027: Apple is targeting its developers’ conference to unveil the glasses [8][3]

Technical Details

  • Camera processing: Prototype reports describe a dedicated engine for analyzing visual information in real time, alongside custom components for wireless connectivity [14][7].
  • On-device architecture: Prototype reports describe a reduced version of visionOS 28 and a processor derived from older Apple Watch chips [14][7].

Industry Impact

  • AI-glasses competition: Meta held 81.3% of global AI-glasses shipments in the first half of 2026, while Samsung and Google are introducing products based on Google’s Gemini assistant [32].

Historical Background

Apple began developing the glasses in the early 2020s, according to reporting on the project [15]. The product was previously expected to debut in late 2026 before the reported target moved to 2027 [15].

Business Angle

  • Competing integrations: Meta already offers Spotify and Audible integrations, while Google has demonstrated Gemini completing tasks such as booking rides and preparing DoorDash orders [23].

Q&A

Q: What privacy measures might Apple include?

A: Apple is reportedly testing additional privacy features on the front of the frames, but reports do not detail them or confirm what will reach the final product [21][15].

Q: How will the glasses charge?

A: Some prototypes use a flat, three-pin magnetic connector near the tip of the left temple, allowing charging while worn. It remains unclear whether Apple will keep it in the shipping product [3][14].
