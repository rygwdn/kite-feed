Israel marks Oct. 7 anniversary as Gaza strikes and election debate continue

Category: World

Israel marked the third anniversary of the Hamas-led Oct. 7 attack on Wednesday with memorials and protests, while Israeli strikes in Gaza killed at least three people, Gaza officials said. It was the first anniversary since all hostages and the remains of those who died had been returned, but fighting has continued despite a ceasefire that began in October 2025 [16][22][34][5]. The commemorations renewed demands for an independent inquiry into the security failures that allowed the attack. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has rejected personal responsibility and resisted an inquiry. The issue is central to campaigning ahead of Israel’s Oct. 27 election, the first since the attack [12][1][10]. The war that followed has killed more than 74,000 Palestinians, according to Gaza’s Health Ministry, and displaced most of Gaza’s population. In Israel, memorials at sites including the Nova festival and Kfar Aza were marked by grief and public anger over unanswered questions about how the attack happened [36][31][19].

Before the attack, female surveillance soldiers stationed along the Gaza border had raised concerns with superiors about possible preparations for an attack, according to the BBC [12]. NPR cited people with direct knowledge who said warnings also came from the UAE leader, Egypt’s intelligence chief and Hamas’ leader; Netanyahu denies receiving advance warning [28]. Israel’s military investigation into the response at Be’eri found that the initial operation lacked coordination and command, leaving residents largely on their own for seven hours [3].
