Meta launches enterprise AI platform, hires MongoDB chief

Category: AI

Meta announced the Meta Enterprise Platform on Sept. 28, a new business unit that will sell AI products and services to companies and developers. The unit will combine Meta’s Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API, Muse Code and other tools. MongoDB chief executive Chirantan “CJ” Desai will lead the effort as chief enterprise platform officer and report to Mark Zuckerberg [3][10][17]. Meta said the platform will turn its AI systems and infrastructure into products businesses can deploy. The company has invested substantially in AI technology [1][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Over the coming years, AI will fundamentally redefine how organizations of all sizes innovate, grow, serve customers, and run business operations

— Chirantan “CJ” Desai (TechCrunch)

Key Points

  • MongoDB transition: MongoDB named former chief executive Dev Ittycheria interim CEO while its board searches for Desai’s permanent replacement [3][9].
  • Share reaction: MongoDB shares closed down 17% after Desai’s departure was announced, after falling as much as 27% during the session [3][13].
  • Desai’s background: Before leading MongoDB, Desai held product and engineering leadership roles at Cloudflare and spent nearly eight years at ServiceNow [13][9].

Perspectives

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg: Zuckerberg said Meta’s experience helping millions of advertisers and hundreds of millions of businesses scale, alongside its models, agents and infrastructure, gives it a distinctive position in enterprise AI [4][10].

Sources: Gizmodo

Piper Sandler: The brokerage called Desai’s departure disappointing but said MongoDB remained strategically well-positioned and judged the share-price reaction overdone [9].
