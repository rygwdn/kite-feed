Apple delays smart glasses reveal to WWDC 2027

Category: Technology

Apple plans to unveil its first smart glasses at WWDC in June 2027, with sales expected before the end of that year, according to reports citing Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman [2][4][8][19]. The device, internally codenamed N50, had previously been expected to appear in late 2026 and launch in early 2027, but Apple pushed the schedule back as it works through privacy questions tied to camera-equipped eyewear [12][13][24][38]. Apple has not officially announced the product, launch date or features [18]. The privacy work centers on whether the glasses should include cameras, and if so, what they should be allowed to do [4][5][9]. Apple has reportedly considered versions with no cameras, versions whose cameras support AI context but cannot save photos or video, and versions with capture functions paired with added safeguards [4][8][15]. Reported safeguards include recording indicators, disabling camera use if an indicator is covered or altered, on-device processing, avoiding facial recognition and not using recorded videos to train AI models [6][13][14][29]. Apple’s work follows public concern over smart glasses being used to record people without clear consent in restaurants, gyms, streets and other public places [2][13][11]. Meta’s Ray-Ban glasses already use a capture light, but users have found ways to hide or remove it, and Meta says it disables cameras when wearers try to remove or block the light [2][7][13].

Key Points

  • Feature set: Apple’s N50 glasses are expected to support hands-free Siri access, music playback, phone calls and AI-powered camera features for understanding surroundings [13][17][24].
  • No display: The first-generation glasses are not expected to include an AR display and would rely instead on cameras, speakers and microphones [15][24].
  • Developer timing: A WWDC reveal would give developers several months to build apps and optimize experiences before a consumer launch later in 2027 [19][34].
  • Market momentum: Meta’s Ray-Ban smart glasses have sold millions of units, while multiple tech giants prepare competing products [21][3].

Timeline

  • 2026: N50 had been expected to be revealed in late 2026 [12]
  • June 2027: Apple now targets WWDC 2027 for the smart glasses reveal [2]
  • 2027: Retail availability is expected before the end of 2027 [4]

Technical Details

  • Dual-camera design: Prototypes include two camera sensors, one for photo and video capture and another for computer vision tasks, so the glasses could provide AI context without using the capture camera. [24]
  • Next-generation privacy hardware: Apple is developing new hardware-level privacy features not found in any current Apple products. [17][19]

Industry Impact

  • Smart glasses makers: Apple’s reported entry would put it in competition with Meta’s Ray-Ban glasses and Samsung’s planned eyewear partnership with Warby Parker and Gentle Monster [2][3].
  • Privacy compliance: Smart glasses restrictions have been reported in courts, schools, hospitals, gyms and casinos, which could limit where camera-equipped eyewear can be used [35][34].

Historical Background

Apple introduced the Vision Pro at WWDC 2023 before releasing it later; reports compare that sequence with the expected smart glasses rollout [19][36]. Apple has also built privacy into its public brand positioning for more than a decade, including its refusal to unlock an iPhone for the FBI in a case it framed as a civil liberties issue [8].

Business Angle

  • Apple has reportedly shelved plans for a cheaper Vision Pro to focus resources on smart glasses. [11]
  • The global smart glasses market is projected to reach $40 billion. [26]
  • Meta’s Ray-Ban glasses have already sold millions of pairs. [21]
