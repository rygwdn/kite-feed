Apple iPhone 18 Pro rumors point to September launch

Category: Technology

Apple is expected to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and a foldable “iPhone Ultra” in the first half of September, with sales expected in the second half of the month, according to MacRumors [4]. Reports say Apple may move the regular iPhone 18, a lower-end iPhone 18e and a second-generation iPhone Air to around March 2027, splitting the lineup across two launch windows [4][3][5]. The rumored Pro updates include a smaller Dynamic Island, LTPO+ displays, a variable-aperture 48-megapixel main camera, Apple’s C2 modem with 5G over satellite, an N2 wireless chip and an A20 Pro processor built on TSMC’s 2-nanometer process [4][5][6]. CNET said high RAM prices and memory shortages could affect specifications and pricing, while 9to5Mac said price increases for the iPhone 18 Pro lineup are “inevitable” [3][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Battery change: The iPhone 18 Pro Max is rumored to become slightly thicker to make room for a battery with about 10% more capacity, while LTPO+ display technology could also reduce power draw [6][4].
  • Color lineup: Rumored finishes include light blue, dark cherry and dark gray variants, while CNYES said Apple may drop the iPhone 17 Pro’s deep orange and deep blue options [3][6].
  • Camera control: Apple may redesign Camera Control to reduce accidental activations, with CNYES describing a simplified physical button that keeps pressure-sensitive touch operation [5][6].
