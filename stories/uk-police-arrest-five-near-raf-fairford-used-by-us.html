UK police arrest five near RAF Fairford, used by U.S. forces

Category: World

British police arrested five men near RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire on Sept. 27 on suspicion of explosives offenses and preparing a terrorist act. Officers responded to a call at about 12:45 a.m. reporting three vehicles apparently heading toward the base, which the U.S. Air Force has used during the war with Iran [8][4][5]. Army bomb-disposal specialists examined the vehicles, and police established a 400-meter cordon as about 85 households in nearby Whelford were evacuated [4][10][54]. The Guardian reported that investigators were examining materials found in the vans and initially believed they were consistent with improvised explosive devices; police had not publicly identified a motive [12][19].

Historical Background

The U.S. Air Force has used RAF Fairford under a U.K.-U.S. agreement dating to the 1950s [7]. The base supported U.S. operations during the Gulf War and the 2003 Iraq war [7].
