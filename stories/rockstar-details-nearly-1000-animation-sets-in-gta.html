Rockstar details nearly 1,000 animation sets in GTA VI

Category: Technology

Rockstar Games says it built close to 1,000 animation sets for everyday background activities in Grand Theft Auto VI, alongside custom sequences and one-off events, to make the game world feel inhabited [42]. The open-world crime game began its phased release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on Nov. 19, returning the series to Vice City after a 13-year gap between main installments [15][76]. Neowin reported that heavy launch demand appeared to take Sony’s PlayStation Network offline for several hours. Separately, a group called Cyberleek claimed it had leaked a 209GB Xbox build, but the file lacked the main executable and was not a confirmed playable copy [36]. Players also posted clips of glitches, including a character falling through a prison floor and a ship briefly moving sideways over water [8].
