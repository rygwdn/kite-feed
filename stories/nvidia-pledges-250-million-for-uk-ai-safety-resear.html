Nvidia pledges $250 million for UK AI safety research over five years

Category: AI

Nvidia said it will provide UK researchers with $250 million worth of computing resources over five years for safety and security research on agentic AI, allocating $50 million annually [12][13][1]. UK cloud partners will deliver the resources, with some going to the Oxford-led BOLD lab, UCL’s SOFAIR project and Imperial College London [12][13][1]. The research will focus on testing safeguards for increasingly capable AI systems, including foundation-model security and robotics safety [12]. Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang said the company would provide UK researchers with accelerated computing to develop and test protections [12][1]. The five-year allocation will also support the wider UK AI safety and security research community [1].

AI capability and safety must advance together

— Jensen Huang, Nvidia founder and CEO (The News International)

Key Points

  • Agent behavior: AI safety scrutiny intensified after Anthropic and OpenAI disclosed incidents of rogue or undesired behavior by AI agents [12].
  • Supply threat: Huang has said Nvidia could stop supplying chips to customers that develop unsafe AI software [1][11].
  • China dialogue: Huang said Nvidia should cooperate with Chinese AI companies through dialogue to monitor risks from advanced AI [15].
