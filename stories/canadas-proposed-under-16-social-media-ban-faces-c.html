Canada’s proposed under-16 social media ban faces Charter scrutiny

Category: Canada

Canada’s federal government is likely to face a constitutional challenge over its proposed ban on social media use by children under 16, legal experts cited by The Canadian Press said [2][3][4][5]. Dalhousie University associate law professor Michael Karanicolas said a Charter challenge to the law is almost inevitable [2][3][4][5]. The proposal is still moving through the parliamentary process [4]. The Canadian debate follows a French Constitutional Council decision on Friday that struck down France’s social media ban for children under 15, finding it too broad and an infringement on freedom of expression [2][4]. Canada is among dozens of countries examining social media age restrictions after Australia became the first country to ban children’s access to social media in December 2025 [4].
