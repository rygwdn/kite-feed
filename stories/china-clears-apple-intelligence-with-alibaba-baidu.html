China clears Apple Intelligence with Alibaba, Baidu as partners

Category: AI

China's Cyberspace Administration approved or registered Apple Intelligence for use in the country, removing a regulatory hurdle that had delayed Apple's generative AI features in China [3][5][6]. The China version of Apple Intelligence will use Alibaba's Qwen model across iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS, while Baidu said it is working with Apple on Apple Intelligence features for Chinese users [3][6][8]. Apple's local partners give it a route to offer AI features in a market where overseas models such as ChatGPT cannot legally operate in mainland China [6][7]. Apple generated $20.5 billion in Greater China sales in the second quarter, up 28% from a year earlier [3][6]. The regulator did not give a launch date [6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Baidu role: Baidu is developing AI-based search that can process images and text and is expected to support upgrades to the China version of Siri [8][7].
  • Device scope: One report said the listed approval applied specifically to iPhone, without clarifying whether Apple Intelligence would also be allowed on iPad or Mac in China [9].
  • Market reaction: Alibaba and Baidu shares both rose more than 3% after reports that their models would be used inside Apple's China AI ecosystem [7].

Perspectives

Alibaba: Alibaba said its Qwen models would be integrated into Apple Intelligence experiences and would support capabilities including text and image understanding and generation.

Sources: TechCrunch

Baidu: A Baidu spokesperson confirmed the company is working with Apple to develop Apple Intelligence features for Chinese users.

Sources: TechCrunch

Apple: Apple did not immediately respond to a request for comment after the approval was reported [6].

Sources: Beijing Bulletin

Industry Impact

  • Chinese smartphone AI: The same approval batch included Samsung’s Galaxy AI and AI services from Huawei, Oppo, Vivo, Xiaomi and ZTE [9].
  • Local AI providers: Apple’s China rollout gives Alibaba’s Qwen and Baidu’s AI features placement inside Apple’s operating systems for Chinese users [3][8].
  • Apple ecosystem: Alibaba said the integration spans iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro operating systems in China, though one report said the regulatory approval notice referred only to iPhone [6][9].

Historical Background

Apple Intelligence debuted in 2024 but was not available in China while Apple awaited regulatory clearance for generative AI features [3]. Reports had linked Apple to talks with Alibaba and Baidu before the approval [3].

Business Angle

  • Apple had been selling China iPhones without the AI features it markets elsewhere; no launch date has been announced [6].
  • Alibaba and Baidu gain placement inside Apple’s ecosystem, though the sources disclosed no contract economics [3].
  • Maintaining separate AI stacks for China and other markets can increase software complexity and compliance costs over time [7][common].
