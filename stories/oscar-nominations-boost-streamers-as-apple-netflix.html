Oscar nominations boost streamers as Apple, Netflix shine

Category: Technology

The Academy unveiled nominations for the 98th Oscars, with Ryan Coogler’s “Sinners” setting a new record at 16 nods and landing across major categories [3][1][11][9]. The list also underscored how central streaming platforms have become to prestige filmmaking: Apple’s racing blockbuster “F1” made the Best Picture lineup, while Netflix earned Best Picture nominations for “Frankenstein” and “Train Dreams,” plus recognition for animated hit “KPop Demon Hunters” [3][12][13]. The ceremony is scheduled for March 15, 2026, with Conan O’Brien set to host and ABC carrying the live broadcast [3]. For viewers, many contenders are expected to be readily watchable via online services, turning awards season into a broad “streamathon” moment for film discovery [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Engadget reported that the Oscars broadcast will remain on ABC for now but is scheduled to move to YouTube in 2029 [3].

Key Points

  • Apple’s footprint: Apple said “F1” received six nominations in total, including Best Sound, Best Film Editing and Best Visual Effects, and noted the film grossed over $631 million worldwide during its theatrical run [12][13].
  • Netflix breadth: Netflix picked up Best Picture nominations for “Frankenstein” and “Train Dreams,” while “KPop Demon Hunters” landed nods for Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song [3].
  • Career milestone: Delroy Lindo earned his first-ever Oscar nomination for Best Supporting Actor after roughly 50 years in the industry, according to Engadget’s roundup [3].
  • Surprises and snubs: Mashable highlighted “Wicked: For Good” as a notable omission after drawing attention at other awards shows, while also pointing to unexpected nomination outcomes across the slate [mashable.com#6][mashable.com#7][5].
  • Viewing logistics: TechRadar published a guide on how to stream all 10 Best Picture nominees “from anywhere,” reflecting how distribution and tech access shape modern awards-season viewing [2].

Technical Details

  • Best Picture lineup: Best Picture nominees include “F1,” “Sinners,” “One Battle After Another,” “Frankenstein,” “Hamnet,” “Marty Supreme,” “Bugonia,” “Sentimental Value,” “The Secret Agent,” and “Train Dreams” [12][11].
  • Technical categories: Apple’s “F1” also received nominations in craft/technical categories—Sound, Film Editing, and Visual Effects—illustrating how blockbuster filmmaking often competes strongly in these fields [12][13].
  • Streaming distribution: TechRadar’s watch guide reflects a technology reality of awards season: many nominees become available via streaming subscriptions or digital rentals, and access can vary by region [2].

Industry Impact

  • Streaming competition: With Apple and Netflix both placing films in Best Picture, the nominations spotlight how streamers compete not only on libraries and price but also on awards-driven prestige and creator relationships [3][12][13].
  • Theatrical strategy: Apple’s “F1” pairing of major box-office performance with awards recognition may encourage more hybrid release strategies that combine cinemas for revenue and visibility with streaming for long-tail viewing [12].
  • Global animation: Recognition for “KPop Demon Hunters” in Animated Feature and Original Song underscores the growing mainstream reach of international animation distributed via global platforms [3].

Historical Background

Apple TV+ became the first streaming service to win Best Picture with “CODA” in 2022, and it later earned a Best Picture nomination for “Killers of the Flower Moon” in 2024, setting context for “F1” joining the Best Picture lineup in 2026 [15]. Separately, the Academy continues to evolve its awards structure, with Mashable noting Casting as a newly introduced category in this year’s nominations [5].

Q&A

Q: Which categories besides Best Picture did Apple’s “F1” land in?

A: MacRumors reports “F1” also earned nominations for Best Sound, Best Film Editing and Best Visual Effects [12].

Q: Which Apple titles besides “F1” received nominations?

A: MacRumors says the documentary “Come See Me in the Good Light” received a Best Documentary Feature nomination, and “The Lost Bus” was nominated for Best Visual Effects [12].

Q: Who announced this year’s nominations in the live presentation?

A: Mashable reports Danielle Brooks and Lewis Pullman announced the nominations from the Academy’s Samuel Goldwyn Theater [9].
