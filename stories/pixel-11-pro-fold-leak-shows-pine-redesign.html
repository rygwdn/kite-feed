Pixel 11 Pro Fold leak shows Pine redesign

Category: Technology

A render shared by Mystic Leaks on Telegram appears to show Google’s Pixel 11 Pro Fold in a “Pine” colorway, weeks before Google’s Made by Google event scheduled for August 12 in New York [1][2][4][5][6]. The shade appears to be a muted or darker green paired with a light gold frame, and the same color is expected for the Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL [1][4][5]. The leaked image shows a familiar foldable design with changes to the rear camera area: a narrower or cleaner camera island, camera cutouts closer to the edge, and a larger flash inside the pill-shaped module rather than in a separate strip [1][4][5][6]. Reports also noted that the rumored Pixel Glow notification feature is not clearly visible in the render, leaving open whether Google hid it behind a cover, integrated it with the flash, or omitted it from this model [1][6].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Modem shift: An FCC filing hints that Google is replacing Samsung's Exynos modem with MediaTek's M90 in the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, potentially bringing faster 5G and satellite connectivity. [8]
  • Next-gen performance: The Pixel 11 Pro Fold is expected to deliver faster speeds and better battery life, powered by a new 2nm Tensor G6 chip. [7]
  • Slimmer design: The latest render suggests a slightly thinner chassis than the Pixel 10 Pro Fold. [5]

Perspectives

9to5Google: The site observed that the back of the Pixel 11 Pro Fold looks nearly identical to the Pixel 10 Pro Fold, with only minor tweaks to the camera bar.

Sources: 9to5Google

Android Authority: The publication framed the leak as the clearest look yet at the Pine color and said the render matched earlier Pixel 11 Pro Fold images.

Sources: Android Authority

Android Central: The outlet highlighted the FCC filing as the strongest evidence yet of Google's modem switch, calling it the 'biggest modem upgrade yet' for the Pixel Fold line.

  • 12 August 2026: Google plans its Made by Google event in New York. [5]

Technical Details

  • MediaTek M90 modem: The reported modem would add up to 12Gbps 5G speeds, satellite connectivity, and dual active 5G SIM support, according to Android Central [8].
  • 2nm Tensor G6: The move to a 2nm process node represents a major shrink from previous mobile chips, enabling higher transistor density for improved performance and power efficiency. [7][common].

Industry Impact

  • Supplier impact: A move from Samsung's Exynos to MediaTek's M90 would shift a Pixel Fold component from Samsung to MediaTek and could reduce Samsung's component role in Google's Pixel line. [8]
  • Foldable competition: IntoMobile noted that Samsung is set to release its next foldables sooner than Google’s August 12 event, putting Google’s Pixel 11 Pro Fold launch into direct timing competition with Samsung’s foldable cycle [4].

Historical Background

Google’s Pixel Fold line competes in a foldable-phone market where Samsung has released several generations of Galaxy Z Fold devices [common]. Earlier Pixel foldables used Google Tensor chips and Samsung-linked modem hardware, while Android Central said Tensor G5 had already been reported as moving chip manufacturing to TSMC before the Pixel 11 Pro Fold’s expected modem change [8].
