xAI’s Grok returns gibberish for some users

Category: AI

xAI’s Grok chatbot returned nonsensical text to some users this week, including one response to a PDF-generation request that began, “match it without and your they and two for planets can practical and often cheese,” TechCrunch reported [1][2]. Users who spoke with TechCrunch said they were using Grok Lite and noticed the issue as early as Wednesday morning, while TechCrunch said it could not reproduce the problem and that it likely affected only a small subset of users [1][2]. TechCrunch reported that users posted complaints in Grok’s Reddit community and that refreshing a session often restored normal responses, though some users said the gibberish continued after repeated refreshes [1]. The issue appeared limited to direct queries on Grok.com, and the Grok account on X said Thursday morning that the “rare temporary generation glitch” did not show up as an incident on xAI’s official status page [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Source checks: TechCrunch and Gizmodo reported that one affected user, checking Grok’s sources, found links to reinforcement-learning research sites [1][2].
  • User ratings: Gizmodo cited a recent YouGov UK poll that gave Grok 24.6% positive user satisfaction, above only Siri’s 23.7% among major AI platforms [2].

Perspectives

Affected Grok users: Some users told TechCrunch the malfunction continued after multiple refreshes, while Reddit users posted examples such as a reply to the word “balls” that began, “app scale designs with that and to your horse choose batch,” and another that started with “people stable stage right professional on steel” [1][2].

Sources: TechCrunch, Gizmodo

xAI: TechCrunch said the company did not respond to its request for comment on the malfunction.

Sources: TechCrunch

Action Items

  • Recover a bad Grok session: Start a fresh chat or regenerate the answer, which Grok’s X account said usually clears the temporary glitch [1].
  • Check service status: Visit https://status.x.ai to see whether xAI lists an active Grok incident before troubleshooting your browser or account [1].
