UK Biobank data of 500,000 listed online

Category: Technology

UK ministers said de-identified health data linked to all 500,000 volunteers in the UK Biobank research project was advertised for sale in three listings on Alibaba. The material was later removed after the UK contacted Alibaba and the Chinese government [1][5][3][4][7]. Officials said they do not believe any sales took place and that the listings did not expose personally identifiable information [1][5][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: UK Biobank recruited around 500,000 volunteers in a relatively narrow age band of 40 to 69, one reason the project became unusually powerful for long-term health tracking [common].

Key Points

  • Three listings: The data appeared in three separate Alibaba listings before the company took them down, according to the government statement to Parliament [1][5].
  • Security response: Computer Weekly reported that Biobank’s operator is now strengthening security after biological, health and lifestyle information was offered for sale [4].
  • Marketplace role: The listings appeared on an Alibaba-owned platform, and the UK said it worked with both the company and Chinese authorities to get them removed [3][1].

Historical Background

UK Biobank launched in 2006 to build a long-term health resource using data from about 500,000 UK volunteers recruited between 2006 and 2010 [common]. Over time, it became a major dataset for large-scale biomedical research, especially studies that combine genetics, lifestyle and health records across a population [2][common].
