Meghan Markle's father charged in US with possessing child sexual abuse material

Category: World

Thomas Markle Sr., the 82-year-old estranged father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, was arrested in Lakeland, Florida, on Nov. 6 and charged with one federal count of possessing child sexual abuse material. A criminal complaint alleges he kept the material in two Los Angeles-area storage units he had rented since 2008. Agents seized about 78 items, including slides, film negatives and books. The complaint says some images appear to show Markle with underage girls. He denies the allegations and has not entered a plea [7][37][16]. After Markle’s initial appearance in Tampa, a judge released him on personal recognizance, subject to home detention and electronic monitoring. The judge also ordered a psychiatric examination to assess his ability to understand the proceedings and assist his lawyer. He may not travel outside specified parts of Florida and California. Prosecutors said they expected his case to move to California, with an arraignment likely several weeks away. A Florida news report listed Nov. 20 as a possible next court date [12][16][42]. The US investigation followed a report by his son, Thomas Markle Jr., who said he found suspected material among belongings at their father’s former home in Cebu, Philippines. Philippine authorities have reported finding thousands of images and a video on devices tied to the inquiry, while saying the findings do not establish who owned or distributed the material. Investigators are also examining a separate cache in Mexico [5][16].
