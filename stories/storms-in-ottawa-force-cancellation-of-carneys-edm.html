Storms in Ottawa force cancellation of Carney’s Edmonton Canada Day speech

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney canceled a planned Canada Day speech in Edmonton after thunderstorms in Ottawa prevented his plane from taking off on time, his spokesperson said [4][6][9]. Carney spoke earlier at the national ceremony at LeBreton Flats in Ottawa, where Canadians marked 159 years of Confederation [4]. The Edmonton appearance was expected to include a message about national unity as Albertans prepare for an Oct. 19 referendum and as separatist movements emerge in parts of the country [3][4][5][6]. In Ottawa, severe weather also led Canadian Heritage to cancel the national evening show and fireworks, citing public safety, site conditions, heavy rain, violent winds, lightning and a risk of hail [4][6].
