Canadiens beat Maple Leafs 4-2 as Fowler extends point streak

Category: Canada

The Montreal Canadiens defeated the Toronto Maple Leafs 4-2 in Toronto on Saturday, Nov. 7, as Alex Newhook scored twice and rookie goalie Jacob Fowler stopped 26 shots. Chris Kreider and Zachary Bolduc also scored for Montreal; William Nylander and rookie Gavin McKenna scored for Toronto. The Leafs cut a 3-0 deficit to one goal with two goals 55 seconds apart early in the third period, but Bolduc restored Montreal’s two-goal lead on a power play [8][7][6]. Montreal extended its point streak to 10 games and improved to 11-3-3, while Toronto fell to 8-8-2 after losing four of its past five games in regulation [8][2]. The Canadiens killed a late double-minor penalty assessed to Juraj Slafkovsky to secure the win. Toronto hosts the New Jersey Devils on Sunday, and Montreal hosts the Minnesota Wild on Tuesday [8][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Injury update: Maple Leafs defenceman Morgan Rielly left with a lower-body injury and did not return; captain John Tavares returned after missing nine games with a lower-body injury [2][7].
  • Power-play context: Montreal entered with the NHL’s 30th-ranked power play at 14% [7][10].
  • McKenna benched: Toronto benched its 18-year-old first-overall pick for the remainder of the second period after his intercepted pass led to Kreider’s goal [8][2].
  • Toronto’s homestand: The Maple Leafs are on a season-high six-game homestand, with Colorado, Minnesota and Edmonton among their upcoming opponents [3][15].

Perspectives

Maple Leafs coach Jim Hiller: Hiller called Rielly’s absence a big blow, praised his recent play and hoped the injury would not keep him out long [3].

Sources: Times Colonist (The Canadian Press)

Maple Leafs forward John Tavares: Tavares said every game mattered regardless of the opponent’s reputation and that Toronto needed to regroup after the loss [3].
