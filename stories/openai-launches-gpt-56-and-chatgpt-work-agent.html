OpenAI launches GPT-5.6 and ChatGPT Work agent

Category: AI

OpenAI released GPT-5.6 on July 9 as a three-model family called Sol, Terra and Luna, making it available across ChatGPT, Codex and the OpenAI API after a limited preview and a U.S. government security review [13][17][24][74]. Sol is the flagship model, Terra targets everyday work at lower cost, and Luna is the fastest and lowest-cost option, according to OpenAI and coverage of the launch [13][9][32][36]. The company also introduced ChatGPT Work, an agent inside ChatGPT that can act across connected apps, files and workflows to produce documents, spreadsheets, presentations, dashboards, reports and web apps [12][16][61][67]. OpenAI said the product is powered by GPT-5.6 and Codex technology, can stay with projects for hours when needed, and requests approval before sensitive actions [12][16][25]. The release followed a delay tied to U.S. national security concerns over advanced AI systems' coding, biology and cybersecurity capabilities; Sam Altman told CNBC that officials tested the model and OpenAI made "many changes" during the process [24][27][68]. The White House denies that the process amounts to formal approval and says companies submit models voluntarily for scrutiny [24][74].

Key Points

  • Tiered pricing: API pricing is $5 per million input tokens and $30 per million output tokens for Sol, $2.50/$15 for Terra, and $1/$6 for Luna [69][15][32].
  • App consolidation: The redesigned ChatGPT desktop app merges Chat, Work and Codex, while the older desktop app transitions to ChatGPT Classic [16][29][50].
  • User base: OpenAI engineer Thibault Sottiaux said during a livestream that ChatGPT has almost 1 billion users, while OpenAI said Codex has 5 million weekly users [29][41].
  • Higher-compute modes: GPT-5.6 adds Max for longer reasoning and Ultra, which coordinates four agents by default across parallel workstreams for demanding tasks [16][36][63].
  • Enterprise push: OpenAI said GPT-5.6 is now the preferred model in Microsoft 365 Copilot across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Chat and Cowork [11][1].
  • Security limits: OpenAI says GPT-5.6 has stronger biology and cybersecurity capabilities than earlier models but remains below its “Critical” preparedness threshold in both categories [36][28].

  • Voice upgrade: GPT-Live can delegate more complex requests to GPT-5.5 for search, reasoning or other work while keeping the spoken conversation active [77][6][18].

  • Full-duplex voice architecture: GPT-Live processes listening and speaking simultaneously, unlike the previous cascaded speech-to-text, language-model and text-to-speech pipeline that handled turns sequentially [77][6][95].
  • Context and output limits: Simon Willison reported that all three GPT-5.6 models have a February 16, 2026 knowledge cutoff, a 1 million-token context window and 128,000 maximum output tokens [15].
