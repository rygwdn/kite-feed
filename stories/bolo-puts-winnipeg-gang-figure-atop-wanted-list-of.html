BOLO puts Winnipeg gang figure atop wanted list, offers C$250,000

Category: Canada

The BOLO Program has placed Satinderjeet Singh, known as Goldy Brar, at the top of Canada’s 25 most-wanted list and is offering up to C$250,000 for information leading to his arrest. He faces first-degree murder and conspiracy charges in the 2023 killing of Sukhdool Singh Gill in Winnipeg. Police say Singh is also wanted in India, while U.S. prosecutors have charged him in a separate cross-border case [5][6][1]. The list was unveiled in Toronto with AI-generated, life-size animated depictions of the fugitives. It includes suspects wanted in cases outside Toronto, including Edmonton’s Devin Norgaard, who has 47 outstanding warrants for alleged firearms and drug offences, and two Ottawa men wanted in a Perth homicide [1][3][4]. Hours after the unveiling, police arrested Filmon Fesshaghirgis, No. 14 on the list, in Winnipeg in connection with a 2021 Bolton homicide [10].

Key Points

  • Edmonton fugitive: Devin Norgaard, 38, is the list’s only Alberta fugitive; he is described as 6-foot-2, 147 pounds, with a thin build, blue eyes and light-brown hair. Police say he may be armed and dangerous and advise the public not to approach him [3].
  • Perth case: Ontario Provincial Police are offering separate rewards of up to $50,000 for information leading to the arrests of Joseph Madore and Brayton Kennedy, wanted in the death of Christo Allison Richards [4].
  • Program record: Toronto police said 19 of the 28 Toronto cases featured by the BOLO initiative since 2018 have ended in arrests [1][6].

Perspectives

Phil Gurski, Canadian intelligence expert: Gurski said Brar’s alleged involvement in the Winnipeg killing would suggest the gang believes it can operate relatively freely in Canada; he also cautioned that fear of reprisals may stop people from sharing tips [5].

Winnipeg Police Service Deputy Chief Cam Mackid: Mackid said investigators believe Brar remains in Canada, but do not know where; he said police expect further arrests in the ongoing Gill homicide investigation [5][13].

BOLO Program: Executive director Max Langlois said the life-sized animated figures are intended to make fugitives recognizable in everyday settings, including shops and airports [1].

Q&A

Q: Who was arrested after the announcement?

A: Filmon Fesshaghirgis, wanted in a 2021 Bolton homicide, was arrested in Winnipeg by RCMP and local police. He faces three charges, including first-degree murder [10].

Q: What warning did police give about approaching the suspects?

A: Police said Brar and Karanbir Singh are considered armed and dangerous and should not be approached [5].
