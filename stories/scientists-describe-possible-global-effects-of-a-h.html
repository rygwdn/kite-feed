Scientists describe possible global effects of a hypothetical Yellowstone supereruption

Category: Science

A SciTechDaily article outlines a worst-case scenario for a Yellowstone supereruption, describing ash damage across parts of North America and disruption to critical systems. Possible longer-term effects include food shortages, economic turmoil, migration and years of volcanic cooling [1]. The article links the scenario to a series of immense eruptions about 631,000 years ago in what is now northwestern Wyoming. Ash spread across much of North America, while volcanic particles affected climate and weather worldwide [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Ash estimates: Discover Magazine says some cities could receive up to three feet of ash in the hypothetical scenario [2].
  • Near-term likelihood: Discover Magazine says a Yellowstone supervolcano eruption is not likely in the foreseeable future [2].
