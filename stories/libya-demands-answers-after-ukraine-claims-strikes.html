Libya demands answers after Ukraine claims strikes on Russian ships

Category: World

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy claimed Monday that Ukrainian forces used seven sea drones to sink the Russian corvette Merkury and strike the cargo ship MV Angara near Benghazi, Libya [2][10][27]. He said the Angara carried weapons and that a Russian Ka-27 helicopter was destroyed. Two Libyan officials told The Associated Press the corvette was heavily damaged, while satellite imagery showed it capsized [41][2]. Libya’s internationally recognized Government of National Unity, based in Tripoli, condemned foreign military operations on Libyan soil and demanded an explanation from Kyiv. The eastern administration, based in Benghazi and aligned with Russia, called for a U.N. investigation into alleged Ukrainian military activity in western Libya and said it would investigate the ship strikes jointly with Moscow [57][52][39]. The U.N. Support Mission in Libya called for an immediate, independent and transparent investigation, warning against actions that could draw Libya into a foreign conflict [61]. Tripoli also wants investigators to establish whether Libyan territory or facilities were used to plan or support the operation; the eastern administration named areas around Misrata and Zawiya in its allegations [54][56].

  • Intelligence preparation: Ukrainian special forces in Libya spent months monitoring Russian warships and commercial vessels, according to a Wall Street Journal report cited by Firstpost [14].
  • Port stopover: The eastern Libyan administration said the Merkury had stopped in Benghazi to refuel and resupply under a cooperation agreement with Russia [39][7].
  • Baltic incident: A suspected Ukrainian drone strike set the Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze in the Gulf of Finland on Nov. 21; all 16 crew members were evacuated, with one reported slightly injured [10][30].

Technical Details

  • Mother-ship deployment: Ukraine’s Security Service said sea drones were carried to a designated location and launched from a separate vessel, extending their reach beyond the Black Sea; officials have not disclosed the launch point [50][14].
  • Corvette defenses: The Merkury was reported to carry a Zaslon multifunction radar, TK-25-02 electronic-warfare system and Redut air-defense system, which can guide up to 16 missiles [36].

Historical Background

Libya has been divided since the 2011 overthrow of Muammar Gaddafi, with a U.N.-recognized government in Tripoli and a rival eastern administration backed by military commander Khalifa Haftar [20][61]. Haftar’s forces maintain close ties with Moscow, while Tripoli has cultivated relations with Ukraine and its Western allies [39]. Russia has sought to strengthen its military presence in Libya since the fall of Syrian President Bashar al-Assad in 2024 made its former hub in Syria less viable [52].

Humanitarian Impact

El País reported that 10 people were injured, including Russians and Libyans. Other reports said crews were rescued and described injuries as minor, while eastern Libyan authorities initially reported no casualties [23][25][35].
