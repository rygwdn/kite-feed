Update: Canada PM Carney to visit Tumbler Ridge

Category: World

Canada is mourning after a deadly shooting in the remote British Columbia community of Tumbler Ridge left eight people dead. The suspected shooter also died, according to multiple reports [3][11][6][1]. Authorities say the violence started at a private home and then moved to Tumbler Ridge Secondary School, rattling a small town where residents say nearly everyone knows someone affected [6][1][10]. Police have identified the suspect as 18-year-old Jesse Van Rootselaar and say they are still working to determine a motive [6][1][4][7]. On Feb. 13, Prime Minister Mark Carney plans to travel to Tumbler Ridge to meet residents and attend a vigil as the country grieves [4][7][8].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Victims remembered: Reports said the dead ranged in age from 11 to 39 and described several victims as children and a school staff member. Families and classmates have been sharing memories while planning vigils [11][8][5].
  • Families sought information: One account described relatives scrambling for answers in the hours after the attack, capturing the confusion and fear during the initial response [2].
  • Firearms previously seized and returned: Reporting citing officials said police seized firearms from the suspect’s home about two years ago but later returned them, raising questions about earlier intervention [14].
  • Questions on firearms: Police said the suspect’s firearms licence expired about two years ago, and reporting noted it remains unclear who owned the guns used in the attack [15][14].
  • Online activity: The New York Times reported the suspect’s online posts referenced mental health problems, substance abuse, and an interest in weapons and online violence, including violent extremism content [13].

Perspectives

Prime Minister Mark Carney: Carney plans to travel to Tumbler Ridge on Feb. 13 to meet mourners and attend a vigil. He has also invited leaders from all political parties to join him, according to reports.

Sources: The Guardian

Local residents: Residents described a tight-knit town where nearly everyone knows someone affected. Coverage also highlighted neighbors rallying around grieving families and students.

Sources: BBC News

Police and investigators: Authorities said they have not established a motive. They also described repeated prior police responses tied to concerns about the suspect’s mental health and weapons, as investigators continue to piece together what happened.
