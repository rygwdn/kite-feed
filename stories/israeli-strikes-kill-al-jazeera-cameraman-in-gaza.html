Israeli strikes kill Al Jazeera cameraman in Gaza

Category: World

Israeli strikes in Gaza on Saturday killed Al Jazeera cameraman Ahmed Wishah in the Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza, Al Jazeera said. Palestinian health officials and hospitals reported several other deaths across the enclave that day [5][2][12][1]. Al Jazeera said Wishah died in a strike on a house and identified him as a cameraman for Al Jazeera Mubasher; reports citing local officials said two other people were killed in the Bureij strike [5][2][12][46]. The Israeli military confirmed it carried out the strike and said Wishah was a Hamas member; some Israeli-linked accounts described him as a sniper operative. Several accounts noted that the military initially provided no evidence for the allegation [1][12][8][40][53]. Al Jazeera condemned what it called the deliberate killing of its employee and said Wishah was the 12th Al Jazeera media worker killed in Gaza since October 2023 [5][45][14]. Earlier Saturday, an overnight strike hit an apartment in Gaza City, killing members of the Safadi family, including Zina, 4, and Lana, 14, according to Palestinian health officials and relatives [2][11][13]. Palestinian and medical sources put the day’s toll at at least six, nine, 10 or 11 people killed in Israeli strikes and gunfire, depending on the source and timing [2][7][5][11].

Key Points

  • Ceasefire toll: Gaza's Health Ministry says Israeli attacks have killed more than 1,000 Palestinians since the October ceasefire, while Israeli figures cited elsewhere put the number of Israeli soldiers killed by militants in the same period at four [2][59][54].
  • Press deaths: Reporters Without Borders said Israeli forces had killed more than 220 journalists in Gaza by the end of 2025, including at least 70 who were working at the time [8][17][30].
  • Other locations: Medics also reported deaths in Beit Lahiya and Khan Younis, including a woman shot dead in the north and one person killed and eight wounded in the south [7][46][61].
  • Family loss: Wishah's brother Mohammed, also an Al Jazeera journalist, was killed in April in an Israeli strike or shelling while traveling in a vehicle, according to Al Jazeera and Palestinian civil defense authorities [1][5][8].

Historical Background

The Gaza war began after Hamas-led attackers killed 1,221 people and took 251 hostages in Israel on October 7, 2023, according to figures cited by n-tv [41]. Israel then launched a military campaign in Gaza, where the Hamas-run health ministry has reported more than 70,000 Palestinian deaths over the following two years [41]. A ceasefire between Israel and Hamas took effect in October 2025, but both sides have accused the other of violations since then [2][21].
