Carney says Qatar will back Canadian nation-building projects

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney said Qatar has committed to “significant” investments to help move forward Canada’s major building projects, following meetings with Qatar’s Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani in Doha [2][3][4]. Carney also said Canada plans to finalize a long-stalled investment agreement with Qatar, framing the step as a practical way to speed up priority projects and support job creation at home [3][4]. The announcement signals a warmer, more cooperative phase in Canada–Qatar ties, with Carney describing it as a “new chapter” that also includes efforts to broaden trade and cultural connections [4].
