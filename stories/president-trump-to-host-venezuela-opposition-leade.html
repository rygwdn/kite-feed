President Trump to host Venezuela opposition leader Machado

Category: World

President Donald Trump is set to meet Venezuelan opposition leader and Nobel Peace Prize laureate María Corina Machado at the White House on Thursday, US media and multiple international outlets reported [6][1][5][7][3]. The meeting comes as Venezuela has been freeing more prisoners, amid calls to accelerate releases of political detainees [2][5]. Several reports frame the visit within the fast-moving post-Maduro political transition, including accounts that US forces seized Nicolás Maduro on January 3 and that Washington announced it would be “running” Venezuela—claims that have heightened scrutiny of how Venezuela’s interim leadership is being managed [2][4].

  • Nobel spotlight: BBC reported Machado won the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize and had offered to share the honour with President Trump, whom the report says has long coveted the prize [1].

  • Interim pressure: France 24 said the meeting comes as pressure grows on interim leaders in Caracas to speed up releases of political prisoners [5].

Historical Background

Venezuela has experienced prolonged political conflict between governments led by Nicolás Maduro and opposition movements that argue elections and institutions have not reflected the popular will [common]. International diplomacy around Venezuela has often centered on human rights concerns, political detentions, and pathways to a peaceful transfer of power [common]. In the current cycle of reporting, multiple outlets describe a sudden rupture in power following Maduro’s reported seizure on January 3, reshaping how external actors engage Venezuelan opposition figures [2][4].
