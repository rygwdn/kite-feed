Meta adds voice and video calls to WhatsApp Web

Category: Technology

Meta has begun rolling out voice and video calling on WhatsApp Web, letting users make and receive one-on-one and group calls from a browser without downloading the WhatsApp desktop app [4][6][2][1][11]. The browser calls support end-to-end encryption and add a Calls tab with call history and favorites, matching features already available on WhatsApp mobile and desktop apps [4][5][6][7]. WhatsApp also introduced Call Transfer for moving an active call between phone, tablet, desktop app and web without hanging up, plus Waiting Room controls for group-call links when hosts enable “Require approval to join” [4][12][11][13]. The update adds screen sharing and reactions on web calls, noise suppression for background sound, and QuickHD so video reaches high definition in the first few seconds of a call [3][4][7][18]. WhatsApp is releasing the features gradually, with several reports citing availability expanding to all users over the coming days or weeks [9][11][23][22]. Meta announced the update on July 28, and WhatsApp told 9to5Mac the broader web-calling rollout would soon reach all users [12].

Key Points

  • Group capacity: PCMag reported that WhatsApp Web video calls can include up to 32 people when using the new browser calling feature [7].
  • Browser use: Canaltech reported that users must grant camera and microphone access in the browser to join web-based WhatsApp calls [20].
  • Recent testing: 9to5Mac reported that WhatsApp began adding web voice and video support earlier in 2026 and opened an early beta trial in June [12].

Perspectives

Kunal Shah: NDTV reported that Kunal Shah tested WhatsApp's new browser calling feature with Mark Zuckerberg.

Sources: NDTV

Timeline

  • 2015: WhatsApp Web launched with browser-based messaging access [17]
  • June 2026: WhatsApp offered early web-calling access to beta testers [12]
  • 28 July 2026: Meta announced the broader WhatsApp Web calling rollout [6]
  • 29 July 2026: Reports described staged availability to remaining users [9]

Historical Background

WhatsApp Web evolved over a decade from a basic messaging companion to a platform supporting file sharing, group management, and multi-device sync without the phone staying online, but voice and video calls were missing until this update [17]. TechCrunch reported that WhatsApp calls had been limited for years to the smartphone app or the native desktop app before Meta added web calling [4].

Action Items

  • Try a call in WhatsApp Web: Open WhatsApp Web in a browser, choose a chat, and use the call controls if the feature has reached your account [21][11].
  • Enable approval for group-call links: When creating a WhatsApp call link, turn on “Require approval to join” to place participants in a waiting room before admission [4][12].
