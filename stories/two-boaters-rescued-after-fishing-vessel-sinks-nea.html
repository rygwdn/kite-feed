Two boaters rescued after fishing vessel sinks near Tsawwassen

Category: Canada

Two people were rescued Sunday afternoon after their fishing vessel sank in the waters between British Columbia’s Lower Mainland and Vancouver Island, B.C. Ferries said. The Spirit of British Columbia and Coastal Renaissance each launched a rescue craft after receiving reports that people were in the water. Crews brought the boaters to the Tsawwassen terminal on the mainland [1][2][3]. The rescue disrupted ferry service in the region. B.C. Ferries said the 3 p.m. sailing to Swartz Bay was expected to arrive 40 minutes late, while later sailings from Swartz Bay and Tsawwassen were also expected to be delayed [3]. B.C. Emergency Health Services met the rescued boaters, and police were called. B.C. Ferries said it had no details on their condition [1][3].
