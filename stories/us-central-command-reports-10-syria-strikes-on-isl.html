US Central Command reports 10 Syria strikes on Islamic State

Category: World

The U.S. military says it carried out a series of airstrikes in Syria in early February, targeting Islamic State (IS) infrastructure such as weapons storage sites [1][2][4][5][6][7]. U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) said the strikes were retaliation for a December ambush that killed two U.S. soldiers and an American civilian interpreter [1][5][6][7]. Several outlets described the strikes as part of a broader push to disrupt IS remnants operating in Syria and Iraq [4]. Al Jazeera, however, called the campaign “Operation Hawkeye” and reported a wider scope and a higher fighter toll than other accounts [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Public disclosure: The U.S. military announced the strikes on February 14, after carrying them out earlier in the month [1][5][6].
  • Broader campaign framing: The Guardian framed the strikes as part of an ongoing campaign against IS in Iraq and Syria [4].
  • Operation Hawkeye: Al Jazeera reported the campaign name and described a larger effort—about 100 targets—saying more than 50 ISIL fighters were killed or captured [3].
