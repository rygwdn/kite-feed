Northern Ontario wildfires force evacuations in First Nations

Category: Canada

Mandatory evacuation orders are in effect for several northwestern Ontario communities as nearby forest fires spread, including Armstrong, Whitesand First Nation, Namaygoosisagagun First Nation, Lac des Mille Lacs First Nation, Gakijiwanong Anishinaabe Nation and Kiashke Zaaging Anishinaabek, also known as Gull Bay First Nation [1][2][3][4]. Emergency Management Ontario told residents under evacuation orders to leave and head south toward Thunder Bay [2][4][5]. Ontario fire officials said the province was fighting 160 active wildfires, including 128 in the northwest region, as of Monday evening [2]. Heat and air-quality warnings remained in place across much of the region, with Environment Canada forecasting highs of 36 C and humidex values as high as 40 C in much of northern Ontario [3][5]. Evacuees from Armstrong, Whitesand First Nation and Collins First Nation were sent to Thunder Bay, while Kiashke Zaaging Anishinaabek said Thunder Bay accommodations were unavailable and residents still in the community Wednesday morning would be flown to Toronto [3][4]. The OPP said people in Ignace, Crystal Lake and the Highway 633 area should prepare for possible evacuations [4][5].

  • Fire conditions: Wildland fire hazards ranged from moderate to extreme across northwestern Ontario, while smoke plumes were visible near Thunder Bay [3].

Humanitarian Impact

Evacuations affect multiple small communities and First Nations in northwestern Ontario, with immediate needs including safe transport, temporary lodging and protection from smoke and heat exposure [3][4][5]. Collins First Nation has between 30 and 40 residents [3].

Action Items

  • Confirm evacuation routing: Follow OPP and Emergency Management Ontario instructions before travelling, because highway closures and host locations have changed during the evacuation response [2][4].
  • Prepare essential documents: If you are in an area told to prepare for evacuation, gather identification, medications, chargers and pet supplies so you can leave quickly if an order is issued [common].
