President Trump urges AI firms to fund power

Category: AI

President Trump said the White House had negotiated a “ratepayer protection pledge” meant to ensure large AI and cloud companies cover electricity costs tied to fast-growing data centers, instead of passing those costs on to ordinary utility customers [1][2][3]. In remarks highlighted in coverage of his State of the Union address, he also urged the biggest AI players to “produce their own electricity,” casting self-supply as a way to ease pressure on the grid as data-center demand rises [2]. Several major tech firms have already publicly committed to covering some types of data-center-related power costs, and TechCrunch reported that much of what the White House wants may overlap with pledges hyperscalers have already made [3]. Still, The New York Times reported that Trump offered few operational details about the deal he described. Experts also warned that turning broad pledges into enforceable, real-world outcomes can be hard—especially when regulated utility rate-setting is involved and grid upgrades can take years [1].
