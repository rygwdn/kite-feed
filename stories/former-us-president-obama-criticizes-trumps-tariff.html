Former U.S. President Obama criticizes Trump’s tariffs on Canada ahead of midterms

Category: Canada

Former U.S. President Barack Obama criticized President Donald Trump’s tariffs on Canadian goods at a Democratic campaign rally in Detroit on Oct. 24, sarcastically calling Canada a “well-known evil empire” and arguing that the duties raise consumer prices. Obama was campaigning for Democratic candidates Abdul El-Sayed and Jocelyn Benson ahead of the Nov. 3 U.S. midterm elections [1][3][4]. The dispute affects industries including automotive manufacturing, where parts move between Canadian and U.S. factories [4][7]. After trade talks collapsed in August, Trump imposed 50% tariffs on Canadian goods; Prime Minister Mark Carney responded with counter-tariffs in September [1][3]. On Oct. 23, Trump announced he would lift some duties at the end of October while imposing tariffs on other products, including Canadian garbage and sewage. The changes also include exemptions for some goods used in Maine agriculture [3][1].
