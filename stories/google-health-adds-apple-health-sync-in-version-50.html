Google Health adds Apple Health sync in version 5.05

Category: Technology

Google is rolling out Google Health version 5.05, adding two-way Apple Health syncing for iPhone users after the app previously could import HealthKit data but could not write Fitbit data back to Apple Health [10][3][7][9]. The update lets users send exercise records, steps, vitals, sleep and other Google Health data to Apple Health, including data collected through Fitbit devices such as the Fitbit Air [12][5][1][4]. The same release adds Smart Health Links in the United States, allowing users to generate a URL or QR code with summaries of selected medical records for sharing with healthcare providers or family members [2][12][5]. Google says the links are not meant to replace official clinical health records, and version 5.05 also includes fixes affecting VO2 Max, exercise maps and workout editing [2][12][16].

Primary Source: View original article

Share your data like your exercise records, sleep, vitals, steps, and more from Google Health to Apple Health.

— Google Health release notes (9to5Mac)

Did you know: Fitbit’s lack of native Apple Health sync dated back to 2014, the year Apple introduced HealthKit [10].

Key Points

  • HRV omission: Heart rate variability does not transfer to Apple Health. Users attributed the gap to different HRV calculations in Google Health and Apple Health [3][4][6][7].
  • Platform rollout: Google Health 5.05 is available for iOS and is rolling out to Android. The Apple Health writing feature applies only to iPhone users [12][3][21].

Perspectives

Google: Google described the release as two sharing updates: Apple Health integration for iOS users and Smart Health Links for U.S. users who want to share medical-record summaries with providers or family members.

Sources: 9to5Google

Reddit users: Reddit user posting_purple first spotted version 5.05’s Apple Health sync option.

Sources: Android Authority

Timeline

  • 2014: Fitbit said it had no plans to support HealthKit [10].
  • May 2026: Google Health launched with the Fitbit Air and one-way Apple Health integration [10].
  • 3 August 2026: Version 5.05 began adding Apple Health write support [10][12].
  • 4 August 2026: U.S. Smart Health Links details appeared in version 5.05 coverage [2].

Historical Background

Apple introduced HealthKit with iOS 8 in 2014, and Fitbit said shortly afterward that it had no plans to support Apple’s platform [10][15]. Before Google Health version 5.05, Fitbit users who wanted their data in Apple Health generally needed a third-party syncing utility or an indirect connection through another service [10][7][16]. Google Health launched in May alongside the Fitbit Air [10][13].

Action Items

  • Enable Apple Health syncing: Update Google Health to version 5.05, open your profile, choose Partner apps, select Apple Health and grant the data permissions you want [12][9].
  • Review medical-record sharing before sending: In the Health tab’s Medical section, select Shareable records and choose only the data you want to include before generating a Smart Health Link [2][12].
  • Check HRV inside Google Health: If you track heart rate variability, keep checking it in Google Health because that metric does not currently sync to Apple Health [4][3].
