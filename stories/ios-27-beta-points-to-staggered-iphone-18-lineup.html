iOS 27 beta points to staggered iPhone 18 lineup

Category: Technology

Apple's iOS 27 beta 5 contains references to six unreleased iPhone models, according to Macworld findings described by ARY News and Khaleej Times [5][4]. The references appeared in battery drivers and Battery Intelligence files and listed the iPhone Air 2 (V62), iPhone 18 Pro (V63), iPhone 18 Pro Max (V64), iPhone 18 (V67), foldable iPhone Ultra (V68) and iPhone 18e (V69), ARY News reported [5]. The code does not disclose hardware specifications, but ARY News said its placement in power-management files suggests Apple is preparing iOS 27 to support the battery needs of the new devices before release [5]. Reports cited by ARY News, MacRumors, Kien Thuc and Ada Derana say Apple may split its release schedule, with the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and foldable iPhone Ultra expected in fall 2026, while the standard iPhone 18, iPhone 18e and iPhone Air 2 could move to early 2027 [5][9][7][8].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Beta changes: iOS 27 beta 5 updates Liquid Glass icons for Safari, Siri, Settings, Remote, App Store and Preview, with some icons redesigned and others given more detail [6].
  • Pricing: MacRumors said it expects the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max to cost $100 to $300 more, while the iPhone 17 currently starts at $799 [9].

Timeline

  • 10 August 2026: Apple released iOS 27 beta 5, build 24A5408d [10]
  • 15 August 2026: Macworld found six model references in beta power files [5]
  • 2026: Reports said the premium iPhones and foldable Ultra are expected at the fall event [5][8]
  • first quarter of 2027: The standard iPhone 18 reportedly shifts to the first quarter [8]

Technical Details

  • Foldable battery support: Earlier iOS 27 betas suggested support for foldable screens and multiple battery setups, which aligns with the rumored iPhone Ultra reference in beta 5 files [5].
  • On-device Siri voice controls: iOS 27 beta 5 expands Siri AI Pace and Expressivity controls across seven American voices and four British voices, with processing limited to the iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPhone Air [6].

Industry Impact

  • Foldable phones: Ada Derana said the rumored iPhone Ultra could use a book-style design with a large internal display and smaller cover screen, putting Apple in competition with Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold and Google's Pixel Fold lines [8].
  • Accessory makers: MacRumors said third-party accessories revealed information about the foldable iPhone Ultra, indicating accessory vendors are already preparing for unreleased hardware designs [1].

Historical Background

Apple has traditionally introduced standard and Pro iPhone models together in September, according to Ada Derana [8]. Khaleej Times noted that unreleased iPhone references have surfaced through Apple software updates before [4].

Action Items

  • Check trade-in value before the fall launch: If you plan to exchange an older iPhone, compare current trade-in offers before new models arrive because MacRumors says values typically fall after each iPhone launch [9].
  • Compare iPhone 17 pricing before waiting: If you want a non-Pro model, review iPhone 17 prices now because reports say the standard iPhone 18 may not arrive until early 2027 and existing iPhone prices could rise [9][8].
