Pakistan school collapse kills 32 children and a teacher; inquiry examines construction

Category: World

A roof collapse at the privately run Al-Khair Education Center in Peshawar killed at least 32 students and one teacher on Thursday, Oct. 15, and injured more than 45 people, officials said. Most of the children were under 13. The collapse happened during morning classes, when about 110 pupils were in three classrooms, according to police [11][2][38]. Rescue officials said the roof of an upper floor fell onto classrooms below, bringing down parts of the building. Peshawar police chief Mian Saeed said the structure was weak and rooftop construction had added excessive weight; the cause remains under investigation. Police arrested the school’s owner, and a provincial inquiry committee was tasked with examining the building and possible administrative negligence [17][34][44]. Rescuers later reported that everyone trapped beneath the rubble had been recovered. The provincial committee was ordered to submit a detailed report within 24 hours, while Governor Faisal Karim Kundi called for a safety audit of schools across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa [11][44][17].
