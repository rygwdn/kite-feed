Germany arrests former BND chief over alleged sale of state secrets

Category: World

German authorities arrested former foreign intelligence chief August Hanning on Tuesday on suspicion of espionage, treason and obtaining state secrets. Hanning, 80, led the Federal Intelligence Service, or BND, from 1998 to 2005. Authorities also arrested a former aide, identified as Manfred D., on suspicion of helping him [8][16][32]. Federal prosecutors allege that Hanning and Manfred D. agreed in 2010 that the aide would provide classified intelligence in exchange for payment. They say Manfred D. supplied about 2,000 documents over several years and that Hanning used the information in private consulting work, including to prepare an analysis for a foreign intelligence officer. Prosecutors have not identified the foreign services involved and say it is unclear whether the analysis reached its intended recipient [16][4][32]. The investigation began after investigators found evidence during a search of Hanning’s home in a separate case in 2025. Federal prosecutors opened a covert investigation in May 2026. Some material reportedly came from senior-level briefings that included information from partner agencies [7][1]. Both suspects were expected to appear before a judge [15].

Key Points

  • Sensitive briefings: Documents reportedly included internal BND material and intelligence briefings for senior officials at the Chancellery, including assessments of Iran’s nuclear program and the Russian armed forces [7][15].
  • Aide’s access: Manfred D. allegedly served as a senior BND chief of staff under successive agency presidents from 2012 until March 2026, giving him access to confidential information [7][16].
  • Unresolved recipient: Prosecutors described a separate case in which Hanning allegedly shared political information with an employee of another foreign intelligence service [16][32].
  • Agency powers: Lawmakers are debating expanding BND powers. The agency is seeking authority to respond to sabotage, cyberattacks and disinformation [9][27].

Perspectives

Martin Jäger, BND president: Jäger said the agency began extensive internal investigations as soon as suspicions emerged and that they corroborated the allegations.

Timeline

  • 2010: Prosecutors allege that Hanning and Manfred D. agreed the aide would provide intelligence in exchange for payment [16].
  • June 2022: The most recent documents found so far reportedly date from June 2022 [7].
  • September 2025: Police searching Hanning’s home in a separate case found BND documents [7].
  • May 2026: Federal prosecutors opened a covert investigation into Hanning and Manfred D. [7].
  • 6 October 2026: Police arrested Hanning and the former aide on suspicion of espionage-related offenses [16][8].

Historical Background

The BND has faced earlier espionage cases: in the 1960s, counterintelligence division head Heinz Felfe worked for the Soviet KGB while serving in the agency [7]. In 2014, a BND employee was exposed as a spy for the U.S. CIA [7]. A separate trial is also under way against former BND agent Carsten L., accused of passing secrets to Russia in 2022 [7][32].

Q&A

Q: What separate investigation led police to the documents at Hanning’s home?

A: Police searched Hanning’s residence in a 2025 investigation connected to the alleged abduction of businesswoman Christina Block’s children; the search uncovered an electronic storage device containing classified BND documents [7][16].
