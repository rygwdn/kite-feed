Update: US says Gaza ceasefire plan enters phase two

Category: World

In the latest development, the United States says the Gaza ceasefire framework Israel and Hamas have been following since October has moved into a second phase that shifts the focus from sustaining the truce to Gaza’s demilitarisation, governance arrangements, and reconstruction [1][2][9][8][13]. US Middle East envoy Steve Witkoff said phase two is part of President Trump’s 20-point plan and includes steps toward setting up a Palestinian technocratic administration for day-to-day management of Gaza [8][9][6]. While the transition offers a pathway toward more stable civilian governance and rebuilding, multiple outlets report that the core obstacles remain: reaching agreement on Hamas disarmament and implementing further Israeli withdrawal provisions without the truce breaking down [2][3][7][1].

Primary Source: View original article

We are announcing the launch of phase two of the president’s 20-Point plan to End the Gaza conflict, moving from ceasefire to demilitarisation, technocratic governance, and reconstruction

— Steve Witkoff (Dawn)

Did you know: The plan discussed in these reports is described as a “20-point” framework presented by President Trump’s team for ending the Gaza conflict [8][2].

Key Points

  • Hostage exchange: Under the deal’s first phase, Hamas released all but one hostage it was holding in exchange for hundreds of Palestinians held in Israeli prisons, a step cited as a key confidence-building outcome [5].
  • Gaza governance: Egypt announced the formation of a 15-person Palestinian technocratic committee intended to administer post-war Gaza, aligning with plans for a panel of technocrats described by US and regional mediators [8][3][13].
  • US warning: Witkoff said the US expects Hamas to meet obligations that include the “immediate return of the final deceased hostage,” warning of “serious consequences” if it does not [11][8].
  • Aid surge context: Phase one included a partial Israeli withdrawal and a surge of humanitarian aid into Gaza, which several reports note helped underpin the October truce [13].
  • Supervision plan: PBS reports the second phase envisions Gaza’s day-to-day affairs being run by Palestinian experts “under American supervision,” a structure likely to shape how services and reconstruction are managed [6].

Perspectives

White House / US government: US officials say phase two has begun and centers on demilitarising Gaza alongside political and rebuilding steps, while acknowledging substantial hurdles ahead.

Timeline

  • October (last year): Phase one began with ceasefire and hostage-prisoner exchange [13].
  • Wednesday: US envoy announced phase two launch in 20-point plan [8][6].

Technical Details

  • Demilitarisation (in this plan): The phase-two framework describes moving Gaza toward demilitarisation, including disarmament of “unauthorised personnel,” with Hamas disarmament treated as a central issue [8][2].
  • Technocratic administration: Multiple reports describe a committee or panel of Palestinian technocrats intended to manage daily governance in post-war Gaza, separating civil administration from factional politics [3][9][8].
  • Three-phase structure: Several outlets describe the ceasefire as a multi-phase arrangement, with phase one centered on truce measures and exchanges and phase two oriented toward longer-term governance and rebuilding [5][13][1].

Historical Background

Israel and Hamas have fought repeated wars over Gaza, with Hamas controlling the territory since 2007 after a split with the Palestinian Authority [common]. The latest war began after Hamas’s October 7, 2023 attack on Israel and Israel’s subsequent military campaign in Gaza, producing a prolonged humanitarian crisis and intense international diplomacy [common]. The current ceasefire framework described in these reports began in October 2025 and has proceeded in phases that link calm on the ground to hostage-prisoner exchanges and governance and security steps [13][5].
