Canada’s Mark Carney to meet King Charles in London

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney will meet King Charles III in London on March 16 during an overseas trip [2][3][4]. The audience will be Carney’s third with the King since he became prime minister about a year ago [3]. Carney visits Norway first, then heads to the U.K., with plans to arrive in London on Sunday [2][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Trip sequence: After his stop in Norway, Carney is scheduled to arrive in London on Sunday [2][4].
  • Defence link: While in Norway, Carney plans to watch NATO Cold Response exercises [2].
  • Regional summit: He is also set to take part in a Nordic-Canada Council summit, according to his published itinerary [2].
