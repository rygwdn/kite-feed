President Trump honors Artemis II crew after lunar flyby

Category: Science

President Trump presented the Congressional Space Medal of Honor to the four Artemis II astronauts on Friday at NASA’s Johnson Space Center in Houston, recognizing the crew’s April lunar flyby as the first crewed trip to the moon in more than half a century [2][3][4][5]. The honorees were NASA astronauts Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover and Christina Koch, and Canadian Space Agency astronaut Jeremy Hansen [2][3][4][5]. The Artemis II mission launched April 1 on NASA’s Space Launch System rocket and Orion spacecraft, named Integrity, and splashed down safely in the Pacific Ocean on April 10 [4][5]. Artemis II traveled 252,756 miles from Earth, surpassing the human-spaceflight distance record held by Apollo 13 since 1970, and covered 695,081 miles over the full mission, including its lunar flyby [4][5]. Trump also announced the creation of a U.S. Space Academy to serve the Space Force, NASA and the commercial space sector, and said its location had not been determined [2][3]. NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman and other dignitaries took part in the ceremony at the Johnson Space Center, which houses Mission Control [2][3].
