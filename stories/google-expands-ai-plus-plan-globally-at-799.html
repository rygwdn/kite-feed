Google expands AI Plus plan globally at $7.99

Category: AI

Google is expanding its Google AI Plus subscription to all markets where its AI plans are offered, adding 35 countries and territories and making the plan available in the U.S. as of this rollout [2][5]. The plan costs $7.99 per month in the United States and bundles access to Gemini 3 Pro alongside other Google AI tools, positioning it as a lower-priced option in Google’s paid AI lineup [5][1]. Google also paired the launch with changes to Google One storage benefits, including an upgrade tied to the 2 TB tier, as the company continues to integrate AI features into its consumer subscriptions [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Many consumer subscription bundles succeed because they combine a frequently used benefit (like storage) with an “aspirational” add-on (like AI tools), which can improve retention even when add-on usage varies [common].

Key Points

  • Where it lands: Google’s announcement frames this as a completion step: AI Plus now matches the footprint of Google’s other AI plans rather than being region-limited [2].
  • What’s included: TechCrunch says the bundle centers on Gemini 3 Pro but also adds other AI tools, signaling a “suite” approach instead of à la carte features [5].
  • Storage packaging: 9to5Google reports Google One’s 2 TB plan gets an upgrade alongside AI Plus, hinting at cross-selling between storage and AI memberships [1].
  • Price strategy: TechCrunch calls AI Plus the more affordable tier, suggesting Google is testing a mass-market price point to widen paid AI adoption [5].
