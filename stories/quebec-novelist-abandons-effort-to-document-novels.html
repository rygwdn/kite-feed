Quebec novelist abandons effort to document novel’s origins after AI allegations

Category: Canada

Quebec author Thélyson Orélien has stopped gathering evidence to support his denial that artificial intelligence wrote his novel, “C’était ça ou mourir,” his publisher confirmed on Oct. 17 [1][2][3][4]. The publisher said it respects Orélien’s decision to stop documenting how the book was written [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Award status: The novel was removed from consideration for France’s Prix Goncourt earlier in October [1].
