SK Telecom targets 15 gigawatts of AI data center capacity by 2035

Category: AI

SK Telecom said it plans to develop up to 15 gigawatts of AI data center capacity across South Korea by 2035, saying the project is part of a push to make the country an AI infrastructure hub in Asia [1][2][3][4][5][6]. The company linked the plan to the government’s AI G3 strategy and said growing demand for AI model training and inference requires more high-performance computing infrastructure [4][6]. The buildout will start with SK Telecom’s first AI data center, under construction in Ulsan, and expand into a southeastern cluster exceeding 2 gigawatts [3][4][6]. SK Telecom also plans to add 1 gigawatt in the southwest, bringing domestic capacity to about 5 gigawatts in stages from 2029, before expanding toward 15 gigawatts by 2035 as it reviews power supply, sites, operations and investment conditions [3][4][6].

Primary Source: View original article

The AI data center initiative is designed to proactively prepare the computing infrastructure needed for the global AI ecosystem

— SK Telecom CEO Jung Jai-hun (The Korea Times)

Key Points

  • Capacity gap: Tech in Asia said SK Telecom’s planned 15-gigawatt capacity would far exceed South Korea’s current 591-megawatt data center base [5].
  • Funding model: Etoday reported that a typical 1-gigawatt AI data center can cost about 70 trillion won and may require strategic partner investments, long-term customer contracts and project financing in addition to SK Telecom’s own capital [6].
  • Tenant strategy: SK Telecom aims to use the southeastern cluster to attract global technology companies seeking to diversify AI infrastructure beyond the United States [4][6].
  • Supply outlook: Etoday cited McKinsey as forecasting global data center demand growth of 19% to 22% annually and a roughly 15-gigawatt supply shortage in the United States alone by 2030 [6].
