Iran flogs singer Parastoo Ahmadi over hijab-free online concert

Category: World

Iranian authorities flogged singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four members of her concert team 74 times each in Qom on Monday, Nov. 23, their lawyers said. The five also received two-year bans on artistic activity and travel [1][5][15][14]. The case arose from a video posted online in December 2024 showing Ahmadi performing without a headscarf alongside male musicians at the Deir-e Gachin Caravanserai near Qom. The performance had no in-person audience and drew millions of views. A Qom criminal court convicted the group of publishing material it called obscene and immoral; the defendants denied the allegations [5][14][8][10]. The floggings followed unsuccessful efforts by the defence team to overturn or delay the sentences. Nine people were sentenced in the case, but it remained unclear whether the other four had also been flogged [1][20][6].

Parastoo Ahmadi's flogging for singing and dressing as she chooses is a flagrant violation of her fundamental rights and a chilling demonstration of the Iranian authorities' determination to suppress women's freedoms and to violently punish anyone who dares to challenge their discriminatory laws and policies

— Bahar Ghandehari, director of advocacy at the Center for Human Rights in Iran (BBC News)

Key Points

  • Rights criticism: Amnesty International urged Iran to quash the punishment, calling flogging cruel, inhuman and degrading and saying it violates the absolute prohibition on torture [5][4].
  • Other artists: Singer Mehdi Yarrahi received 74 lashes in March 2025 over a song supporting women who opposed compulsory hijab rules [4][2].
  • Enforcement warning: Tehran prosecutor Ali Salehi threatened “decisive and deterrent measures” in late October against government bodies that fail to enforce hijab rules, Al-Monitor reported [8].
  • Further prosecution: Singer Hiva Seyfizadeh received a four-year prison sentence after performing traditional music in Tehran in February 2025 and said she planned to appeal [14].

Perspectives

Iranian authorities: Iran rejects accusations about its human-rights record and says Western countries should focus on their own human-rights problems [9].

Timeline

  • 11 December 2024: Ahmadi performed at Deir-e Gachin without a headscarf [5].
  • June 2026: A Qom court convicted nine people and imposed lashes and bans [1][8].
  • September 2026: A Qom appeals court rejected the defendants’ challenge to their sentences [1][10].
  • 23 November 2026: Authorities carried out five of the flogging sentences in Qom [1][5].

Historical Background

Iran has required women to cover their hair and wear loose-fitting clothing in public since the 1979 Islamic Revolution [23]. The death in custody of Mahsa Amini in 2022, after her arrest over an alleged dress-code violation, sparked the Woman, Life, Freedom protest movement; demonstrations challenged compulsory hijab rules and the authorities’ restrictions on women [5][1].
