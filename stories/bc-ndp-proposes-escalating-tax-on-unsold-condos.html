B.C. NDP proposes escalating tax on unsold condos

Category: Canada

British Columbia NDP Leader David Eby said Sept. 25 that, if re-elected on Oct. 24, his government would tax completed condominiums left unsold for more than a year. The proposed levy would start at 2% and rise by one percentage point for each additional year a unit remained unsold. Eby said the plan would encourage developers to put completed homes on the market [1][2][3][4]. Eby also proposed raising the existing speculation and vacancy tax. He announced the measures at a campaign stop in Burnaby during the first week of the provincial snap-election campaign [1][2][3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Unsold inventory: Eby cited a Real Property Data estimate that thousands of empty Metro Vancouver units worth $4.4 billion remain unsold [2][3].
  • Reaction: The proposal drew criticism from the Conservatives and mixed reactions from experts [2][3].
