Amazon redesigns Kindle lineup, adds color models and $35 page-turn remote

Category: Technology

Amazon unveiled a redesigned Kindle family on Oct. 1, refreshing its basic Kindle, Paperwhite and Colorsoft models ahead of the e-reader’s 20th anniversary in 2027 [2][1]. The devices replace recessed screen edges with a flush-front design, have thinner bodies and come in colors including Ube, Graphite and Fig; select versions have aluminum backs. U.S. prices start at $149.99 for the basic Kindle, $199.99 for Paperwhite and $289.99 for Colorsoft [9][15]. Amazon also introduced the $34.99 Kindle Click, a Bluetooth remote for turning pages and adjusting brightness, and a $79.99 magnetic Page-Turn Cover with physical buttons for select Signature models [8][25]. The new basic Kindle is 6.8 millimeters thick and weighs 140 grams. Amazon says it opens books 30% faster than its predecessor and its battery lasts up to six weeks [6][3]. Paperwhite and Colorsoft remain waterproof, and the Paperwhite is rated for up to 12 weeks per charge [5][23]. Amazon says the redesigned Kindles have user-replaceable batteries to comply with European Union rules [8]. The basic Kindle is available now, while Paperwhite, Colorsoft and the Kindle Click are scheduled to ship Oct. 28 [45][8].

Key Points

  • Color display: Colorsoft retains a 7-inch panel with a resolution of 300 pixels per inch in black and white and 150 pixels per inch in color; its Signature Edition adds 32GB of storage [23].
  • Kids editions: Amazon also offers Kids versions of the new Kindle family, priced at $179.99 for the basic Kindle, $229.99 for Paperwhite and $299.99 for Colorsoft in the U.S. [19][45].
  • Price increase: The new Colorsoft models cost $40 more than the previous generation. Amazon cited higher memory and storage costs when it raised Kindle prices in August [23][16].
  • Reading volume: Amazon says Kindle use has grown by double digits globally for three consecutive years and that readers turned more than 720 billion pages in 2025 [4].
  • Remote compatibility: Kindle Click works with Kindle devices released in 2024 or later, charges via USB-C and includes side buttons for adjusting brightness [8].

Perspectives

PCMag reviewer Will Greenwald: After trying the devices at Amazon’s New York offices, Greenwald said the bodies were sleeker but “very little has changed under the hood,” and noted the base Kindle still lacks the Paperwhite’s IPX8 water-resistance rating [10].

Six Colors reviewer Jason Snell: Snell welcomed the return of page-turn options but criticized the $79.99 cover as a paid add-on, contrasting it with Kobo’s Libra Colour, which has buttons built into the reader [11].

Technical Details

  • Colorsoft Signature Edition: Adds automatic front-light adjustment and support for a pogo-pin charging dock [23].
  • Reverse-stack construction: Amazon says its reverse-stack display construction places the screen flush with the front frame, replacing the recessed or raised-edge design used on earlier Kindle models [49][24].

Historical Background

Amazon refreshed the Kindle and Paperwhite in 2024 and introduced Colorsoft that year, according to Xataka [49].

Q&A

Q: Is the Page-Turn Cover water-resistant?

A: Yes. The cover has an IPX8 water-resistance rating, matching the Kindle underneath [25].

Q: Can readers choose which Page-Turn Cover button advances the page?

A: Yes. Kindle settings let readers choose which button turns the page forward or back [25].
