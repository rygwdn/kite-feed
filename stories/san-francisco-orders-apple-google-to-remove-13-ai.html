San Francisco orders Apple, Google to remove 13 AI apps

Category: Technology

San Francisco City Attorney David Chiu sent cease-and-desist letters to Apple and Google demanding they remove 13 AI “nudify” or face-swap apps from their app stores, including eight from Apple’s App Store and five from Google Play [2][3][5][9][11][14]. The apps can create AI-generated nonconsensual nude images, and Chiu’s letters told the companies to stop “aiding and abetting” the sale of explicit deepfake images and cut business ties with the developers [1][2][3][7][12]. Chiu’s office argues that Apple and Google have hosted and profited from the apps through in-app payments despite California laws that prohibit knowingly facilitating or recklessly aiding the creation of nonconsensual deepfake pornography [11][9][5]. Google said it removed the five apps named in the letters and has deleted “hundreds” of apps with nudifying features for policy violations, while the reports did not include a public comment from Apple on the letters [3][7][8][14].

Primary Source: View original article

Generating non-consensual intimate images is illegal, harmful, and completely unacceptable

— David Chiu (Wired)

Did you know: One targeted app had more than 1 million downloads while advertising sexualized image styles and “free and uncensored” video features, according to WIRED material quoted by 9to5Mac [5].

Key Points

  • App volume: In May 2026, researchers identified 420 face-swapping apps across both stores, illustrating how widespread these tools have become [13].
  • App reach: Nudify apps generated about $122 million in revenue and 483 million downloads, according to figures cited by Tech in Asia [4].
  • Child access: Researchers found some nudification apps carried age ratings suitable for children and could be found by searching terms such as “nudify” or “undress” [1][12][13].
  • Prior enforcement: Chiu’s office previously sued 16 websites that let users turn images of real women and girls into AI-generated pornography [1][12].

Perspectives

San Francisco City Attorney David Chiu: Chiu said Apple and Google were profiting from apps that exploit women and girls and argued that cutting ties with some apps does not replace proactive enforcement.

Sources: TechCrunch

Google: Google told WIRED it had deleted the five apps named in the letters and has removed “hundreds” of apps with nudifying features for policy violations. A spokesperson also confirmed Google restricted related search terms such as “nudify” on its platform.

Sources: MacRumors

Tech Transparency Project: The watchdog said Apple and Google had “steered” users toward such apps and called the companies “key participants” in the spread of AI tools that sexualize real people.

Sources: TechCrunch

Michael Tsai: Tsai wrote that if the 13 apps were clear about what they do, Apple’s App Review had no excuse for missing them.

Sources: Michael Tsai blog

Timeline

  • January 2026: TTP warned companies about payment-enabled deepfake NCII apps [11]
  • April 2026: TTP again reported app-store apps selling deepfake NCII [11]
  • May 2026: Researchers identified 420 general face-swapping apps across both stores [13]
  • June 2026: Apple tightened App Store language on developer pornographic-content responsibility [3]
  • 16 July 2026: Chiu sent cease-and-desist letters to Apple and Google [3][14]

Historical Background

In 2025, California enacted a law allowing victims to bring civil actions against third-party facilitators of such material [11]. In June 2026, Apple tightened App Store guideline language on developer responsibility for pornographic content [3].

Humanitarian Impact

Chiu said the images are used to bully, humiliate and threaten women and girls, and that some victims have been suicidal [7][9].

Action Items

  • Report nonconsensual AI sexual imagery: Use the app store’s abuse-reporting tools and preserve screenshots, app names, URLs and payment records before filing a report with the platform or local authorities [common].
  • Check child device restrictions: Review Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link settings and block app downloads or searches tied to terms such as “nudify” or “undress” [1][common].
