Quebec PQ sets $130 million plan for sovereignty referendum

Category: Canada

Parti Québécois Leader Paul St-Pierre Plamondon says a sovereignty referendum would cost at least $130 million if his party forms Quebec’s next government. The plan allocates $20 million for studies and consultations during the early years of a PQ mandate, including a commission on a provisional constitution beginning in 2027, and $110 million to hold the vote in 2029 or 2030 [1][2][5][7]. St-Pierre Plamondon said unforeseen expenses could increase the total [4][8]. He has pledged to hold the referendum even if the PQ wins only a minority government and said it would take place after President Donald Trump leaves office [2][5]. Three of the five party leaders campaigning in the provincial election criticized the proposed spending, while Québec solidaire defended it [1][4][7]. The PQ plans to publish the referendum figures in its financial framework on Sept. 15, ahead of the Oct. 5 election [5][7].
