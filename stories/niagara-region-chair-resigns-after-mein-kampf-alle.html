Niagara Region chair resigns after Mein Kampf allegations

Category: Canada

Niagara Region chair Bob Gale has resigned after the Niagara Region Anti-Racism Association alleged he bought and owns a copy of Adolf Hitler’s book “Mein Kampf” that is signed by Hitler [2][3][4][1]. His departure leaves open questions about what happens next in the chair role. The coverage provided does not lay out a replacement process or timeline [1][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Who received it: Reports say Gale submitted his resignation to Municipal Affairs and Housing Minister Rob Flack [2][4].
  • When it happened: National Observer said he resigned shortly after the association issued its press release [2].
  • How the role works: The Toronto Star described Gale as an Ontario government appointee serving as Niagara Region chair [3].

Perspectives

Niagara Region Anti-Racism Association: The group said it released documents it believes back up its allegation about an autographed copy of “Mein Kampf,” including an item that appears to authenticate Hitler’s signature [2][4].

Sources: National Observer

Ontario government: Coverage said the Ford government is reviewing its options after Gale’s resignation as Niagara Region chair [1][3].
