B.C. wildfire prompts expanded evacuations near Clinton

Category: Canada

The Pear Lake wildfire near Clinton, B.C., prompted expanded evacuation orders Friday in the south Cariboo region, with the Thompson-Nicola Regional District adding 375 properties in the Loon Lake area and more than 500 properties on the south side of Green Lake to existing orders [1][2][3][5]. The Thompson-Nicola order now covers nearly 1,500 properties, while the Cariboo Regional District issued a separate order for about 366 land parcels on the north side of Green Lake [1][2][5][6]. The fire has grown to more than 900 square kilometres and is one of several in the Big Bar complex [2][6]. Officials continued rapid damage assessments after the blaze surged the previous week, with dozens of properties affected south of Clinton along the Highway 97 corridor and Pavilion-Clinton Road. Crews had not yet assessed areas north of the village, where officials said there may be additional losses [1][2][7]. The B.C. Wildfire Service completed a 160-hectare planned ignition Friday evening north of Clinton, using aerial and hand ignition to remove unburned fuel between Big Bar Road and an area to the south where the fire had burned to Highway 97 [5]. Officials warned that a cold front expected to cross British Columbia by Saturday could bring gusts of 30 to 40 km/h and create conditions for rapid fire spread in dry areas [3].

Key Points

  • Provincewide fires: More than 30 evacuation orders were in effect across British Columbia as more than 100 wildfires continued to burn [5][4].
  • Damage notices: Thompson-Nicola Regional District information officer Colton Davies said officials were calling owners after rapid assessments identified properties affected by fire [1][2].
  • Village update: The Village of Clinton said Friday it had identified no new property impacts since a rapid damage assessment Thursday [5].
  • Dry fuels: The B.C. Wildfire Service said many areas were extremely dry, especially in southern British Columbia, where highly combustible fuels were present [3].

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: Evacuation orders covered properties around Loon Lake and both sides of Green Lake; residents under order should leave immediately and follow regional district instructions [1][2][5].
  • Roads: Officials used the Highway 97 corridor as a key damage-assessment area, and firefighting work occurred where the fire had burned to Highway 97 [1][5]. Check Highway 97 conditions before travel
  • Weather: A cold front forecast for Saturday was expected to bring gusts of 30 to 40 km/h and conditions that could accelerate fire spread [3]. Avoid discretionary travel near active fires during gusty winds

Action Items

  • Confirm your evacuation status: Check the latest notices from the Thompson-Nicola Regional District or Cariboo Regional District if you are near Loon Lake, Green Lake or Clinton [1][2].
  • Prepare property documentation: If you own property in affected areas, keep contact information current and gather insurance details because officials said they were calling owners after rapid damage assessments [1][2].
