UK arrests dual national over suspected plot at US-used RAF Fairford

Category: World

British counterterrorism police arrested a 25-year-old British-Iranian dual national in Westminster on Oct. 1 on suspicion of preparing terrorist acts, expanding an investigation into suspicious activity near RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire [6][19][16]. Police searched two London properties. They corrected an initial statement that listed the man’s age as 27 and said a second man had not been questioned [19]. Five British men arrested near the base on Sept. 27 remain on police bail and have not been charged. Officers found petrol but no explosives in vans linked to the men. Residents near the base were evacuated during the initial operation [14][21]. Prime Minister Andy Burnham said there were “strong indications” Iran played a part but gave no details, citing the continuing investigation. Tehran denies involvement and summoned the British ambassador on Oct. 1. Police say they are examining all lines of inquiry, including possible foreign-state involvement [23][6][12].

There are strong indications that Iran played a part in what happened over the weekend at RAF Fairford, but of course it remains an ongoing, complex, and serious police investigation.

— Andy Burnham, UK prime minister (The Jewish Chronicle)

  • Initial alert: Police launched the Sept. 27 operation after reports of three suspicious vehicles near the base, including a report from a local farmer [34][16].

Timeline

  • 27 September 2026: Police arrested five men near RAF Fairford after reports of suspicious vehicles [16].
  • 28 September 2026: The five suspects were released on police bail pending investigation [12].
  • 30 September 2026: Burnham publicly cited strong indications of Iranian involvement [23].
  • 1 October 2026: Police arrested a British-Iranian man as Tehran summoned Britain’s ambassador [6][12].

Technical Details

  • Arrest basis: Police said they arrested the man on suspicion of preparing terrorist acts under Section 5 of the Terrorism Act 2006 [16].

Historical Background

RAF Fairford has supported U.S. bomber operations in Middle East conflicts for decades [21]. Britain authorized U.S. use of the base for strikes against Iran, and U.S. bombers used it during the war with Iran in 2026 [15][12].

Humanitarian Impact

Residents near RAF Fairford were evacuated during the initial police operation. Reports describe scores of people leaving their homes but give no details of injuries or urgent needs [21][35].

International Reactions

  • 🇮🇷 Iran: Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Burnham was “barking up the wrong tree” [6][9].
  • 🇺🇸 United States: President Trump and Secretary of State Marco Rubio criticized the release on bail of the five men arrested first [14].
  • 🇮🇱 Israel: Israel said it warned Britain before the incident of a planned Iran-sponsored attack on the base [30].
