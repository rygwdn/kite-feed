Police examine motives in fatal Montreal shooting

Category: Canada

Police from several jurisdictions are investigating possible motives after a June 22 shooting in Montreal killed Montreal police officer Mohamed Lamine Benredouane, civilian bystander Michel Mizrahi and alleged gunman Seth Scott Hatfield [7][10]. Police said two officers were shot, one fatally, while a bystander was killed and the shooter died in an exchange of gunfire [9]. The suspect, identified as 25-year-old Seth Scott Hatfield of Lethbridge, Alta., allegedly left a manifesto of more than 100 pages. Sources described grievances tied to capitalism, pornography, bourgeois society, hatred of women and incel ideology [6][7][9]. A federal official said investigators are examining whether the attack targeted police officers and Pornhub, while Quebec's police watchdog is investigating the shooting and Quebec provincial police have opened a parallel criminal probe [7]. Authorities have not publicly established a motive. Calgary police said they found no credible co-ordinated threat to public safety in Calgary after the manifesto circulated online, while Lethbridge investigators continued assisting Quebec authorities [6].

Key Points

  • Online footprint: Activity linked to Hatfield included videos about weapons, the outdoors and a mix of conspiracy theories and grievances. Experts warned against drawing firm conclusions from that material alone [9][1].
  • Manifesto themes: The document allegedly includes rhetoric associated with the incel movement and calls for violence [6].
  • Jewish victim: Members of Montreal's Jewish community visited the scene after Mizrahi, identified as Jewish, was killed near an area The JC described as having a significant Jewish population [7][10].
  • Public warning: Experts urged people not to circulate online images of the shooting, as graphic videos spread in the hours after the attack [9][5].
