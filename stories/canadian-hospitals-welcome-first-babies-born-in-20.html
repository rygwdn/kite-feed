Canadian hospitals welcome first babies born in 2026

Category: Canada

Hospitals across Canada marked the start of 2026 by welcoming their first newborns of the year, with families and care teams celebrating milestone arrivals in multiple provinces on New Year’s Day [2][7][4]. In British Columbia, the provincial government said the first baby of 2026 arrived shortly after midnight at Kelowna General Hospital, while other early births were also reported across the province [5][3][1]. In Alberta, Alberta Health Services announced the first babies in Edmonton and Calgary were born at the same minute, and in Windsor the city’s first newborn drew extra attention from maternity staff during a quieter overnight stretch [7][2].

I was so excited to have the first New Year’s baby — she’s already bringing so much joy

— Grace Babasemi (Windsor Star)

Did you know: B.C. Lotteries said only about six per cent of bettors picked Kelowna General Hospital to host the province’s first baby this year [3].

Key Points

  • Windsor milestone: Windsor’s first baby of 2026 was a girl named Favour Jesuseum Josiah, born at 2:53 a.m. at Windsor Regional Hospital weighing seven pounds, one ounce; her mother said staff gave “a lot of extra attention” because no other babies arrived in the ward between midnight and mid-morning [2].
  • Alberta timing: Alberta Health Services said Edmonton’s first baby—a boy born at Grey Nuns Community Hospital—arrived at 12:03 a.m., and Calgary’s first baby, Theodore, was also born at 12:03 a.m. at South Health Campus hospital [7].
  • B.C. odds game: B.C. Lotteries Corp. offered a “game of chance” on which hospital would have the province’s first baby, posting 20-1 odds for Kelowna General and saying about six per cent of bettors chose it [3].
  • VCH first baby: Vancouver Coastal Health’s first baby of 2026 was born at 1:30 a.m. at Richmond General Hospital to Haruna and Jian Suzuki, with the parents sharing gratitude for the support they received [3].
  • Quebec midnight: In Quebec, one of the province’s first babies of 2026 was born exactly at midnight, as hospitals and parents welcomed New Year’s arrivals [4].
