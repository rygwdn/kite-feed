US drafts strict AI procurement rules amid Anthropic clash

Category: AI

The U.S. is drafting tougher AI procurement guidelines for civilian agencies as a dispute involving Anthropic and the Pentagon puts new pressure on how the government buys AI, according to the Financial Times and Reuters [4][3][6]. Under the draft rules, AI models purchased through civilian government contracts would need to be available for “any lawful” use. That requirement could curb vendors’ ability to enforce use restrictions and may reshape how AI firms sell to the federal government [4][3][6].
