Google starts selling $99 Google Home Speaker

Category: Technology

Google’s new Google Home Speaker went on sale this week as the company’s first smart speaker in roughly six years, priced at $99 or $99.99 depending on the listing, and built around Gemini for Home rather than the older Google Assistant experience [3][1][14]. Reviews describe a compact orb-style speaker with 360-degree audio, microphones for voice commands and a role as Google’s current entry point for Gemini in the smart home [2][1][6]. The launch changes Google’s speaker lineup: Engadget says the model replaces both the $99 Nest Audio and $49 Nest Mini, removing Google’s sub-$99 smart speaker option [2]. Reviewers broadly cited stronger sound and more natural voice control than older Google smart speakers, while also flagging limits such as some Gemini features sitting behind a Google Home subscription, occasional missed microphone pickups and recent bugs in the Google Home platform [2][1][10]. Before wider availability, a Reddit user bought the speaker early at Walmart and confirmed a permanently attached power cable of about 5 feet; Google’s own store specs list a 30W USB-C adapter and a “captive cable” [17][3][15]. The retail launch coincided with Google Home updates, including Gemini for Home Early Access changes aimed at reducing accidental hotword triggers and speeding up simple tasks such as alarms, timers and lists [16].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Voice behavior: Gemini can follow more natural, contextual commands than Google Assistant, and Android Headlines said users can interrupt it during responses [1][9].
  • Gemini engagement: ZDNET said that after 48 hours with the speaker, the natural conversation flow could lead users to rely on voice commands more often than with older Google Assistant devices, even with occasional imperfections [8].
  • Setup process: 9to5Google said setup took less than 10 minutes for an already configured Google Home user, with most device information handled in the Google Home app [7].
  • Device placement: 9to5Google said the taller and heavier speaker cannot be wall-mounted like the Nest Mini and needs shelf or counter space [6].

Perspectives

PCMag: PCMag gave the speaker an Editors' Choice rating of 4.5, calling it the strongest option for Google's ecosystem while saying Alexa users may find better value in the Amazon Echo Dot Max.

Sources: PCMag

Engadget: Engadget rated the device 8 out of 10 and framed the main question as whether Gemini for Home is good enough to run a smart home, not whether the hardware changes the category.

Sources: Engadget

Android Authority: Android Authority argued that a Gemini-powered Nest Hub now makes more sense than another speaker, and noted that $99 has become Google's recurring price point across products including the Fitbit Air, questioning whether the Home Speaker is the smart home device users have actually been waiting for [5].

Sources: Android Authority

Early Walmart buyer: The Reddit user who bought the device early praised the sound quality and simple setup process but identified the non-removable power cable as the main surprise in the unboxing.

Sources: Android Authority

Technical Details

  • Matter hub support: PCMag lists the Google Home Speaker as a Matter hub, while TechSpot says the device adds Gemini and expanded smart home capabilities alongside Matter support [1][12].
  • Local filtering: Google Home’s June update uses improved local filtering for Gemini for Home Early Access to reduce false starts from background noise or television audio [16].
  • On-device AI hardware: MakeUseOf says the new speaker includes an upgraded processor and neural processing unit designed for AI tasks [13].

Industry Impact

  • Smart display demand: Android Authority's reader poll found 60% preferred a new Nest Hub smart display and 29% wanted a larger Nest Hub Max successor, compared with 1% who said they were happy with the new Home Speaker [5].
  • Installed base: Gemini for Home support across nearly every Google Assistant speaker and display released in the past decade gives older Nest and Google Home devices a software upgrade path [13].
  • Home automation: Google's June Home updates extend beyond the speaker to Nest camera AI arrays and Nest climate hardware, including better face detection and HVAC alerts [16].
  • Market shift: The removal of the $49 Nest Mini option ends Google's budget smart speaker offering while Amazon continues to sell lower-priced competing speakers [2][common].

Action Items

  • Check cable reach before buying: Measure the distance from your intended shelf or counter location to the outlet, because the attached cable is about 5 feet long and cannot be swapped [17][15].
  • Try Gemini on existing devices first: If you own an older Google Assistant speaker or display, test Gemini for Home there before spending $99 on the new model [13].
