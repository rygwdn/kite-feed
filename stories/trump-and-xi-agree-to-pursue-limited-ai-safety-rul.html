Trump and Xi agree to pursue limited AI safety rules

Category: AI

President Donald Trump said he and Chinese President Xi Jinping want to work together on “reasonable” artificial-intelligence safety rules after Trump told Asia-Pacific leaders the United States opposes international rules that could control or stifle AI [19][32][8]. Trump described the proposed rules as “moderate” in a Fox News interview after meeting Xi in Shenzhen on Nov. 19 [19][30]. The Chinese Embassy in Washington said the countries should coordinate to prevent AI security risks and keep the technology under human control; Chinese state media attributed a similar call to Xi after the leaders met [19][8]. Trump said the two leaders had not discussed Nvidia or its former chief executive, Jensen Huang, in depth [19]. Trump did not specify what “minimal” rules would cover, and the reports described no detailed joint AI framework [32][30]. The United States and China compete over advanced AI models and chips [19][8].
