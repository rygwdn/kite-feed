Apple CEO presses software chief to accelerate AI plans as Siri agent plans emerge

Category: Technology

Apple CEO John Ternus has pressed software chief Craig Federighi to accelerate Apple’s AI plans as the company explores turning its Siri assistant into one that can pursue tasks without repeated instructions. Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reported that Apple could make Siri more agent-like as early as 2027, but Apple has not announced such a product or confirmed a release schedule [1][7][19]. The nearer-term changes are more limited: Siri is expected to gain finer control over Apple apps, including Photos and Safari, with iOS 27.3, while support for third-party app functions could follow in spring 2027. Those features would let users request actions such as editing pictures or booking a restaurant, but would not let the assistant independently manage an ongoing goal [1][26][22]. Rival assistants from OpenAI and Meta can track goals and continue work in the background. Apple is also changing its software-development process for upcoming operating systems; the Siri agent remains a reported ambition, not a confirmed feature [9][6][23].

Key Points

  • Development cycle: For the first iOS 28 development milestone, Apple moved from four weeks of coding and four weeks of integration to six weeks of coding and exploration followed by two weeks of integration [6][9].
  • Research spending: Apple reported fiscal 2026 research-and-development spending of $45.765 billion, up from $34.55 billion a year earlier, partly because of increased AI infrastructure investment [13].
  • Team uncertainty: Siri engineering vice president Mike Rockwell is reportedly considering leaving his role or Apple in 2027; reports identified Amar Subramanya and Sebastien Marineau as possible successors for some of his responsibilities [5][12][15].
  • App rollout: Apple’s iOS 27.3 developer beta allows third-party developers to test connecting app functions to Siri, while users are expected to gain access to those integrations later [18][22].

Perspectives

John Ternus, Apple CEO: On Apple’s Nov. 2 earnings call, Ternus praised the revamped Siri as “a new chapter in AI” for Apple and “the kind of Siri that we always wanted” [26].

Sources: PhoneArena

Craig Federighi, Apple software engineering chief: Reports on the internal debate describe Federighi as concerned about AI’s implications for privacy, security, society and employment [5][13].

Sources: Gizmodo

Apple: The company says data handled through Private Cloud Compute for Siri AI requests is not stored or made accessible to Apple [13].

Timeline

  • 1 September 2026: John Ternus took over as Apple CEO from Tim Cook [13].
  • 2 November 2026: Ternus discussed Siri during Apple’s earnings call [26][23].
  • 8 November 2026: Mark Gurman reported that Ternus had pressed Federighi to accelerate Apple’s AI plans [1][7].
  • December 2026: The public release of iOS 27.3 was expected to bring expanded Siri app controls [26][22].
  • Spring 2027: Third-party app integrations were expected to begin reaching users [1][22].

Historical Background

Apple first committed to a more personalized Siri in 2024. The revamped Siri shipped with iOS 27 after a delay of about two years [5][6].
