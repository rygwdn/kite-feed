Disney hires Character.AI chief as first companywide CTO

Category: AI

Disney has appointed Karandeep Anand, the former chief executive of generative-AI chatbot company Character.AI, as its first companywide chief technology officer. Anand will join as a senior executive vice president on October 2 and report directly to Disney CEO Josh D’Amaro, overseeing enterprise technology, infrastructure, data and AI platforms, product, and engineering [2][6][7]. Several Character.AI technical employees are expected to move to Disney with him [2][13]. The appointment follows Disney’s September 2025 cease-and-desist demand accusing Character.AI of hosting unauthorized chatbots impersonating Disney characters; Character.AI subsequently removed the characters [6][9]. Disney said Anand’s mandate is to modernize how the company develops and delivers technology across its businesses, while Character.AI said its app remains online and that it will provide further information about its future [2].

Key Points

  • Executive background: Anand previously spent more than 15 years at Microsoft, about six years at Facebook, and held a senior product role at fintech company Brex before joining Character.AI [6][13].
  • Corporate restructuring: The appointment came one day after Disney promoted former YouTube executive Adam Smith to lead its streaming business and reassigned Joe Earley to franchise and content strategy [11][1].
  • Safety scrutiny: Character.AI faced litigation alleging that its chatbots encouraged self-harm and suicide [6].
  • Selection: TechCrunch reported that new Disney CEO Josh D’Amaro chose Anand for the role after taking over from Bob Iger in March [6].

Perspectives

Disney: CEO Josh D’Amaro said the role will support three priorities: storytelling, technology serving creativity, and operating as one company [17].

Sources: Engadget

Character.AI: The company framed the move as a way to help Disney connect fans with its stories and characters [2].

Sources: Gizmodo

Disney employees: D’Amaro’s internal memo said Anand brings a rare mix of experience across infrastructure, consumer technology and AI [1].

Sources: Business Insider

Timeline

  • 2021: Character.AI was founded as an AI chatbot company [6].
  • May 2025: Anand became Character.AI chief executive after serving as board adviser [6].
  • 2 October 2026: Anand is scheduled to begin Disney’s companywide CTO role [2].

Industry Impact

  • Disney created its first companywide CTO position, assigning Anand responsibility for enterprise technology, infrastructure, data and AI platforms, product, and engineering [7][12].
  • Character.AI barred users under 18 from open-ended chat two months after Disney’s cease-and-desist letter [9].

Historical Background

Disney previously assigned technology chiefs to individual business units rather than the company as a whole [16][13]. Character.AI’s platform lets users create and interact with chatbots based on fictional characters, celebrities and original personas [2].

Business Angle

  • Disney has not disclosed financial targets or measurable returns for the technology reorganization [16][1].
  • Any Disney consumer-AI initiative will face a visible test of how it handles copyrighted characters alongside child-safety risks [6][9].
  • Disney’s streaming and direct-to-consumer reorganization creates a potential distribution channel for new AI-enabled products, but the sources provide no financial forecast for the strategy [11][20].
