Google adds Gemini-powered Ask Maps and immersive navigation

Category: AI

Google is rolling out new Google Maps features powered by its Gemini AI, including “Ask Maps,” a chat-style interface that lets people ask more complex questions about places and get help planning routes or trips in the mobile app [7][9][6][5]. Google and multiple outlets call it the biggest Maps update in more than a decade, pairing the new conversational layer with a redesigned navigation experience [9][6][8]. Alongside Ask Maps, Google is introducing “Immersive Navigation,” which adds 3D visuals of nearby buildings and landmarks to help users get their bearings while following directions [6][9][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Google’s framing: Google describes Ask Maps as a way to handle multi-part prompts (like building an itinerary) and surface suggestions directly in Maps, while Immersive Navigation aims to make real-world orientation easier [5][1].
  • Skepticism noted: Gizmodo flags a key concern: users will want strong safeguards against AI “hallucinations” when the system suggests routes or guidance [4].
  • Smoother voice cues: Voice guidance is also getting a more natural tone, including prompts that point out the turn after the next when that’s more useful [26][18].
  • Everyday use cases: PCMag highlights practical prompts — like getting unstuck when lost or finding somewhere to eat — framing Gemini as a hands-on helper inside Maps [6].
  • Navigation UI overhaul: Ars Technica says the update also refreshes the turn-by-turn navigation screen and controls — separate from the Gemini chat experience — along with additional AI-driven features [8].
  • UI and results format: Ask Maps shows up as a dedicated button near the search bar and can return answers as locations pinned on a custom map, aiming to replace manual review-sifting with a map-first response [11][18].
  • Community scale: Google says Ask Maps can pull from information across more than 300 million places and reviews contributed by the Google community [11][28].
  • Personalized suggestions: Ask Maps can tailor recommendations using your prior searches and saved places, so the same question may return different suggestions for different users [10][11][30].
  • Where it appears first: Wired says Ask Maps is rolling out in Google Maps on mobile, so desktop and web users may not see it at first [7].

Action Items

  • Use Immersive Navigation in unfamiliar areas: When the upgraded navigation experience reaches you, use the 3D building and landmark visuals to better match what’s on-screen to what’s around you [6][5].
  • Preview a drive before leaving: When Immersive Navigation reaches your device, start a route and use the 3D preview to spot tricky interchanges, lane changes, and arrival details earlier [18][26].
  • Try Ask Maps with a specific constraint: In Google Maps on iOS/Android (U.S. or India rollout), tap the “Ask Maps” button and add constraints like time, amenities, or vibe to see more tailored results [11][10].
  • Try Ask Maps for trip planning: In Google Maps on mobile, try the new Ask Maps experience and ask multi-part questions (for example, planning an afternoon itinerary) as the feature rolls out [7][5].
