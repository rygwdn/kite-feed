Nvidia CEO disputes stalled $100B OpenAI investment

Category: Technology

Nvidia and OpenAI are still discussing a memorandum of understanding signed in September 2025 that floated an investment figure of $100 billion, but some reporting says the eventual commitment could be revised down to “tens of billions” instead [1]. In response, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang publicly rejected claims of a cooling relationship, calling the idea of a rift “nonsense” and saying Nvidia will take part in OpenAI’s latest funding round, which he described as Nvidia’s largest investment to date [3][2][4]. The back-and-forth highlights how closely major AI labs and the companies that supply their computing infrastructure are linked—and how deal size, governance, and business strategy can shape the pace of AI buildouts even when partnerships remain intact [1][3].
