Cuban envoy urges Canada to oppose U.S. embargo at UN vote

Category: Canada

Cuba’s ambassador to Canada, Rodrigo Malmierca Diaz, is urging Ottawa to maintain its opposition to the U.S. embargo ahead of a United Nations vote next week on a Cuban-backed resolution calling for an end to the embargo. Canada has supported the motion in previous years, but Global Affairs Canada says it has not decided how it will vote this time [1][2][3]. Washington has halted Venezuelan oil shipments to Cuba and threatened tariffs on countries selling fuel to the island. Malmierca says Washington is also pressing countries to change their votes; U.S. Ambassador to the U.N. Mike Waltz has urged countries to reject the resolution, calling it propaganda. Cuba produces only 40 per cent of the fuel it needs, and shortages have caused blackouts and disrupted health care. Ottawa says it continues to press Havana on political and economic rights and the release of political prisoners [1][2][4].

The blockade and this intensification is, in practice, killing people.

— Rodrigo Malmierca Diaz, Cuban ambassador to Canada (The Canadian Press, republished by The Toronto Star)

  • Aid channel: Canada has pledged $13 million in humanitarian assistance for Cubans, to be delivered through international organizations rather than the Cuban government [1][2][3].
  • Business exits: Mining company Sherritt and hotel operator Royalton have both ceased operations in Cuba [1][2].
  • Reform agenda: Malmierca says Cuba is implementing 176 measures intended to expand private-sector activity, encourage foreign investment and increase municipal autonomy [1][2].
