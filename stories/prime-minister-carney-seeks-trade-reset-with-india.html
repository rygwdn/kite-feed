Prime Minister Carney seeks trade reset with India

Category: Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney has arrived in India for stops in Mumbai and New Delhi, aiming to deepen trade and economic ties and continue Canada’s diplomatic reset with Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s government [2]. The trip includes a Canadian delegation and is framed as a push to rebuild working trust after a strained period, with Ottawa looking for practical cooperation while reopening political channels with New Delhi [1][5]. Several outlets cast the mission as part of a broader Indo-Pacific push. Carney is also slated to visit Australia and Japan as Canada tries to deepen ties with other regional partners and reduce its reliance on the United States by building “middle power” relationships [6]. Coverage notes the Canada-India relationship has been weighed down by the fallout from Canada’s 2023 allegation of Indian involvement in the killing of Sikh separatist Hardeep Singh Nijjar in Canada [5]. Even so, the reporting strikes a cautiously hopeful tone. Both governments appear to be testing whether cooperation on energy, technology, and other economic priorities can build enough momentum to steady the relationship, even as sensitive issues remain unresolved [3][5].

Historical Background

Canada–India relations deteriorated sharply in 2023 after then prime minister Justin Trudeau alleged Indian involvement in the killing of Sikh separatist Hardeep Singh Nijjar on Canadian soil. Trade talks that had been described as close to an initial agreement were suspended, according to The Walrus [5]. The Walrus also reports New Delhi has long criticized Canada’s handling of Sikh separatist activism, while Ottawa points to liberal-democratic limits on restricting political expression [5].
