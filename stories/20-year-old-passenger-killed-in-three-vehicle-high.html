20-year-old passenger killed in three-vehicle Highway 401 crash

Category: Canada

A 20-year-old passenger from Stouffville was killed Sunday evening when a pickup truck struck a sedan in the westbound collector lanes of Highway 401 near Kennedy Road in Scarborough, pushing it into an SUV, Ontario Provincial Police said. The collision happened at about 7:40 p.m. [1][2][3] Police said the passenger died at the scene. The sedan’s driver was taken to a trauma centre with serious but non-life-threatening injuries. Police said Sunday night they were still gathering information. [1][3]

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Road reopened: The highway, which was expected to remain closed for at least six hours Sunday night, has since reopened [3].
  • Police appeal: Investigators asked anyone with information about the collision to contact police [2].
