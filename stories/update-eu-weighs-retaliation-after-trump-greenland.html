Update: EU weighs retaliation after Trump Greenland tariff threat

Category: World

In the latest development, President Trump said the U.S. will impose an additional 10% import tariff from February 1 on goods from eight European countries—Denmark, Norway, Sweden, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Finland and the UK—linking the move to U.S. demands to be allowed to buy Greenland [21][14][5][33]. Trump said the tariff rate would rise to 25% on June 1 if no deal is reached [14][5]. European leaders and the targeted governments rejected the pressure as an attack on sovereignty and warned of a “dangerous downward spiral” in transatlantic ties, while also signalling they prefer dialogue first to avoid a wider trade war [21][20][19]. The dispute is playing out alongside high-level diplomacy this week, including the World Economic Forum in Davos, where leaders are expected to discuss the standoff and broader security issues [30][26].
