Apple extends iPhone support, ships AirTag 2 updates

Category: Technology

Apple rolled out a broad set of iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac software updates in late January 2026, spanning both current releases and unusually old versions of iOS, to keep key services working and improve compatibility with new hardware [1][9][7]. A standout is iOS 12.5.8 for iPhone 5s and iPhone 6, which Apple says extends the certificate needed for iMessage, FaceTime, and device activation so those features keep working after January 2027 [7][9][19]. In parallel, Apple launched a second-generation AirTag and issued iOS 26.2.1/iPadOS 26.2.1/watchOS 26.2.1 to support it, while iOS 26.3 beta 3 and macOS Tahoe 26.3 beta 3 rolled out to developers and public beta testers ahead of an expected end-of-January release [4][32][5].

Did you know: AirTag 2 can be identified by revised back text printed in all capital letters, according to MacRumors’ feature rundown [38].

Key Points

  • AirTag upgrades: AirTag 2 adds a second-generation Ultra Wideband chip, claims up to 50% longer Precision Finding range, and increases speaker volume (reported as up to 50% louder) to make finding items easier and improve anti-stalking audibility [39][38][40].
  • Watch precision: Precision Finding support expands beyond iPhone to Apple Watch models with UWB (Series 9 and later, and Ultra 2 and later), with iOS 26.3 described as adding AirTag 2 support including Precision Finding on Apple Watch [5][40][38].
  • Australia emergency: Apple updated guidance in an Australia-focused support document saying iPhone 12 or earlier users should install the latest updates to help ensure 000 emergency calls work reliably, following a December network failure that affected emergency calling and prompted software mitigations [8].
  • Switching tool: iOS 26.3 introduces a setup-time tool to move data from an iPhone to an Android device without requiring a dedicated transfer app, according to MacRumors’ beta coverage [5].
  • EU interoperability: Beta notes point to EU-related changes that affect third-party wearables, including a new Notification Forwarding settings area for non-Apple devices, and other EU-mandated interoperability adjustments [5][29].

Technical Details

  • Service certificate extension: iOS 12.5.8 (and related older-OS patches) extend a certificate required for iMessage, FaceTime, and device activation so those services keep functioning after January 2027 [7][19][9].
  • Ultra Wideband (UWB) 2: AirTag 2 uses Apple’s second-generation UWB chip, which enables longer-range Precision Finding guidance in the Find My app compared with the original AirTag [38][39].
  • Precision Finding: Precision Finding uses UWB for close-range direction and distance cues to an item tag; Apple says AirTag 2 can be located from farther away than before and now supports compatible Apple Watches with UWB [38][40].
  • Background security updates: Apple’s developer beta cycle for the 26-generation OSes included additional background security updates delivered outside the usual update flow, which AppleInsider noted may be a test of the mechanism [2].

Industry Impact

  • Device longevity: By issuing iOS 12.5.8 for 2013–2014-era iPhones and patches for iOS 15 and iOS 16 lines, Apple extends usable life for older hardware and could modestly reduce immediate replacement pressure for some users [7][9][28].
  • Bluetooth tracker market: AirTag 2’s longer range and louder alerts raise competitive expectations for consumer trackers, while keeping the same price positions Apple aggressively against other tags that rely on Bluetooth crowdsourcing networks [49][36].
  • Travel operations: Coverage highlighted expanded airline support for locating luggage, which may encourage more passengers to use tracker-based recovery workflows and push airlines to further integrate with Find My-style systems [48].
  • Cross-platform onboarding: iOS 26.3’s setup-time iPhone-to-Android transfer tool and EU-driven wearable changes reflect growing regulatory and competitive pressure toward interoperability, potentially changing how platform lock-in works at the margins [5][29].

Historical Background

Apple commonly continues issuing security-only iOS updates for a period after a device stops receiving the latest major iOS version, but revisiting very old releases years later is relatively rare [9]. The original AirTag launched about five years ago, and Apple added anti-stalking protections over time after reports of misuse; the AirTag 2 arrives as the first major hardware refresh since that debut [39].

Q&A

Q: Where is AirTag 2 launching later, even if it’s available in the US now?

A: Apple lists Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam as “coming soon,” with South Korea orders starting January 28 (local time) [37].

Q: Which carriers support the new “limit precise location” carrier setting in iOS 26.3 beta?

A: Macworld reported support for EE or BT in the U.K. and Boost Mobile in the U.S., with availability depending on carrier support [29].

Q: What’s the practical downside of AirTag 2’s unchanged physical design?

A: Commentary noted the tag still lacks a built-in keyring hole, so many users will need a holder or accessory to attach it to keys or luggage [40][47].

Action Items

  • Update older iPhones still on iOS 12: On iPhone 5s/6, go to Settings → General → Software Update and install iOS 12.5.8 to keep iMessage/FaceTime and activation working after January 2027 [7][19].
  • If you’re in Australia, prioritize emergency-call fixes: If you use an iPhone 12 or earlier (or a cellular Apple Watch), install the latest available iOS/watchOS updates per Apple’s guidance to reduce the risk of 000 calling issues [8].
  • Check AirTag 2 compatibility before buying: Confirm your iPhone/iPad/Apple Watch meets AirTag 2 and Precision Finding requirements (including the Apple Watch models that support it) before upgrading existing tags [5][40][46].
