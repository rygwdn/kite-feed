Rockstar says GTA VI generated $3.79 billion on launch day

Category: Technology

Rockstar Games said Grand Theft Auto VI generated $3.79 billion in first-day retail sales and drew 32.5 million preorders, setting a record for an entertainment launch. The open-world crime game arrived Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 (PS5) and Xbox Series consoles, 13 years after Grand Theft Auto V. It follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in Leonida, a fictional version of Florida [40][28][37]. Rockstar’s figure exceeds market tracker Sensor Tower’s early estimate of $2.36 billion in revenue and about 24.6 million copies [2][40]. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two Interactive had published a detailed sales breakdown to explain the difference [43]. The game is available on those consoles; no dates have been announced for a PC release or online mode [22]. Rockstar developed the game over several years. Take-Two Interactive Chief Executive Strauss Zelnick said the pace of releases across its major franchises will look “dramatically different over the next decade” [39][36].

Did you know: Spotify said the GTA VI artist page had 29 million monthly listeners before the game launched [68].

Key Points

  • Network surge: Internet exchange DE-CIX recorded traffic of 33.03 terabits per second on Nov. 18, while Comcast said the preload period produced the busiest day in its network’s history [22][34].
  • Console engagement: Circana data showed that 61% of U.S. PS5 accounts were used to play the game on Nov. 19, while 56% of Xbox players globally played it; average play time was 513 minutes on PS5 and 494 on Xbox [34].
  • Critical reception: Metacritic’s critic score reached 99 out of 100 [44][35].
  • Platform split: Sensor Tower estimated that roughly three-quarters of early purchases were for PS5, and GameSpot reported that Sony receives a 30% share of those sales [43][55].

Perspectives

Rockstar developers seeking unionization: Developers were reportedly trying to unionize over concerns that the studio might cut staff now that large-scale production work is complete [40].

Technical Details

  • Download footprint: The digital game requires more than 200 gigabytes of storage; New Zealand network operator Chorus described its 212GB download as more than a quarter of average monthly household data use [27][25].
  • Console frame rate: A PCWorld review said only the PS5 Pro maintained a stable 30 frames per second and identified that frame-rate ceiling as a limitation of the current console versions [5].

Industry Impact

  • Game discovery and media: IGN and Rockstar jointly launched a free, interactive online strategy guide with a map and task trackers. IGN said it would update the guide after launch [65].
  • Console sales: A Turkish online retailer cited in reporting recorded a 67% rise in console sales and an 88% rise in PS5 sales around the game’s release [53].

Historical Background

Grand Theft Auto V, released in 2013, accounts for more than 230 million of the franchise’s reported 470 million global sales [2]. Development of GTA VI did not begin in earnest until after Red Dead Redemption 2 arrived in 2018. Rockstar also refined the technology and processes needed to make games at this scale [36][39].

Business Angle

  • Reported development costs exceed $1 billion, making sustained sales and post-launch monetization important to recovering the investment [43][56].
