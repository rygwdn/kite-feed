Update: Hydro-Québec restores most power after ice storm

Category: Canada

An ice storm swept across southern Quebec on Wednesday, cutting electricity to more than 200,000 Hydro‑Québec customers at its peak [2][4]. By Thursday morning, crews had restored service to most homes. About 8,300 customers were still without power as repairs continued [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Schools affected: Thousands of students missed school for a second straight day as outages and icy conditions lingered in parts of Quebec, La Presse reported [4].
  • Elsewhere in Canada: Strong wind gusts hit southern B.C., where BC Hydro said about 17,000 customers lost power, according to The Globe and Mail [5].

Perspectives

Hydro‑Québec: The utility said crews kept working Thursday to restore service after ice brought down branches and left some streets and sidewalks dangerously slick in parts of southern Quebec [2].

Sources: The Lethbridge Herald

La Presse (regional reporting): La Presse said some regions took a harder hit than others. The outlet described the freezing-rain event as less severe than expected, even as disruptions continued in several areas [4].

Sources: La Presse

CTV News: CTV said Hydro‑Québec used a new, previously untried technique to protect the grid during the ice storm [1].

Sources: Google News (CTV link)

National Observer (storm overview): The outlet framed the system as part of a broader storm stretching from Central Canada to the Atlantic, bringing a mix of heavy rain, freezing rain, and heavy snow [3].
