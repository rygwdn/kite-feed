OpenAI executive Fidji Simo exits full-time role

Category: AI

Fidji Simo, one of OpenAI’s top executives, said she will leave her full-time role and become a part-time adviser after an extended medical leave tied to a chronic illness she has lived with for seven years [1][2][17][19][30]. Simo said in a Thursday staff note and X post that her recovery had proved longer and more complex than expected, after she first took medical leave in April following a relapse or severe exacerbation of postural tachycardia syndrome, known as POTS [2][17][22][30]. Simo joined OpenAI’s board in 2024, then moved into a senior operating role in 2025 overseeing applications, product and business operations under CEO Sam Altman [2][17][21]. Before OpenAI, she led Instacart from 2021 through its 2023 IPO and previously spent more than a decade at Meta, including work running the Facebook app [2][21][22]. After she leaves, OpenAI will divide applications and business operations duties among President Greg Brockman, CFO Sarah Friar and Chief Strategy Officer Jason Kwon, according to Blognone and ETNews [20][26].

Key Points

  • Reporting shift: When Simo took the OpenAI role, COO Brad Lightcap, CFO Sarah Friar and CPO Kevin Weil began reporting to her as Altman shifted attention toward research, compute and safety [2].
  • Executive turnover: Simo’s April medical-leave memo also disclosed Lightcap’s move into special projects and CMO Kate Rouch’s departure to recover from cancer, while Weil has since left the company [2].
  • Product oversight: OpenAI later reorganized product teams by putting Thibault Sottiaux in charge of core products including ChatGPT, while Brockman took over product strategy [17].
  • Business focus: TechCrunch reported that ChatGPT growth cooled late last year and missed internal revenue targets, prompting OpenAI to lean more heavily into coding tools, where it trailed Anthropic [2].
