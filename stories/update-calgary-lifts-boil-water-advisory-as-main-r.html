Update: Calgary lifts boil-water advisory as main repairs continue

Category: Canada

Calgary has lifted the boil-water advisory for several northwest neighbourhoods after water testing confirmed it met health guidelines, even as crews continue repairing the Bearspaw south feeder main break that normally supplies most of the city’s drinking water [4][3][1]. Stage 4 water restrictions remain in place while the damaged pipe section is excavated and removed, and the city is urging residents to keep conserving to protect system supply as people head back to work and school [3]. Traffic disruptions around 16 Avenue N.W. near Sarcee Trail are also continuing during the repair work, with partial reopening steps underway [3][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Calgary directed drivers to find current detours and updates through its online traffic information page (calgary.ca/trafficinfo) during the ongoing repair work [3].

Key Points

  • Repair progress: City crews removed water from the rupture site and excavated the damaged section, with pipe removal work expected to begin Sunday afternoon as the next phase of repairs [3].
  • Neighbourhoods affected: The advisory had applied to Parkdale, Montgomery, Point McKay and West Hillhurst before being lifted Sunday evening [3].
  • Water demand: On Saturday, Calgary’s water use rose to 495 million litres, which officials said strained supply as restrictions remain in effect [3][2].
  • Road detours: Westbound 16th Avenue N.W. between 49th Street and Sarcee Trail remains closed, with reduced speeds and posted detours through the work zone [3].
  • Commuter update: CTV reported the city expects eastbound 16 Avenue to reopen Monday as traffic changes continue around the repair area [2].
