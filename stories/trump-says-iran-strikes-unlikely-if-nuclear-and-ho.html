Trump says Iran strikes unlikely if nuclear and Hormuz talks advance

Category: World

President Donald Trump said on Nov. 8 that renewed U.S. strikes on Iran were “highly unlikely” if negotiations produce a strong agreement on Tehran’s nuclear program and reopening the Strait of Hormuz, while warning that U.S. forces remain ready to attack. He said the military had identified 38 bridges as potential targets. The conflict, now in its ninth month, remains at a stalemate, with shipping incidents continuing around the strait [30][4][8]. U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Washington was not close to a diplomatic breakthrough and called Iran’s demands unacceptable. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said mediators had stopped traveling after the Nov. 3 U.S. midterm elections. He reiterated Tehran’s offer to reopen the strait within two days if Washington meets its conditions, including ending the war on all fronts [21][17][22]. U.S. Central Command has established a roughly 20-person task force to disrupt routes carrying weapons and sanctioned goods across Iran’s land borders and the Caspian Sea, expanding U.S. efforts beyond its naval blockade [36][38]. Trump is scheduled to meet Qatar’s prime minister on Monday, Nov. 9, as Doha continues mediation [21].
