Hunter Biden says Joe Biden’s cancer has spread further

Category: World

Hunter Biden said in a BBC Newsnight interview broadcast late Friday that former U.S. President Joe Biden’s prostate cancer has spread further in his body and is causing significant pain [6][5][20][8]. “The cancer has spread, metastasized into his bones and further,” Hunter Biden said, adding that it is “very painful” and “very debilitating in many respects” [4][11][8]. Biden, 83, disclosed in May 2025, less than four months after leaving the White House, that he had an aggressive form of prostate cancer that had already spread to his bones [11][10][9]. His office said the cancer had a Gleason score of 9, placing it in Grade Group 5, and in October said he underwent radiation therapy and hormone therapy [20][14][13]. A Biden spokesman declined to comment Saturday on Hunter Biden’s interview [11][13][42].
