B.C. trial hears Chinese officers vanished during Vancouver visit

Category: Canada

A British Columbia Supreme Court trial heard that three Chinese police officials disappeared for about six hours during an RCMP-escorted visit to Vancouver in 2018, raising concern inside the force that they might be trying to return someone to China outside Canadian legal processes [1][2][4]. The testimony came on the second day of the trial of William Majcher, a former RCMP officer who has pleaded not guilty to a charge under Canada’s Security of Information Act tied to allegations that he acted on behalf of China [2]. According to testimony, Canadian officials treated the visit as highly sensitive and kept the Chinese delegation under close supervision while it was in Vancouver [2].
