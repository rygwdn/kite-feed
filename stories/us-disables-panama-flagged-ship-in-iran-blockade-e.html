US disables Panama-flagged ship in Iran blockade enforcement

Category: World

U.S. Central Command said a U.S. Navy MH-60 helicopter fired two Hellfire missiles into the engine room of the Panama-flagged M/V Vela Nova on Tuesday after its civilian crew ignored repeated warnings while sailing through the Gulf of Oman toward a U.S.-blockaded Iranian port [5][3][11][4][43][28]. CENTCOM said the strike disabled the ship’s steering gear and that the vessel was “no longer transiting to Iran” in violation of the blockade [5][3][11][14][36]. The operation was the third time U.S. forces had forcibly halted a vessel since Washington reinstated its blockade of Iranian ports on July 14 [3][11][7][20][48]. Iran has demanded that the United States end the port blockade as part of its conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz [3][28][61].
