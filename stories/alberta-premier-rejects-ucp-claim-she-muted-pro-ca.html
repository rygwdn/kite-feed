Alberta premier rejects UCP claim she muted pro-Canada ads

Category: Canada

Alberta Premier Danielle Smith rejected UCP president Rob Smith's claim that the provincial government's referendum advertising campaign was neutral on separation. She said she supports Alberta remaining in Canada and is campaigning for that position in the Oct. 19 vote [4][1]. The dispute concerns how the government presents the referendum questions in a $4 million advertising campaign, with the UCP president criticizing its treatment of the pro-Canada position [4][1].

— Danielle Smith (CBC News)

Key Points

  • Party identity: Danielle Smith has said the UCP is not a separatist party and should listen to alternative voices within its membership [6].
  • Internal criticism: UCP president Rob Smith said many party members had expressed disappointment with the government’s handling of the advertising campaign [4].
  • Campaign framing: Smith has described her position as seeking a sovereign Alberta in a united Canada [2].
