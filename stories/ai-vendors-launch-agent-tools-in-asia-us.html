AI vendors launch agent tools in Asia, US

Category: AI

AI companies, cloud providers and public agencies announced agentic AI products, infrastructure tools and commercialization programs on Aug. 3-4. Microsoft released Orchard, an open-source framework built around reusable environments to train and evaluate software-engineering, web-navigation and personal-assistant agents, while Google Cloud made Cortex Framework v7 generally available for building AI agents on SAP data in BigQuery and Knowledge Catalog [12][22]. Google Cloud Korea also said it helped South Korean travel platform Yeogi Eottae build YAPP, an internal agent platform that routes natural-language requests to specialized agents and sub-agents [29]. In Asia, Sakura Internet began offering Preferred Networks' PLaMo 3.0 Prime through Sakura's AI Engine, describing it as a Japanese foundation model for reasoning, business documents and agent use cases [23]. South Korea's science ministry and KOSA opened AX360°, a portal that aggregates government AI transformation programs, private-sector AI resources and domestic AI company information [21]. South Korea's health ministry and KHIDI selected six digital medical-device consortia to support commercialization of AI tools used in areas including stroke, Parkinson's disease, chest X-rays, breast cancer and endoscopy [24]. Financing announcements included Intelligence, the company behind Design Arena, which announced a $7.9 million seed round led by Index Ventures after building a feedback platform used by 5.3 million people [11][6]. Ant Group's embodied AI division Robbyant began seeking external funding for work on a general-purpose robot brain [20]. Other AI-related announcements included Scale Social AI's new financing for enterprise content infrastructure, Searchdoc's construction AI seminar for about 30 Korean construction companies and government agencies, Woori Bank's plan to launch generative-AI customer-service functions in phases through May 2027, and you.bot's API gateway offering access to more than 70 AI models [17][19][25][18].

Key Points

  • Agent benchmarks: Microsoft said Orchard-SWE reached 69.7% on SWE-bench Verified and 73.0% with value-model reranking using about 3 billion active parameters [12].
  • Korean healthcare: Lunit's selected consortium plans to apply chest X-ray and breast-cancer diagnostic support AI at 10 hospitals and accumulate 250,000 real-world data cases [24].
  • Travel platform: Yeogi Eottae's YAPP uses a coordinator agent to distribute tasks among 10 specialist agents and more than 30 sub-agents [29].
  • Model access: you.bot describes a credit-based gateway where 1 credit equals $0.01 and failed or empty model calls receive automatic refunds [18].

Perspectives

Microsoft Research: Microsoft framed Orchard as a response to reliance on proprietary sandboxes, closed training pipelines and private datasets that limit reproducibility in agentic AI research.

Technical Details

  • Orchard supports training agents directly inside real deployment harnesses such as Codex, OpenClaw, and ZeroClaw, enabling reuse of environments, data pipelines, and evaluation workflows across tasks [12].
  • PLaMo context window: PLaMo 3.0 Prime expands supported context length from 64k to 256k, a change Sakura linked to practical AI-agent use [23].
  • Hybrid cloud integration: Yeogi Eottae adopted MCP in YAPP so existing assets and external tools can connect through plug-in style integrations despite Google Cloud serving as the main platform [29].

Industry Impact

  • Enterprise software: Google Cloud Cortex Framework v7 provides purpose-built data product accelerators for SAP, turning ERP records into agent-ready data products that feed Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform [22].
  • Medical technology: The selected consortia include JLK for stroke AI at 22 hospitals, Lunit for chest X-ray and breast cancer AI at 10 hospitals, and others, aiming to accumulate real-world data and pursue insurance reimbursement [24].
  • Marketing technology: Scale Social AI said its financing will support AI content intelligence, multi-location activation, enterprise approvals and content performance measurement [17].
  • Construction technology: Searchdoc said participants saw workflows for extracting bid conditions, comparing project standards and automatically verifying risk items in construction documents [19].

Business Angle

  • Agent infrastructure companies may capture recurring value if they become the operating layer for workflows rather than a replaceable interface [12][22][common].
  • Healthcare AI commercialization has a clear need, but reimbursement and hospital-adoption barriers remain central execution risks [24][common].
  • Model gateways can draw cost-conscious developers, but pricing advantages alone may be weak if providers change access terms or customers demand direct vendor relationships [18][common].
  • Localized models and language-specific tools can create differentiation in Japan, Korea and India, where generic English-first products may not address business documents, accents or public-sector needs [23][27][common].
