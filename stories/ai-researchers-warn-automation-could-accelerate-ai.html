AI researchers warn automation could accelerate AI development

Category: AI

A group of 22 researchers released a paper through the University of Cambridge’s AI Science & Policy Programme on Sept. 28 examining whether automating AI research and development could trigger an “intelligence explosion” [17][37]. The authors include Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio and Andrew Barto, along with researchers from major AI companies [17][37]. They warn that AI systems could help build more capable systems at an accelerating pace, potentially compressing years of progress into months or less [17][37]. The authors examine the potential effects of rapid progress and propose policy responses [17]. They warn that an intelligence explosion could pose extreme risks, including the possibility that humans lose control of advanced AI systems [17][37].

  • Current automation: The paper says AI systems already write most of the code at the companies developing them and could automate most AI R&D within a few years [17].
