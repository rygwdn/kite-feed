Bear attacks 3-year-old girl at Maple Ridge home

Category: Canada

A three-year-old girl was hospitalized after an adult black bear attacked her Sunday while she was playing on a backyard deck at her family’s home in Maple Ridge, B.C. [1][2][3][5]. A nearby parent intervened and chased the bear away, and the child was flown by air ambulance to hospital with injuries RCMP described as non-life-altering [1][2][3][5]. Police said the bear returned to the residence and nearby area several times while officers were at the scene and showed signs of aggression. Officers shot the bear around noon after the attack, but it fled into a wooded area after being struck as authorities advised nearby residents to stay indoors or remain vigilant [2][3][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Street location: RCMP placed the attack near 240 Street and 124 Avenue, and the Vancouver Sun identified the call location as the 12300-block of 240 Street [2][3].
  • Hospital destination: The Conservation Officer Service said the child was taken to BC Children’s Hospital for treatment [1][5].
  • Response teams: Maple Ridge RCMP, the B.C. Conservation Officer Service and B.C. Ambulance Service personnel responded to the scene [2][3].

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: Police advised residents near the 12300-block of 240 Street to stay indoors and remain vigilant while officials searched for the injured bear. [5][2]
