Swiss police investigate Kerzers postal bus fire deaths

Category: World

A postal bus burst into flames on the main street of Kerzers in western Switzerland on Tuesday evening, killing at least six people and injuring others, according to police and multiple media reports [1][2][3][5][6][7][9]. Authorities said the fire started around 6:25pm local time (5:25pm GMT). Emergency crews rushed in as thick smoke and large flames poured from the vehicle [6][9]. Investigators say early signs point to a deliberate act, but they stress the motive is still unclear and they are not drawing conclusions yet [1][5][8][9]. Some coverage cited accounts that a person on board may have set themselves on fire, though police have not publicly confirmed any specific scenario [3][4]. Investigators are still working to establish exactly what happened [7][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Injured count: Early reports put the number of injured at either three or five, suggesting the casualty figures are still being clarified [3][6][9].
  • Where it happened: Kerzers is about 20km (12 miles) west of Switzerland’s capital, Bern [6][9].
  • Police caution: Police said a "voluntary act" may be behind the fire, but they have avoided discussing hypotheses such as suicide or terror while inquiries continue [1][4][5].
  • Video evidence: Social media videos showed flames several metres high and black smoke rising from the bus [6].
