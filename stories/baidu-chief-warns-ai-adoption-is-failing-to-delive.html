Baidu chief warns AI adoption is failing to deliver business returns

Category: AI

Baidu Chief Executive Robin Li said at an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) business summit in Shenzhen on Nov. 17 that AI adoption in business is falling short of expectations. Despite rapid increases in computing and cloud spending and token use, many companies have not seen clear gains in revenue or profit, he said. He warned that failure to find effective applications could turn the AI boom into a bubble [58][57]. Microsoft Chief Executive Satya Nadella raised a separate concern at a Microsoft Ignite roundtable in San Francisco. He said AI’s economic and political effects will depend on whether its benefits spread widely, warning that a single model could expand into health care, financial services and government [30][23]. Li said companies need to identify real-world uses and adapt how they work to benefit from AI. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping were expected to discuss AI safety at APEC talks on Thursday, Nov. 19 [58][57].

Key Points

  • Uneven returns: A Deloitte survey of 1,278 senior leaders across 18 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation economies and India found that only 7% of companies met its criteria for “AI compounders.” Of those companies, 64% said AI generated material enterprise-wide or new value, compared with 13% of companies classified as adopters [43].
  • Small businesses: Meituan said the number of merchants using its AI assistant in Chinese cities below the top two tiers grew more than 200% month over month, while merchant-initiated conversations nearly tripled [40].
  • Engineering output: Octopus’s blog cited figures from LaunchDarkly’s Release Autopilot announcement: with roughly the same number of engineers, the number of pull requests merged each month more than doubled, while time spent in major and critical incidents fell about 70% [4].
  • Usage growth: Google’s AI model APIs processed more than 32 billion tokens per minute, up 40% from the second quarter and more than fourfold from a year earlier, according to Global Finance [26].

Perspectives

Robin Li, Baidu chief executive: Li said companies should change how they organize work by breaking down job boundaries, preserving employees’ tacit knowledge and building complete workflows around AI; he cited autonomous driving as an example of AI’s practical value [58].
