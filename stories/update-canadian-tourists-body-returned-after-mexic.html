Update: Canadian tourist's body returned after Mexico shooting

Category: Canada

In the latest development, Mexican officials said the family of a Canadian tourist killed in the shooting at the Teotihuacán archaeological site identified her body and that authorities turned it over to them [1][4]. Officials say she was the only person killed when a gunman opened fire from the Pyramid of the Moon on Monday before taking his own life [1][4][5]. The attack struck one of Mexico's best-known tourist and archaeological destinations outside Mexico City, sending visitors running for cover [2][3][5]. Witness and official accounts indicate several other visitors were injured, including six Americans, two of whom suffered gunshot wounds [5]. The site later reopened even as Canadian and Mexican authorities continued responding to the aftermath [1].

Historical Background

Teotihuacán is an ancient archaeological complex northeast of Mexico City and one of the country's most visited heritage sites [common]. The Pyramid of the Moon and Pyramid of the Sun are its central landmarks, which makes the location especially significant because it is known internationally as a cultural tourism destination rather than a conflict zone [common].
