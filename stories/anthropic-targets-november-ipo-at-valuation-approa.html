Anthropic targets November IPO at valuation approaching $2 trillion

Category: AI

Anthropic is preparing for a possible mid-November initial public offering that could value the Claude developer at $1.8 trillion to $2 trillion, according to people familiar with the discussions. The company could begin marketing the offering during the week of Nov. 9 and aims to start trading before the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday on Nov. 26, though the schedule remains under discussion and could change [5][28][1][37]. The proposed valuation contrasts with 2025 revenue of about $4.6 billion, up from $386 million in 2024, and an operating loss exceeding $8 billion. Its reported net loss was nearly $42 billion, including more than $34 billion from changes in the fair value of liabilities, rather than operating expenses alone [28][29][37][30]. Anthropic had previously delayed its listing timetable and was preparing to file publicly after the summer. It plans to meet prospective investors at its San Francisco headquarters on Oct. 14; the company is still expected to list by year-end, according to people familiar with the plans [9][40][1].

  • IPO scale: Anthropic reportedly aims to match or exceed the scale of SpaceX’s IPO [5][28][1].
