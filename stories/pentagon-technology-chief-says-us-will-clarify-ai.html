Pentagon technology chief says U.S. will clarify AI rules this month

Category: AI

The U.S. government plans to publish a guide to the laws and regulations covering domestic artificial intelligence labs later in November, Pentagon Chief Technology Officer Emil Michael said Nov. 4 at a Wall Street Journal technology conference in Napa, California. Michael, vice chair of President Donald Trump’s intergovernmental Super Intelligence Task Force, said the task force expects to outline the regulatory landscape “in the next week or two” [3][2]. Michael said the goal is to give the public confidence that AI companies will act responsibly. He warned that without a clear framework, lawmakers could produce separate rules that might harm the industry. He did not specify which parts of AI development the guidance would cover. The guidance is intended to clarify existing requirements for U.S. AI labs. Michael also said companies had agreed to safety measures, including third-party audits and an independent board committee for safety complaints [1][5].
