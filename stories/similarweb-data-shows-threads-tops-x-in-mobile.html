Similarweb data shows Threads tops X in mobile

Category: Technology

New estimates from market analytics firm Similarweb indicate Meta’s Threads has surpassed Elon Musk’s X in global daily active users on iOS and Android as of early January 2026, reflecting a steady shift in mobile social habits rather than a one-off spike [2][3][5][4][1][8][7]. The same data suggests X still commands far more attention on the web, highlighting that the two services’ strengths now differ by device and use case [2][3][5]. Overall, the moment is a cheerful sign of real competition in text-first social apps—more choices, more feature experimentation, and more ways for creators and communities to find their people [2][5].

Did you know: Similarweb’s figures cited in coverage put Threads at more than 400 million monthly active users [2].

Key Points

  • Growth snapshot: Similarweb’s year-over-year view shows Threads rising sharply (about 37.8% globally on mobile) while X’s daily mobile audience declined (about 11.9%) over the same period, pointing to a longer-run trend rather than a sudden migration [5].
  • US still tight: In the United States, Similarweb estimates X retains a narrow mobile edge (roughly 21.2 million daily users vs. about 19.5 million for Threads), even as Threads’ US usage has grown faster over the past year [5][3].
  • Meta playbook: TechCrunch-reported analysis cited by Slashdot attributes Threads’ momentum partly to cross-promotion inside Instagram and Facebook, plus a rapid cadence of new features aimed at creators and communities [2].
  • Feature rollouts: Over the past year, Threads has added or tested product ideas including interest-based communities, stronger filters, DMs, long-form text, disappearing posts, and even games in testing, as it tries to deepen daily engagement habits [2].
  • Safety guardrails: Engadget notes X faced renewed criticism tied to xAI’s Grok image feature producing sexualized edits of women’s photos without consent; the company responded by disabling image generation for nonsubscribers and adding stronger guardrails [5].

Timeline

  • Late October to early November 2025: Similarweb data suggests Threads moved ahead of X on mobile [3].
  • January 7, 2026: Similarweb estimates 141.5M Threads daily mobile users globally [3][2].
  • January 13, 2026: Similarweb estimates X far ahead in daily web visitors [3].

Technical Details

  • Daily active users (DAU): DAU is a metric for how many unique people use an app in a day; Similarweb’s DAU estimates are the basis for the reported mobile lead by Threads [3][2][5].
  • Cross-promotion: Cross-promotion means marketing a product inside a company’s other apps; coverage credits Meta’s Instagram/Facebook promotion as one factor supporting Threads’ mobile growth [2].
  • Web vs mobile usage: Reports separate mobile app activity from web visits, showing X’s relative strength on desktop/web even as Threads gains on phones [3][2][5].

Industry Impact

  • Creators and community managers: Threads’ feature push (communities, DMs, long-form, and other engagement tools) gives creators additional places to build audiences and diversify platform dependence [2].
  • Ad measurement and agencies: A widening split between mobile and web dominance can push brands to rethink channel mix and creative formats, since reported usage patterns differ sharply by device [3][5].
  • Platform safety tooling: The backlash described around Grok image generation and subsequent restrictions underscores growing pressure for stronger consent and age-safety guardrails across social apps using generative features [5].

Historical Background

X is the service formerly known as Twitter, rebranded after Elon Musk’s acquisition and subsequent product and policy changes [common]. Meta launched Threads as a text-based social network linked to Instagram, positioning it as an alternative place for public posts and creator-led discussion [common]. By late October to early November 2025, Similarweb’s tracking (as reported) suggests Threads crossed ahead of X on mobile daily usage, setting up the current early-2026 milestone [3].

Q&A

Q: What makes a platform “bigger” if mobile and web leaders differ?

A: It depends on the yardstick: some reports emphasize daily active users in mobile apps, while others include daily web visitors too, which can change who appears larger overall [3][2].

Q: Which product changes are most associated with Threads’ engagement gains?

A: Coverage points to Meta’s frequent feature additions—like interest-based communities, improved filters, DMs, long-form text, and disappearing posts—as part of its engagement strategy [2].

Q: How is Bluesky trending compared with Threads and X?

A: Similarweb data cited in reporting puts Bluesky at about 3.6 million daily mobile users and down 44.4% year-over-year [5].

Action Items

  • Audit your privacy and DM settings on Threads and X: Review account settings in each app—especially messaging and content visibility—since Threads has added DMs and other engagement features that can change how people can contact you [2].
  • Diversify where you publish text updates: If you post professionally, try cross-posting the same update to Threads and X for a few weeks and compare which device segment (mobile vs web) drives more replies and clicks for your audience [3][5].
