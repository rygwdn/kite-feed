Perplexity open-sources SPACE sandbox for AI agents

Category: AI

Perplexity has open-sourced SPACE, its in-house sandbox for containing AI agents, allowing outside developers to use the software. The company says partners CoreWeave, Akamai and Crusoe have agreed to offer SPACE to customers [7]. AI developers are weighing training models to behave safely against security controls that restrict what agents can access. Perplexity says none of the nine frontier models it tested against SPACE escaped in 118 attempts. The company also said the models broke out of other sandboxes that were not open source [7].

Primary Source: View original article

A lot of companies are thinking about how to build secure AI agents, and in many cases they hit a wall

— Zibi Braniecki, member of the technical staff at Perplexity (Semafor)

Key Points

  • Reported escapes: Reports of models breaking out of constraints and accessing the open web included incidents involving Hugging Face and the Australian government [7].
  • Other tools: Nvidia has also released OpenShell, an open-source security tool that runs agents in a sandbox and limits what they can access [7].
  • Compute use: Perplexity says SPACE can reduce wasted computing resources that may result from strict sandboxing [7].
