Anthropic adds Accenture evaluators ahead of IPO

Category: AI

Anthropic said Faculty, Accenture’s AI unit, will embed evaluators at the company to test frontier models, conduct red-team exercises, assess alignment and examine safeguards. The evaluators will have access comparable to that of Anthropic employees. Anthropic and Accenture each plan to invest at least $1 billion over five years in evaluation capabilities. Anthropic said the arrangement is nonexclusive and that it is discussing additional evaluators, including METR [17][16][20][21]. The announcement followed Chief Executive Dario Amodei’s September 12 call for the industry to slow improvements in advanced-model capabilities. Anthropic is also considering a new model to respond to OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra and preparing for a possible November IPO. Its annualized revenue exceeded $65 billion by the end of July, and some reports projected it could exceed $100 billion this year [32][38][18][47].

Did you know: Anthropic said its Claude models were not fully autonomous in any measured subset of its research-and-development work [34].

Key Points

  • Internal metrics: Anthropic said about 30,000 AI agents were conducting research and engineering work on its most-used internal platform at any given time [34][45].
  • Safety allocation: A July 13–20 compute snapshot found that roughly 6% of research-and-development compute, and 12% of compute for AI-driven R&D, went to safety work [34][45].
  • Competitive pressure: Ramp data put OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra at about 13% of tracked enterprise AI spending versus roughly 8% for Anthropic’s Claude Fable, while OpenRouter recorded OpenAI model spending above Anthropic model spending for the first time in more than two years [35][38].
  • Evaluator safeguards: An AI Evaluator Forum called for scientific independence, protection from retaliation, unfiltered communication with company boards and access to relevant systems, data, tools and physical spaces [46].

Perspectives

Anthropic: The company said embedded evaluators can observe operations, identify blind spots, report incidents and create more verifiable accountability, while stressing that Anthropic retains ultimate responsibility for model safety [20][41].

Technical Details

  • AI contribution: Anthropic said Claude now leads more than a quarter of its research-and-development work and works alongside human employees on more than 90% of tasks [49].
  • Compute spending: Anthropic’s computing-infrastructure spending rose from $3.4 billion in the first quarter to $5.6 billion in the second, while quarterly revenue increased from $4.7 billion to more than $11.5 billion [33].

Industry Impact

  • AI assurance: The agreement gives Accenture’s Faculty unit a role in model red-teaming, alignment assessments and safety monitoring. Anthropic said it is discussing arrangements with other independent evaluators [17][19].
  • Enterprise AI: Ramp data put OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra at about 13% of tracked enterprise AI spending, compared with roughly 8% for Anthropic’s Claude Fable [35].
  • AI applications: Anthropic has expanded into life sciences by establishing a physical biology lab, acquiring Coefficient Bio and launching Claude Science tools [33].

Scientific Significance

  • Measurement limits: Anthropic’s three metrics describe development activity, agent oversight and compute allocation; the company said they should complement, rather than replace, evaluations of what models can do [45].
  • Open methodology: Anthropic said no standards yet exist for evaluator access or communications and that its approach will evolve as more evaluators join [17].

Business Angle

  • Enterprise growth: The number of companies spending at least $100,000 on Anthropic software over a 12-month period rose from about 1,500 at the end of 2025 to about 6,000 by the end of the second quarter [33].
  • Customer concentration: More than 100 companies spent at least $10 million each over 12 months, while more than 1,000 spent at least $1 million [33].
  • Infrastructure commitments: Anthropic agreed to pay SpaceX about $1.25 billion per month for computing infrastructure over the coming years [33].
  • Cash use: Anthropic held about $120 billion to $130 billion in cash in early August but planned to accelerate consumption as infrastructure investment increased [33].
