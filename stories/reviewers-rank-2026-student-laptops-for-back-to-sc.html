Reviewers rank 2026 student laptops for back-to-school

Category: Technology

Two technology buying guides published July 25 ranked laptops for students preparing for the 2026 school year. Digitech Bytes compared 15 current models and named the Apple 2026 MacBook Air 13-inch its top overall pick, citing its M5 chip, 16GB of memory, fast SSD storage and all-day battery life. It chose the HP 14-inch Laptop PC (2026 Edition) for value and the HP 14-inch Chromebook for tight budgets [1]. Mashable said its reviewers tested laptops from multiple brands before selecting six models for college students [2][3].
