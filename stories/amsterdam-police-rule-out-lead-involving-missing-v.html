Amsterdam police rule out lead involving missing Vancouver man

Category: Canada

Amsterdam police said a man known as “Lucky,” who has been in long-term care after collapsing in Amsterdam in 2023, is not John Russell Kenny, a Vancouver man missing since 1993, after investigators checked the lead with Canadian police [3][4][5][6][2]. Police said the comparison gained attention after a public appeal about Lucky spread online [3][4]. After releasing a video of Lucky, police said they received more than 500 tips, including the Kenny lead. Detectives are focusing on at least nine other names suggested by tipsters [3][4][5].

This lead was investigated together with the Canadian police, and it has now been definitively established that Lucky and Mr. Kenny are not the same person
