Trump and Zelenskyy set March Geneva talks

Category: World

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said he spoke by phone with President Donald Trump as Ukraine prepared for a fresh diplomatic push to end Russia’s war. Ukrainian officials are set to meet U.S. envoys in Geneva as talks pick up again [1][3][4][5][6][7][8]. Zelenskyy said the next round of three-sided talks involving Ukraine, Russia, and the United States is expected in early March, with the aim of clearing the way for a leaders-level meeting [1][2][6][8].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Timing: The call came ahead of a Geneva meeting expected on Thursday between Ukrainian and U.S. envoys, according to multiple reports [7][8].
  • Who was on the call: SCMP said U.S. negotiator Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner also joined the Trump-Zelenskyy phone call [6].
  • Political context: RFE/RL said the diplomatic push comes as pressure grows in parts of the policy debate for a tougher line on Russia [3].

Perspectives

Volodymyr Zelenskyy: He said the negotiating teams are working intensively and publicly thanked them for their role in efforts to end the war.

Sources: The Hindu

White House: It confirmed the phone call with Zelenskyy as diplomacy picked up ahead of renewed negotiations in Geneva.

Sources: Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty

PBS NewsHour: It described a plan in which Ukrainian representatives meet Trump’s envoys first, ahead of another round of trilateral talks that would include Russia.
