Apple macOS leak appears to show camera-equipped AirPods demo

Category: Technology

A short demo video found in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate appears to show Apple’s unreleased camera-equipped AirPods using Visual Intelligence. MacRumors identified the clip, and Aaron Perris posted it on X on August 18 [8][2][1][6][7][9][12]. In the clip, a man wearing AirPods holds up a book so the earbuds can read its title, while a voiceover says: “With Visual Intelligence, your world becomes savable. See something you like? Just ask me to save it for later” [8][2][6][7][9][12]. The software references indicate the AirPods camera would send information to Visual Intelligence, allowing Siri to answer questions about the wearer’s surroundings and log information for later use [8][1][6][11][12][22]. The build also includes a setup reference and an alert telling users to keep AirPods uncovered for more accurate information if hair blocks the sensors [8][1][14][15][18][22]. The product carries the internal codename B790. MacRumors said the codename appears multiple times in macOS Tahoe 26.7, and Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman had previously reported it in connection with camera-equipped AirPods [8][9][14][16]. Gurman suggested the AirPods could launch as soon as September, possibly at Apple’s iPhone-focused event, where Apple is expected to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and foldable iPhone Ultra [8][1][22]. Apple has not officially announced the product, its final name, camera specifications or launch date [20][18].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Visible design: The earbuds in the demo look similar to current AirPods Pro models, though 9to5Mac and Free Press Journal noted a somewhat thicker stem and no visible camera module [9][12][13].
  • Image stream: 9to5Mac said macOS 26.7 RC includes B790 references and code for an “image stream,” while ITavisen reported a direct mention of image streaming from the left earbud [9][23].
  • Separate model: Several reports said B790 differs from a second camera-equipped AirPods project, B798, which Bloomberg has reportedly tied to a 2027 launch window [9][20][16].
  • Other references: The leaked macOS build also references other unreleased Apple hardware, including smart-home products, new HomePod mini identifiers and additional internal device codes [8][23][21].

Perspectives

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman: Gurman previously linked the B790 codename to camera-equipped AirPods and described them as a more affordable alternative to Vision Pro, with a possible launch as soon as September.

Sources: Yahoo

Gizmodo: Gizmodo said the video supports a reasonable conclusion that the AirPods include at least one camera or camera-like sensor, while stressing that little else can be stated with certainty.

  • September 2026: Gurman suggested B790 AirPods could launch as soon as September [8]
  • 2027: Bloomberg reportedly expects separate B798 camera AirPods in 2027 [9]

Technical Details

  • Image streaming: The B790 references reportedly include an “image stream” path, and ITavisen said the code mentions image streaming from the left earbud. The references suggest sensor output would feed into Apple’s visual-processing pipeline [9][23][19].
  • Sensor type: Cult of Mac described the sensors as built-in IR cameras, while other articles described outward-facing cameras or camera-like sensors without confirming placement or specifications [11][2][7][20].

Industry Impact

  • AI wearables: The rumored B790 AirPods would add a camera-based input layer to Apple’s wearable lineup, alongside reports of smart glasses and a neck-worn device in Apple’s wearable-AI strategy [16].
  • Smart home expansion: The same macOS build reportedly references a security camera and other home devices, in addition to the camera-equipped AirPods [21][23].

Historical Background

Apple introduced AirPods in 2016 as wireless earbuds tied closely to the iPhone ecosystem [common]. The camera-equipped model follows Apple’s recent effort to add Visual Intelligence features that use camera input to identify objects and text, while prior reports from Bloomberg linked Apple’s wearable-AI plans to camera AirPods, smart glasses and a neck-worn device [16].
