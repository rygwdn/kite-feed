B.C. questions OpenAI disclosures after Tumbler Ridge shooting

Category: Canada

British Columbia’s government says OpenAI had a pre-scheduled meeting with provincial officials the day after the mass shooting at Tumbler Ridge Secondary School, but did not mention any concerns about the accused shooter’s interactions with ChatGPT that OpenAI employees had flagged earlier, according to reporting cited by the province [1][2][5][9]. Premier David Eby said the allegations that relevant information may have existed before the attack are “disturbing,” and the province is focused on preserving potential digital evidence held by online platforms and AI services as investigations continue [1][7]. OpenAI has said the account was suspended after posts were flagged by an automated screening system, and that the reviewed material did not meet its threshold to alert police before the attack [2][3][10]. Multiple outlets also report OpenAI contacted the RCMP after the shooting about the account [4][10]. In the community, RCMP say they are investigating threats circulating online and locally in the shooting’s aftermath and have put a safety plan in place for those impacted, while provincial and federal officials say they are engaging on safety expectations for powerful online tools [14][13][11].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Accused shooter identified: The Toronto Star report identifies the accused as Jesse Van Rootselaar [9].
  • Timing detail: OpenAI says the ChatGPT account was banned in June—described as eight months before the February shooting [9][2].
  • Post-meeting follow-up: B.C. says that after the day-after meeting, OpenAI later requested contact information (as the province sought clarity about what was known and when) [5].
  • What prompted the political response: Eby’s comments follow reporting (including a Wall Street Journal account referenced in Canadian coverage) alleging OpenAI staff had flagged concerns before the shootings [7].
  • Community impact of threats: Police-linked reporting says a victim’s funeral was cancelled and the family moved to a safe location amid the post-shooting threat investigation [13].
