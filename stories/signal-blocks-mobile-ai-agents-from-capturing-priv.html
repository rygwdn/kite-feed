Signal blocks mobile AI agents from capturing private chats

Category: Technology

Signal has added protections to block operating-system AI assistants from reading chats on iOS 27 and Android, saying screen access could expose conversations outside its encrypted messaging service [1][4][12]. On iOS, the app blocks screenshots and recordings by default, hides chats from the App Switcher and prevents Siri AI from viewing its screen. On Android, it enables Screen Security when it detects an AI assistant trying to read the screen [2][6]. Signal says messages can be captured after the app decrypts them for display and then made available to an AI system’s local database or remote server, beyond Signal’s control [6][7]. Users can turn off the protection, but Signal warns that doing so could expose their own messages and those of their contacts. The app cannot offer the same protection on macOS; Signal advises Mac users to exclude it from Siri in system settings [2][1]. Signal is asking Apple, Google and Microsoft for clearer technical documentation and a way for developers to opt apps out of AI access [8][7].

Primary Source: View original article

We will now be taking similar measures to restrict agentic systems built into iOS 27 and on Android versions where we can detect an AI assistant attempting to read your Signal screen

— Meredith Whittaker, Signal president (BleepingComputer)

Key Points

  • Earlier protection: Signal introduced a safeguard in May 2025 to block Microsoft’s Recall screen-capture feature from capturing chats in its Windows 11 app [1][5][9].
  • Temporary override: On iOS, users can temporarily disable screenshot blocking; Signal restores it automatically after 10 minutes [2].
  • Other access routes: Signal says screenshot controls cannot prevent agents from reading app data through device files, while third-party agents such as Meta Muse may use Android Accessibility permissions [3][8].
  • Messages at risk: Signal’s concern extends to disappearing messages: a separate screen-capture system such as Microsoft Recall could use optical character recognition to read them and store searchable records [7][10].

Action Items

  • Check Signal’s screen-protection settings: Update Signal and check that “Block Siri AI” is enabled under Settings > App Security on iOS; Mac users can add Signal to the Siri Excluded Apps list in System Settings [1][2].
