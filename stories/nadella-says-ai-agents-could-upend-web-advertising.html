Nadella says AI agents could upend web advertising and retail

Category: AI

Microsoft Chief Executive Satya Nadella said at the New York Times DealBook Summit on Nov. 18 that AI agents could reorder the economics of the internet, changing how companies attract customers and earn money. Nadella has also warned that powerful models could reshape firms and concentrate influence across industries [3][5]. Nadella said services built around human visits and advertising may have to rely more on cost-per-click or transaction revenue as agents handle searches and purchases. The shift could benefit platforms that let agents use their sites: Amazon currently blocks agents from browsing its retail catalog and buying for users, while Nadella suggested Shopify could gain if it allows such access. He compared the potential change to the earlier shift from web portals such as Yahoo to search engines such as Google [3].

Q: How does Microsoft propose to prevent AI power from concentrating?

A: Microsoft is promoting tools that let businesses choose models for different tasks, run their own models and control their data [5].
