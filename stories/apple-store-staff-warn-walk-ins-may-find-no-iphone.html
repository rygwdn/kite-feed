Apple Store staff warn walk-ins may find no iPhone Duo stock

Category: Technology

Apple Store employees have reportedly been told to warn customers without reservations that they may not be able to buy the iPhone Duo, Apple’s first foldable smartphone, in stores on its Oct. 23 launch day [5][2]. A preorder backlog has pushed estimated delivery dates for new orders to late January in several markets; Apple has not disclosed how many orders it has received [18][2][15]. Bloomberg journalist Mark Gurman says Apple knew the foldable faced severe manufacturing constraints but opened preorders in more than 70 countries at once, rather than starting in the United States and China as reportedly planned [5][13]. He called the decision a mistake, arguing Apple prioritized getting the phone out early in its fiscal first quarter over meeting demand. Apple has described preorder demand as “nothing short of extraordinary” and said it is working to deliver the phone quickly [18]. The company is still accepting online orders. Apple has directed customers to check its website on Oct. 24 for possible store pickup availability, but stock is not guaranteed [9][14].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Production estimate: Analysts at market-research firms estimated Apple could produce 6 million Duo phones by year-end, but the company has not disclosed its actual production or preorder totals [15].
  • Reseller activity: Reports of automated ordering and resellers listing devices above retail price complicate efforts to gauge consumer demand from long delivery estimates alone [13].
  • Pro models: Gurman said some customers unable to get a Duo promptly could switch to the iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max; a separate report cited a reduction of at least 15% in October component orders for the Pro line [13].
  • Carrier delays: U.S. mobile carriers, like Apple’s online store, pushed back delivery estimates for new Duo orders [18].

Timeline

  • 16 October 2026: Apple opened Duo preorders in more than 70 markets [13][19]
  • 18 October 2026: MacRumors reported that Apple had told staff to discourage launch-day store visits by customers without reservations [5]
  • 23 October 2026: Apple scheduled first customer deliveries and retail sales [18][19]
  • 24 October 2026: Apple told customers to check for possible store pickup availability [9]

Historical Background

Reports before the launch said production had faced significant problems, and Apple had considered delaying the release until November before proceeding with the Oct. 23 date [16][1]. Nikkei Asia reported that output had slowed to a few hundred devices a day around August, mainly because of Apple's high quality-control standards [1].
