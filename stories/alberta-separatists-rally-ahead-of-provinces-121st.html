Alberta separatists rally ahead of province's 121st birthday

Category: Canada

Alberta separatists rallied outside the legislature in Edmonton on Saturday afternoon ahead of celebrations marking the province’s 121st birthday, carrying Alberta flags, praying, listening to sermons and saying the province would be better off outside Canada [4][2][3][1]. The Alberta Prosperity Project organized the rally. Its head, Mitch Sylvestre, urged supporters to stand up to Ottawa in remarks to the crowd [4][2]. The rally took place seven weeks before an Oct. 19 provincial referendum ordered by Premier Danielle Smith, after the Alberta Prosperity Project filed a petition earlier this year seeking a question on whether Alberta should leave Canada and become an independent state [6][4]. Smith’s ballot will instead ask whether Alberta should remain in Canada or hold a second, binding referendum on leaving; she has said she will vote for Alberta to stay in Canada and that petitioners on both sides deserve to be heard [6]. Alberta officially became a province on Sept. 1, 1905, with anniversary festivities planned for the weekend before the Tuesday anniversary [4][2].
