Alberta premier criticizes Edmonton police hate-reporting campaign

Category: Canada

Alberta Premier Danielle Smith criticized an Edmonton Police Service social media campaign urging residents to report hate after the force posted, “If you see hate, report hate,” and said, “Not all hate looks the same, but all hate causes harm” [3]. Smith said in a video posted online that “free speech is a foundational Alberta value” and that the province does not support policing “unpopular or religious-based statements even if they are controversial” [3]. She said criminal law already restricts statements that subject groups to hatred or violence, but called that “a very high bar” [3]. The Calgary Herald reported that the debate followed an incident at a Calgary café where staff painted over the words “Hang Danielle Smith” after finding them scrawled on a washroom wall the previous Saturday [5].
