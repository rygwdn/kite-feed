Reported tornadoes hit Ontario during Saturday severe storms

Category: Canada

Severe thunderstorms moved across southern Ontario on Saturday as residents reported at least two tornadoes, with the first reports shortly after 1 p.m. north of Lindsay, about 30 minutes west of Peterborough [1]. The Northern Tornadoes Project confirmed a touchdown near Lindsay after reviewing photos and videos and said minor damage had been reported [1]. A second tornado report came near Woodstock around 3:30 p.m.; damage reports and scattered power outages followed. Northern Tornadoes Project experts planned to survey the area to assess whether a tornado occurred [1]. Earlier forecasts warned that a cold front, muggy air and lake-breeze boundaries could fuel severe storms across southern Ontario and parts of Quebec, with risks including strong winds, heavy rain, hail and isolated rotation [1][3]. The storms followed other recent severe weather in Ontario: researchers confirmed an EF0 tornado near Kemptville from Aug. 2, while Southwold, south of London, declared a state of emergency Friday morning after heavy rain, becoming at least the third Ontario municipality to do so in the past week [4][6].
