Instagram brings Reels, longer videos and live shows to Apple TV

Category: Technology

Meta has released an Instagram app for Apple TV, bringing Reels, longer creator videos and live broadcasts to Apple’s streaming device for the first time. The app appeared in Apple’s App Store on Oct. 23 and requires tvOS 26 or later [5][2][6]. The release extends Instagram’s TV rollout beyond Amazon Fire TV, Google TV and Samsung smart TVs. Users can discover videos by creators and resume playback across devices. Meta describes the television experience as a way to watch together. Unlike earlier TV versions, which focused on interest-based Reels channels, the Apple TV app also supports longer videos and live broadcasts [5][2][9].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Device support: The app is free to download and works on existing Apple TV HD and Apple TV 4K models if they run tvOS 26 or later [7].
  • App Store purchases: The listing describes the app as a free download, with optional in-app purchases for Instagram Badges and Meta Verified [6].
  • Quiet release: Meta did not issue a launch announcement. The App Store listing added Apple TV compatibility; an archived version listed only iPhone and iPad support [1][5][9].

Timeline

  • September 2025: Instagram released a dedicated app for iPad [5]
  • December 2025: Instagram began testing its television app on Amazon Fire TV [5][2]
  • February 2026: Instagram expanded its TV app to Google TV [2][9]
  • June 2026: Instagram brought the TV app to Samsung smart TVs [5][2]
  • 23 October 2026: Instagram’s Apple TV app appeared in the App Store [5][1]

Action Items

  • After downloading Instagram from the Apple TV App Store, follow the on-screen prompts to sign in to your account [7].
