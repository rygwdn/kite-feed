U.S. proposes AI incident-alert mechanism with China ahead of Trump-Xi summit

Category: World

The United States proposed a notification mechanism for serious artificial-intelligence incidents during talks with China in New York on Sunday. Under the proposal, the countries would notify each other when an AI-related event rises to the level of a national-security concern. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the proposal was part of discussions about establishing a “US-China AI dialogue” and increasing transparency between the two leading AI powers [1][2][6][7]. Bessent and Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng also discussed trade and investment issues with U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer ahead of President Donald Trump’s scheduled September 24 White House meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping. The sides agreed to hold further talks on AI, while the Chinese state news agency Xinhua described the economic discussions as “candid, in-depth and constructive” without publicly detailing Beijing’s response to the notification proposal [3][4][26][46].
