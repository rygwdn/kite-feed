UAE says Flydubai co-pilot tried to seize Israel-bound jet

Category: World

The United Arab Emirates said its investigation found that a Flydubai co-pilot attacked the captain with a cockpit crash axe and tried to take control of an Israel-bound flight on Sept. 30. Passengers and crew intervened after the captain opened the cockpit door, and the Boeing 737 landed in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, with 182 people aboard [14][22][5]. The UAE attorney general called the assault an attempted “terrorist act” but said investigators were still establishing the motive, circumstances and possible connections [3][12]. The suspect, identified by media reports and people familiar with the case as Omani national Hamam al-Hammami, had reportedly been barred from flying for Oman Air over concerns about extremist views before joining Flydubai. Flydubai’s vetting remains under scrutiny. A diplomat cited by AP said standard checks appeared to focus on criminal convictions, while the investigation had not found a connection to a militant group [5][22]. Australian counterterrorism authorities are also investigating al-Hammami’s links to the country, where he reportedly studied from 2015 to 2017. The UAE says it is examining physical and digital evidence and will release final findings when the investigation is complete [10][14].
