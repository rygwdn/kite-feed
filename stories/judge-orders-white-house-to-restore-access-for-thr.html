Judge orders White House to restore access for three outlets

Category: World

U.S. District Judge Timothy Kelly on Thursday, Sept. 24, ordered the Trump administration to immediately restore White House access for journalists from CNN, MS NOW and Politico. His 14-day temporary restraining order bars officials from enforcing the ban while the outlets’ legal challenge proceeds [6][7][11]. Kelly said the outlets were likely to show that officials revoked their press credentials without constitutionally adequate due process, including prior notice and an opportunity to be heard. He rejected the administration’s national-security justification, finding that the court record did not support it; Trump announced the ban on Sept. 18, accusing the organizations of reporting “FICTION and LIES” [3][6][15].

Key Points

  • Vague letters: Kelly said letters explaining the ban arrived only after the lawsuit and were so vague that they did little to identify the alleged violations [9][7].
  • Stories cited: The judge noted that reporters who wrote the stories identified in the administration’s letters did not hold White House hard passes [32].
  • Press pool response: Other major television outlets halted rotating pool coverage of Trump’s events and trips in protest, and Fox News also objected to the exclusion [45].

Perspectives

Trump administration: The administration argued that White House access is a privilege rather than a right and defended the ban by citing national security and concerns about the outlets’ reporting [48].
