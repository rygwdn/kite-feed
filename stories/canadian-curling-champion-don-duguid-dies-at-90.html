Canadian curling champion Don Duguid dies at 90

Category: Canada

Two-time world curling champion and longtime CBC broadcaster Don Duguid has died at 90, his son Terry Duguid, the member of Parliament for Winnipeg South, announced on X [2][3]. Terry Duguid said his father died peacefully Wednesday night; he did not give a cause of death [2][3]. Duguid, a Winnipeg curler, won three Brier titles, including back-to-back championships in 1970 and 1971, and skipped Canada to consecutive world titles in those years [2][3]. After his playing career, he spent nearly 30 years as CBC’s curling analyst and later called curling for NBC at the 2002, 2006 and 2010 Olympic Winter Games [2][3].

Key Points

  • Hall of Fame recognition: Duguid was inducted into the Canadian Curling Hall of Fame, Canada’s Sports Hall of Fame and the World Curling Hall of Fame [3].
  • Survivors: Duguid is survived by his wife, Barb, as well as his children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren [3].
