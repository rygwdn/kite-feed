Trump names seven-member Gaza ‘Board of Peace’

Category: World

The White House has named members of a new Gaza “Board of Peace,” an executive body that President Trump will chair as part of his plan for a transitional period of governance and reconstruction in the Palestinian territory [1][2][3][10][11]. The announced members include US Secretary of State Marco Rubio, former UK prime minister Tony Blair, Trump envoy Steve Witkoff, Trump adviser and son-in-law Jared Kushner, World Bank Group President Ajay Banga, and Apollo Global Management CEO Marc Rowan [2][3][7][5][11]. The announcement comes as Gaza continues to experience deadly violence despite a ceasefire that took effect in October, with the administration framing the board as a vehicle to coordinate rebuilding and governance arrangements [1][2].

Greatest and Most Prestigious Board ever assembled at any time, any place.

— President Donald Trump (Deutsche Welle)

Did you know: Le Monde noted Tony Blair served for years as a representative of the Middle East Quartet (UN, EU, US, Russia) after leaving office in 2007 [8].

Key Points

  • Board remit: The White House said the board’s work will span governance capacity-building, regional relations, reconstruction, investment attraction, large-scale funding, and capital mobilization [5].
  • Palestinian committee: A separate Palestinian-led body, the National Committee for the Administration of Gaza, held its first meeting in Cairo, with its leader Ali Sha’ath saying he wants to move quickly on immediate needs such as shelter and estimating reconstruction and recovery could take about three years [9][5].
  • Further appointments: The White House said additional board members will be announced in the coming weeks, indicating the structure may expand beyond the initial seven [7][11].
  • Mixed reception: Al Jazeera reported expectations of widespread condemnation tied to Blair’s and Kushner’s past roles in Middle East policy debates, underscoring that the roster is likely to face strong scrutiny from parts of the region and beyond [6].

Perspectives

White House: The administration presents the “Board of Peace,” chaired by President Trump, as the executive mechanism to supervise transitional governance and drive reconstruction, investment, and funding coordination for Gaza.

Timeline

  • October: Fragile ceasefire took effect, though deadly violence continued [1][5].
  • Thursday: NCAG held its first meeting in Cairo, per RT [5].
  • Friday: White House released founding Board of Peace membership list [1][3][7].

Technical Details

  • Transitional governance: A temporary governing arrangement intended to administer Gaza during a defined interim period while longer-term political structures are negotiated or built [1][4].
  • Capital mobilization: A financing approach focused on attracting investment and assembling large pools of funding for reconstruction, which the White House listed as part of the board’s remit [5].

Industry Impact

  • Humanitarian logistics: Ali Sha’ath’s stated focus on immediate shelter needs highlights near-term demand for temporary housing, debris removal, and basic-services restoration as precursors to longer reconstruction programs [9].
  • Development finance: Including the World Bank Group president on the board may connect Gaza planning to established development-finance expertise, potentially shaping how projects are prioritized and monitored [7][5].

Historical Background

After leaving Downing Street in 2007, Tony Blair spent years working on the Israeli-Palestinian file as a representative of the Middle East Quartet (the UN, EU, US and Russia), giving him prior involvement in international efforts around the conflict [8]. Several outlets note Blair remains closely associated with the 2003 invasion of Iraq, a legacy that continues to shape how some audiences in the region view his appointment today [8][5].

Q&A

Q: What specific powers, if any, will the board have over day-to-day administration in Gaza?

A: The reporting describes the board as supervising transitional governance and coordinating reconstruction and funding, but it does not spell out detailed legal authorities or enforcement powers [1][2][4].

Q: How will the Board of Peace relate to the National Committee for the Administration of Gaza in practice?

A: Sources describe both bodies as part of the US plan’s governance architecture, with the NCAG meeting in Cairo and the board positioned as an overseeing executive body, but operational lines of responsibility are not fully detailed in the coverage [9][5].

Q: What additional members are expected to be added to the board?

A: The White House said more appointments would be announced in the coming weeks, but the articles do not identify who those additions might be [7][11].
