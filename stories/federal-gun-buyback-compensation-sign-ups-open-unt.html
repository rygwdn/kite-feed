Federal gun buyback compensation sign-ups open until March 31

Category: Canada

Canada’s federal compensation program for certain firearms opened for owners to declare their interest in either turning in the guns or permanently deactivating them, with a deadline of March 31 [1][2]. The program is framed by the federal government as a public-safety measure, while creating a clear process and payout pathway for affected owners [1][2]. Coverage also highlights practical concerns from parts of the firearms retail community about what the program does and does not include [3].
