Vancouver birders gather for first Canadian taiga flycatcher

Category: Canada

A taiga flycatcher—an Old World species birders say has never been officially recorded in Canada—was spotted over the holidays at Sunset Beach Park in Vancouver, drawing steady crowds of observers hoping for a brief look [1][2][3]. The sighting turned a local shoreline park into an impromptu outdoor classroom, with experienced birders and curious passersby sharing tips on how to find and identify the small songbird [2][1]. For many, it was a rare, upbeat moment of community science: people bundled up, traded field notes, and enjoyed a reminder that unexpected wildlife surprises can still happen in urban spaces [3].
