Apple plans spring 2027 launches for five iPhone, iPad and Mac products

Category: Technology

Apple is reportedly preparing five product updates for February or March 2027: the iPhone 18 and lower-cost iPhone 18e, an entry-level iPad, a new MacBook Neo and a MacBook Air with an M6 chip. A second group—the iPhone Air 2, OLED iPad Air and refreshed iPad Pro—is expected later in the first half of the year, possibly in late March or April. Apple has not announced the products, specifications or launch dates [2][8][15][27]. The first-wave iPhones are expected to use Apple’s A20 chip and 9GB of memory, while the entry-level iPad could gain Apple Intelligence support. Reports say the MacBook Neo may move from an A18 Pro chip and 8GB of memory to an A19 Pro and 12GB; the later iPad Air is expected to add an OLED screen, and the iPad Pro may use a vapor-chamber cooling system [2][5][14]. The staggered schedule follows Apple’s decision to release its premium iPhones in autumn while holding back the standard iPhone 18 for spring. The reported calendar places the first releases in the iOS 27.4 cycle, but the company has not confirmed the schedule [19][26][3].

Key Points

  • OLED supply: Samsung Display and LG Display are expected to begin mass-producing OLED panels in late December or early January for a reported March phone launch; Samsung is said to be the sole display supplier for the iPhone Air 2 [22].
  • Neo market: Apple is reportedly planning annual MacBook Neo updates aimed at Chromebook and lower-cost Windows PC buyers; Apple sold 170,000 units to the education sector in the Philippines in November 2026 [7][18].
  • Siri updates: A report says iOS 27.3, expected in December 2026, will add support for Siri’s multi-step actions in Apple apps, with full support for third-party apps deferred until 2027 [26][25].
  • Price pressure: The MacBook Neo’s U.S. starting price reportedly rose from $599 at launch to $699 in June 2026, while the next model’s price remains unknown [1].

Timeline

  • September 2026: Apple introduced the iPhone 18 Pro line and foldable iPhone Duo [19].
  • October 2026: Apple released HomeView, a new iPad mini and touchscreen MacBook Pro [19].
  • February 2027: Reports place the first wave of product launches in February or March [2][8].
  • March 2027: The iPhone Air 2 and later iPad launches may follow in late March or April [12][27].

Technical Details

  • Vapor-chamber cooling: The rumored iPad Pro cooling system would spread heat away from its processor to reduce performance throttling during sustained workloads such as gaming, video editing and AI tasks [14].
  • Stacked motherboard: A report says the standard iPhone 18 may use a high-density stacked motherboard to reduce the board’s size and free up internal space for the battery and heat-management components [26][19].

Industry Impact

  • Sales mix: Standard iPhone and Air models typically account for more than 30 percent of iPhone lineup sales, with the standard model outselling the Air [22].

Historical Background

From the iPhone 11 through the iPhone 17, Apple released standard iPhone models alongside the Pro models in autumn, according to reports on the company’s launch schedule [19][26].
