RCMP investigate suspected homicide involving two seniors

Category: Canada

Police in Saskatchewan are investigating after two seniors were found dead in the village of Love. RCMP say they believe one of the deaths was a homicide [1][2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Where it happened: Love is a Saskatchewan village northeast of Saskatoon, RCMP said [2][1].
  • What RCMP has (and hasn’t) shared: RCMP call it an active investigation and, in early reporting, had not released details such as the victims’ identities [1][2].
