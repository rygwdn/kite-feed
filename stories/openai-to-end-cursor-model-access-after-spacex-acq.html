OpenAI to end Cursor model access after SpaceX acquisition

Category: Technology

OpenAI said Friday it plans to stop supplying its AI models directly to Cursor, the AI coding tool now owned by Elon Musk’s SpaceX, and proposed Nov. 12, 2026, as the cutoff date for direct model access [2][3][6][7][9][30]. The company said SpaceX’s acquisition of Cursor parent Anysphere triggered a limited change-of-control cancellation window and that OpenAI gave the maximum notice allowed under the agreement [3][13][19][30]. SpaceX announced the $60 billion all-stock deal in June and completed the acquisition earlier in August; some sources identified Aug. 14 as the closing date [10][19][24][22]. OpenAI said it made the decision because it could not be confident SpaceX would use its technology within OpenAI’s terms of service, citing alleged contract breaches by X after Musk acquired Twitter and Musk’s admission under oath that xAI violated OpenAI’s terms [6][10][13][30][38]. Musk said on X that he “couldn’t care less” and accused Sam Altman and OpenAI President Greg Brockman of being untrustworthy, while Cursor co-founder Michael Truell said Cursor was speaking with OpenAI to resolve the issue [6][10][18][19].

Primary Source: View original article

Cursor was one of the very first users of OpenAI, we’ve worked closely with their team for years, and we’ve trusted their platform to be neutral infrastructure for our business

— Michael Truell (Tuoi Tre News)

Did you know: OpenAI previously paid about $2 million a year for full Twitter data access before Musk cut off that access after learning of the agreement in December 2022, Yahoo reported [31].

Key Points

  • Astra withheld: OpenAI said it will not provide future models, including its forthcoming Astra model, to Cursor after invoking the contract provision [3][13][30][1].
  • Limited traffic: Truell said OpenAI models account for about 5% of Cursor’s total model traffic [5][24][28][1][44].
  • Alternative models: Cursor supports models from Anthropic, Google and SpaceXAI, and Cursor and SpaceXAI shipped Grok 4.5 together in July for users on every plan [3][19][23].
  • User workarounds: Users can still access GPT models in Cursor through their own OpenAI API keys, and OpenAI said it would continue access through its IDE extension [31].
  • Anthropic move: Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown said after OpenAI’s announcement that Anthropic planned to increase compute resources supporting Claude models on Cursor [16][18][39].

Perspectives

OpenAI: OpenAI said the planned cutoff was based on trust and compliance concerns and said large integrations require custom contracts to enforce terms of service and safety at scale.

Elon Musk: Musk dismissed the cutoff on X and renewed his allegation that Altman and Brockman had taken control of an open-source nonprofit.

Anthropic: Anthropic said it would add Claude compute for Cursor after OpenAI’s announcement.

Timeline

  • 2015: OpenAI was founded as a nonprofit research lab [44].
  • 2018: Musk left after a rejected board-control proposal [7].
  • 2024: Musk sued over OpenAI’s proposed for-profit transition [7].
  • June 2026: SpaceX announced the $60 billion Anysphere acquisition [10][19].
  • 14 August 2026: Cursor announced the SpaceX acquisition had closed [24][22].
  • 28 August 2026: OpenAI notified SpaceX it would wind down Cursor access [2][30].
  • 12 November 2026: OpenAI proposed ending direct Cursor model access [3][9].

Technical Details

  • Model distillation: OpenAI’s terms bar customers from using model outputs to train competing systems, and Musk said it was “partly” true that xAI distilled OpenAI models for Grok, according to Business Insider’s account of earlier reporting [30][13][31].

Industry Impact

  • Platform access: Yahoo reported earlier precedents in which Anthropic blocked Windsurf from Claude access after reports of an OpenAI acquisition bid and later revoked OpenAI API access for internal benchmarking [31].

Historical Background

Earlier in 2026, a jury ruled against Musk, and Engadget reported that the judge dismissed claims of breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment [7][18].

Business Angle

  • OpenAI said it would give up distribution through Cursor to enforce restrictions on model outputs and partner compliance [30][common].
  • The acquisition put Cursor under a company with its own AI model business [common].

Action Items

  • Check Cursor model dependencies: If your projects depend on OpenAI inside Cursor, identify workflows that use direct OpenAI model selection before the proposed November 12 shutoff [3][1].
  • Prepare alternate access: Test supported Cursor alternatives such as Anthropic, Google or SpaceXAI models, or set up alternate access methods [19].
