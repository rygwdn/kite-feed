Toronto police charge man in arson at Liberal MP’s home

Category: Canada

Toronto police arrested 24-year-old Roshvin Crofton after someone set vehicles on fire outside Liberal MP Salma Zahid’s home in Scarborough, Ont., last week [1][3][4]. Crofton faces charges of arson causing damage to property, mischief over $5,000, conspiracy to commit an indictable offence, possession of incendiary material and failure to comply with a probation order [1][3][4]. Police said they were searching for a second suspect believed to be involved in the arson [1][3][4]. Zahid, who represents Scarborough Centre-Don Valley East, said the motive remained unknown while the investigation continued [3][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Early-morning fire: The arson took place shortly before 1:30 a.m. on Aug. 27 in the area of Bellamy Road North and Lawrence Avenue East [3][4].
  • Video evidence: Surveillance video released by police showed a person pouring fuel on five vehicles, setting two on fire and leaving a lit gas can in the driveway [3][4].
  • Additional damage: Police said the fire also damaged a third vehicle [3][4].
  • Court date: Investigators said Crofton is scheduled to appear in court next week [3][4].

Perspectives

Salma Zahid: Zahid welcomed the arrest on social media, thanked Toronto police and said she would wait for the facts before commenting further on motive.
