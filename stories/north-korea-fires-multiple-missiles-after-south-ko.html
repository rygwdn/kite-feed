North Korea fires multiple missiles after South Korea’s hypersonic weapon test

Category: World

North Korea fired multiple ballistic missiles from the Pyongyang area toward the East Sea on Monday at about 9:20 a.m. local time, South Korea’s military said. At least one flew more than 900 kilometers. Japan detected at least two missiles and said they landed outside its exclusive economic zone [31][8][1]. Seoul and Washington were analyzing the weapons’ specifications, and South Korea said it was watching for further launches [9][32]. The launches followed South Korea’s first test of a domestically developed hypersonic glide vehicle on Thursday, Oct. 8 [31][36]. South Korea’s Joint Chiefs of Staff initially assessed the launches as a protest against the test and a demonstration of North Korea’s missile capabilities, but officials had not identified the missile types [31][21]. Seoul convened an emergency security meeting and said the launches violated U.N. Security Council resolutions [1]. A Sept. 21 landmine blast injured three South Korean soldiers. A joint investigation by South Korea and the U.N. Command blamed mines planted by North Korean troops; Pyongyang denies responsibility [32][4]. Nuclear talks between North Korea and Washington have stalled since 2019, and Pyongyang has rejected offers to resume them [8].

Key Points

  • Flight paths: Japan’s deputy defense minister said one missile traveled about 220 kilometers and another more than 910 kilometers. The second followed an “irregular” trajectory. Japan reported no damage to aircraft or vessels [8][27].
  • Launch frequency: The launches marked North Korea’s 16th round of ballistic missile launches this year and its fourth in a month, according to South Korea’s Joint Chiefs of Staff [1][20].
  • Other weapons demonstrations: North Korea has also conducted live-fire drills and commissioned a new warship as it seeks to expand its nuclear-capable arsenal [8].
  • Possible motive: Defense analyst Song Seong-jong said the repeated tests could be intended to increase Pyongyang’s leverage ahead of a possible meeting between Kim Jong Un and President Trump [20].
  • Sanctions history: The United Nations has imposed sanctions on North Korea since 2006 to curb its nuclear and ballistic-missile programs [20].

Perspectives

Japan: Deputy Defense Minister Takashi Koyari said Tokyo lodged a protest through its embassy in Beijing after detecting the launches [27].

Historical Background

The Korean War ended in 1953 with an armistice, not a peace treaty, leaving the two Koreas technically at war [9]. Kim has halted diplomacy and cooperation with South Korea and described it as a permanent enemy [4].

Humanitarian Impact

The Sept. 21 landmine blast in the Demilitarized Zone injured three South Korean soldiers, two critically, according to South Korea’s military [32].

Q&A

Q: Why did South Korea test its hypersonic weapon?

A: South Korean officials said the system, once fully developed, would give the country a new deterrent and the ability to strike key targets by surprise on the Korean Peninsula [8][9].
