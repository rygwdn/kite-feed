Microsoft refreshes compact Surface PCs with Snapdragon X2 Plus

Category: Technology

Microsoft announced second-generation 12-inch Surface Pro and 13-inch Surface Laptop models at Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Summit on September 23. Both use Qualcomm’s six-core Snapdragon X2 Plus processor [24]. Microsoft says the chip delivers up to 17% faster Office performance, more than 60% faster graphics, up to 18% better battery efficiency and 95% faster on-device AI processing than the previous generation. The refreshed computers retain their existing designs and go on sale October 13 in select markets. The Surface Pro starts at $1,149.99 and the Surface Laptop at $1,199.99 [17][2][3]. Both devices have 500-nit touchscreens, 25% brighter than their predecessors [8][11]. Microsoft also announced a redesigned Surface Mouse with haptic feedback and a customizable action button, due next month [25].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Microsoft’s new Surface Mouse is the first update to the standalone Surface Mouse in 10 years, following the original model’s 2016 debut [27].

Key Points

  • Optional 5G: The 12-inch Surface Pro will have an optional 5G configuration, one of Microsoft’s most-requested additions [8].
  • Memory options: The new models start with 16GB of RAM and offer 24GB configurations, eliminating the 8GB option; storage is available in 256GB or 512GB configurations [5][8].
  • Pen features: Microsoft Ink Canvas is an AI-powered pen feature [2].

Perspectives

Microsoft: The company says Windows AI experiences can use the PC, cloud services or both to reduce latency and reliance on cloud calls while keeping data on-device for supported tasks [17].

Sources: Microsoft Windows Blogs

Digital Trends reviewer: During demonstrations across Affinity, DaVinci Resolve and PowerPoint, haptic feedback gave users tactile pulses when aligning objects, snapping items into place or scrubbing a timeline [4].

Sources: Digital Trends

Technical Details

  • Neural processing unit: The Snapdragon X2 Plus includes an 80-TOPS Hexagon NPU [13][2].

Historical Background

Microsoft introduced the first 12-inch Surface Pro and 13-inch Surface Laptop in May 2025 [24]. The larger Surface Pro and Surface Laptop models received Snapdragon X2 upgrades earlier in 2026, before the smaller versions were refreshed [11][8].

Business Angle

  • The Surface Pro’s new starting price is $350 above its predecessor’s original starting price, while the Laptop’s is $300 higher, increasing the value hurdle for buyers [10].

Q&A

Q: Does the Surface Pro include its keyboard in the listed starting price?

A: No. The Surface Pro keyboard is sold separately; Engadget listed it at $150 [9].

Q: How long does the 13-inch Surface Laptop claim to run on video playback?

A: Microsoft lists up to 22.5 hours of video playback for the Laptop [21].
