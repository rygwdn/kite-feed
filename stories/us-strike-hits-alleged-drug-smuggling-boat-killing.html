US strike hits alleged drug-smuggling boat, killing three

Category: World

The US military said it carried out an airstrike on an alleged drug-smuggling vessel in the eastern Pacific on Friday, killing three people, according to statements from US Southern Command and the Pentagon [1][2][3][4][6][7]. US Southern Command said intelligence indicated the boat was moving along “known narco-trafficking routes” and was involved in drug trafficking [1][4]. The strike is the second reported US attack on suspected smuggling boats this week, and the sixth known strike this year. That brings the reported death toll from these operations to at least 148 people [1][2][6][7].

  • This week’s toll: PBS reported that US boat strikes this week left 11 people dead across three boats suspected of carrying drugs in Latin American waters [5].

  • Video released: SOUTHCOM posted video it said shows a boat burning after the strike [6].
