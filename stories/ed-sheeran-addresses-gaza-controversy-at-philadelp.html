Ed Sheeran addresses Gaza controversy at Philadelphia concert

Category: World

British pop star Ed Sheeran addressed the controversy surrounding his US Loop Tour during a Sept. 19 concert at Philadelphia’s Lincoln Financial Field. He said the Oct. 7, 2023 attacks in Israel were horrific, but the situation in Gaza was “catastrophic,” “unjustifiable” and “disproportionate.” He said civilian deaths and what he called systemic injustice in the West Bank could not be ignored, and apologized for mistakes during the dispute [8][18][17]. The dispute began after rapper Macklemore, the tour’s original US opening act, shouted “Free Palestine” during a New Jersey performance earlier this month. Sheeran said the promoter, not he, decided to remove Macklemore after venue owners objected. Four other supporting acts, including Sheeran’s backing band, later withdrew. Sheeran began the Philadelphia show without opening acts or his usual support musicians, while pro-Palestinian demonstrators gathered outside the stadium [3][11][37][41].

  • Tour economics: Ticket prices fell and sales declined for remaining US dates after the dispute, according to the BBC [8].
  • Aid discussions: Sheeran said he was speaking with people across the political spectrum about providing aid to victims and acknowledged that he needed to listen and learn [23].

Perspectives

Ed Sheeran: Sheeran said concerts should be safe spaces focused on unity and humanity rather than politics, but said venues should not pre-approve performers’ content [9][55].
