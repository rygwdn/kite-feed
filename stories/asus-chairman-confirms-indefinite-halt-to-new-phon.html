Asus chairman confirms indefinite halt to new phones

Category: Technology

Asus chairman Jonney Shih said the Taiwanese company will stop adding new smartphone models going forward, meaning no new Zenfone or ROG Phone releases are expected in 2026 and beyond [6][3][2][8][1]. Shih framed the shift as a focus change toward AI-oriented device categories such as robotics and smart glasses, as Asus reallocates attention to areas it sees as more promising [6][3][1][8]. For existing customers, Asus said it will continue providing software updates and honor warranties, which should help current owners keep their phones running smoothly while the product line pauses [3][2][10].

Primary Source: View original article

Asus will no longer add new mobile phone models in the future.

— Jonney Shih (Ars Technica)

Did you know: Engadget noted Asus continued unveiling non-phone hardware at CES, including dual-screen laptops and gaming smartglasses, even as its smartphone plans cooled [3].

Key Points

  • What prompted this: Several outlets noted Asus phones were niche products and never big sellers compared with leading Android brands, providing context for why the company might step back [5][6][10].
  • How firm is it: Reports describe the plan as an “indefinite” hiatus and “wait-and-see,” with Shih not fully ruling out a possible future return to phones [6][2][3].
  • Android implications: Coverage emphasized that this likely means Asus leaving the Android smartphone market, at least for new releases, even if support continues for current devices [8][2][4].
  • Other Asus bets: Asus highlighted other hardware directions—such as smart glasses and robotics—while continuing to ship non-phone products like PCs and other CES-announced devices [3][5][6].
  • Gaming-phone fans: Android-focused coverage pointed out that the ROG Phone line built a following with gaming-centric features (like add-on controls and cooling accessories), making the pause especially notable for that community [10][6].

Timeline

  • Jan. 16: Jonney Shih says no new phone models ahead [2][10].
  • Earlier this month: Reports say Asus won’t introduce new 2026 smartphones [3][6].

Technical Details

  • Software updates and warranty support: Asus says it will keep supporting existing phone owners through software updates and warranty assistance even without new handset releases [3][2][10].
  • AI device categories: Shih and multiple reports link Asus’s pivot to AI-oriented devices, citing areas like robots, smart glasses, and potentially smartglasses as future priorities [6][3][1].
  • Gaming phone accessories: Coverage highlights ROG Phone’s gaming ecosystem—such as cooling fan accessories—as part of what distinguished Asus phones from mainstream competitors [6][10].

Industry Impact

  • Android competition: If Asus stops releasing Android phones, the already concentrated Android handset market loses a niche competitor known for gaming-focused devices, potentially pushing some enthusiasts toward other Android brands [8][10].
  • Device-category shift: Asus’s pivot underscores how consumer hardware companies are repositioning around AI-adjacent form factors (like smart glasses and robotics) rather than treating smartphones as the primary growth engine [3][6][1].
  • After-sales expectations: By explicitly committing to updates and warranty coverage despite pausing new models, Asus may raise the bar for how smaller phone makers manage end-of-life and transition periods [2][3].

Historical Background

Asus has made smartphones for more than a decade, including the Zenfone line and the gaming-focused ROG Phone series, but its handset business remained relatively small versus major phone brands [5][10]. In January 2026, earlier reporting suggested Asus would not introduce new phone models in 2026, and Shih’s later comments indicated the pause could extend beyond just one year [3][2][6].

Q&A

Q: Will Asus discontinue existing Zenfone and ROG Phone sales immediately, or just stop launching new models?

A: The reports focus on halting new models, while emphasizing continued support for existing users; they do not clearly say sales stop immediately [3][6][2].

Q: Which specific “AI devices” will Asus prioritize first—robots, smart glasses, or something else?

A: Sources cite robots and smart glasses (including smartglasses) as directions Shih discussed, but they do not list a detailed product roadmap [6][3][1].

Q: What happens to Asus’s Android skin and phone-specific software tools if no new phones ship?

A: The coverage mentions ongoing updates and warranty help, but it does not specify how long Asus will maintain phone-specific software features or apps [2][3].

Action Items

  • Check your Asus phone’s warranty status: Use your purchase receipt and Asus support channels to confirm remaining warranty coverage, since Asus says warranty assistance will continue during the transition [2][3].
  • Enable and verify automatic system updates: On Zenfone/ROG Phone, make sure system update checks are enabled so you receive the ongoing software updates Asus says it will provide [3][10].
  • Plan for accessory compatibility if you own a ROG Phone: If you rely on gaming add-ons (like cooling accessories), confirm compatibility with your current model and keep firmware/apps up to date while new devices are on hold [6].
