Harper says Canada had no choice but to end U.S. trade talks

Category: Canada

Former Prime Minister Stephen Harper said Prime Minister Mark Carney had no choice but to end trade negotiations with the United States, arguing that the current U.S. administration sees Canada’s economic integration with the country as incompatible with Canadian sovereignty. Harper made the remarks in closing comments at Canada’s first national investment summit in Toronto on Sept. 15, saying Canada should reduce its reliance on the United States [2][4][3]. Harper said reducing Canada’s dependence on the United States would carry significant costs but could create opportunities in energy [4][3].

It is clear that the current US administration views our level of economic integration as incompatible with our separate sovereignty. Thus, to maintain that sovereignty, we must pursue diminished reliance upon the United States,

— Stephen Harper (National Observer)
