Ontario Conservative MP Larry Brock resigns House seat

Category: Canada

Conservative MP Larry Brock formally resigned from the House of Commons on Sept. 18, ending his tenure as the representative for Brantford-Brant South-Six Nations in Ontario [1][4]. Brock announced plans to leave the previous month and said he would return to the Crown prosecutor’s office in Brantford [3][4]. His resignation creates another vacancy in Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre’s caucus and triggers a byelection in the riding [4].
