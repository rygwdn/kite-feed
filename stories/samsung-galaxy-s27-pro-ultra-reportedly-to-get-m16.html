Samsung Galaxy S27 Pro, Ultra reportedly to get M16 OLED panels

Category: Technology

Samsung is reportedly preparing M16 OLED panels for the Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra, marking the first new OLED material in its flagship phone lineup in three years. Samsung Display supplies the panels, which are associated with higher peak brightness, improved power efficiency, better color accuracy and longer display life. Samsung Display has separately demonstrated an M16 panel capable of reaching up to 10,000 nits [1][3][5]. The two premium models are also expected to pair the panels with Color Filter on Encapsulation and Privacy Display technology [1][5].

  • Pixel comparison: Android Authority reported that the Pixel 11 did not receive the M16 panel, giving Samsung’s upcoming premium models a display distinction within Google’s latest lineup [2].
