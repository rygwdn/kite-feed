Toronto-area highways and TTC Line 1 close weekend

Category: Canada

Several Toronto-area transportation corridors are closing over the Jan. 31 weekend for planned construction and roadwork, affecting both drivers and transit riders [1][2][3]. Sources say parts of the Don Valley Parkway (DVP) and Gardiner Expressway will be shut down through Monday morning, while Halton-area highway work will affect sections of Highway 403 and the QEW [1][2]. For Toronto residents, the closures are expected to reshape weekend travel into and through downtown, with CP24 also reporting a TTC Line 1 closure over the same period [3]. While disruptive, the work is described as planned—an effort aimed at maintaining and improving key infrastructure that keeps the region moving [1][2].
