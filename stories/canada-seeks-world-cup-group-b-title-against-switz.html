Canada seeks World Cup Group B title against Switzerland

Category: Canada

Canada plays Switzerland at BC Place in Vancouver on Wednesday, June 24, in its final Group B match at the 2026 FIFA World Cup, with both teams level on four points after two games [10][26][27]. Canada leads the group on goal difference after a 1-1 draw with Bosnia and Herzegovina and a 6-0 win over Qatar, while Switzerland drew 1-1 with Qatar and beat Bosnia 4-1 [26][21][33]. A win or draw would give Canada first place and its first trip beyond the World Cup group stage [17][24][15]. First place would keep Canada in Vancouver for a round-of-32 match at BC Place on July 2, while second place would send Canada to Los Angeles for a June 28 match if it advances [10][27]. Coach Jesse Marsch said Tuesday that Canada would not set up simply to protect a draw, and the team must make at least one lineup change because midfielder Ismaël Koné broke his leg against Qatar and is out for the tournament [14][16][39]. Alphonso Davies is expected to make his first 2026 World Cup appearance, The Athletic reported [30].
