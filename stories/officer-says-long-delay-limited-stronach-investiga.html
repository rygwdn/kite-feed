Officer says long delay limited Stronach investigation

Category: Canada

A Toronto court heard testimony from a police officer who worked on the investigation into sexual assault allegations against billionaire businessman Frank Stronach. The officer said investigators were “limited” in what they could do because so much time had passed since the alleged incidents [1][2][3]. Stronach has pleaded not guilty to the charges [2].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Charges count: The case includes 12 charges tied to allegations from seven complainants, and Stronach has pleaded not guilty [2].
  • Investigative reality: The officer described practical limits on what police could do, not a view on whether the allegations are true — something the court will weigh using the full trial record [1][2][3].
