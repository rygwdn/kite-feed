B.C. charter boat sinking leaves six presumed drowned

Category: Canada

Six people are presumed drowned after a fishing charter boat sank Sunday in the Strait of Georgia off Richmond, B.C., police said. The boat, with 10 people aboard, had left Richmond’s Steveston neighbourhood and began taking on water about 10 nautical miles southwest of Vancouver International Airport, CBC reported; The Canadian Press reported the site was about 18 kilometres southwest of the airport [3][5][2][4][1]. Four people were rescued after a sailboat spotted survivors and issued a mayday call. Four men and two women remained missing and were later presumed drowned by RCMP [3][4][1][2]. CBC reported that a 26-year-old man and a 33-year-old woman had been released from hospital, while a 33-year-old man and a 28-year-old woman remained in critical condition [3][4]. The search was suspended at 9:45 p.m. Sunday, and police shifted the operation to recovery. An RCMP underwater team was expected to use sonar to locate the vessel in very deep water [4][3]. Police said the cause of the sinking was unknown and under investigation [4].
