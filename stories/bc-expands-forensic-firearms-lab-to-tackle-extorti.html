B.C. expands forensic firearms lab to tackle extortion

Category: Canada

British Columbia is putting new resources into its response to a recent surge of extortion-related violence, announcing an expansion of the province’s forensic firearms laboratory as part of a broader crackdown [2][3]. The government says the lab expansion will help investigators more quickly trace and analyze firearms evidence linked to shootings and threats tied to extortion cases [2]. The move comes as community concern grows in Metro Vancouver, where some residents and local leaders say extortion-related incidents and shootings have shaken confidence and may be underreported [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Lab investment: The province committed $600,000 to expand the Provincial Forensic Firearms Lab, describing it as a direct response to extortion violence [3][2].
  • Investigation support: The provincial release links the added lab capacity to helping police connect firearms evidence across cases as extortion investigations continue [2].
  • Surrey resourcing: Surrey’s police chief has acknowledged a new surge in extortion files and asked for additional funding, including support for a helicopter [1].
  • Political pressure: Surrey’s mayor has urged Ottawa to co-ordinate a national approach to extortion, while the head of a B.C. task force apologized amid the latest incidents [1].
  • Public anxiety: A Surrey resident’s call for hundreds of gun licences to confront extortionists shows how strongly some locals feel about personal safety and deterrence [1].
