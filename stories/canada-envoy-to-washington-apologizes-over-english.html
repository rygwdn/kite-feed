Canada envoy to Washington apologizes over English-only invite

Category: Canada

Canada's ambassador to the United States, Mark Wiseman, apologized at a House of Commons committee meeting in Ottawa after his embassy sent MPs an invitation in English only for a May 12 reception in Washington [2][3][1]. Wiseman called the invitation an "unacceptable error" and said his office had reissued it in both official languages, after Prime Minister Mark Carney said publicly that the situation was "unacceptable" and that he was disappointed in Wiseman and his office [3]. The episode quickly turned into a broader political test of how Ottawa will represent Quebec and francophone interests in Canada-U.S. relations, especially as Wiseman faces scrutiny over past comments on supply management [3][4]. At the same committee session, MPs also pressed him on trade negotiations and his earlier criticism of supply management, which he said was not relevant to his current role as ambassador [2][4].
