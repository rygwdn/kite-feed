Apple’s OLED iPad mini may cost $799, up $200 from current model

Category: Technology

Apple is expected to unveil an OLED iPad mini later this month, with Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman estimating a starting price near $799, up from $599 for the current model [2][1]. That would put the small tablet above the 11-inch iPad Air, which starts at $749 [2][1]. Apple has not confirmed the price or launch details. The rumored upgrade includes an OLED screen, an A20 Pro chip, a relocated FaceTime camera and a redesigned speaker system [1][2]. The rumored price increase would follow a $100 rise for the existing iPad mini earlier this year, which reports attributed to higher memory and storage costs [1][10]. Reports put Apple’s next hardware presentation on or around Oct. 27, with new Macs also expected; the date remains unconfirmed [7][11].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Display uncertainty: Reports conflict over whether the OLED panel will support 120Hz ProMotion; some describe a 60Hz display, and Apple has not confirmed the specification [2][8][1]
  • MacBook redesign: The rumored 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pros may add touchscreens, OLED panels and Dynamic Island, with macOS adapted for touch input [7][15]
  • Other launches: Apple’s Oct. 13 “Welcome home” event is expected to focus on smart-home products, including a rumored display hub [2][8]
  • Base iPad delay: Bloomberg’s reported launch schedule puts a revised entry-level iPad in early 2027 rather than this year [4]

Timeline

  • October 2024: Apple launched the iPad mini 7 at $499 [10]
  • June 2026: Apple raised the current iPad mini’s starting price to $599 [1]
  • 13 October 2026: Apple scheduled a “Welcome home” event focused on smart-home products [2]
  • 27 October 2026: Apple may introduce new Macs and a new iPad mini around this date [7]

Q&A

Q: Will the iPad mini’s OLED screen match the iPad Pro display?

A: That remains unclear. Reports say the new mini’s panel may not match the iPad Pro’s tandem OLED display, and Apple has not confirmed its specifications [2][8].
