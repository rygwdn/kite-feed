Google gives Gemini control of Mac apps for paid US subscribers

Category: Technology

Google has begun adding “computer use” to its Gemini app for Mac, letting the AI carry out multi-step tasks across desktop apps, local files and websites. The feature can click, type, navigate menus and fill forms in a separate background window, with a live preview in the chat; users can keep using their main screen and cursor [21][3][5]. Google’s examples include extracting shipping details from PDFs and entering them into quote forms, assembling presentations from dashboard data, and preparing resale listings from clothing photos. The company says users choose which apps Gemini can access, can pause its work and must approve key actions [21][2]. The feature is rolling out in English in the United States to adults with personal Google accounts and Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscriptions. It requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later; Google has not announced when it will reach other markets [21][3]. Rival companies also offer Mac computer-use agents, while Apple’s Siri currently has more limited reach into third-party apps [20][19].

Key Points

  • Permission risks: Google warns that Gemini may interact with active sessions and visible multifactor-authentication codes. Malicious instructions embedded in one app could also redirect it to another app it can access [9].
  • Recovery window: If enabled, temporary backups of changed files are deleted after 24 hours or when a new task begins. Google warns that some apps do not support backups and some changes cannot be undone [17][9].
  • Competing agents: Meta’s Muse can use a cloud-based virtual machine to keep performing tasks even when the user’s Mac is offline [15].

Perspectives

Apple: Apple has warned that granting apps broad Full Disk Access can expose files, messages, email and browsing history [9].

Sources: AppleInsider

Apple software users: Apple has released frameworks intended to let developers add abilities to Siri through their apps, which could broaden the assistant’s reach beyond Apple’s own software [20].

Timeline

  • October 2025: Google released Gemini computer-use technology to developers [28][30]
  • October 2026: Apple warned broad Full Disk Access could expose personal files and messages [9]
  • 16 November 2026: Apple added granular app-control permissions in macOS 27.3 developer beta [9]
  • 19 November 2026: Google announced the consumer Mac rollout of Gemini computer use [21]

Technical Details

  • Graphical interface: Unlike integrations that call an app’s application programming interface (API), computer use acts through the graphical interface, allowing it to work in desktop programs the user authorizes [30].
  • File access: Gemini can access only files or directories users add to Connected folders; with permission, it can view and edit most folders [17].

Historical Background

Google’s Gemini 2.5 Computer Use model, released to developers in October 2025, was initially aimed at browser-oriented tasks; the consumer Mac feature brings computer use to local desktop apps as well [28].

Q&A

Q: Can Gemini complete logins or payments without stopping?

A: Google says the system pauses for logins and payments and requires confirmation for sensitive actions [2].

Action Items

  • Check setup permissions: Before Gemini can control Mac apps, users must enable macOS Accessibility and Screen & System Audio Recording permissions if they are not already enabled; Gemini also requests permission for individual apps [9].
  • Keep sensitive information out of agent workflows: Avoid tasks involving sensitive files or actions, and review recipients and file contents before approving sharing; Google warns that connected data may be exposed or changed [17].
