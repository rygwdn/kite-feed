Alberta delays school flag limits and anthem mandate

Category: Canada

Alberta’s government has postponed rules that would limit which flags schools can fly or display, require schools to play the Canadian national anthem weekly and give Education and Childcare Minister Demetrios Nicolaides authority to name or rename public schools [1][2][3][4]. CBC reported that school trustees now hold that authority [2]. The Education Ministry said the government “remains committed” to changes under An Act to Remove Politics and Ideology from Classrooms and Amend the Education Act, 2026, but will take “a thoughtful and measured approach” so education partners have support during the transition [2]. The Globe and Mail reported that other provisions will still take effect, including a ban on school boards issuing statements or taking positions on political or social issues [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Draft rules: Draft regulations listed which flags would be permitted on school property and how long some flags could be displayed, while not mentioning Pride flags, the Edmonton Journal reported [4].
  • Permitted flags: The draft regulation permits Canadian, Alberta, municipal, school board, school, and First Nation or Métis settlement flags, CBC News reported [2].
  • Effective date: The flag and anthem rules were originally set to take effect on Sept. 1, 2026, but are now delayed, according to CBC News [2].
