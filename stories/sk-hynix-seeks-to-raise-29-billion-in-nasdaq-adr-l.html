SK Hynix seeks to raise $29 billion in Nasdaq ADR listing

Category: Business

SK Hynix said it plans to raise up to 45.45 trillion won ($29.4 billion) by issuing 17.79 million new shares as American depositary receipts for a Nasdaq listing expected to start trading on July 10 [1][6][10][17][23][18]. The South Korean memory-chip maker filed with South Korean regulators and the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission as it seeks to expand its U.S. investor base during strong demand for AI-related memory chips [12][17][23]. The company said proceeds will fund semiconductor facilities and equipment, including its first fab at the Yongin semiconductor cluster, an advanced packaging facility in Cheongju and purchases such as extreme ultraviolet scanners [10][12][23][8]. SK Hynix is a major supplier of high-bandwidth memory used in AI data centers and is part of Nvidia's supply chain, while Counterpoint Research data cited in the articles put its global HBM revenue share at about 57% in the fourth quarter of last year [10][13][24]. SK Hynix shares rose after the filing and after Micron Technology reported results and guidance that lifted memory-chip stocks, with reports showing gains of about 10% to 13% in Seoul trading on Thursday [3][5][2][29]. South Korea's Kospi fell nearly 10% on Tuesday, rebounded 3.3% on Wednesday and gained more than 5% early Thursday [3].

Did you know: TSMC's ADR fell 38% by the end of 1997 after the Asian financial crisis, before its 1999 ADR price rose 285% [19].

Key Points

  • Offering scale: If completed near the indicated size, the ADR sale would rank among the largest ever and could exceed Alibaba's $25 billion 2014 U.S. listing [25][19][24].
  • Valuation gap: Dow Jones Market Data cited SK Hynix at 6.97 times forward earnings, below Micron at 9.46 times and SanDisk at 10.47 times [19][26].
  • Listing mechanics: Yonhap reported new common shares would be sold at 2.55 million won, with ADRs priced at 255,500 won apiece, before final size and pricing are set through bookbuilding [22][23].
  • Market spillover: Samsung Electronics rose more than 5%, and SK Hynix closed up 13.06% at 2,917,000 won on Thursday after Micron's earnings and the ADR plan [29].

Perspectives

SK Hynix: The company said the ADR listing should broaden its investor base, support what it considers a more appropriate market valuation and fund chip facilities and equipment.

Sources: Malay Mail

D.A. Davidson analyst Gil Luria: Luria said a valuation gap exists between U.S.-traded stocks and shares listed only overseas, and expected a Nasdaq listing to help narrow SK Hynix's gap with Micron and SanDisk.

Sources: Yonhap News Agency

Fibonacci Asset Management Global CEO Jung In Yun: Jung said investor attention may shift from demand to execution, especially whether SK Hynix can expand HBM capacity and meet aggressive U.S. production targets.

Sources: Sing Tao Headline

FTSE Russell research head Indrani De: De attributed SK Hynix's lead over rivals to early positioning in HBM and strong AI demand, and said Korean companies control about 80% of the HBM market.

Timeline

  • December 2025: Regulators queried market rumors about a U.S. listing [17]
  • March 2026: SK Hynix submitted a confidential F-1 draft [21][31]
  • 24 June 2026: The board approved an ADR filing targeting 45.45 trillion won [14][23]
  • 10 July 2026: Nasdaq trading is expected under ticker SKHY [21][31]

Industry Impact

  • AI memory supply: SK Hynix's funded projects are not expected to produce memory in time to ease the current shortage; new capacity is not expected online until 2027 at the earliest [12].
  • Semiconductor benchmarks: A U.S. listing could make SK Hynix a more direct benchmark for Micron and SanDisk, with analysts citing potential effects on relative valuation and investor allocation [4][19][26].
  • ETF flows: Money Today cited Barron's as saying a U.S. listing could raise the chance of inclusion in semiconductor ETFs such as VanEck Semiconductor ETF and iShares Semiconductor ETF, potentially adding passive-fund demand [26].
  • Korean supply base: SK Hynix said proceeds would go to domestic semiconductor projects including Yongin and Cheongju, keeping much of the planned production investment in South Korea [10][8].

Historical Background

Analysts cited Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.'s Oct. 8, 1997, New York ADR listing as a precedent in discussing whether SK Hynix can narrow its overseas valuation discount [19].

Business Angle

  • SK Hynix said the listing proceeds will fund capacity at Yongin and Cheongju and purchases of EUV equipment [10][12][23].
  • Dow Jones Market Data cited SK Hynix at a lower forward earnings multiple than Micron; multiple expansion alone does not increase free cash flow [19][common].
  • Memory remains cyclical, and SK Hynix's planned capacity additions could face oversupply risk if demand weakens [common][28].

Action Items

  • Check ADR timing before trading: Confirm final pricing after bookbuilding and verify the July 10 Nasdaq start date, since SK Hynix said the offering size and schedule can change with market conditions [23][18].
  • Review semiconductor exposure: Compare direct holdings in SK Hynix, Micron and semiconductor ETFs because analysts cited possible valuation and allocation shifts once SK Hynix trades in the U.S. [4][26].
