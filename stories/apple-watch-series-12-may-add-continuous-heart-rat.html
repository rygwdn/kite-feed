Apple Watch Series 12 may add continuous heart-rate tracking

Category: Technology

Apple is testing continuous heart-rate collection for future Apple Watch models, including the expected Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, according to reports citing Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman [2][3][4][6][7][10][11][12]. The change would move heart-rate sensing closer to all-day monitoring, instead of relying on continuous readings during workouts and less frequent readings outside exercise [4][6][10][11]. Apple is also said to be reworking its Health and Fitness apps to surface more wellness-related metrics, with an approach compared with Whoop and Oura, while this year’s watches are not expected to bring major exterior design changes [2][3][7][11][12]. The Series 12 may bring back a ceramic case option, with white and dark gray versions reportedly tested, and Apple is expected to unveil the new watches at its September 9 event [3][7][10][11][12][13].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Apple internally refers to the reported ceramic case effort as “iceman,” according to Stadt Bremerhaven and Digital Trends [10][7].

Key Points

  • Chip upgrade: The Series 11 and Ultra 3 use the S10 chip also found in the Series 10 and Ultra 2, so an S11 or newer chip would be the first Apple Watch performance upgrade in several years [3][4][11].
  • Battery tradeoff: Cult of Mac noted that continuous heart-rate monitoring could reduce battery life, while a newer S-series chip may help offset added power use through efficiency gains [4].
  • SE timing: Gurman does not expect an Apple Watch SE 4 this year, leaving the reported changes focused on the flagship Series and Ultra models [3][11][12].
  • Redesign window: A larger Apple Watch redesign remains in development but is reportedly more likely for 2027 or 2028 than for the 2026 lineup [7][10][12].

Perspectives

Mark Gurman: Gurman reiterated that he does not expect Touch ID on the Series 12 or Ultra 4, despite earlier rumors about fingerprint authentication.

Sources: MacRumors

Digital Trends: Digital Trends said denser heart-rate data could help Apple account for activity that users do not log as workouts, such as long walks, physically demanding workdays or short bursts of exertion.

Sources: Digital Trends

PCMag: PCMag’s Andrew Gebhart argued that holding the flagship Series watch at its long-running $399 starting price would be one of the upgrades needed to make the next model more compelling.

Sources: PCMag

Timeline

  • 2017: Series 3 offered white and dark gray ceramic cases [3].
  • 2018: Flagship Series watch pricing settled at $399 [1].
  • 2024: S10 chip debuted in Apple Watch Series 10 [4].
  • 9 September 2026: Apple event expected to include new Watch models [3][12].

Industry Impact

  • Wearable competition: The reported software changes would push Apple closer to Whoop and Oura, whose apps emphasize wellness metrics derived from large volumes of biometric data [2][7][3].
  • Smartwatch benchmarks: Digital Trends said Google’s Pixel Watch 3 measures heart rate once every second throughout the day, while Apple Watch checks less frequently outside workouts [6].

Historical Background

The Apple Watch Series 10 introduced a thinner case with a larger display, while the Series 11 brought the first significant battery-life increase since the original model, according to PCMag [1].
