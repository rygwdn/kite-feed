Suspected Ukrainian drones sink Russian corvette, hit second ship off Libya

Category: World

Libya’s eastern administration said a suspected drone attack caused an explosion aboard a Russian ship docked at Benghazi on Sunday. Satellite imagery identified the vessel as the Russian navy corvette Merkury and showed it capsized, with only its bow above water [12][15][3]. Two Libyan military officials told the Associated Press that Ukrainian sea drones launched from a base run by Ukrainian forces in western Libya struck the ship. They said four drones were intercepted and two hit. Ukraine has not claimed responsibility, and Russia has not commented [10][8]. Medical sources said 10 people, including Russians and Libyans, suffered minor injuries. Later Sunday, a drone boat hit a second vessel off Benghazi, reportedly a Malta-flagged commercial ship. Libyan naval forces rescued its crew. Military sources said Libyan forces intercepted more than 20 drone boats off the coast [12][6]. The eastern administration said it would investigate jointly with Russia. Its prime minister, Osama Hamad, said the government had formally asked the U.N. General Assembly and Security Council for an international inquiry, including into suspicions that the drones came from western Libya. The U.N. mission in Libya called for an immediate, independent and transparent investigation [12][11].
