UK inflation forecast to hit 2.9% in July

Category: Business

UK inflation is expected to rise when the Office for National Statistics publishes July consumer-price data on Wednesday, Aug. 19, with economists forecasting the headline rate will increase to 2.9% [3][4]. The forecast reflects higher household gas and electricity bills after the energy regulator lifted its cap in July, while Bloomberg also identified an AI-driven memory-chip shortage as another source of price pressure [1][3][4]. The figures will arrive as the Bank of England monitors the risk that inflation stays above target, according to Bloomberg [1]. The data will come before the new PM's government prepares an autumn budget focused on easing pressure on households and businesses [3].
