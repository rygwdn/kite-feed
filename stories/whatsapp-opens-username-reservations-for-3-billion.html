WhatsApp opens username reservations for 3 billion users

Category: Technology

WhatsApp began letting users reserve unique usernames on June 29 ahead of a full launch later in 2026, giving people an optional way to connect without sharing phone numbers when starting a chat with a new person, business or group contact [3][10][5][22][13]. The Meta-owned messaging service said it opened the reservation period early because more than 3 billion people use WhatsApp and many names are likely to overlap [2][5][22][21]. The feature does not remove phone numbers from account creation: users will still need a phone number to create a WhatsApp account, but once usernames are active, people who message a user for the first time will see the username instead of the phone number if the user has enabled it [3][18][54]. WhatsApp said reservations will roll out gradually by country over the coming months, with in-app notifications when access becomes available [9][22][25]. WhatsApp is presenting the system as a privacy feature rather than a social-media handle system: there will be no public directory, no username suggestions and first-time contacts will need to know the exact username [10][5][13][20]. Users can also set an optional username key for additional control over who can start a first conversation [3][9][21].

Key Points

  • Handle rules: Usernames can be 3 to 35 characters long, and WhatsApp can suggest available alternatives through a built-in generator [3][5][6][22].
  • Name protections: WhatsApp has reserved some names for high-profile people, businesses, governments and organizations to reduce impersonation risks before the feature becomes active [3][13][63].
  • Reservation path: Users who receive access can reserve a handle from Settings > Account > Username after updating to the latest version of the app [2][3][14][21].
  • Optional use: The feature remains optional, and current contacts that already have a user’s number saved are not expected to lose the contact details they already see [5][34][64].

Perspectives

WhatsApp global leadership: Kunal Shah, global head of WhatsApp, posted on X that he had claimed his preferred username before the public launch and urged users to reserve theirs.

Privacy experts: Experts welcomed the username option but said Meta still collects user metadata even though WhatsApp’s end-to-end encryption protects private-message content.

Industry Impact

  • Creators and small businesses: Creators, small businesses and organizations can claim existing Instagram or Facebook usernames on WhatsApp, helping them maintain consistent identities across Meta services [2][3][20].
  • Messaging competition: Several technology publications compared the move with rival messaging apps that already support usernames, including Signal and Telegram, while noting WhatsApp’s version avoids a public username directory [6][12][63].
  • Fraud prevention: OpIndia reported concerns that username-style contact could create opportunities for impersonation and investment scams, especially because WhatsApp functions as a contact channel for many businesses including banks [42].

Historical Background

WhatsApp launched in 2009 and built its identity system around phone numbers rather than public handles [common]. The username feature follows years of testing and beta sightings, with several reports saying WhatsApp had worked on usernames for years before detailing the rollout [3][19][12].

Business Angle

  • Meta is letting eligible accounts carry Facebook or Instagram handles into WhatsApp, keeping usernames consistent across Meta services without changing WhatsApp’s phone-number sign-up model [3][20][16].
  • Sources noted concerns that lookalike handles and copied profiles could help scammers pose as businesses or trusted contacts, making impersonation controls important for user retention [42].
  • The reservation window addresses name scarcity across a service with billions of users, while popular handles can be claimed before the full rollout [5][22][13].

Action Items

  • Check username availability: Update WhatsApp, then check Settings > Account > Username; WhatsApp says it will notify users when reservations become available in their country [3][22][21].
  • Reserve a brand handle: If eligible, use the in-app option to claim an existing Instagram or Facebook username so the same handle appears across Meta services [2][3][20].
  • Add contact protection: Enable the optional username key when available so new contacts need both the exact username and the key before messaging [9][13][21].
