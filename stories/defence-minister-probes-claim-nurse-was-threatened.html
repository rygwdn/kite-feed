Defence minister probes claim nurse was threatened over Poilievre photo

Category: Canada

Canadian Defence Minister David McGuinty says he is trying to resolve a dispute involving government contractor Calian Health after Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre alleged a nurse’s contract was at risk because she spoke with him at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright. Maureen Carpenter, who works at the Wainwright hospital under contract with Calian, met Poilievre after an Oct. 17 fire at the Alberta base that killed five soldiers and injured 39 people, 19 critically [1][2][5]. Poilievre raised a point of privilege in the House of Commons on Wednesday, saying the alleged employment consequences interfered with his work as Carpenter’s MP. McGuinty said he asked his deputy minister to raise the matter with Calian but cannot intervene directly in a private contract. He has spoken with Carpenter’s brother, but not with Carpenter. Calian had not responded to media requests for comment. The House Speaker is expected to rule on Poilievre’s privilege claim [6][7][1].

Primary Source: View original article

It’s not right that a member of Parliament should not be able to speak to a constituent.

— David McGuinty, Canada’s defence minister (Winnipeg Free Press)

Key Points

  • Contract concerns: Poilievre said Carpenter was told she could face scrutiny from Calian’s legal department and that other expected work opportunities had disappeared; he said she appealed reprimands and sought an apology [7][9].
  • Meeting permission: Poilievre said Carpenter spoke with him on base with permission from the unit commander, and that he had permission from the base commander to visit [6][9].
  • Cross-party response: Liberal MP Kevin Lamoureux said he found the claims upsetting and recognized health workers’ service, while Bloc Québécois MP Gabriel Ste-Marie said his party would examine the matter [2][8].

Perspectives

Pierre Poilievre, Conservative Party leader: He said Carpenter was told she violated Calian Health policy by meeting with her MP, but argued the company has no such rule and that meeting an MP is a basic Charter right [9].
