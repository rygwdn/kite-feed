Alberta separatists launch campaign ahead of fall referendum

Category: Canada

Alberta separatist advocates launched a campaign in Calgary on June 19 to promote making the province an independent country ahead of a fall referendum on whether to proceed toward an independence vote [4][3]. The campaign, called Let Alberta Decide and using the slogan Alberta’s Done Waiting, is led by St. Albert constitutional lawyer Keith Wilson and co-chair Tanya Clemens, a southern Alberta farmer and former math and science teacher [4][3]. Wilson told the Calgary event that Alberta lacks influence in Canada’s electoral and constitutional structures because decisions are made in Ottawa and shaped by voters in Quebec and Ontario [4].

Primary Source: View original article

The way the electoral structure is, and the constitutional structure of Canada, Alberta never gets a voice. We can’t have a voice, because decisions are always made in Ottawa, as determined by the wishes of the voters in Quebec and Ontario.

— Keith Wilson (Calgary Herald)

Key Points

  • Campaign structure: Wilson and Clemens registered as third-party advertisers, saying separate organizations allow them more room to spend than operating under one banner [4].
  • Spending limit: Clemens said each third-party organization has a $607,000 spending limit and denied that her campaign was colluding with other separatist groups [4].
  • Launch setting: Wilson made the pitch to 23 people at Hotel Arts in Calgary, according to the Calgary Herald [4].

Perspectives

Let Alberta Decide: Wilson said the independence movement became serious with the campaign launch, responding to arguments that the remain-in-Canada side would win and that polls showed independence support was not growing.
