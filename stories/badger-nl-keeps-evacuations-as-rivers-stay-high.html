Badger, N.L., keeps evacuations as rivers stay high

Category: Canada

Parts of Badger, Newfoundland and Labrador, remained under evacuation orders as river flooding persisted, driven by ice jams that backed water up into the community [1][3]. Town officials said water levels had dipped somewhat, but remained high enough that residents still could not safely return everywhere [1]. In response to flood-related risks to drinking water, a precautionary boil water advisory was issued for the town while crews monitor conditions and residents manage immediate needs [2].
