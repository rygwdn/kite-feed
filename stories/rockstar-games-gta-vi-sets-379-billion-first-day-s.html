Rockstar Games’ GTA VI sets $3.79 billion first-day sales record

Category: Technology

Grand Theft Auto VI generated an estimated $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day, Nov. 19, while Rockstar Games said 32.5 million copies had sold before launch. The company called it the biggest entertainment launch in history; the previous entry in the series, Grand Theft Auto V, took three days to reach $1 billion in sales after its 2013 release [10][27]. The new game puts players in the roles of Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a criminal couple in the fictional, Florida-inspired state of Leonida. Lucia is the franchise’s first non-optional female lead, and their relationship is central to the story alongside the series’ open-world crime and satire [17][18]. The game launched for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series consoles; Rockstar has not announced a date for its online mode [31][26]. GTA VI received eight nominations for The Game Awards, including game of the year. The ceremony is scheduled for Dec. 10 [18].

We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you.

Key Points

  • Console performance: GTA VI is capped at 30 frames per second on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PlayStation 5 Pro, with no 60-fps performance mode, according to the report [32].
  • Score split: Metacritic showed a critic score of 99 based on more than 150 reviews, while its player score fell to 5.6 by Nov. 22 as GTA and Zelda fans targeted each other’s games with negative reviews [6].
  • Digital copies: The reported retail editions contain activation codes rather than game discs, leaving buyers without a disc to lend or resell [11].
  • Easter eggs: Players’ early discoveries in Leonida included a recreation of Dexter Morgan’s Florida apartment and kill-room setup [1].
  • Livestream audience: Viewers watched more than 132 million hours of GTA VI streams on Twitch, YouTube and Kick on launch day, with concurrent audiences nearing 8 million [26].

Perspectives

Rockstar Games design team: Vice president of design Stephen Donkin said the team built missions to blur the boundary between on-mission and off-mission play, letting dialogue connect open-world activity with the protagonists’ story [8].

Industry Impact

  • Telecommunications: Chorus New Zealand forecast about 35 petabytes of extra network traffic around the game’s release as customers downloaded its 212-gigabyte file [18].
  • Food delivery: DoorDash recorded a surge in late-night energy-drink orders around launch, while Uber Eats offered food promotions designed for players [26].
  • Local promotion: Miami-Dade County agreed to a $3.9 million promotional deal with Rockstar, including a “Welcome to Vice City” display at the Miami Heat arena [18].

Historical Background

Grand Theft Auto began in 1997, and the series’ third installment moved it into a three-dimensional open world in 2001 [11][15]. Grand Theft Auto V, released in 2013, has sold more than 230 million copies [10][18].

Q&A

Q: Why do the reported first-day sales estimates differ?

A: Sensor Tower initially estimated $2.36 billion from about 24.6 million copies; Rockstar later reported $3.79 billion using its own worldwide retail-sales estimate through launch day [10].
