Update: US and Israel expand strikes as Iran retaliates

Category: World

In the latest development in the US-Israel war with Iran, Israel said it launched a new, wide wave of strikes on Tehran, while also hitting targets in Lebanon, including Beirut. The fighting entered a seventh day as fears grew that the conflict could widen further across the region [5][14][2][10]. US officials also signaled that American operations are intensifying: the Pentagon warned the bombardment is set to surge dramatically, even as some officials said Iran’s retaliatory missile activity appears to be easing [2][4][14]. Several outlets described a broader target set than in last summer’s 12-day war. Analysts and officials said the campaign now reaches beyond nuclear enrichment sites to Iranian military assets and what Israel called “regime infrastructure.” Iran, meanwhile, kept launching missiles and drones toward Israel and at or near US bases and other sites across the Gulf region [1][10][8][13]. The spillover has touched numerous countries, including Gulf states that host US forces. Qatar said it intercepted a drone targeting a US base, and reporting said strikes and counterstrikes have rippled across a wide swath of the Middle East [10][8][6]. Diplomatic and political stakes are rising alongside the military escalation. President Trump was reported as saying Iran is looking for a deal to end the war, while the US Senate voted down a resolution that would have restricted the President’s authority to order further military action in Iran [17][9].

Did you know: PBS reported that the escalation had affected an additional 14 countries across the Middle East and beyond [8].

Key Points

  • Targeting scope: US Central Command said it hit more than 2,000 targets in under a week — a pace described as heavier than any American bombing campaign in the Middle East in more than a decade [1].
  • Gulf spillover: Qatar said it intercepted a drone targeting a US base, a reminder of the risks for countries hosting US forces as the conflict reverberates across the region [10].
  • Lebanon toll: Lebanon said the death toll from Israeli strikes has risen to 123 as fighting expands beyond Iran and Israel [10].
  • Interceptor strain: CBS reported growing concern that efforts by the US and Israel to blunt incoming fire are straining missile-interceptor stockpiles, with some Gulf states also running low [6].
  • Iraq front: The Guardian said Iran-backed militias have stepped up attacks on the US, Israel, and allies, with Iraq emerging as a key arena in the confrontation [18].

Perspectives

Israeli military: Israel called its latest operations a “new phase,” saying it is striking “regime infrastructure” in Tehran while also carrying out attacks in Lebanon [10].
