Israeli raid in Lebanon search sparks deadly clashes

Category: World

Israeli forces carried out an overnight airborne raid in Lebanon’s eastern Bekaa Valley, targeting the village of Nabi Sheet/Nabi Chit, as part of a renewed effort to locate the remains of Ron Arad, an Israeli air force navigator missing for decades [1][3][6][5][4]. Lebanese health authorities said at least 41 people were killed and around 40 injured during the operation and the clashes that followed, as residents described intense violence and widespread damage [3][1][4][6][2]. Israel’s military said the mission did not recover any findings related to Arad [1][4]. The raid came as the Israel–Hezbollah conflict sharply escalated: Israel also struck Beirut and warned Lebanon it would pay a “heavy price” if its government does not act against Hezbollah, while Hezbollah has continued attacks on Israel across the border [4][2].

  • Rare insertion: Reports described Israeli helicopters flying troops deep into the Bekaa Valley in an unusually direct airborne ground operation, rather than relying only on stand-off strikes [4][3][6].
  • Graves disturbed: Al Jazeera said Israeli forces dug up graves during the raid while searching for the missing airman [7].
  • Beirut strikes: Separate from the Bekaa raid, Israeli airstrikes hit Beirut’s southern suburbs after evacuation orders, leaving damaged buildings and streets covered in rubble, according to Reuters video cited by Dawn [4].
  • No territorial claims: In a warning to Lebanon’s President Joseph Aoun, Israel’s defence minister said Israel has no territorial claims against Lebanon, while threatening severe consequences if Beirut does not act against Hezbollah [4].
  • Regional trigger: The Hindu linked Lebanon’s deeper involvement to cross-border exchanges that intensified after Hezbollah launched rockets and drones into Israel [2].

Historical Background

Ron Arad, an Israeli air force navigator, went missing in Lebanon in 1986 after parachuting from a damaged aircraft during an operation; Israel has periodically sought information and remains connected to his disappearance [5][1]. Hezbollah is a powerful Iran-backed armed group and political force in Lebanon that has fought multiple rounds of conflict with Israel over the past decades [common]. The reporting also points to a 2024 agreement that Israel says obliges the Lebanese government to disarm Hezbollah — a claim now central to Israel’s warnings and demands [4].
