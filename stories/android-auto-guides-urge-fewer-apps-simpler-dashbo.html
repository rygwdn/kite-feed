Android Auto guides urge fewer apps, simpler dashboards

Category: Technology

Two consumer technology guides published June 20 focused on reducing Android Auto clutter and making the system easier to use while driving. Android Police writer Parth Shah said he installed 10 Android Auto apps before a long road trip but found only four useful for navigation, entertainment, communication and practical assistance [2][3]. ZDNET writer Artie Beaty listed seven Android Auto setup changes, saying users can adjust the app launcher, interface behavior and notifications through phone settings, Android Auto settings and some developer-mode options [1]. The guide said the changes take only minutes and can reduce scrolling to frequently used apps [1].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • App overload: Shah said installing 10 apps made the Android Auto dashboard busier and added more choices than he could realistically use while driving [2].
  • Launcher control: ZDNET said Android Auto users can open Settings, search for Android Auto, choose Customize Launcher, then hide unwanted apps or set a custom order [1].

Perspectives

Parth Shah: Shah said he loaded his dashboard with 10 apps before a long road trip, but found that a leaner setup focused on mapping, music, messaging and utility apps reduced distraction and helped him find what he needed faster.

Sources: Android Police

Artie Beaty: Beaty focused on configuration rather than app selection, pointing readers to built-in settings that can change Android Auto's appearance, operation and notifications.

Sources: ZDNET

Action Items

  • Trim your launcher before driving: In Android Auto's launcher settings, hide apps you rarely use and reorder the rest to put essentials within one tap and reduce scrolling.
  • Curate your road trip dashboard: Load only the apps you expect to need for navigation, entertainment and communication, then test them before you leave; this keeps the dashboard simpler and reduces distractions.
