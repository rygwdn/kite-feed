Calgary Zoo says grizzly cub scaled three-metre fence

Category: Canada

One-year-old grizzly bear Fitz briefly escaped his habitat at the Wilder Institute's Calgary Zoo at about 7:30 a.m. on Sunday, Aug. 16, before the zoo opened to the public [4][6][1]. The zoo said animal care and security teams activated emergency procedures, Calgary police responded, and Fitz was immobilized or anesthetized before being moved safely to a back-of-house space [4][6][1]. Zoo officials said Monday that Fitz escaped by scaling a three-metre wire-and-mesh fence and reaching an area just outside and above his habitat [2][5][6]. Colleen Baird, the zoo's director of animal care, health and welfare, said the zoo will investigate ways to prevent another escape. She said no guests, staff, volunteers or animals were injured [2][4][6].
