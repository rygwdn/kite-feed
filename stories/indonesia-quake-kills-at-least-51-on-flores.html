Indonesia quake kills at least 51 on Flores

Category: World

A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck eastern Indonesia’s Flores Island early Saturday, killing at least 51 people and forcing about 5,000 to evacuate as rescuers searched collapsed buildings and landslide debris [4][9][16][29]. The U.S. Geological Survey placed the epicenter about 68 km north-northwest of Ende in East Nusa Tenggara province, at a shallow depth of about 10 km. Indonesia’s disaster authorities said blocked roads and damaged communications slowed access to remote communities [4][38][12][17]. Deputy Health Minister Benjamin Paulus Octavianus said 36 people were seriously injured and 77 others suffered minor injuries. Damage assessments listed more than 1,300 homes damaged and damage to public facilities across the affected region [4][29][63]. Authorities initially issued a tsunami warning and told coastal residents to move to higher ground, then lifted it after monitoring showed no significant sea-level changes posing a coastal threat, though small tsunami waves were reported in some areas [14][31][38].

Key Points

  • Aftershock count: About 341 aftershocks followed the main quake, keeping many residents outdoors and complicating damage checks [4][8][9].
  • Worst-hit areas: Earlier BNPB figures listed 24 deaths in Manggarai and 17 in East Manggarai, with additional fatalities in Sikka, Ngada, Ende and West Manggarai [8][12][17].
  • Relief supplies: Indonesia sent aid consignments, including 50,000 packages weighing 275 tonnes, while relief workers distributed rice, meals, water, blankets and medicine [28][26][12][63].
  • Second quake: A separate strong earthquake later struck Sumatra, thousands of kilometres west of Flores, with no initial reports of damage or casualties [14][19][55].

Industry Impact

  • Fuel distribution: State energy firm Pertamina said power outages shut down at least 20 petrol stations in affected areas [29].
  • Public services: BNPB preliminary data cited damage to dozens of schools and health facilities, including 87 educational facilities and 18 healthcare centres in one damage summary [26][37].
  • Tourism and transport: Maumere port infrastructure was damaged. Video from the port showed sections of a terminal building collapsing as people waited for an inter-island ferry [27][45].

Historical Background

Indonesia lies on the Pacific Ring of Fire, where colliding tectonic plates make earthquakes and volcanic activity frequent [8][42][common]. A 2022 earthquake in West Java killed hundreds, and several sources described the Flores quake as Indonesia’s deadliest since that disaster [9][16][29].

Humanitarian Impact

About 5,000 people have been displaced or evacuated. Authorities are prioritizing shelter, logistics, food, drinking water, blankets, medicine, electricity restoration and communications repairs [4][8][12][29].

International Reactions

  • 🏳️ International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies: The IFRC said it was preparing to bring aid to about 2,000 people in temporary shelters [28][55].

Travel Advisory

  • Safety: Avoid damaged buildings. Local officials urged residents to stay away from damaged structures because aftershocks could bring them down [33][19].
  • Safety: Expect disrupted movement in affected areas. Landslides and damaged communications are disrupting travel to isolated communities [3][8][12].
  • Emergency: Know earthquake response steps. Seismologist Adam Pascale advised travellers in Indonesia to use 'drop, cover and hold on' to protect themselves from falling debris [34].

Action Items

  • Check on relatives in East Nusa Tenggara: Use SMS or low-bandwidth messaging where possible because power and communications disruptions have affected several communities [8][14].
  • Prepare for aftershocks if in the region: Move away from cracked buildings, keep essentials ready, and follow local disaster agency instructions as aftershocks continue [8][33].
