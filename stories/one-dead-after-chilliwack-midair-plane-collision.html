One dead after Chilliwack midair plane collision

Category: Canada

One man died after two Cessna 172 aircraft collided in midair near Chilliwack Airport in British Columbia’s Fraser Valley on Monday, Aug. 3, police and the Transportation Safety Board of Canada said [1][2][5]. RCMP said one aircraft crashed into a pond near Access Road or Industrial Access Road, where the RCMP Underwater Recovery Team found the man’s body [2][5]. The second aircraft returned safely to Chilliwack Airport, and the two people aboard were not hurt, RCMP said [1][5]. The Transportation Safety Board is investigating the collision. RCMP did not provide a possible cause and said they were assisting the safety board’s work [1][2][5].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Emergency response: B.C. Emergency Health Services said it dispatched five ambulances to Wolfe Road and Schweyey Road at about 5:15 p.m. PT, and no patients were taken to hospital [2][5].
  • Recovery effort: RCMP said crews had begun recovering the man’s body and the submerged Cessna 172 from the pond by Tuesday [1][5].
