Toronto police arrest Canada’s ninth-most-wanted suspect in Scarborough murder

Category: Canada

Toronto police arrested Devondre Hall, a 25-year-old Toronto man, on Friday, Nov. 20, and charged him with first-degree murder in the death of Marcus Charles, whose body was found in a burned vehicle in Scarborough in September [3][6][7]. Hall ranked ninth on the Be On The Lookout (BOLO) Program’s list of Canada’s 25 most wanted fugitives, released the day before his arrest [3][5]. Police also arrested 26-year-old Shyanne Miller and charged her with being an accessory after the fact to murder [6][3]. Two other people had already been arrested in the case: Rushon Powell, charged with first-degree murder, and Shakurwa Jeffrey, charged with being an accessory after the fact [3][6]. Police have not said whether the killing is connected to a second fatal shooting in which a body was found in a burned vehicle in Etobicoke the same night. The investigation continues [7][3][4].

Primary Source: View original article

Key Points

  • Scene details: Charles, 32, was found Sept. 21 near Reesor Road and Steeles Avenue East; investigators said he had been shot before the vehicle was burned [3][7].
  • Separate killing: A second man, 30-year-old Mohamud Kheyre, was found shot dead in a burned vehicle near Royal York Road and Edenbridge Drive in Etobicoke at about 1 a.m. on Sept. 22 [7][3].
  • Police appeal: Investigators are asking anyone with information about Charles’s death to contact Toronto police [4][7].

Perspectives

Toronto police: Spokesperson Stephanie Miceli said investigators would not disclose whether Hall’s arrest followed a tip after the BOLO listing or whether a reward was offered, citing the need to protect informants’ confidentiality and safety.
