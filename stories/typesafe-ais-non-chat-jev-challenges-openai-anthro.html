TypeSafe AI's non-chat Jev challenges OpenAI, Anthropic

Category: AI

TypeSafe AI’s Jev is an artificial-intelligence model without a chat interface, marketed as a cheaper, faster alternative to large language models. The Financial Times described the start-up’s pitch as faster, more efficient AI for developers [4]; Bloomberg said Jev was built by a former OpenAI researcher [5]. Jev drew attention in the week before Bloomberg’s Sept. 25, 2026, report, which said the model was taking on larger competitors [5].
