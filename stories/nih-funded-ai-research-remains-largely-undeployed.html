NIH-funded AI research remains largely undeployed, study finds

Category: AI

Artificial intelligence accounted for 15.9% of the National Institutes of Health’s 2025 research portfolio, but only 14.7% of those projects involved clinical deployment or implementation, according to an analysis of 58,746 funded projects. The researchers found that 79% of AI projects remained at the research or development stage, while health-disparities research accounted for 5.7% of AI-funded work [1]. The UK announced an investment package worth more than £600 million, including funds for AI safety, cybersecurity and domestic businesses [10]. The Philippines’ 2026-33 masterplan sets a $34.4 billion target for AI infrastructure and aims to expand data-center capacity from about 50 megawatts to 1.5 gigawatts by 2033 [21]. A Dartmouth survey found that students see both learning benefits and risks to academic skills from AI, while Purdue researchers observed that students did little to question or build on AI answers during an engineering assignment [5][7]. Dartmouth says it is surveying employers about how AI is changing entry-level jobs and required skills [5].

If we can tell apart the memorization a model needs from the memorization that exposes patients, institutions can share and deploy these models with much greater confidence, and patients can trust that their data is protected.

— Luca Bonomi, assistant professor of biomedical informatics (Vanderbilt University Medical Center)

Key Points

  • Student perceptions: In the Dartmouth survey, 70% of U.S. college students said take-home work had become unfair because of AI, and 39% felt pressure to use it because peers did [5].
  • Classroom study: In Purdue’s exercise, students solved a linear-programming problem manually before asking AI to solve the same task. The system also supplied sensitivity-analysis insights students had not calculated themselves [7].
  • Health deployment: The UK said NHS technology analyzes more than 210,000 chest X-rays monthly and has contributed to an 11.5% increase in suspected lung-cancer cases identified within 24 hours [10].
  • Utility readiness: A study of electricity distributors in Tamil Nadu and Odisha found that data integration, digital infrastructure and in-house analytics capacity—not access to AI tools alone—shape readiness to deploy AI [11].
  • Privacy research: Luca Bonomi, an assistant professor of biomedical informatics at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, received a five-year NIH Director’s New Innovator Award. The award is part of the Common Fund’s High-Risk, High-Reward program for early-career investigators who have not received an NIH R01 or equivalent [22].

Perspectives

Dartmouth survey researchers: Bruce Sacerdote said students report wanting to use AI to learn while assuming their peers are cutting corners, shaping their concerns about fairness. He said universities can teach students to use AI critically and set expectations for its use.

Purdue University researchers: Aparajita Jaiswal and Gaurav Nanda urged instructors to design tasks that require students to verify AI output and explain whether they agree with its reasoning, rather than simply ask for an answer.

Chimei Hospital: Hospital president Hong-Rong Lin said the hospital has not put AI agents into service because it still needs stronger data governance and a secure sandbox to prevent agents from accessing unauthorized records or systems.

Technical Details

  • Model-development plans: The team will incorporate its privacy methods into standard AI development choices, including model selection and pretraining configurations, giving data holders safeguards to consider when adopting models for health care and research [22].
  • Retrieval-augmented generation: Chimei Hospital links its medication-safety AI to the Medi-Span drug database using retrieval-augmented generation, grounding suggestions in updated pharmacy information before a pharmacist makes the final assessment [18].

Industry Impact

  • Public administration: The UK said its AI-powered HMRC digital assistant handled approximately 6.3 million customer interactions during the 2025-26 fiscal year [10].
  • AI workforce and infrastructure: The Philippines’ plan calls for reskilling 1.3 million IT-BPM workers and projects more than 500,000 AI-related jobs, plus 175,000 infrastructure-related jobs, by 2033 [21].

Scientific Significance

  • Research portfolio analysis: The authors used a human-in-the-loop large-language-model method to classify and summarize descriptions of 58,746 NIH-funded projects, enabling large-scale semantic extraction from unstructured research documents [1].
