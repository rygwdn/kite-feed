Houthi strike sets Saudi Aramco office on fire; four injured in Riyadh

Category: World

A missile hit an administrative building at Saudi Aramco’s Dhahran complex on Tuesday evening, starting what the Saudi Energy Ministry described as a limited fire. The ministry reported no injuries there. A company source told Reuters the missile struck the building’s upper floors, while the Saudi-led coalition said it had intercepted projectiles over Dhahran and other areas [27][21][17][3]. Debris from two intercepted missiles injured four people and damaged 15 homes in Riyadh, according to Saudi Civil Defense [22][29]. Hours earlier, the Saudi-led coalition struck the Al-Sabaha fuel station near Sanaa and other Houthi-held energy sites. The coalition said the facilities supported Houthi missile and drone operations. Yemen’s state oil company said Al-Sabaha had no fuel stocks, and a Houthi military spokesman said the site had no military value. The group threatened further retaliation [7][4][42]. The exchanges followed Houthi warnings that Saudi oil facilities were targets after the coalition’s strikes. Saudi forces reported further interceptions on Wednesday, including a cruise missile over Al-Kharj, south of Riyadh [14].
