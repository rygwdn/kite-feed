Update: Manitoba Hydro restores power to Pimicikamak

Category: Canada

In the latest development, Manitoba Hydro says it has repaired a downed power line in northern Manitoba and started restoring electricity to Pimicikamak Cree Nation after an outage that lasted for days [2][4][5]. The outage began late Sunday when a transmission line crossing the Nelson River snapped, leaving residents without heat in freezing winter conditions and prompting a local state of emergency [1][5]. Community leaders had urged the federal government to send military support to help manage the crisis and its knock-on effects while repairs were underway [1][4].
