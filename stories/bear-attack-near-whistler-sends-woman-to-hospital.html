Bear attack near Whistler sends woman to hospital

Category: Canada

A woman was taken to hospital after a bear attacked her Friday evening in the backcountry near Whistler, B.C., according to the B.C. Conservation Officer Service [2][3][4][1]. The service said three hikers encountered two bears near Conflict Lake in the Callaghan Conservancy at about 5 p.m., and one bear attacked the woman before disengaging [2][3][4]. The Conservation Officer Service’s Predator Attack Team, Squamish RCMP and Squamish Search and Rescue flew to the area by helicopter, and rescuers gave the woman first aid at the scene before airlifting her to hospital [2][3][4]. B.C. Emergency Health Services said she was transported in stable condition, while the Conservation Officer Service described her injuries as non-life-threatening [4][2]. Conservation officers said they had not confirmed whether a black bear or grizzly bear was responsible [2][4].
