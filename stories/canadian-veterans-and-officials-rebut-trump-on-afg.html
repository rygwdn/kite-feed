Canadian veterans and officials rebut Trump on Afghanistan role

Category: Canada

Canadian veterans and senior Canadian officials pushed back after President Trump downplayed the contribution of NATO partners in Afghanistan during a Fox News interview, saying the comments diminished allies’ sacrifices [2][3]. Defence Minister David McGuinty highlighted Canada’s military service and losses, emphasizing that Canadians remember what troops and families endured during the mission [1]. The reaction has reopened a sensitive conversation in Canada about how the Afghanistan war is remembered and how allies speak about one another, even as many Canadians continue to express pride in the country’s NATO role [1].
