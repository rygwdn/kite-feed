Canadian pork producers press Iowa trade case before tariff deadline

Category: Canada

A Manitoba-led Canadian delegation went to the Iowa State Fair in Des Moines to argue that cross-border pork trade benefits both countries as Canada-U.S. tariff tensions continued [3][2][4]. Manitoba Pork chair Rick Prejet and general manager Cam Dahl attended the fair, where pork is a major draw for visitors, and Manitoba officials described the trip as a chance to take their message directly to key U.S. stakeholders [3][4]. The trade pitch centered on a supply chain in which Manitoba ships more than 3 million young pigs to the United States each year, with about 2 million going directly to Iowa [3]. The Globe and Mail reported that Ottawa had intensified negotiations in Washington to find a way to avoid 50% duties set to take effect Wednesday, Aug. 19 [4].
