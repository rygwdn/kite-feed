Carney urges Commonwealth to drop Andrew from succession

Category: Canada

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney says Prince Andrew should be removed from the royal line of succession [2][3][4]. As the reports point out, changing the line of succession is procedurally difficult: all 14 Commonwealth realms that share the same monarch would need to sign off, so Canada can’t act on its own [2].
