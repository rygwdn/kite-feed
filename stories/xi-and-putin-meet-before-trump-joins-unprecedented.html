Xi and Putin meet before Trump joins unprecedented three-power summit

Category: World

Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin held talks in Shenzhen on Wednesday, a day before they are due to join U.S. President Donald Trump for the first trilateral meeting of the three countries’ leaders on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit [1][12][4]. Xi and Putin have met five times this year, including once by video link [1][4]. Washington says Thursday’s meeting may address the war in Ukraine, the Iran conflict and nuclear disarmament. The White House initially indicated Ukraine would not be discussed, but Trump later said it would; Beijing has described the agenda more generally as exchanges on peace and strategic stability [1][4]. The last U.S.-Russia nuclear arms treaty expired in February. China says Washington and Moscow should reduce their larger arsenals before Beijing discusses limits on its own [5][12]. The prospect of discussing Ukraine without Kyiv or European governments has caused concern, while analysts interviewed by DW and Al Jazeera expect no major breakthrough [5][10][17]. The trilateral meeting is scheduled for Thursday, Nov. 19, in Shenzhen [12].
