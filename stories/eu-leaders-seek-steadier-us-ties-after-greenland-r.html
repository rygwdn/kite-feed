EU leaders seek steadier US ties after Greenland row

Category: World

European Union leaders met in Brussels for emergency talks after two weeks of tension triggered by President Donald Trump’s renewed threats to take control of Greenland and warnings of possible US tariffs on Europe [2][9][5]. In the latest development, President Trump announced he was backing away from tariff threats tied to countries sending troops to Greenland, citing a “framework” with NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte, which eased immediate pressure but left questions about the details and Denmark’s sovereignty concerns [10][4]. EU leaders said they still want transatlantic cooperation and a trade deal back on track, while also signaling they will defend EU interests if threatened again [1][7].
