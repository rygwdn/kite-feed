Trump endorses Netanyahu days before Israel vote, linking support to Iran war

Category: World

U.S. President Donald Trump endorsed Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Oct. 18, nine days before Israel’s parliamentary election, after months of declining to back his ally publicly [9][14]. Trump praised Netanyahu’s role in the war with Iran and warned that if Israelis chose another leader, the United States would have to continue the fight alone, adding that he was “not inclined” to do so [12][10]. Netanyahu thanked Trump and used the endorsement in a campaign video, saying they would continue their efforts against Iran [22]. Opinion polls show neither Netanyahu’s bloc nor the opposition has a clear route to a governing majority. The Oct. 27 vote is Israel’s first national election since Hamas’s Oct. 7, 2023, attack, and Netanyahu faces criticism over security failures before the assault and his government’s conduct during the ensuing conflicts [10][9]. Trump’s late endorsement followed months of tension over Iran and other regional conflicts, including disagreements over Israeli military actions and Washington’s approach to ending the war [14][50]. Analysts quoted by The Times of Israel said the intervention might not change voters’ minds, though it could draw attention away from other campaign issues [18]. Israelis vote on Oct. 27 [10].

Key Points

  • Coalition threshold: Israel’s parliament has 120 seats, and a government needs a 61-seat majority; polls cited by Sky News Arabia show neither bloc has a clear path to a majority [49].
  • Inquiry dispute: Netanyahu has rejected a state inquiry with access to government documents and the power to compel testimony, favoring a commission with narrower authority [10].
  • Campaign pitch: In a separate video, Netanyahu warned that a nuclear-armed Iran would affect gas prices and said he and Trump would prevent it [12].
  • Diverging positions: Netanyahu has argued that diplomacy with Iran’s leadership cannot succeed, while Trump had pursued a negotiated end to the months-long conflict [22].

Trump has endorsed Netanyahu in earlier Israeli elections; last November, he also wrote to Israeli President Isaac Herzog asking him to pardon Netanyahu in corruption cases [9][14].
