Apple's camera-equipped AirPods reportedly delayed until 2027

Category: Technology

AirPods with cameras are reportedly no longer on Apple’s 2026 release schedule, despite an accidental macOS 26.7 release-candidate leak showing the unreleased earbuds using Visual Intelligence [4][6][8][12][13][18][16][22]. Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reported that Apple had planned a 2026 introduction, but supply-chain and software problems pushed the product to 2027 after advanced employee testing earlier this spring [6][8][12][15][24][25]. The leaked demo, first highlighted by MacRumors and also described by several Apple-focused sites, showed a wearer holding up a book for Siri to identify and save information through Visual Intelligence [8][11][13][15][22][27]. Reports said the AirPods’ cameras are intended to supply real-world context to Siri and other AI tools rather than capture conventional photos or videos [3][2][10][12][22][27]. Apple declined to comment, according to Deccan Chronicle’s account of the Bloomberg report [12].

By the summer [of 2026], when the company had sought to begin producing the earbuds, supply chain and software snags led Apple to postpone the product until 2027

Key Points

  • Two variants: Reports described two internal camera-equipped AirPods projects: B790, a modified AirPods Pro 3 model shown in the leaked video, and B798, a newer AirPods Pro generation still targeted for 2027 [13][18][15][22][29][27].
  • Event speculation: The leak appeared shortly before Apple’s expected September iPhone event, prompting speculation that camera-equipped AirPods could appear with the iPhone 18 Pro lineup before Gurman’s updated 2027 timing report [6][8][12][20][28].
  • Privacy reaction: Deccan Chronicle reported social media criticism of the concept, including users objecting to a wearable that might record people without their knowledge, while the report said the sensors would be low-resolution and not designed for photos or video [12][34][27].
  • Other leaks: The same macOS 26.7 release-candidate code reportedly included references to other unreleased Apple hardware, including AirPods Pro 4 indicators, additional AirPods identifiers, Beats codenames, smart-home devices, Macs, iPhones, an iPad model, and a Vision-related model [32][11][7].

Technical Details

  • Physical design: The Verge noted the AirPods in the demo had noticeably thicker and longer stems than standard AirPods Pro, likely to house the visual sensors [27].
  • Hair obstruction alert: MacRumors reported a setup warning telling users, “To get the most accurate information about things in your environment, make sure AirPods are not covered,” indicating the sensor field of view can be blocked by hair [11][27].
  • Device codenames: B790 reportedly refers to the AirPods Pro 3-derived model shown in the leaked demo, while B798 refers to a more complete next-generation AirPods Pro hardware platform [13][18][15][22].

Industry Impact

  • AI wearables: Reports described the product as part of Apple’s push into AI wearables, with AirPods using Visual Intelligence to supply real-world context to Siri rather than relying only on an iPhone camera [12][9][27].
  • Consumer privacy: Coverage linked camera-equipped AirPods to the same privacy debate around smart glasses, because observers may not be able to tell when a familiar-looking wearable is sensing its surroundings [34][12][27].
  • Apple roadmap: The macOS 26.7 leak also surfaced references to other unreleased hardware categories beyond the AirPods demo [32][11].

Historical Background

Apple introduced Visual Intelligence on the iPhone 16, giving the camera system AI features for identifying text, places and real-world objects [9][27]. Reports said Apple later explored moving that visual input beyond the phone by adding optical sensors to AirPods, while earlier analyst and reporter accounts had pointed to possible 2026 production or launch timing before the latest 2027 reports [28][13][33].
