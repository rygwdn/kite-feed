Samsung sets Wednesday Unpacked as Galaxy S26 leaks spread

Category: Technology

Samsung will hold its Galaxy Unpacked event on Wednesday, Feb. 25, with livestream viewing options widely promoted by outlets covering the show [1][7]. Reports expect Samsung to announce the Galaxy S26, S26 Plus, and S26 Ultra; pre-reservation programs and a steady drip of leaks have already pushed many alleged details into public view [1][2][7][14]. In the run-up, several publications have focused on hands-on leaks tied to the Galaxy S26 Ultra, including early unboxing-style videos and images from a creator who says he bought the device early in the UAE [4][6][18]. The leaked material points to practical themes—less shoulder-surfing on crowded commutes via a rumored “Privacy Display,” possible changes to how the S Pen is handled, and claims of better sustained performance and thermals—while also hinting that camera updates may feel incremental for people upgrading from last year’s model [2][17][11][12][19]. There’s also a cost-and-supply-chain angle. A South Korea report cited by Android Headlines says the highest-end S26 Ultra configuration (16GB RAM/1TB storage) could jump in price in Korea, driven by rising memory component costs [13]. For shoppers, the hopeful takeaway is simple: even when design changes are modest, better efficiency, cooler performance, and stronger on-device privacy—if Samsung confirms them at Unpacked—can make everyday phone use feel smoother and more comfortable [17][15][11].

Did you know: Android Headlines reports that a Samsung employee attached a screen recording to a public Chromium Issue Tracker bug report and included a note saying the test device was an S26—an accidental breadcrumb about the phone [9].

Key Points

  • How to watch: Samsung’s Galaxy Unpacked streams from San Francisco at 10 a.m. PT / 1 p.m. ET, and several outlets have shared instructions for viewing the livestream ahead of time [1][7].
  • Surprise hardware: Beyond the S26 lineup, ZDNET says Samsung may use Unpacked to tease or introduce additional devices, such as a new foldable form factor or smart glasses [8].
  • Colors hint: A leak cited by Digital Trends suggests the S26 Ultra may come in six colors total, including two Samsung.com online-exclusive options described as silver and pink gold [3].
  • Battery expectations: Android Headlines suggests the S26 Ultra may stick with a 5,000mAh capacity, but still improve real-world battery life through efficiency tweaks—even if headline playback figures don’t move much [15].
  • Price pressure: A report cited by Android Headlines says the 16GB/1TB S26 Ultra in South Korea could cost about 2.5 million KRW—roughly a 20% jump versus the prior model—amid rising DRAM and NAND prices [13].
