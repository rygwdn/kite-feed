Apple cannot predict when foldable iPhone supply will meet demand

Category: Technology

Apple chief executive John Ternus said Monday that the company cannot predict when supply of its first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, will catch up with demand. Orders through Apple’s store showed January 2027 delivery estimates, while Apple said production was ramping up [2][3]. The $1,999 phone went on sale Oct. 23 [3][18]. Customers have also reported inner-screen damage. Ternus said a “very small percentage” of devices had been returned for display issues and that Apple is replacing affected units. Customers have reported cracks and lines near the screen’s fold [2][9][12]. Apple has not disclosed the number of affected phones or identified a cause [30]. Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman reported that Apple initially planned a November launch limited to the United States and China but instead released the Duo in more than 70 countries in October. He said Apple launched without enough inventory to meet demand [29]. Apple has not given a date for when supply will meet demand [3].

Primary Source: View original article

Duo is constrained as of today. The ramp is going well, and we're working hard to get it to as many customers as quickly as possible, but at this point, we're not able to predict when supply and demand will be in balance.

— John Ternus, Apple chief executive (The Verge)

Key Points

  • Preorder backlog: Within 30 minutes of preorders opening on Oct. 16, delivery estimates for all eight configurations had slipped to Jan. 25, 2027 [11].
  • Carrier response: Verizon apologized to customers facing delayed Duo orders and offered gift cards, TechCrunch reported [4][20].
  • Launch allocation: Mark Gurman reported that about 80% of initial stock was reserved for Apple Store walk-ins, leaving 20% for preorders and launch-day pickup [23].
  • App support: A developer interviewed by Huxiu said some apps still show black bars or stretched layouts on the inner screen, while a few apps support two-column views [33].

Perspectives

App developer Kyle said Apple’s established screen-layout tools and large user base could encourage developers to adapt apps, but building large-screen experiences still requires more extensive, app-specific work [33].

Sources: Huxiu

Timeline

  • 16 October 2026: Preorders opened; all eight configurations reached January 25 delivery estimates within 30 minutes [11].
  • 23 October 2026: Apple began selling the foldable phone in more than 70 countries [29].
  • 26 October 2026: Apple acknowledged an inner-display issue and directed affected owners to AppleCare [16].
  • 2 November 2026: Ternus said Apple could not forecast when supply would meet demand [2].

Action Items

  • Contact AppleCare if your iPhone Duo develops an inner-display problem. Apple has directed affected owners to AppleCare for help and replacement. The reported process does not require AppleCare+ and also covers phones purchased through a carrier [16].
