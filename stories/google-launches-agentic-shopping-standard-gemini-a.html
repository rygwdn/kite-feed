Google launches agentic shopping standard; Gemini adds checkout

Category: Technology

Google is rolling out new “agentic commerce” capabilities that let people browse and buy products directly inside Gemini and Google’s AI shopping experiences, rather than hopping out to individual retailer websites [2][3][6]. A centerpiece is the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), which Google describes as an open standard meant to connect AI shopping agents with retailer systems and payment options, with support from a roster of large retailers and payments companies [2][4][7]. Walmart and Sam’s Club are among the first highlighted partners, with an experience designed to surface relevant items in conversation and complete purchases within the chat flow, starting in the US with plans to expand internationally [1][5][8].

Primary Source: View original article

Did you know: Google says more than 20 partners have endorsed UCP, spanning retailers, payment networks, and commerce platforms [2].

Key Points

  • Retail conference debut: Google announced UCP and related retail tools at the National Retail Federation “Big Show” in New York, positioning shopping as a major AI focus for 2026 [5][7].
  • Payments roster: Google says UCP has endorsements from more than 20 partners, including American Express, Mastercard, Visa, Stripe, and others; PayPal is described as joining “soon,” and checkout can use Google Pay with shipping info pulled from Google Wallet [2].
  • Business Agent tool: Google is also launching “Business Agent,” described as a brand-voiced virtual sales associate that can answer product questions and support purchasing, with partners including Lowe’s, Michael’s, Poshmark, and Reebok, and Google says it is live starting today [2].
  • Walmart account perks: Android Headlines reports that linking Walmart accounts can enable complementary-item recommendations based on past purchases and surface benefits tied to Walmart+ and Sam’s Club memberships [5].
  • Amazon absent: GeekWire notes that Amazon was not included in Google’s announcement of UCP backers, raising the prospect of a new alternative pathway for product discovery and checkout outside Amazon’s infrastructure [7].

Perspectives

Google: Google frames UCP as an open standard to simplify how AI shopping agents connect to retailers and payments, enabling purchases directly from AI conversations [2].

Sources: Mashable

Walmart and Sam’s Club: Walmart positions the Gemini integration as a way to make shopping more intuitive and “reliable,” with product discovery woven into chat and a smoother purchase flow for Walmart and Sam’s Club items [5].

Sources: Android Headlines

Retail ecosystem partners: Retailers and platforms such as Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target, and Walmart are described as co-developing or backing UCP to reduce the need for one-off integrations across different AI agents [2][7].

Sources: GeekWire

Skeptical observers: The Register characterizes Google’s approach as encouraging shoppers not to leave Google, implying a shift in control over traffic and transactions away from retailer sites [3].

Sources: The Register

Timeline

  • Over the weekend: Google unveiled UCP at NRF’s Big Show in New York [7].
  • Starting today: Google says Business Agent is live with initial brand partners [2].

Technical Details

  • Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP): UCP is described as an open standard for “agentic commerce” that lets AI agents access retailer data and capabilities such as catalogs, pricing, availability, promotions, loyalty programs, and checkout flows [2][7].
  • In-chat checkout: The new flow aims to let shoppers select items and complete a purchase inside an AI conversation, instead of being sent to a retailer website, using Google Pay and Google Wallet details where available [2][3].
  • Brand voice agents: Business Agent is presented as a retailer/brand-controlled “virtual sales associate” that answers product questions in a brand’s voice and can support conversion within the conversation interface [2].

Industry Impact

  • Retail integrations: UCP is positioned as a shared “language” so retailers don’t need separate custom builds for each AI platform, potentially reducing technical overhead for merchants and platforms [7][4].
  • Payments competition: By listing partners across card networks and processors and tying checkout to Google Pay and Google Wallet, Google is signaling that payment choice and identity/shipping rails will be part of the AI-shopping stack [2].
  • Last-mile expectations: Walmart highlights fast delivery claims—thousands of items in under three hours and as fast as 30 minutes—suggesting AI-driven shopping could further raise consumer expectations for speed and inventory accuracy [5].

Historical Background

Chat-based shopping has been evolving alongside voice assistants and conversational commerce, but it has typically required separate retailer-specific integrations or redirects to complete checkout [common]. The new push toward “agentic” shopping reflects a broader trend in which AI systems handle multi-step tasks—like search, comparison, and purchase—inside a single interface [7][9].

Q&A

Q: How will returns, cancellations, and customer support work if checkout happens inside an AI chat?

A: The coverage emphasizes in-chat purchase and retailer connections but does not spell out a universal returns/support flow; details may vary by retailer partner and their existing policies [4][6].

Q: Will UCP work only in Gemini, or can other AI assistants use it too?

A: Google describes UCP as an open standard for agentic commerce with multiple retailer and payment partners, implying it is meant to be usable beyond a single assistant [2][4].

Q: What information does an AI agent need to complete a checkout?

A: Google’s described capabilities include accessing product catalogs, pricing, availability, promotions, loyalty programs, and checkout flows, with payments via Google Pay and shipping info from Google Wallet [2][7].

Action Items

  • Review payment and wallet settings: If you plan to try in-chat checkout, confirm Google Pay is set up and that your Google Wallet has current shipping details, since Google describes these as part of the purchase flow [2].
  • Try the Walmart/Sam’s Club chat experience when available: In Gemini, look for Walmart/Sam’s Club product options surfaced during relevant shopping conversations; the partnership is described as launching first in the US with plans to expand internationally [1][5].
