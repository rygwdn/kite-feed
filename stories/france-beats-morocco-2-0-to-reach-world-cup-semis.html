France beats Morocco 2-0 to reach World Cup semis

Category: World

France beat Morocco 2-0 in a FIFA World Cup quarterfinal at Gillette Stadium outside Boston on July 9, with Kylian Mbappé and Ousmane Dembélé scoring six minutes apart in the second half to send Les Bleus into the semifinals [14][22][10][30]. Mbappé had a first-half penalty saved by Yassine Bounou after a VAR review, then put France ahead in the 60th minute before setting up Dembélé for the second goal six minutes later [24][31][58][54]. The result ended Morocco’s run as the last Arab and African team left in the tournament and repeated France’s 2-0 knockout win over Morocco from the 2022 World Cup, when the teams met in the semifinals in Qatar [11][10][47][59]. France will face Spain or Belgium in the semifinals in the Dallas area on Tuesday, July 14. Mbappé left late with ice on his ankle [9][14][24][31].

Key Points

  • French control: Morocco did not put a shot on goal until Azzedine Ounahi’s 83rd-minute free kick, which Mike Maignan saved [14][24][31][35].
  • Mbappé records: Mbappé’s eighth goal of the tournament put him level with Lionel Messi in the Golden Boot race, and his 20th career World Cup goal left him one behind Messi on the all-time list [30][58][54].
  • Crowd size: The quarterfinal drew 63,811 spectators at Gillette Stadium [30][40][58].
  • Tournament streak: France reached the World Cup semifinals for a third consecutive edition, a first for the French national team [42][15][32].
