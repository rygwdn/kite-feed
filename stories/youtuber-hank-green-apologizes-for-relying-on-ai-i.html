YouTuber Hank Green apologizes for relying on AI in research

Category: AI

YouTuber, novelist and science communicator Hank Green apologized to viewers after criticism of his use of AI tools in content research, saying he had relied too heavily on ChatGPT and would slow his output [4][7][8]. TechCrunch reported that the dispute began after Green used the phrase “I appreciate the pushback” in a Complexly educational video, leading viewers to suspect that a chatbot-generated line had entered the script [4][3]. In a since-deleted X post, Green said he had made the video “under a ton of pressure” and used ChatGPT “for research on this script.” He said the disputed phrase was a response to the episode’s guest, not to an AI prompt [4][8]. He later wrote on Reddit that he was “mortified” and that viewers needed to know his words were his own; he said Hankschannel would post less often or possibly pause for a period [4][8]. Green said he used ChatGPT to find papers and other learning resources, not to generate his final words or “takes,” but acknowledged that the tool had affected his process and that criticism of him for “diluting” himself was fair [4][8]. He also said he was not “a pure AI-hater” and cited concerns about climate change, model training practices and large technology companies consolidating economic power [4][8].

Key Points

  • Output cuts: Green told readers that Hankschannel would slow down and could pause; IT之家 reported that his daily word games SMUSH and 4x3 would also stop updating for now [8].
  • Family feedback: Green said his wife, Katherine, and his brother, John Green, “did not pull any punches” when discussing whether his LLM use had been healthy [3].
  • Audience scale: Green has about 3.2 million YouTube subscribers [4].
  • ChatGPT scrutiny: IT之家 connected the dispute to lawsuits over ChatGPT and mental health claims, plus a June 2026 multistate U.S. investigation into ChatGPT’s effects on teenagers [8].

Perspectives

Green said interacting with LLMs gave him a dopamine hit that was “not healthy for me or good for the world,” and said it had made him careless and disconnected from what people care about.
