U.S. grand jury indicts alleged Tumbler Ridge shooting conspirator

Category: Canada

A U.S. grand jury indicted James Cody Bryant on Oct. 14 on a charge of conspiracy to murder people in a foreign country in connection with the Feb. 10 mass shooting in Tumbler Ridge, B.C. [5][3][4] Prosecutors allege Bryant agreed to record and livestream the attack, advised Jesse Van Rootselaar on weapons and targets, and sent money to buy a lighter for an improvised explosive device. The indictment’s allegations have not been proven; Bryant faces up to life in prison if convicted [5][3]. Bryant, 30, is scheduled to be arraigned on Oct. 22 in federal court in Seattle [3]. A U.S. Attorney’s Office spokesperson said an indictment would eliminate a preliminary hearing scheduled for Oct. 20. The grand jury’s decision establishes probable cause for prosecutors to pursue the case [5][4].

Key Points

  • Online contact: The pair communicated through Discord in thousands of messages during the six months before the shooting, according to police records [3].
  • Livestream tests: Prosecutors allege Bryant tested the livestream several times before the attack and discussed concealing their communications and the stream’s origin [3][6].
  • Joint investigation: The FBI, U.S. Homeland Security and the RCMP are collaborating on the investigation [3].
