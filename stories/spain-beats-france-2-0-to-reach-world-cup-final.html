Spain beats France 2-0 to reach World Cup final

Category: World

Spain defeated France 2-0 Tuesday at Dallas Stadium in Arlington, Texas, to advance to the 2026 FIFA World Cup final [3][5][27][32]. Mikel Oyarzabal put Spain ahead with a penalty in the 22nd minute after Lamine Yamal drew a foul from Lucas Digne, and Pedro Porro made it 2-0 in the 58th after combining with Dani Olmo [3][26][20][44]. France, the tournament favorite, was shut out [10][17][44]. Spain will face either defending champion Argentina or England in Sunday's final in East Rutherford, New Jersey, after the second semifinal in Atlanta on Wednesday; France will play the third-place match in Miami on Saturday [3][27][32][14].

Did you know: France played the semifinal on Bastille Day, the national holiday marking the 1789 storming of the Bastille prison [21].

Key Points

  • Defensive control: Spain limited France to no shots on target in the first half, while Kylian Mbappé, Ousmane Dembélé and Michael Olise did not score [52][17][28].
  • French setback: William Saliba left before halftime after a recurrence of a lower back injury and was replaced by Maxence Lacroix [10][52].
  • Late chance: France's clearest opportunity came in the 82nd minute, when Unai Simón recovered to save from Désiré Doué after coming far off his line [27][32].
  • Midfield edge: Rodri and Fabián Ruiz controlled Spain's midfield, while France's Adrien Rabiot and Aurélien Tchouaméni struggled to establish possession [20][29][30].

Perspectives

Kylian Mbappé: France's captain said the team failed tactically and technically, adding that France wanted to press high to stop Spain's controlled rhythm but did not execute that plan.

Historical Background

Spain won its only men's World Cup title in 2010 and had not returned to the final until this tournament [3][37]. France won the 2018 World Cup and lost the 2022 final to Argentina before trying to become only the third team to reach three consecutive men's World Cup finals [37][44]. Spain also beat France in tournament semifinals in 2024 at the European Championship and in the 2025 UEFA Nations League [44][48].
