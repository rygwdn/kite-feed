Samsung teasers point to wider Galaxy Z Fold 8 design

Category: Technology

Samsung began a social media teaser campaign for its next Galaxy foldables, posting short videos of everyday objects such as chocolate, pizza, puzzles and paint being reshaped in a nod to a shorter, wider device format [2][5][6][7][8]. Samsung also wiped or nearly emptied its Samsung Mobile Instagram accounts before posting teasers that spell out or point to the phrase “New Shape, New Joy” [11][1][17][22]. The videos do not name a product, but several reports connect the campaign to the Galaxy Z Fold 8, a foldable expected to move away from Samsung’s tall-and-narrow Fold proportions toward a boxier, wider body [2][9][11][20][38]. Other leaks and render reports point to a three-device lineup: Galaxy Z Fold 8, Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Flip 8, with the Fold 8 using the wider design and the Ultra retaining a more traditional Fold-style shape [13][29][33][37][34]. Samsung has not announced a launch date, though Korean and international sources cite July 22 as the expected Galaxy Unpacked date [8][18][20][47]. Korean reports said Samsung described the eighth-generation foldable lineup as adding a new choice alongside the Fold and Flip while expanding use cases for work, creativity, communication and content consumption [15][18][23].

Key Points

  • Leaked dimensions: Leaked specs put the Galaxy Z Fold 8 at 123.9 x 81.9 x 9.7mm folded and 123.9 x 161.4 x 4.5mm unfolded, compared with the Z Fold 7’s reported 158.4 x 72.8 x 8.9mm folded size [25][24][35].
  • Display format: Leaks point to a 7.6-inch QHD+ inner display with a 4:3 aspect ratio and a 5.5-inch QHD+ cover display with a 16:10 aspect ratio [25][24][26].
  • Hardware leak: Leaked specifications list a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chip, 12GB of RAM, storage options up to 1TB, a 4,800mAh battery, 45W wired charging and 15W wireless charging [25][24][26].
  • Camera lineup: A Korean camera-supply report cited by 9to5Google lists the Fold 8 with 50MP main and 50MP ultrawide rear cameras, while the Fold 8 Ultra is described with a 200MP main camera, 50MP ultrawide, 10MP telephoto and two 10MP selfie cameras [28][43].
  • Accessory leak: Official-looking case renders show clear, kickstand and aramid fiber cases for Samsung’s next foldables, including some with magnets for wireless charging alignment [42][40][39].

Technical Details

  • 4:3 inner display: The rumored 4:3 inner display would make the open Fold 8 closer to a small tablet shape than the taller Fold layout, while the 16:10 cover display would also be wider than Samsung’s recent narrow outer screens [25][24][16].
  • Qi2-ready cases: Leaked case images suggest Samsung may again rely on first-party cases with magnets for wireless charging alignment rather than placing Qi2 magnets directly in every phone body [40][42].
  • Sensor sourcing: 9to5Google said the Fold 8 Ultra’s reported 200MP main camera uses Samsung’s ISOCELL HP2 sensor and attributed other Z Fold sensors to suppliers including Namuga, Powerlogics, MCnex and Camsys [28].

Historical Background

Samsung introduced its first Galaxy Fold in 2018 and later built its foldable phone strategy around two lines: the book-style Galaxy Z Fold and the clamshell Galaxy Z Flip [18][23]. Korean reports said Samsung has now accumulated seven generations of foldable development before preparing the eighth-generation lineup [23].

Business Angle

  • Samsung’s wider Fold strategy adds a premium variant aimed at a distinct use case, according to reports describing the model as a new choice alongside the Fold and Flip [23][13].
  • More foldable models increase supply-chain and inventory risk because leaks point to different bodies, camera setups, batteries and accessories across the Fold 8, Fold 8 Ultra and Flip 8 [28][42].
  • Reports that Samsung may reduce emphasis on the Flip line suggest management could be reallocating resources toward larger foldables and future rollables [46][45].
  • Reports frame the wider Fold 8 as a response to rival foldables, including a long-rumored Apple foldable [9][36][20].

Action Items

  • Wait for confirmed launch details before buying accessories: Samsung has not officially named the devices or confirmed all specifications, while leaks show multiple Fold 8 variants with different sizes and case designs [8][42][13].
  • Compare the Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra formats: Use leaked dimensions and renders to decide whether a shorter, wider Fold 8 or a taller Fold 8 Ultra better fits one-handed cover-screen use, reading, video and multitasking [25][29][35].
