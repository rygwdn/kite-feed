Analysts say GTA VI needs PC release to expand beyond console market

Category: Technology

Analysts estimate Rockstar Games’ Grand Theft Auto VI sold 40.2 million to 47 million copies over its launch weekend, with several forecasting at least 50 million by year-end. The game, released Nov. 19, generated $3.79 billion on its first day, while Sony said more than 39 million people played it on PlayStation 5 during its first four days [1][17][19]. Analysts say console sales face a limited audience: Video-game market analyst Joost van Dreunen estimates about 137 million current-generation consoles are in use and says roughly one-quarter of that base bought the game before release. They identify a PC version, which has not been announced, as the main route to a larger market. Van Dreunen forecasts lifetime sales of 200 million to 250 million copies, while Enders Analysis’ Gareth Sutcliffe says reaching 100 million quickly would require more platforms [1]. Sony also reported 683 million hours played on PS5 from Nov. 19 through Nov. 22, an average of 17.4 hours per player. Near-term sales depend on holiday demand and console availability; analysts say Sony and Microsoft have limited ability to increase supply this quarter and in the first quarter [19][1].

Key Points

  • Streaming audience: Streams Charts counted more than 420 million hours of GTA VI viewing across platforms in its first seven days, nearly three times the previous record held by Valorant’s 2020 closed beta [17].
  • Physical sales: Despite being sold in a box without a disc, the game generated £32 million in UK physical-retail revenue in three days, marking the country’s largest physical launch since 2019 [12].

Perspectives

Video-game market analysts: Enders Analysis’ Gareth Sutcliffe identifies support for more Apple-silicon iPhones and iPads from recent generations as a potential mobile expansion that could bring the game’s market to hundreds of millions of devices [1].

Technical Details

  • Xbox One cloud streaming: Digital Foundry found that the older Xbox One streams the Xbox Series S build rather than running GTA VI locally. The streamed image looked broadly similar, but frame pacing was less smooth [3].
  • PS5 power draw reports: A Polish console technician cited measurements of about 220 watts at the wall during light gameplay and 274.2 watts in a separate maximum-load test; no official finding linked those readings to reported console failures [6][19].

Industry Impact

  • Game platforms: Sony said GTA VI was the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles connected since the game’s launch. The release also set the platform’s concurrent-player record [19][17].
  • Retail formats: Estimates put physical sales in France above 1 million copies in the first three days, comparable to GTA V’s launch. In Spain, GTA VI had the largest physical launch in chart history [12].

Historical Background

Rockstar dismissed about 34 employees in the United Kingdom and Canada in October 2025, saying they had shared confidential company information in a Discord server; 23 former employees later brought a case alleging anti-union retaliation [9]. Researchers studying game-industry work practices have described “crunch” as sustained, heavy overtime and traced how it became normalized in the industry [9].

Business Angle

  • The launch could bolster Sony’s decision to end disc production from 2028, but physical formats still account for close to half of Nintendo’s first-party sales on Switch and Switch 2 [12].
