Google opens Dreambeans to U.S. Google Account holders, prepares Gemini Notebook interactive reports

Category: AI

Google made Dreambeans, a Google Labs app that creates personalized illustrated daily stories, free to all users in the United States with a Google Account after initially limiting access to Google AI Ultra subscribers [2][1]. The Android and iOS app uses Google’s AI to generate “beans” based on user interests, and PCMag reported it can draw on Gmail, Calendar, Photos, YouTube and Search history if a user grants permission [2]. Google is also preparing an Interactive Reports feature for Gemini Notebook, TestingCatalog reported after finding a new Studio-panel notice and a new “Interactive” option inside the report creator [3]. The interface describes the option as “An interactive report with embedded Studio content,” and TestingCatalog said the feature is aimed at research-heavy workflows that combine multiple sources into structured outputs [3].
