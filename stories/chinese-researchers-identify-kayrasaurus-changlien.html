Chinese researchers identify Kayrasaurus changliensis, a giant-clawed dinosaur

Category: Science

Paleontologists identified Kayrasaurus changliensis, a new therizinosaur species from a nearly complete fossil found in China’s Yixian Formation in Liaoning. The fossil is about 125 million years old and belonged to an animal more than 3.6 meters long that was not yet fully grown; researchers estimated its weight at 117 to 274 kilograms [4]. Researchers consider the species the largest therizinosaur known from the Jehol ecosystem, at least 1.6 times the size of Beipiaosaurus, previously the largest known local species [4].
